### Verb — verb

## skrive

**English:** to write

**Topics:** communication, school

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å skrive | Jeg liker å skrive. | I like to write. |
| Presens | skriver | Hun skriver et brev. | She is writing a letter. |
| Preteritum | skrev / skreiv | Jeg skrev et brev i går. / Jeg skreiv et brev i går. | I wrote a letter yesterday. / I wrote a letter yesterday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | skrevet | Brevet er skrevet. | The letter is written. |
| Presens perfektum | har skrevet | Jeg har skrevet et brev. | I have written a letter. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde skrevet | Jeg hadde skrevet brevet før hun kom. | I had written the letter before she arrived. |
| Futurum | skal skrive / vil skrive | Jeg skal skrive i morgen. / Jeg vil skrive mer i morgen. | I am going to write tomorrow. / I will write more tomorrow. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha skrevet / vil ha skrevet | Innen fredag skal jeg ha skrevet rapporten. / Innen fredag vil jeg ha skrevet rapporten. | By Friday, I will have written the report. / By Friday, I will have written the report. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville skrive | Jeg ville skrive hvis jeg fikk tid. | I would write if I had time. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha skrevet | Jeg ville ha skrevet mer hvis jeg hadde hatt tid. | I would have written more if I had had time. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle skrive | Hun sa at hun skulle skrive senere. | She said that she would write later. |
| Imperativ | skriv | Skriv navnet ditt her! | Write your name here! |
| Presens partisipp | skrivende | Han er et skrivende menneske. | He is someone who writes. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | skrives | Brevet må skrives i dag. | The letter must be written today. |
| S-passiv, presens | skrives | Rapporten skrives nå. | The report is being written now. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir skrevet | Brevet blir skrevet nå. | The letter is being written now. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble skrevet | Brevet ble skrevet i går. | The letter was written yesterday. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt skrevet | Rapporten har blitt skrevet. | The report has been written. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | skrevet / skreven | Dette er en skrevet tekst. / Dette er en skreven tekst. | This is a written text. / This is a written text. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | skrevet | Dette er et skrevet brev. | This is a written letter. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | skrevne | Den skrevne teksten ligger her. | The written text is here. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | skrevne | De skrevne tekstene ligger her. | The written texts are here. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword/forms checked; compound constructions and original example sentences supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** Skrev and skreiv are accepted past forms. Skulle skrive below expresses a future viewed from a past context; it is not labelled as interchangeable with conditional ville skrive. Compound tenses and passive constructions are shown in context. The agreeing past-participle forms are adjectival uses. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Sources:**

- [Bokmålsordboka 53628](https://ordbokene.no/bm/53628)

### Adjektiv — adjective

## god

**English:** good

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | god | Han er en god lærer. | He is a good teacher. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | god | Hun er ei god venninne. | She is a good friend. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | godt | Dette er et godt forslag. | This is a good suggestion. |
| Positiv flertall | gode | Vi har gode venner. | We have good friends. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | gode | Den gode læreren hjelper oss. | The good teacher helps us. |
| Komparativ | bedre | Denne boka er bedre enn den andre. | This book is better than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | best | Denne boka er best. | This book is the best. |
| Superlativ bestemt | beste | Dette er den beste boka. | This is the best book. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword/forms checked; compound constructions and original example sentences supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** This adjective has irregular comparison: god → bedre → best. The definite superlative is beste. For other adjectives, use mer/mest only where the meaning permits comparison.

### Pronomen — pronoun

## han

**English:** he; him

**Topics:** family, communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | personlig pronomen | Han bor her. | He lives here. |
| Person | 3. person | Han leser en bok. | He is reading a book. |
| Tall | entall | Han kommer alene. | He is coming alone. |
| Subjektsform | han | Han liker kaffe. | He likes coffee. |
| Objektsform | ham / han | Jeg så ham i går. / Jeg så han i går. | I saw him yesterday. / I saw him yesterday. |
| Tilhørende refleksivt pronomen | seg | Han vasker seg. | He washes himself. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword/forms checked; compound constructions and original example sentences supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** Both ham and han are accepted object forms. Seg is the related third-person reflexive pronoun, not a case form of han. The reflexive example is separated from the subject/object forms. Person, number and type are grammatical features, not extra inflections.

### Determinativ — determiner

## min

**English:** my; mine

**Topics:** family, home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | possessivt determinativ | Dette er bilen min. | This is my car. |
| Hankjønn | min | Bilen min er rød. | My car is red. |
| Hunkjønn | mi | Boka mi ligger her. | My book is here. |
| Intetkjønn | mitt | Huset mitt er lite. | My house is small. |
| Flertall | mine | Vennene mine kommer i kveld. | My friends are coming tonight. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword/forms checked; compound constructions and original example sentences supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** Possessive min agrees with the thing possessed, not the owner. After the noun, use a definite noun: bilen min. Before the noun, use an indefinite noun: min bil. Mi is the feminine alternative; boken min is also accepted.

### Subjunksjon — subordinating conjunction

## fordi

**English:** because

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | subjunksjon | Vi blir hjemme fordi det regner. | We are staying home because it is raining. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: fordi | Hun smiler fordi hun er glad. | She is smiling because she is happy. |
| Konstruksjon | fordi + leddsetning | Jeg drar fordi bussen går nå. | I am leaving because the bus leaves now. |
| Leddsetning med ikke | fordi + subjekt + ikke + verb | Jeg blir hjemme fordi jeg ikke er frisk. | I am staying home because I am not well. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword/forms checked; compound constructions and original example sentences supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** Fordi introduces a subordinate clause. In the neutral subordinate-clause order shown here, ikke precedes the finite verb. The rows illustrate syntax, not inflection.

- [Bokmålsordboka 16057](https://ordbokene.no/bm/16057)

### Uttrykk — multiword expression

## ta hensyn til

**English:** to take into account; to be considerate of

**Topics:** society, family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å ta hensyn til | Det er viktig å ta hensyn til barna. | It is important to take the children into account. |
| Presens | tar hensyn til | Hun tar hensyn til naboene. | She is considerate of the neighbours. |
| Preteritum | tok hensyn til | Han tok hensyn til været. | He took the weather into account. |
| Presens perfektum | har tatt hensyn til | Vi har tatt hensyn til innspillene. | We have taken the feedback into account. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde tatt hensyn til | Vi hadde tatt hensyn til alt før vi bestemte oss. | We had taken everything into account before we decided. |
| Futurum | skal ta hensyn til | Vi skal ta hensyn til alle. | We will take everyone into account. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville ta hensyn til | Jeg ville ta hensyn til det hvis jeg kunne. | I would take that into account if I could. |
| Imperativ | ta hensyn til | Ta hensyn til naboene! | Be considerate of the neighbours! |
| S-passiv, upersonlig | tas hensyn til | Det tas hensyn til naboene. | The neighbours are being taken into account. |
| Bli-passiv, upersonlig | blir tatt hensyn til | Det blir tatt hensyn til barna. | The children are being taken into account. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword/forms checked; compound constructions and original example sentences supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** The verb ta changes form; hensyn til remains fixed. Til normally needs a complement. These tense and passive rows are construction examples, not a claim that every expression admits the same patterns. The two passive examples use impersonal det.

**Sources:**

- [Bokmålsordboka 59502](https://ordbokene.no/bm/59502)
- [Bokmålsordboka 23263](https://ordbokene.no/bm/23263)

### Tallord — numeral

## to

**English:** two

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | grunntall | Jeg har to bøker. | I have two books. |
| Grunntall | to | Vi trenger to billetter. | We need two tickets. |
| Kjønnsbøyning | ubøyelig: to | Jeg ser to biler, to bøker og to hus. | I see two cars, two books and two houses. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | andre | Hun bor i andre etasje. | She lives on the second floor. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword/forms checked; compound constructions and original example sentences supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** To is an invariable cardinal numeral. Andre is the related ordinal meaning second; it is a separate word, not an inflected form of to. Ordinal numerals are classified as adjectives in Bokmålsordboka. Numerals are included as a separate learning category in this project.

**Sources:**

- [Bokmålsordboka 61288](https://ordbokene.no/bm/61288)
- [Bokmålsordboka 107465](https://ordbokene.no/bm/107465)
