Other English meanings are carried over from the attachment and have not all been independently verified.

CEFR values are source English levels, not verified Norwegian difficulty ratings. Phrases retain their complements; verb phrase templates use the dictionary head verb. Multiword expressions and unmatched entries are marked separately.
