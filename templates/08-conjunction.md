## Copyable structure

Copy the structure below into a vocabulary file, replace every placeholder, and add the metadata described in the project guide. Each applicable form needs its own example and English translation. For a form that does not apply, write — and explain the reason; do not invent a sentence for it.

## Worked example

## og

**English:** and

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | konjunksjon | Ola og Kari kommer. | Ola and Kari are coming. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: og | Vi kjøper melk og brød. | We are buying milk and bread. |
| Konstruksjon, ord | ord + og + ord | Jeg liker te og kaffe. | I like tea and coffee. |
| Konstruksjon, helsetninger | helsetning + og + helsetning | Jeg lager mat, og hun dekker bordet. | I am cooking, and she is setting the table. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword/forms checked; compound constructions and original example sentences supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** Og is invariable and coordinates words, phrases or clauses. It does not introduce the subordinate-clause word order illustrated under fordi.

**Sources:**

- [Bokmålsordboka 41861](https://ordbokene.no/bm/41861)
