## Copyable structure

Copy the structure below into a vocabulary file, replace every placeholder, and add the metadata described in the project guide. Each applicable form needs its own example and English translation. For a form that does not apply, write — and explain the reason; do not invent a sentence for it.

```markdown
## [Word]

**English:** [Meaning]

**Topics:** [Topic(s)]

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | [Form or feature] | [One sentence using this form] | [English translation] |
| Bøyning | [Form or feature] | [One sentence using this form] | [English translation] |
| Bruk | [Form or feature] | [One sentence using this form] | [English translation] |

**Form status:** [Verified / derived / unverified / inapplicable]

**Notes:** [Alternatives, restrictions, grammar and source uncertainty]

**Sources:**

- [Source title](https://example.org)
```

## Worked example

## hei

**English:** hi; hello

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | interjeksjon | Hei! Hvordan går det? | Hi! How are you? |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: hei | Hei, Anna! | Hi, Anna! |
| Bruk | hilsen | Hei! Hyggelig å se deg. | Hello! Nice to see you. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword/forms checked; compound constructions and original example sentences supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** Hei is invariable. Punctuation and tone depend on the greeting; punctuation is not grammatical inflection.

**Sources:**

- [Bokmålsordboka 22718](https://ordbokene.no/bm/22718)
