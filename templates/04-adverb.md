## Copyable structure

Copy the structure below into a vocabulary file, replace every placeholder, and add the metadata described in the project guide. Each applicable form needs its own example and English translation. For a form that does not apply, write — and explain the reason; do not invent a sentence for it.

## Worked example

## fort

**English:** quickly; fast

**Topics:** time, sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform / positiv | fort | Han løper fort. | He runs fast. |
| Komparativ | fortere | Hun løper fortere enn meg. | She runs faster than me. |
| Superlativ | fortest | Per løper fortest av alle. | Per runs the fastest of all. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword/forms checked; compound constructions and original example sentences supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** Fort is listed as an adjective in Bokmålsordboka and is used adverbially here. The examples illustrate adverbial comparison. Many lexical adverbs, such as alltid, have no comparative or superlative; mark those rows — and explain why.
