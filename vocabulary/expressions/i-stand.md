## i stand

**English:** able

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Konstruksjon | i stand til å | Hun er i stand til å gå uten hjelp. | She is able to walk without help. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The ability construction is i stand til å followed by an infinitive.
