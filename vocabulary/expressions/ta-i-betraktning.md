## ta i betraktning

**English:** to take into account

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å ta i betraktning | Vi må ta været i betraktning. | We need to take the weather into account. |
| Presens | tar i betraktning | De tar kostnadene i betraktning. | They take the costs into account. |
| Preteritum | tok i betraktning | Vi tok reisetida i betraktning. | We took travel time into account. |
| Presens perfektum | har tatt i betraktning | Hun har tatt alle innspillene i betraktning. | She has taken all the feedback into account. |
| Imperativ | ta i betraktning | Ta trafikken i betraktning når du velger avreisetid. | Take traffic into account when choosing your departure time. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Ta inflects. The object may occur before i betraktning.
