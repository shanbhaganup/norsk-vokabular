## i forveien

**English:** in advance, beforehand

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast uttrykk | i forveien | Vi bestilte billetter i forveien. | We booked tickets in advance. |
| Tidsmål + uttrykk | i god tid i forveien | Si fra i god tid i forveien. | Let us know well in advance. |
