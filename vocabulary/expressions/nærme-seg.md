## nærme seg

**English:** to approach, to get closer

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å nærme seg | Skipet begynte å nærme seg land. | The ship began to approach land. |
| Presens | nærmer seg | Fristen nærmer seg. | The deadline is approaching. |
| Preteritum | nærmet seg | Hun nærmet seg huset i mørket. | She approached the house in the dark. |
| Presens perfektum | har nærmet seg | Toget har nærmet seg stasjonen. | The train has drawn closer to the station. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The verb inflects and the reflexive pronoun follows the subject.
