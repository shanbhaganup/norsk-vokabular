## det går bra

**English:** I’m fine; it’s going well

**Topics:** greetings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Svarformel | det går bra | «Hvordan går det?» «Det går bra, takk.» | “How are things?” “I’m fine, thanks.” |

**Form status:** Spelling and contextual usage reviewed.

**Notes:** A conventional answer or reassurance. Går is the present of gå. NoW source form: “det går bra!”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
