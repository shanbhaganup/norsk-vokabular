## vise fram

**English:** to show, to display

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å vise fram | Hun ville vise fram tegningene sine. | She wanted to show her drawings. |
| Infinitiv, alternativ | å vise frem | De fikk lov til å vise frem modellen. | They were allowed to show the model. |
| Presens | viser fram | Han viser fram bildene fra turen. | He’s showing the pictures from the trip. |
| Preteritum | viste fram | Hun viste fram det nye kjøkkenet. | She showed us the new kitchen. |
| Presens perfektum | har vist fram | Vi har vist fram samlingen på skolen. | We’ve displayed the collection at school. |
| Imperativ | vis fram | Vis fram billetten ved døra. | Show your ticket at the door. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Vise inflects. Fram and frem are accepted alternatives.
