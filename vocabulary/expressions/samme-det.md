## samme det

**English:** it doesn’t matter, whatever

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast uttrykk | samme det | «Skal vi ta buss eller tog?» «Samme det.» | “Should we take the bus or the train?” “Either is fine.” |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** An informal response that can sound dismissive.
