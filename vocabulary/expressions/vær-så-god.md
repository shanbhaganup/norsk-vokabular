## vær så god

**English:** here you are; please; you’re welcome

**Topics:** greetings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Overrekkelse | vær så god | Vær så god, her er billetten din. | Here you are, this is your ticket. |
| Invitasjon | vær så god | Vær så god, bare sett deg. | Please, have a seat. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** A polite formula when handing someone an item, inviting them to proceed, or answering thanks. NoW source form: “vær så god”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
