## ja da

**English:** yes, of course; sure

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Svarformel | ja da | «Rekker du bussen?» «Ja da, jeg går nå.» | “Will you make the bus?” “Sure, I’m leaving now.” |

**Form status:** Spelling and contextual usage reviewed.

**Notes:** An affirmative response. Tone can make it reassuring or impatient. NoW source form: “ja da”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
