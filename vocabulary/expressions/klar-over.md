## klar over

**English:** aware of

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entall | klar over | Hun er klar over problemet. | She is aware of the problem. |
| Flertall | klare over | De er klare over risikoen. | They are aware of the risk. |
| Konstruksjon | klar over at + leddsetning | Er du klar over at døra står åpen? | Are you aware that the door is open? |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Klar agrees with the subject. Over introduces a fact or situation.
