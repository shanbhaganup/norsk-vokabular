## det spørs

**English:** it depends

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Svarformel | det spørs | «Blir du med?» «Det spørs.» | “Are you coming along?” “It depends.” |
| Med indirekte spørsmål | det spørs | Det spørs om vi får tid. | It depends on whether we have time. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** A fixed impersonal expression used in the present. It can stand alone or be followed by an indirect question. NoW source form: “spørs”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
