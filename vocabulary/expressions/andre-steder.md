## andre steder

**English:** elsewhere, in other places

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnuttrykk | andre steder | Vi måtte lete andre steder. | We had to look elsewhere. |
| Konstruksjon, sted | andre steder + stedsledd | Andre steder i byen er det lettere å parkere. | It’s easier to park elsewhere in the city. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Andre is plural here and agrees with steder. The whole phrase functions as a place adverbial.
