## en gang

**English:** once

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast uttrykk | en gang | Jeg har vært der en gang. | I’ve been there once. |
| Konstruksjon, frekvens | en gang + per tidsenhet | Vi møtes en gang i uka. | We meet once a week. |
