## samle inn

**English:** to collect

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å samle inn | De prøver å samle inn penger til et nytt tak. | They’re trying to raise money for a new roof. |
| Presens | samler inn | Læreren samler inn oppgavene. | The teacher is collecting the assignments. |
| Preteritum | samlet inn | Vi samlet inn gamle bøker. | We collected old books. |
| Preteritum, alternativ | samla inn | Hun samla inn underskrifter til oppropet. | She collected signatures for the petition. |
| Presens perfektum | har samlet inn | De har samlet inn nok penger. | They’ve raised enough money. |
| Presens perfektum, alternativ | har samla inn | Han har samla inn alle skjemaene. | He’s collected all the forms. |
| Imperativ | samle inn | Samle inn arkene før dere går. | Collect the sheets before you leave. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Samle inflects and inn remains fixed.
