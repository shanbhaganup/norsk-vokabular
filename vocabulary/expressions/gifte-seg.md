## gifte seg

**English:** to get married

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å gifte seg | De planlegger å gifte seg neste år. | They plan to get married next year. |
| Presens | gifter seg | Søsteren min gifter seg i juni. | My sister is getting married in June. |
| Preteritum | giftet seg | De giftet seg på rådhuset. | They got married at city hall. |
| Preteritum, alternativ | gifta seg | Venninna mi gifta seg i fjor. | My friend got married last year. |
| Presens perfektum | har giftet seg | Naboen har giftet seg. | The neighbour has got married. |
| Presens perfektum, alternativ | har gifta seg | De har gifta seg i all hemmelighet. | They have got married in secret. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Gifte inflects and the reflexive pronoun changes with the subject.
