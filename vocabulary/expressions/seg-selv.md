## seg selv

**English:** himself, herself, itself, themselves

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnuttrykk | seg selv | Barnet så seg selv i speilet. | The child saw itself in the mirror. |
| Alternativ skrivemåte | seg sjøl | De måtte klare seg sjøl. | They had to manage by themselves. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Seg refers back to a third-person subject, singular or plural. Sjøl is an accepted spelling of selv.
