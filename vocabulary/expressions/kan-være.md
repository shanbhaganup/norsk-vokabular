## kan være

**English:** may be, could be

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Presens, konstruksjon | kan være | Det kan være for sent nå. | It may be too late now. |
| Preteritum, konstruksjon | kunne være | Det kunne være vanskelig å finne veien. | It could be difficult to find the way. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The modal expression marks possibility. The reference gloss maybe is an idiomatic translation in some contexts.
