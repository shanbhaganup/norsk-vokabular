## hvem sin

**English:** whose

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | hvem sin | Hvem sin sykkel står ute? | Whose bike is outside? |
| Hunkjønn | hvem si | Hvem si jakke ligger her? | Whose jacket is lying here? |
| Intetkjønn | hvem sitt | Hvem sitt skjerf er dette? | Whose scarf is this? |
| Flertall | hvem sine | Hvem sine sko står i gangen? | Whose shoes are in the hall? |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The possessive agrees with the thing possessed. This construction is common in speech.
