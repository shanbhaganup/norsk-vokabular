## en rekke

**English:** a number of, a series of

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnuttrykk | en rekke | Hun tok opp en rekke spørsmål. | She raised a number of questions. |
| Konstruksjon | en rekke + substantiv i flertall | Vi fikk en rekke klager. | We received a number of complaints. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** En rekke followed by a plural noun can quantify a group.
