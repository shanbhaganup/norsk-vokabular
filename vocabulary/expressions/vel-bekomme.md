## vel bekomme

**English:** you’re welcome; enjoy your meal

**Topics:** greetings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Svarformel | vel bekomme | «Takk for maten!» «Vel bekomme!» | “Thank you for the meal!” “You’re welcome!” |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** A conventional response, particularly after thanks for the meal. It can also wish someone enjoyment of a meal. NoW source form: “vel bekomme”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
