## gå i stykker

**English:** to break, to stop working

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å gå i stykker | Den gamle låsen kan gå i stykker. | The old lock could break. |
| Presens | går i stykker | Koppen går i stykker hvis den faller. | The cup will break if it falls. |
| Preteritum | gikk i stykker | Vaskemaskinen gikk i stykker i går. | The washing machine broke yesterday. |
| Presens perfektum | har gått i stykker | Klokka har gått i stykker. | The clock has broken. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source gått i stykker is the past-participle construction. Gå is the inflecting element.
