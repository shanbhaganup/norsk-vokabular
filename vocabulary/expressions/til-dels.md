## til dels

**English:** partly, to some extent

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast uttrykk | til dels | Resultatet skyldtes til dels godt vær. | The result was partly due to good weather. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The fixed phrase qualifies the extent of a statement.
