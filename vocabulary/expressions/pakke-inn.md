## pakke inn

**English:** to wrap

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å pakke inn | Jeg glemte å pakke inn gaven. | I forgot to wrap the present. |
| Presens | pakker inn | Hun pakker inn bøkene. | She is wrapping the books. |
| Preteritum | pakket inn | Han pakket inn glassene i avispapir. | He wrapped the glasses in newspaper. |
| Preteritum, alternativ | pakka inn | Vi pakka inn koppen i et håndkle. | We wrapped the cup in a towel. |
| Presens perfektum | har pakket inn | De har pakket inn gavene. | They’ve wrapped the presents. |
| Presens perfektum, alternativ | har pakka inn | Jeg har pakka inn den siste boka. | I’ve wrapped the last book. |
| Imperativ | pakk inn | Pakk inn den store esken først. | Wrap the big box first. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Pakke inflects and inn remains fixed.
