## for å

**English:** in order to

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Formål | for å + infinitiv | Jeg åpnet døra for å slippe inn frisk luft. | I opened the door to let in some fresh air. |
