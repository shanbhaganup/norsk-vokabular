## takk skal du ha

**English:** thank you

**Topics:** greetings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Til én person | takk skal du ha | Takk skal du ha for hjelpen! | Thank you for your help! |
| Til flere | takk skal dere ha | Takk skal dere ha for maten! | Thank you all for the meal! |

**Form status:** Spelling and contextual usage reviewed.

**Notes:** A fixed thanks formula. Dere can address more than one person. NoW source form: “takk skal du ha”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
