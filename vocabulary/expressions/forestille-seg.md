## forestille seg

**English:** to imagine

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å forestille seg | Det er vanskelig å forestille seg en vinter uten snø. | It’s hard to imagine a winter without snow. |
| Presens | forestiller seg | Hun forestiller seg livet ved kysten. | She imagines life on the coast. |
| Preteritum | forestilte seg | Han forestilte seg et helt annet sted. | He imagined a completely different place. |
| Presens perfektum | har forestilt seg | De har forestilt seg hvordan huset skal se ut. | They have imagined what the house will look like. |
| Imperativ | forestill deg | Forestill deg at du står ved sjøen. | Imagine that you’re standing by the sea. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The verb inflects and the reflexive pronoun follows its subject.
