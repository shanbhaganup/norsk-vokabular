## vondt i magen

**English:** stomachache; pain in the stomach

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Smertested | vondt i magen | Jeg har vondt i magen i dag. | I have a stomachache today. |

**Form status:** Spelling and contextual usage reviewed.

**Notes:** Common after ha. The part i magen identifies the place of the pain. NoW source form: “vondt i magen”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
