## deg selv

**English:** yourself

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnuttrykk | deg selv | Fortell litt om deg selv. | Tell us a little about yourself. |
| Alternativ skrivemåte | deg sjøl | Du må passe på deg sjøl også. | You need to take care of yourself too. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Deg is the object or reflexive form of du. Selv emphasizes the referent. Sjøl is an accepted spelling variant.
