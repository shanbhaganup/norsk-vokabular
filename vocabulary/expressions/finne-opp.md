## finne opp

**English:** to invent

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å finne opp | De prøver å finne opp et nytt spill. | They’re trying to invent a new game. |
| Presens | finner opp | Hun finner opp en ny lek hver uke. | She invents a new game every week. |
| Preteritum | fant opp | Barna fant opp et hemmelig språk. | The children invented a secret language. |
| Presens perfektum | har funnet opp | Elevene har funnet opp et nytt brettspill. | The pupils have invented a new board game. |
| Imperativ | finn opp | Finn opp en lek vi kan spille ute! | Invent a game we can play outside! |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Finne inflects and opp stays fixed.
