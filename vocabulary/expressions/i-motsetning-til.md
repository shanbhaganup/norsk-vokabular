## i motsetning til

**English:** unlike, in contrast to

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast uttrykk | i motsetning til | I motsetning til søsteren sin liker han å stå tidlig opp. | Unlike his sister, he likes getting up early. |
