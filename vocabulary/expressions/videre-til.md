## videre til

**English:** on to, onward to

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Konstruksjon, retning | videre til + substantivfrase | Vi tok bussen videre til sentrum. | We took the bus on to the city centre. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Videre marks continuation and til introduces the next destination.
