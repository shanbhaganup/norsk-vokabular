## oppholde seg

**English:** to stay, to spend time

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å oppholde seg | Det er forbudt å oppholde seg her etter stengetid. | It’s forbidden to stay here after closing time. |
| Presens | oppholder seg | Hun oppholder seg i Norge denne måneden. | She is staying in Norway this month. |
| Preteritum | oppholdt seg | Han oppholdt seg på biblioteket hele ettermiddagen. | He spent the whole afternoon at the library. |
| Presens perfektum | har oppholdt seg | De har oppholdt seg i utlandet siden mai. | They’ve been staying abroad since May. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The verb inflects and the reflexive pronoun follows the subject.
