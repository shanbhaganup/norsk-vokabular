## ta opp

**English:** to record

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å ta opp | Vi trenger tillatelse til å ta opp samtalen. | We need permission to record the conversation. |
| Presens | tar opp | Hun tar opp lyden med mobilen. | She’s recording the sound on her phone. |
| Preteritum | tok opp | Vi tok opp hele konserten. | We recorded the whole concert. |
| Presens perfektum | har tatt opp | Han har tatt opp foredraget. | He has recorded the lecture. |
| Imperativ | ta opp | Ta opp intervjuet hvis hun sier ja. | Record the interview if she agrees. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Ta inflects. These examples illustrate recording audio or video.
