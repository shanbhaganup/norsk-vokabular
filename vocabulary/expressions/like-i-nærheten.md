## like i nærheten

**English:** very close by

**Topics:** directions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stedsuttrykk | like i nærheten | Apoteket ligger like i nærheten. | The pharmacy is very close by. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Like intensifies the proximity expressed by i nærheten. NoW source form: “like i nærheten”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
