## i gang

**English:** underway, in progress

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast uttrykk | i gang | Reparasjonen er allerede i gang. | The repair is already underway. |
| Konstruksjon med utfylling | være i gang med + substantivfrase | De er i gang med ryddingen etter festen. | They are already cleaning up after the party. |

**Form status:** Spelling and usage checked in Bokmålsordboka. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source spelling igang is corrected to i gang. The expression stays fixed when the accompanying verb changes form.
