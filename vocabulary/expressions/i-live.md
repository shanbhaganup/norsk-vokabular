## i live

**English:** alive

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast uttrykk | i live | Alle om bord var i live. | Everyone on board was alive. |
| Konstruksjon | holde + objekt + i live | Hun holdt planten i live gjennom vinteren. | She kept the plant alive through the winter. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The fixed phrase commonly follows være or holde.
