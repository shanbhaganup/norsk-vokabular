## et sted

**English:** somewhere

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnuttrykk | et sted | Nøklene ligger et sted i huset. | The keys are somewhere in the house. |
| Konstruksjon, sted | et sted + stedsledd | Hun bor et sted sør for byen. | She lives somewhere south of town. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This adverbial phrase refers to an unspecified place.
