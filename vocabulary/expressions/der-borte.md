## der borte

**English:** over there

**Topics:** directions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stedsuttrykk | der borte | Bussen stopper der borte ved brua. | The bus stops over there by the bridge. |

**Form status:** Spelling and contextual usage reviewed.

**Notes:** Refers to a more distant location. Compare its use after a stative verb with dit after motion toward a destination. NoW source form: “der borte”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
