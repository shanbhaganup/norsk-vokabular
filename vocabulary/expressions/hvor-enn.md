## hvor enn

**English:** wherever

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast uttrykk | hvor enn | Hvor enn hun dro, tok hun med seg boka. | Wherever she went, she took the book with her. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Hvor enn introduces a clause with unrestricted reference to place.
