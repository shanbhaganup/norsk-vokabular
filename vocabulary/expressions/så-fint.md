## så fint

**English:** how nice

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Utrop | så fint | Så fint at du kunne komme! | How nice that you could come! |

**Form status:** Spelling and contextual usage reviewed.

**Notes:** An exclamation about a pleasing situation. Fint is the neuter form of fin. NoW source form: “så fint!”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
