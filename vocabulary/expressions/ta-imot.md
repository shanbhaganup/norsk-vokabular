## ta imot

**English:** to receive

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å ta imot | Vi kan komme til å ta imot gjestene. | We may receive the guests. |
| Presens | tar imot | Vi tar imot gjestene. | We receive the guests. |
| Preteritum | tok imot | I går tok vi imot gjestene. | Yesterday, we received the guests. |
