## i kveld

**English:** this evening, tonight

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast tidsuttrykk | i kveld | Vi spiser hos naboene i kveld. | We’re having dinner at the neighbours’ tonight. |
| Tidsuttrykk først i setningen | i kveld | I kveld begynner forestillingen klokka åtte. | Tonight the show starts at eight. |

**Form status:** Spelling and usage checked in Bokmålsordboka. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source spelling ikveld is corrected to i kveld. The expression is invariable and refers to the evening of the current day.
