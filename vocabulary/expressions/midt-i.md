## midt i

**English:** in the middle of

**Topics:** directions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sted | midt i | Bordet står midt i rommet. | The table is in the middle of the room. |
| Forløp | midt i | Telefonen ringte midt i møtet. | The phone rang in the middle of the meeting. |

**Form status:** Spelling and contextual usage reviewed.

**Notes:** The complement names an area, period, or situation within which something occurs. NoW source form: “midt i”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
