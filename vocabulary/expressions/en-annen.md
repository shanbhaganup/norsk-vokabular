## en annen

**English:** another, a different one

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | en annen | Vi tok en annen buss. | We took a different bus. |
| Hunkjønn | ei anna | Hun valgte ei anna rute. | She chose a different route. |
| Hunkjønn, alternativ | en annen | Hun valgte en annen rute. | She chose a different route. |
| Intetkjønn | et annet | Vi trenger et annet bord. | We need a different table. |
| Flertall | andre | Vi fikk andre oppgaver. | We were given different tasks. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The article and the determiner agree with the noun. In the plural the indefinite article drops out.
