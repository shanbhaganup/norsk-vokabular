## gå inn

**English:** to go inside, to enter

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å gå inn | Det er på tide å gå inn. | It’s time to go inside. |
| Presens | går inn | Hun går inn gjennom sidedøra. | She goes in through the side door. |
| Preteritum | gikk inn | Vi gikk inn da det begynte å regne. | We went inside when it started raining. |
| Presens perfektum | har gått inn | Barna har gått inn for å spise. | The children have gone inside to eat. |
| Imperativ | gå inn | Gå inn og vent på meg! | Go inside and wait for me! |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Gå inflects and inn marks movement to the inside.
