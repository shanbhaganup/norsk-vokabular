## fra hverandre

**English:** apart, from one another

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast uttrykk | fra hverandre | De bor langt fra hverandre. | They live far apart. |
| Konstruksjon, skille | skille + objektsledd + fra hverandre | Jeg kan ikke skille stemmene fra hverandre. | I can’t tell the voices apart. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The fixed phrase commonly indicates separation or distinction between two or more things.
