## alt i alt

**English:** overall, all in all

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast uttrykk | alt i alt | Alt i alt ble det en fin helg. | All in all, it turned out to be a lovely weekend. |
| Konstruksjon, oppsummering | alt i alt + helsetning | Alt i alt er jeg fornøyd med resultatet. | Overall, I’m happy with the result. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** A fixed phrase that introduces an overall assessment.
