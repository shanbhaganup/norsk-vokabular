## skynde seg

**English:** to hurry

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å skynde seg | Han måtte skynde seg til bussen. | He had to hurry to the bus. |
| Presens | skynder seg | Hun skynder seg hjem før middag. | She’s hurrying home before dinner. |
| Preteritum | skyndte seg | Barna skyndte seg ut da det ringte. | The children hurried out when the bell rang. |
| Presens perfektum | har skyndt seg | Hun har skyndt seg for å rekke toget. | She’s hurried to catch the train. |
| Imperativ | skynd deg | Skynd deg, bussen kommer! | Hurry, the bus is coming! |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The verb inflects and the reflexive pronoun follows the subject. The reference attachment uses skynde deg.
