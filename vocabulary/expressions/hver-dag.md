## hver dag

**English:** every day

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnuttrykk | hver dag | Hun går til skolen hver dag. | She walks to school every day. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The phrase is an adverbial of frequency. English every day is written as two words in this use.
