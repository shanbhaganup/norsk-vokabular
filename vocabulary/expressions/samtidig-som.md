## samtidig som

**English:** while, at the same time as

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Konstruksjon | samtidig som + leddsetning | Hun lyttet til radio samtidig som hun lagde mat. | She listened to the radio while she cooked. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The phrase can introduce an event happening at the same time.
