## meg selv

**English:** myself

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnuttrykk | meg selv | Jeg måtte minne meg selv på avtalen. | I had to remind myself of the appointment. |
| Alternativ | meg sjøl | Jeg så meg sjøl i speilet. | I saw myself in the mirror. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Selv emphasizes the object or reflexive meg. Sjøl is an accepted variant.
