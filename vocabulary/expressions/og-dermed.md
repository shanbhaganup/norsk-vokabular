## og dermed

**English:** and thus, and as a result

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Konstruksjon | og dermed + verbal | Hun fant nøkkelen, og dermed kunne vi gå inn. | She found the key, so we were able to go inside. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Og links the clauses. Dermed points to a consequence.
