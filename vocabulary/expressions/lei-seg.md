## lei seg

**English:** sad, upset

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tredje person | lei seg | Hun ble lei seg da hun hørte det. | She got upset when she heard it. |
| Første person entall | lei meg | Jeg er lei meg for det jeg sa. | I’m sorry about what I said. |
| Andre person entall | lei deg | Ble du lei deg da han dro? | Were you upset when he left? |
| Første person flertall | lei oss | Vi ble lei oss da turen ble avlyst. | We were sad when the trip was cancelled. |
| Andre person flertall | lei dere | Dere trenger ikke bli lei dere. | You don’t have to be upset. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The reflexive pronoun changes with the subject.
