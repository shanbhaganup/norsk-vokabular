## som regel

**English:** usually, as a rule

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast uttrykk | som regel | Jeg tar som regel toget til jobb. | I usually take the train to work. |
