## sørge for

**English:** to make sure, to see to

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å sørge for | Det er din jobb å sørge for at alle får plass. | It’s your job to make sure everyone has a seat. |
| Presens | sørger for | Hun sørger for at døra blir låst. | She makes sure the door gets locked. |
| Preteritum | sørget for | Han sørget for at alle fikk beskjed. | He made sure everyone was told. |
| Preteritum, alternativ | sørga for | Vi sørga for at maten holdt seg varm. | We made sure the food stayed warm. |
| Presens perfektum | har sørget for | De har sørget for at veien er åpen. | They’ve made sure the road is open. |
| Presens perfektum, alternativ | har sørga for | Hun har sørga for at vi har nok stoler. | She’s made sure we have enough chairs. |
| Imperativ | sørg for | Sørg for at vinduene er lukket. | Make sure the windows are shut. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Sørge inflects. The following clause can specify what is to be ensured.
