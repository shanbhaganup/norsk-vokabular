## stå imot

**English:** to resist, to withstand

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å stå imot | Det er vanskelig å stå imot fristelsen. | It’s hard to resist the temptation. |
| Presens | står imot | Muren står imot trykket fra vannet. | The wall withstands the pressure of the water. |
| Preteritum | sto imot | De sto imot presset om å endre planen. | They resisted pressure to change the plan. |
| Preteritum, alternativ | stod imot | Demningen stod imot flommen. | The dam held against the flood. |
| Presens perfektum | har stått imot | Huset har stått imot flere stormer. | The house has withstood several storms. |
| Imperativ | stå imot | Stå imot fristelsen til å gi opp! | Resist the temptation to give up! |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Stå is the inflecting element. Imot introduces what is resisted.
