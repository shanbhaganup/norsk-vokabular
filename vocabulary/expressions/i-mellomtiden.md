## i mellomtiden

**English:** in the meantime, meanwhile

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fast uttrykk | i mellomtiden | Bussen kommer snart. I mellomtiden kan vi gå inn. | The bus is coming soon. In the meantime, we can go inside. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The fixed time phrase refers to the intervening period.
