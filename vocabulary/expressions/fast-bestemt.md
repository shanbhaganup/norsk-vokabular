## fast bestemt

**English:** firmly determined

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnuttrykk | fast bestemt | Hun var fast bestemt på å fullføre kurset. | She was firmly determined to finish the course. |
| Konstruksjon | fast bestemt på å + infinitiv | De er fast bestemt på å finne en løsning. | They are firmly determined to find a solution. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Here fast intensifies bestemt. The phrase is commonly predicative before på å.
