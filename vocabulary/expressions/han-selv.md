## han selv

**English:** he himself

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnuttrykk | han selv | Han selv ville bli hjemme. | He himself wanted to stay home. |
| Alternativ | han sjøl | Han sjøl tok telefonen. | He answered the phone himself. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Selv emphasizes the subject. Sjøl is an accepted variant.
