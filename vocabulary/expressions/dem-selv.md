## dem selv

**English:** them themselves, the people themselves

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnuttrykk | dem selv | Spør dem selv, så får du høre hva de mener. | Ask them directly and you’ll hear what they think. |
| Alternativ skrivemåte | dem sjøl | Jeg vil heller høre det fra dem sjøl. | I’d rather hear it from them personally. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Dem selv emphasizes a third-person plural object that is distinct from the subject. Use seg selv for a reflexive object referring back to the subject.
