## diverse

**English:** various, assorted

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | kvantor | Hun kjøpte diverse grønnsaker. | She bought various vegetables. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Vi fant diverse feil i teksten. | We found various errors in the text. |
| Konstruksjon | diverse + substantiv i flertall | Kontoret trenger diverse kabler. | The office needs various cables. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** As a quantifying determiner diverse is invariable and occurs with a plural noun.
