## annen

**English:** another, other, different

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | demonstrativt determinativ | Jeg vil prøve en annen rute. | I want to try a different route. |
| Hankjønn | annen | Vi tok en annen buss hjem. | We took another bus home. |
| Hunkjønn | anna | Hun lånte ei anna jakke. | She borrowed a different jacket. |
| Hunkjønn | annen | Vi møtes ei annen uke. | We'll meet another week. |
| Intetkjønn | anna | De fant et anna sted å spise. | They found somewhere else to eat. |
| Intetkjønn | annet | Kan vi velge et annet bord? | Can we choose another table? |
| Flertall | andre | Andre studenter brukte biblioteket om kvelden. | Other students used the library in the evening. |
| Bestemt entall | andre | Katten ligger på den andre stolen. | The cat is lying on the other chair. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the word class and tabulated forms. Examples are original.

**Notes:** The snapshot merged the source form annet under annen and cited the ordinal entry for “second.” The English gloss “another / other” matches the demonstrative determiner annen I. Its regular definite singular is andre. Bokmålsordboka also illustrates formal or fixed definite uses such as det annet kjønn and på den annen side.
