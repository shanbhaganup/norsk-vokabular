## mest

**English:** most; the greatest amount

**Topics:** quantity

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | kvantor / superlativ mengde | De brukte mest tid på oppgaven. | They spent the most time on the assignment. |
| Masseord | mest | Mest kaffe ble servert om morgenen. | The most coffee was served in the morning. |
| Grad | mest | Av alle var hun mest fornøyd. | Of everyone, she was the most satisfied. |
| Bestemt mengde | det meste | Det meste av maten er spist. | Most of the food has been eaten. |
| Adverbial bruk | mest | Jeg liker mest å lese om kvelden. | I mostly like reading in the evening. |

**Form status:** Dictionary use and alternatives checked; examples are original.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka treats mest as an adjective/quantifier and records adverbial uses; it also occurs substantivally in det meste. This entry focuses on quantitative determiner syntax.
