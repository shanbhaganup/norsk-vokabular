## ingen

**English:** no, none

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | ingen | Det går ingen buss etter midnatt. | There’s no bus after midnight. |
| Hunkjønn | ingen | Jeg fikk ingen melding fra skolen. | I got no message from the school. |
| Intetkjønn | intet | De fant intet spor etter katten. | They found no trace of the cat. |
| Intetkjønn, konstruksjon | ikke noe | Jeg har ikke noe kart. | I don’t have a map. |
| Flertall | ingen | Det er ingen ledige seter. | There are no free seats. |
| Selvstendig bruk | ingen | Ingen visste svaret. | Nobody knew the answer. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Ingen is used with masculine and feminine singular and with plural nouns. Intet or ikke noe is used with neuter singular in this quantifier construction.
