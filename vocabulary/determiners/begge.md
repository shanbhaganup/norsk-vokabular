## begge

**English:** both

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entall | — | — | Begge always refers to two entities. |
| Med hankjønnsord | begge | Begge stolene er opptatt. | Both chairs are taken. |
| Med hunkjønnsord | begge | Begge lampene virker. | Both lamps work. |
| Med intetkjønnsord | begge | Begge vinduene står åpne. | Both windows are open. |
| Selvstendig | begge | De kom begge på festen. | They both came to the party. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Invariable when referring to two people or things. Both bare and noun-modifying uses are common. NoW source form: “begge”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
