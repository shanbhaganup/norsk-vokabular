## sånn

**English:** such; that kind of; like that

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | sånn | Jeg vil ha en sånn stol. | I want that kind of chair. |
| Hunkjønn | sånn | Har du ei sånn veske? | Do you have that kind of bag? |
| Intetkjønn | sånt | Jeg har aldri sett et sånt dyr. | I’ve never seen that kind of animal. |
| Flertall | sånne | Sånne sko passer til turen. | Shoes like that are suitable for the hike. |
| Måte | sånn | Ikke gjør det sånn. | Don’t do it like that. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Common in speech. Agreement follows the noun. Standalone sånn can refer to a manner or way. NoW source form: “sånn”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
