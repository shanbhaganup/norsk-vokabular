## minst

**English:** at least; the least

**Topics:** quantity

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | kvantor / superlativ mengde | Vi trenger minst tre dager. | We need at least three days. |
| Hankjønn / hunkjønn | minst | Hun brukte minst tid av alle. | She spent the least time of all. |
| Intetkjønn | minst | Det kostet minst mulig. | It cost as little as possible. |
| Flertall | minst | Minst fem personer meldte seg. | At least five people signed up. |
| Bestemt / substantivisk | det minste | Det minste vi kan gjøre, er å ringe. | The least we can do is call. |

**Form status:** Dictionary quantifier and adverb uses checked; examples are original.

**Notes:** Minst is the superlative of liten in adjective use (linked through the existing liten entry) and also functions as a quantitative determiner/adverb.
