## denne

**English:** this, these

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | denne | Denne stolen er ledig. | This chair is free. |
| Hunkjønn | denne | Denne jakka er for stor. | This jacket is too big. |
| Intetkjønn | dette | Dette eplet er surt. | This apple is sour. |
| Flertall | disse | Disse koppene tåler oppvaskmaskin. | These cups are dishwasher-safe. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The reference form dette is the neuter of the demonstrative denne.
