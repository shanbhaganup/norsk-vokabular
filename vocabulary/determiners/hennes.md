## hennes

**English:** her, hers

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | hennes | Bilen hennes står ute. | Her car is outside. |
| Hunkjønn | hennes | Jakka hennes er våt. | Her jacket is wet. |
| Intetkjønn | hennes | Huset hennes er det blå. | Her house is the blue one. |
| Flertall | hennes | Skoene hennes står i gangen. | Her shoes are in the hall. |
| Selvstendig bruk | hennes | Den røde sykkelen er hennes. | The red bike is hers. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The non-reflexive possessive is invariable.
