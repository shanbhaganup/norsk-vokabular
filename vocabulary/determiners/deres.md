## deres

**English:** their, your (plural)

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | deres | Hunden deres bjeffet hele natta. | Their dog barked all night. |
| Hunkjønn | deres | Jeg fant jakka deres i gangen. | I found their jacket in the hall. |
| Intetkjønn | deres | Er det huset deres der borte? | Is that their house over there? |
| Flertall | deres | Har dere husket billettene deres? | Have you remembered your tickets? |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The possessive determiner has a single form across genders and numbers. Context identifies the possessor.
