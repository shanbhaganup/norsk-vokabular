## din

**English:** your (singular)

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | din | Hvor står sykkelen din? | Where is your bike? |
| Hunkjønn | di | Jakka di henger i gangen. | Your jacket is hanging in the hall. |
| Intetkjønn | ditt | Jeg har nummeret ditt. | I have your number. |
| Flertall | dine | Skoene dine står ved døra. | Your shoes are by the door. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The possessive agrees with the thing possessed.
