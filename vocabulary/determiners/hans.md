## hans

**English:** his

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | hans | Bilen hans står ute. | His car is outside. |
| Hunkjønn | hans | Jeg fant jakka hans. | I found his jacket. |
| Intetkjønn | hans | Huset hans ligger ved sjøen. | His house is by the sea. |
| Flertall | hans | Bøkene hans ligger på bordet. | His books are on the table. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Hans is a non-reflexive possessive. It has one form across all genders and numbers.
