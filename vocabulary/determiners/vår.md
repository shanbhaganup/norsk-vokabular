## vår

**English:** our, ours

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | vår | Bilen vår står ute. | Our car is outside. |
| Hunkjønn | vår | Hytta vår ligger ved vannet. | Our cabin is by the lake. |
| Intetkjønn | vårt | Huset vårt har rødt tak. | Our house has a red roof. |
| Flertall | våre | Bøkene våre ligger på bordet. | Our books are on the table. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The possessive agrees with the thing possessed.
