## all

**English:** all; the whole amount

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | all | All maten er klar. | All the food is ready. |
| Hunkjønn | all | All suppa ble spist opp. | All the soup was eaten. |
| Intetkjønn | alt | Alt vannet er borte. | All the water is gone. |
| Flertall | alle | Alle gjestene kom i tide. | All the guests arrived on time. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Singular forms usually quantify mass nouns. Alle quantifies plural nouns or refers to everyone. NoW source form: “all”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
