## enhver

**English:** each, every, anyone

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | enhver | Enhver deltaker kan stille spørsmål. | Every participant can ask questions. |
| Hunkjønn | enhver | Hun griper enhver anledning til å øve. | She takes every opportunity to practise. |
| Intetkjønn | ethvert | De undersøkte ethvert forslag nøye. | They examined every proposal carefully. |
| Selvstendig bruk | enhver | Dette kan enhver lære. | Anyone can learn this. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This determiner is normally singular. It can also stand without an overt noun.
