## flere

**English:** several

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | kvantor | Flere elever kom for sent. | Several pupils arrived late. |
| Konstruksjon, flertall | flere + substantiv | Vi fikk flere meldinger i går. | We received several messages yesterday. |
| Selvstendig bruk | flere | Flere ville bli med. | Several people wanted to come along. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This use quantifies plural count nouns. The same spelling can also express the comparative ‘more’.
