## slik

**English:** such, that kind of

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | slik | Jeg har aldri sett en slik stol. | I’ve never seen a chair like that. |
| Hunkjønn | slik | Vi trenger ei slik hylle. | We need a shelf like that. |
| Intetkjønn | slikt | Han ønsker seg et slikt kamera. | He wants a camera like that. |
| Flertall | slike | De lager slike kopper for hånd. | They make cups like those by hand. |
