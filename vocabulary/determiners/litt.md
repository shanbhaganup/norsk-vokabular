## litt

**English:** a little, some

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | kvantor | Jeg helte litt melk i kaffen. | I poured a little milk into my coffee. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: litt | Vi trenger litt mel til sausen. | We need a little flour for the sauce. |
| Bruk uten substantiv | litt | Jeg sparte litt til senere. | I saved a little for later. |

**Form status:** Spelling and usage checked in Bokmålsordboka. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This use quantifies a small amount. The source gloss can also cover adverbial litt, as in litt kald (‘a little cold’).
