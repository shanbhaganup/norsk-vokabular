## sin

**English:** his own; her own; its own; their own

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | sin | Han hentet sykkelen sin. | He fetched his bike. |
| Hunkjønn | si | Hun pakket veska si. | She packed her bag. |
| Intetkjønn | sitt | De solgte huset sitt. | They sold their house. |
| Flertall | sine | Hun ringte foreldrene sine. | She called her parents. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Refers to the subject of the clause. Agreement follows the possessed thing. NoW source form: “sin”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
