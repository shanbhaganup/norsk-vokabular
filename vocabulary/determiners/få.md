## få

**English:** few (comparative: fewer)

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | kvantor | Det var få ledige seter igjen. | There were few seats left. |
| Entall, alle kjønn | — | Kvantoren brukes om et antall i flertall. | The quantifier refers to a number of items in the plural. |
| Flertall, positiv | få | Få butikker holder åpent på søndager. | Few shops are open on Sundays. |
| Flertall, komparativ | færre | Vi fikk færre søknader i år enn i fjor. | We received fewer applications this year than last year. |
| Flertall, ubestemt superlativ | færrest | Dette laget slapp inn færrest mål. | This team conceded the fewest goals. |
| Flertall, bestemt superlativ | færreste | De færreste deltakerne hadde prøvd dette før. | Very few of the participants had tried this before. |
| Bruk uten substantiv | færre | Tolv meldte seg på, men færre møtte opp. | Twelve people signed up, but fewer showed up. |

**Form status:** Spelling and usage checked in Bokmålsordboka. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The assigned source gives færre (‘fewer’) and labels it ADJ. Bokmålsordboka lists it as a quantifier and identifies it as the comparative of få. De færreste commonly means ‘very few’. The homographic verb få has a separate paradigm.
