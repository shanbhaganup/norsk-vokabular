## disse

**English:** these

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entall | — | — | Disse is the plural demonstrative. |
| Flertall med substantiv | disse | Disse bøkene skal tilbake til biblioteket. | These books need to go back to the library. |
| Selvstendig flertall | disse | Jeg tar disse. | I’ll take these. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Plural demonstrative used with a definite plural noun or independently. De, den and det are related demonstratives with their own usage patterns. NoW source form: “disse”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
