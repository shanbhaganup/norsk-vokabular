## det

**English:** the; that (neuter singular)

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bestemt artikkel | det | Hun tok det røde eplet. | She took the red apple. |
| Demonstrativ | det | Jeg vil ha det eplet der. | I want that apple over there. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** The unstressed form stands before an adjective and a definite neuter noun. The stressed demonstrative can point to a specific thing. NoW source form: “det”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
