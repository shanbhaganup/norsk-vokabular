## hvilken

**English:** which, what

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | hvilken | Hvilken buss skal vi ta? | Which bus should we take? |
| Hunkjønn | hvilken | Hvilken jakke vil du ha? | Which jacket do you want? |
| Intetkjønn | hvilket | Hvilket rom er ledig? | Which room is free? |
| Flertall | hvilke | Hvilke dager passer for deg? | Which days work for you? |
