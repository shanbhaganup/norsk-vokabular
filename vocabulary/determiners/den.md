## den

**English:** the

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | den | Den røde stolen står ute. | The red chair is outside. |
| Hunkjønn | den | Den blå jakka er mi. | The blue jacket is mine. |
| Intetkjønn | det | Det gamle huset står tomt. | The old house is empty. |
| Flertall | de | De nye bøkene kom i går. | The new books arrived yesterday. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Den, det and de appear before an adjective in a definite noun phrase.
