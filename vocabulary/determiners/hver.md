## hver

**English:** each, every

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | hver | Hver deltaker får et kart. | Each participant gets a map. |
| Hunkjønn | hver | Hun leser hver uke. | She reads every week. |
| Intetkjønn | hvert | Hvert barn fikk en blyant. | Each child received a pencil. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** In this distributive use, it agrees with the singular noun.
