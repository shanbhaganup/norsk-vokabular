## en

**English:** a, an

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | en | Han kjøpte en stol. | He bought a chair. |
| Hunkjønn | ei | Hun leste ei bok. | She read a book. |
| Hunkjønn, alternativ | en | Jeg pakket en bok i sekken. | I put a book in my bag. |
| Intetkjønn | et | De leier et rom. | They rent a room. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The indefinite article has masculine, feminine and neuter forms. En is also accepted with feminine nouns. Indefinite plural noun phrases have no corresponding indefinite article.
