## noen

**English:** some, any

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hankjønn | noen | Har du noen plan for helga? | Do you have any plan for the weekend? |
| Hunkjønn | noen | Fant du noen løsning? | Did you find a solution? |
| Intetkjønn | noe | Har du noe forslag? | Do you have any suggestion? |
| Flertall | noen | Vi kjøpte noen epler. | We bought some apples. |
| Selvstendig bruk | noen | Noen banket på døra. | Someone knocked at the door. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Noen can modify plural nouns and common-gender singular nouns in questions. Noe occurs with neuter singular nouns.
