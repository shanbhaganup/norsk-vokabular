## nitti

**English:** ninety

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | nitti | Filmen varer i nitti minutter. | The film lasts ninety minutes. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | nittiende | Det var hennes nittiende fødselsdag. | It was her ninetieth birthday. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The cardinal is invariable. Nittiende is a related ordinal.
