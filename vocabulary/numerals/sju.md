## sju

**English:** seven

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | sju | Det er sju dager i ei uke. | There are seven days in a week. |
| Grunntall, alternativ | syv | Vi trenger syv stoler. | We need seven chairs. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | sjuende | Hun kom på sjuende plass. | She finished seventh. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall, alternativ | syvende | De bor i syvende etasje. | They live on the seventh floor. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Sju and syv are accepted spellings. Sjuende and syvende are related ordinals.
