## to

**English:** two

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | grunntall | Jeg har to bøker. | I have two books. |
| Grunntall | to | Vi trenger to billetter. | We need two tickets. |
| Kjønnsbøyning | ubøyelig: to | Jeg ser to biler, to bøker og to hus. | I see two cars, two books and two houses. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | andre | Hun bor i andre etasje. | She lives on the second floor. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword/forms checked; compound constructions and original example sentences supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** To is an invariable cardinal numeral. Andre is the related ordinal meaning second; it is a separate word, not an inflected form of to. Ordinal numerals are classified as adjectives in Bokmålsordboka. Numerals are included as a separate learning category in this project.
