## ti

**English:** ten

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | ti | Vi ventet i ti minutter. | We waited for ten minutes. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | tiende | Dette er den tiende boka i serien. | This is the tenth book in the series. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The cardinal is invariable. Tiende is the related ordinal.
