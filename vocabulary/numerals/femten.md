## femten

**English:** fifteen

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | femten | Det kom femten gjester. | Fifteen guests came. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | femtende | Møtet er den femtende i måneden. | The meeting is on the fifteenth of the month. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The cardinal is invariable. Femtende is the related ordinal.
