## tre

**English:** three

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | tre | Hun har tre søsken. | She has three siblings. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | tredje | Vi bor i tredje etasje. | We live on the third floor. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Tre is invariable. Tredje is the related ordinal.
