## tjue

**English:** twenty

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | tjue | Vi trenger tjue stoler. | We need twenty chairs. |
| Grunntall, alternativ | tyve | Det kom tyve gjester. | Twenty guests came. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | tjuende | De reiser den tjuende. | They’re leaving on the twentieth. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall, alternativ | tyvende | Hun kom på tyvende plass. | She finished twentieth. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Tjue and tyve are accepted cardinal forms. Tjuende and tyvende are related ordinals.
