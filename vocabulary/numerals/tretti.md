## tretti

**English:** thirty

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | tretti | Vi ventet i tretti minutter. | We waited for thirty minutes. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | trettiende | Hun ble nummer tretti og kom i mål som trettiende løper. | She was the thirtieth runner to finish. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Tretti is invariable. Trettiende is the related ordinal.
