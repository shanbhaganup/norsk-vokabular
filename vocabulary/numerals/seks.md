## seks

**English:** six

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | seks | Vi har seks kopper. | We have six cups. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | sjette | Hun bor i sjette etasje. | She lives on the sixth floor. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The cardinal is invariable. Sjette is the related ordinal.
