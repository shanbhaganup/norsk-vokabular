## atten

**English:** eighteen

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | grunntall | Vi er atten i klassen. | There are eighteen of us in the class. |
| Grunntall | atten | Hun har atten bøker i sekken. | She has eighteen books in her bag. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | attende | Han ble nummer atten og kom i mål som attende deltaker. | He finished eighteenth. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The cardinal number is invariable. The ordinal attende is a related word.
