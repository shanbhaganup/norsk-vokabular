## syttito

**English:** seventy-two

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | syttito | Det er syttito elever på skolen. | There are seventy-two pupils at the school. |

**Form status:** Spelling and contextual usage reviewed.

**Notes:** The cardinal is invariable. The written number can also be shown as 72. NoW source form: “syttito”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
