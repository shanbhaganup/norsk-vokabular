## sytten

**English:** seventeen

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | sytten | Det er sytten elever i rommet. | There are seventeen pupils in the room. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | syttende | De reiser den syttende i måneden. | They’re leaving on the seventeenth of the month. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The cardinal is invariable. Syttende is the related ordinal.
