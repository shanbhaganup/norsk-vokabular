## tolv

**English:** twelve

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | tolv | Det er tolv egg i esken. | There are twelve eggs in the carton. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | tolvte | Hun bor i tolvte etasje. | She lives on the twelfth floor. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The cardinal is invariable. Tolvte is the related ordinal.
