## hundre

**English:** hundred

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | hundre | Boka har hundre sider. | The book has a hundred pages. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | hundrede | Dette var den hundrede forestillingen. | This was the hundredth performance. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Hundre is invariable as a cardinal. Hundrede is the related ordinal.
