## tusen

**English:** thousand

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | tusen | Bordet kostet tusen kroner. | The table cost a thousand kroner. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | tusende | Den tusende besøkende fikk en bok. | The thousandth visitor received a book. |
