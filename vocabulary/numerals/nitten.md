## nitten

**English:** nineteen

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | nitten | Han var nitten år da han flyttet. | He was nineteen when he moved. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | nittende | Bussen går fra den nittende holdeplassen. | The bus leaves from the nineteenth stop. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The cardinal is invariable. Nittende is a related ordinal.
