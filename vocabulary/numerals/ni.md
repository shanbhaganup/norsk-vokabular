## ni

**English:** nine

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | ni | Det er ni elever i gruppa. | There are nine pupils in the group. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | niende | Hun kom på niende plass. | She finished ninth. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The cardinal is invariable. Niende is a related ordinal.
