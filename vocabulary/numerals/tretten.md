## tretten

**English:** thirteen

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | tretten | Det kom tretten deltakere. | Thirteen participants came. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | trettende | Boka kom ut den trettende i måneden. | The book came out on the thirteenth of the month. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Tretten is invariable. Trettende is the related ordinal.
