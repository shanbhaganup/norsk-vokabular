## tiende

**English:** tenth

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordenstall | tiende | Kurset begynner den tiende juni. | The course starts on June tenth. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling checked.

**Notes:** The ordinal has the same form across genders and numbers. Bokmålsordboka classifies ordinals as adjectives. NoW source form: “tiende”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
