## trettiåtte

**English:** thirty-eight

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | trettiåtte | Hun har lest trettiåtte sider. | She has read thirty-eight pages. |

**Form status:** Spelling and contextual usage reviewed.

**Notes:** The cardinal is invariable. The written number can also be shown as 38. NoW source form: “trettiåtte”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
