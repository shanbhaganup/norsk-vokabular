## elleve

**English:** eleven

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | elleve | Det er elleve spillere på banen. | There are eleven players on the pitch. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | ellevte | Hun kom på ellevte plass. | She finished eleventh. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The cardinal is invariable. Ellevte is the related ordinal.
