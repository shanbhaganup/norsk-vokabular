## åtti

**English:** eighty

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | åtti | Bussen har plass til åtti passasjerer. | The bus can carry eighty passengers. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | åttiende | Vi feiret hennes åttiende fødselsdag. | We celebrated her eightieth birthday. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The cardinal is invariable. Åttiende is the related ordinal.
