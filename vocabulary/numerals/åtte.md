## åtte

**English:** eight

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | åtte | Det står åtte stoler rundt bordet. | There are eight chairs around the table. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | åttende | Hun kom på åttende plass. | She finished eighth. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The cardinal is invariable. Åttende is the related ordinal.
