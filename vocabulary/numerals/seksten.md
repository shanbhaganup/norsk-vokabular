## seksten

**English:** sixteen

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunntall | seksten | Han fyller seksten i juni. | He turns sixteen in June. |
| Tilhørende ordenstall | sekstende | De møtes den sekstende i måneden. | They’re meeting on the sixteenth of the month. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The cardinal is invariable. Sekstende is the related ordinal.
