## hva

**English:** what

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | spørrepronomen | Hva skjedde i går? | What happened yesterday? |
| Subjektsbruk | hva | Hva ligger i skuffen? | What is in the drawer? |
| Objektsbruk | hva | Hva leser du? | What are you reading? |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Hva asks about a thing, an action or other information. It is invariable.
