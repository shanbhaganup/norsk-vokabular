## hun

**English:** she, her

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | personlig pronomen | Hun ringer i morgen. | She’ll call tomorrow. |
| Person | tredje person | Jeg så henne ved inngangen. | I saw her by the entrance. |
| Tall | entall | Hun jobber her på mandager. | She works here on Mondays. |
| Subjektsform | hun | Hun kjenner veien. | She knows the way. |
| Objektsform | henne | Kan du vente på henne? | Can you wait for her? |
| Tilhørende refleksivt pronomen | seg | Hun satte seg ved vinduet. | She sat down by the window. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The adverb source file lists henne. It is the object form of the personal pronoun hun.
