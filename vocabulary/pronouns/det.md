## det

**English:** it; that

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subjektsform | det | Hvor er kortet? Det ligger på bordet. | Where is the card? It’s on the table. |
| Objektsform | det | Jeg fant kortet og la det i lomma. | I found the card and put it in my pocket. |
| Formelt subjekt | det | Det snør ute. | It’s snowing outside. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Can refer back to a neuter noun or a situation. It also occurs as a dummy subject and can be the object. NoW source form: “det”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
