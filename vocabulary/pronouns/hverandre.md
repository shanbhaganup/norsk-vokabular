## hverandre

**English:** each other; one another

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

| Subjektsform | — | — | This reciprocal or reflexive form does not serve as a clause subject. |
| Objektsform | hverandre | Naboene hjelper hverandre. | The neighbors help each other. |

**Notes:** Reciprocal pronoun used for two or more participants, usually as an object or complement. It can take a genitive ending. NoW source form: “hverandre”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
