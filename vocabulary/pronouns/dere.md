## dere

**English:** you (plural)

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subjektsform | dere | Dere kan sette dere her. | You can sit here. |
| Objektsform | dere | Jeg sender dere kartet i morgen. | I’ll send you the map tomorrow. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** The subject and object forms have the same spelling. NoW source form: “dere”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
