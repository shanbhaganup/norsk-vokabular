## man

**English:** one; you (generic)

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subjektsform | man | Man må bestille på forhånd. | You have to book in advance. |
| Objektsform | — | — | Man is a subject form. En can be used as a generic object. |
| Tilhørende refleksivt pronomen | seg | Man bør kle på seg før man går ut. | You should get dressed before you go outside. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Generic human subject. En commonly serves as the generic object. Seg is available as a related reflexive form. NoW source form: “man”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
