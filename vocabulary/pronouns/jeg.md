## jeg

**English:** I, me

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | personlig pronomen | Jeg skal til byen. | I’m going into town. |
| Person og tall | første person entall | Jeg bor i Tromsø. | I live in Tromsø. |
| Subjektsform | jeg | Jeg venter her. | I’ll wait here. |
| Objektsform | meg | Hun vinket til meg. | She waved at me. |
| Tilhørende refleksiv bruk | meg | Jeg kledde på meg. | I got dressed. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Jeg is the subject form and meg the object form. Meg is also used reflexively.
