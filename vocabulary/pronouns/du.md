## du

**English:** you (singular)

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | personlig pronomen | Du kan sitte her. | You can sit here. |
| Person | andre person | Jeg snakker med deg. | I’m talking to you. |
| Tall | entall | Kommer du alene? | Are you coming on your own? |
| Subjektsform | du | Du har nøkkelen. | You have the key. |
| Objektsform | deg | Jeg venter på deg. | I’m waiting for you. |
| Tilhørende refleksiv bruk | deg | Du kan sette deg her. | You can sit down here. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Du is the subject form and deg the object form. Deg also serves as the corresponding reflexive.
