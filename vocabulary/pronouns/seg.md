## seg

**English:** oneself; himself; herself; itself; themselves

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subjektsform | — | — | This reciprocal or reflexive form does not serve as a clause subject. |
| Refleksivt objekt | seg | Hun vasket seg før frokost. | She washed herself before breakfast. |
| Etter preposisjon | seg | Hun tok med seg en venn. | She brought a friend along. |
| Etter preposisjon | seg | Han la jakka fra seg. | He set his jacket down. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Refers back to a third-person subject. Meg, deg, oss and dere are used for other persons as appropriate. NoW source form: “seg”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
