## hvem

**English:** who, whom

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | spørrepronomen | Hvem er det ved døra? | Who is at the door? |
| Subjektsbruk | hvem | Hvem tok telefonen? | Who answered the phone? |
| Objektsbruk | hvem | Hvem møtte du på toget? | Who did you meet on the train? |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Hvem is invariable and asks about a person.
