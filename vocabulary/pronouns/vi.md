## vi

**English:** we, us

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | personlig pronomen | Vi bor ved sjøen. | We live by the sea. |
| Person og tall | første person flertall | Vi kom tidlig. | We arrived early. |
| Subjektsform | vi | Vi venter utenfor. | We’ll wait outside. |
| Objektsform | oss | Hun ga oss nøkkelen. | She gave us the key. |
| Tilhørende refleksiv bruk | oss | Vi satte oss ved vinduet. | We sat down by the window. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source gloss ourselves fits reflexive oss or oss selv, while vi is the subject form.
