## dele

**English:** to divide; to share; to distribute

**Topics:** communication, everyday life

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å dele | Barna liker å dele leker. | The children like to share toys. |
| Presens | deler | Vi deler oppgavene mellom oss. | We divide the tasks between us. |
| Preteritum | delte | De delte arven i går. | They divided the inheritance yesterday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | delt | De har delt rom i flere år. | They have shared a room for several years. |
| Presens perfektum | har delt | Hun har delt nyheten med familien. | She has shared the news with the family. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde delt | Vi hadde delt ut alle billettene før dørene åpnet. | We had handed out all the tickets before the doors opened. |
| Futurum | skal dele / vil dele | Vi skal dele regningen. / Jeg vil dele bildet senere. | We are going to split the bill. / I will share the picture later. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha delt / vil ha delt | Innen kvelden skal vi ha delt oppgavene. / Innen i morgen vil hun ha delt bildet. | By this evening we will have divided the tasks. / By tomorrow she will have shared the picture. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville dele | Jeg ville dele rom hvis vi hadde nok plass. | I would share a room if we had enough space. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha delt | Hun ville ha delt nyheten hvis hun hadde fått lov. | She would have shared the news if she had been allowed. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle dele | Han sa at han skulle dele dokumentet senere. | He said he would share the document later. |
| Imperativ | del | Del oppgavene rettferdig! | Divide the tasks fairly! |
| Presens partisipp | delende | En delende leder gir alle ansvar. | A sharing leader gives everyone responsibility. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | deles | Oppgavene må deles mellom gruppene. | The tasks must be divided between the groups. |
| S-passiv, presens | deles | Bildet deles med hele klassen. | The picture is shared with the whole class. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir delt | Arven blir delt mellom søsknene. | The inheritance is divided among the siblings. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble delt | Oppgavene ble delt i går. | The tasks were divided yesterday. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt delt | Bildet har blitt delt mange ganger. | The picture has been shared many times. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | delt | En delt mening skapte debatt. | A divided opinion sparked debate. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | delt | Et delt ansvar krever samarbeid. | A shared responsibility requires cooperation. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | delte | Den delte regningen lå på bordet. | The split bill was on the table. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | delte | De delte meningene skapte debatt. | The divided opinions sparked debate. |

**Form status:** Headword and dictionary forms checked; compound constructions and examples are authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The dictionary also lists a distinct neuter noun *dele*; this entry documents only the verb. The reflexive construction *dele seg* is not an inflected form. Transitive uses permit passive constructions.
