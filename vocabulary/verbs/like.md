## like

**English:** to like; to enjoy; to approve of

**Topics:** emotions, preferences

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å like | Jeg håper å like filmen. | I hope to like the film. |
| Presens | liker | Hun liker klassisk musikk. | She likes classical music. |
| Preteritum | likte | Likte du filmen? | Did you like the film? |
| Perfektum partisipp | likt | Hun er godt likt av kollegene. | She is well liked by her colleagues. |
| Presens perfektum | har likt | Vi har likt alle konsertene så langt. | We have liked all the concerts so far. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde likt | Jeg hadde likt maten bedre uten så mye salt. | I would have liked the food more without so much salt. |
| Futurum | skal like | Du skal like den nye boka. | You will like the new book. |
| Futurum | vil like | Han vil like overraskelsen. | He will like the surprise. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha likt | Innen helgen skal hun ha likt alle forslagene. | By the weekend, she will have liked all the suggestions. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha likt | Innen turen er over, vil de ha likt flere av stoppene. | By the end of the trip, they will have liked several of the stops. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville like | Jeg ville like å bli med hvis jeg hadde fri. | I would like to come along if I had the day off. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha likt | Hun ville ha likt konserten hvis hun hadde kommet. | She would have liked the concert if she had come. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle like | Han sa at jeg skulle like stedet. | He said I would like the place. |
| Imperativ | lik | Lik gjerne innlegget hvis du er enig! | Feel free to like the post if you agree! |
| Presens partisipp | Unverified | — | — |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | Unverified | — | — |
| S-passiv, presens | Unverified | — | — |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir likt | Forslaget blir likt av mange lesere. | The proposal is liked by many readers. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble likt | Bildet ble likt av tusenvis av brukere. | The picture was liked by thousands of users. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt likt | Innlegget har blitt likt av mange. | The post has been liked by many. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | likt | Et godt likt band spilte på torget. | A well-liked band played in the square. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | likt | Et godt likt forslag ble vedtatt. | A well-liked proposal was adopted. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | likte | Den godt likte læreren fikk en gave. | The well-liked teacher received a gift. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | likte | De godt likte sangene ble spilt igjen. | The well-liked songs were played again. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. S-passive is not verified as natural for the ordinary “like” sense; the common passive expression *være godt likt* and bli-passive examples are included.

**Notes:** This is the verb “like/enjoy,” not the adjective *lik* (“similar/equal”). The dictionary also lists *like seg* meaning “feel at home/be comfortable.” Although *likende* appears in the dictionary paradigm, its use as a present participle of “like/enjoy” is unclear; it remains Unverified rather than using the separate sense “similar.” The attributive participle examples use *godt likt*, a natural passive-adjective phrase. S-passive *likes* from the source is not verified as idiomatic for this meaning.
