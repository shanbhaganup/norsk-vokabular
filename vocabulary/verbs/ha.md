## ha

**English:** to have, own, possess, experience, or be in a state

**Topics:** General / unassigned

**Notes:** Ha has many meanings, including possession, experience, and its use as an auxiliary in compound tenses. Examples focus on possession and experience. It is also used as a modal-like verb and auxiliary, but those are constructions rather than additional inflections. The dictionary lists the rare present participle haende but supplies no usage example; its use remains unverified. The snapshot lists s-passive has, but an ordinary possessive passive is not idiomatic in the sense taught here. Past participle hatt is invariant and is not normally used attributively; adjectival agreement rows are inapplicable.
