**Form status:** Simple forms and participial agreement checked against Bokmålsordboka. Applicable compound tenses and passives are constructions. The bilingual examples are original.

**Notes:** The reference imported “risk” from a noun/verb source row. Bokmålsordboka gives movement senses for the verb. The English verb “to risk” corresponds to risikere. The motion use has no direct object. Both passive constructions are marked impersonal uses with det. The examples use fare hardt fram mot, meaning to deal harshly with someone. The dictionary lists the agreeing forms fart and farte. Their attributive use with this motion sense is marginal, so the agreement rows record the spellings in sentences about the forms.
