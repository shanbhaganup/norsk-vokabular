## tygge

**English:** to chew; figuratively, to think over (*tygge på*)

**Topics:** food, thought

**Form status:** Simple forms and participial alternatives checked against Bokmålsordboka. Compound tenses and passive constructions are productive and illustrated with the transitive chewing sense.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists *tygde/tygget* as past tense and *tygd/tygget* as perfect participle alternatives. In adjectival agreement it lists *tygd/tygget* for indefinite forms and *tygde/tyggede/tyggete* for definite singular and plural. The fixed expression *tygge på noe* can mean “think over something”; it is a construction, not a separate inflection. The future-from-past example illustrates that expression.
