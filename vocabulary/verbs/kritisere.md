## kritisere

**English:** to criticize; to review or assess critically

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å kritisere | Det er lett å kritisere uten å foreslå løsninger. | It is easy to criticize without suggesting solutions. |
| Presens | kritiserer | Avisen kritiserer kommunens plan. | The newspaper criticizes the municipality’s plan. |
| Preteritum | kritiserte | Hun kritiserte avgjørelsen i går. | She criticized the decision yesterday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | kritisert | Forslaget er kritisert av flere eksperter. | The proposal has been criticized by several experts. |
| Presens perfektum | har kritisert | De har kritisert rapporten offentlig. | They have criticized the report publicly. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde kritisert | Han hadde kritisert planen før møtet begynte. | He had criticized the plan before the meeting began. |
| Futurum | skal kritisere | Jeg skal kritisere argumentene i neste avsnitt. | I will critique the arguments in the next paragraph. |
| Futurum | vil kritisere | Kommentatoren vil kritisere beslutningen på radioen. | The commentator will criticize the decision on the radio. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha kritisert | Innen fredag skal hun ha kritisert alle forslagene. | By Friday, she will have critiqued all the proposals. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha kritisert | Innen møtet vil gruppen ha kritisert utkastet. | By the meeting, the group will have critiqued the draft. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville kritisere | Jeg ville kritisere planen hvis jeg hadde bedre grunnlag. | I would criticize the plan if I had better grounds. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha kritisert | Hun ville ha kritisert vedtaket dersom hun hadde lest det. | She would have criticized the decision if she had read it. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle kritisere | Han sa at han skulle kritisere rapporten senere. | He said that he would critique the report later. |
| Imperativ | kritiser | Kritiser ideen, men forklar hvorfor! | Criticize the idea, but explain why! |
| Presens partisipp | kritiserende | En kritiserende tone gjorde samtalen vanskelig. | A critical tone made the conversation difficult. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | kritiseres | Forslaget må kritiseres grundig. | The proposal must be criticized thoroughly. |
| S-passiv, presens | kritiseres | Avgjørelsen kritiseres i dagens lederartikkel. | The decision is criticized in today’s editorial. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir kritisert | Planen blir kritisert av naboene. | The plan is being criticized by the neighbors. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble kritisert | Rapporten ble kritisert etter lanseringen. | The report was criticized after its release. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt kritisert | Forslaget har blitt kritisert fra flere hold. | The proposal has been criticized from several quarters. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | kritisert | En kritisert dommer svarte på spørsmålene. | A criticized judge answered the questions. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | kritisert | Et kritisert vedtak ble tatt opp på nytt. | A criticized decision was reconsidered. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | kritiserte | Den kritiserte planen ble endret. | The criticized plan was changed. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | kritiserte | De kritiserte forslagene ble diskutert. | The criticized proposals were discussed. |

**Form status:** Headword and dictionary forms checked; compound constructions and examples are authored teaching material.

**Notes:** The verb is transitive in the senses shown. Perfect and passive participles use *kritisert*; agreeing forms here are adjectival. Examples illustrate criticism or critical assessment, not quotations.
