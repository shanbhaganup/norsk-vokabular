**Form status:** Simple forms and participle spellings checked against Bokmålsordboka. Compound tenses and the impersonal S-passive are shown in context. The dictionary gives no established adjectival participle paradigm for the joking/fooling-around sense; unavailable rows are marked below.

**Notes:** This entry covers joking, fooling around, speaking foolishly, and acting or moving aimlessly. Bokmålsordboka lists past and perfect participles *tulla* and *tullet*; definite and plural adjectival forms are *tullede* and *tullete*, but those describe other, contextually distinct uses of *tullet/tullete*, so they are not assigned to this verb sense. The verb in this sense is intransitive or used with a prepositional complement, so ordinary personal bli-passives are not applicable. The dictionary lists an S-passive form, illustrated impersonally. “—” in adjective rows means no adjective inflection is established for the sense taught here.
