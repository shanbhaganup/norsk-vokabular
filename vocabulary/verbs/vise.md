## vise

**English:** to show; to point out

**Topics:** communication

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; productive constructions and original examples reviewed.

**Notes:** The entry covers showing, letting someone see, and pointing something out. The dictionary lists the present participle visende, but it is uncommon; the example treats it as a descriptive participle. Infinitive and present S-passive have the same spelling, shown in different contexts. Adjectival agreement of vist as a resultative participle is uncertain here and marked Unverified; the separate noun vise (“display”) and expression vise fram are distinct entries, not this verb sense. Compound constructions are examples, not dictionary inflections.
