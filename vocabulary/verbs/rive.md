## rive

**English:** to tear; to pull or rip; to demolish

**Topics:** home, repair, daily life

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å rive | Vi må rive den gamle tapeten. | We have to tear down the old wallpaper. |
| Presens | river | Vinden river i presenningen. | The wind tears at the tarpaulin. |
| Preteritum | reiv | Hun reiv arket i to. | She tore the sheet in two. |
| Preteritum, alternative | rev | Han rev av en bit av brødet. | He tore off a piece of the bread. |
| Perfektum partisipp | revet | Jeg har revet opp brevet. | I have torn up the letter. |
| Presens perfektum | har revet | De har revet den gamle garasjen. | They have demolished the old garage. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde revet | Hun hadde revet stoffet før hun oppdaget hullet. | She had torn the fabric before she noticed the hole. |
| Futurum | skal rive | Vi skal rive skuret neste uke. | We are going to demolish the shed next week. |
| Futurum | vil rive | Han vil rive av den løse tapen. | He will pull off the loose tape. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha revet | Innen fredag skal de ha revet veggen. | By Friday, they will have demolished the wall. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha revet | Innen kvelden vil jeg ha revet alle de gamle brevene. | By evening, I will have torn up all the old letters. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville rive | Jeg ville rive ned gjerdet hvis det var mitt. | I would tear down the fence if it were mine. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha revet | De ville ha revet huset hvis det ikke hadde vært verneverdig. | They would have demolished the house if it had not been protected. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle rive | Han sa at han skulle rive opp gulvet. | He said he would tear up the floor. |
| Imperativ | riv | Riv av plasten før bruk. | Tear off the plastic before use. |
| Presens partisipp | rivende | Den rivende vinden gjorde turen ubehagelig. | The biting wind made the trip unpleasant. |

| Konstruksjon | rive noe i stykker | Barnet rev kartongen i stykker. | The child tore the cardboard into pieces. |
| Konstruksjon | rive ned noe | De skal rive ned den gamle låven. | They are going to tear down the old barn. |
| Konstruksjon | rive opp noe | Hun rev opp konvolutten forsiktig. | She carefully tore open the envelope. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka lists *reiv/rev* as past alternatives and *reven/revet* as masculine/feminine participle alternatives. The neuter participle is *revet*; definite singular and plural are *revne*.

**Notes:** This entry covers *rive* 2, including tearing, pulling, grating, and demolishing. The imported record also merges *rive* 3, a distinct homonym meaning “treat someone / pay for something”; that sense is not included here. Examples are original teaching material.
