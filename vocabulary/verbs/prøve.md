## prøve

**English:** to try, attempt, test

**Topics:** learning, daily life

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å prøve | Jeg vil prøve å lage suppa selv. | I want to try making the soup myself. |
| Presens | prøver | Han prøver en ny sykkel i butikken. | He is trying a new bicycle at the store. |
| Preteritum | prøvde | Vi prøvde den nye oppskriften i går. | We tried the new recipe yesterday. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; constructions are distinct patterns, not inflected forms.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers examining/testing something, rehearsing a performance, attempting to do something, and experiencing adversity. It also lists the constructions *prøve noe på*, *prøve seg fram*, *prøve seg på*, *prøve krefter*, and *prøve lykken*. Examples here focus on trying and testing; the legal passive example illustrates the “case is tried” sense. The adjectival participle *prøvd* is established in the sense “tested/experienced.” Examples are original.
