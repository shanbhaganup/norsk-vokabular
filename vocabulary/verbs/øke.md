**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists three past and participial forms (*øka, øket, økte* as past; *øka, øket, økt* as participle) and agreement variants. *Øke på* is a separate fixed expression meaning intensify/increase; it is not included in this lemma's inflection table.
