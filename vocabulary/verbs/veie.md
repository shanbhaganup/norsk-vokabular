## veie

**English:** to weigh; to have a weight

**Topics:** food, health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å veie | Det tar tid å veie pakken. | It takes time to weigh the parcel. |
| Presens | veier | Pakken veier to kilo. | The parcel weighs two kilos. |
| Preteritum | veide | I går veide hun pakken. | She weighed the parcel yesterday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | veid | Pakken er veid. | The parcel has been weighed. |
| Presens perfektum | har veid | Hun har veid pakken. | She has weighed the parcel. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde veid | Hun hadde veid pakken før hun sendte den. | She had weighed the parcel before sending it. |
| Futurum | skal veie / vil veie | Jeg skal veie suppen i kveld. / Jeg vil veie suppen når jeg kommer hjem. | I am going to weigh it tonight. / I will weigh it when I get home. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha veid / vil ha veid | Før sending skal hun ha veid pakken. / Før sending vil hun ha veid pakken. | Before dispatch, she will have weighed the parcel. / Before dispatch, she will have weighed the parcel. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville veie | Jeg ville veie suppen hvis den var kald. | I would heat the soup if it were cold. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha veid | Jeg ville ha veid suppen hvis den hadde blitt kald. | I would have heated the soup if it had become cold. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle veie | Hun sa at hun skulle veie suppen senere. | She said that she would heat the soup later. |
| Imperativ | vei | Vei pakken før du sender den! | Weigh the parcel before you send it! |

| Bli-passiv, perfektum | veies | Pakken har blitt veid. | The parcel has been weighed. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | veid | En veid pakke får en etikett. | A weighed parcel gets a label. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed inflections checked; compound tense and passive examples are contextual teaching constructions.

**Notes:** This entry follows veie, “find or state the weight” / “have a certain weight”. The dictionary also accepts the Bokmål variant vege with its own paradigm; this entry preserves the source anchor lemma veie. Intransitive weight statements do not form a natural passive, so passive rows are inapplicable for that use. Examples are original teaching sentences. Futurum rows distinguish intention (skal) from prediction/volition (vil). Conditional rows use ville; skulle is reserved for future viewed from a past context.
