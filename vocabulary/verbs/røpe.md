## røpe

**English:** to reveal; to disclose; to give away

**Topics:** communication, privacy

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å røpe | Hun lovet å ikke røpe hemmeligheten. | She promised not to reveal the secret. |
| Presens | røper | Ansiktsuttrykket hans røper nervøsiteten. | His facial expression gives away his nervousness. |
| Preteritum | røpa | Han røpa planen for søsteren sin. | He revealed the plan to his sister. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka lists *røpa/røpet/røpte* for past and *røpa/røpet/røpt* for the participle. Definite and plural adjective forms include *røpa, røpede, røpete,* and *røpte*. Every listed alternative is shown in an example.

**Notes:** The verb means disclose something unknown or secret, and can also mean that a sign or expression betrays/reveals something. The reflexive construction *røpe seg* means to give oneself away. Examples are original.
