## lide

**English:** to suffer; to undergo hardship or pain

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å lide | Ingen skal måtte lide i stillhet. | No one should have to suffer in silence. |
| Presens | lider | Hun lider av migrene. | She suffers from migraines. |
| Preteritum | led | Mange led av kulde den vinteren. | Many suffered from the cold that winter. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked for the assigned infinitive *lide*. Passive and adjectival participle use is not verified as natural for this intransitive verb.

**Notes:** Ordbøkene groups *lide* with the alternate infinitive *li* and lays out their paradigms together. The forms *lei* and *lidd* belong to *li*; this entry for the assigned infinitive *lide* uses *led* and *lidt*. The verb is intransitive and commonly occurs with *av* (“suffer from”) or *under* (“suffer under”). The imperative is grammatical but uncommon and literary in ordinary conversation. Adjectival cells remain Unverified rather than forcing rare participles into unnatural sentences.
