## bety

**English:** to mean; to signify; to matter

**Topics:** communication, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å bety | Hva prøver du å bety med det svaret? | What are you trying to mean by that answer? |
| Presens | betyr | Dette skiltet betyr at veien er stengt. | This sign means that the road is closed. |
| Preteritum, alternative 1 | betydde | Hun sa at avtalen betydde mye for henne. | She said that the agreement meant a lot to her. |

**Form status:** Dictionary-checked simple forms, including both accepted preterites; compound tenses are productive constructions. Passive and adjectival uses marked inapplicable where unnatural or unsupported.

**Notes:** The dictionary gives *betydde* and *betød* as alternative preterites and *betydd* as the perfect participle. S-passive *betys* is included in the reference list but is not a natural productive passive in the senses “signify” or “matter.” *Betyende* occurs in the fixed collocation *et betyende blikk* (“a meaningful look”). Examples are original teaching sentences.
