## rekke

**English:** to reach, manage to do in time

**Topics:** daily life, time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å rekke | Hun prøvde å rekke opp til den øverste hylla. | She tried to reach up to the top shelf. |
| Presens | rekker | Han rekker så vidt fram til håndtaket. | He can just reach the handle. |
| Preteritum | rakk | Jeg rakk ikke å spise før møtet. | I did not have time to eat before the meeting. |

**Form status:** The assigned sense matches Bokmålsordboka *rekke* 3 (rakk, rukket). The source snapshot also merges other homonyms with different paradigms; passive, present participle, and adjectival uses remain Unverified for this sense.

**Notes:** The source's form list combines three distinct dictionary entries: *rekke* 3 “reach/have time/be enough” (47952), *rekke* 4 “stretch out/hand something” (47953), and *rekke* 5 (47954), including an unrelated knitting sense. This entry follows the assigned English “to reach” and uses only the *rekke* 3 paradigm: past *rakk*, participle *rukket*. Passive and participial-adjective forms are Unverified because no natural examples in the assigned sense were established. Examples are original.
