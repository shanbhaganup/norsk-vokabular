## anta

**English:** to assume, to suppose

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å anta | Det er rimelig å anta at møtet blir kort. | It is reasonable to assume that the meeting will be short. |
| Presens | antar | Jeg antar at døra fortsatt er åpen. | I assume the door is still open. |
| Preteritum | antok | Vi antok at siste buss gikk ved midnatt. | We assumed the last bus left at midnight. |
| Perfektum partisipp | antatt | Turen tok lenger tid enn først antatt. | The trip took longer than initially assumed. |
| Presens perfektum | har antatt | Vi har antatt at alle kan delta digitalt. | We have assumed everyone can attend online. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde antatt | Jeg hadde antatt at butikken var stengt på mandager. | I had assumed the shop was closed on Mondays. |
| Futurum | skal anta | I neste regnestykke skal vi anta at renta holder seg stabil. | In the next calculation we’ll assume that the interest rate stays steady. |
| Futurum | vil anta | De fleste vil anta at prisen inkluderer frokost. | Most people will assume the price includes breakfast. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha antatt | Før vi går videre, skal dere ha antatt at de to linjene er parallelle. | Before we move on, you are to have assumed that the two lines are parallel. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha antatt | Innen da vil mange allerede ha antatt at møtet er avlyst. | By then many people will already have assumed that the meeting is cancelled. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville anta | Jeg ville anta at du var bortreist hvis jeg ikke fikk svar. | I would assume you were away if I got no reply. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha antatt | Uten meldingen din ville jeg ha antatt at alt var i orden. | Without your message I would have assumed everything was fine. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle anta | Planen var at vi skulle anta lik vekst i begge kommunene. | The plan was for us to assume equal growth in both municipalities. |
| Imperativ | anta | Anta at alle billetter koster like mye. | Assume all tickets cost the same. |
| Presens partisipp | antakende | Hun svarte i en antakende tone, som om hun tok svaret for gitt. | She replied in a tone that suggested she took the answer for granted. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | antas | Det kan antas at begge gruppene fikk samme beskjed. | It can be assumed that both groups received the same message. |
| S-passiv, presens | antas | Skrinet antas å være fra 1700-tallet. | The chest is assumed to date from the eighteenth century. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir antatt | I modellen blir antatt det at renta ligger fast. | The model assumes a fixed interest rate. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble antatt | Det ble antatt at feilen skyldtes en løs ledning. | It was assumed that a loose wire had caused the fault. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt antatt | Så langt har blitt antatt det at tallene er sammenlignbare. | So far the figures have been assumed to be comparable. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | antatt | Kartet viser en antatt grense mellom eiendommene. | The map shows a presumed boundary between the properties. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | antatt | Rapporten bygger på et antatt folketall. | The report is based on an assumed population figure. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | antatte | Det antatte tapet er tatt med i budsjettet. | The assumed loss is included in the budget. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | antatte | De antatte årsakene ble undersøkt hver for seg. | The presumed causes were investigated separately. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka confirms the simple forms and participial agreement. Compound tenses and passive constructions are illustrated in original bilingual examples.

**Notes:** Source spelling: anta. The examples illustrate ‘suppose’. Impersonal bli-passive and antakende are formal or uncommon. Antas is common in formal reporting.
