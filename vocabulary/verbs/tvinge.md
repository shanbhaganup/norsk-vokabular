## tvinge

**English:** to force; to compel; to press or bend physically

**Topics:** society, action

**Notes:** This entry follows the verb sense “press, push, or bend by physical force” and “make someone do something against their will,” including forcing a result through. The dictionary lists *tvungen* for masculine/feminine agreement, *tvunget* for neuter, and *tvungne* for definite singular/plural. *Tvunget* also occurs adjectivally in the “strained/stiff” sense, as in *et tvunget smil*.
