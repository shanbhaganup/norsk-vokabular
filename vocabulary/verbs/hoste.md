## hoste

**English:** to cough; of an engine, to sputter or cough

**Topics:** health, machines

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the headword and listed inflection alternatives. The adjectival uses are left unverified because the basic sense is intransitive. S-passive is used impersonally.

**Notes:** The dictionary also uses *hoste* for an engine making intermittent forceful sounds. *Hoste opp* takes an object meaning cough up, and figuratively “produce/procure”; this transitive construction can support passive forms, but this entry's passive rows illustrate general coughing only.
