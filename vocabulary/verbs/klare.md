**Form status:** The assigned sense “manage/cope” is verified in Bokmålsordboka 29921. The linked source record also includes article 29920, a distinct transitive sense “clear/clarify” with the same lemma and overlapping paradigm. Adjectival participle uses are unverified for the manage sense.

**Notes:** This entry follows the imported meaning “manage,” as in *klare en oppgave*, *klare å gjøre noe* and *klare seg*. Dictionary article 29920 is a distinct transitive sense “clear/clarify” (for example *klare kaffen* or *klare opp et problem*) and is not merged into this sense entry. The source's forms pair *klara* with *klara*, *klaret* with *klaret*, and *klarte* with *klart*. *Klarende* and participle agreement in the manage sense remain uncertain. Examples are original.
