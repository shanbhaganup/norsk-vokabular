## organisere

**English:** to organize; arrange

**Topics:** describing, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å organisere | Det er krevende å organisere møtet. | It is difficult to organize the meeting. |
| Presens | organiserer | Hun organiserer møtet. | She is organizing the meeting. |
| Preteritum | organiserte | I går organiserte vi møtet. | We organized the meeting yesterday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | organisert | Vi har organisert møtet. | The meeting is organized. |
| Presens perfektum | har organisert | Vi har organisert møtet allerede. | We have already organized the meeting. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde organisert | De hadde organisert møtet før kvelden. | They had organized the meeting before evening. |
| Futurum | skal organisere | Vi skal organisere møtet i morgen. | We are going to organize the meeting tomorrow. |
| Futurum | vil organisere | Dette vil organisere møtet. | This will organize the meeting. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha organisert | Innen i morgen vil vi ha organisert møtet. | By tomorrow, we will have organized the meeting. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha organisert | Innen i morgen vil vi ha organisert møtet. | By tomorrow, we will have organized the meeting. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville organisere | Jeg ville organisere møtet hvis jeg fikk ansvar. | I would organize the meeting if I were responsible. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha organisert | Jeg ville ha organisert møtet hvis jeg hadde hatt tid. | I would have organized the meeting if I had had time. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle organisere | Hun sa at hun skulle organisere møtet dagen etter. | She said she would organize the meeting the next day. |
| Imperativ | organiser! | Organiser møtet forsiktig! | Organize the meeting carefully! |
| Presens partisipp | organiserende | Den organiserende personen arbeidet videre. | The person organizing the meeting continued working. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | organiseres | Objektet må organiseres før arbeidet starter. | The object must be organized before work starts. |
| S-passiv, presens | organiseres | Objektet organiseres av en erfaren medarbeider. | The object is organized by an experienced employee. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir organisert | Objektet blir organisert nå. | The object is being organized now. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble organisert | Objektet ble organisert i går. | The object was organized yesterday. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt organisert | Objektet har blitt organisert allerede. | The object has already been organized. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | organisert | En organisert plan står her. | An organized plan is on display. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | organisert | Et organisert system er på plass. | An organized system is in place. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | organiserte | Den organiserte planen er klar. | The organized plan is ready. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | organiserte | De organiserte planene er klare. | The organized plans are ready. |
| Konstruksjon | organisere seg | De organiserte seg i en fagforening. | They organized themselves into a union. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and sense checked. Regular weak paradigm and productive constructions are derived; each form and participle alternative is separately illustrated.

**Notes:** Regular -ere verb; covers arranging/putting in order and organizing people into an association. The examples focus on a common sense and are original teaching examples. Some dictionary-listed secondary senses are not illustrated in every tense.
