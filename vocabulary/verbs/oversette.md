## oversette

**English:** to translate

**Topics:** communication, languages

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å oversette | Hun liker å oversette korte tekster. | She likes translating short texts. |
| Presens | oversetter | Han oversetter brevet fra norsk til engelsk. | He translates the letter from Norwegian into English. |
| Preteritum | oversatte | Vi oversatte menyen før restauranten åpnet. | We translated the menu before the restaurant opened. |
| Perfektum partisipp | oversatt | Romanen er oversatt til norsk. | The novel has been translated into Norwegian. |
| Presens perfektum | har oversatt | Jeg har oversatt hele artikkelen. | I have translated the entire article. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde oversatt | Hun hadde oversatt instruksjonene før møtet startet. | She had translated the instructions before the meeting began. |
| Futurum | skal oversette | Vi skal oversette nettsiden neste måned. | We are going to translate the website next month. |
| Futurum | vil oversette | Byrået vil oversette rapporten til flere språk. | The agency will translate the report into several languages. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha oversatt | Innen fredag skal jeg ha oversatt de siste sidene. | By Friday, I will have translated the final pages. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha oversatt | Innen lanseringen vil de ha oversatt appen til seks språk. | By launch, they will have translated the app into six languages. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville oversette | Jeg ville oversette dokumentet hvis jeg hadde mer tid. | I would translate the document if I had more time. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; present-participle usage remains Unverified.

**Notes:** *Oversette* is transitive and commonly takes a target-language phrase with *til*; a source language may be introduced with *fra*. The dictionary lists *oversettende* as a present participle, but a natural example in this translation sense was not established, so it remains Unverified. Examples are original teaching material.
