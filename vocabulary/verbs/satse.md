## satse

**English:** to bet; to stake; to invest

**Topics:** money, sport, work

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka lists *satsa/satset* past and participle alternatives and *satsa, satsede,* and *satsete* definite/plural forms. The assigned “bet” sense is expressed by staking money or backing an outcome; the dictionary also includes investing and taking off from a springboard.

**Notes:** The participial adjective examples are contextually possible but less common than finite forms; the entry marks the dictionary forms separately. Examples are original.
