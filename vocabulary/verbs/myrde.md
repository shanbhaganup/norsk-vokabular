## myrde

**English:** to murder; to kill deliberately

**Topics:** society, law

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; *myrda/myrdet* are accepted past and participle alternatives. The verb is transitive and permits passive constructions.

**Notes:** The literal sense is murder, killing deliberately; the dictionary also records figurative *myrde noen med blikket* (“give someone a murderous look”). The adjective-like participle *myrdede/myrdete* alternatives are retained; *myrdede* is the more common formal form. Examples are original fictional teaching sentences.
