## koste

**English:** to cost; entail or require; spend money on

**Topics:** money, daily life

**Form status:** The cost sense and forms are verified in Bokmålsordboka 31810. The source also links 31809, which is a separate verb sense “sweep with a broom”; its S-passive and participial forms are not transferred to this cost sense.

**Notes:** The assigned sense includes a price, expenses or consequences (*det kostet henne jobben*). *Koste på seg* means spend money on/treat oneself to something. The source's S-passive *kostes* corresponds to the separate sweeping homonym, so it is excluded as inapplicable here. *Kostende* remains unverified for the price sense. Examples are original.
