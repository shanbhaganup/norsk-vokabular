## klappe

**English:** to clap; pat or stroke gently; to slap

**Topics:** communication, people, animals

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the forms and *-a/-et* alternatives. Transitive senses such as patting and applauding allow passive forms; intransitive uses such as a heart beating are not shown here.

**Notes:** *Klappe* can mean clap hands, pat/stroke someone or an animal, strike lightly, clap something shut, or of a vessel, come alongside a quay. The *-ede/-ete* adjectival forms are listed but less common than *-a*. Examples are original.
