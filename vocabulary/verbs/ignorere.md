## ignorere

**English:** to ignore, disregard

**Topics:** communication, behavior

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å ignorere | Det er vanskelig å ignorere støyen. | It is difficult to ignore the noise. |
| Presens | ignorerer | Han ignorerer meldingene mine. | He is ignoring my messages. |
| Preteritum | ignorerte | Hun ignorerte advarselen. | She ignored the warning. |
| Perfektum partisipp | ignorert | Et ignorert varsel kan føre til problemer. | An ignored warning can lead to problems. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the regular paradigm and transitive sense. Passive forms are shown with a direct object.

**Notes:** The dictionary defines *ignorere* as not caring about or disregarding something. *Ignorerende* is the listed present participle; attributive use is possible but less frequent than the ordinary finite verb. Examples are original.
