## miste

**English:** to lose; to no longer have; to miss (a bus, opportunity, etc.)

**Topics:** general, travel

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. Passive constructions are generally avoided for the intransitive-like sense “lose”; the verb is transitive in active sentences.

**Notes:** *Miste* means no longer have something and also “fail to catch/attend” (for example, *miste bussen*). The past and participle alternatives are *mista/mistet*. The dictionary lists *mistende*, but no natural use in the assigned meaning was confirmed; it remains Unverified. Likewise, s-passive *mistes* is listed in the source paradigm but not verified as idiomatic; ordinary statements use active clauses. The rarer definite/plural participles *mistede/mistete* are retained from the dictionary paradigm, with usage-naturalness pending review.
