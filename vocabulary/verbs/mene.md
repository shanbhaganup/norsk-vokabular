## mene

**English:** to mean, intend, think, or believe

**Topics:** communication, opinions

**Form status:** Forms checked for the assigned spelling *mene*. Ordbøkene also lists the alternate infinitive *meine* with a distinct paradigm.

**Notes:** *Mene* can mean have in mind/mean, intend, think/believe, and emphasize a fact. It commonly takes an *at*-clause, an object, or an infinitive (*mene å gjøre noe*). The reference records source spelling *mente*; this is the regular preterite of the assigned infinitive *mene*, not a separate lemma. Ordbøkene also lists *meine/meiner/meinte/meint*; those forms are not imported into this *mene* entry. The dictionary lists present participle *menende*, but its natural use is unclear here, so it remains Unverified. Examples are original.
