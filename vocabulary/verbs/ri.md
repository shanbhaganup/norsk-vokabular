## ri

**English:** to ride (an animal or vehicle)

**Topics:** animals, sport, travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å ri | Hun liker å ri på islandshest. | She likes riding Icelandic horses. |
| Presens | rir | De rir langs stranden hver søndag. | They ride along the beach every Sunday. |
| Preteritum | red | Han red hesten hjem etter turen. | He rode the horse home after the trip. |
| Preteritum, alternative | rei | Hun rei over jordet i rolig tempo. | She rode across the field at an easy pace. |
| Perfektum partisipp | ridd | Jeg har ridd den hesten før. | I have ridden that horse before. |
| Presens perfektum | har ridd | Barna har ridd på ponni i dag. | The children have ridden ponies today. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde ridd | Han hadde ridd i flere timer før pausen. | He had ridden for several hours before the break. |
| Futurum | skal ri | Vi skal ri i fjellet i morgen. | We are going to ride in the mountains tomorrow. |
| Futurum | vil ri | Hun vil ri den nye hesten alene. | She wants to ride the new horse alone. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha ridd | Innen lunsj skal de ha ridd gjennom dalen. | By lunchtime, they will have ridden through the valley. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha ridd | Innen kvelden vil jeg ha ridd hele runden. | By evening, I will have ridden the whole route. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville ri | Jeg ville ri hvis været var bedre. | I would ride if the weather were better. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha ridd | Hun ville ha ridd videre hvis hesten ikke var blitt halt. | She would have ridden on if the horse had not gone lame. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle ri | Han fortalte at han skulle ri etter frokost. | He said that he would ride after breakfast. |
| Imperativ | ri | Ri rolig ned bakken. | Ride slowly down the hill. |
| Presens partisipp | riende | Den riende jenta vinket til oss. | The girl riding waved to us. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | ris | Hesten må ris regelmessig for å holde seg i form. | The horse must be ridden regularly to stay fit. |
| S-passiv, presens | ris | Denne hesten ris av erfarne ryttere. | This horse is ridden by experienced riders. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir ridd | Den unge hesten blir ridd korte økter. | The young horse is ridden in short sessions. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble ridd | Hesten ble ridd hjem etter konkurransen. | The horse was ridden home after the competition. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt ridd | Denne ponnien har blitt ridd av mange barn. | This pony has been ridden by many children. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | ridd | En ridd hest trenger å kjøles ned. | A horse that has been ridden needs to cool down. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | ridd | Et ridd dyr bør få hvile. | An animal that has been ridden should be allowed to rest. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | ridde | Den ridde hesten fikk vann. | The horse that had been ridden was given water. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | ridde | De ridde hestene ble stelt etter turen. | The horses that had been ridden were groomed after the ride. |
| Konstruksjon | ri på en hest | Han rir på en rolig fjording. | He rides a calm fjord horse. |
| Konstruksjon | ri i galopp | Rytteren red i galopp over enga. | The rider galloped across the meadow. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka lists *red* and *rei* as accepted past forms. Passive rows use the transitive “ride an animal” construction.

**Notes:** The source covers several extensions of the basic sense (sitting astride, sailing over waves, and figurative uses). This entry presents the common animal-riding sense; no separate unrelated sense is inferred from the imported English gloss. Examples are original.
