## oppgi

**English:** to state, provide (information)

**Topics:** communication, administration

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å oppgi | Du må oppgi fullt navn i skjemaet. | You must provide your full name on the form. |
| Presens | oppgir | Hun oppgir telefonnummeret sitt. | She gives her phone number. |
| Preteritum | oppga | Han oppga adressen til politiet. | He gave the police the address. |
| Preteritum | oppgav | Vitnet oppgav tidspunktet for møtet. | The witness stated the time of the meeting. |
| Perfektum partisipp | oppgitt | Beløpet er oppgitt i kroner. | The amount is stated in kroner. |
| Presens perfektum | har oppgitt | Vi har oppgitt alle nødvendige opplysninger. | We have provided all the required information. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde oppgitt | Hun hadde oppgitt feil postnummer før hun oppdaget feilen. | She had given the wrong postal code before she noticed the error. |
| Futurum | skal oppgi | Jeg skal oppgi referansene i søknaden. | I will provide the references in the application. |
| Futurum | vil oppgi | Rapporten vil oppgi resultatene for hvert år. | The report will state the results for each year. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha oppgitt | Innen fristen skal alle deltakerne ha oppgitt kontaktinformasjonen sin. | By the deadline, all participants must have provided their contact information. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha oppgitt | Innen fredag vil hun ha oppgitt de endelige tallene. | By Friday, she will have stated the final figures. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville oppgi | Jeg ville oppgi navnet mitt hvis det var nødvendig. | I would give my name if it were necessary. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha oppgitt | De ville ha oppgitt adressen hvis de hadde visst den. | They would have provided the address if they had known it. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle oppgi | Hun sa at hun skulle oppgi beløpet senere. | She said she would state the amount later. |
| Imperativ | oppgi | Oppgi fødselsdatoen din her. | Enter your date of birth here. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; compound constructions and examples are original.

**Notes:** This entry covers *oppgi* in the sense “state/provide information.” The separate sense “give up/relinquish” is outside the assigned reference sense. The past tense alternative *oppgav* is accepted, though less common than *oppga*. The dictionary lists *oppgiende* as a present participle, but an idiomatic example in this sense was not established, so usage remains Unverified.
