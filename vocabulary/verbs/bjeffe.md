## bjeffe

**English:** to bark; to snap or bark out an order

**Topics:** animals, communication

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; passive marked inapplicable for the intransitive bark sense; compound constructions are productive examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary gives *bjeffa / bjeffet* for preteritum and perfect participle, plus *bjeff* as imperative and *bjeffende* as present participle. Its figurative sense “speak in a short, forceful way” is also covered by these forms; that sense can take an object in phrases such as *bjeffe ut en ordre*, but the passive of this verb is not a natural ordinary construction.
