## dø

**English:** to die; to cease to exist or fade away

**Topics:** life, nature

**Form status:** Inflection and principal senses verified against Bokmålsordboka. Passive and agreeing perfect-participle adjective rows are inapplicable to this intransitive change-of-state verb.

**Notes:** Dø means to stop living; it also appears in combinations such as dø ut and dø bort for ending or fading away. The reference snapshot lists døs as an S-passive, but this is not a passive of the intransitive verb in the sense “die”; it is excluded here. The imperative is morphologically regular but pragmatically unusual. An adjectival “dead” is død, a related adjective, not an inflection of dø.
