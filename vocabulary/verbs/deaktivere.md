## deaktivere

**English:** to deactivate; to disable

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å deaktivere | Det er enkelt å deaktivere varselet. | It is easy to deactivate the alert. |
| Presens | deaktiverer | Hun deaktiverer varselet. | She deactivates the alert. |
| Preteritum | deaktiverte | Hun deaktiverte varselet i går. | She deactivated the alert yesterday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | deaktivert | Varselet er deaktivert. | The alert is deactivated. |
| Presens perfektum | har deaktivert | Hun har deaktivert varselet. | She has deactivated the alert. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde deaktivert | Hun hadde deaktivert varselet før møtet. | She had deactivated the alert before the meeting. |
| Futurum | skal deaktivere / vil deaktivere | Jeg skal deaktivere funksjonen nå. / Jeg vil deaktivere kontoen senere. | I am going to deactivate the feature now. / I will deactivate the account later. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha deaktivert / vil ha deaktivert | Innen fredag skal jeg ha deaktivert funksjonen. / Innen fredag vil jeg ha deaktivert kontoen. | By Friday I will have deactivated the feature. / By Friday I will have deactivated the account. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville deaktivere | Jeg ville deaktivere funksjonen hvis den skapte problemer. | I would deactivate the feature if it caused problems. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha deaktivert | Jeg ville ha deaktivert kontoen hvis jeg hadde fått beskjed. | I would have deactivated the account if I had been told. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle deaktivere | Han sa at han skulle deaktivere varselet senere. | He said he would deactivate the alert later. |
| Imperativ | deaktiver | Deaktiver varselet før presentasjonen! | Deactivate the alert before the presentation! |
| Presens partisipp | deaktiverende | Den deaktiverende funksjonen stanser varsler. | The deactivating function stops alerts. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | deaktiveres | Kontoen må deaktiveres manuelt. | The account must be deactivated manually. |
| S-passiv, presens | deaktiveres | Funksjonen deaktiveres automatisk om natten. | The feature is deactivated automatically at night. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir deaktivert | Kontoen blir deaktivert etter søknaden. | The account is deactivated after the application. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble deaktivert | Funksjonen ble deaktivert i går. | The feature was deactivated yesterday. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt deaktivert | Varselet har blitt deaktivert. | The alert has been deactivated. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | deaktivert | En deaktivert konto kan ikke brukes. | A deactivated account cannot be used. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | deaktivert | Et deaktivert varsel vises ikke. | A deactivated alert is not displayed. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | deaktiverte | Den deaktiverte kontoen ble slettet. | The deactivated account was deleted. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | deaktiverte | De deaktiverte varslene vises ikke. | The deactivated alerts are not displayed. |

**Form status:** Headword and dictionary forms checked; compound constructions and examples are authored for this entry.

**Notes:** A regular verb formed with the productive prefix *de-* from *aktivere*. Passive forms are natural for devices, functions and accounts.
