## drikke

**English:** to drink; consume a liquid; also to drink alcohol

**Topics:** food, health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å drikke | Jeg prøver å drikke mer vann. | I am trying to drink more water. |
| Presens | drikker | Hun drikker te hver morgen. | She drinks tea every morning. |
| Preteritum | drakk | Vi drakk kaffe etter lunsj. | We drank coffee after lunch. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; compound tenses and passive examples are productive constructions.

**Notes:** The headword covers drinking liquids, drinking alcohol, and some extended uses. Bokmålsordboka lists *drukken* and *drukket* as masculine/feminine participle agreement alternatives; the adjectival meaning “intoxicated” is associated with *drukken*. The paired alternatives share one idiomatic example because the participle variants do not both convey “intoxicated” naturally. The definite and plural adjectival *drukne* means “drunk/intoxicated” in the examples.
