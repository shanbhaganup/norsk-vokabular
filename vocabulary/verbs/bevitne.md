## bevitne

**English:** to witness; to attest; to certify

**Topics:** law, communication

**Notes:** The dictionary lists two alternatives for past tense and perfect participle, two imperatives, and the adjectival forms shown above. The passive examples use the sense “certify / attest”; passive is less common for the witness-to-an-event sense.
