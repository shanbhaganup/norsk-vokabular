## streve

**English:** to struggle; to work hard; to strive

**Topics:** work, learning

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å streve | Det kan være tungt å streve alene. | It can be hard to struggle alone. |
| Presens | strever | Hun strever med den vanskelige oppgaven. | She is struggling with the difficult task. |
| Preteritum | strevet / strevde | De strevet hele dagen. / Han strevde for å nå målet. | They worked hard all day. / He struggled to reach the goal. |
| Perfektum partisipp | strevd / strevet | Jeg har strevd med søknaden. / Vi har strevet lenge. | I have struggled with the application. / We have worked hard for a long time. |
| Presens perfektum | har strevd / har strevet | Jeg har strevd med uttalen. / Vi har strevet lenge. | I have struggled with the pronunciation. / We have worked hard for a long time. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde strevd / hadde strevet | Hun hadde strevd før hun fikk hjelp. / De hadde strevet med prosjektet i månedsvis. | She had struggled before she got help. / They had worked hard on the project for months. |
| Futurum | skal streve / vil streve | Vi skal streve videre sammen. / Han vil streve med oppgaven uten veiledning. | We will keep working hard together. / He will struggle with the task without guidance. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha strevd / skal ha strevet | Innen sommeren skal hun ha strevd seg gjennom hele kurset. / Innen sommeren skal de ha strevet lenge med planen. | By summer, she will have worked her way through the whole course. / By summer, they will have worked on the plan for a long time. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville streve | Jeg ville streve mindre hvis jeg fikk hjelp. | I would struggle less if I got help. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha strevd / ville ha strevet | Han ville ha strevd videre hvis målet var viktigere. / Hun ville ha strevet mer dersom hun hadde hatt tid. | He would have kept struggling if the goal mattered more. / She would have worked harder if she had had time. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle streve | Hun visste at hun skulle streve med overgangen. | She knew she would struggle with the transition. |
| Imperativ | strev | Strev videre, så kommer du nærmere målet! | Keep striving and you’ll ga closer to the goal! |
| Presens partisipp | strevende | En strevende elev ba læreren om hjelp. | A struggling student asked the teacher for help. |

**Form status:** Simple forms checked against Bokmålsordboka; compound tenses and original examples are authored teaching material.

**Notes:** The verb means work hard or strive toward a goal; a separate noun strev exists but is not included here. The dictionary lists strevde/strevet in the past and strevd/strevet as participle, with additional adjectival agreement variants. Although the dictionary lists adjectival agreement forms, they do not yield natural examples for this intransitive sense; use an active clause.
