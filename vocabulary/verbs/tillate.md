## tillate

**English:** to allow; permit; make possible

**Topics:** rules, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å tillate | Skolen valgte å tillate mobiltelefoner i friminuttet. | The school chose to allow mobile phones during recess. |
| Presens | tillater | Loven tillater ikke parkering her. | The law does not allow parking here. |
| Preteritum | tillot | Rektor tillot elevene å gå tidligere. | The principal allowed the students to leave early. |
| Perfektum partisipp | tillatt | Dette er ikke tillatt. | This is not permitted. |
| Presens perfektum | har tillatt | Foreldrene har tillatt datteren å reise alene. | The parents have allowed their daughter to travel alone. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde tillatt | De hadde tillatt hunder før reglene ble endret. | They had allowed dogs before the rules were changed. |
| Futurum | skal tillate / vil tillate | Kommunen skal tillate uteservering. / Kommunen vil tillate uteservering. | The municipality will allow outdoor dining. / The municipality will allow outdoor dining. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha tillatt / vil ha tillatt | Innen sommeren skal de ha tillatt flere arrangementer. / Innen sommeren vil de ha tillatt flere arrangementer. | By summer, they will have allowed more events. / By summer, they will have allowed more events. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville tillate | Jeg ville tillate det hvis sikkerheten var ivaretatt. | I would allow it if safety were asured. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha tillatt | De ville ha tillatt besøket hvis de hadde fått beskjed. | They would have allowed the visit if they had been informed. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle tillate | Hun sa at de skulle tillate adgang etter klokka seks. | She said they would allow access after six o’clock. |
| Imperativ | tillat! | Tillat meg å forklare! | Allow me to explain! |
| Presens partisipp | tillatende | Den tillatende holdningen overrasket oss. | The permissive attitude surprised us. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | tillates | Hunder tillates ikke i bygningen. | Dogs are not allowed in the building. |
| S-passiv, presens | tillates | Overtid tillates bare etter avtale. | Overtime is allowed only by agreement. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir tillatt | Besøk blir tillatt etter registrering. | Visits are permitted after registration. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble tillatt | Adgang ble tillatt først etter kontroll. | Access was permitted only after inspection. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt tillatt | Flere unntak har blitt tillatt i år. | Several exceptions have been permitted this year. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | tillatt | Et tillatt avvik må dokumenteres. | A permitted deviation must be documented. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | tillatt | Et tillatt beløp står i vedtaket. | The permitted amount is stated in decision. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | tillatte | Den tillatte hastigheten er 50 km/t. | The permitted speed is 50 km/h. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | tillatte | De tillatte hjelpemidlene ligger på bordet. | The permitted aids are on the table. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and principal paradigm forms checked; compound tenses and original example sentences supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists preteritum tillot and participle tillatt; present tillater. S-passive tillates and bli-passive are shown. Tillatte is the adjective form in definite/plural contexts, distinct from the invariant participle in compound tenses. The — marker means the construction is genuinely inapplicable to the stated intransitive use. Other forms follow the Bokmålsordboka paradigm; alternatives are each illustrated in the same order.
