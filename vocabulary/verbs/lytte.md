## lytte

**English:** to listen; to listen attentively or heed

**Topics:** communication, music

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. Passive examples are impersonal constructions, commonly with *til*; attributive perfect-participle agreement is not verified as natural.

**Notes:** The verb means to try to hear, listen attentively, or heed. It normally takes *etter*, *på*, or *til* depending on the sense. The s-passive and bli-passives are shown impersonally with an expressed *til*-complement. Adjectival past participles are left Unverified because the verb is intransitive and no natural agreeing use was confirmed. Both *lytta* and *lyttet* are accepted past and participle forms.
