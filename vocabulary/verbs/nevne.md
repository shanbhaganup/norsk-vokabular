## nevne

**English:** to mention, name, or list

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å nevne | Det er nok å nevne ett eksempel. | It is enough to mention one example. |
| Presens | nevner | Hun nevner ikke navn i intervjuet. | She does not mention names in the interview. |
| Preteritum | nevnte | Han nevnte saken for kollegaen sin. | He mentioned the matter to his colleague. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; the transitive verb permits passive constructions.

**Notes:** *Nevne* means say/name something, list it, or speak about it briefly. Common constructions include *nevne noen ved navn*, *nevne noe for noen*, and *ikke nevne navn*. *Den/de nevnte* is a frequent adjectival participle meaning “the aforementioned.” Although the dictionary lists present participle *nevnende*, a natural example as an active participle was not established; it remains Unverified.
