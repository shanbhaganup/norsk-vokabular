## bringe

**English:** to bring; to cause; to present

**Topics:** communication, travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å bringe | Det er tungt å bringe alle kassene opp trappen. | It is hard to carry all the boxes up the stairs. |
| Presens | bringer | Bussen bringer oss til sentrum. | The bus takes us to the city centre. |
| Preteritum | brakte | Hun brakte saken videre til ledelsen. | She brought the matter to management. |
| Perfektum partisipp | brakt | En brakt gave sto på bordet. | A brought gift stood on the table. |
| Presens perfektum | har brakt | De har brakt nådvendig utstyr. | They have brought the necessary equipment. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde brakt | Han hadde brakt fram et nytt forslag får måtet sluttet. | He had presented a new proposal before the meeting ended. |
| Futurum (skal) | skal bringe | Jeg skal bringe dokumentene i morgen. | I will bring the documents tomorrow. |
| Futurum (vil) | vil bringe | Dette vil bringe oss nårmere en låsning. | This will bring us closer to a solution. |
| Futurum perfektum (skal) | skal ha brakt | Innen fredag skal hun ha brakt ut alle invitasjonene. | By Friday she will have delivered all the invitations. |
| Futurum perfektum (vil) | vil ha brakt | Innen året er omme, vil tiltaket ha brakt resultater. | By the end of the year, the measure will have produced results. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville bringe | Jeg ville bringe saken opp hvis det ble nådvendig. | I would raise the matter if necessary. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha brakt | De ville ha brakt mer mat hvis de hadde visst om besåket. | They would have brought more food if they had known about the visit. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle bringe | Han lovet at han skulle bringe nåkkelen tilbake. | He promised that he would bring the key back. |
| Imperativ | bring | Bring koppene inn fra terrassen! | Bring the cups in from the terrace! |
| Presens partisipp | bringende | Den bringende båten nårmet seg kaia. | The boat carrying people was approaching the quay. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | bringes | Varene må bringes forsiktig inn. | The goods must be brought in carefully. |
| S-passiv, presens | bringes | Posten bringes til åya med båt. | The mail is brought to the island by boat. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir brakt | Utstyret blir brakt inn får åpning. | The equipment is being brought in before opening. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble brakt | Forslaget ble brakt fram under måtet. | The proposal was presented during the meeting. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt brakt | Saken har blitt brakt inn for retten. | The matter has been brought before the court. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | brakt | En brakt pakke ligger ved dåra. | A delivered package is by the door. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjånn | brakt | Et brakt tilbud kom overraskende. | A brought offer came as a surprise. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | brakte | Den brakte rapporten manglet vedlegg. | The delivered report was missing an attachment. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | brakte | De brakte varene ble kontrollert. | The delivered goods were inspected. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed inflections verified; compound constructions are productive examples.

**Notes:** Brakte and brakt are the dictionary forms. Passive constructions are natural when the verb has an object; use active or a different construction where no patient is implied. Examples are original teaching sentences.
