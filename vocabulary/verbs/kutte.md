## kutte

**English:** to cut, chop, interrupt, or reduce

**Topics:** general, food, work

**Form status:** Dictionary alternatives checked; compound tense/passive examples are productive constructions. Alternative spellings are illustrated separately.

**Notes:** The preterite and past participle have *kutta* and *kuttet*. The dictionary also lists agreeing participial forms *kuttede* and *kuttete*; these less common forms are retained as listed and should be reviewed for register and naturalness. Reflexive *kutte seg* means to cut oneself; *kutte ned på* means reduce/curb, and *kutte ut* means stop/eliminate. They are constructions, not inflected forms. Examples show transitive uses suitable for passives.
