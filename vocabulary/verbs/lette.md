## lette

**English:** to lighten, ease, relieve; to lift or take off; of fog, to clear

**Topics:** general, health, weather

**Form status:** Dictionary forms and senses checked. Bli-passive and s-passive are shown only for transitive uses; the verb also has intransitive senses such as “take off” and “clear.”

**Notes:** *Lette* is polysemous. This entry emphasizes “make lighter/easier,” “relieve,” and “lift,” while also noting the intransitive senses “take off” (aircraft) and “clear” (fog). Past and participle alternatives are *letta* and *lettet*. The dictionary lists *lettede* and *lettete* as definite/plural adjectival participle alternatives; these are retained, though *lettete* is less common and should be reviewed for naturalness. Examples are original.
