**Form status:** Source past-tense and participle alternatives retained; productive compound constructions supplied. Present participle marked Unverified.

**Notes:** The phrase is formed from *kutte* plus particle *opp*. The reference source records *kutta* and *kuttet* as alternatives in finite and perfect forms and gives no s-passive. For adjective use, Norwegian normally places the particle before the participle (*oppkutta*, *oppkuttet*); these are shown separately from verbal perfect tenses. The unattested source ordering *kuttende opp* is left Unverified. The English sense is the specific act of cutting something into pieces.
