## leie

**English:** rent (obtain temporary use for payment)

**Topics:** travel, housing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å leie | Familien ønsker å leie en hytte ved sjøen. | The family wants to rent a cabin by the sea. |
| Presens | leier | Vi leier en leilighet i sentrum. | We rent an apartment in the city center. |
| Preteritum | leide | De leide en bil i sommerferien. | They rented a car during the summer holiday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | leid | Hytta er leid for hele uka. | The cabin is rented for the whole week. |
| Presens perfektum | har leid | Jeg har leid et rom nær universitetet. | I have rented a room near the university. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde leid | Hun hadde leid en sykkel før hun kom til byen. | She had rented a bicycle before she arrived in the city. |
| Futurum | skal leie | Vi skal leie et møterom i morgen. | We are going to rent a meeting room tomorrow. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha leid | Innen fredag skal de ha leid et lokale til festen. | By Friday, they will have rented a venue for the party. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville leie | Jeg ville leie en større leilighet hvis jeg hadde råd. | I would rent a larger apartment if I could afford it. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha leid | Vi ville ha leid et hus hvis det hadde vært ledig. | We would have rented a house if it had been available. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle leie | Han sa at han skulle leie bil på flyplassen. | He said that he would rent a car at the airport. |
| Imperativ | lei | Lei et rom p\u00e5 forh\u00e5nd hvis du kommer sent. | Rent a room in advance if you arrive late. |
| Presens partisipp | leiende | —; this verb’s present participle is not normally used as an independent adjective or noun in this paid-use sense. | —; no ordinary standalone use in this sense. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | leies | Leiligheten må leies ut før m\u00e5neden er omme. | The apartment must be rented out before the end of the month. |
| S-passiv, presens | leies | Hytta leies ut hele sommeren. | The cabin is rented out all summer. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir leid | Rommet blir leid av en student denne våren. | The room is being rented by a student this spring. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble leid | Huset ble leid av et ungt par i fjor. | The house was rented by a young couple last year. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt leid | Lokalet har blitt leid til flere arrangementer. | The venue has been rented for several events. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | leid | En leid bil står utenfor hotellet. | A rented car is parked outside the hotel. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | leid | Et leid rom må tømmes innen klokka tolv. | A rented room must be vacated by noon. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | leide | Den leide bilen står ved inngangen. | The rented car is by the entrance. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | leide | De leide rommene ligger i andre etasje. | The rented rooms are on the second floor. |

**Notes:** Dictionary alternatives are kept distinct in rows. Compound tenses and passive constructions are productive constructions, not extra dictionary headwords.  This entry is the paid-use verb; the homonymous verb meaning to lead someone by the hand is a separate dictionary sense.
