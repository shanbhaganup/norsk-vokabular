**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the *-a/-et* alternatives, both imperative forms and the senses “argue” and “malfunction.” S-passive is impersonal; adjectival past participles are unverified for natural usage.

**Notes:** The argument sense commonly takes *med* or *om*. The dictionary also uses *krangle* of a machine, body part or arrangement that does not work properly. Examples are original.
