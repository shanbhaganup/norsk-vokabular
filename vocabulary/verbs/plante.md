**Notes:** *Plante* is transitive in the assigned sense. The dictionary lists *planta* and *plantet* for past and participle forms, plus *planta/plantede/plantete* for definite and plural adjectival forms. The source groups *ville/skulle* under conditional; *skulle plante* is shown separately as a future viewed from the past, following project grammar conventions. Examples are original teaching material.
