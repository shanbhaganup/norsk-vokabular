**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the *-a/-et* forms, alternatives for imperative, and participle agreement. The verb is transitive in the connection senses.

**Notes:** *Koble* also means associate or bring into relation, and appears in compounds such as *koble av*, *koble fra*, *koble inn* and *koble opp*. Bokmålsordboka lists the parallel spelling *kople* with a separate conjugation (*kopler, kopla/koplet, kopl/kople*); the source record and this entry use *koble*. The *-ede/-ete* participles are dictionary-listed but less common than *-a*. Examples are original.
