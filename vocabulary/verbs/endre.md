## endre

**English:** to change; to alter

**Topics:** communication, work

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; compound tenses and passive examples are authored teaching constructions.

**Notes:** *Endra* and *endret* are accepted alternatives in the dictionary. The source also lists *endrede* and *endrete* for definite and plural adjectival participles. The S-passive is appropriate for general or procedural changes; *endre seg* (“change, become different”) is a separate reflexive construction, not an inflection.
