**Form status:** Dictionary headword, inflection and listed participle agreement forms checked; compound constructions and examples supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** The dictionary gives both -a and -et past and participle forms. It lists forandra and forandret for masculine/feminine and neuter adjectival participles; definite singular and plural also include forandra, forandrede and forandrete. *Forandre seg* means “to become different”; the reflexive pronoun changes with the subject, as in *Situasjonen forandret seg raskt* (The situation changed quickly). The passive forms are productive constructions. Examples are original teaching sentences.
