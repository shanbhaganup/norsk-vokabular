**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; compound tenses are productive.

**Notes:** Mislykkes is a lexical -s verb, not a passive. The dictionary lists mislykkes and mislyktes as perfect-participle variants and in compound forms; examples show both. It lists the imperative mislykkes, though imperative use is uncommon and normally avoided; the example is a general, proverbial instruction. The dictionary does not provide a present-participle or adjectival-participle paradigm; those forms remain unverified. Examples are original teaching sentences.
