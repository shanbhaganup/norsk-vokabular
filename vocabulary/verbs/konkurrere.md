## konkurrere

**English:** to compete, contend; be a competitor in a market

**Topics:** sport, work

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the paradigm and senses. The verb is intransitive, usually with *med*, *mot*, *om* or *på*. S-passive is impersonal; adjectival past participles are unverified for natural use.

**Notes:** *Konkurrere* means to contend in a contest or to compete in a market. The dictionary also lists *konkurrere ut* (“outcompete”). The adjective *konkurrerende* is common and means competing/competitor. Examples are original.
