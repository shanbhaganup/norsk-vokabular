## angre

**English:** to regret

**Topics:** emotions

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the simple forms and participial agreement. Compound tenses and passive rows illustrate productive constructions. The bilingual examples are original.

**Notes:** Source spelling: angre. The active constructions angre noe and angre på noe are common. Passive and agreeing past-participle examples use the direct-object construction and sound formal or literary. The imperatives angr and angre are both accepted and fit exhortations. Commercial uses meaning to cancel or withdraw a purchase fall outside this sense.
