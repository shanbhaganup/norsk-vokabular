**Form status:** Simple forms and participial agreement checked against Bokmålsordboka. Applicable compound tenses and passives are constructions. The bilingual examples are original.

**Notes:** The imported “border” gloss corresponds to grense til or grense opp til. The latter also has a figurative use. The imported “limit” gloss as a transitive verb corresponds to begrense. The geographic use is stative and takes a prepositional complement, so the passive rows are inapplicable to this sense. The imperative is illustrated in a figurative writing instruction. The dictionary lists agreeing past participles, but ordinary geographic usage favors grensende or a relative clause. The agreement rows record the dictionary spellings in sentences about the forms.
