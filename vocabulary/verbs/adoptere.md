## adoptere

**English:** to adopt a child

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å adoptere | De ønsker å adoptere et barn. | They want to adopt a child. |
| Presens | adopterer | Familien adopterer ei jente i år. | The family is adopting a girl this year. |
| Preteritum | adopterte | Tanta mi adopterte to søsken. | My aunt adopted two siblings. |
| Perfektum partisipp | adoptert | De fikk adoptert begge barna. | They managed to adopt both children. |
| Presens perfektum | har adoptert | De har adoptert en gutt. | They have adopted a boy. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde adoptert | De hadde adoptert sønnen sin før de flyttet hit. | They had adopted their son before they moved here. |
| Futurum | skal adoptere | De skal adoptere et barn neste år. | They are going to adopt a child next year. |
| Futurum | vil adoptere | Jeg tror flere familier vil adoptere i årene som kommer. | I think more families will adopt in the years ahead. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha adoptert | Etter planen skal de ha adoptert barnet innen jul. | They plan to have adopted the child by Christmas. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha adoptert | Jeg tror de vil ha adoptert barnet innen sommeren. | I think they will have adopted the child by summer. |
