## late som

**English:** to pretend; to give the impression of something untrue

**Topics:** communication

**Form status:** Inflectional forms checked in Bokmålsordboka; compound tenses are productive constructions. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Past tense has the accepted alternatives lata, latet and lot; the perfect participle has lata, latet and latt. The multiword expression takes idiomatic complements such as om-clauses and ingenting. It has no natural passive or agreeing adjectival use as a phrase.
