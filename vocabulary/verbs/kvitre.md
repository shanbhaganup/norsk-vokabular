**Form status:** Headword and listed forms checked. Bli-passive and adjectival participle agreement are inapplicable or unverified for this intransitive sound verb.

**Notes:** *Kvitre* is used intransitively for birds making short chirping sounds and figuratively for chirping-style chatter. An impersonal *det kvitres* is possible; this is not a personal bli-passive. The source lists two imperatives (*kvitr*, *kvitre*) and the past/past participle alternatives *kvitra*, *kvitret*. Participle agreement as an adjective is not established, so the forms remain Unverified rather than mechanically generated. Examples are original teaching sentences.
