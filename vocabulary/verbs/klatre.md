## klatre

**English:** to climb; rise in rank; grow upward along something

**Topics:** sport, nature

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the finite forms, alternatives and both imperatives. The verb is normally intransitive; S-passive is impersonal. Adjectival past participles are dictionary-listed but unverified for natural use.

**Notes:** *Klatre* also means rise in rank (*klatre oppover rangstigen*) and, of plants, grow upward along a support. Examples are original.
