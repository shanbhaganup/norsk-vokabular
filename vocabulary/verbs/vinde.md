## vinde

**English:** to wind; to wrap or coil

**Topics:** crafts, household

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed inflections checked; compound tense and passive examples are contextual teaching constructions.

**Notes:** The English source label “wind2” refers to vinde 2: wrap/coil something around something, or hoist/lower something using a winch. This is a rare, somewhat formal or technical verb. Past is vandt; participle is vundet, with vunden as an accepted masculine/feminine adjectival form. The hoisting sense takes an object; passive examples use that transitive sense. Examples are original teaching sentences. Futurum rows distinguish intention (skal) from prediction/volition (vil). Conditional rows use ville; skulle is reserved for future viewed from a past context.
