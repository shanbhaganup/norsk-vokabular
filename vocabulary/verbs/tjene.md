## tjene

**English:** to earn; to serve; to be useful for

**Topics:** work, money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å tjene | Hun håper å tjene nok penger til reisen. | She hopes to earn enough money for the trip. |
| Presens | tjener | Han tjener godt som elektriker. | He earns a good salary as an electrician. |
| Preteritum | tjente | Hun tjente ekstra penger i sommer. | She earned extra money this summer. |
| Perfektum partisipp | tjent | Hun har tjent pengene selv. | She has earned the money herself. |
| Presens perfektum | har tjent | Han har tjent godt på arbeidet sitt. | He has earned well from his work. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde tjent | Hun hadde tjent nok til å kjøpe sykkelen. | She had earned enough to buy the bicycle. |
| Futurum | skal tjene / vil tjene | Han skal tjene penger i sommer, og hun vil tjene nok til reisen. | He is going to earn money this summer, and she will earn enough for the trip. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha tjent / vil ha tjent | By August he is to have earned enough; by then she will have earned the same amount. | By August he will have earned enough; by then she will have earned the same. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville tjene | Hun ville tjene mer hvis hun jobbet heltid. | She would earn more if she worked full time. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha tjent | Han ville ha tjent mer hvis han hadde jobbet heltid. | He would have earned more if he had worked full time. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle tjene | Hun sa at hun skulle tjene penger på sommerjobben. | She said that she would earn money at the summer job. |
| Imperativ | tjen | Tjen penger på en ærlig måte! | Earn money honestly! |
| Presens partisipp | tjenende | Han har en tjenende rolle i organisasjonen. | He has a serving role in the organization. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | tjenes | Det må tjenes inn nok penger til å dekke utgiftene. | Enough money must be earned to cover the expenses. |
| S-passiv, presens | tjenes | Det tjenes godt i denne bransjen. | People earn good money in this industry. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir tjent | Pengene blir tjent gjennom hardt arbeid. | The money is earned through hard work. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble tjent | Pengene ble tjent på lovlig vis. | The money was earned legally. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt tjent | Pengene har blitt tjent gjennom flere småjobber. | The money has been earned through several odd jobs. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | tjent | En krone tjent på arbeid er bedre enn en krone lånt. | A krone earned through work is better than a krone borrowed. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | tjent | Et beløp tjent på arbeid ble satt inn på konto. | The amount earned was deposited into the account. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | tjente | Den tjente krona ble satt inn på konto. | The earned krone was deposited into the account. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | tjente | De tjente kronene ble satt inn på konto. | The earned kroner were deposited into the account. |

**Form status:** Dictionary-listed simple forms and participles checked; teaching examples authored and reviewed. Productive compound constructions reviewed for naturalness.

**Notes:** The entry records the reference’s broad lemma senses: earn money, be employed/serve, and be of use. The verb takes different complements by sense. Impersonal S-passive is natural in the money sense (Det tjenes penger …); bli-passive is used with money as the object. Adjectival participles are restricted; the listed tjent/tjente forms are shown in a natural context.
