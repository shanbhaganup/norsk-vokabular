## le

**English:** to laugh

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å le | Det er godt å le sammen. | It is good to laugh together. |
| Presens | ler | Hun ler av historien. | She laughs at the story. |
| Preteritum | lo | Vi lo så tårene trillet. | We laughed until the tears rolled. |
| Perfektum partisipp | ledd | Han har ledd av den vitsen før. | He has laughed at that joke before. |
| Presens perfektum | har ledd | Barna har ledd hele ettermiddagen. | The children have laughed all afternoon. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde ledd | Jeg hadde ledd hvis situasjonen ikke var så alvorlig. | I would have laughed if the situation had not been so serious. |
| Futurum | skal le | Vi skal le mye på hytteturen. | We are going to laugh a lot on the cabin trip. |
| Futurum | vil le | Hun vil le når hun hører historien. | She will laugh when she hears the story. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha ledd | Innen kvelden skal vi ha ledd av dette. | By this evening, we will have laughed about this. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha ledd | Innen filmen er slutt, vil barna ha ledd mange ganger. | By the end of the film, the children will have laughed many times. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville le | Han ville le hvis han kjente hele historien. | He would laugh if he knew the whole story. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha ledd | Hun ville ha ledd dersom hun hadde forstått spøken. | She would have laughed if she had understood the joke. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle le | De sa at de skulle le av det senere. | They said they would laugh about it later. |
| Imperativ | le | Le litt sammen med oss! | Laugh a little with us! |
| Presens partisipp | leende | En leende gjest vinket fra døren. | A laughing guest waved from the doorway. |

| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | ledd | En ledd gjest prøvde å skjule smilet. | A laughed-at guest tried to hide a smile. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. Passive examples use the established transitive expression *le noen ut* (“mock someone”); bare intransitive *le* does not take a personal passive.

**Notes:** The verb means to make sounds expressing amusement, joy, or mockery. The phrasal construction *le noen ut* means to ridicule someone, and its passive is *bli ledd ut*. The dictionary lists *ledd* and adjectival *ledde*. Adjectival examples are purposefully tied to the mockery construction. Examples are original.
