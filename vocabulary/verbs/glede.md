**Form status:** Simple forms and participial agreement checked against Bokmålsordboka. Applicable compound tenses and passives are constructions. The bilingual examples are original.

**Notes:** The transitive use has the person who feels joy as its object. Glede seg til means “look forward to” and glede seg over means “be glad about.” The reflexive pronoun agrees with the subject. Gledes in the passive rows is formal. The agreeing past participles are uncommon, and the examples use a formal register.
