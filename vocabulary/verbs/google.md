**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the simple forms and participle spellings. Compound tenses and passive constructions are productive. Examples are original.

**Notes:** The dictionary sense is searching online. Bokmålsordboka illustrates the reflexive construction google seg fram til noe. Past and participle have the alternatives googla/googlet; definite and plural adjectival forms also include googlede/googlete. The dictionary lists imperative googl. The verb is used transitively for search phrases and in reflexive seg fram til constructions. Passive examples use transitive use.
