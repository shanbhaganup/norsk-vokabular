## pleie

**English:** to usually do something; to be in the habit of

**Topics:** everyday-life

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Presens | pleier | Jeg pleier å gå til jobb. | I usually walk to work. |
