## beundre

**English:** to admire; to be impressed by; to look up to

**Topics:** emotions, people, culture

**Form status:** Dictionary-checked alternatives for preterite, perfect participle, and imperative; compound tenses and passive constructions are illustrated with both participle alternatives where applicable.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists *beundra / beundret* for preterite and perfect participle, and *beundr / beundre* for imperative. In adjective agreement, *beundra* is the -a form; *beundret* is the common neuter/indefinite form and takes *beundrede* in definite singular and plural. The verb is transitive and permits both S-passive and *bli*-passive. Examples are original teaching sentences.
