## reise

**English:** to travel, leave

**Topics:** travel, daily life

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å reise | Vi liker å reise med tog. | We like traveling by train. |
| Presens | reiser | Hun reiser til Bergen i morgen. | She is traveling to Bergen tomorrow. |
| Preteritum | reiste | De reiste hjem etter møtet. | They traveled home after the meeting. |
| Perfektum partisipp | reist | Jeg har reist med nattog før. | I have traveled by night train before. |
| Presens perfektum | har reist | Han har reist rundt i Europa. | He has traveled around Europe. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde reist | Vi hadde reist langt før vi tok en pause. | We had traveled far before we took a break. |
| Futurum | skal reise | Jeg skal reise til Tromsø neste uke. | I am going to travel to Tromsø next week. |
| Futurum | vil reise | De vil reise med buss gjennom Sverige. | They will travel by bus through Sweden. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha reist | Innen fredag skal familien ha reist gjennom tre land. | By Friday, the family will have traveled through three countries. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha reist | Innen sommeren vil hun ha reist til mange nye steder. | By summer, she will have traveled to many new places. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville reise | Jeg ville reise oftere hvis jeg hadde mer ferie. | I would travel more often if I had more vacation time. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha reist | De ville ha reist tidligere hvis flyet ikke var forsinket. | They would have traveled earlier if the flight had not been delayed. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle reise | Han sa at han skulle reise dagen etter. | He said that he would travel the next day. |
| Imperativ | reis | Reis lett hvis du skal bytte tog flere ganger. | Travel light if you have to change trains several times. |
| Presens partisipp | reisende | De reisende ventet på perrongen. | The travelers were waiting on the platform. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; passive and resultative participle-adjective forms are inapplicable to this intransitive travel sense.

**Notes:** This entry covers *reise* 3, “go/travel from place to place.” The reference links both *reise* 2 (raise/set upright) and *reise* 3 (travel); those are distinct senses. *Reise* also has a noun record in the reference snapshot, a separate word type. Passive and resultative participle-adjective forms do not apply to the intransitive travel sense. Examples are original.
