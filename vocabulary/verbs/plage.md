**Notes:** *Plage* is transitive and commonly takes a person, animal, or thing as its object. The source accepts *plaga, plagd,* and *plaget* as past and participle forms, and lists *plaga, plagde, plagede,* and *plagete* in definite/plural adjective forms. The -a forms are Bokmål alternatives. Some inflected participles are less frequent in ordinary prose; examples are original and use the listed spellings intentionally.
