## etterforske

**English:** to investigate (a possible crime, accident, or similar event)

**Topics:** law, society

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; compound tenses and passive examples are authored teaching constructions.

**Notes:** *Etterforska* and *etterforsket* are accepted alternatives. The dictionary lists *etterforskede* and *etterforskete* as definite and plural adjectival participles alongside *etterforska*. The sense is specifically to investigate a possible crime, accident, or similar event. Passive examples retain an appropriate case or accident as the patient.
