## hilse

**English:** to greet, say hello or goodbye; to pass on greetings; also to visit someone

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å hilse | Det er høflig å hilse når man kommer inn. | It is polite to greet people when you enter. |
| Presens | hilser | Han hilser på naboene hver morgen. | He says hello to the neighbors every morning. |
| Preteritum | hilste | Vi hilste på den nye læreren etter møtet. | We greeted the new teacher after the meeting. |
| Perfektum partisipp | hilst | Hun er hilst velkommen av hele gruppa. | She has been welcomed by the whole group. |
| Presens perfektum | har hilst | Jeg har hilst på søsteren din før. | I have met your sister before. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the headword and cited forms. Compound tenses and applicable passive constructions are shown. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Hilse covers greeting someone, passing on a greeting, and visiting someone (hilse på). It can also mean to receive or welcome a proposal or other thing, as in hilse et forslag med glede. The passive examples use this transitive sense. The dictionary lists hilst/hilste as agreeing participle forms, but natural attributive examples could not be verified.
