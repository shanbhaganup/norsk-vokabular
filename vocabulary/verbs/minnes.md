## minnes

**English:** remember; recall

**Topics:** memory, communication

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** Minnes is a lexical -s verb; its -s forms are not passive constructions, so no separate passive paradigm applies. The dictionary gives minnes/mins in the present and mintes in the past and perfect participle. The entry preserves the anchored “recall” sense; the dictionary also gives “hold a commemoration for” (as in *minnes de omkomne*), which is illustrated in the appropriate rows. Presens partisipp and adjectival participle forms are not supplied by the dictionary and remain unverified. Examples are original teaching sentences.
