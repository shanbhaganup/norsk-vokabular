## interessere

**English:** to interest, engage someone's attention

**Topics:** emotions, communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å interessere | Historien ser ut til å interessere barna. | The story seems to interest the children. |
| Presens | interesserer | Naturfag interesserer henne mest. | Science interests her the most. |
| Preteritum | interesserte | Filmen interesserte hele familien. | The film interested the whole family. |
| Perfektum partisipp | interessert | Et interessert publikum stilte mange spørsmål. | An interested audience asked many questions. |
| Presens perfektum | har interessert | Temaet har interessert meg siden skolen. | The topic has interested me since school. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde interessert | Hun hadde interessert seg for astronomi lenge. | She had been interested in astronomy for a long time. |
| Futurum, plan | skal interessere | Jeg skal interessere meg mer for lokalpolitikk. | I am going to take more interest in local politics. |
| Futurum, prediksjon | vil interessere | Denne utstillingen vil interessere mange besøkende. | This exhibition will interest many visitors. |

| Futurum perfektum, prediksjon | vil ha interessert | Innen sommeren vil boka ha interessert et bredt publikum. | By summer, the book will have interested a broad audience. |

| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | interessert | Et interessert barn fulgte nøye med. | An interested child followed closely. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | interesserte | Den interesserte deltakeren ble igjen etter kurset. | The interested participant stayed after the course. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | interesserte | De interesserte leserne meldte seg på. | The interested readers signed up. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the forms and the transitive sense “arouse interest”. The S-passive is possible but formal and uncommon. Bli + *interessert* is usually a change-of-state or adjectival construction, not a straightforward event passive.

**Notes:** In the common transitive pattern, the thing is the subject and the interested person is the object: *Boka interesserer meg*. The reflexive *interessere seg for* means “take an interest in” and is a construction, not a separate inflection. The participle *interesserende* is dictionary-listed but less common in everyday speech than *interessant*; it is retained here as the verb's form. Examples are original.
