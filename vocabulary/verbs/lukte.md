## lukte

**English:** to smell; to give off a smell

**Topics:** health, senses, food

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked for both senses: perceive a smell (transitive) and emit a smell (intransitive). Passives and agreeing past-participle adjectives are not verified as natural uses.

**Notes:** *Lukte på* means smell/sniff something; *lukte av* describes the odor emitted. The dictionary lists *lukta/luktet* as past and participle alternatives. Since the verb is also intransitive and adjectival forms overlap with unrelated uses, passive and agreeing participle cells remain Unverified rather than being mechanically supplied. *Luktende* is a present participle, often used with the sense “smelly.”
