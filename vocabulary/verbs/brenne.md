## brenne

**English:** to burn; to feel strongly about

**Topics:** nature, everyday life

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å brenne | Det tar tid å brenne våt ved. | It takes time to burn wet firewood. |
| Presens | brenner | Låven brenner etter lynnedslaget. | The barn is burning after the lightning strike. |
| Preteritum (brant) | brant | Sola brant over åkeren. | The sun blazed over the field. |
| Preteritum (brente) | brente | Vi brente låv i hagen i går. | We burned leaves in the garden yesterday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | brent | Ved er brent i ovnen. | Firewood has been burned in the stove. |
| Presens perfektum | har brent | Hun har brent seg på kjelen. | She has burned herself on the kettle. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde brent | Da vi kom, hadde lyset brent ned. | When we arrived, the candle had burned down. |
| Futurum (skal) | skal brenne | Vi skal brenne kvistene i morgen. | We are going to burn the branches tomorrow. |
| Futurum (vil) | vil brenne | Tårt papir vil brenne raskt. | Dry paper will burn quickly. |
| Futurum perfektum (skal) | skal ha brent | Innen kvelden skal bålet ha brent ut. | By evening, the fire will have burned out. |
| Futurum perfektum (vil) | vil ha brent | Innen du kommer, vil veden ha brent lenge. | By the time you arrive, the wood will have burned for a long time. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed inflections verified; compound constructions are productive examples.

**Notes:** Dictionary entry 7433 lists brant as the paradigm preterite; brente is also accepted. Which preterite is natural depends on sense and usage. Passive examples use the transitive åburn somethingå sense; many intransitive burning uses do not passivize naturally. Examples are original teaching sentences.
