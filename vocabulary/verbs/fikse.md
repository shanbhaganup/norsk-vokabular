## fikse

**English:** to fix, repair; to arrange or sort out

**Topics:** home, everyday life

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and inflection checked; examples and productive tense/passive constructions are authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Fikse has several senses, including repair, arrange/provide, and manage; examples here use repair and arrange senses. Both fiksa and fikset are accepted in past and perfect participle. Adjectival participle forms include fiksa/fikset in indefinite forms and fiksa/fiksede/fiksete in definite singular and plural. The present participle is used attributively in a descriptive clause; it is not a passive form.
