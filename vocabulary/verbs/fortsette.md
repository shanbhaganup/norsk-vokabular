## fortsette

**English:** to continue

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å fortsette | Vi må fortsette arbeidet etter pausen. | We must continue the work after the break. |
| Presens | fortsetter | Byggingen fortsetter gjennom vinteren. | Construction continues through the winter. |
| Preteritum | fortsatte | Byggingen fortsatte gjennom vinteren. | Construction continued through the winter. |
| Perfektum partisipp | fortsatt | Prosjektet har fortsatt uten avbrudd. | The project has continued without interruption. |
