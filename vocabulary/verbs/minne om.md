**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified for minne; the phrase retains om. Compound tenses are productive.

**Notes:** The person reminded is an object (for example, meg or henne). The ordinary passive rows are inapplicable for this verb phrase in the intended sense; use an active subject or a different construction. Dictionary variants include minna/minnet/minte in the past and minna/minnet/mint as participles. The rare mint/mint om paradigm is included because the dictionary lists it. Examples are original teaching sentences.
