## rette

**English:** to correct; to straighten, direct, or adjust

**Topics:** learning, communication, repair

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka gives *retta/rettet* as accepted past and participle alternatives. It also lists *retta, rettede,* and *rettete* for definite singular and plural adjectival participles; each alternative is illustrated. Present participle is marked Unverified rather than guessed.

**Notes:** *Rette* has multiple related uses: correct errors, straighten or set right, direct something toward a target, and *rette seg etter* (“comply with”). This entry follows the assigned correction sense while showing common constructions. Examples are original. The present participle is left Unverified because a natural example in the assigned sense was not confirmed.
