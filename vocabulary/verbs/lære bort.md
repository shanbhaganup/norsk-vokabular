## lære bort

**English:** teach; pass on knowledge

**Topics:** school

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and inflectional forms verified against Bokmålsordboka; compound tenses and voice constructions are productive constructions shown in context.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka records lære bort under lære as “formidle kunnskap eller ferdighet til andre”. The compound has regular finite/perfect forms; lært bort is not naturally used attributively in this sense, and lærende bort is not a natural participial phrase. Passive examples use knowledge or skills as the subject. Examples are original teaching sentences.
