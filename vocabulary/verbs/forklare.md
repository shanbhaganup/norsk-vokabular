## forklare

**English:** to explain; to make clear and understandable

**Topics:** communication, education

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å forklare | Læreren prøver å forklare regelen. | The teacher is trying to explain the rule. |
| Presens | forklarer | Hun forklarer oppgaven tydelig. | She explains the task clearly. |
| Preteritum | forklarte | Han forklarte dem framgangsmåten. | He explained the procedure to them. |
| Perfektum partisipp | forklart | Hun har forklart saken godt. | She has explained the matter well. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene verifies the headword, core paradigm, participle agreement and present participle. Productive tense and passive constructions are illustrated in original examples.

**Notes:** This entry covers the ordinary sense “make clear and understandable” and its common object patterns (*forklare noe*, *forklare noen noe*). The dictionary also lists a rare, older religious sense “glorify”; that sense is not included here. The verb's reflexive construction *forklare seg* (“give an explanation of oneself”) is related usage, not an inflection.
