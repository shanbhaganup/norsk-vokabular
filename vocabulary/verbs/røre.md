## røre

**English:** to stir; to mix

**Topics:** food, cooking

**Form status:** Regular verb with dictionary forms *rørte*, *rørt*, *rørende*, and *rør*. The listed present participle commonly functions as an adjective meaning “moving/touching”; a natural present-participle example in the assigned stirring sense could not be confirmed, so its use here is Unverified. This entry follows the cooking sense of *røre* 3.

**Notes:** The dictionary entry has other uses such as move, touch, affect emotionally, and *røre seg* (“move”). This entry centers on mixing/stirring food, with other senses excluded from the examples. Examples are original.
