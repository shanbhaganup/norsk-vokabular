## engasjere

**English:** to engage; to involve; to hire for a specific period

**Topics:** work, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; compound tenses and passive examples are authored teaching constructions.

**Notes:** *Engasjere* has several senses: hire/contract, invite to dance, arouse interest, and involve oneself or others. Examples here cover hiring and involving/engaging. The adjective “committed/engaged” uses the same participle form. Passive *engasjeres* is possible in contexts such as involving participants or hiring staff; the construction must retain a suitable human or group object.
