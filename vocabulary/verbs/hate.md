**Notes:** The dictionary includes both deep hatred and strong dislike. Hate commonly takes a person, thing, activity, or subordinate clause as its object. The snapshot lists s-passive hates, but natural passive uses are uncertain for this stative verb, so those forms remain Unverified. Bli-passive is included as an attested productive construction with the two participle alternatives.
