**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked for the counting/calculation homonym. The reference snapshot's linked dictionary entry is the separate rain sense; the source's *regnte/regnt* forms are not imported into this sense.

**Notes:** The assigned English gloss “to count” matches Bokmålsordboka *regne 2* (47822), not the snapshot's linked *regne 1* (47821), which means “rain.” This entry follows the counting/calculation sense and the correct homonym's forms: *regna/regnet* for past/participle and *regna/regnede/regnete* for definite/plural adjectival participles. The dictionary lists *regnende* as present participle, but a natural example in the counting sense was not established, so it remains Unverified. The dictionary also lists *regne med*, *regne opp*, *regne over*, *regne på*, and *regne sammen* as constructions; *regne med* and *regne ut* are illustrated here. Examples are original.
