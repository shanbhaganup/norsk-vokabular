## investere

**English:** to invest; to buy or acquire something (often an expensive item)

**Topics:** money, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å investere | Det lønner seg å investere langsiktig. | It pays to invest for the long term. |
| Presens | investerer | Selskapet investerer i ny teknologi. | The company is investing in new technology. |
| Preteritum | investerte | Hun investerte sparepengene i et fond. | She invested her savings in a fund. |
| Perfektum partisipp | investert | Kapitalen er investert i lokale bedrifter. | The capital is invested in local businesses. |
| Presens perfektum | har investert | Vi har investert i et mer energieffektivt hus. | We have invested in a more energy-efficient house. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde investert | De hadde investert før prisene steg. | They had invested before prices rose. |
| Futurum, plan | skal investere | Jeg skal investere litt av bonusen. | I am going to invest some of the bonus. |
| Futurum, prediksjon | vil investere | Fondet vil investere i fornybar energi. | The fund will invest in renewable energy. |
| Futurum perfektum, plan | skal ha investert | Innen året er omme, skal vi ha investert i nytt utstyr. | By the end of the year, we will have invested in new equipment. |
| Futurum perfektum, prediksjon | vil ha investert | Innen da vil selskapet ha investert betydelige beløp. | By then, the company will have invested substantial sums. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville investere | Jeg ville investere mer hvis risikoen var lavere. | I would invest more if the risk were lower. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha investert | Hun ville ha investert hvis hun hadde hatt råd. | She would have invested if she had been able to afford it. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle investere | De sa at de skulle investere i et nytt lokale. | They said that they would invest in a new premises. |
| Imperativ | invester | Invester i bedre verktøy hvis du skal jobbe mye ute! | Invest in better tools if you are going to work outdoors a lot! |
| Presens partisipp | investerende | Det investerende selskapet ansatte flere rådgivere. | The investing company hired several advisers. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | investeres | Midlene bør investeres forsiktig. | The funds should be invested cautiously. |
| S-passiv, presens | investeres | En stor del av overskuddet investeres i forskning. | A large part of the profit is invested in research. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir investert | Pengene blir investert i et indeksfond. | The money is being invested in an index fund. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble investert | Kapitalen ble investert i selskapet i fjor. | The capital was invested in the company last year. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt investert | Store summer har blitt investert i prosjektet. | Large sums have been invested in the project. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | investert | En investert sum kan svinge i verdi. | An invested sum can fluctuate in value. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | investert | Et investert beløp bør følges opp. | An invested amount should be monitored. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | investerte | Den investerte kapitalen ga avkastning. | The invested capital generated a return. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | investerte | De investerte midlene støttet flere bedrifter. | The invested funds supported several businesses. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the forms and meanings. *Investere i* is the usual construction for putting capital into something; passive examples use the same transitive sense.

**Notes:** The dictionary also lists *investere* meaning to buy or acquire something, for example an outfit for a special occasion. Examples are original.
