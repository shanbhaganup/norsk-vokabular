## oppkalle

**English:** to name after someone

**Topics:** family, culture

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; compound constructions and examples are original.

**Notes:** *Oppkalle* takes *etter* before the person or thing honored. The entry follows the assigned sense “give a name after someone”; the related expression *kalle opp* also has a radio-contact sense, which is not included here. The dictionary lists *oppkallende*, but a natural example in the assigned sense was not established, so usage remains Unverified. Examples are original teaching material.
