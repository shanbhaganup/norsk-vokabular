## møtes

**English:** to meet each other; to converge

**Topics:** communication, travel

**Form status:** Dictionary confirms *møtes, møttes, har møttes*. It is a reciprocal/lexical s-verb, not an ordinary active verb with a productive passive paradigm.

**Notes:** *Møtes* means meet one another or converge, and is related to but not an inflection of the separate verb *møte*. The -s is lexical reciprocal marking, not passive voice. The dictionary does not list an imperative; the reference lists *møtes*, which is usable as a short appointment instruction but remains an inferred pragmatic imperative rather than a separately verified dictionary form. Present participle and passive/adjectival-participle rows are inapplicable for this reciprocal verb. The second sense “converge” is illustrated by rivers meeting in the valley in the dictionary; this entry emphasizes reciprocal meeting.
