## invadere

**English:** to invade; to arrive in large numbers

**Topics:** society, conflict, travel

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the headword, forms and both senses. Passive forms are shown with the transitive military sense.

**Notes:** The figurative sense means that many people or things arrive in an area, as when tourists flood a mountain region. The word often has a forceful or negative tone. Examples are original.
