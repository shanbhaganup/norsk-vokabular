**English:** to bring up, foster, or raise a child

**Topics:** family

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; *oppfostra/oppfostret* are accepted past and participle alternatives. The transitive verb permits passive constructions.

**Notes:** *Oppfostre* means bring up or rear, especially children, and is close in meaning to *oppdra*. It is transitive in the examples. The dictionary lists two imperative forms, *oppfostr* and *oppfostre*, and participial alternatives *oppfostra/oppfostret*. Definite/plural *oppfostrede/oppfostrete* are less common; forms are retained as listed. Examples are original.
