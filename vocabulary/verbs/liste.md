**Form status:** Simple forms and participial agreement checked against Bokmålsordboka. Applicable compound tenses and passives are constructions. The bilingual examples are original.

**Notes:** The reference groups multiple homographs. This entry follows liste II, the sense supported by the imported gloss “list.” Liste opp means “list off” or “enumerate.” The homographs meaning “sneak” and “fit with trim” are separate verbs.
