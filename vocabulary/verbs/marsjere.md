## marsjere

**English:** to march; to move in an organized group or under command

**Topics:** sport, military, movement

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. The intransitive “march” sense has no personal passive; passive examples use the dictionary-listed transitive military sense “lead a unit in formation.”

**Notes:** The verb usually means move in an organized group, often in step. The dictionary also permits a transitive military sense, “lead a unit in close-order formation,” used for the passive examples. Adjectival perfect participle agreement is not established as natural and remains Unverified. Examples are original teaching material.
