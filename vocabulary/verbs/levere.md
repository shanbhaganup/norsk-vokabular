## levere

**English:** to deliver, hand in, submit; to produce or perform

**Topics:** work, education, transport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å levere | Hun må levere rapporten før fredag. | She has to submit the report before Friday. |
| Presens | leverer | Butikken leverer varer hver onsdag. | The shop delivers goods every Wednesday. |
| Preteritum | leverte | Jeg leverte søknaden personlig. | I submitted the application in person. |
| Perfektum partisipp | levert | Pakken er levert til resepsjonen. | The package has been delivered to reception. |
| Presens perfektum | har levert | Artisten har levert en sterk konsert. | The artist has given a strong performance. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde levert | De hadde levert bevisene før fristen. | They had submitted the evidence before the deadline. |
| Futurum | skal levere | Vi skal levere møblene i morgen. | We will deliver the furniture tomorrow. |
| Futurum | vil levere | Turbinen vil levere nok strøm til bygningen. | The turbine will provide enough electricity for the building. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha levert | Innen mandag skal de ha levert alle varene. | By Monday, they will have delivered all the goods. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha levert | Innen sesongslutt vil laget ha levert gode resultater. | By the end of the season, the team will have produced good results. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville levere | Hun ville levere rapporten tidligere hvis hun kunne. | She would submit the report earlier if she could. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha levert | Vi ville ha levert pakken hvis adressen hadde vært riktig. | We would have delivered the package if the address had been correct. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle levere | Han sa at han skulle levere nøklene dagen etter. | He said he would hand in the keys the next day. |
| Imperativ | lever | Lever skjemaet i skranken! | Hand in the form at the desk! |
| Presens partisipp | leverende | Den leverende sjåføren ringte på døren. | The delivering driver rang the doorbell. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | leveres | Dokumentene må leveres innen fristen. | The documents must be submitted by the deadline. |
| S-passiv, presens | leveres | Varene leveres hver morgen. | The goods are delivered every morning. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir levert | Maten blir levert til kontoret. | The food is delivered to the office. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble levert | Rapporten ble levert før møtet. | The report was submitted before the meeting. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt levert | Utstyret har blitt levert til laboratoriet. | The equipment has been delivered to the laboratory. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | levert | En levert pakke lå ved inngangen. | A delivered package lay by the entrance. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | levert | Et levert skjema manglet en underskrift. | A submitted form was missing a signature. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | leverte | Den leverte rapporten ble godkjent. | The submitted report was approved. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | leverte | De leverte varene ble kontrollert. | The delivered goods were checked. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms and main senses checked; productive compound tenses and passive constructions illustrated.

**Notes:** The verb can mean bring or hand something over, produce a result, or perform well. Most examples are transitive; *levere på* (“perform/deliver”) can also occur without a direct object. Participial adjective agreement is *levert* indefinite singular and *leverte* definite/plural. Examples are original teaching sentences.
