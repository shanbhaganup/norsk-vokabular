## ta vekk

**English:** to remove; to take away

**Topics:** home, actions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å ta vekk | Det er lett å ta vekk flekken med såpe. | It is easy to remove the stain with soap. |
| Presens | tar vekk | Hun tar vekk koppene etter frokosten. | She removes the cups after breakfast. |
| Preteritum | tok vekk | Vi tok vekk den gamle hylla. | We removed the old shelf. |
| Perfektum partisipp | tatt vekk | Skiltet er tatt vekk fra veggen. | The sign has been taken off the wall. |
| Presens perfektum | har tatt vekk | Jeg har tatt vekk bildene fra bordet. | I have taken the pictures off the table. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde tatt vekk | De hadde tatt vekk snøen før bilen kom. | They had cleared away the snow before the car arrived. |
| Futurum | skal ta vekk / vil ta vekk | Jeg skal ta vekk eska i kveld. / Vaktmesteren vil ta vekk den ødelagte stolen. | I will remove the box tonight. / The caraaker will remove the broka chair. |

**Form status:** Simple forms checked against Bokmålsordboka; compound tenses and original examples are authored teaching material.

**Notes:** The phrase consists of ta plus the particle vekk; the verb ta inflects (tar, tok, tatt) and vekk remains unchanged. Bli-passive is possible. The dictionary lists takende as the present participle of ta, but takende vekk is not a natural expression; therefore the phrase-level present participle is marked inapplicable with a reason. The same applies to adjectival forms of the whole phrase: the whole phrase has no natural attributive participle form; prefer a relative clause or passive clause.
