## motsette

**English:** oppose

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å motsette seg | Det er viktig å motsette seg planen. | It is important to oppose the plan. |
| Presens | motsetter seg | Hun motsetter seg planen. | She opposes the plan. |
| Preteritum | motsatte | Hun motsatte seg planen i går. | She opposed the plan yesterday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | motsatt | Hun har motsatt seg planen. | She has opposed the plan. |
| Presens perfektum | har motsatt | Hun har motsatt seg planen. | She has opposed the plan. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde motsatt | Hun hadde motsatt seg planen før møtet. | She had opposed the plan before the meeting. |
| Futurum | skal motsette seg / vil motsette seg | Hun skal motsette seg planen i morgen. / Hun vil motsette seg planen i morgen. | She is going to oppose the plan tomorrow. / She will oppose the plan tomorrow. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha motsatt / vil ha motsatt | Innen fredag skal hun ha motsatt seg planen. / Innen fredag vil hun ha motsatt seg planen. | By Friday, she will have opposed the plan. / By Friday, she will have opposed the plan. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville motsette seg | Hun ville motsette seg planen hvis hun fikk muligheten. | She would oppose the plan if she had the chance. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha motsatt | Hun ville ha motsatt seg planen hvis hun hadde fått muligheten. | She would have opposed the plan if she had had the chance. |

| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt motsatt | Forslaget har blitt møtt med motstand. | The proposal has been met with opposition. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and paradigm checked against Bokmålsordboka; authored teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The source gloss is broad; the dictionary headword is principally used as motsette seg, “oppose/object to.” Examples show this reflexive construction. The source uses the base verb motsette; ordinary usage is motsette seg.
