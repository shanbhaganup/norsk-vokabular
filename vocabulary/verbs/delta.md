## delta

**English:** to participate; to take part

**Topics:** communication, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å delta | Hun ønsker å delta i møtet. | She wants to take part in the meeting. |
| Presens | deltar | Hun deltar i møtet. | She takes part in the meeting. |
| Preteritum | deltok | Hun deltok i møtet i går. | She took part in the meeting yesterday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | deltatt | Hun har deltatt i møtet. | She has taken part in the meeting. |
| Presens perfektum | har deltatt | Flere har deltatt i undersøkelsen. | Several people have taken part in the survey. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde deltatt | Han hadde deltatt før han flyttet. | He had participated before he moved. |
| Futurum | skal delta / vil delta | Jeg skal delta i debatten. / Hun vil delta i løpet senere. | I am going to take part in the debate. / She will take part in the race later. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha deltatt / vil ha deltatt | Innen fredag skal hun ha deltatt i tre møter. / Innen juni vil han ha deltatt i konkurransen. | By Friday she will have taken part in three meetings. / By June he will have taken part in the competition. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville delta | Jeg ville delta hvis jeg fikk fri. | I would participate if I got time off. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha deltatt | Hun ville ha deltatt hvis hun hadde vært frisk. | She would have participated if she had been well. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle delta | Han sa at han skulle delta i møtet. | He said he would take part in the meeting. |
| Imperativ | delta | Delta i samtalen! | Take part in the conversation! |

| S-passiv, infinitiv | deltas | Det må deltas i møtet. | People must take part in the meeting. |
| S-passiv, presens | deltas | Det deltas i konkurransen hvert år. | People take part in the competition every year. |

**Form status:** Headword and listed dictionary forms checked; compound constructions and examples are authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The ordinary sense is intransitive and takes *i* plus a noun. The source lists the impersonal s-passive *deltas*, which is formal and context-dependent. Ordinary *bli*-passive is inapplicable because the verb is intransitive. Perfect participle agreement as an adjective is not natural for this sense and is therefore inapplicable.
