**Form status:** The assigned sports sense is dictionary entry *misse* 2; its forms are *missa/misset*. Source forms *miste/mist* belong to homonymous *misse* 1 “lose” and are excluded from this sense.

**Notes:** Ordbøkene distinguishes *misse* 1 “lose” (forms *miste/mist*) from *misse* 2, from English *miss*, meaning fail to hit or score (forms *missa/misset*). The reference record merges both homonyms. The present entry covers only the assigned English sense “to miss” and cites both dictionary entries to document the split. S-passive is not established; bli-passive examples use a direct-object sports construction. Less common participial adjective forms *missede/missete* are retained from the second entry’s paradigm.
