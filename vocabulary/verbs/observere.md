## observere

**English:** to observe, watch, notice, or record

**Topics:** communication, science

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å observere | Forskerne skal observere fuglene over tid. | The researchers will observe the birds over time. |
| Presens | observerer | Vakten observerer trafikken fra vinduet. | The guard observes the traffic from the window. |
| Preteritum | observerte | Vi observerte et uvanlig lys på himmelen. | We observed an unusual light in the sky. |
| Perfektum partisipp | observert | Et sjeldent dyr er observert i området. | A rare animal has been observed in the area. |
| Presens perfektum | har observert | Legen har observert en bedring. | The doctor has observed an improvement. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde observert | De hadde observert mønsteret før forsøket startet. | They had observed the pattern before the experiment began. |
| Futurum | skal observere | Klassen skal observere hvordan isen smelter. | The class will observe how the ice melts. |
| Futurum | vil observere | Teknikeren vil observere maskinen under belastning. | The technician will observe the machine under load. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha observert | Innen fredag skal forskerne ha observert alle prøvene. | By Friday, the researchers will have observed all the samples. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha observert | Innen kvelden vil vaktene ha observert hele området. | By evening, the guards will have observed the entire area. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville observere | Jeg ville observere forsøket hvis jeg hadde tid. | I would observe the experiment if I had time. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha observert | De ville ha observert elgen hvis de hadde blitt lenger. | They would have observed the moose if they had stayed longer. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle observere | Hun sa at hun skulle observere trafikken fra brua. | She said she would observe the traffic from the bridge. |
| Imperativ | observer | Observer hvordan lyset endrer seg! | Observe how the light changes! |
| Presens partisipp | observerende | Et observerende kamera fulgte bevegelsene. | An observing camera tracked the movements. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | observeres | Dyr må observeres på avstand. | Animals must be observed from a distance. |
| S-passiv, presens | observeres | Trafikken observeres fra kontrollrommet. | The traffic is observed from the control room. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir observert | Fuglene blir observert uten å bli forstyrret. | The birds are observed without being disturbed. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble observert | En lysende gjenstand ble observert over byen. | A glowing object was observed over the city. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt observert | Endringen har blitt observert flere ganger. | The change has been observed several times. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | observert | En observert forskjell ble notert i rapporten. | An observed difference was noted in the report. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | observert | Et observert fenomen ble undersøkt videre. | An observed phenomenon was studied further. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | observerte | Den observerte endringen var liten. | The observed change was small. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | observerte | De observerte mønstrene ble sammenlignet. | The observed patterns were compared. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; transitive verb supports the listed passive and adjectival participle forms.

**Notes:** *Observere* means watch carefully, notice, or register something. It is typically transitive, and can take an object or a clause. Examples are original and illustrate observation rather than dictionary quotations.
