## komme

**English:** to come, arrive, reach; appear or occur

**Topics:** travel, movement, daily life

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the core paradigm and sense. The source lists *kommes*, but a natural current passive use was not verified. Past-participle adjective forms are listed but their ordinary attributive use remains uncertain.

**Notes:** *Komme* has many idiomatic constructions, including *komme fram*, *komme seg*, *komme over*, *komme til å* and *komme godt med*. The reference's inferred topic “travel” is only one context for the broad verb. The listed S-passive is not treated as established without a natural example. Examples are original.
