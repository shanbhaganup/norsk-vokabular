**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka lists three preterite forms and three participle forms. The paradigms are paired as *innrømma*, *innrømmet*, and *innrømte/innrømt*. All paired forms are shown without mixing endings.

**Notes:** *Innrømme* means to acknowledge or admit something; it can also mean to grant/allow someone something, as in *innrømme kunden rabatt*. The snapshot groups *ville/skulle* forms under conditional; they are not interchangeable. *Skulle innrømme* is shown as future viewed from the past, while conditional is illustrated with *ville*. Dictionary-listed adjectival forms *innrømmede/innrømmete* are less common; *innrømte* is the short form paired with *innrømt*. Examples are original.
