**Form status:** Dictionary forms; compound tenses and passive constructions are original examples based on the verified lemma and paradigm.

**Notes:** Fixed verb phrase built on *be*; the particle *om* stays with the verb. The present participle is not idiomatic for this phrase and is marked inapplicable. Passive is not used with this phrase. Examples are authored teaching examples. The source record gives the English gloss “ask for; request”; this entry illustrates the principal sense. The dictionary lists the basic inflection and participle forms; compound tenses are productive constructions.
