## gå

**English:** to go, walk, travel on foot; also to function, proceed, or happen in several common constructions

**Topics:** travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å gå | Vi pleier å gå til skolen sammen. | We usually walk to school together. |
| Presens | går | Bussen går fra holdeplassen klokka åtte. | The bus leaves the stop at eight. |
| Preteritum | gikk | De gikk langs elva etter middag. | They walked along the river after dinner. |
| Perfektum partisipp | gått | Vi har gått en lang tur i skogen. | We have walked a long way in the forest. |
| Presens perfektum | har gått | Hun har gått hjem allerede. | She has already walked home. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde gått | Toget hadde gått før vi kom til stasjonen. | The train had left before we reached the station. |
| Futurum, skal | skal gå | Jeg skal gå til butikken etter jobb. | I am going to walk to the store after work. |
| Futurum, vil | vil gå | Han vil gå videre når han har hvilt litt. | He will continue walking when he has rested a little. |
| Futurum perfektum, skal | skal ha gått | Innen kvelden skal vi ha gått hele ruta. | By evening we will have walked the entire route. |
| Futurum perfektum, vil | vil ha gått | Når vi kommer fram, vil de ha gått i flere timer. | By the time we arrive, they will have been walking for hours. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the headword and cited forms. Productive constructions are shown where they apply. Examples are original.

**Notes:** The source topic is inherited from the snapshot. Gå has many senses and fixed expressions; the examples cover movement, travel, and several common intransitive uses. Gås is listed in the snapshot as s-passive. Passive use of this intransitive verb is limited to impersonal constructions such as det gås tur. Bli-passive is inapplicable because the movement sense has no direct object. Gåtte is listed as definite and plural adjectival participle but is uncommon, so these attributive uses should be treated cautiously.
