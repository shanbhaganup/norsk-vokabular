## havne

**English:** to end up, arrive, or land in a place or situation

**Topics:** General / unassigned

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the headword and cited forms. Compound tenses and applicable passive constructions are shown. Examples are original.

**Notes:** The snapshot merges the “end up” sense with Bokmålsordboka homonym hamne/havne II (entry 22144), meaning “put out to pasture.” This entry covers havne I (22613) only and gives the accepted forms havna/havnet. The verb is intransitive, so passive constructions are inapplicable. The dictionary lists participle forms, but their attributive use is uncommon; examples are marked Unverified where a natural example could not be confirmed.
