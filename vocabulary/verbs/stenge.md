## stenge

**English:** to close

**Topics:** transport

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Presens | stenger | Bestyreren stenger butikken klokka åtte. | The manager closes the shop at eight. |
| Preteritum | stengte | Bestyreren stengte butikken klokka åtte. | The manager closed the shop at eight. |
