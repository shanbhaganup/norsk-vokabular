**Form status:** Simple forms and participial agreement checked against Bokmålsordboka. Compound tenses and passive constructions are shown in context. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** This is the verb vitne, distinct from the noun vitne and the separate verb bevitne. Past and perfect forms accept vitna and vitnet. Imperatives vitn and vitne are both listed. The passive is chiefly formal or impersonal in this sense; examples place it in a legal context.
