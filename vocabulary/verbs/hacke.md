## hacke

**English:** to hack into computer systems or networks; also to test their security or create solutions with IT

**Topics:** technology

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the headword and cited forms. Productive constructions are shown where they apply. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka gives both unauthorized access and legitimate security testing or IT development. The reflexive construction hacke seg inn means to break into a system. Past and participle have the alternatives hacka/hacket. Definite and plural adjectival forms also include hacka/hackede/hackete. Passive examples use transitive object hacking or testing.
