## presentere

**English:** to present, introduce

**Topics:** communication, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å presentere | Hun skal presentere et nytt forslag. | She is going to present a new proposal. |
| Presens | presenterer | Han presenterer resultatene for gruppa. | He presents the results to the group. |
| Preteritum | presenterte | De presenterte den nye løsningen i går. | They presented the new solution yesterday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | presentert | Rapporten ble presentert på møtet. | The report was presented at the meeting. |
| Presens perfektum | har presentert | Jeg har presentert planen for ledelsen. | I have presented the plan to management. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde presentert | Hun hadde presentert alle alternativene før avstemningen. | She had presented all the options before the vote. |
| Futurum | skal presentere | Vi skal presentere budsjettet på fredag. | We will present the budget on Friday. |
| Futurum | vil presentere | Teateret vil presentere to nye stykker i høst. | The theater will present two new plays this fall. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha presentert | Innen lunsj skal hun ha presentert forslaget. | By lunchtime, she will have presented the proposal. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha presentert | Innen helgen vil de ha presentert den ferdige prototypen. | By the weekend, they will have presented the finished prototype. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville presentere | Jeg ville presentere ideen hvis jeg fikk ordet. | I would present the idea if I were given the floor. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha presentert | Han ville ha presentert resultatene hvis dataene hadde vært klare. | He would have presented the results if the data had been ready. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle presentere | Hun sa at hun skulle presentere gjesten for kollegaene. | She said she would introduce the guest to her colleagues. |
| Imperativ | presenter | Presenter deg med navn og stilling. | Introduce yourself by name and position. |
| Presens partisipp | presenterende | Den presenterende forskeren viste fram modellen. | The researcher giving the presentation showed the model. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | presenteres | Resultatene må presenteres tydelig. | The results must be presented clearly. |
| S-passiv, presens | presenteres | Nye produkter presenteres på messen. | New products are presented at the trade fair. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir presentert | Forslaget blir presentert for styret i morgen. | The proposal will be presented to the board tomorrow. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble presentert | Prisen ble presentert under seremonien. | The prize was presented during the ceremony. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt presentert | Flere løsninger har blitt presentert for kommunen. | Several solutions have been presented to the municipality. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | presentert | En presentert kandidat svarte på spørsmål. | A candidate who had been introduced answered questions. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | presentert | Et presentert forslag ble diskutert. | A presented proposal was discussed. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | presenterte | Den presenterte rapporten inneholder nye tall. | The presented report contains new figures. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | presenterte | De presenterte alternativene er ulike. | The options presented are different. |
| Konstruksjon | presentere noen for noen | Hun presenterte den nye kollegaen for teamet. | She introduced the new colleague to the team. |
| Konstruksjon | presentere seg | Han presenterte seg før møtet begynte. | He introduced himself before the meeting began. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; compound constructions and examples are original.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes presenting or laying something out, presenting/performing a work, and introducing one person to another. *Presentere seg* is the fixed reflexive construction “introduce oneself”; it is shown as a construction, not a separate inflection. Examples are original teaching material.
