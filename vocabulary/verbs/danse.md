**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked in Bokmålsordboka. Compound tenses, conditional constructions and bli-passives are productive constructions illustrated with original examples.

**Notes:** Both dansa and danset are accepted past and perfect participles. The dictionary lists adjectival definite/plural alternatives dansa, dansede and dansete; attributive use is uncommon, so examples use explicitly choreographed or performed sequences. Passive examples use danse with an object such as vals or folkedans; intransitive dancing is not passivized.
