## sanse

**English:** to sense; to perceive

**Topics:** perception, health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å sanse | Det er vanskelig å sanse bevegelse i mørket. | It is difficult to sense movement in the dark. |
| Presens | sanser | Hun sanser lukten av røyk med en gang. | She senses the smell of smoke immediately. |
| Preteritum | sansa | Jeg sansa ikke at noen kom inn. | I did not sense that someone came in. |

**Form status:** Past and participle alternatives *sansa/sanset* are listed by Bokmålsordboka; adjectival participle use in this sense is Unverified.

**Notes:** The dictionary defines *sanse* as noticing or perceiving; *sanse seg* means to come to oneself. Several passive examples are formal/technical. Examples are original.
