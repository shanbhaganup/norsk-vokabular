## åke

**English:** to travel/go; to end up in a place or situation

**Topics:** travel, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Presens | åker | Varebilen åker rett i grøfta. | The van goes straight into the ditch. |
| Preteritum | åkte | Hun åkte inn i bystyret etter valget. | She ended up on the city council after the election. |
| Perfektum partisipp | åkt | Han har åkt dit flere ganger. | He has travelled there several times. |
