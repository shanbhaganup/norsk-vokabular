## mislike

**English:** to dislike; to have an aversion to

**Topics:** emotions

**Form status:** Core dictionary forms checked. The present-participle and s-passive forms are listed in the source paradigm but not verified as idiomatic for the ordinary dislike sense.

**Notes:** *Mislike* is transitive and means not to like something. The dictionary lists *mislikende* as a present participle, but a natural example in this sense was not established, so it remains Unverified. The source also lists *mislikes* as s-passive; the passive is normally expressed with *bli mislikt* or *være mislikt*, so the s-passive rows remain Unverified. Examples are original.
