## oppdra

**English:** to raise, bring up, or educate a child

**Topics:** family, education

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked, including the listed past and participle alternatives. The verb is transitive and permits passive constructions.

**Notes:** *Oppdra* means raise or educate, especially children. The dictionary lists preterite *oppdro/oppdrog* and past participle *oppdradd/oppdratt*. Adjectival participles *oppdradde/oppdratte* are retained as dictionary-listed alternatives; *oppdratt/oppdratte* are more familiar. Examples are original.
