## hyle

**English:** to howl, wail or shriek

**Topics:** sound, emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å hyle | Det er vondt å hyle så høyt. | It hurts to howl so loudly. |
| Presens | hyler | Ulven hyler mot månen. | The wolf howls at the moon. |
| Preteritum | hylte | Barnet hylte da det slo kneet. | The child wailed when it hit its knee. |
| Perfektum partisipp | hylt | Hun har hylt av latter. | She has howled with laughter. |
| Presens perfektum | har hylt | Alarmen har hylt i flere minutter. | The alarm has been shrieking for several minutes. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde hylt | Hunden hadde hylt før eieren kom hjem. | The dog had howled before its owner came home. |
| Futurum, plan | skal hyle | Jeg skal hyle hvis du skremmer meg igjen. | I will scream if you scare me again. |
| Futurum, prediksjon/vilje | vil hyle | Sirenen vil hyle når strømmen går. | The siren will wail when the power goes out. |
| Futurum perfektum, plan | skal ha hylt | Innen forestillingen er slutt, skal hun ha hylt av latter. | By the end of the show, she will have howled with laughter. |
| Futurum perfektum, prediksjon | vil ha hylt | Når vi kommer fram, vil alarmen ha hylt lenge. | By the time we arrive, the alarm will have been shrieking for a long time. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville hyle | Jeg ville hyle hvis jeg så en mus der. | I would scream if I saw a mouse there. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha hylt | Han ville ha hylt hvis han hadde sett edderkoppen. | He would have screamed if he had seen the spider. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle hyle | Hun sa at hun skulle hyle hvis laget vant. | She said she would scream if the team won. |
| Imperativ | hyl | Hyl hvis du trenger hjelp! | Shout if you need help! |
| Presens partisipp | hylende | En hylende alarm vekket hele huset. | A wailing alarm woke the whole house. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | hyles | Det kan hyles av glede på tribunen. | People can howl with joy in the stands. |
| S-passiv, presens | hyles | Det hyles fra publikum når laget scorer. | The crowd lets out shouts when the team scores. |
| Bli-passiv | — | — | Inapplicable to the ordinary intransitive sense “howl/shriek”; no direct object is promoted. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | hylt | En hylt alarm høres fortsatt i bygningen. | A shrieked alarm can still be heard in the building. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | hylt | Et hylt rop kom fra skogen. | A howled cry came from the forest. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | hylte | Det hylte ropet skremte hunden. | The shrieked cry frightened the dog. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | hylte | De hylte ropene kunne høres langt unna. | The howled cries could be heard from far away. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the paradigm and participle forms. The verb is normally intransitive; S-passive examples are impersonal. Bli-passive does not apply to the selected sense.

**Notes:** *Hyle* means to make a loud, piercing sound, including crying, screaming, laughter or an alarm. Adjectival past participles can sound unusual because the verb is normally intransitive; examples here are deliberately transparent and may be less idiomatic than relative clauses. Examples are original.
