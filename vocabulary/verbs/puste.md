**English:** to breathe

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; *pusta/pustet* alternatives are separately illustrated. Passive and resultative participle-adjective forms are inapplicable to the assigned intransitive sense.

**Notes:** *Puste* is intransitive in the main “breathe” sense and also describes something that lets air pass through, as with breathable fabric. The dictionary lists *puste ut* (“relax/rest”) and *puste på* (“pause and gather strength”) as fixed constructions. They are not inflected forms. Examples are original teaching material.
