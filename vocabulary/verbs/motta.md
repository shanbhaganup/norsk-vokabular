## motta

**English:** to receive; to welcome; to receive a response or review

**Topics:** communication, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å motta | Det er hyggelig å motta et personlig brev. | It is nice to receive a personal letter. |
| Presens | mottar | Hun mottar en pris for arbeidet sitt. | She receives an award for her work. |
| Preteritum | mottok | Vi mottok pakken i går. | We received the parcel yesterday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | mottatt | Søknaden er mottatt av kontoret. | The application has been received by the office. |
| Presens perfektum | har mottatt | Jeg har mottatt meldingen din. | I have received your message. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde mottatt | De hadde mottatt invitasjonen før fristen. | They had received the invitation before the deadline. |
| Futurum | skal motta | Vinneren skal motta prisen på fredag. | The winner will receive the award on Friday. |
| Futurum | vil motta | Alle deltakerne vil motta et kursbevis. | All participants will receive a course certificate. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha mottatt | Innen mandag skal vi ha mottatt alle svarene. | By Monday, we will have received all the replies. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha mottatt | Innen møtet vil hun ha mottatt resultatet. | By the meeting, she will have received the result. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville motta | Jeg ville motta pakken hjemme hvis det var mulig. | I would receive the parcel at home if that were possible. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha mottatt | De ville ha mottatt nyheten tidligere hvis brevet var sendt. | They would have received the news earlier if the letter had been sent. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle motta | Han sa at han skulle motta gjestene ved inngangen. | He said he would welcome the guests at the entrance. |
| Imperativ | motta | Motta gjestene med et smil! | Welcome the guests with a smile! |
| Presens partisipp | mottakende | Den mottakende parten bekreftet at dokumentene var kommet fram. | The receiving party confirmed that the documents had arrived. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | mottas | Søknaden må mottas innen fristen. | The application must be received by the deadline. |
| S-passiv, presens | mottas | Klager mottas skriftlig. | Complaints are received in writing. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir mottatt | Gjestene blir mottatt av vertskapet. | The guests are welcomed by the hosts. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble mottatt | Beskjeden ble mottatt med lettelse. | The message was received with relief. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt mottatt | Forslaget har blitt mottatt godt av publikum. | The proposal has been well received by the public. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | mottatt | En mottatt klage ble registrert samme dag. | A received complaint was registered that same day. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | mottatt | Et mottatt brev lå på pulten. | A received letter lay on the desk. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | mottatte | Den mottatte betalingen ble registrert. | The received payment was recorded. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | mottatte | De mottatte søknadene ble gjennomgått. | The received applications were reviewed. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; verb is transitive and permits the listed passive constructions.

**Notes:** The senses include receiving something, welcoming a person, and the reception or assessment of a work (*filmen ble godt mottatt*). The perfect participle agrees as *mottatt* indefinite singular and *mottatte* definite/plural. Examples are original teaching material.
