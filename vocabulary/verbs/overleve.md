## overleve

**English:** to survive, live through

**Topics:** health, nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å overleve | Det er mulig å overleve en lang vinter med gode forberedelser. | It is possible to survive a long winter with good preparation. |
| Presens | overlever | Planten overlever godt i skyggen. | The plant survives well in the shade. |
| Preteritum | overlevde | De overlevde ulykken uten alvorlige skader. | They survived the accident without serious injuries. |
| Perfektum partisipp | overlevd | Hun har overlevd mange vanskelige perioder. | She has survived many difficult periods. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; passive and resultative adjective forms are inapplicable to this intransitive verb.

**Notes:** In the meaning “survive,” *overleve* is intransitive and does not form passive constructions. The dictionary also records the fixed expression *overleve seg selv*, meaning to become outdated or obsolete; it is shown separately as a construction, not as an inflected form. *Overlevende* can be used substantivally as in the example. Examples are original teaching material.
