## irritere

**English:** to irritate; medically, to make a body part sore or red

**Topics:** emotions, health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å irritere | Støvet kan irritere øynene. | The dust can irritate the eyes. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the paradigm, the medical transitive sense and the sense “annoy/anger.” The reflexive expression and *bli irritert* are constructions, not inflectional forms.

**Notes:** The transitive pattern is *noe irriterer noen*. The dictionary's emotional sense commonly occurs as *irritere seg over noe* or *bli irritert på/over noe*. The *bli* examples express becoming irritated and can be read as adjectival/result-state constructions rather than eventive passives. Examples are original.
