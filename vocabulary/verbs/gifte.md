## gifte

**English:** to get married (gifte seg). To marry someone off (gifte bort).

**Topics:** family

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the simple forms and participle spellings. Compound tenses and applicable passives are productive constructions. Unverified marks usage for which no natural teaching example was confirmed. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Use gifte seg med noen for getting married to someone. The reflexive pronoun changes with the subject. The imperative example uses deg. The ordinary reflexive construction has no passive counterpart with the same meaning. The passive rows use the transitive expression gifte bort. Bokmålsordboka lists giftende, the indefinite forms gifta/giftet, and the definite and plural forms gifta/giftede/giftete. Natural teaching examples for these bare adjectival uses are unverified here. For marital status use the separate adjective [gift](../adjectives/gift.md). The skulle row shows future viewed from the past.
