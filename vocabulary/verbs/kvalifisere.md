**English:** qualify; make eligible

**Topics:** General / unassigned

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and forms checked; compound constructions and original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** The dictionary gives two related uses: making someone suitable for a task or profession, and earning the right to continue in a sports competition. This entry covers both as related applications of “become/make eligible”; examples distinguish the contexts. Reflexive *seg* appears when the subject qualifies itself. The past participle has the same masculine/feminine and neuter form, with *kvalifiserte* in definite singular and plural.
