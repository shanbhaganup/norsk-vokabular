## jobbe

**English:** to work; to labor or work on something

**Topics:** work, daily life

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the finite forms and *-a/-et* alternatives. S-passive is used impersonally. The verb is normally intransitive, so bli-passive does not apply to the selected sense; listed adjectival participle forms are marked unverified for natural use.

**Notes:** *Jobbe* means to have a job or work at a task, often with *med*. The dictionary also lists a specialized sense of short-term, risky speculation. The reference topic “work” was inferred from the sense because the source topic page was unavailable. Examples are original.
