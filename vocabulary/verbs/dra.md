## dra

**English:** to pull, draw; go or leave (context-dependent)

**Topics:** travel, movement

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å dra | Jeg liker å dra på hytta om høsten. | I like going to the cabin in autumn. |
| Presens | drar | Hun drar kofferten over gulvet. | She pulls the suitcase across the floor. |
| Preteritum, alternative 1 | dro | Han dro døra mot seg. | He pulled the door toward himself. |
| Preteritum, alternative 2 | drog | Hun drog tauet langs bakken. | She pulled the rope along the ground. |
| Perfektum partisipp, alternative 1 | dradd | Skuffen er dradd helt ut. | The drawer has been pulled all the way out. |
| Perfektum partisipp, alternative 2 | dratt | Skuffen er dratt helt ut. | The drawer has been pulled all the way out. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; compound tenses and passive examples are productive constructions.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists *dro* and *drog*, and participles *dradd* and *dratt*. The source entry gives a broad pull/drag sense; English “go/leave” is a common contextual sense, but should be checked against the separate dictionary sense if a sense-specific expansion is needed. *Dragende* is the dictionary present participle, but its example uses the established “enticing” descriptive sense and is sense-sensitive.
