## kunne

**English:** can; to be able to; may, depending on context

**Topics:** general, communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å kunne | Hun håper å kunne bli med på turen. | She hopes to be able to join the trip. |
| Presens | kan | Jeg kan svømme. | I can swim. |

**Form status:** Irregular dictionary forms checked; compound modal constructions are derived. S-passive and agreeing participial uses are not verified.

**Notes:** *Kunne* is a modal verb and normally takes an infinitive without *å* (for example, *kan svømme*). It has no ordinary imperative. A personal bli-passive does not apply to these ability/possibility senses. The reference lists *kunnes* as an s-passive, but the dictionary entry does not establish a usable construction; it is marked Unverified. The dictionary lists *kunnet* as a neuter participle; the neuter example shows a restrictive participial use and should be reviewed for naturalness. Do not mechanically infer masculine/feminine or definite/plural forms. *Skulle kunne* here is future viewed from a past context.
