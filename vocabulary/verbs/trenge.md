## trenge

**English:** to need; to press or force one’s way; to penetrate

**Topics:** daily life, movement

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Preteritum | trengte | Vi trengte mer tid til å bli ferdige. | We needed more time to finish. |

**Form status:** Dictionary paradigms checked; compound constructions and examples are authored.

**Notes:** *Trengs* is the natural impersonal S-passive for “is needed.” Personal bli-passive does not fit this sense; adjectival use of *trengt* in the “need” sense remains Unverified. *Trengende* is used for “in need.”
