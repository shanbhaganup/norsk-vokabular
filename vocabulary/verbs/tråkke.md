## tråkke

**English:** to step; to tread; to walk around; to trample or flatten with the feet

**Topics:** movement, nature

**Form status:** Simple forms and participle alternatives checked against Bokmålsordboka; compound tenses and bli-passive are productive constructions. Examples use stepping, walking, and trampling senses documented in the dictionary.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka gives the accepted headwords *tråkke* and *trakke* with their respective paradigms. This entry follows the source lemma *tråkke* and does not mix in forms of *trakke*. The dictionary lists *tråkka/tråkket* as past and perfect participle alternatives; adjectival definite and plural forms additionally include *tråkkede* and *tråkkete*. The verb has transitive uses such as *tråkke ned gresset*, permitting the illustrated bli-passives.
