## kunne ikke

**English:** not be able to; cannot

**Topics:** General / unassigned

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked for *kunne*; negative phrase syntax reviewed. Productive future and conditional constructions are derived.

**Notes:** The reference source gives phrase forms such as *skal kunne ikke* and *kunnet ikke*. Standard Norwegian places *ikke* before the infinitive or participle in these constructions (*skal ikke kunne*, *ikke kunnet*); examples correct that source-ordering error. The lemma is the negative modal phrase, not an independent inflectional paradigm. Passive, imperative and adjectival-participle rows are inapplicable. The English sense is inability, not permission or possibility.
