**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the *innlede* paradigm, including the accepted *-a/-et* past and participle alternatives and adjective agreement. The same article also lists the distinct parallel form *innleie* with a different paradigm.

**Notes:** *Innlede* means to begin or set something in motion, including opening a discussion, event or lecture. The dictionary also lists *innleie* as a parallel spelling/form, conjugated *innleier, innleide, innleid*; that full alternative paradigm is outside this requested lemma entry. The adjective *innledende* means introductory/opening. The *-ede/-ete* attributive participles are dictionary-listed; *-ede* is the more familiar written form. Examples are original.
