## lønne

**English:** to reward or compensate; to pay someone; reflexively, to pay off or be worthwhile

**Topics:** money, work

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; *lønna, lønnet,* and *lønte* are listed past forms; *lønna, lønnet,* and *lønt* are participle alternatives.

**Notes:** *Lønne noen* means pay or reward someone; *lønne seg* means be profitable or worthwhile. These are distinct constructions, not inflected forms. The participle alternatives *lønt* and definite/plural *lønte* are less common than *lønnet/lønnede*. Some adjectival alternatives are rare; they are retained as dictionary-listed forms. Examples are original.
