## sladre

**English:** to gossip

**Topics:** language learning

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å sladre | Jeg liker å sladre. | I like to gossip. |
| Presens | sladrer | Hun sladrer aldri om kollegene. | She never gossips about her colleagues.] |
| Preteritum | sladra | Han sladra om naboen. | She never gossips about her colleagues.] |
| Preteritum | sladret | Han sladret om naboen. | He gossiped about the neighbor.] |
| Perfektum partisipp | sladra | Arbeidet er sladra. | The work is gossiped. |
| Perfektum partisipp | sladret | Arbeidet er sladret. | The work is gossiped. |
| Presens perfektum | har sladra / sladret | De har sladra / sladret om saken. | She never gossips about her colleagues.] |
