## skylde

**English:** to owe

**Topics:** language learning

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å skylde | Jeg liker å skylde. | I like to owe. |
| Presens | skylder | Jeg skylder naboen en tjeneste. | I owe my neighbor a favor.] |
| Preteritum | skyldte | Han skyldte meg penger. | I owe my neighbor a favor.] |
| Perfektum partisipp | skyldt | Arbeidet er skyldt. | The work is oweed. |
| Presens perfektum | har skyldt | Vi har skyldt dem et svar lenge. | I owe my neighbor a favor.] |
