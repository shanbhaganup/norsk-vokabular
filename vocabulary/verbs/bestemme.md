# bestemme

**English:** to decide; to determine

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å bestemme | Vi må bestemme tidspunktet snart. | We must decide the time soon. |
| Presens | bestemmer | Hun bestemmer tidspunktet. | She decides the time. |
| Preteritum | bestemte | De bestemte tidspunktet i går. | They decided the time yesterday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | bestemt | Tidspunktet er bestemt. | The time has been decided. |
| Presens perfektum | har bestemt | Vi har bestemt tidspunktet. | We have decided the time. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde bestemt | Vi hadde bestemt tidspunktet før lokalet ble stengt. | We had decided the time before the venue closed. |

| Futurum perfektum | skal ha bestemt | Innen fredag skal vi ha bestemt tidspunktet. | By Friday, we will have decided the time. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha bestemt | Innen fredag vil de ha bestemt tidspunktet. | By Friday, they will have decided the time. |

**Form status:** Dictionary simple forms checked; compound constructions are productive teaching examples.

**Notes:** The cited entry covers deciding/determining and the phrase bestemme seg. This entry models the transitive decision sense and avoids conflating beslutte and bestemme as identical in every context. Examples are original teaching sentences. The transitive passive examples use ordinary agentless contexts; S-passive is most natural in formal or rule-like contexts.
