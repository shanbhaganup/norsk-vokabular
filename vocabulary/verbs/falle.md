## falle

**English:** to fall; to move downward; also to decrease or be defeated, depending on context

**Topics:** movement, weather, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Form status:** Core forms and participle forms checked in Bokmålsordboka; compound tenses and conditional examples are productive constructions.

**Notes:** The reference supplies a broad, one-line gloss, while the dictionary distinguishes several senses: downward movement, decrease, defeat, occurrence/assignment, and others. This entry centers on physical falling and decreasing. Falle is normally intransitive, so passive forms are inapplicable: there is no object that can become the passive subject. Use Unverified if a particular transitive or lexicalized use is intended. Adjectival falt uses the listed inflection falte in definite singular and plural. Examples are authored teaching examples.
