## forsvare

**English:** to defend, support or justify someone, a position or a title; to defend militarily

**Topics:** society, communication, law, sports

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å forsvare | Hun vil forsvare standpunktet sitt. | She wants to defend her position. |
| Presens | forsvarer | Advokaten forsvarer klienten i retten. | The lawyer defends the client in court. |
| Preteritum | forsvarte | De forsvarte byen under angrepet. | They defended the town during the attack. |
| Perfektum partisipp | forsvart | Landet ble forsvart av lokale styrker. | The country was defended by local forces. |
| Presens perfektum | har forsvart | Hun har forsvart doktoravhandlingen sin. | She has defended her doctoral thesis. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde forsvart | Han hadde forsvart laget før treneren kom. | He had defended the team before the coach arrived. |
| Futurum | skal forsvare / vil forsvare | Jeg skal forsvare forslaget i møtet. / Jeg tror laget vil forsvare tittelen. | I am going to defend the proposal at the meeting. / I think the team will defend the title. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha forsvart / vil ha forsvart | Innen sommeren skal hun ha forsvart avhandlingen. / Innen finalen vil laget ha forsvart tittelen i ett år. | By summer she will have defended her thesis. / By the final, the team will have held the title for a year. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville forsvare | Jeg ville forsvare henne hvis hun ba om hjelp. | I would defend her if she asked for help. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha forsvart | De ville ha forsvart byen hvis de hadde hatt nok soldater. | They would have defended the town if they had had enough soldiers. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle forsvare | Hun sa at hun skulle forsvare forslaget dagen etter. | She said she would defend the proposal the next day. |
| Imperativ | forsvar | Forsvar synspunktet ditt med gode argumenter! | Defend your point of view with good arguments! |
| Presens partisipp | forsvarende | Den forsvarende mesteren vant finalen. | The defending champion won the final. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | forsvares | Prinsippet må forsvares. | The principle must be defended. |
| S-passiv, presens | forsvares | Forslaget forsvares av flere medlemmer. | The proposal is defended by several members. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir forsvart | Landet blir forsvart av hæren. | The country is being defended by the army. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble forsvart | Hun ble forsvart av advokaten. | She was defended by the lawyer. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt forsvart | Standpunktet har blitt forsvart offentlig. | The position has been defended publicly. |

**Form status:** Core forms verified in Bokmålsordboka; compound tenses and passive examples are original productive constructions.

**Notes:** Sense covers taking someone's side, justifying a position, military defense, and retaining a title. The reference snapshot's *skulle* conditional alternatives are omitted: *skulle* is useful for future-in-the-past, but does not simply replace *ville* in a conditional. Source spelling agrees with the dictionary; examples are authored teaching sentences.
