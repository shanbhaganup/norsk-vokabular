## dyrke

**English:** to cultivate; to grow crops; to practise or devote oneself to; to worship or idolize

**Topics:** nature, food, hobbies, culture

**Form status:** Inflection and senses checked against Bokmålsordboka. All cited alternatives are represented in examples.

**Notes:** Dyrke covers growing or working land, growing crops, worshipping or idolizing, and cultivating a hobby or relationship. The examples focus mostly on agriculture, with a couple of figurative uses. Dyrka and dyrket are accepted past and participle forms; definite/plural adjectival participles also allow dyrkede and dyrkete. Passive is natural for the transitive cultivation sense.
