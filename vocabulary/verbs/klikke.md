**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the finite paradigm and alternatives. The verb is both intransitive (make a click, malfunction) and used with *på* for mouse clicking. The S-passive is impersonal; attributive participles are unverified.

**Notes:** *Klikke* can mean make a clicking sound, fail (including an event or plan), or click a mouse button. The S-passive example is a general impersonal instruction. Examples are original.
