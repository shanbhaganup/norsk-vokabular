**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; compound constructions and original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka accepts *kurva* and *kurvet* as past and perfect participle forms. For adjectival definite singular and plural, it lists *kurva*, *kurvede* and *kurvete*. The taught verb sense is “bend/curve” (fold or bend something), as in the dictionary entry; it is distinct from the homographic noun *kurve*.
