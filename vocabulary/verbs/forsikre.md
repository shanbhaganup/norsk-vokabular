## forsikre

**English:** to assure, assure someone that something is true; to insure

**Topics:** communication, finance

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

**Form status:** Dictionary-listed forms and alternatives are retained; compound tenses and passive constructions are productive examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary distinguishes “state as certain/guarantee” from “take out insurance on.” *Forsikre seg om* (“make sure”) is a fixed reflexive construction and is not an inflected form; it is not expanded into a separate sense entry here. Both imperative forms and accepted participial alternatives from the source paradigm are illustrated. English glosses have been reviewed against dictionary senses.
