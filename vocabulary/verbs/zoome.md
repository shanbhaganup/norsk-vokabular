## zoome

**English:** to zoom (adjust a camera or screen view)

**Topics:** technology, media

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å zoome | Det er lett å zoome på skjermen med to fingre. | It is easy to zoom on the screen with two fingers. |
