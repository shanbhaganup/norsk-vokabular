## kaste

**English:** to throw; toss, put, move abruptly, or cast

**Topics:** movement, everyday actions

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the *-a/-et* forms, participle agreement and many senses. Transitive uses support both passive constructions.

**Notes:** *Kaste* has numerous literal and figurative senses, including throw, put aside/away, move abruptly, cast a shadow, and *kaste opp* “vomit.” Reflexive *kaste seg* and phrasal expressions are constructions, not inflections. The definite/plural *-ede/-ete* participles are dictionary-listed; *-ede* is more common. Examples are original.
