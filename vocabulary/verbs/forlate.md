## forlate

**English:** to leave; to depart from or abandon

**Topics:** travel, family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å forlate | Vi måtte forlate hotellet tidlig. | We had to leave the hotel early. |
| Presens | forlater | Bussen forlater stasjonen klokka åtte. | The bus leaves the station at eight. |
| Preteritum | forlot | Hun forlot byen etter studiene. | She left the city after her studies. |
| Perfektum partisipp | forlatt | Familien følte seg forlatt. | The family felt abandoned. |
| Presens perfektum | har forlatt | Gjestene har forlatt bygningen. | The guests have left the building. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde forlatt | Vi hadde forlatt stranden før regnet kom. | We had left the beach before the rain came. |
| Futurum | skal forlate / vil forlate | Vi skal forlate hotellet i morgen. / Hun vil forlate stillingen ved årets slutt. | We are going to leave the hotel tomorrow. / She will leave the position at the end of the year. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha forlatt / vil ha forlatt | Innen klokka ni skal vi ha forlatt byen. / Innen neste måned vil han ha forlatt landet. | By nine o'clock, we will have left the city. / By next month, he will have left the country. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville forlate | Jeg ville forlate stedet hvis situasjonen ble farlig. | I would leave the place if the situation became dangerous. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha forlatt | De ville ha forlatt huset hvis brannen hadde spredd seg. | They would have left the house if the fire had spread. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle forlate | Hun sa at hun skulle forlate landet neste uke. | She said that she would leave the country the following week. |
| Imperativ | forlat | Forlat rommet rolig! | Leave the room calmly! |
| Presens partisipp | forlatende | Det forlatende skipet forsvant i tåka. | The departing ship disappeared into the fog. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | forlates | Huset må forlates før klokka seks. | The house must be vacated before six o'clock. |
| S-passiv, presens | forlates | Bygningen forlates gjennom hovedinngangen. | The building is exited through the main entrance. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir forlatt | Leiren blir forlatt når vinteren kommer. | The camp is abandoned when winter comes. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble forlatt | Landsbyen ble forlatt etter flommen. | The village was abandoned after the flood. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt forlatt | Skipet har blitt forlatt av mannskapet. | The ship has been abandoned by its crew. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | forlatt | En forlatt katt satt ved døra. | An abandoned cat sat by the door. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | forlatt | Et forlatt hus sto ved veien. | An abandoned house stood by the road. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | forlatte | Den forlatte stasjonen var stille. | The abandoned station was quiet. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | forlatte | De forlatte bygningene skal rives. | The abandoned buildings are to be demolished. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene verifies the headword, core paradigm, participle agreement and present participle. Productive tense and passive constructions are illustrated in original examples.

**Notes:** The entry covers “depart from” and “abandon.” The dictionary also lists “forgive” as an older biblical sense; this is not the selected sense here. Present participle *forlatende* is grammatically formed but uncommon; the example uses the literal sense “departing.”
