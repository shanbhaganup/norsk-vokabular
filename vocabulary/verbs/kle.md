## kle

**English:** to dress oneself or another; clothe; cover; suit

**Topics:** clothing, daily life

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the paradigm and senses. The present participle *kleende* is dictionary-listed but left unverified for natural modern usage. Passive examples use transitive dressing or covering.

**Notes:** *Kle seg* means dress oneself; *kle noen* means dress another; *kle noe med* means cover it with something. *Kle* can also mean that clothing or behavior suits someone. The dictionary lists a related headword *klede* with its own forms; this entry follows the assigned lemma *kle*. Examples are original.
