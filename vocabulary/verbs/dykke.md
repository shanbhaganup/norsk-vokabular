## dykke

**English:** to dive; to submerge oneself, especially to do something underwater

**Topics:** sport, water, nature

**Form status:** Forms and accepted Bokmål alternatives checked against Ordbøkene. Examples for adjectival participle alternatives follow their stated forms.

**Notes:** Dykka and dykket are accepted past and perfect participle variants. The definite and plural participles also include dykkede and dykkete. Dykke is normally intransitive in the sense “go underwater”; passive forms are therefore marked inapplicable here. The adjectival participle means submerged and is less routine than the verbal use. A diving person is more naturally described with dykkende.
