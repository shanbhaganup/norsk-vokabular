## peke

**English:** to point, indicate

**Topics:** communication, directions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å peke | Det er uhøflig å peke på folk. | It is impolite to point at people. |
| Presens | peker | Barnet peker på månen. | The child points at the moon. |
| Preteritum | pekte | Hun pekte mot utgangen. | She pointed toward the exit. |
| Perfektum partisipp | pekt | Han har pekt på flere mulige løsninger. | He has pointed out several possible solutions. |
| Presens perfektum | har pekt | Jeg har pekt på feilen i rapporten. | I have pointed out the error in the report. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde pekt | Læreren hadde pekt mot kartet før timen sluttet. | The teacher had pointed toward the map before class ended. |
| Futurum | skal peke | Guiden skal peke ut stien ved veikrysset. | The guide will point out the trail at the junction. |
| Futurum | vil peke | Pilen vil peke mot nord når den står riktig. | The arrow will point north when it is positioned correctly. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha pekt | Innen møtet er over, skal hun ha pekt ut de viktigste avsnittene. | By the end of the meeting, she will have pointed out the most important sections. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha pekt | Innen kvelden vil kartet ha pekt oss mot riktig sti. | By evening, the map will have pointed us toward the right trail. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville peke | Jeg ville peke på kartet hvis jeg visste hvor vi var. | I would point at the map if I knew where we were. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; compound tense and passive constructions are shown in context.

**Notes:** *Peke* can be used literally with a direction or target, and figuratively in *peke på* (“draw attention to”) and *peke ut* (“select/designate”). These are constructions, not inflected forms. The dictionary lists agreeing forms of *pekt*, but attributive/resultative adjective use was not established for the assigned senses, so those rows are inapplicable here. Examples are original.
