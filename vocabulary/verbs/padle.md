**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; *padla/padlet* past and participle alternatives and *padl/padle* imperatives are separately illustrated.

**Notes:** *Padle* can be used without an object or transitively with a canoe, kayak, or other craft. The Bokmålsordboka lists three related uses: propel a canoe/kayak, use arms or another object like a paddle, and use the skating technique in cross-country skiing. This entry's examples focus on paddling a craft. The source groups *ville/skulle* as conditional alternatives; here *ville* is shown as conditional and *skulle* as future viewed from the past, following the project's grammar convention. Examples are original.
