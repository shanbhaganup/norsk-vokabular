## nyte

**English:** to enjoy; to take pleasure in; to benefit from

**Topics:** emotions, food, nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å nyte | Vi vil nyte utsikten fra toppen. | We want to enjoy the view from the summit. |
| Presens | nyter | Hun nyter en kopp te i hagen. | She enjoys a cup of tea in the garden. |
| Preteritum | nøt | De nøt stillheten etter turen. | They enjoyed the quiet after the hike. |
| Preteritum | nøyt | Han nøyt måltidet i ro og mak. | He enjoyed the meal at his leisure. |
| Perfektum partisipp | nytt | Vi har nytt godt av den nye ordningen. | We have benefited from the new arrangement. |

| Preteritum perfektum | hadde nytt | De hadde nytt oppholdet før været snudde. | They had enjoyed the stay before the weather changed. |
| Futurum | skal nyte | Jeg skal nyte fridagen uten planer. | I will enjoy the day off without plans. |
| Futurum | vil nyte | Publikum vil nyte konserten. | The audience will enjoy the concert. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked, including both past forms *nøt/nøyt*. Adjectival perfect-participle agreement is dictionary-listed but not verified as natural in this sense.

**Notes:** *Nyte* is transitive and means take pleasure in something, consume something with pleasure, or benefit from something (*nyte godt av*). S-passive forms are listed and illustrated. A natural bli-passive construction was not confirmed, so those rows remain Unverified. The dictionary lists *nytt/nytte* as adjectival forms, but such attributive uses are unclear and remain Unverified. Examples are original.
