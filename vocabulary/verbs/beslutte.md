**Form status:** Dictionary simple forms checked; compound constructions are productive teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary gives beslutta and besluttet as accepted past and perfect participle alternatives. Bestemme shares the broad sense “decide”; beslutte commonly describes making or reaching a decision, including institutional decisions. Examples are original teaching sentences. The transitive passive examples use ordinary agentless contexts; S-passive is most natural in formal or rule-like contexts.
