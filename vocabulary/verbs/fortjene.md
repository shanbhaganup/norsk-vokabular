## fortjene

**English:** to deserve, merit

**Topics:** society, work, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å fortjene | Alle bør få den respekten de fortjener. | Everyone should receive the respect they deserve. |
| Presens | fortjener | Hun fortjener en pause etter arbeidet. | She deserves a break after the work. |
| Preteritum | fortjente | Laget fortjente å vinne. | The team deserved to win. |
| Perfektum partisipp | fortjent | Han er en fortjent vinner. | He is a deserving winner. |
| Presens perfektum | har fortjent | Du har fortjent litt fri. | You have earned some time off. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde fortjent | Hun hadde fortjent bedre behandling. | She had deserved better treatment. |
| Futurum | skal fortjene / vil fortjene | Han skal fortjene tilliten sin. / Hun vil fortjene anerkjennelse for arbeidet. | He is to earn their trust. / She will deserve recognition for her work. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha fortjent / vil ha fortjent | Innen da skal hun ha fortjent alles tillit. / Etter sesongen vil laget ha fortjent ros. | By then she will have earned everyone's trust. / After the season the team will have deserved praise. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville fortjene | Jeg ville fortjene kritikk hvis jeg løy. | I would deserve criticism if I lied. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha fortjent | De ville ha fortjent seieren hvis de hadde spilt bedre. | They would have deserved the victory if they had played better. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle fortjene | Hun mente at innsatsen skulle fortjene en belønning. | She thought the effort would merit a reward. |
| Imperativ | fortjen | Fortjen tilliten ved å holde løftet ditt! | Deserve the trust by keeping your promise! |
| Presens partisipp | fortjenende | Den fortjenende vinneren takket laget sitt. | The deserving winner thanked her team. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | fortjenes | Anerkjennelsen må fortjenes. | The recognition must be earned. |
| S-passiv, presens | fortjenes | Respekt fortjenes gjennom handling. | Respect is earned through action. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir fortjent | Prisen blir fortjent gjennom hardt arbeid. | The prize is earned through hard work. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble fortjent | Tilliten ble fortjent over tid. | The trust was earned over time. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt fortjent | Anerkjennelsen har blitt fortjent gjennom mange års innsats. | The recognition has been earned through many years of effort. |

**Form status:** Core forms verified in Bokmålsordboka; compound tenses are productive constructions. Passive forms are grammatically possible but less common than active clauses.

**Notes:** Takes the thing deserved as object and may also take an infinitive complement (*fortjene å vinne*). S-passive and *bli* passive are possible, especially when the focus is on merit being earned, but can sound formal or marked; active constructions are more usual. The source snapshot's *skulle* conditional alternative is not treated as interchangeable with *ville*. The participle *fortjent* is shown in a common adjective use. Examples are authored teaching sentences.
