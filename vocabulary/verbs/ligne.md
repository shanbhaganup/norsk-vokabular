**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked for *ligne*. The dictionary also lists the spelling *likne* with its own paradigm; this entry covers only the assigned spelling.

**Notes:** The main sense “resemble” is usually intransitive with *på* (*ligne på noen/noe*) or reciprocal (*ligne hverandre*). The dictionary also includes “compare” and the specialized tax sense “assess/impose tax,” which permits passive forms. Passive examples and adjectival participles here use that tax-assessment sense, not the ordinary resemblance sense. *Ligna/lignet* are accepted past and participle forms; *lignede/lignete* are definite/plural alternatives. Examples are original teaching sentences.
