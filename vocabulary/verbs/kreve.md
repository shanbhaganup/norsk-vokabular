**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies both preterite and participle alternatives. The *krevde* preterite pairs with *krevd* as participle; *krevet* is also listed for both. The verb is transitive and supports passive constructions.

**Notes:** *Kreve* means demand/claim, require or cause a major loss, as in *ulykken krevde mange menneskeliv*. The phrase *kreve inn* means collect a payment. The *krevede/krevete* adjectival alternatives are dictionary-listed but rare; *krevde* is the usual written definite/plural form. Examples are original.
