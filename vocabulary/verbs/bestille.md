# bestille

**English:** to order; to book

**Topics:** food, travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å bestille | Jeg vil bestille et bord for fire. | I want to book a table for four. |
| Presens | bestiller | Hun bestiller billetter på nettet. | She orders tickets online. |
| Preteritum | bestilte | Vi bestilte maten i går. | We ordered the food yesterday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | bestilt | Bordet er bestilt for klokken sju. | The table is booked for seven o’clock. |
| Presens perfektum | har bestilt | De har bestilt togbilletter. | They have ordered train tickets. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde bestilt | Jeg hadde bestilt rom før prisene steg. | I had booked a room before prices rose. |
| Futurum | skal bestille | Vi skal bestille dessert senere. | We are going to order dessert later. |
| Futurum | vil bestille | Han vil bestille en ny time. | He will book a new appointment. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha bestilt | Innen i morgen skal hun ha bestilt billettene. | By tomorrow, she will have booked the tickets. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha bestilt | Innen fristen vil vi ha bestilt alt utstyret. | By the deadline, we will have ordered all the equipment. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville bestille | Jeg ville bestille et større rom hvis det var ledig. | I would book a larger room if it were available. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha bestilt | De ville ha bestilt bord hvis restauranten hadde vært åpen. | They would have booked a table if the restaurant had been open. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle bestille | Hun sa at hun skulle bestille billettene samme kveld. | She said she would book the tickets that evening. |
| Imperativ | bestill | Bestill maten før forestillingen begynner! | Order the food before the show starts! |
| Presens partisipp | bestillende | Den bestillende kunden oppga etternavnet sitt. | The ordering customer gave their surname. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | bestilles | Billettene må bestilles på forhånd. | The tickets must be booked in advance. |
| S-passiv, presens | bestilles | Maten bestilles ved disken. | The food is ordered at the counter. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir bestilt | Utstyret blir bestilt fra leverandøren. | The equipment is ordered from the supplier. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble bestilt | Bordet ble bestilt for seks personer. | The table was booked for six people. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt bestilt | Alle billettene har blitt bestilt. | All the tickets have been booked. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | bestilt | En bestilt vare kan hentes i morgen. | An ordered item can be collected tomorrow. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | bestilt | Et bestilt måltid kan endres før servering. | An ordered meal can be changed before it is served. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | bestilte | Den bestilte varen ligger på lageret. | The ordered item is in the warehouse. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | bestilte | De bestilte varene er klare for henting. | The ordered items are ready for collection. |

**Form status:** Dictionary simple forms checked; compound constructions are productive teaching examples.

**Notes:** The entry covers ordering or booking. The dictionary also has a less common sense “do / occupy oneself with”; that sense is not the focus here. Examples are original teaching sentences. The transitive passive examples use ordinary agentless contexts; S-passive is most natural in formal or rule-like contexts.
