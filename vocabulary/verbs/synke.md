## synke

**English:** to sink; to descend; to fall

**Topics:** nature, movement

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å synke | Steinen begynte å synke i vannet. | The stone began to sink in the water. |
| Presens | synker | Båten synker sakte. | The boat is sinking slowly. |
| Preteritum | sank | Temperaturen sank i løpet av natten. | The temperature fell during the night. |
| Perfektum partisipp | sunket | Skipet er sunket nær kysten. | The ship has sunk near the coast. |
| Presens perfektum | har sunket | Vannstanden har sunket siden i går. | The water level has fallen since yesterday. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde sunket | Da redningsmannskapet kom, hadde båten sunket. | By the time the rescue crew arrived, the boat had sunk. |
| Futurum | skal synke / vil synke | Nivået skal synke når ventilen åpnes. / Temperaturen vil synke etter solnedgang. | The level is going to drop when the valve is opened. / The temperature will fall after sunset. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha sunket / vil ha sunket | Innen morgenen skal vannet ha sunket. / Innen uka er omme, vil prisene ha sunket. | By morning, the water will have receded. / By the ad of the week, prices will have fallen. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville synke | Båten ville synke hvis skroget fikk en sprekk. | The boat would sink if the hull developed a crack. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha sunket | Vannstanden ville ha sunket mer hvis det ikke hadde regnet. | The water level would have fallen further if it had not rained. |

**Form status:** Simple forms checked against Bokmålsordboka; compound tenses and original examples are authored teaching material.

**Notes:** This is intransitive in the senses “sink/descend/fall,” so passive constructions do not apply. It can also be transitive in the dictionary sense “digest” (synke maten); that distinct sense is outside the reference gloss “sink.” The dictionary lists sunken/sunket in indefinite masculine/feminine agreement, sunket in neuter, and sunkne in definite/plural.
