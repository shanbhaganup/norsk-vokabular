**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; *ramla/ramlet* alternatives and *raml/ramle* imperatives are separately illustrated.

**Notes:** *Ramle* is intransitive. The dictionary meanings include making a loud hollow noise, falling/tumbling, and coming out suddenly or unexpectedly (for example, words “ramling out”). Passive and resultative adjective forms do not apply to these intransitive uses. The source groups *ville/skulle* as conditional; *skulle ramle* is shown as future viewed from the past, following project grammar conventions. Examples are original.
