## hente

**English:** to fetch, go and bring back; also to collect, obtain, or retrieve

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å hente | Jeg må hente pakken på postkontoret. | I have to pick up the package at the post office. |
| Presens | henter | Hun henter barna etter skolen. | She picks up the children after school. |
| Preteritum | henta | Vi henta ved fra skjulet. | We fetched firewood from the shed. |
| Preteritum | hentet | Han hentet meg på stasjonen. | He picked me up at the station. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the headword and cited forms. Compound tenses and applicable passive constructions are shown. Examples are original.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes fetching a person or thing, obtaining something, and retrieving material from a source. Hente is transitive in the examples, so both s-passive and bli-passive are applicable. Past and participle have the alternatives henta/hentet. Definite and plural adjective forms also include hentede/hentete.
