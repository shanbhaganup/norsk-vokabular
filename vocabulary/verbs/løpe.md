## løpe

**English:** to run; to race or run errands; to flow or extend

**Topics:** sport, movement

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked, including both perfect-participle forms *løpet/løpt*. Passive and agreeing past-participle adjective uses are not verified as natural for the intransitive running sense.

**Notes:** *Løpe* is primarily intransitive. It also appears in expressions such as *løpe ærend*, *løpe fra ansvaret*, and *fristen løper ut*. In the idiom *løpe ut*, *løpet* is the listed participle alternative, while *løpt* is also listed for the verb generally. S-passive *løpes* in the source is not established as natural for ordinary running; it remains unverified. Adjectival participle cells are unverified rather than mechanically generated. Examples are original.
