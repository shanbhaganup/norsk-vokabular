## angripe

**English:** to attack

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å angripe | Gaupa gjorde seg klar til å angripe byttet. | The lynx got ready to attack its prey. |
| Presens | angriper | Måkene angriper kråka når den kommer nær reiret. | The gulls attack the crow when it comes near the nest. |
| Preteritum | angreip | Reven angreip en høne i skumringen. | The fox attacked a hen at dusk. |
| Preteritum | angrep | En løs hund angrep sauene. | A loose dog attacked the sheep. |
| Perfektum partisipp | angrepet | Bonden fant ei høne som var angrepet av en rev. | The farmer found a hen that had been attacked by a fox. |
| Presens perfektum | har angrepet | En hund har angrepet to sauer. | A dog has attacked two sheep. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde angrepet | Reven hadde angrepet hønene før bonden kom ut. | The fox had attacked the hens before the farmer came outside. |
| Futurum | skal angripe | I neste scene skal katten angripe musa. | In the next scene, the cat is going to attack the mouse. |
| Futurum | vil angripe | Jeg tror måkene vil angripe kråka hvis den kommer nærmere. | I think the gulls will attack the crow if it comes closer. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha angrepet | Ifølge manuset skal reven ha angrepet høna før bonden kommer ut. | According to the script, the fox is supposed to have attacked the hen before the farmer comes outside. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha angrepet | I løpet av natta vil reven ha angrepet hønene hvis luka står åpen. | By the end of the night, the fox will have attacked the hens if the hatch stays open. |

| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha angrepet | Hunden ville ha angrepet katten hvis eieren ikke hadde holdt den igjen. | The dog would have attacked the cat if its owner hadn’t held it back. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle angripe | I fortellingen visste ikke høna at reven skulle angripe. | In the story, the hen didn’t know the fox was going to attack. |
| Imperativ | angrip | «Angrip trollet!» ropte ridderen i eventyret. | “Attack the troll!” cried the knight in the fairy tale. |
| Presens partisipp | angripende | Hønene flyktet fra den angripende reven. | The hens fled from the attacking fox. |

| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | angrepet | Et angrepet lam fikk behandling. | A lamb that had been attacked received treatment. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | angrepne | Den angrepne sauen ble holdt under oppsyn. | The sheep that had been attacked was kept under observation. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | angrepne | De angrepne hønene ble undersøkt av veterinæren. | The hens that had been attacked were examined by the vet. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the simple forms and participial agreement. Compound tenses and passive rows illustrate productive constructions. The bilingual examples are original.

**Notes:** Source spelling: angripe. The examples use the direct attack sense and its familiar use for animals attacking prey. In adjectival agreement, both angrepen and angrepet are accepted before indefinite masculine and feminine nouns.
