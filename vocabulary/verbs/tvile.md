## tvile

**English:** to doubt; to be uncertain

**Topics:** thought, communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å tvile | Jeg begynte å tvile på resultatet. | I began to doubt the result. |
| Presens | tviler | Hun tviler på at toget kommer i tide. | She doubts that the train will arrive on time. |
| Preteritum | tvilte | Vi tvilte på forklaringen hans. | We doubted his explanation. |
| Perfektum partisipp | tvilt | Jeg har tvilt på avgjørelsen. | I have doubted the decision. |
| Presens perfektum | har tvilt | Han har tvilt på valget sitt i flere dager. | He has doubted his choice for several days. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde tvilt | Hun hadde tvilt på planen før hun fikk de nye opplysningene. | She had doubted the plan before she got the new information. |
| Futurum | skal tvile | Jeg skal tvile på alle påstander jeg ikke kan kontrollere. | I am going to question every claim I cannot verify. |
| Futurum | vil tvile | Han vil tvile på svaret hvis ingen kan forklare det. | He will doubt the answer if no one can explain it. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha tvilt | Innen da skal jeg ha tvilt på planen lenge nok. | By then I will have questioned the plan long enough. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha tvilt | Innen i morgen vil hun ha tvilt på avgjørelsen i en uke. | By tomorrow she will have doubted the decision for a week. |

**Form status:** Simple forms and participial forms checked against Bokmålsordboka. Compound tenses and impersonal S-passive are productive constructions. Bli-passive is not applicable to this intransitive verb. The participle’s adjectival uses are lexical and context-sensitive.

**Notes:** Normally used with *på* when naming what is doubted. Bokmålsordboka lists *tvilt* for indefinite masculine/feminine and neuter participle agreement, and *tvilte* for definite singular and plural; these participial adjective uses are rare/context-sensitive and are not interchangeable with the ordinary active perfect construction. The dictionary’s S-passive is impersonal (for example, *det tviles på …*); a personal bli-passive is not used in this sense. The dictionary also lists *tvilende* as a present participle, as in *stille seg tvilende til noe*.
