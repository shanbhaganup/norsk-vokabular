## bry

**English:** to bother; to disturb; bry seg: to care

**Topics:** emotions, interaction

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed inflections verified; compound constructions are productive examples.

**Notes:** The reference gloss åto careå is not the ordinary transitive meaning of bry; Bokmålsordboka gives åforstyrre, uroeå (bother, disturb). åCareå is expressed in the reflexive construction bry seg (om), which is a construction with a reflexive pronoun, not a separate inflected form. The dictionary gives both past forms brydde and bråd, and participle brydd. Passive is marked inapplicable because the ordinary lexical verb is used with an object or in reflexive bry seg; passive versions are not natural. Bråd is a formal/less common alternative. Examples are original teaching sentences.
