## lyde

**Topics:** society

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and inflectional forms verified against Bokmålsordboka; compound tenses and voice constructions are productive constructions shown in context.

**Notes:** This entry covers lyde meaning “obey”, distinct from lyde meaning “sound”. Lød and lydde are accepted preterites; participles are lydt and lydd, with the matching definite/plural adjective forms lydte and lydde. Passive and participle alternatives are demonstrated individually. Examples are original teaching sentences.
