**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the finite forms and alternatives. The verb is generally intransitive with *på* or *etter*. S-passive is illustrated impersonally; adjectival participle agreement is listed in the dictionary but marked unverified for natural use.

**Notes:** *Jakte på/etter* means to hunt or pursue something. The dictionary also uses it figuratively for striving to obtain something, such as wealth or gold. The reference source spelling *hunte* is a source error; the normalized Bokmål lemma is *jakte*. Examples are original.
