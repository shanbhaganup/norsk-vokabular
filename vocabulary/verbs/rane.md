**Notes:** The dictionary lists *rana, ranet,* and *rante* as past forms, and *rana, ranet,* and *rant* as perfect participles. In definite/plural adjectival participles, it lists *rana, ranede, ranete,* and *rante*. Some alternatives are uncommon; each is retained because it is dictionary-listed, with its own example. The dictionary lists *ranende* as a present participle, but a natural example in this sense was not established, so it is marked Unverified. Examples are original and frame the crime sense without graphic detail.
