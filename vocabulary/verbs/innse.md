## innse

**English:** to realize, become aware

**Topics:** thought, communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å innse | Det tok tid å innse hva som hadde skjedd. | It took time to realize what had happened. |
| Presens | innser | Hun innser at hun trenger hjelp. | She realizes that she needs help. |
| Preteritum | innså | Vi innså at toget allerede hadde gått. | We realized that the train had already left. |
| Perfektum partisipp | innsett | De har innsett alvoret. | They have realized how serious it is. |
| Presens perfektum | har innsett | Jeg har innsett at planen må endres. | I have realized that the plan must change. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde innsett | Han hadde innsett feilen før noen sa noe. | He had realized the mistake before anyone said anything. |
| Futurum, plan | skal innse | Du skal innse hvor viktig dette er. | You will realize how important this is. |
| Futurum, prediksjon | vil innse | Hun vil snart innse at hun tok feil. | She will soon realize that she was wrong. |
| Futurum perfektum, plan | skal ha innsett | Innen møtet er over, skal de ha innsett konsekvensene. | By the time the meeting is over, they will have realized the consequences. |
| Futurum perfektum, prediksjon | vil ha innsett | Før eller senere vil han ha innsett sannheten. | Sooner or later, he will have realized the truth. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville innse | Jeg ville innse problemet hvis jeg fikk mer informasjon. | I would realize the problem if I got more information. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha innsett | De ville ha innsett faren hvis de hadde sett skiltene. | They would have realized the danger if they had seen the signs. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle innse | Hun sa at hun skulle innse hvor vanskelig det var. | She said that she would realize how difficult it was. |
| Imperativ | innse | Innse at vi må begynne på nytt! | Realize that we have to start again! |
| Presens partisipp | innseende | Unverified | Unverified: dictionary lists *innseende*, but a natural participial use was not confirmed. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv, -es form | innsees | Det må innsees at tiden er knapp. | It must be realized that time is short. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv, -s form | innses | Det må innses at tiden er knapp. | It must be realized that time is short. |

| S-passiv, presens, -s form | innses | Det innses ikke alltid hvor stor risikoen er. | It is not always realized how great the risk is. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir innsett | Problemet blir innsett for sent. | The problem is realized too late. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble innsett | Alvoret ble innsett etter samtalen. | The seriousness was realized after the conversation. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt innsett | Feilen har blitt innsett av alle. | The mistake has been realized by everyone. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | innsett | En innsett begrensning må tas med i planen. | A recognized limitation must be taken into account in the plan. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | innsett | Et innsett faktum er vanskelig å overse. | A recognized fact is difficult to overlook. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | innsette | Den innsette feilen ble rettet. | The realized mistake was corrected. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | innsette | De innsette begrensningene ble tatt med i rapporten. | The recognized limitations were included in the report. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies *innså*, *innsett* and *innsette*. Both S-passive spellings are retained from the source paradigm, though *innses* is the more familiar contemporary form. The verb is normally used with a clause or object; passive uses are impersonal and formal.

**Notes:** *Innse* means to become aware of or realize something. The dictionary lists *innseende* as present participle, but natural use is uncertain, so that row remains unverified. The *innsees* spelling is also less common. Bli-passive examples may sound formal; active *innse* is more usual. Examples are original.
