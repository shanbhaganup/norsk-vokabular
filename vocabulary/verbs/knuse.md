## knuse

**English:** to crush, smash; defeat decisively; be crushed or break

**Topics:** everyday actions, conflict

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å knuse | Pass på å ikke knuse glasset. | Be careful not to break the glass. |
| Presens | knuser | Hun knuser nøttene med en stein. | She crushes the nuts with a stone. |
| Preteritum | knuste | Gutten knuste koppen ved et uhell. | The boy accidentally broke the cup. |
| Perfektum partisipp | knust | Vinduet er knust av en stein. | The window has been smashed by a stone. |
| Presens perfektum | har knust | De har knust isen med en hammer. | They have broken the ice with a hammer. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde knust | Hun hadde knust pepperkakene før hun la dem i røra. | She had crushed the gingerbread before adding it to the batter. |
| Futurum, plan | skal knuse | Vi skal knuse steinen til mindre biter. | We are going to crush the stone into smaller pieces. |
| Futurum, prediksjon | vil knuse | Den sterke vinden vil knuse de gamle vinduene. | The strong wind will smash the old windows. |
| Futurum perfektum, plan | skal ha knust | Innen kvelden skal de ha knust all isen. | By evening, they will have broken all the ice. |
| Futurum perfektum, prediksjon | vil ha knust | Innen morgenen vil frosten ha knust rørene. | By morning, the frost will have burst the pipes. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville knuse | Jeg ville knuse nøttene hvis jeg fant en nøtteknekker. | I would crush the nuts if I found a nutcracker. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha knust | Hun ville ha knust glasset hvis hun hadde mistet det. | She would have broken the glass if she had dropped it. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle knuse | Han sa at han skulle knuse steinen med en slegge. | He said that he would crush the stone with a sledgehammer. |
| Imperativ | knus | Knus isen i små biter! | Crush the ice into small pieces! |
| Presens partisipp | knusende | Et knusende nederlag satte laget tilbake. | A crushing defeat set the team back. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | knuses | Nøttene må knuses før de blandes i deigen. | The nuts must be crushed before they are mixed into the dough. |
| S-passiv, presens | knuses | Isen knuses med en tung maskin. | The ice is crushed with a heavy machine. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir knust | Glasset blir knust hvis det faller i gulvet. | The glass will be broken if it falls on the floor. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble knust | Rekorden ble knust under mesterskapet. | The record was smashed at the championship. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt knust | Flere steiner har blitt knust til grus. | Several rocks have been crushed into gravel. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | knust | En knust kopp lå på gulvet. | A broken cup lay on the floor. |

| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | knuste | Den knuste koppen ble kastet. | The broken cup was thrown away. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | knuste | De knuste rutene ble skiftet ut. | The broken panes were replaced. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the headword, forms and literal, figurative and intransitive senses. Transitive uses license passive constructions; the intransitive “break” use is also noted.

**Notes:** *Knuse* means break/crush into pieces, defeat or suppress decisively, and can also be intransitive (“break/be crushed”), as in *vinduet knuser*. *Knusende* also has the figurative sense “crushing,” as in *knusende nederlag*. Examples are original.
