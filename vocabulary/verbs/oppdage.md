## oppdage

**English:** to discover, notice, or become aware of

**Topics:** science, travel, general

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; *oppdaga/oppdaget* are accepted past and participle alternatives. Transitive uses permit passive constructions.

**Notes:** *Oppdage* means become aware of something, notice it, or discover something new. It can also mean discover or explore a place. The verb is transitive in the examples. Definite/plural participles *oppdagede/oppdagete* are dictionary-listed alternatives; *oppdagede* is more common in formal prose. Although *oppdagende* appears in the dictionary paradigm, a natural active-participle example was not established; this row remains Unverified. Examples are original.
