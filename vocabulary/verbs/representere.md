## representere

**English:** to represent; to act on behalf of; to stand for

**Topics:** communication, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å representere | Hun ønsker å representere klassen i elevrådet. | She wants to represent the class on the student council. |
| Presens | representerer | Advokaten representerer familien i saken. | The lawyer represents the family in the case. |
| Preteritum | representerte | Han representerte Norge under møtet. | He represented Norway at the meeting. |
| Perfektum partisipp | representert | Flere grupper er representert i utvalget. | Several groups are represented on the committee. |
| Presens perfektum | har representert | Hun har representert laget i tre turneringer. | She has represented the team in three tournaments. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde representert | De hadde representert kommunen før reformen. | They had represented the municipality before the reform. |
| Futurum | skal representere | Jeg skal representere studentene på konferansen. | I will represent the students at the conference. |
| Futurum | vil representere | Han vil representere bedriften på messen. | He will represent the company at the trade fair. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha representert | Innen sommeren skal hun ha representert skolen i fem år. | By summer, she will have represented the school for five years. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha representert | Innen møtet starter, vil vi ha representert begge avdelingene. | By the time the meeting starts, we will have represented both departments. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville representere | Jeg ville representere gruppa hvis de spurte meg. | I would represent the group if they asked me. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha representert | Hun ville ha representert laget hvis hun ikke var skadet. | She would have represented the team if she had not been injured. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle representere | Han sa at han skulle representere skolen på møtet. | He said he would represent the school at the meeting. |
| Imperativ | representer | Representer synspunktene deres tydelig. | Represent your views clearly. |
| Presens partisipp | representerende | Den representerende delegasjonen møtte pressen etterpå. | The representing delegation met the press afterward. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | representeres | Alle regioner må representeres i panelet. | All regions must be represented on the panel. |
| S-passiv, presens | representeres | Foreningen representeres av lederen. | The association is represented by its chair. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir representert | Partiet blir representert i alle fylkene. | The party is represented in every county. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble representert | Skolen ble representert av rektor. | The school was represented by the principal. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt representert | Ulike interesser har blitt representert i forhandlingene. | Different interests have been represented in the negotiations. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | representert | En representert gruppe får uttale seg. | A represented group gets to speak. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | representert | Et representert område får en egen plass. | A represented area gets its own seat. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | representerte | Den representerte organisasjonen sendte et skriftlig svar. | The represented organization sent a written response. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | representerte | De representerte landene møttes i Oslo. | The represented countries met in Oslo. |
| Konstruksjon | representere noen/noe i en rolle eller et organ | Hun representerer de ansatte i arbeidsgruppen. | She represents the employees on the working group. |
| Konstruksjon | representere noe / stå for noe | Den røde fargen representerer fare på kartet. | The red color represents danger on the map. |
| Konstruksjon | representere et eksempel/en gruppe | Disse tallene representerer bare en liten del av materialet. | These figures represent only a small part of the material. |

**Form status:** Regular *-ere* verb. Dictionary forms checked; examples are original. The present participle is uncommon as a verb form and is shown in a natural attributive use.

**Notes:** The verb covers representing a person, group, organization, or country; acting in an official capacity; and standing for or exemplifying something. The English sense is reviewed separately from the dictionary paradigm.
