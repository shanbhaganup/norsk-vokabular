**Topics:** communication, media

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and inflection checked; examples and productive tense/passive constructions are authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Filma and filmet are accepted past and perfect-participle forms. The dictionary lists adjectival forms filma/filmet in indefinite agreement, then filma/filmede/filmete in definite singular and plural; those alternatives have separate rows. Filme also has senses “act in a film” and “simulate an injury”; this entry focuses on making a recording. S-passive forms are listed by Ordbøkene.
