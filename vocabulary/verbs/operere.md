## operere

**English:** operate

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å operere | Det er viktig å operere maskinen. | It is important to operate the machine. |
| Presens | opererer | Hun opererer maskinen. | She operates the machine. |
| Preteritum | opererte | Teknikeren opererte maskinen i går. | The technician operated the machine yesterday. |
| Perfektum partisipp | operert | Hun har operert maskinen. | She has operated the machine. |
| Presens perfektum | har operert | Hun har operert maskinen. | She has operated the machine. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde operert | Teknikeren hadde operert maskinen før møtet. | The technician had operated the machine before the meeting. |
| Futurum | skal operere / vil operere | Hun skal operere maskinen i morgen. / Hun vil operere maskinen i morgen. | She is going to operate the machine tomorrow. / She will operate the machine tomorrow. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha operert / vil ha operert | Innen fredag skal teknikeren ha operert maskinen. / Innen fredag vil teknikeren ha operert maskinen. | By Friday, the technician will have operated the machine. / By Friday, the technician will have operated the machine. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville operere | Hun ville operere maskinen hvis hun fikk muligheten. | She would operate the machine if she had the chance. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha operert | Teknikeren ville ha operert maskinen hvis hun hadde fått tillatelse. | The technician would have operated the machine if she had been given permission. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle operere | Hun sa at hun skulle operere maskinen senere. | She said that she would operate the machine later. |
| Imperativ | operer | Operer! | Operate the machine! |
| Presens partisipp | opererende | Det opererende teamet arbeider på sykehuset. | The surgical team is working at the hospital. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | opereres | Pasienten må opereres så snart som mulig. | The patient must be operated on as soon as possible. |
| S-passiv, presens | opereres | Det opereres mange pasienter her hvert år. | Many patients are operated on here each year. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir operert | Pasienten blir operert i morgen. | The patient is being operated on tomorrow. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble operert | Pasienten ble operert i går. | The patient was operated on yesterday. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt operert | Pasienten har blitt operert. | The patient has been operated on. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | operert | Dette er en operert sak. | This is a operated case. |

| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | opererte | Den opererte saken er grundig dokumentert. | The operated case is well documented. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | opererte | De opererte dokumentene ligger i mappen. | The operated documents are in the folder. |
