**Form status:** Dictionary headword, inflection and listed participle agreement forms checked; compound constructions and examples supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists three past forms: forbød, forbøy and forbydde; participles are forbudt and forbydd. Its participle table gives forbudt / forbydd in masculine/feminine and neuter, and forbudte / forbydde in definite singular and plural. *Forby* can mean “prohibit” or “prevent” (sense 2); *Min samvittighet forbød meg å lyve* means “My conscience prevented me from lying.” Conditional examples use *ville* in the result clause and a past tense or past perfect in the *hvis*-clause. The passive and compound forms are productive constructions. Examples are original teaching sentences.
