**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; compound tense and passive examples are original teaching sentences. The adjective forms follow the dictionary’s participle table.

**Notes:** The source English *charge* is interpreted here as replenishing electrical energy. Bokmålsordboka also records other senses of *lade*; they are not treated as forms of this sense. The dictionary accepts the infinitives *la* and *lade* and the listed past and participle variants. *La* is homographic with the separate verb *la* (“let”), so its intended meaning is clear from the object and context. The present participle *laende* is rare; it is retained because it appears in the dictionary table. Examples are authored teaching examples.
