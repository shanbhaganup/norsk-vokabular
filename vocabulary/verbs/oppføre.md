## oppføre

**English:** to behave, conduct oneself

**Topics:** communication, society

**Form status:** This entry covers the reflexive construction *oppføre seg* “behave.” The pronoun changes with the subject; passive and participial adjective forms do not apply to this intransitive reflexive sense.

**Notes:** *Oppføre* has other transitive senses (“perform,” “build,” “enter in a register”), but the assigned reference sense is “behave.” In this meaning it occurs as *oppføre seg*; use *meg/deg/seg/oss/dere/seg* according to the subject. The infinitive row shows the dictionary construction; the imperative uses *deg/dere*, not *seg*. The dictionary's present participle *oppførende* belongs to other senses and is not supplied here. Examples are original.
