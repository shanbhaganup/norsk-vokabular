## fortelle

**English:** to tell, recount, relate

**Topics:** communication, stories

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å fortelle | Han liker å fortelle historier. | He likes to tell stories. |
| Presens | forteller | Hun forteller hva som skjedde. | She tells what happened. |
| Preteritum | fortalte | Bestefar fortalte et eventyr. | Grandpa told a fairy tale. |
| Perfektum partisipp | fortalt | Historien ble fortalt på nytt. | The story was told again. |
| Presens perfektum | har fortalt | Jeg har fortalt dem nyheten. | I have told them the news. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde fortalt | Hun hadde fortalt sannheten før møtet. | She had told the truth before the meeting. |
| Futurum | skal fortelle / vil fortelle | Jeg skal fortelle deg det senere. / Han vil fortelle om reisen sin. | I will tell you later. / He will tell about his trip. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha fortalt / vil ha fortalt | Innen kvelden skal hun ha fortalt alle nyheten. / Da vil han ha fortalt historien mange ganger. | By evening she will have told everyone the news. / By then he will have told the story many times. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville fortelle | Jeg ville fortelle hva jeg så hvis du spurte. | I would tell what I saw if you asked. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha fortalt | Han ville ha fortalt oss sannheten hvis han hadde visst den. | He would have told us the truth if he had known it. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle fortelle | Hun lovet at hun skulle fortelle alt senere. | She promised that she would tell everything later. |
| Imperativ | fortell | Fortell meg hva du synes! | Tell me what you think! |
| Presens partisipp | fortellende | Den fortellende stemmen var rolig. | The narrative voice was calm. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | fortelles | Historien må fortelles med omtanke. | The story must be told with care. |
| S-passiv, presens | fortelles | Det fortelles mange historier om stedet. | Many stories are told about the place. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir fortalt | Nyheten blir fortalt til familien i kveld. | The news is being told to the family tonight. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble fortalt | Eventyret ble fortalt av læreren. | The fairy tale was told by the teacher. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt fortalt | Historien har blitt fortalt i generasjoner. | The story has been told for generations. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | fortalt | En fortalt historie kan endres litt hver gang. | A story told aloud can change a little each time. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | fortalt | Et fortalt minne blir ofte husket lenge. | A recounted memory is often remembered for a long time. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | fortalte | Den fortalte historien gjorde inntrykk. | The story that was told made an impression. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | fortalte | De fortalte historiene samlet familien. | The stories that were told brought the family together. |

**Form status:** Core inflection verified in Bokmålsordboka; participle agreement and compound constructions are illustrated in context.

**Notes:** Covers giving an oral account, recounting, describing, or informing. The verb commonly takes a person and/or the content as complement. The source snapshot's *skulle* conditional alternative is not treated as interchangeable with *ville*. Adjectival participle examples are marked explicitly as attributive; they are not the perfect construction. Examples are authored teaching sentences.
