**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked for the card-game sense; participial and passive uses are Unverified or inapplicable as noted.

**Notes:** The source record's English gloss “to pass” is broad and links three homonymous Bokmålsordboka entries. This entry covers only *passe* 3, “declare pass in a card game.” Other entries are *passe* 4, “look after/watch,” and *passe* 5, “fit/be suitable”; they are separate senses and are not merged here. Passive and resultative adjective forms are not natural for this intransitive card-game use. The dictionary lists *passende* across the homonyms, but it belongs to the “suitable” sense, so this sense's present participle remains Unverified. Examples are original.
