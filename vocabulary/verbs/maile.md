**Notes:** *Maile* means send an email; *e-poste* is a synonym. The Ordbøkene headword page was accessible, but its conjugation table was not exposed in the captured page; the forms *maila/mailet* and derived participle alternatives follow the assigned reference record and need paradigm-level confirmation. The verb is transitive, so passive constructions are possible. Attributive participles such as *mailet dokument* are grammatically formed but stylistically less natural than “dokument som er sendt på e-post.”
