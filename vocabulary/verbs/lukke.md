## lukke

**English:** to close, shut; to lock or shut someone/something in or out

**Topics:** home, general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å lukke | Husk å lukke vinduet før du går. | Remember to close the window before you leave. |
| Presens | lukker | Butikken lukker klokka seks. | The shop closes at six. |
| Preteritum | lukka | Hun lukka døra forsiktig. | She closed the door gently. |
| Preteritum | lukket | Han lukket boka etter siste side. | He closed the book after the last page. |
| Perfektum partisipp | lukka | Vinduet er lukka nå. | The window is closed now. |
| Perfektum partisipp | lukket | Døra er lukket med nøkkel. | The door is locked with a key. |
| Presens perfektum | har lukka | Jeg har lukka alle vinduene. | I have closed all the windows. |
| Presens perfektum | har lukket | De har lukket butikken for dagen. | They have closed the shop for the day. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde lukka | Hun hadde lukka døra før alarmen gikk. | She had closed the door before the alarm went off. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde lukket | Vi hadde lukket kofferten før bilen kom. | We had closed the suitcase before the car arrived. |
| Futurum | skal lukke | Jeg skal lukke porten etterpå. | I will close the gate afterward. |
| Futurum | vil lukke | Vakten vil lukke inngangen ved midnatt. | The guard will close the entrance at midnight. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha lukka | Innen kvelden skal hun ha lukka alle sakene. | By this evening, she will have closed all the cases. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha lukket | Innen vi kommer, vil de ha lukket dørene. | By the time we arrive, they will have closed the doors. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville lukke | Jeg ville lukke vinduet hvis det begynte å regne. | I would close the window if it started raining. |

| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | lukka | Et lukka vindu holdt kulden ute. | A closed window kept the cold out. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; both *lukka* and *lukket* are accepted past and participle forms.

**Notes:** The verb means close/shut, and can also mean lock or shut someone/something into or out of a place. The verb is transitive in the examples and permits passive constructions. The dictionary lists *lukkede/lukkete* as definite and plural alternatives; *lukkete* is less frequent. Examples are original teaching sentences.
