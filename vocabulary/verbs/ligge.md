## ligge

**English:** to lie; to be located or situated; to be in a condition

**Topics:** general, travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å ligge | Jeg liker å ligge på ryggen når jeg leser. | I like to lie on my back when I read. |
| Presens | ligger | Boka ligger på bordet. | The book is lying on the table. |
| Preteritum | lå | Katten lå ved ovnen hele kvelden. | The cat lay by the stove all evening. |
| Perfektum partisipp | ligget | Han har ligget våken siden midnatt. | He has been lying awake since midnight. |

**Form status:** Core forms checked. The verb is intransitive; passive and agreeing past-participle adjective cells from the source paradigm are not verified as natural uses.

**Notes:** *Ligge* describes a horizontal position, location, and several states or conditions. Its past is *lå* and perfect participle *ligget*. Ordbøkene also mechanically lists *liegen/ligget* and definite/plural *ligne* as adjectival participle forms; these are not confidently usable as ordinary adjectives and remain Unverified. Do not confuse *ligne* in that table with the separate verb *ligne* “resemble.” *Liggende* is the natural present-participle/adjectival form. S-passive *ligges* is not established for the ordinary intransitive senses.
