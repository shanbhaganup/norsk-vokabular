## kapre

**English:** to seize or get hold of by a coup or trick; historically, to capture with a privateer

**Topics:** society, travel

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the sense, inflection alternatives and both imperative forms. The verb is transitive and supports passive constructions.

**Notes:** The ordinary sense is to get hold of something by a coup or trick, including seizing a ship or grabbing a desirable place. The dictionary also marks the historical privateer sense. The *-ede/-ete* attributive participle alternatives are dictionary-listed, though *-ede* is more usual. Examples are original.
