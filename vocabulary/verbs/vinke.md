**English:** to wave (a hand or arm)

**Topics:** communication

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; productive constructions and original examples reviewed.

**Notes:** Both accepted past and participle spellings are illustrated. The entry covers waving as a greeting or signal. Impersonal passive examples describe the action of waving. Adjectival perfect-participle agreement forms are listed in the dictionary, but their natural use in this wave sense is uncertain and marked Unverified. Compound constructions are examples, not dictionary inflections.
