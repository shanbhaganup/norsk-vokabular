## undervise

**English:** to teach; to instruct

**Topics:** education

**Form status:** Simple forms, participles, and dictionary alternatives checked against Bokmålsordboka. Compound forms are productive constructions.

**Notes:** Often used with an indirect object and a subject area (undervise noen i noe). Passive examples use the impersonal construction “det undervises i …”. All Norwegian examples are original teaching sentences.
