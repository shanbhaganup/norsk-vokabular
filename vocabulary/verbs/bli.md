## bli

**English:** to remain; to become; to get (into a state)

**Topics:** change, states

**Form status:** Simple forms *ble / blei*, *blitt*, imperative *bli* and present participle *blivende* verified in Bokmålsordboka. Compound tenses are productive constructions.

**Notes:** The source record also lists *bleiv / blev*, but Bokmålsordboka treats these under the separate, rare verb *blive* (sense “drown”), not the common verb *bli* meaning “remain/become”; they are therefore excluded here. *Bli* commonly serves as a passive auxiliary, for example *Brevet blir sendt i morgen* (“The letter will be sent tomorrow”); this is a construction with another verb, not a passive form of lexical *bli*. The perfect participle *blitt* is invariant in standard use as a compound-tense form; no adjectival agreement paradigm is listed for this lemma.
