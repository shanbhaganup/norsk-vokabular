**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; compound tenses and passive constructions are productive forms. Examples are original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The source English *save* is treated as storing or preserving information and files. Bokmålsordboka also records other dictionary senses for *lagre*, including maturing food and attaching a shaft in a bearing; those are separate senses and are outside this source translation. The past and participle forms *lagra* and *lagret* are accepted alternatives; *lagr!* and *lagre!* are both dictionary imperatives. *Lagrende* is the dictionary present participle. The adjectival participle variants follow the dictionary table.
