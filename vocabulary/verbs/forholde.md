## forholde

**English:** to relate; most commonly in *forholde seg (til)*, to relate to, deal with, or take a position on

**Topics:** communication, society

**Form status:** Ordbøkene verifies the core forms and participle table. The usual reflexive constructions are demonstrated. Participial adjective use and present-participle use in this sense remain unverified.

**Notes:** In this sense, the verb normally appears as *forholde seg* or *forholde seg til*. The dictionary also lists other fixed meanings, including *det forholder seg slik at ...*; these are outside this entry's selected sense. Passive forms are marked inapplicable for the usual reflexive construction, not claimed impossible for every specialized use. The reference's bare English “to relate” is narrowed to the attested reflexive senses.
