## forsvinne

**English:** to disappear, go missing

**Topics:** general, change

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å forsvinne | Nøkkelen kan forsvinne i rotet. | The key can disappear in the clutter. |
| Presens | forsvinner | Tåken forsvinner når sola kommer fram. | The fog disappears when the sun comes out. |
| Preteritum | forsvant | Katten forsvant inn mellom husene. | The cat disappeared between the houses. |
| Perfektum partisipp | forsvunnet | Sporene var forsvunnet om morgenen. | The tracks had disappeared by morning. |
| Presens perfektum | har forsvunnet | Meldingen har forsvunnet fra skjermen. | The message has disappeared from the screen. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde forsvunnet | Bussen hadde forsvunnet rundt svingen før jeg kom ut. | The bus had disappeared around the bend before I came out. |
| Futurum | skal forsvinne / vil forsvinne | Skyene skal forsvinne utover dagen. / Jeg tror smerten vil forsvinne snart. | The clouds are expected to disappear during the day. / I think the pain will disappear soon. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha forsvunnet / vil ha forsvunnet | Innen kvelden skal tåken ha forsvunnet. / Innen du kommer, vil støvet ha forsvunnet. | By evening the fog should have disappeared. / By the time you arrive, the dust will have disappeared. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville forsvinne | Jeg ville forsvinne fra festen hvis jeg ble uvel. | I would leave the party if I felt ill. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha forsvunnet | Katten ville ha forsvunnet helt hvis ingen hadde lett. | The cat would have disappeared completely if no one had searched. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle forsvinne | Hun trodde at tåken skulle forsvinne før lunsj. | She thought the fog would disappear before lunch. |
| Imperativ | forsvinn | Forsvinn ut av syne! | Get out of sight! |
| Presens partisipp | forsvinnende | Det var en forsvinnende liten forskjell. | It was a vanishingly small difference. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified. Compound tenses and participle agreement are contextual constructions; passive is inapplicable for this intransitive change-of-state sense.

**Notes:** Means becoming absent or no longer visible, including a person or object going missing. The dictionary confirms the masculine/feminine adjectival alternatives *forsvunnet/forsvunnen*. *Forsvinnende* is shown in the established degree expression *forsvinnende liten*. Source spelling and forms match the cited entry. Examples are authored teaching sentences.
