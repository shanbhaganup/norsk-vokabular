**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; compound tense and passive examples are productive constructions.

**Notes:** The anchored source sense is “brand”; the dictionary entry also covers marking/labeling, tracing/affecting, and noticing/experiencing. This entry uses the source sense and nearby marking/labeling use, not the separate “notice” sense. The dictionary accepts merka/merket in the simple past and participle, and merka/merket/merkede/merkete in adjectival forms. Conditional skulle is retained as an accepted source alternative; it can also express obligation depending on context. Examples are original teaching sentences.
