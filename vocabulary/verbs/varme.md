**English:** to heat; to warm

**Topics:** home, food

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed inflections checked; compound tense and passive examples are contextual teaching constructions.

**Notes:** The meaning here is to raise temperature or make warm. Bokmålsordboka accepts both varma and varmet for the past and participle, with the agreeing participle variants listed below. S-passive is natural in general heating contexts. The dictionary also has a separate figurative sense “make glad”; this entry follows the reference anchor’s “heat”. Examples are original teaching sentences. Futurum rows distinguish intention (skal) from prediction/volition (vil). Conditional rows use ville; skulle is reserved for future viewed from a past context.
