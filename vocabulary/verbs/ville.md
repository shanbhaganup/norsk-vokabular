## ville

**English:** to want; to wish

**Topics:** communication

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; productive constructions and original examples reviewed.

**Notes:** Ville is also widely used as an auxiliary expressing future or conditional meaning. These examples focus on the independent verb meaning “want/wish.” The dictionary lists villende, but its natural contemporary use as a participle in this sense is uncertain; marked Unverified. Passive is inapplicable to the independent “want” sense. The dictionary-listed future-like auxiliary chains are awkward in isolation but retained with contexts. The S-passive is inapplicable to the independent “want” sense. Compound constructions are examples, not dictionary inflections.
