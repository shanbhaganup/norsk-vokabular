## ledsage

**English:** accompany; be accompanied by

**Topics:** travel, communication

**Notes:** Dictionary alternatives are kept distinct in rows. Compound tenses and passive constructions are productive constructions, not extra dictionary headwords. The dictionary also uses ledsage for something that occurs together with another thing (for example, a lecture accompanied by slides); this entry emphasizes accompanying a person.
