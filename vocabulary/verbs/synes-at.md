## synes at

**English:** to think that; to feel that

**Topics:** communication, opinions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å synes at | Jeg synes at planen er god. | I think that the plan is good. |
| Presens | synes / syns | Hun synes at filmen er morsom. / Han syns at maten smaker godt. | She thinks the film is funny. / He thinks the food tastes good. |
| Preteritum | syntes | Vi syntes at møtet var nyttig. | We thought the meeting was useful. |
| Perfektum partisipp | syntes | Jeg har syntes at oppgaven var krevende. | I have thought that the task was demanding. |
| Presens perfektum | har syntes | De har syntes at kurset var lærerikt. | They have thought the course was educational. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde syntes | Hun hadde syntes at rommet var for lite. | She had thought that the room was too small. |
| Futurum | skal synes at / vil synes at | Jeg skal si hva jeg synes at vi bør gjøre. / Du vil synes at utsikten er flott. | I will say what I think we should do. / You will think the view is wonderful. |

**Form status:** Simple forms checked against Bokmålsordboka; compound tenses and original examples are authored teaching material.

**Notes:** This source phrase is built from the verb synes (“think/feel”) followed by the subjunction at and a finite clause; at remains unchanged. It is not a separate verb paradigm. Bokmålsordboka lists å synes, present synes/syns, past syntes, and perfect har syntes. The phrase-level future perfect and conditional perfect are marked inapplicable here because a completed opinion construction is not a natural target meaning in this entry; use context-specific clauses with synes instead.
