## seire

**English:** to win; to triumph; to prevail

**Topics:** sport, society

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka lists *seira/seiret* for past and participle, and *seira, seirede,* and *seirete* for definite/plural adjectival forms. The verb is intransitive; bli-passive is therefore inapplicable.

**Notes:** The dictionary sense includes winning a contest and an abstract quality taking over or prevailing. Attributive participle examples are uncommon; dictionary forms are shown for completeness. Examples are original.
