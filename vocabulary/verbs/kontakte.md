## kontakte

**English:** to contact, get in touch with

**Topics:** communication

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies *-a/-et* finite and participle forms. The verb is transitive and supports both passive types.

**Notes:** *Kontakte noen* means get in touch with someone, including by telephone. The definite/plural participles *kontaktede/kontaktete* are dictionary-listed but less common than *kontakta*. Examples are original.
