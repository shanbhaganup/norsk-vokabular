## konfrontere

**English:** to confront, face; bring face to face; challenge someone with prior words or actions

**Topics:** communication, conflict

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å konfrontere | Hun bestemte seg for å konfrontere naboen. | She decided to confront the neighbor. |
| Presens | konfronterer | Journalisten konfronterer politikeren med tallene. | The journalist confronts the politician with the figures. |
| Preteritum | konfronterte | Han konfronterte henne med det hun hadde sagt. | He confronted her with what she had said. |
| Perfektum partisipp | konfrontert | Den konfronterte lederen svarte rolig. | The confronted leader answered calmly. |
| Presens perfektum | har konfrontert | De har konfrontert selskapet med dokumentasjonen. | They have confronted the company with the documentation. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde konfrontert | Hun hadde konfrontert ham før møtet begynte. | She had confronted him before the meeting began. |
| Futurum, plan | skal konfrontere | Jeg skal konfrontere ham om fraværet. | I am going to confront him about the absence. |
| Futurum, prediksjon | vil konfrontere | Reporteren vil konfrontere ministeren med påstandene. | The reporter will confront the minister with the allegations. |
| Futurum perfektum, plan | skal ha konfrontert | Innen fredag skal hun ha konfrontert leverandøren. | By Friday, she will have confronted the supplier. |
| Futurum perfektum, prediksjon | vil ha konfrontert | Innen kvelden vil politiet ha konfrontert vitnet med opplysningene. | By evening, the police will have confronted the witness with the information. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville konfrontere | Jeg ville konfrontere ham hvis jeg visste hvor han var. | I would confront him if I knew where he was. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha konfrontert | Hun ville ha konfrontert dem hvis hun hadde hatt bevis. | She would have confronted them if she had had evidence. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle konfrontere | Han sa at han skulle konfrontere henne med brevet. | He said that he would confront her with the letter. |
| Imperativ | konfronter | Konfronter problemet før det vokser! | Confront the problem before it grows! |
| Presens partisipp | konfronterende | Et konfronterende spørsmål gjorde rommet stille. | A confrontational question made the room go quiet. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | konfronteres | Vitnet må konfronteres med de nye opplysningene. | The witness must be confronted with the new information. |
| S-passiv, presens | konfronteres | Ekstremister konfronteres åpent i debatten. | Extremists are confronted openly in the debate. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir konfrontert | Lederen blir konfrontert med kritikken. | The leader is confronted with the criticism. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble konfrontert | Vitnet ble konfrontert med fotografiet. | The witness was confronted with the photograph. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt konfrontert | Flere ansatte har blitt konfrontert med påstandene. | Several employees have been confronted with the allegations. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | konfrontert | En konfrontert person kan trenge tid til å svare. | A person who has been confronted may need time to answer. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | konfrontert | Et konfrontert vitne fikk en pause. | A witness who had been confronted was given a break. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | konfronterte | Den konfronterte politikeren svarte på spørsmålene. | The politician who had been confronted answered the questions. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | konfronterte | De konfronterte lederne møtte pressen. | The leaders who had been confronted met the press. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the paradigm and senses. The verb is transitive and supports both passive constructions.

**Notes:** Common patterns include *konfrontere noen med noe* and *konfrontere noen*. It can mean facing someone directly, challenging them with previous statements or actions, or openly facing a feared issue. Examples are original.
