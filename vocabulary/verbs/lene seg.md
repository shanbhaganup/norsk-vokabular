## lene seg

**English:** to lean (oneself), to lean against or back

**Topics:** body, movement

**Notes:** The dictionary gives lenet/lent, lente, lenede/lenete/lente, and lenende for the verb. In the reflexive phrase, the pronoun agrees with the subject (meg, deg, seg, oss, dere).
