**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked in Bokmålsordboka. Compound tenses, conditional constructions and bli-passives are productive constructions illustrated with original examples.

**Notes:** The reference and dictionary list data and datet alternatives in the date paradigm; the dictionary also lists the related spelling deite with a separate paradigm, which is excluded because this entry is specifically date. The s-passive and passive voice are grammatically possible but stylistically uncommon for this relationship verb; examples illustrate marked contexts. Participial adjectives are unusual and used descriptively. The source headword sense is romantic dating, not scheduling a date.
