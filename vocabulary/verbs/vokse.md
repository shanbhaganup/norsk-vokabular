## vokse

**English:** to grow; to increase; to develop

**Topics:** nature, growth

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å vokse | Barn trenger plass til å vokse. | Children need room to grow. |
| Presens | vokser | Trærne vokser raskt om våren. | The trees grow quickly in spring. |
| Preteritum | vokste | Planten vokste mye i fjor. | The plant grew a lot last year. |
| Perfektum partisipp | vokst | Barnet er vokst fra skoene sine. | The child has outgrown their shoes. |
| Presens perfektum | har vokst | Barnet har vokst flere centimeter. | The child has grown several centimetres. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde vokst | Elva hadde vokst etter flere dager med regn. | The river had risen after several days of rain. |
| Futurum | skal vokse / vil vokse | Planten skal vokse videre. / Planten vil vokse videre. | The plant is going to keep growing. / The plant will keep growing. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha vokst / vil ha vokst | Innen høsten skal treet ha vokst en meter. / Innen høsten vil treet ha vokst en meter. | By autumn the tree will have grown a metre. / By autumn the tree will have grown a metre. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville vokse / skulle vokse | Planten ville vokse bedre i mer sol. / De trodde treet skulle vokse raskt. | The plant would grow better in more sun. / They thought the tree would grow quickly. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; productive constructions and original examples reviewed.

**Notes:** Scoped to the intransitive sense “grow/develop/increase” (Bokmålsordboka 68947), not the separate transitive verb “apply wax” (68946). Therefore only vokste/vokst are included; source alternatives voksa/vokset belong to the other homonym. Passive forms are inapplicable to this intransitive sense. Adjectival agreement of the intransitive participle is uncertain in this sense and marked Unverified; source alternatives voksa/vokset belong to the separate transitive homonym “apply wax.” Compound constructions are examples, not dictionary inflections.
