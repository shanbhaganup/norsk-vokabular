**Form status:** Promise-sense headword and forms checked; compound constructions and examples are original teaching material.

**Notes:** This entry covers *love* 3 (“promise/pledge”), not *love* 4 (“praise”). The source record combines forms belonging to both homonymous entries: for the promise sense, Ordbøkene 35841 supports *lova/lovde/lovet/lovte* in preterite and *lova/lovd/lovet/lovt* as participle. Definite/plural forms are *lova, lovde, lovede, lovete, lovte*. Some alternatives are rare; *lovet* and *lovte* are common Bokmål choices. The present participle *lovende* is also used adjectivally as “promising.” Examples use promise/pledge, not praise. The s-passive example “kundene loves” is formal/stilted; *bli lovet* is the natural passive in most contexts.
