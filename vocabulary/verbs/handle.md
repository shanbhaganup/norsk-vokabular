## handle

**English:** to act, conduct business, or deal with an issue; also to shop

**Topics:** General / unassigned

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the headword and cited forms. Compound tenses and applicable passive constructions are shown. Examples are original.

**Notes:** The dictionary distinguishes shopping, trading, and acting. It also has handle om “be about,” a fixed construction. Examples cover shopping and acting. Past and participle spellings are handla/handlet; adjectival forms include handla/handlede/handlete. The s-passive handles is dictionary-listed in the snapshot but natural use is limited; its use here is marked Unverified. Presens partisipp handlende is uncommon and is shown in the established phrase den handlende parten.
