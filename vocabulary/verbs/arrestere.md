## arrestere

**English:** to arrest

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å arrestere | Politiet kom for å arrestere mannen. | The police came to arrest the man. |
| Presens | arresterer | Politiet arresterer en av de mistenkte. | The police are arresting one of the suspects. |
| Preteritum | arresterte | Politiet arresterte henne på flyplassen. | The police arrested her at the airport. |
| Perfektum partisipp | arrestert | Den mistenkte er arrestert. | The suspect has been arrested. |
| Presens perfektum | har arrestert | Politiet har arrestert en kvinne i saken. | The police have arrested a woman in connection with the case. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde arrestert | Politiet hadde arrestert føreren før vi kom fram. | The police had arrested the driver before we arrived. |
| Futurum | skal arrestere | I neste episode skal politiet arrestere hovedpersonen. | In the next episode, the police are going to arrest the main character. |
| Futurum | vil arrestere | Jeg tror de vil arrestere ham når han kommer hjem. | I think they will arrest him when he gets home. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha arrestert | Ifølge manuset skal politiet ha arrestert mannen før siste scene. | According to the script the police are to have arrested the man before the final scene. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha arrestert | Ved slutten av episoden vil politiet ha arrestert begge de mistenkte. | By the end of the episode the police will have arrested both suspects. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville arrestere | De ville arrestere henne hvis hun kom tilbake. | They would arrest her if she came back. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha arrestert | Politiet ville ha arrestert ham hvis de hadde funnet ham. | The police would have arrested him if they had found him. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle arrestere | Hun skjønte at politiet skulle arrestere henne. | She realized that the police were going to arrest her. |
| Imperativ | arrester | «Arrester ham», sa politisjefen i filmen. | “Arrest him,” said the police chief in the film. |
| Presens partisipp | arresterende | Forsvareren stilte spørsmål til den arresterende betjenten. | The defense lawyer questioned the arresting officer. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | arresteres | En person kan arresteres i forbindelse med saken. | A person may be arrested in connection with the case. |
| S-passiv, presens | arresteres | I filmen arresteres helten ved grensa. | In the film, the hero is arrested at the border. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir arrestert | Hovedpersonen blir arrestert før han rekker toget. | The main character is arrested before he catches the train. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble arrestert | Tre personer ble arrestert i går kveld. | Three people were arrested last night. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt arrestert | Den andre mistenkte har blitt arrestert. | The other suspect has been arrested. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | arrestert | En arrestert demonstrant ba om å få ringe hjem. | A protester who had been arrested asked to call home. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | arrestert | Et arrestert gjengmedlem ble løslatt. | A gang member who had been arrested was released. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | arresterte | Den arresterte sjåføren fikk snakke med en advokat. | The driver who had been arrested was allowed to speak to a lawyer. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | arresterte | De arresterte demonstrantene ble løslatt samme kveld. | The arrested protesters were released that evening. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka confirms the simple forms and participial agreement. Compound tenses and passive constructions are illustrated in original bilingual examples.

**Notes:** Source spelling: arrestere. The examples illustrate taking someone into custody. Anholde overlaps in this sense and is a separate lemma. The present participle sounds formal.
