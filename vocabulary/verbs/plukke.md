**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; *plukka/plukket* alternatives and adjectival participle variants are separately illustrated.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists *plukka* and *plukket* for past and participle forms, and *plukka/plukkede/plukkete* for definite and plural adjectival forms. The verb has several senses and constructions, including picking plants, plucking feathers, picking at something, picking up, and selecting; this entry's examples focus on picking/gathering. Examples are original teaching material.
