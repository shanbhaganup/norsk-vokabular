## hviske

**English:** to whisper

**Topics:** communication

**Form status:** Dictionary verifies the listed forms and alternatives. The verb is transitive in “whisper something” and also occurs with *om* and indirect objects. Examples are original.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes the impersonal S-passive *det hviskes* and the construction *hviske noen noe i øret*. Alternative participle endings are separated here so each has its own example. The *-ede* and *-ete* attributive forms are dictionary-listed and less common than *-a*. Examples are teaching sentences, not dictionary quotations.
