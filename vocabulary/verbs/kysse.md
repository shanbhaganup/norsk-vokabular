## kysse

**English:** to kiss; figuratively, to express contempt in fixed insults

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; productive tense/passive constructions and original examples supplied. Some adjectival variants are rare and the constructed phrases are intentionally literal.

**Notes:** The ordinary sense is to touch someone or something with the lips. A contemptuous use occurs in fixed insults such as *kyss meg bak*; it is not treated here as a productive separate paradigm. Dictionary-listed past forms are *kyssa, kysset, kysste*; participles are *kyssa, kysset, kysst*. Rare definite/plural participial spellings are retained from the source paradigm and may sound unnatural outside set contexts. Examples are authored teaching examples, not dictionary quotations.
