## vinne

**English:** to win; to gain, secure

**Topics:** sport, achievement

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å vinne | Laget håper å vinne finalen. | The team hopes to win the final. |
| Presens | vinner | Laget vinner kampen. | The team wins the match. |
| Preteritum | vant | De vant turneringen i fjor. | They won the tournament last year. |
| Perfektum partisipp | vunnet | Seieren er vunnet. | The victory has been won. |
| Presens perfektum | har vunnet | Vi har vunnet kampen. | We have won the match. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde vunnet | De hadde vunnet før regnet begynte. | They had won before the rain began. |
| Futurum | skal vinne / vil vinne | Vi skal vinne neste kamp. / Jeg tror laget vil vinne. | We are going to win the next match. / I think the team will win. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha vunnet / vil ha vunnet | Innen søndag skal laget ha vunnet gruppa. / Innen søndag vil laget ha vunnet gruppa. | By Sunday the team will have won the group. / By Sunday the team will have won the group. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville vinne / skulle vinne | De ville vinne hvis de trente mer. / Hun sa at hun skulle vinne prisen året etter. | They would win if they trained more. / She said she would win the prize the following year. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha vunnet / skulle ha vunnet | De ville ha vunnet hvis de hadde trent mer. / Hun sa at hun skulle ha vunnet dersom juryen valgte henne. | They would have won if they had trained more. / She said she would have won if the jury chose her. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle vinne | Treneren sa at laget skulle vinne. | The coach said the team would win. |
| Imperativ | vinn | Vinn kampen! | Win the match! |
| Presens partisipp | vinnende | Det vinnende laget jublet. | The winning team cheered. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | vinnes | Finalen må vinnes på banen. | The final must be won on the field. |
| S-passiv, presens | vinnes | Kampen vinnes av laget med flest mål. | The match is won by the team with the most goals. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir vunnet | Finalen blir vunnet av hjemmelaget. | The final is won by the home team. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble vunnet | Kampen ble vunnet på overtid. | The match was won in extra time. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt vunnet | Flere medaljer har blitt vunnet av klubben. | Several medals have been won by the club. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | vunnet / vunnen | En vunnet / vunnen seier gir laget selvtillit. / En vunnet / vunnen kamp gir laget selvtillit. | A victory that has been won gives the team confidence. / A match that has been won gives the team confidence. |

| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | vunne | Den vunne kampen ble feiret. | The won match was celebrated. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; productive constructions and original examples reviewed.

**Notes:** Scoped to “win/obtain” (Bokmålsordboka 68557). The separate homonym for working at the harvest (68556) has other forms and is excluded. Vunnen is a listed masculine/feminine adjectival participle alternative; the neuter form is vunnet. The noun vinne (“win”) is a separate homonym with its own paradigm (reference noun record); this file covers only the verb. Compound constructions are examples, not dictionary inflections.
