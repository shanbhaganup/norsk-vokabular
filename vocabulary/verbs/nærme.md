## nærme

**English:** to approach; to come closer; to near

**Topics:** movement, time

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. The usual approach construction is reflexive *nærme seg*; the dictionary also lists s-passive *nærmes*. Bli-passive and agreeing participial adjectives are not verified as natural.

**Notes:** The verb commonly appears as *nærme seg* (“come closer/approach”), which requires the reflexive pronoun matching the subject. The dictionary lists *nærma/nærmet* as past and participle alternatives. The passive examples use a direct location complement; intransitive/reflexive uses do not take a personal bli-passive. The supplied participial adjective forms remain Unverified. Examples are original.
