## melde

**English:** to report, notify, announce; to register or sign up

**Topics:** communication, work, education

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å melde | Du må melde fra om skaden. | You must report the damage. |
| Presens | melder | Hun melder brannen til nødetatene. | She reports the fire to the emergency services. |
| Preteritum | meldte | De meldte sin ankomst på forhånd. | They announced their arrival in advance. |
| Perfektum partisipp | meldt | Gutten er meldt savnet. | The boy has been reported missing. |
| Presens perfektum | har meldt | Jeg har meldt meg på kurset. | I have signed up for the course. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde meldt | Hun hadde meldt fra om feilen før fristen. | She had reported the error before the deadline. |
| Futurum | skal melde | Vi skal melde resultatet etter kampen. | We will report the result after the match. |
| Futurum | vil melde | Kontoret vil melde søkerne om avgjørelsen. | The office will notify the applicants of the decision. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha meldt | Innen kvelden skal de ha meldt alle deltakerne på. | By this evening, they will have registered all the participants. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha meldt | Innen fristen vil hun ha meldt seg til eksamen. | By the deadline, she will have registered for the exam. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville melde | Jeg ville melde fra hvis jeg så noe mistenkelig. | I would report it if I saw anything suspicious. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; the verb is transitive in report/notify senses and has the reflexive registration construction *melde seg*.

**Notes:** *Melde* has several senses, including report an incident, notify/announce, send a text, review publicly, and declare a card hand. Its reflexive constructions include *melde seg på* (sign up), *melde seg inn/ut* (join/leave), and *melde seg* (show up or volunteer). These are constructions, not inflected forms. Passive examples use transitive report/announce senses; *meldt savnet* is a common participial use. Examples are original.
