## ete

**English:** to eat; to consume; figuratively, to use up or gnaw at

**Topics:** food, nature

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; adjectival participle forms follow the dictionary paradigm. Some constructions are marked in Notes as less common.

**Notes:** This verb is *ete*, with present *eter*, past *åt*, and participle *ett*; do not confuse *ett* (participle) with the determiner *ett* (“one”). The participle is used naturally in perfect tenses and in the compound *ete opp*. The dictionary lists *ette* for definite and plural adjectival participle forms, but these are rare and can sound marked; use *spist* or *spist opp* in ordinary prose. *Ete* also has figurative uses, including “use up” and “gnaw at.”
