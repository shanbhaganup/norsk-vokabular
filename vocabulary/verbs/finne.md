## finne

**English:** to find; to discover

**Topics:** everyday life, problem-solving

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and inflection checked; examples and productive tense/passive constructions are authored for this entry.

**Notes:** This entry covers the core sense “come across, encounter, discover.” Finne also occurs in established expressions such as finne ut, finne på and finne seg i; these are constructions with their own meanings, not inflected forms. In adjectival use the dictionary allows both funnen and funnet for masculine/feminine singular; neuter is funnet and definite singular/plural are funne. S-passive finnes is grammatically available, though context can make it resemble the separate verb finnes “exist”; these examples use a finding/result reading.
