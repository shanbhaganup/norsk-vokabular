## leve

**English:** to live, exist; to make a living; to live in a particular way

**Topics:** general, health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å leve | Mange ønsker å leve nær naturen. | Many people want to live close to nature. |
| Presens | lever | Hun lever et aktivt liv. | She lives an active life. |
| Preteritum | levde | Bestefaren min levde i hundre år. | My grandfather lived to be a hundred. |
| Perfektum partisipp | levd | Han har levd et langt liv. | He has lived a long life. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms and common senses checked. Passive constructions are inapplicable to the intransitive “live/exist” senses.

**Notes:** The verb is normally intransitive, though it can take an object in expressions such as *leve et liv*. No passive is formed for the senses shown. *Levende* is also a lexicalized adjective; the row illustrates the dictionary-listed present participle used attributively. Participial adjective examples with *levd* use the phrase “a life that has been lived.”
