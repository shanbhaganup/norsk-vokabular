## høste

**English:** to harvest; reap or gain as a result

**Topics:** food, agriculture

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the headword, paradigm, alternatives and the transitive and figurative senses. Both S-passive and bli-passive are applicable to the transitive sense.

**Notes:** Besides gathering a crop, *høste* means to obtain a result or reward, as in *høste anerkjennelse* and *høste applaus*. The set phrase *det høstes* can also mean that autumn is approaching; it is not a passive of a harvested object. *Høstet* and the *-ede/-ete* adjective forms are accepted but some can sound formal or less common. Examples are original.
