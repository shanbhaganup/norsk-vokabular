## rydde

**English:** to tidy; to clear; to remove

**Topics:** home, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å rydde | Vi må rydde kjøkkenet før gjestene kommer. | We have to tidy the kitchen before the guests arrive. |
| Presens | rydder | Hun rydder skrivebordet hver fredag. | She tidies her desk every Friday. |
| Preteritum | rydda | Jeg rydda av bordet etter middagen. | I cleared the table after dinner. |
| Preteritum, alternative | ryddet | De ryddet rommet på en halvtime. | They tidied the room in half an hour. |
| Perfektum partisipp | rydda | Stua er rydda og klar til bruk. | The living room has been tidied and is ready to use. |
| Perfektum partisipp, alternative | ryddet | Kontoret er ryddet før møtet. | The office has been tidied before the meeting. |
| Presens perfektum | har rydda | Vi har rydda hele kjelleren. | We have cleared the whole basement. |
| Presens perfektum, alternative | har ryddet | Hun har ryddet bort verktøyet. | She has put away the tools. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde rydda | De hadde rydda veien før ambulansen kom. | They had cleared the road before the ambulance arrived. |
| Preteritum perfektum, alternative | hadde ryddet | Jeg hadde ryddet pulten før arbeidsdagen startet. | I had tidied the desk before the workday started. |
| Futurum | skal rydde | Vi skal rydde boden i helgen. | We will clear out the storage room this weekend. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka lists *rydda/ryddet* for past and participle; definite and plural adjective forms include *rydda, ryddede,* and *ryddete*. Alternatives are individually illustrated.

**Notes:** The verb covers tidying, clearing/removing, and preparing land or a route. Examples focus on tidying and clearing. Examples are original teaching sentences.
