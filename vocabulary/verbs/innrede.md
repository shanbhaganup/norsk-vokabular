**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the headword and forms, including *-a/-et* alternatives and participle agreement. The verb is transitive in the furnishing sense.

**Notes:** The dictionary also lists *innreie* as a parallel headword with its own paradigm (*innreier, innreide, innreid*). This entry follows the assigned *innrede* form only. *Innredede/innredete* are accepted definite and plural participles; they are less common than *innreda*. Examples are original.
