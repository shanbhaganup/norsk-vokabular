## gi

**English:** to give, hand over, or provide.

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

**Notes:** Ga and gav are both standard Bokmål. Attributive gitt often means “given” or “specified.” Giende is dictionary-listed and uncommon in everyday speech. The skulle row shows future viewed from the past.
