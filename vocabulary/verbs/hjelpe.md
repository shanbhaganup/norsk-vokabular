## hjelpe

**English:** to help, assist; to rescue

**Topics:** people, assistance

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å hjelpe | Det er fint å hjelpe naboen. | It is good to help the neighbor. |
| Presens | hjelper | Hun hjelper en ny kollega. | She is helping a new colleague. |
| Preteritum | hjalp | Vi hjalp familien i helgen. | We helped the family at the weekend. |
| Perfektum partisipp | hjulpet | De er hjulpet av frivillige. | They are helped by volunteers. |
| Presens perfektum | har hjulpet | Jeg har hjulpet ham flere ganger. | I have helped him several times. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde hjulpet | Hun hadde hjulpet oss før møtet begynte. | She had helped us before the meeting began. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and inflection forms checked. Compound tenses, passive constructions and authored examples are supplied in context.

**Notes:** The entry covers giving assistance or rescuing someone. Hjelpe also occurs in constructions such as *hjelpe til* and *hjelpe noen med noe*. The dictionary lists *hjulpen* and *hjulpet* as masculine/feminine adjectival participles; their use can be context-sensitive. Examples are original teaching sentences.
