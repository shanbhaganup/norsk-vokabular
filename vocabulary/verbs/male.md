## male

**English:** to paint; to depict or portray

**Topics:** culture, home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å male | De liker å male landskapsbilder sammen. | They like to paint landscapes together. |
| Presens | maler | Hun maler gjerdet grønt. | She is painting the fence green. |
| Preteritum | malte | Vi malte stua i fjor. | We painted the living room last year. |
| Perfektum partisipp | malt | Veggen er malt i en lys farge. | The wall is painted a light color. |
| Presens perfektum | har malt | Han har malt et portrett av søsteren sin. | He has painted a portrait of his sister. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde malt | De hadde malt huset før vinteren kom. | They had painted the house before winter arrived. |
| Futurum | skal male | Jeg skal male kjøkkenet i helgen. | I will paint the kitchen this weekend. |
| Futurum | vil male | Kunstneren vil male et nytt bilde til utstillingen. | The artist will paint a new picture for the exhibition. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha malt | Innen fredag skal vi ha malt alle rommene. | By Friday, we will have painted all the rooms. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha malt | Innen åpningen vil hun ha malt ferdig bildet. | By the opening, she will have finished painting the picture. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville male | Jeg ville male veggen hvis jeg hadde mer tid. | I would paint the wall if I had more time. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha malt | Han ville ha malt taket hvis stigen hadde vært høy nok. | He would have painted the ceiling if the ladder had been tall enough. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle male | Hun sa at hun skulle male et bilde av fjorden. | She said she would paint a picture of the fjord. |
| Imperativ | mal | Mal døra rød! | Paint the door red! |
| Presens partisipp | malende | En malende kunstner satt ved vinduet. | A painting artist sat by the window. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | males | Huset må males før vinteren. | The house must be painted before winter. |

| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt malt | Flere bilder har blitt malt til utstillingen. | Several paintings have been created for the exhibition. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked for the assigned painting sense; past is *malte* and past participle is *malt*.

**Notes:** This entry covers *male* 2 (“cover with paint/create a painting/portray”). The reference record also lists *mol*, which belongs to *male* 1 “grind” (a different homonym and sense), not the assigned paint verb; it is excluded here. The verb is transitive in the examples and takes ordinary passive constructions. Examples are original.
