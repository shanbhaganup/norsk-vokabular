## forføre

**English:** to tempt or lure into wrongdoing; to seduce

**Topics:** communication, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å forføre | Han prøvde å forføre vakten med falske løfter. | He tried to tempt the guard with false promises. |
| Presens | forfører | Romanen forfører leseren med et mysterium. | The novel seduces the reader with a mystery. |
| Preteritum | forførte | Hun forførte ham med sin sjarm. | She seduced him with her charm. |
| Perfektum partisipp | forført | Han følte seg forført av løftene. | He felt tempted by the promises. |
| Presens perfektum | har forført | Historien har forført mange lesere. | The story has captivated many readers. |

**Notes:** The dictionary gives both senses: luring someone into something wrong and luring someone into an erotic relationship. Examples cover both literal and figurative uses. The adjectival participle can be understood as 'seduced' or 'deceived' by context.
