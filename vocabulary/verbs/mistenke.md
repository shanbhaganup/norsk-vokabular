## mistenke

**English:** to suspect; to have a suspicion or guess

**Topics:** communication, law

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å mistenke | Politiet begynte å mistenke en av vitnene. | The police began to suspect one of the witnesses. |
| Presens | mistenker | Jeg mistenker at vi har gått feil. | I suspect that we have gone the wrong way. |
| Preteritum | mistenkte | De mistenkte ham for bedrageri. | They suspected him of fraud. |
| Perfektum partisipp | mistenkt | En mistenkt person ble avhørt. | A suspected person was questioned. |
| Presens perfektum | har mistenkt | Hun har mistenkt ham for å skjule noe. | She has suspected him of hiding something. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde mistenkt | Politiet hadde mistenkt sjåføren før de fant bevisene. | The police had suspected the driver before they found the evidence. |
| Futurum | skal mistenke | Du skal ikke mistenke noen uten grunn. | You should not suspect anyone without reason. |
| Futurum | vil mistenke | Jeg vil mistenke en lekkasje hvis lukten kommer tilbake. | I will suspect a leak if the smell returns. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha mistenkt | Innen avhøret er ferdig, skal de ha mistenkt flere personer. | By the end of the interview, they will have suspected several people. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha mistenkt | Innen da vil hun ha mistenkt at noe var galt. | By then, she will have suspected that something was wrong. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville mistenke | Jeg ville mistenke en feil hvis tallene ikke stemte. | I would suspect an error if the figures did not add up. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha mistenkt | De ville ha mistenkt ham hvis han hadde vært der. | They would have suspected him if he had been there. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle mistenke | Hun sa at hun skulle mistenke lekkasje hvis vanntrykket falt. | She said she would suspect a leak if the water pressure dropped. |
| Imperativ | mistenk | Mistenk ikke alle som stiller spørsmål! | Do not suspect everyone who asks questions! |
| Presens partisipp | mistenkende | Det mistenkende blikket gjorde ham ukomfortabel. | The suspicious look made him uncomfortable. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | mistenkes | En person kan mistenkes for å ha lekket opplysningene. | A person can be suspected of leaking the information. |
| S-passiv, presens | mistenkes | Flere personer mistenkes for bedrageri. | Several people are suspected of fraud. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir mistenkt | Han blir mistenkt for å ha tatt pengene. | He is suspected of having taken the money. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble mistenkt | Sjåføren ble mistenkt etter ulykken. | The driver was suspected after the accident. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt mistenkt | Flere ansatte har blitt mistenkt uten bevis. | Several employees have been suspected without evidence. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | mistenkt | En mistenkt kvinne ble avhørt. | A suspected woman was questioned. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | mistenkt | Et mistenkt tilfelle ble undersøkt. | A suspected case was investigated. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | mistenkte | Den mistenkte personen fikk en advokat. | The suspected person received a lawyer. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | mistenkte | De mistenkte personene ble avhørt hver for seg. | The suspected people were questioned separately. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. Transitive “suspect someone/something” allows the illustrated passive and adjectival participle uses.

**Notes:** *Mistenke* means suspect someone or have a suspicion about something; it can take *for* + noun/infinitive or an *at*-clause. Do not confuse the participle *mistenkt* (“suspected”) with the noun phrase *en mistenkt* (“a suspect”); the same form is used in both. Examples are original teaching sentences.
