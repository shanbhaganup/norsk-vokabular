## tiltenke

**English:** to intend for; earmark for

**Topics:** planning

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å tiltenke | De ønsker å tiltenke plassen en annen bruk. | They want to intend the space for another use. |
| Presens | tiltenker | Komiteen tiltenker stipendet unge forskere. | The committee intends the grant for young researchers. |
| Preteritum | tiltenkte | Stipendet var tiltenkt en annen søker. | The grant was intended for another applicant. |
| Perfektum partisipp | tiltenkt | Plassen er tiltenkt ungdomsaktiviteter. | The space is intended for youth activities. |
| Presens perfektum | har tiltenkt | De har tiltenkt bygningen et nytt formål. | They have intended a new purpose for the building. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde tiltenkt | Hun hadde tiltenkt midlene et annet prosjekt. | She had earmarked the funds for another project. |
