## prate

**English:** to chat, talk

**Topics:** communication, relationships

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; *prata/pratet* alternatives are separately illustrated.

**Notes:** *Prate* is generally intransitive and commonly takes *med* for a conversation partner or *om* for a topic. Impersonal passive constructions such as *det prates om ...* are natural. The past participle does not normally form a resultative adjective in the assigned “chat/talk” sense, so adjectival agreement is marked inapplicable. The source groups *ville/skulle* under conditional; *skulle prate* is shown as future viewed from the past, per project grammar conventions. Examples are original.
