## informere

**English:** to inform, notify

**Topics:** communication, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å informere | Det er viktig å informere alle berørte. | It is important to inform everyone affected. |
| Presens | informerer | Læreren informerer foreldrene om turen. | The teacher informs the parents about the trip. |
| Preteritum | informerte | Hun informerte oss om endringen. | She informed us about the change. |
| Perfektum partisipp | informert | De berørte er informert. | Those affected have been informed. |
| Presens perfektum | har informert | Selskapet har informert kundene om forsinkelsen. | The company has informed customers about the delay. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde informert | Vi hadde informert naboene før arbeidet startet. | We had informed the neighbors before the work began. |
| Futurum, plan | skal informere | Jeg skal informere gruppen etter møtet. | I am going to inform the group after the meeting. |
| Futurum, prediksjon | vil informere | Kontoret vil informere deg når saken er behandlet. | The office will inform you when the case has been processed. |
| Futurum perfektum, plan | skal ha informert | Innen fredag skal vi ha informert alle søkerne. | By Friday, we will have informed all the applicants. |
| Futurum perfektum, prediksjon | vil ha informert | Innen lunsj vil hun ha informert hele avdelingen. | By lunch, she will have informed the entire department. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville informere | Jeg ville informere deg hvis jeg visste mer. | I would inform you if I knew more. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha informert | De ville ha informert oss hvis planen hadde endret seg. | They would have informed us if the plan had changed. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle informere | Hun sa at hun skulle informere styret. | She said that she would inform the board. |
| Imperativ | informer | Informer resepsjonen om at du er her! | Inform reception that you are here! |
| Presens partisipp | informerende | Et informerende foredrag ga oss nyttig bakgrunn. | An informative lecture gave us useful background. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | informeres | Alle ansatte må informeres om rutinene. | All employees must be informed about the procedures. |
| S-passiv, presens | informeres | Kundene informeres på e-post. | Customers are informed by email. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir informert | Pasientene blir informert før behandlingen. | The patients are informed before the treatment. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble informert | Familien ble informert samme kveld. | The family was informed that same evening. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt informert | Alle deltakerne har blitt informert om endringen. | All participants have been informed about the change. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | informert | En informert kilde bekreftet opplysningen. | An informed source confirmed the information. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | informert | Et informert valg krever god kunnskap. | An informed choice requires good knowledge. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | informerte | Den informerte kilden ønsket å være anonym. | The informed source wished to remain anonymous. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | informerte | De informerte deltakerne stilte gode spørsmål. | The informed participants asked good questions. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the forms and meaning. Transitive use licenses both S-passive and bli-passive.

**Notes:** The usual construction is *informere noen om noe*. *Informert* also functions as an adjective, as in *en informert kilde* and *informert samtykke*. Examples are original teaching sentences.
