## vite

**English:** to know; to be aware

**Topics:** knowledge

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å vite | Jeg vil vite hva som skjedde. | I want to know what happened. |
| Presens | vet / veit | Vet du hvor nøklene er? / Veit du hvor nøklene er? | Do you know where the keys are? / Do you know where the keys are? |
| Preteritum | visste | Hun visste ikke svaret. | She did not know the answer. |
| Perfektum partisipp | visst | Dette har vært visst lenge. | This has been known for a long time. |
| Presens perfektum | har visst | Jeg har visst det lenge. | I have known it for a long time. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde visst | De hadde visst om endringen før oss. | They had known about the change before we did. |
| Futurum | skal vite / vil vite | Etter møtet skal jeg vite mer. / Jeg vil vite mer om saken. | After the meeting I will know more. / I want to know more about the matter. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; productive constructions and original examples reviewed.

**Notes:** Both accepted present spellings, vet and veit, are illustrated. The S-passive vites is dictionary-listed but formal and mainly impersonal. Bli-passive examples are marked Unverified because natural use in this sense is not established. The example with visst uses a fact-clause complement. Adjectival use of visst/visste as a participle is uncertain and marked Unverified; do not confuse it with the separate adjective/adverb meaning “certain/indeed.” Compound constructions are examples, not dictionary inflections.
