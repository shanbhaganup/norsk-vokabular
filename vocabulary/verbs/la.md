## la

**English:** to let, allow; to have or cause something to be done

**Topics:** communication, general

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked for *la* ‘allow/cause’; S-passive is unverified and personal bli-passive does not apply to the ordinary construction.

**Notes:** The usual construction is *la* + object + infinitive without *å* (*la barna leke*). The source record incorrectly combines the forms of this verb with *lade* ‘load/charge’: *ladde/ladd* and *laende* belong to *lade*, while Ordbøkene gives *lot/latt* and *latende* for this assigned verb. The participial adjective examples are grammatically possible but uncommon; their lexical naturalness merits review. The dictionary gives *latt* in all indefinite genders, with *latte* definite/plural. Passivization is generally avoided in the core allowing/cause senses; the source’s *las* is not confirmed for this dictionary entry.
