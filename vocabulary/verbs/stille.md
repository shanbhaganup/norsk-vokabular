## stille

**English:** to place; to present; to ask or set (a question/condition)

**Topics:** communication, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å stille | Jeg vil stille et spørsmål etter foredraget. | I want to ask a question after the lecture. |
| Presens | stiller | Hun stiller koppen på bordet. | She puts the cup on the table. |
| Preteritum | stilte | Legen stilte en presis diagnose. | The doctor made an accurate diagnosis. |
| Perfektum partisipp | stilt | Kravet er tydelig stilt i brevet. | The requirement is clearly stated in the letter. |
| Presens perfektum | har stilt | Vi har stilt flere spørsmål. | We have asked several questions. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde stilt | Hun hadde stilt stolen ved vinduet før gjestene kom. | She had placed the chair by the window before the guests arrived. |
| Futurum | skal stille / vil stille | Jeg skal stille ut bildene i mai. / De vil stille nye krav. | I am going to exhibit the pictures in May. / They will set new requirements. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha stilt / vil ha stilt | Innen møtet skal hun ha stilt diagnosen. / Innen fredag vil vi ha stilt alt utstyret klart. | By the meeting, she will have made the diagnosis. / By Friday, we will have set all the equipment up. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville stille | Jeg ville stille et spørsmål hvis det var tid. | I would ask a question if there were time. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha stilt | Hun ville ha stilt opp stigen hvis hun hadde hatt hjelp. | She would have set up the ladder if she had had help. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle stille | Han sa at han skulle stille som kandidat. | He said he would stand as a candidate. |
| Imperativ | still | Still klokka før du går. | Set the clock before you leave. |
| Presens partisipp | stillende | Den stillende parten må dokumentere kravet. | The party putting forward the claim must document it. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | stilles | Spørsmålet må stilles på en tydelig måte. | The question must be asked clearly. |
| S-passiv, presens | stilles | Det stilles strenge krav til søkerne. | Strict requirements are placed on the applicants. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir stilt | Utstyret blir stilt i gangen. | The equipment is placed in the hallway. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble stilt | Spørsmålet ble stilt på slutten. | The question was asked at the end. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt stilt | Det har blitt stilt flere diagnoser. | Several diagnoses have been made. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | stilt | En stilt betingelse må være tydelig. | A stated condition must be clear. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | stilt | Et stilt krav må begrunnes. | A requirement that has been made must be justified. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | stilte | Det stilte kravet står i brevet. | The stated requirement is in the letter. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | stilte | De stilte kravene ble diskutert. | The requirements that were made were discussed. |

**Form status:** Simple forms checked against Bokmålsordboka; compound tenses and original examples are authored teaching material.

**Notes:** This is the verb stille (place, put forward, ask/set), not the adjective/adverb stille (“quiet/quietly”) nor the noun stille. Bokmålsordboka has two verb articles with distinct meanings and participle paradigms; this entry follows the verb sense “place/set/put forward” (entry stille 5 V). Dictionary forms for that sense are stilt in neuter and stilte in definite/plural. The masculine/feminine participle form is illustrated with betingelse; other attributive participle uses are context-dependent. Examples are original teaching sentences.
