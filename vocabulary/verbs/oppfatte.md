## oppfatte

**English:** to perceive, hear, understand, or interpret

**Topics:** communication, senses

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; *oppfatta/oppfattet* are accepted past and participle alternatives. Transitive uses allow passive constructions.

**Notes:** *Oppfatte* can mean register something with the senses, understand it, or interpret it in a particular way. The dictionary lists *oppfattende* as present participle, but a natural example in these senses was not established, so the row remains Unverified. Definite/plural participles *oppfattede/oppfattete* are dictionary-listed alternatives; *oppfattede* is more common in formal text. Examples are original.
