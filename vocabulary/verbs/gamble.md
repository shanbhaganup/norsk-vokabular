## gamble

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists the agreeing past-participle spellings gambla/gamblet in the indefinite singular and gambla/gamblede/gamblete in the definite singular and plural. Natural bare attributive examples are unverified for the constructions taught here, where gamble typically takes no direct object and uses med or på. The passive examples are impersonal.
