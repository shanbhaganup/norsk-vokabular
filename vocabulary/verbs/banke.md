# banke

**English:** to knock; to strike

**Topics:** communication

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Form status:** Dictionary-listed forms are retained. Compound tenses and bli-passive are productive constructions.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists banka and banket as past and participle forms. For an actual physical impact, banke can also mean strike or beat; the examples here focus on knocking.
