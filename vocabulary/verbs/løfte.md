## løfte

**English:** to lift, raise; figuratively, to elevate

**Topics:** work, general

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; both *løfta* and *løftet* are accepted. Productive tense and passive constructions illustrated.

**Notes:** The core sense is physically raising something; figurative use includes raising standards or spirits. The verb is transitive in the senses shown, so passive forms apply. The dictionary lists *løftede/løftete* as definite and plural alternatives; the latter is less common. Examples are original teaching sentences.
