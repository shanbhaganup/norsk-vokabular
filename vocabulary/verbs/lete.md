## lete

**English:** to search; to look for

**Topics:** general

**Form status:** Dictionary-listed inflections checked. Passive examples are impersonal and use *lete etter*; adjectival participle use is rare and should be reviewed.

**Notes:** *Lete* is normally used with *etter* or a direction/particle such as *lete opp*, *lete fram*, or *lete seg fram*. Ordbøkene also lists the alternate infinitive *leite* and its own paradigm; this entry records only the assigned lemma *lete* and its forms (*leter, lette, lett, let, letende*). Although the reference snapshot mechanically supplies participial adjective cells, *lett/lette* are indistinguishable from the adjective meaning “easy/light,” and no natural attributive example clearly shows a participle from this intransitive verb; those cells remain Unverified. The passive examples are impersonal constructions.
