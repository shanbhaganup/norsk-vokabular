## advare

**English:** to warn

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å advare | Jeg ringte for å advare deg mot svindelen. | I called to warn you about the scam. |
| Presens | advarer | Kommunen advarer om glatte veier. | The municipality is warning of icy roads. |
| Preteritum | advarte | Naboen advarte oss mot den tynne isen. | The neighbor warned us about the thin ice. |
| Perfektum partisipp | advart | Jeg fikk advart naboen før han dro. | I managed to warn my neighbor before he left. |
| Presens perfektum | har advart | Politiet har advart mot falske meldinger. | The police have warned people about fake messages. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde advart | Hun hadde advart meg før jeg åpnet fila. | She had warned me before I opened the file. |
| Futurum | skal advare | Jeg skal advare de andre før møtet. | I’ll warn the others before the meeting. |
| Futurum | vil advare | Jeg tror politiet vil advare om nye svindelforsøk. | I think the police will warn people about new scam attempts. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha advart | Innen klokka sju skal vi ha advart alle som bor langs elva. | By seven we are to have warned everyone who lives along the river. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha advart | Med dette systemet vil vi ha advart hele bygda innen ti minutter. | With this system we’ll have warned the whole village within ten minutes. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville advare | Jeg ville advare henne hvis jeg visste hvor hun var. | I would warn her if I knew where she was. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha advart | Jeg ville ha advart deg hvis jeg hadde kjent til faren. | I would have warned you if I had known about the danger. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle advare | Hun sa at hun skulle advare de andre. | She said she was going to warn the others. |
| Imperativ | advar | Advar de andre mot den tynne isen! | Warn the others about the thin ice! |
| Presens partisipp | advarende | Hun løftet en advarende finger. | She raised a finger in warning. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | advares | Alle beboerne må advares før vannet stiger. | All residents must be warned before the water rises. |
| S-passiv, presens | advares | Det advares mot kraftige vindkast langs kysten. | Warnings are being issued for strong gusts along the coast. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir advart | Nye elever blir advart mot å dele passordet sitt. | New students are warned against sharing their passwords. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble advart | Vi ble advart om rasfaren. | We were warned about the risk of landslides. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt advart | Beboerne har blitt advart om flomfaren. | The residents have been warned about the risk of flooding. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | advart | En advart syklist kan velge en annen rute. | A cyclist who has been warned can choose another route. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | advart | Et advart publikum reagerer raskere når alarmen går. | An audience that has been warned reacts faster when the alarm goes off. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | advarte | Den advarte bilisten snudde før brua. | The driver who had been warned turned around before the bridge. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | advarte | De advarte turgåerne valgte en tryggere sti. | The hikers who had been warned chose a safer path. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the simple forms and participial agreement. Compound tenses and passive rows illustrate productive constructions. The bilingual examples are original.

**Notes:** Source spelling: advare. Use advare noen mot noe for warning someone against something, or advare om noe for warning of a danger. Deverbal adjectival agreement is possible but often sounds formal.
