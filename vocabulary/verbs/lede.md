## lede

**English:** to lead, guide, direct; to conduct electricity or heat; to be in the lead

**Topics:** work, education, science, sport

**Form status:** Headword, senses, and dictionary forms checked; tense and passive constructions are productive.

**Notes:** This entry is the verb *lede*, “guide/direct/lead,” and is distinct from the noun *lede* in the reference snapshot. Both *leda* and *ledet* are accepted past and past-participle forms. Definite/plural participles *ledede* and *ledete* are listed alternatives; *ledete* is less common and retained with a usage note. Other senses include conducting heat/electricity and being ahead in a competition. Examples are original teaching sentences.
