## påvirke

**English:** to influence, affect

**Topics:** society, health

**Notes:** This entry covers “have an influence on/affect.” The dictionary also uses *påvirket* adjectivally to mean “under the influence/intoxicated”; that distinct adjectival reading is noted, while examples primarily use “affected.” Examples are original teaching material.
