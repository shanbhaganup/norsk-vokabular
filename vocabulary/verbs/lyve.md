**Form status:** Infinitive, present, and imperative verified. Ordbøkene's paradigm combines several near-synonymous infinitives and does not supply past or perfect forms specifically under *lyve*; these cells remain Unverified.

**Notes:** The verb means to say something known to be false and can also describe something giving a false impression. Ordbøkene groups *lyve* with *juge, ljuge,* and *lyge*, but its displayed *løy/løyet* paradigm belongs to *ljuge*; the reference record appears to import those forms into *lyve*. No past, participle, perfect, passive, or present-participle form is guessed here. The dictionary lists *lyvende* in the combined paradigm, but its attachment to *lyve* rather than another alternate is not explicit, so it remains Unverified. Check before promoting these missing forms.
