## nekte

**English:** to refuse, deny, or refuse to admit

**Topics:** communication, law

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å nekte | Han valgte å nekte å svare på spørsmålet. | He chose to refuse to answer the question. |
| Presens | nekter | Hun nekter å gi opp. | She refuses to give up. |
| Preteritum | nekta | De nekta oss adgang til bygningen. | They refused us entry to the building. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; *nekta/nektet* are accepted past and participle alternatives. Passive forms apply to the transitive sense “deny someone something.”

**Notes:** *Nekte å gjøre noe* means refuse to do something; *nekte for noe* means deny something; *nekte noen adgang/servering* means deny someone entry/service. These patterns have different complements. Definite/plural *nektede/nektete* are dictionary-listed; *nektede* is more common in formal writing. Examples are original.
