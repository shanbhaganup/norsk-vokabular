**Notes:** This entry covers feeding a person or animal. Bokmålsordboka accepts matet; mata is also an accepted Bokmål alternative and is illustrated in each applicable listed alternative. The dictionary’s “supply a machine” sense is outside this entry. Examples are original teaching sentences.
