## rusle

**English:** to stroll; to walk slowly

**Topics:** travel, daily life

**Form status:** The source combines *rusle* 1 (“make a faint rustling sound”) and *rusle* 2 (“walk slowly / potter”). This entry follows the assigned “stroll” sense and uses the latter entry’s *rusla/ruslet* paradigm, including imperative alternatives. The dictionary lists participial adjective forms, but their natural use in this sense could not be confirmed, so those uses are Unverified. Bli-passive is inapplicable to this intransitive motion sense.

**Notes:** *Rusle med noe* can mean to potter or occupy oneself with something. The other homonym for making a faint sound has a different sense and is excluded. Examples are original.
