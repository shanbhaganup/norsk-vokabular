## vises

**English:** to be visible; to be seen

**Topics:** perception

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

**Form status:** The dictionary verifies only å vises, vises, vistes and har vistes. Other listed constructions are Unverified.

**Notes:** This is the standalone Bokmålsordboka headword *vises*, “be visible; can be seen,” separate from *vise* “show.” The imported workbook supplies additional compounds and an imperative, but the dictionary does not; they are not inferred as ordinary active forms. Future and conditional rows show productive auxiliary constructions and need separate usage review. The imported English gloss “appear” is narrowed to the dictionary sense.
