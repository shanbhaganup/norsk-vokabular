# be

**English:** to ask; to request; to pray

**Topics:** general / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | be | Hun vil be om hjelp. | She wants to ask for help. |
| Presens | ber | Jeg ber om et tydelig svar. | I am asking for a clear answer. |
| Preteritum | ba / bad | De ba om mer tid. / De bad om mer tid. | They asked for more time. / They asked for more time. |
| Perfektum partisipp | bedt | Vi har bedt om innsyn. | We have requested access. |
| Presens perfektum | har bedt | Hun har bedt om en forklaring. | She has asked for an explanation. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde bedt | Han hadde bedt om hjelp før møtet. | He had asked for help before the meeting. |
| Futurum | skal be / vil be | Jeg skal be om råd. / Jeg vil be om råd. | I am going to ask for advice. / I will ask for advice. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha bedt / vil ha bedt | Innen fredag skal hun ha bedt om svar. / Innen fredag vil hun ha bedt om svar. | By Friday, she will have asked for an answer. / By Friday, she will have asked for an answer. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville be / skulle be | Jeg ville be om unnskyldning. / Jeg skulle be om unnskyldning. | I would ask for forgiveness. / I was supposed to ask for forgiveness. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha bedt / skulle ha bedt | Han ville ha bedt om hjelp. / Han skulle ha bedt om hjelp. | He would have asked for help. / He was supposed to have asked for help. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle be | Hun sa at hun skulle be om støtte. | She said that she would ask for support. |
| Imperativ | be | Be om hjelp hvis du trenger det! | Ask for help if you need it! |
| Presens partisipp | beende | Hun satt med beende hender ved senga. | She sat with praying hands by the bed. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | bes | Det kan bes om innsyn i dokumentene. | Access to the documents can be requested. |
| S-passiv, presens | bes | Det bes om at alle møter presis. | Everyone is asked to arrive on time. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir bedt | Hun blir bedt om å vente utenfor. | She is asked to wait outside. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble bedt | De ble bedt om å sende inn skjemaet. | They were asked to submit the form. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt bedt | Han har blitt bedt om å forklare valget. | He has been asked to explain the choice. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | bedt | En bedt deltaker fikk ordet. | A person who had been asked to speak was given the floor. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | bedt | Et bedt vitne møtte opp. | A witness who had been asked to attend showed up. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | bedte | Den bedte gjesten kom tidlig. | The invited guest arrived early. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | bedte | De bedte vitnene møtte i retten. | The requested witnesses appeared in court. |

**Form status:** Dictionary lists the simple forms and passive bes. Participial adjective forms are rare and require usage review.

**Notes:** The reference gloss “to be” is incorrect for this Norwegian lemma. Bokmålsordboka defines be as “ask/request” and “pray.” Passive bes is formal, often used impersonally. The adjective-like participle examples are included for review because this use is uncommon.
