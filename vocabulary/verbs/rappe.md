**Notes:** The source English gloss says “to rap,” but the cited Bokmålsordboka entry *rappe 1* means “slå/smekke” and gives *rappe til noen*. The examples and English gloss here follow the verified dictionary sense “slap/smack”; they do not teach performing rap music. The source gloss may be an imported translation error and should be reviewed during indexing. The dictionary lists adjectival participle forms, but a natural resultative adjective example was not established for this sense, so those rows are marked inapplicable. Examples are original.
