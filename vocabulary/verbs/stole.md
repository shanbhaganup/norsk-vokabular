**English:** to trust; to rely on

**Topics:** relationships, work

**Form status:** Simple forms checked against Bokmålsordboka; compound tenses and original examples are authored teaching material.

**Notes:** Normally used in the fixed construction stole på (“trust/rely on”). The dictionary entry supplies the phrase and sense; the listed paradigm is stolte/stolt. Passives and resultative adjectival participles are not applicable to this intransitive construction. Adjectival participle agreement is marked inapplicable because the verb sense stole på does not yield a natural attributive resultative adjective; use an active clause.
