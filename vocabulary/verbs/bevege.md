## bevege

**English:** to move; to affect emotionally; to persuade

**Topics:** movement, emotions, communication

**Form status:** Simple forms and participle alternatives verified in Bokmålsordboka; compound constructions are productive examples.

**Notes:** Transitive senses allow passive constructions. *Bevege seg* is also used reflexively for moving oneself. *Hadde beveg(d)* is included as Unverified only to distinguish it from *hadde bevegd*; use the listed dictionary participles. Adjectival forms are included as shown in the dictionary; some are uncommon.
