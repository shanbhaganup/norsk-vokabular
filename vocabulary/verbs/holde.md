## holde

**English:** to hold; keep or maintain

**Topics:** everyday actions, communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å holde | Hun lærte å holde en baby trygt. | She learned to hold a baby safely. |
| Presens | holder | Han holder koppen med begge hendene. | He is holding the cup with both hands. |
| Preteritum | holdt | De holdt døren åpen for oss. | They held the door open for us. |
| Perfektum partisipp | holdt | Døren er holdt åpen med en kile. | The door is held open with a wedge. |
| Presens perfektum | har holdt | Vi har holdt kontakten i mange år. | We have kept in touch for many years. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde holdt | Hun hadde holdt barnet i hånden hele veien. | She had held the child's hand the whole way. |
| Futurum, plan | skal holde | Jeg skal holde et kort innlegg i morgen. | I am going to give a short talk tomorrow. |
| Futurum, prediksjon/vilje | vil holde | Dette batteriet vil holde hele dagen. | This battery will last all day. |
| Futurum perfektum, plan | skal ha holdt | Innen klokken tre skal vi ha holdt alle intervjuene. | By three o'clock, we will have conducted all the interviews. |
| Futurum perfektum, prediksjon | vil ha holdt | Om en time vil hun ha holdt på i seks timer. | In an hour, she will have kept going for six hours. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville holde | Jeg ville holde talen selv hvis jeg turte. | I would give the speech myself if I dared. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and inflection forms checked. The verb is transitive in the cited physical-holding, keeping and event-conducting uses. Productive compound tenses and passive forms are illustrated.

**Notes:** *Holde* has many senses and idioms. This entry illustrates holding, keeping something in a state, and conducting an event. The dictionary lists *holdte* in definite and plural adjectival participle cells; that attributive form is comparatively uncommon, so the example is included as a transparent teaching use. Examples are original.
