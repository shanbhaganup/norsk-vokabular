## tilhøre

**English:** to belong to; to be part of

**Topics:** general

**Notes:** The entry covers the reference sense “belong.” The verb is intransitive with an indirect/possessive complement, so passive constructions are inapplicable. The dictionary lists tilhør! as the imperative; it is grammatically possible but pragmatically unusual, so the example uses a direct command in a context of group membership. The participle tilhørt is used in perfect tenses; adjectival passive uses are not established here and are marked Unverified.
