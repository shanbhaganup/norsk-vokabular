## forfølge

**English:** to pursue; to chase; to persecute; to continue pursuing (a goal or idea); to prosecute

**Topics:** communication, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å forfølge | De bestemte seg for å forfølge saken videre. | They decided to pursue the case further. |
| Presens | forfølger | Hunden forfølger haren gjennom skogen. | The dog chases the hare through the forest. |
| Preteritum | forfulgte | Regimet forfulgte flere journalister. | The regime persecuted several journalists. |
| Perfektum partisipp | forfulgt | Han følte seg forfulgt av uflaks. | He felt pursued by bad luck. |
| Presens perfektum | har forfulgt | Hun har forfulgt målet sitt i mange år. | She has pursued her goal for many years. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde forfulgt | Politiet hadde forfulgt bilen før den stanset. | The police had pursued the car before it stopped. |
| Futurum, plan | skal forfølge | Advokaten skal forfølge saken i retten. | The lawyer is going to pursue the case in court. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms and senses verified; compound tenses and passive constructions are productive constructions. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The dictionary distinguishes following/chasing to capture, following and harassing or persecuting, continuing work on an idea or goal, and pursuing a case in court. Examples illustrate these meanings. English glosses are reviewed interpretations, not dictionary-verified translations.
