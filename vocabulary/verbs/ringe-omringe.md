**Form status:** This is *ringe* 4. The past and participle spellings *ringa* and *ringet* are listed in the dictionary; adjectival definite/plural forms also include *ringede* and *ringete*. Present participle is Unverified.

**Notes:** This is the homonym meaning “put a ring on” or “surround,” not the verb for bells/telephone. It has its own Ordbøkene entry and paradigm. The source also gives the reflexive construction *ringe seg* (“form a ring/coil up”). Examples are original.
