**Notes:** Fange is transitive in both literal “catch/capture” and figurative “capture/grip” uses. The reference lists fanga/fanget for past and participle, and fanga/fangede/fangete for definite singular and plural participial adjective forms. Illustrations cover each alternative, but the exact adjectival alternatives should be checked against the current dictionary before treating this paradigm as fully verified. English “to catch” alone does not convey the figurative senses. Examples are authored teaching examples.
