## meddele

**English:** to inform; to communicate or announce formally

**Topics:** communication, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å meddele | Vi må meddele kundene endringen. | We must inform the customers of the change. |
| Presens | meddeler | Ordføreren meddeler resultatet etter møtet. | The mayor announces the result after the meeting. |
| Preteritum | meddelte | Hun meddelte nyheten til familien. | She informed the family of the news. |
| Perfektum partisipp | meddelt | Beslutningen er meddelt skriftlig. | The decision has been communicated in writing. |
| Presens perfektum | har meddelt | De har meddelt at butikken stenger tidligere. | They have announced that the shop is closing earlier. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde meddelt | Vi hadde meddelt gjestene om tidsendringen. | We had informed the guests about the time change. |
| Futurum | skal meddele | Direktøren skal meddele avgjørelsen i morgen. | The director will announce the decision tomorrow. |
| Futurum | vil meddele | Skolen vil meddele foreldrene at møtet er flyttet. | The school will inform the parents that the meeting has been moved. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha meddelt | Innen klokka to skal de ha meddelt alle søkerne. | By two o’clock, they will have informed all the applicants. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha meddelt | Innen fredag vil hun ha meddelt resultatet til gruppa. | By Friday, she will have communicated the result to the group. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville meddele | Jeg ville meddele beslutningen personlig hvis jeg kunne. | I would communicate the decision in person if I could. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha meddelt | Han ville ha meddelt dem om faren hvis han hadde visst det. | He would have informed them of the danger if he had known. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle meddele | Hun sa at hun skulle meddele svaret senere. | She said that she would communicate the answer later. |
| Imperativ | meddel | Meddel svaret skriftlig innen fristen! | Communicate the answer in writing by the deadline! |
| Presens partisipp | meddelende | Det meddelende organet publiserte en kort uttalelse. | The notifying body published a brief statement. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | meddeles | Resultatet må meddeles alle deltakerne. | The result must be communicated to all participants. |
| S-passiv, presens | meddeles | Avgjørelsen meddeles skriftlig. | The decision is communicated in writing. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir meddelt | Endringen blir meddelt på nettsiden. | The change is announced on the website. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble meddelt | Nyheten ble meddelt familien samme kveld. | The news was communicated to the family that same evening. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt meddelt | Resultatet har blitt meddelt alle søkerne. | The result has been communicated to all the applicants. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, m./f. ubestemt | meddelt | En meddelt beslutning kan ikke holdes hemmelig. | A communicated decision cannot be kept secret. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, intetkjønn | meddelt | Et meddelt svar sto i brevet. | A communicated answer was in the letter. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, bestemt entall | meddelte | Den meddelte avgjørelsen gjaldt fra mandag. | The communicated decision applied from Monday. |
| Partisipp som adjektiv, flertall | meddelte | De meddelte opplysningene ble kontrollert. | The communicated details were checked. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; the verb is transitive and the passive constructions are applicable.

**Notes:** *Meddele* is formal compared with *si fra*, *fortelle*, or *informere*. The common sense is inform/communicate; it can also mean grant or give in formal contexts, and *meddele seg* means express oneself. Examples are original and use the formal register of the verb.
