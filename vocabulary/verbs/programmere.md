## programmere

**English:** to program, write software for

**Topics:** technology, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å programmere | Hun vil lære å programmere en enkel robot. | She wants to learn to program a simple robot. |
| Presens | programmerer | Jeg programmerer en nettside for skolen. | I am programming a website for the school. |
| Preteritum | programmerte | De programmerte sensoren til å slå seg av om natten. | They programmed the sensor to switch off at night. |
| Perfektum partisipp | programmert | Systemet er programmert til å varsle ved feil. | The system is programmed to alert when there is an error. |
| Presens perfektum | har programmert | Han har programmert flere små spill. | He has programmed several small games. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde programmert | Vi hadde programmert roboten før konkurransen startet. | We had programmed the robot before the competition began. |
| Futurum | skal programmere | Klassen skal programmere en liten bil i dag. | The class is going to program a small car today. |
| Futurum | vil programmere | Utvikleren vil programmere en ny funksjon. | The developer will program a new feature. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha programmert | Innen fredag skal hun ha programmert hele løsningen. | By Friday, she will have programmed the whole solution. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha programmert | Innen sommeren vil teamet ha programmert prototypen. | By summer, the team will have programmed the prototype. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville programmere | Jeg ville programmere et verktøy hvis jeg kunne nok. | I would program a tool if I knew enough. |

| Konstruksjon | programmere noe til å gjøre noe | Vi programmerte appen til å sende en påminnelse. | We programmed the app to send a reminder. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; compound constructions and examples are original.

**Notes:** The dictionary sense is configuring a program, especially a computer, to process data or perform an operation. The productive pattern *programmere noe til å gjøre noe* is shown separately from inflection. Examples are original teaching material.
