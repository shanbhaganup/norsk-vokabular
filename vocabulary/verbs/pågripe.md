## pågripe

**English:** to arrest, apprehend

**Topics:** society, law

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. The source lists *pågripende* as present participle, but usage in this arrest sense remains Unverified.

**Notes:** *Pågripe* is transitive and means arrest or apprehend. The reference record notes the source spelling/form *pågrepe*; the verified Bokmål lemma is *pågripe*, with the past alternatives *pågreip/pågrep*. The dictionary lists *pågrepen* and *pågrepet* as masculine/feminine singular participles; *pågrepen* is a less common alternative, retained here with a separate example. Examples are original teaching material.
