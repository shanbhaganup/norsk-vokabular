## huske

**English:** to remember, recall; to keep in mind

**Topics:** memory, communication

**Notes:** This is the memory verb, article 24687. The reference record also links article 24686, which is a different verb homonym meaning to move or rock up and down. That separate sense is not included here. The attributive participle forms are dictionary-listed but can sound marked; prefer ordinary relative clauses where naturalness is uncertain. *Huske på* means to remember/keep in mind.
