## gjengjelde

**English:** to reciprocate, return a similar action or feeling

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å gjengjelde | Det er vanskelig å gjengjelde en slik tjeneste. | It is difficult to return such a favor. |
| Presens | gjengjelder | Hun gjengjelder alltid vennlighet med vennlighet. | She always repays kindness with kindness. |
| Preteritum | gjengjeldte | Laget gjengjeldte tapet med en seier uka etter. | The team responded to the defeat with a win the following week. |
| Perfektum partisipp | gjengjeldt | Han har gjengjeldt invitasjonen med en middag hjemme. | He has returned the invitation with a dinner at his home. |
| Presens perfektum | har gjengjeldt | Hun har gjengjeldt hjelpen flere ganger. | She has repaid the help several times. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde gjengjeldt | De hadde gjengjeldt støtten før prosjektet var ferdig. | They had returned the support before the project was finished. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the headword, inflection and participle forms. Compound tenses, reflexive use, and applicable passive constructions are illustrated as productive constructions. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Usually takes a direct object. The s-passive is uncommon, especially in ordinary conversation. The passive examples use formal impersonal contexts. Gjenngjeldt is the invariant perfect participle; gjengjeldte is its definite and plural adjectival form.
