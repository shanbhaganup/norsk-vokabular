## protestere

**English:** to protest, object

**Topics:** society, communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å protestere | Flere ansatte valgte å protestere mot beslutningen. | Several employees chose to protest the decision. |
| Presens | protesterer | Hun protesterer mot behandlingen av saken. | She is protesting the way the case is being handled. |
| Preteritum | protesterte | Naboene protesterte mot den nye veien. | The neighbors protested the new road. |
| Perfektum partisipp | protestert | De har protestert mot forslaget. | They have protested against the proposal. |
| Presens perfektum | har protestert | Arbeiderne har protestert flere ganger. | The workers have protested several times. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde protestert | Hun hadde protestert før møtet ble avbrutt. | She had protested before the meeting was interrupted. |
| Futurum | skal protestere | Vi skal protestere dersom vedtaket blir stående. | We will protest if the decision stands. |
| Futurum | vil protestere | Studentene vil protestere mot de nye reglene. | The students will protest the new rules. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha protestert | Innen møtet begynner, skal gruppa ha protestert skriftlig. | By the time the meeting starts, the group will have protested in writing. |
| Futurum perfektum | vil ha protestert | Innen fredag vil flere organisasjoner ha protestert mot planen. | By Friday, several organizations will have protested the plan. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville protestere | Jeg ville protestere hvis avgjørelsen var urettferdig. | I would protest if the decision were unfair. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha protestert | De ville ha protestert tidligere hvis de hadde kjent til endringen. | They would have protested earlier if they had known about the change. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle protestere | Hun sa at hun skulle protestere på vegne av gruppa. | She said she would protest on behalf of the group. |
| Imperativ | protester | Protester fredelig mot forslaget. | Protest peacefully against the proposal. |
| Presens partisipp | protesterende | De protesterende innbyggerne samlet seg utenfor rådhuset. | The protesting residents gathered outside the town hall. |
| S-passiv, infinitiv | protesteres | Det må protesteres mot slike vilkår. | One must protest against such terms. |
| S-passiv, presens | protesteres | Det protesteres mot vedtaket i flere kommuner. | People are protesting the decision in several municipalities. |
| Bli-passiv, presens | blir protestert | Det blir protestert mot endringen på møtet. | People are protesting the change at the meeting. |
| Bli-passiv, preteritum | ble protestert | Det ble protestert utenfor bygningen hele dagen. | People protested outside the building all day. |
| Bli-passiv, perfektum | har blitt protestert | Det har blitt protestert mot ordningen i lang tid. | People have protested the arrangement for a long time. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; passive examples use impersonal passive constructions. Resultative participle adjective forms are inapplicable to the assigned intransitive sense.

**Notes:** *Protestere* is normally intransitive and takes *mot* before the issue being opposed. The dictionary also lists a formal financial sense, “protest a bill”; this entry focuses on the common “object/oppose” meaning. Examples are original teaching material.
