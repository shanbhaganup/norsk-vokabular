**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; *redda/reddet* alternatives and adjectival participle alternatives are separately illustrated.

**Notes:** *Redde* means bring someone or something to safety and also, in ball games, prevent the ball from going into the goal. The dictionary lists *redda/reddet* for past and participle forms and *redda/reddede/reddete* for definite and plural adjectival participles. Examples are original teaching material.
