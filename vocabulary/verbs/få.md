## få

**English:** to receive, get, obtain; to be allowed or able to; to manage to cause or accomplish

**Topics:** everyday life, communication, health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å få | Jeg håper å få svar snart. | I hope to get an answer soon. |
| Presens | får | Hun får besøk i kveld. | She is having visitors tonight. |
| Preteritum | fikk | Vi fikk en hyggelig melding. | We got a pleasant message. |
| Perfektum partisipp | fått | De har fått nye naboer. | They have got new neighbors. |
| Presens perfektum | har fått | Jeg har fått vondt i ryggen. | I have developed back pain. |
| Preteritum perfektum | hadde fått | Hun hadde fått tillatelse før hun dro. | She had received permission before she left. |
| Futurum | skal få / vil få | Du skal få beskjed i morgen. / Jeg tror han vil få jobben. | You will be given notice tomorrow. / I think he will get the job. |
| Futurum perfektum | skal ha fått / vil ha fått | Innen fredag skal de ha fått svar. / Innen fredag vil de ha fått svar. | By Friday they are expected to have received an answer. / By Friday they will have received an answer. |
| Kondisjonalis | ville få | Hun ville få bedre plass med et mindre bord. | She would have more room with a smaller table. |
| Kondisjonalis perfektum | ville ha fått | Jeg ville ha fått pakken hvis jeg hadde vært hjemme. | I would have received the package if I had been home. |
| Framtid sett fra fortiden | skulle få | Han sa at vi skulle få resultatet dagen etter. | He said we would get the result the next day. |

**Form status:** Dictionary paradigm verified; compound tense forms are productive. Passive constructions beyond dictionary-listed `fås` remain unverified.

**Notes:** The selected entry includes receiving/obtaining, being permitted or able to do something, and managing to cause or complete something. `Fått` and `fåtte` are listed as adjectival participle forms, but natural attributive uses were not verified and remain Unverified. `Fående` is listed in the dictionary but is rarely usable as a present participle; it remains Unverified. The dictionary records impersonal/passive `fås`, not ordinary `bli fått`. Do not read the dictionary-verified Norwegian paradigm as verification of the English gloss.
