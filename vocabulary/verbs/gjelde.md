## gjelde

**English:** to apply, be valid, or concern.

**Topics:** General / unassigned

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the simple forms and participle spellings. Compound tenses and applicable passives are productive constructions. Unverified marks usage for which no natural teaching example was confirmed. Examples are original.

**Notes:** The snapshot conflates gjelde I with gjelde II, “to castrate.” Gjelda, gjeldet and gjeldte as finite past forms belong to gjelde II. In the validity sense, gjelds ikke is a fixed expression meaning “doesn't count” rather than a regular present passive. Passive constructions are inapplicable to the validity sense taught here. The snapshot's regular passive gjeldes is unverified for the assigned sense. Bokmålsordboka lists the imperative gjeld and agreeing past participles gjeldt/gjeldte, but natural examples for these uses in the assigned sense are unverified. The skulle row shows future viewed from the past.
