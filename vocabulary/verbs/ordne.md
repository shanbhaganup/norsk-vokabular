## ordne

**English:** to arrange, organize

**Topics:** home, work, administration

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; compound constructions and examples are original.

**Notes:** Both *ordna* and *ordnet* are accepted past and participle forms; *ordna* is the -a variant. The dictionary also lists imperative alternatives *ordn* and *ordne*, and definite/plural adjectival participle alternatives *ordna*, *ordnede* and *ordnete*; each is illustrated separately. In participial adjective rows, the examples show agreement/attributive use; some examples are intentionally less common, so ordinary prose often favors an active adjective such as *ryddig*. Examples are original teaching material.
