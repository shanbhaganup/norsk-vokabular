## savne

**English:** to miss; to lack; to notice something is missing

**Topics:** emotions, daily life

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å savne | Jeg kommer til å savne familien min. | I am going to miss my family. |
| Presens | savner | Hun savner vennene sine. | She misses her friends. |
| Preteritum | savna | Vi savna den gamle kafeen etter at den stengte. | We missed the old café after it closed. |

**Form status:** The dictionary lists *savnet/savna* alternatives and definite/plural participles *savna, savnede,* and *savnete*. The present participle is left Unverified for the assigned senses.

**Notes:** The dictionary distinguishes feeling the absence of someone or something, lacking something, and noticing that something has gone missing. It also lists *sakne* as a related alternative lemma; this entry retains the assigned headword *savne*. Examples are original.
