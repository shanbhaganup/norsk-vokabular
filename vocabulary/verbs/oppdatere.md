## oppdatere

**English:** to update; to bring up to date; to inform about the latest developments

**Topics:** technology, communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Infinitiv | å oppdatere | Husk å oppdatere programmet før bruk. | Remember to update the program before using it. |

| Preteritum | oppdaterte | De oppdaterte registeret i går. | They updated the register yesterday. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. The verb is transitive and permits the listed passive constructions; present-participle use is not confirmed as natural.

**Notes:** Meanings include bringing records or software up to date, adding new online content, and informing someone about the latest developments. The dictionary lists *oppdaterende* as a present participle, but a natural example in the assigned senses was not established, so it remains Unverified. Examples are original teaching material.
