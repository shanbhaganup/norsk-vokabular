## for

**English:** for; because

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Vi tok bussen, for det regnet ute. | We took the bus because it was raining. |
