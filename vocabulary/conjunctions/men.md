## men

**English:** but

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | konjunksjon | Hun ville bli, men hun måtte gå. | She wanted to stay, but she had to leave. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Han sa ja, men ombestemte seg. | He said yes but changed his mind. |
| Konstruksjon, ord | adjektiv + men + adjektiv | Rommet var lite, men lyst. | The room was small but bright. |
| Konstruksjon, helsetninger | helsetning + men + helsetning | Jeg ringte, men ingen svarte. | I called, but nobody answered. |
