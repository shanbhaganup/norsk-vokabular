## enten

**English:** either

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | konjunksjon | Vi tar enten buss eller tog. | We’ll take either the bus or the train. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Du kan enten ringe eller skrive. | You can either call or write. |
| Konstruksjon | enten … eller | Enten vi går nå eller vi venter, må vi ta med lykta. | Whether we leave now or wait, we need to bring the torch. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Enten typically pairs with eller in alternatives.
