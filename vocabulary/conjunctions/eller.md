## eller

**English:** or

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | konjunksjon | Vil du ha te eller kaffe? | Would you like tea or coffee? |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Vi kan gå eller ta bussen. | We can walk or take the bus. |
| Konstruksjon | verken … eller | Han drikker verken te eller kaffe. | He drinks neither tea nor coffee. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Eller links alternatives. In verken … eller it corresponds to ‘nor’.
