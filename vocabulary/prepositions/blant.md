## blant

**English:** among

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Hun fant nøklene blant papirene. | She found the keys among the papers. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Han trives blant venner. | He feels at ease among friends. |
| Konstruksjon | blant + substantivfrase | Det var flere nybegynnere blant deltakerne. | There were several beginners among the participants. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Blant introduces the group within which something is found or considered.
