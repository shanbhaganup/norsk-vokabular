## for

**English:** for; on behalf of

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Jeg laget mat for gjestene. | I made food for the guests. |
| Mottaker eller formål | for | Jeg laget mat for gjestene. | I made food for the guests. |
| På vegne av | for | Jeg svarte for gruppa. | I answered on behalf of the group. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** The source also glosses the coordinating conjunction for as “because”. These examples cover the preposition. NoW source form: “for”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
