## ovenfra

**English:** from above

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: ovenfra | Vi hørte et dunk ovenfra. | We heard a thud from above. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: ovafra | Det dryppet vann ovafra. | Water was dripping from above. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: ovenifra | Vi fotograferte bordet ovenifra. | We photographed the table from above. |
| Konstruksjon med utfylling | ovenfra + substantivfrase | Smeltevannet kom ovenfra fjellet. | The meltwater came from up on the mountain. |

**Form status:** Spelling and usage checked in Bokmålsordboka. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists ovenfra, ovafra and ovenifra as accepted spellings. Each is invariable. The examples illustrate a higher physical point of origin.
