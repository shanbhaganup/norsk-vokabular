## under

**English:** under, below, during

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Katten ligger under bordet. | The cat is lying under the table. |
| Konstruksjon, mengde | under + mengde | Billetten kostet under hundre kroner. | The ticket cost less than a hundred kroner. |
| Konstruksjon, tid | under + hendelse | Ingen snakket under filmen. | Nobody spoke during the film. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The examples illustrate location, a lower limit and time.
