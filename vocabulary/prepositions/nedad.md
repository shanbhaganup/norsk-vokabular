## nedad

**English:** down along

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Vannet rant nedad fjellsida. | The water ran down the mountainside. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Vannet fortsatte nedad skråningen. | The water continued down the slope. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Nedad can take a complement naming the route downwards. This usage is less common than ned.
