## bortover

**English:** along

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: bortover | Vi syklet bortover grusveien. | We cycled along the gravel road. |
| Retning uten utfylling | bortover | Barna løp bortover mens jeg låste porten. | The children ran on while I locked the gate. |
