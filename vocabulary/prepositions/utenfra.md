## utenfra

**English:** from outside

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Det kom rop utenfra huset. | Shouts came from outside the house. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | De fikk hjelp utenfra avdelingen. | They received help from outside the department. |
| Konstruksjon | utenfra + substantivfrase | Et lys skinte utenfra vinduet. | A light shone from outside the window. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** In prepositional use the following noun phrase gives the boundary or place.
