## uansett

**English:** regardless of

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Vi møtes uansett vær. | We’ll meet regardless of the weather. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Alle kan delta uansett alder. | Everyone can take part regardless of age. |
| Konstruksjon | uansett + substantivfrase | Prisen er den samme uansett størrelse. | The price is the same regardless of size. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The preposition takes a complement specifying what has no effect.
