## ved

**English:** at; by; near; by means of

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Sykkelen står ved døra. | The bike is by the door. |
| Sted | ved | Sykkelen står ved døra. | The bike is by the door. |
| Tid | ved | Toget kommer ved midnatt. | The train arrives around midnight. |
| Middel | ved å + infinitiv | Du kan spare tid ved å ta toget. | You can save time by taking the train. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Marks proximity, a point in time, or the means of achieving something. NoW source form: “ved”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
