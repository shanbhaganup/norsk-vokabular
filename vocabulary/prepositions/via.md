## via

**English:** via, through

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Vi fløy til Roma via Frankfurt. | We flew to Rome via Frankfurt. |
| Konstruksjon, kanal | via + substantivfrase | Jeg fikk beskjed via e-post. | I was notified by email. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Via introduces a route or channel.
