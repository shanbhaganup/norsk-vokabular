## om

**English:** about, in

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | De snakket om været. | They talked about the weather. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Jeg leser ei bok om fugler. | I’m reading a book about birds. |
| Konstruksjon, tid | om + tidsmål | Bussen går om ti minutter. | The bus leaves in ten minutes. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The preposition can introduce a topic or indicate how far in the future something will happen.
