## i

**English:** in, inside, for

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Melka står i kjøleskapet. | The milk is in the fridge. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Han bor i Bergen. | He lives in Bergen. |
| Konstruksjon, varighet | i + tidsmål | Vi ventet i to timer. | We waited for two hours. |

**Notes:** The examples illustrate location and duration.
