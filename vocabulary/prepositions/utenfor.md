## utenfor

**English:** outside

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Vi ventet utenfor bygningen. | We waited outside the building. |
| Konstruksjon, sted | utenfor + substantivfrase | Hun parkerte utenfor skolen. | She parked outside the school. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The complement identifies a boundary or place.
