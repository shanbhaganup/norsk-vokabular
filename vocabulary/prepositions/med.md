## med

**English:** with

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Hun kom med en venn. | She came with a friend. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Jeg vil ha kaffe med melk. | I’d like coffee with milk. |
| Konstruksjon, redskap | med + substantivfrase | Skjær brødet med den store kniven. | Cut the bread with the large knife. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The examples illustrate company and an instrument.
