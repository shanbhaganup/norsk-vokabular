## oppover

**English:** upwards, up

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: oppover | Vi bar eskene oppover trappa. | We carried the boxes up the stairs. |
| Retning uten utfylling | oppover | Ballongen steg oppover til den forsvant bak skyene. | The balloon rose until it disappeared behind the clouds. |

**Form status:** Spelling and usage checked in Bokmålsordboka. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The snapshot gives the source label ADV. These examples show movement toward a higher point. The same spelling is used with and without a complement.
