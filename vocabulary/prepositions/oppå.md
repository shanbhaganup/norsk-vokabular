## oppå

**English:** on top of

**Topics:** directions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Vottene ligger oppå skapet. | The mittens are on top of the cupboard. |
| Sted | oppå | Vottene ligger oppå skapet. | The mittens are on top of the cupboard. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Typically indicates contact with the upper surface of something. NoW source form: “oppå”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
