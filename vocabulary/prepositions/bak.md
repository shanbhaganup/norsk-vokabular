## bak

**English:** behind

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Sykkelen står bak garasjen. | The bike is behind the garage. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Hun gjemte seg bak døra. | She hid behind the door. |
| Konstruksjon, sted | bak + substantivfrase | Vi satt bak den høye mannen. | We sat behind the tall man. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The preposition identifies a reference point in front of something.
