## ifølge

**English:** according to

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Ifølge avisa åpner brua på fredag. | According to the newspaper, the bridge opens on Friday. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Toget er forsinket ifølge appen. | The train is delayed according to the app. |
| Konstruksjon, kilde | ifølge + substantivfrase | Ifølge den siste rapporten har salget økt. | According to the latest report, sales have risen. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The complement names the source of the information.
