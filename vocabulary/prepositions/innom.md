## innom

**English:** by; via

**Topics:** directions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Jeg stikker innom butikken på vei hjem. | I’ll stop by the shop on my way home. |
