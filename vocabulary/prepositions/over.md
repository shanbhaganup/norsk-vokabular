## over

**English:** over, above, across

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Lampa henger over bordet. | The lamp hangs above the table. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Hun la et teppe over barnet. | She put a blanket over the child. |
| Konstruksjon, retning | over + substantivfrase | Vi gikk over brua. | We walked across the bridge. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The preposition can indicate higher position or movement across.
