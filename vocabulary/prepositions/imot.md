## imot

**English:** against

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | De stemte imot forslaget. | They voted against the proposal. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Vi syklet imot vinden. | We cycled against the wind. |
| Konstruksjon | imot + substantivfrase | Stigen lente seg imot veggen. | The ladder leaned against the wall. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The examples illustrate opposition and physical contact.
