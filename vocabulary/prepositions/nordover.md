## nordover

**English:** northwards, north

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: nordover | Vi fulgte veien nordover dalen. | We followed the road north along the valley. |
| Retning uten utfylling | nordover | Etter frokost kjørte vi nordover. | After breakfast we drove north. |

**Form status:** Spelling and usage checked in Bokmålsordboka. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The snapshot gives the source label ADV. The direction can be expressed with a complement or with nordover on its own.
