## innover

**English:** inward, farther in

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: innover | Vi fulgte stien innover skogen. | We followed the path deeper into the forest. |
| Retning uten direkte utfylling | innover | Båten fortsatte innover mot havna. | The boat continued inward toward the harbour. |
