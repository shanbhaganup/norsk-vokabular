## bakover

**English:** along towards the back

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Hun strøk katten bakover ryggen. | She stroked the cat down its back. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Latteren spredte seg bakover rekkene. | Laughter spread towards the back through the rows. |
| Konstruksjon, retning | bakover + substantivfrase | Barberen førte kammen bakover hodet. | The barber drew the comb towards the back of the head. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka lists prepositional use under the adverb headword. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** In prepositional use the noun phrase identifies what the movement extends along.
