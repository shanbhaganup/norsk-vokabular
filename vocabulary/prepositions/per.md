## per

**English:** per

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Parkering koster femti kroner per time. | Parking costs fifty kroner per hour. |
