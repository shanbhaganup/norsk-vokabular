## innenfor

**English:** inside, within

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Hunden holdt seg innenfor gjerdet. | The dog stayed inside the fence. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Det ligger innenfor budsjettet. | It’s within the budget. |
| Konstruksjon | innenfor + substantivfrase | Ingen får campe innenfor det vernede området. | No one may camp within the protected area. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The word can refer to a physical or figurative boundary.
