## gjennom

**English:** through

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Vi gikk gjennom tunnelen. | We walked through the tunnel. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Lyset kom gjennom gardinen. | Light came through the curtain. |
| Konstruksjon, tid | gjennom + tidsuttrykk | Det regnet gjennom hele natta. | It rained throughout the night. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The examples illustrate passage through a place and duration throughout a period.
