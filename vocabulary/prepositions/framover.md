## framover

**English:** forward

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: framover | Vi gikk framover midtgangen for å finne plassene våre. | We walked forward along the aisle to find our seats. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: fremover | Hun gikk fremover langs perrongen for å finne vogna si. | She walked forward along the platform to find her carriage. |
| Retning uten utfylling | framover | Da bussen bråbremset, falt sekken framover. | When the bus braked sharply, the backpack fell forward. |
| Retning uten utfylling | fremover | Ro litt fremover, så kommer du inn til brygga. | Row forward a little and you’ll reach the dock. |

**Form status:** Spelling and usage checked in Bokmålsordboka. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The assigned source uses fremover and labels it ADV. Framover is the first spelling in Bokmålsordboka, and both spellings are accepted. The examples cover physical direction.
