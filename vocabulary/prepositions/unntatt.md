## unntatt

**English:** except, except for

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Alle unntatt Nora ble med. | Everyone except Nora came along. |
| Konstruksjon | unntatt + substantivfrase | Butikken er åpen hver dag unntatt søndag. | The shop is open every day except Sunday. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Unntatt introduces an exception to the group already mentioned.
