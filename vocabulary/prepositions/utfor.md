## utfor

**English:** down, over the edge of

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Skiløperen satte utfor bakken. | The skier set off down the slope. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Steinen trillet utfor kanten. | The stone rolled over the edge. |
| Konstruksjon | utfor + substantivfrase | Vannet strømmet utfor skrenten. | Water poured over the edge of the steep slope. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The preposition can mark motion down a slope or over an edge.
