## mellom

**English:** between

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Bordet står mellom vinduene. | The table is between the windows. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Hun valgte mellom to bøker. | She chose between two books. |
| Konstruksjon, tidsrom | mellom + tidsuttrykk | Jeg er hjemme mellom to og fire. | I’ll be home between two and four. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The complement names the points that bound an interval or space.
