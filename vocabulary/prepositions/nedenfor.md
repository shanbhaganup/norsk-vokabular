## nedenfor

**English:** below

**Topics:** directions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Det ligger et lite hus nedenfor veien. | There is a small house below the road. |
| Sted | nedenfor | Det ligger et lite hus nedenfor veien. | There is a small house below the road. |
| Plass i tekst | nedenfor | Forklaringen står nedenfor bildet. | The explanation appears below the picture. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Can indicate lower physical position or a later place in written material. NoW source form: “nedenfor”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
