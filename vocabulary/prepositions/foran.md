## foran

**English:** in front of; ahead of

**Topics:** directions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Sykkelen står foran huset. | The bike is in front of the house. |
| Sted | foran | Sykkelen står foran huset. | The bike is in front of the house. |
| Rekkefølge | foran | Hun står foran meg i køen. | She is ahead of me in line. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Can mark both position in space and position in a sequence. NoW source form: “foran”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
