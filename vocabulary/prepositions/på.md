## på

**English:** on; at; other meanings depend on the construction

**Topics:** home, time, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Boka ligger på bordet. | The book is on the table. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: på | Bildet henger på veggen. | The picture hangs on the wall. |
| Konstruksjon, sted | på + substantivfrase | Hun er på jobb. | She is at work. |
| Konstruksjon, tid | på + ukedag | Vi drar på mandag. | We are leaving on Monday. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword/forms checked; compound constructions and original example sentences supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** På is invariable. Its English translation depends on its complement and meaning. These rows show uses, not invented inflected forms. Norwegian prepositions do not generally impose separate noun case forms.
