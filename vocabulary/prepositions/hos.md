## hos

**English:** at someone’s place; with; among

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Vi spiste middag hos naboen. | We had dinner at the neighbor’s place. |
| Sted | hos | Vi spiste middag hos naboen. | We had dinner at the neighbor’s place. |
| Person eller institusjon | hos | Jeg har en time hos legen. | I have an appointment with the doctor. |
| Gruppe | hos | Denne vanen er vanlig hos barn. | This habit is common among children. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Can mark a person’s home, a professional’s premises, or a group in which a property is found. NoW source form: “hos”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
