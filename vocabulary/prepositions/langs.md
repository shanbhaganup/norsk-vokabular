## langs

**English:** along

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Vi gikk langs elva. | We walked along the river. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Det står benker langs stien. | There are benches along the path. |
| Konstruksjon | langs + substantivfrase | De syklet langs den gamle jernbanen. | They cycled along the old railway. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The complement gives the line or route being followed.
