## ovenfor

**English:** above, uphill from

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Huset ligger ovenfor skolen. | The house is uphill from the school. |
| Konstruksjon, sted | ovenfor + substantivfrase | Vi bor rett ovenfor den gamle brua. | We live just uphill from the old bridge. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Ovenfor locates something above a named point.
