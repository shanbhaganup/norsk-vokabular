## mot

**English:** towards, against

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Vi gikk mot stasjonen. | We walked towards the station. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Båten drev mot land. | The boat drifted towards shore. |
| Konstruksjon, motstand | mot + substantivfrase | De protesterte mot vedtaket. | They protested against the decision. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Mot can mark direction or opposition.
