## uten

**English:** without

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Hun drikker kaffe uten melk. | She drinks coffee without milk. |
| Konstruksjon | uten + substantivfrase | Han gikk ut uten paraply. | He went out without an umbrella. |
| Konstruksjon, infinitiv | uten å + infinitiv | De dro uten å si fra. | They left without letting us know. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Uten introduces something absent or omitted.
