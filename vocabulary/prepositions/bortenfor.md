## bortenfor

**English:** beyond, past

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | preposisjon | Butikken ligger bortenfor brua. | The shop is beyond the bridge. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Vi parkerte bortenfor skolen. | We parked past the school. |
| Konstruksjon, sted | bortenfor + substantivfrase | Det står et rødt skur bortenfor den gamle låven. | There is a red shed beyond the old barn. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The preposition locates something on the far side of a reference point.
