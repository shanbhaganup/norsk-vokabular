## nedenunder

**English:** below, beneath

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: nedenunder | Nedenunder fjellveggen lå en liten hytte. | A small cabin stood below the cliff face. |
| Bruk uten utfylling | nedenunder | Vi hørte musikk fra naboene nedenunder. | We heard music from the neighbours downstairs. |

**Form status:** Spelling and usage checked in Bokmålsordboka. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The snapshot gives the source label ADV. Bokmålsordboka also records the standalone use for the floor below.
