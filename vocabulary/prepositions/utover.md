## utover

**English:** out over; beyond

**Topics:** directions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Vi så utover fjorden fra toppen. | We looked out over the fjord from the summit. |
| Retning | utover | Vi så utover fjorden fra toppen. | We looked out over the fjord from the summit. |
| Grense | utover | Møtet fortsatte utover kvelden. | The meeting continued into the evening. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Can refer to outward movement or extension beyond a boundary. NoW source form: “utover”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
