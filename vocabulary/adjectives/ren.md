## ren

**English:** clean; pure; clear

**Topics:** describing, home, nature

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; both accepted Bokmål variants fully illustrated.

**Notes:** The main sense is free from dirt or pollution; other dictionary senses include unobstructed, morally blameless, unmixed, clear/distinct, and emphatic “complete”. Bokmålsordboka accepts both *ren* and *rein* paradigms. This entry preserves both variants and illustrates each form separately.
