## trist

**English:** sad; sorrowful; regrettable; dull

**Topics:** emotions, events

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary sense includes sadness, something strongly regrettable, and something lacking life or variety. Examples here use the sadness sense. The word is also used adverbially in some contexts; that is not adjective inflection.
