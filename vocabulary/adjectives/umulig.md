## umulig

**English:** impossible; extremely difficult; unreasonable

**Topics:** problem-solving, evaluation

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | umulig | Det virket som en umulig oppgave. | It seemed like an impossible task. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | umulig | Hun sto overfor ei umulig avgjørelse. | She faced an impossible decision. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | umulig | Det var et umulig krav å oppfylle. | It was an impossible demand to meet. |
| Positiv flertall | umulige | De umulige forholdene gjorde arbeidet farlig. | The impossible conditions made the work dangerous. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | umulige | Den umulige oppgaven tok flere dager. | The impossible task took several days. |
| Komparativ | umuligere | Etter den siste endringen virket planen enda umuligere. | After the latest change, the plan seemed even more impossible. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | umuligst | Av forslagene var dette umuligst å gjennomføre. | Of the proposals, this was the most impossible to carry out. |
| Superlativ bestemt | umuligste | Det var den umuligste oppgaven i hele prøven. | It was the most impossible task in the whole test. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison forms verified in Bokmålsordboka; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists *umuligere – umuligst – umuligste*, although comparison of “impossible” is semantically unusual and these forms are uncommon. Speakers often rephrase with *enda vanskeligere å gjennomføre* (“even harder to carry out”). The adjective also means exceptionally difficult or troublesome in some contexts.
