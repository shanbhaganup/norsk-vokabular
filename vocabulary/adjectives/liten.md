## liten

**English:** small; little; young (of a child)

**Topics:** describing, size, age

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | liten | Vi kjøpte en liten stol. | We bought a small chair. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | lita / liten | Hun har ei lita veske. / Hun har ei liten veske. | She has a small bag. / She has a small bag. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | lite / smått | De bor i et lite hus. / De bor i et smått hus. | They live in a small house. / They live in a small house. |
| Positiv flertall | små | Barna har små sko. | The children have small shoes. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | lille / vesle | Den lille hunden sov. / Den vesle hunden sov. | The little dog slept. / The little dog slept. |
| Komparativ | mindre | Denne koppen er mindre enn den blå. | This cup is smaller than the blue one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | minst | Av alle rommene er dette minst. | Of all the rooms, this one is the smallest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | minste | Hun valgte den minste pakken. | She chose the smallest package. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The source's base sense is not large or of insignificant size; it also uses *liten* for very young. Both feminine, neuter and definite alternatives are retained and separately illustrated. The neuter *smått* is uncommon as an attributive form; *lite* is the ordinary choice. Source record notes *lite* as its imported form. The dictionary also has other extended senses, such as brief, insignificant, and few in number; those are excluded here.
