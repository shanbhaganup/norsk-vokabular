## defensiv

**English:** defensive

**Topics:** sports

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | defensiv | Vi snakket om en defensiv spiller. | We talked about a defensive player. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | defensiv | Vi snakket om ei defensiv taktikk. | We talked about a defensive tactic. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | defensivt | Vi snakket om et defensivt lag. | We talked about a defensive team. |
| Positiv flertall | defensive | Vi snakket om to defensive spillere. | We talked about two defensive players. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | defensive | Vi snakket om den defensive spilleren. | We talked about the defensive player. |
| Komparativ | mer defensiv | Denne spilleren er mer defensiv enn den andre. | This player is more defensive than the other one. |
