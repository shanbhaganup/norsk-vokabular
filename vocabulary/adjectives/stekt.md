## stekt

**English:** fried; roasted; cooked in strong heat without water

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Positiv hankjønn, alternative | steikt | De serverte en steikt fisk med urter. | They served roasted fish with herbs. |

| Positiv hunkjønn, alternative | steikt | Hun la ei steikt pølse på tallerkenen. | She put a fried sausage on the plate. |
