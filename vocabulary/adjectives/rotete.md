## rotete

**English:** messy

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | rotete | Vi snakket om en rotete gang. | We talked about a messy hallway. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | rotete | Vi snakket om ei rotete stue. | We talked about a messy living room. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | rotete | Vi snakket om et rotete rom. | We talked about a messy room. |
| Positiv flertall | rotete | Vi snakket om to rotete rom. | We talked about two messy rooms. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | rotete | Vi snakket om den rotete gangen. | We talked about the messy hallway. |
| Komparativ | mer rotete | Denne gangen er mer rotete enn den andre. | This hallway is messier than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest rotete | Av de to er denne gangen mest rotete. | Of the two, this hallway is the messiest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest rotete | Vi snakket om den mest rotete gangen. | We talked about the messiest hallway. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and agreement checked.

**Notes:** Comparison with mer and mest is a productive construction. NoW source form: “rotete”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
