## rolig

**English:** calm; quiet; peaceful; steady

**Topics:** describing, emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | rolig | Han bor i en rolig bydel. | He lives in a quiet neighborhood. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | rolig | Hun hadde ei rolig natt. | She had a peaceful night. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | rolig | Det var et rolig hav om morgenen. | The sea was calm in the morning. |
| Positiv flertall | rolige | De rolige omgivelsene hjalp oss å slappe av. | The peaceful surroundings helped us relax. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | rolige | Den rolige stemmen gjorde barna trygge. | The calm voice reassured the children. |
| Komparativ | roligere | Sjøen ble roligere utover dagen. | The sea grew calmer as the day went on. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | roligst | Om natta er gata roligst. | The street is quietest at night. |
| Superlativ bestemt | roligste | Dette var den roligste delen av reisen. | This was the calmest part of the journey. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes barely moving, quiet/peaceful, free from worry, unhurried/composed, and even or uninterrupted senses. Adverbial uses such as *sitte rolig* are not separate adjective forms.
