## svak

**English:** weak; faint; poor; flimsy

**Topics:** describing, health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | svak | Han hadde en svak puls etter ulykken. | He had a weak pulse after the accident. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | svak | Hun kjente ei svak lukt av røyk. | She noticed a faint smell of smoke. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | svakt | Det var et svakt signal på fjellet. | There was a weak signal on the mountain. |
| Positiv flertall | svake | De svake musklene trengte trening. | The weak muscles needed exercise. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | svake | Den svake ledelsen mistet tilliten til gruppa. | The weak leadership lost the group’s trust. |
| Komparativ | svakere | Vinden ble svakere utover kvelden. | The wind grew weaker as the evening went on. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | svakest | Av signalene var dette svakest. | Of the signals, this one was the weakest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | svakeste | Hun fikk støtte til å forbedre det svakeste resultatet. | She received support to improve the weakest result. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers low strength, fragility or poor health, little influence or force, little authority, something barely noticeable, indecisiveness, financial unsoundness, poor performance, and the grammatical sense “weak” inflection. Examples illustrate physical, sensory, institutional and performance meanings. The adverbial and substantivized uses are not adjective forms.
