## kongelig

**English:** royal; regal

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | kongelig | Han fikk en kongelig medalje. | He received a royal medal. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | kongelig | Hun fikk ei kongelig utmerkelse. | She received a royal honour. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | kongelig | Det kongelige slottet ligger i Oslo. | The Royal Palace is in Oslo. |
| Positiv flertall | kongelige | De kongelige gjestene kom tidlig. | The royal guests arrived early. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | kongelige | Den kongelige familien hilste på publikum. | The royal family greeted the public. |
| Komparativ | mer kongelig | Denne mottakelsen virker mer kongelig enn den forrige. | This reception feels more regal than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest kongelig | Av alle mottakelsene virker denne mest kongelig. | Of all the receptions, this one feels the most regal. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest kongelige | Vi fikk den mest kongelige mottakelsen. | We received the most regal reception. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the positive agreement forms. Mer/mest comparison is a productive construction illustrated with the “regal in style” sense. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Also used substantivally in the definite plural de kongelige (“royal family members”) and adverbially in set use; those are separate uses, not adjective inflections. Comparison is periphrastic and most natural in the sense “regal in style.”
