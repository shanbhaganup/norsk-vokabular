## rar

**English:** strange; odd; peculiar

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | rar | Han fortalte en rar historie. | He told a strange story. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | rar | Hun kjente ei rar lukt fra kjelleren. | She heard a strange sound from the basement. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | rart | Det var et rart spørsmål. | It was an odd question. |
| Positiv flertall | rare | De hadde noen rare ideer. | They had some strange ideas. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | rare | Den rare fyren vinket til oss. | The strange guy waved to us. |
| Komparativ | rarere | Historien ble rarere for hver setning. | The story grew stranger with every sentence. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | rarest | Av forslagene var dette rarest. | Of the suggestions, this was the strangest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | rareste | Det var den rareste drømmen jeg har hatt. | It was the strangest dream I have ever had. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means unusual in a way that attracts attention or causes wonder; synonyms include strange, odd and peculiar. The dictionary also gives adverbial uses such as *snakke rart*; those are separate from adjective inflection.
