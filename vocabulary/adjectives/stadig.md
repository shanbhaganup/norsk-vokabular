## stadig

**English:** steady; constant; frequent

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | stadig | En stadig vekst ble observert. | A constant growth was observed. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | stadig | Ei stadig økning ble observert. | A constant increase was observed. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | stadig | Et stadig press øker stresset. | Constant pressure increases stress. |
| Positiv flertall | stadige | Stadige endringer ble observert. | Constant changes were observed. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | stadige | Den stadige økningen ble observert. | The constant increase was observed. |
| Komparativ | stadigere | Veksten var stadigere i år enn i fjor. | Growth was more constant this year than last year. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | stadigst | Av målingene var denne utviklingen stadigst. | Of the measurements, this trend was the most constant. |
| Superlativ bestemt | stadigste | Dette er den stadigste økningen i datasettet. | This is the most constant increase in the data set. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka confirms agreement and the comparison forms stadigere, stadigst, stadigste; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The reference gloss “constantly” is an adverbial use; the adjective sense here is “not changing; steady, frequent or constant.” The dictionary lists stadigere, stadigst and stadigste.
