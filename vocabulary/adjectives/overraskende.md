## overraskende

**English:** surprising; unexpected

**Topics:** emotions, events

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | overraskende | Det kom en overraskende beskjed i posten. | A surprising message arrived in the mail. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | overraskende | Hun fikk ei overraskende melding. | She received a surprising message. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | overraskende | Resultatet var et overraskende tap. | The result was a surprising loss. |
| Positiv flertall | overraskende | De fikk overraskende svar fra ekspertene. | They received unexpected answers from the experts. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | overraskende | Den overraskende vendingen endret alt. | The surprising turn changed everything. |
| Komparativ | mer overraskende | Avslutningen var mer overraskende enn starten. | The ending was more surprising than the beginning. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest overraskende | Det mest overraskende var stillheten. | The most surprising thing was the silence. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest overraskende | Dette var den mest overraskende delen av møtet. | This was the most surprising part of the meeting. |

**Form status:** Invariant agreement checked; analytical comparison used for the gradable sense.

**Notes:** Means causing surprise or confusion. The word is also used adverbially before adjectives and adverbs, but this entry focuses on its invariant adjective use. The dictionary lists no synthetic comparison; *mer/mest* are natural for degree of surprise.
