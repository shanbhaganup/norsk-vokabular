## mindre

**English:** smaller; younger; fewer; lesser

**Topics:** size, age, quantity

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

| Positiv hankjønn | mindre | Han valgte en mindre koffert. | He chose a smaller suitcase. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | mindre | Hun flyttet inn i ei mindre leilighet. | She moved into a smaller apartment. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | mindre | De kjøpte et mindre bord. | They bought a smaller table. |
| Positiv flertall | mindre | Familien vurderte mindre biler. | The family considered smaller cars. |

**Form status:** Invariant agreement checked; this adjective is already comparative, so further comparison is inapplicable.

**Notes:** *Mindre* is the comparative of *liten*, but Bokmålsordboka also lists it as an adjective with established uses meaning ‘not as large’, ‘younger’ (of children), ‘smaller in extent’, or ‘fewer’. This entry captures those adjective uses; it does not duplicate the comparative paradigm in [liten](liten.md). The dictionary also has adverbial and substantival uses, which are not inflections.
