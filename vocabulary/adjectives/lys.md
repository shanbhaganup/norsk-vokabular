## lys

**English:** bright; light-colored

**Topics:** describing, home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | lys | En lys vegg gjorde rommet større. | A light-colored wall made the room look bigger. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | lys | Ei lys jakke var lett å få øye på. | A light-colored jacket was easy to spot. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | lyst | Et lyst rom får mye dagslys. | A bright room gets a lot of daylight. |
| Positiv flertall | lyse | Lyse gardiner slapp morgensola inn. | Light-colored curtains let in the morning sun. |
| Positiv bestemt | lyse | Den lyse veggen reflekterte lyset. | The light-colored wall reflected the light. |
| Komparativ | lysere | Dette rommet er lysere enn gangen. | This room is brighter than the hallway. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | lysest | Rommet mot sør er lysest om dagen. | The south-facing room is brightest during the day. |
| Superlativ bestemt | lyseste | Hun valgte det lyseste rommet. | She chose the brightest room. |
