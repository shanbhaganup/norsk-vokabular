## tankefull

**English:** thoughtful

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | tankefull | Vi snakket om en tankefull gutt. | We talked about a thoughtful boy. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | tankefull | Vi snakket om ei tankefull jente. | We talked about a thoughtful girl. |
