## norsk

**English:** Norwegian; relating to Norway or Norwegians

**Topics:** language, nationality, geography

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | norsk | De kjøpte en norsk bil. | They bought a Norwegian car. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | norsk | Hun leste ei norsk avis. | She read a Norwegian newspaper. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | norsk | Vi smakte et norsk produkt. | We tasted a Norwegian product. |
| Positiv flertall | norske | Norske elever lærer engelsk på skolen. | Norwegian pupils learn English at school. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | norske | Det norske flagget hang fra balkongen. | The Norwegian flag hung from the balcony. |
| Komparativ | norskere | Denne teksten høres norskere ut enn den første. | This text sounds more Norwegian than the first one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | norskest | Av uttalene hørtes denne norskest ut. | Of the pronunciations, this one sounded the most Norwegian. |
| Superlativ bestemt | norskeste | Hun valgte den norskeste formuleringen. | She chose the most Norwegian-sounding wording. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; comparison is contextually restricted.

**Notes:** Means relating to, originating in, or characteristic of Norway and Norwegians. The dictionary lists *norskere, norskest,* and *norskeste*; these degrees are most natural when describing how Norwegian something sounds or appears, not as a ranking of nationality.
