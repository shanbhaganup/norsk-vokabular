## normal

**English:** normal; usual; average

**Topics:** describing, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | normal | Han følger en normal rutine. | He follows a normal routine. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | normal | Hun valgte ei normal løsning. | She chose a normal solution. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | normalt | Det var et normalt år for bedriften. | It was a normal year for the company. |
| Positiv flertall | normale | Temperaturen ligger innenfor normale grenser. | The temperature is within normal limits. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | normale | Den normale prosedyren tar kortere tid. | The usual procedure takes less time. |
| Komparativ | mer normal | Etter noen uker føltes hverdagen mer normal. | After a few weeks, everyday life felt more normal. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest normalt | Dette alternativet virker mest normalt. | This option seems the most normal. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest normale | Dette er den mest normale framgangsmåten. | This is the most normal procedure. |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; analytical comparison used for the gradable ‘usual/typical’ sense.

**Notes:** Means usual, standard, common, or average; it can also mean mentally sound. No synthetic comparison is listed. Analytical *mer/mest* is used for deviation from what is usual, rather than as a fixed paradigm for the mental-health sense.
