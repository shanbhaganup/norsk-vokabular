## berømt

**English:** famous, widely known

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | berømt | En berømt pianist spilte på festivalen. | A famous pianist played at the festival. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | berømt | Ei berømt sangerinne vokste opp i denne gata. | A famous singer grew up on this street. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | berømt | De besøkte et berømt museum i Madrid. | They visited a famous museum in Madrid. |
| Positiv flertall | berømte | Berømte forfattere kom til bokmessa. | Famous authors came to the book fair. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | berømte | Den berømte brua var stengt for biler. | The famous bridge was closed to cars. |
| Komparativ | mer berømt | Hun ble mer berømt etter den andre filmen. | She became more famous after the second film. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest berømt | Av de tre malerne er hun mest berømt. | Of the three painters, she is the most famous. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest berømte | Vi så den mest berømte skulpturen i samlingen. | We saw the most famous sculpture in the collection. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies positive agreement. Comparison with mer/mest is a productive construction. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Berømt for introduces what someone or something is famous for. The mer/mest constructions express degrees of fame.
