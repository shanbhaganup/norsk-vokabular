## kokt

**English:** cooked

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | kokt | Han spiser en kokt potet. | He is eating a boiled potato. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | kokt | Hun spiser ei kokt gulrot. | She is eating a boiled carrot. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | kokt | Barnet spiser et kokt egg. | The child is eating a boiled egg. |
| Positiv flertall | kokte | Vi spiser kokte egg til frokost. | We are eating boiled eggs for breakfast. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | kokte | Det kokte egget ligger på tallerkenen. | The boiled egg is on the plate. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the past participle and its adjectival agreement forms. Comparison is inapplicable to the completed-cooking sense shown. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Adjectival perfect participle of koke. It describes a completed cooking process and is not normally gradable as an adjective; comparative and superlative are genuinely inapplicable. The same spelling is also the neuter and plural participle form.
