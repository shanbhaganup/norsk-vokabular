## absolutt

**English:** absolute, unconditional

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | absolutt | De satte en absolutt grense på ti deltakere. | They set an absolute limit of ten participants. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | absolutt | Vi fikk ei absolutt garanti mot prisøkning. | We received an unconditional guarantee against a price increase. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | absolutt | Skriftlig samtykke er et absolutt vilkår for deltakelse. | Written consent is an absolute requirement for participation. |
