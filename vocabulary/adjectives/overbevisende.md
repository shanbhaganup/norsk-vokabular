## overbevisende

**English:** convincing; persuasive

**Topics:** communication, reasoning

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | overbevisende | Hun la fram en overbevisende forklaring. | She presented a convincing explanation. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | overbevisende | Han kom med ei overbevisende begrunnelse. | He gave a convincing justification. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | overbevisende | Juryen hørte et overbevisende argument. | The jury heard a convincing argument. |
| Positiv flertall | overbevisende | De viste fram overbevisende bevis. | They presented convincing evidence. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | overbevisende | Den overbevisende dokumentasjonen endret vurderingen. | The convincing documentation changed the assessment. |
| Komparativ | mer overbevisende | Det nye argumentet er mer overbevisende enn det gamle. | The new argument is more convincing than the old one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest overbevisende | Av forklaringene var hennes mest overbevisende. | Of the explanations, hers was the most convincing. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest overbevisende | Dette er den mest overbevisende dokumentasjonen vi har. | This is the most convincing evidence we have. |

**Form status:** Invariant agreement checked; analytical comparison used for the gradable sense.

**Notes:** Means highly credible, or so superior that there is no doubt. This is the adjectival present participle of *overbevise*. The dictionary lists *overbevisende* unchanged across agreement forms and gives no synthetic comparison; *mer/mest* are natural for the gradable credibility sense.
