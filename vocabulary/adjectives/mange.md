## mange

**English:** many; numerous; several

**Topics:** quantity, numbers

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** In its quantified adjective use, *mange* is used with plural count nouns and has no gendered singular or definite singular agreement forms; those cells are inapplicable. The comparative alternatives *fler* and *flere* are both listed and illustrated. *Mange* also occurs substantivally (for example, “many left the meeting”), which is a separate syntactic use rather than an inflection.
