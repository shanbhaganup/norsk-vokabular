## urban

**English:** urban; city-related; urbane

**Topics:** city, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | urban | Det ligger en urban bydel øst for sentrum. | There is an urban district east of the centre. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | urban | Hun beskrev ei urban utvikling i kommunen. | She described an urban development in the municipality. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | urbant | De åpnet et urbant kulturhus i den gamle fabrikken. | They opened an urban cultural centre in the old factory. |
| Positiv flertall | urbane | Urbane strøk tiltrekker seg mange nye innbyggere. | Urban areas attract many new residents. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | urbane | Det urbane miljøet endret seg raskt. | The urban environment changed quickly. |
| Komparativ | urbanere | Det nye området virker urbanere enn landsbyen rundt. | The new area seems more urban than the surrounding village. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | urbanest | Av bydelene framstår denne urbanest. | Of the districts, this one appears most urban. |
| Superlativ bestemt | urbaneste | Dette er den urbaneste delen av kommunen. | This is the most urban part of the municipality. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison forms verified in Bokmålsordboka; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary describes what relates to or is typical of cities, and also a cultivated or sophisticated manner. Examples focus on the city-related sense. It also has an adverbial use (*urbant*), which is not adjective inflection.
