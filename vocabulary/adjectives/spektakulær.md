## spektakulær

**English:** spectacular; striking; impressive

**Topics:** culture, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | spektakulær | De så en spektakulær forestilling på torget. | They saw a spectacular performance in the square. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | spektakulær | Det var ei spektakulær utsikt fra toppen. | There was a spectacular view from the summit. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | spektakulært | Vi opplevde et spektakulært nordlys. | We saw a spectacular northern light display. |
| Positiv flertall | spektakulære | De spektakulære bildene fylte hele veggen. | The spectacular pictures covered the whole wall. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | spektakulære | Den spektakulære avslutningen fikk stående applaus. | The spectacular finale received a standing ovation. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer spektakulær | Den nye forestillingen var mer spektakulær enn den forrige. | The new performance was more spectacular than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest spektakulær | Av utsiktene var denne mest spektakulær. | Of the views, this one was the most spectacular. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest spektakulære | Det var den mest spektakulære delen av showet. | It was the most spectacular part of the show. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement checked; mer/mest shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means attracting positive attention or being eye-catching. The dictionary lists no synthetic comparison, but the meaning is gradable; the degree rows are productive mer/mest constructions rather than dictionary-listed forms.
