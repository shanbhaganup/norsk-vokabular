## stygg

**English:** ugly; nasty; bad; dangerous

**Topics:** describing, evaluation

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | stygg | Han hadde en stygg ripe på bilen. | He had an ugly scratch on the car. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | stygg | Det var ei stygg ulykke på veien. | There was a bad accident on the road. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | stygt | Hun fikk et stygt sår på kneet. | She got an ugly wound on her knee. |
| Positiv flertall | stygge | De stygge kommentarene såret henne. | The nasty comments hurt her. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | stygge | Den stygge stormen ødela flere båter. | The severe storm destroyed several boats. |
| Komparativ | styggere | Dette huset er styggere enn det gamle. | This house is uglier than the old one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | styggest | Av bygningene var denne styggest. | Of the buildings, this one was the ugliest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | styggeste | Det var den styggeste beskyldningen i debatten. | It was the nastiest accusation in the debate. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers unattractive appearance, being mean, dangerous or alarming situations, unpleasant or severe events, loud/scary sounds, reckless behavior, foul weather and indecent content. Examples use appearance, harm and severity senses. The source gloss “ugly” is narrower than the dictionary entry.
