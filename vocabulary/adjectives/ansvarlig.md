## ansvarlig

**English:** responsible, accountable, in charge

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | ansvarlig | En ansvarlig redaktør må godkjenne rettelsen. | An editor in charge must approve the correction. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | ansvarlig | Ei ansvarlig vaktsjef tok imot meldingen. | A shift supervisor in charge received the message. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | ansvarlig | Et ansvarlig team følger opp hver klage. | A responsible team follows up on every complaint. |
| Positiv flertall | ansvarlige | Ansvarlige ledere følger opp varsler. | Responsible managers follow up on reports of wrongdoing. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | ansvarlige | Den ansvarlige lederen signerte skjemaet. | The manager in charge signed the form. |
| Komparativ | mer ansvarlig | Den nye planen er mer ansvarlig fordi den tar høyde for vedlikehold. | The new plan is more responsible because it accounts for maintenance. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest ansvarlig | Av de tre forslagene virker dette mest ansvarlig. | Of the three proposals, this one seems the most responsible. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest ansvarlige | Vi valgte den mest ansvarlige løsningen for miljøet. | We chose the most environmentally responsible option. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies positive agreement. Comparison with mer/mest is a productive construction. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Use ansvarlig for to name a responsibility. The mer/mest examples show degrees of responsible conduct. A formally assigned role can simply identify who is in charge.
