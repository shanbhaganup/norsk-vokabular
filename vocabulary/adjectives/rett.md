## rett

**English:** straight; correct; right; proper

**Topics:** describing, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | rett | Tegn en rett linje på arket. | Draw a straight line on the sheet. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | rett | De fulgte ei rett rute gjennom skogen. | They followed a straight route through the forest. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | rett | Dette er et rett svar på spørsmålet. | This is a correct answer to the question. |
| Positiv flertall | rette | Hun skrev de rette ordene i margen. | She wrote the correct words in the margin. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | rette | Den rette veien går langs elva. | The right road runs along the river. |
| Komparativ | rettere | Den nye stolpen står rettere enn den gamle. | The new post stands straighter than the old one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | rettest | Av strekene ble denne rettest. | Of the lines, this one turned out straightest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | retteste | De valgte den retteste veien gjennom dalen. | They chose the straightest road through the valley. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes “not curved/straight”, “in accordance with rules or laws/correct/suitable”, and “just/right”; it also has separate adverbial uses. This entry samples the straight and correct senses. Comparison is most natural for physical straightness; the listed degrees are not equally natural with every sense.
