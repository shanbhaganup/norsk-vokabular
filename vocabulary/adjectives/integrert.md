## integrert

**English:** integrated

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | integrert | Vi snakket om en integrert løsning. | We talked about an integrated solution. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | integrert | Vi snakket om ei integrert tjeneste. | We talked about an integrated service. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | integrert | Vi snakket om et integrert system. | We talked about an integrated system. |
| Positiv flertall | integrerte | Vi snakket om to integrerte løsninger. | We talked about two integrated solutions. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | integrerte | Vi snakket om den integrerte løsningen. | We talked about the integrated solution. |
| Komparativ | mer integrert | Denne løsningen er mer integrert enn den andre. | This solution is more integrated than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest integrert | Av de to er denne løsningen mest integrert. | Of the two, this solution is the most integrated. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest integrerte | Vi snakket om den mest integrerte løsningen. | We talked about the most integrated solution. |

**Form status:** Derived agreement and usage reviewed.

**Notes:** The compound or participial adjective follows integrere. Comparison with mer and mest is a productive construction. NoW source form: “integrert”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
