## nervøs

**English:** nervous; anxious; uneasy

**Topics:** emotions, health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | nervøs | Han ble nervøs før intervjuet. | He became nervous before the interview. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | nervøs | Hun er nervøs for eksamen. | She is nervous about the exam. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | nervøst | Et nervøst barn holdt moren i hånda. | The nervous child held its mother's hand. |
| Positiv flertall | nervøse | De nervøse studentene ventet utenfor. | The nervous students waited outside. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | nervøse | Den nervøse pasienten stilte flere spørsmål. | The nervous patient asked several questions. |
| Komparativ | nervøsere | Jeg blir nervøsere jo lenger jeg må vente. | I get more nervous the longer I have to wait. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | nervøsest | Av alle var han nervøsest før prøven. | Of all of them, he was the most nervous before the test. |
| Superlativ bestemt | nervøseste | Hun var den nervøseste deltakeren. | She was the most nervous participant. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Covers feeling anxious or restless, being oversensitive, and figurative instability. The dictionary lists *nervøsere, nervøsest,* and *nervøseste*. It also has a technical sense relating to nerves and adverbial uses; these are not separate adjective forms.
