## ødelagt

**English:** damaged; broken; ruined

**Topics:** objects, repair

**Notes:** Adjectival perfect participle of *ødelegge*, meaning damaged or made unusable. The official dictionary also lists the spelling variant *øydelegge* and participle *øydelagt*, with agreement forms *øydelagt/øydelagte*. These are included here as dictionary-accepted alternatives. This is an adjective entry; finite verb forms belong to the verb entry.
