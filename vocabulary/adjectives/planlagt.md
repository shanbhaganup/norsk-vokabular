## planlagt

**English:** planned; scheduled

**Topics:** planning, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | planlagt | Han fulgte en planlagt prosess. | They held a planned meeting with the neighbors. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | planlagt | Hun fulgte ei planlagt rute gjennom byen. | She followed a planned route through the city. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | planlagt | Det var et planlagt avbrudd i tjenesten. | It was a planned interruption to the service. |
| Positiv flertall | planlagte | De planlagte aktivitetene ble flyttet inn. | The planned activities were moved indoors. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | planlagte | Den planlagte reisen måtte utsettes. | The planned trip had to be postponed. |
| Komparativ | mer planlagt | Den nye arbeidsdagen er mer planlagt enn den forrige. | The new workday is more planned than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest planlagt | Av ukene var denne mest planlagt. | Of the weeks, this one was the most planned. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest planlagte | Dette var den mest planlagte delen av prosjektet. | This was the most planned part of the project. |

**Form status:** Participle agreement checked; analytical comparison used for degree of prior planning.

**Notes:** Adjectival perfect participle of *planlegge*. Ordbøkene gives *planlagt* before indefinite nouns and *planlagte* in definite singular/plural. No synthetic comparison is listed; *mer/mest planlagt* can describe differing degrees of prior planning.
