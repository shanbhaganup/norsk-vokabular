## direkte

**English:** direct

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | direkte | Dette er en direkte vei. | This is a direct road. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | direkte | Dette er ei direkte rute. | This is a direct route. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | direkte | Dette er et direkte tog. | This is a direct train. |
| Positiv flertall | direkte | Vi fant direkte ruter ved inngangen. | We found direct routes by the entrance. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | direkte | Dette er den direkte ruten. | This is the direct route. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement verified; original teaching examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The adjective is invariant. The source also includes adverb uses of direkte; those are not adjective forms. Its attachment also has an unrelated live2 row, which is not a Norwegian sense of direkte and was excluded. Mer/mest are productive, context-dependent degree constructions.
