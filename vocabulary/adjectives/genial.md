## genial

**English:** brilliant; ingenious

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | genial | Han fant en genial løsning. | He found a brilliant solution. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | genial | Hun fikk en genial idé. | She got a brilliant idea. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | genialt | Det var et genialt forslag. | It was a brilliant suggestion. |
| Positiv flertall | geniale | De kom med geniale forslag. | They came up with brilliant suggestions. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | geniale | Den geniale oppfinnelsen sparte oss tid. | The ingenious invention saved us time. |
| Komparativ | mer genial | Den nye planen er mer genial enn den gamle. | The new plan is more ingenious than the old one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest genial | Av alle forslagene var dette mest genialt. | Of all the suggestions, this was the most ingenious. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest geniale | Dette var den mest geniale løsningen i konkurransen. | This was the most ingenious solution in the competition. |

**Form status:** Agreement verified; comparison is productive, periphrastic.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists no synthetic comparison. Mer/mest are productive with this gradable meaning.
