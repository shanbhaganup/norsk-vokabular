## grå

**English:** gray; grey

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | grå | Han har en grå jakke. | He has a gray jacket. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | grå | Hun malte ei grå dør. | She painted a gray door. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | grått | Det var et grått hus. | It was a gray house. |
| Positiv flertall | grå | De grå skyene dekket himmelen. | The gray clouds covered the sky. |
| Positiv flertall | gråe | De gråe skyene dekket himmelen. | The gray clouds covered the sky. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | grå | Den grå veggen trenger maling. | The gray wall needs painting. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | gråe | Den gråe veggen trenger maling. | The gray wall needs painting. |
| Komparativ | gråere | Veggen ble gråere etter vinteren. | The wall became grayer after the winter. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | gråest | Av prøvene er denne gråest. | Of the samples, this one is the grayest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | gråeste | Dette er den gråeste steinen. | This is the grayest stone. |

**Form status:** Verified; accepted plural and definite alternatives are illustrated.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka accepts both grå and gråe in plural and definite forms. Examples illustrate each alternative. The spelling grå is Bokmål; English gray/grey are both included.
