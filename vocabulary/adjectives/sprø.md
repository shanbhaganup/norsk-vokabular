## sprø

**English:** brittle; crisp; frail; crazy

**Topics:** describing, food, emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | sprø | Han hørte en sprø lyd fra høyttaleren. | He heard a brittle, thin sound from the speaker. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | sprø | Hun bakte ei sprø lefse til kaffen. | She baked a crisp flatbread to have with coffee. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | sprøtt | Vi spiste et sprøtt knekkebrød til lunsj. | We ate crispbread for lunch. |
| Positiv flertall | sprø | De sprø kjeksene knaste mellom tennene. | The crisp biscuits crunched between our teeth. |
| Positiv flertall, alternative | sprøe | De sprøe kjeksene knaste mellom tennene. | The crisp biscuits crunched between our teeth. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | sprø | Den sprø lyden kom fra radioen. | The brittle sound came from the radio. |
| Positiv bestemt entall, alternative | sprøe | Den sprøe lyden kom fra radioen. | The brittle sound came from the radio. |
| Komparativ | sprøere | Disse kjeksene er sprøere enn de vi kjøpte sist. | These biscuits are crispier than the ones we bought last time. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | sprøest | Av kjeksene ble disse sprøest i ovnen. | Of the biscuits, these turned out crispiest in the oven. |
| Superlativ bestemt | sprøeste | Dette er den sprøeste kjeksbunnen jeg har laget. | This is the crispiest biscuit base I have made. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement alternatives and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary has senses including something that crumbles or breaks easily, thin/frail, and crazy/out of one’s mind. Both plural and definite alternatives *sprø/sprøe* are accepted and separately exemplified. Examples here use the crisp/brittle senses.
