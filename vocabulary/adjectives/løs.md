## løs

**English:** loose; unattached; free

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | løs | Katten fant en løs tråd på teppet. | The cat found a loose thread on the rug. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | løs | Hun har ei løs tann. | She has a loose tooth. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | løst | Det var et løst håndtak på døra. | The handle was loose in the door. |
| Positiv flertall | løse | Vi pakket de løse delene hver for seg. | We packed the loose parts separately. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | løse | Den løse skruen lå på gulvet. | The loose screw was on the floor. |
| Komparativ | løsere | Denne knuten er løsere enn den andre. | This knot is looser than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | løsest | Av knutene satt denne løsest. | Of the knots, this one was the loosest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | løseste | Dette er den løseste forbindelsen mellom delene. | This is the loosest connection between the parts. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Core sense: not fastened or attached, or easily loosened. The dictionary accepts both *løs* and *laus* paradigms, including *løsere/løsest/løseste* and *lausere/lausest/lauseste*. This entry follows the source record's *løs* variant. The dictionary lists several extended senses, including not compact, casual, unreliable, and unrestrained; those are not detailed here.
