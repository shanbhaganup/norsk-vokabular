## sulten

**English:** hungry; eager; keen for more

**Topics:** food, feelings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | sulten | Han var sulten etter fotballtreningen. | He was hungry after football practice. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | sulten | Hun ble sulten på vei hjem fra skolen. | She got hungry on her way home from school. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | sultent | Et sultent barn ba om kveldsmat. | A hungry child asked for supper. |
| Positiv flertall | sultne | De sultne gjestene satte seg til bords. | The hungry guests sat down at the table. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | sultne | Den sultne hunden ventet ved matskåla. | The hungry dog waited by the food bowl. |
| Komparativ | sultnere | Jeg blir sultnere når jeg lukter nybakt brød. | I get hungrier when I smell freshly baked bread. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | sultnest | Etter den lange turen var jeg sultnest av alle. | After the long hike, I was the hungriest of all. |
| Superlativ bestemt | sultneste | Den sultneste deltakeren fikk den siste matpakken. | The hungriest participant got the last packed lunch. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Besides feeling hunger, the adjective can mean wanting something eagerly, as in *sulten på mer*. Examples focus on literal hunger. The degree forms are dictionary-listed.
