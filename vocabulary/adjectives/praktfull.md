## praktfull

**English:** magnificent; splendid; wonderful

**Topics:** describing, culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | praktfull | De så en praktfull forestilling på teatret. | They saw a magnificent performance at the theater. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | praktfull | Vi gikk gjennom ei praktfull utstilling. | We walked through a splendid exhibition. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | praktfullt | Det var et praktfullt syn fra fjellet. | It was a magnificent sight from the mountain. |
| Positiv flertall | praktfulle | De praktfulle bygningene sto ved elva. | The magnificent buildings stood by the river. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | praktfulle | Den praktfulle salen var full av gjester. | The magnificent hall was full of guests. |
| Komparativ | mer praktfull | Fasaden virker mer praktfull etter restaureringen. | The palace seems more magnificent after the restoration. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest praktfull | Av salene var denne mest praktfull. | Of the halls, this one was the most magnificent. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest praktfulle | Dette var den mest praktfulle bygningen på torget. | This was the most magnificent building in the square. |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; analytical comparison used for degree of grandeur.

**Notes:** Means magnificent, splendid or wonderful. The dictionary lists no synthetic comparison; *mer/mest* can naturally grade the degree of grandeur.
