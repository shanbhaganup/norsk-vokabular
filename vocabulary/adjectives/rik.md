## rik

**English:** rich; wealthy; abundant

**Topics:** money, nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | rik | Han er en rik forretningsmann. | He is a wealthy businessman. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | rik | Hun kommer fra ei rik familie. | She comes from a wealthy family. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | rikt | Det var et rikt fiske langs kysten. | There was an abundant fishery along the coast. |
| Positiv flertall | rike | De rike landene lovet mer støtte. | The wealthy countries promised more support. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | rike | Den rike jorda gir gode avlinger. | The rich soil yields good crops. |
| Komparativ | rikere | Regionen ble rikere etter at industrien vokste. | The region became richer as industry grew. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | rikest | Av deltakerne var hun rikest. | Of the participants, she was the richest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | rikeste | Han møtte den rikeste familien i byen. | He met the richest family in town. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers owning much, yielding a good return, containing a lot of something, and being varied or abundant. *Rik* is also used substantivally for “the rich”; that is a separate syntactic use.
