## grunn

**English:** shallow; superficial

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | grunn | En grunn elv er lett å krysse. | A shallow river is easy to cross. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | grunn | Ei grunn vik ligger mellom holmene. | A shallow inlet lies between the islets. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | grunt | Et grunt vann dekker stien. | Shallow water covers the path. |
| Positiv flertall | grunne | Grunne kunnskaper er ikke nok. | Superficial knowledge is not enough. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | grunne | Den grunne elva frøs tidlig. | The shallow river froze early. |
| Komparativ | grunnere | Denne delen av elva er grunnere enn den andre. | This part of the river is shallower than the other. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | grunnest | Av stedene er dette grunnest. | Of the places, this is the shallowest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | grunneste | Dette er det grunneste valget. | This is the most shallow; superficial choice. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry; comparison forms are dictionary-listed or explicitly identified as productive. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Source gloss “due” is incorrect. The dictionary sense is “not deep” and figuratively “superficial”. The noun grunn is a separate word type.
