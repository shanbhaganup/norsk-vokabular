## sterk

**English:** strong; powerful; intense

**Topics:** describing, health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | sterk | Han løftet en sterk mann opp fra bakken. | He helped a strong man up from the ground. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | sterk | Hun har ei sterk stemme. | She has a powerful voice. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | sterkt | Det var et sterkt signal om støtte. | It was a strong signal of support. |
| Positiv flertall | sterke | De sterke vindene stanset ferga. | The strong winds stopped the ferry. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | sterke | Den sterke smaken kom fra ingefæren. | The strong flavor came from the ginger. |
| Komparativ | sterkere | Denne muskelen er sterkere enn den andre. | This muscle is stronger than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | sterkest | Av deltakerne var hun sterkest. | Of the participants, she was the strongest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | sterkeste | Han valgte den sterkeste kaffen. | He chose the strongest coffee. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** *Sterk* covers physical strength, intensity, force and potency, among other senses. Examples illustrate physical, vocal, signal, weather and taste uses. The existing adverb *sterkt* is a related but separate POS entry.
