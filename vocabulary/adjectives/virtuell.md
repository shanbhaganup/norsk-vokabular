## virtuell

**English:** virtual; simulated; potential (in physics)

**Topics:** technology, physics

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | virtuell | Vi besøkte en virtuell utstilling. | We visited a virtual exhibition. |

| Positiv intetkjønn | virtuelt | Museet viste et virtuelt landskap. | The museum showed a virtual landscape. |
| Positiv flertall | virtuelle | Elevene utforsket virtuelle verdener. | The pupils explored virtual worlds. |

| Superlativ bestemt | mest virtuelle | Dette er den mest virtuelle utstillingen i samlingen. | This is the most virtual exhibition in the collection. |

**Form status:** Agreement verified; comparison is a productive construction, not a synthetic dictionary paradigm.

**Notes:** Covers the senses “simulated/representing something in physical reality” and the technical physics sense “present but inactive; potential.” Periphrastic comparison is only natural when the context allows degrees of virtuality; it is not a fixed dictionary comparison paradigm.
