## solfylt

**English:** sunny

**Topics:** weather

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | solfylt | Vi snakket om en solfylt dag. | We talked about a sunny day. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | solfylt | Vi snakket om ei solfylt strand. | We talked about a sunny beach. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | solfylt | Vi snakket om et solfylt område. | We talked about a sunny area. |
| Positiv flertall | solfylte | Vi snakket om to solfylte dager. | We talked about two sunny days. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | solfylte | Vi snakket om den solfylte dagen. | We talked about the sunny day. |
| Komparativ | mer solfylt | Denne dagen er mer solfylt enn den andre. | This day is sunnier than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest solfylt | Av de to er denne dagen mest solfylt. | Of the two, this day is the sunniest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest solfylte | Vi snakket om den mest solfylte dagen. | We talked about the sunniest day. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and agreement checked.

**Notes:** Comparison with mer and mest is a productive construction. NoW source form: “solfylt”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
