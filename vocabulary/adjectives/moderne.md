## moderne

**English:** modern, contemporary

**Topics:** technology, culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | moderne | Han bor i en moderne leilighet. | He lives in a modern apartment. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | moderne | Hun arbeider i et moderne bygg. | She works in a modern building. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | moderne | Dette er et moderne system. | This is a modern system. |
| Positiv flertall | moderne | Moderne biler bruker mindre drivstoff. | Modern cars use less fuel. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | moderne | Den moderne kunsten overrasket oss. | Modern art surprised us. |
| Komparativ | mer moderne | Den nye bygningen er mer moderne enn den gamle. | The new building is more modern than the old one. |

| Superlativ bestemt | mest moderne | Dette er den mest moderne løsningen. | This is the most modern solution. |
