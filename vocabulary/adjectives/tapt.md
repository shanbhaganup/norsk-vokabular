## tapt

**English:** lost; forfeited; wasted

**Topics:** condition, outcomes

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

**Form status:** Adjectival perfect participle and agreement checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Adjectival perfect participle of *tape*, including losing or misplacing something and suffering defeat. The dictionary also lists the related present participle *tapende*; it is a separate form and is not an inflection of *tapt*. Comparison is not applicable to the result-state senses illustrated here.
