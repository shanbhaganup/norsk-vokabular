## pen

**English:** pretty; attractive; neat

**Topics:** describing, appearance

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | pen | Han hadde på seg en pen dress. | He was wearing a smart suit. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | pen | Hun plantet ei pen rose ved inngangen. | She planted a pretty rose by the entrance. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | pent | Det var et pent hus med en liten hage. | It was a neat house with a small garden. |
| Positiv flertall | pene | De kjøpte pene klær til festen. | They bought nice clothes for the party. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | pene | Den pene hagen fikk mye ros. | The beautiful garden received a lot of praise. |
| Komparativ | penere | Denne skjorta er penere enn den blå. | This shirt is nicer than the blue one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | penest | Av kjolene synes jeg denne er penest. | Of the dresses, I think this one is the prettiest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | peneste | Hun valgte den peneste kjolen. | She chose the prettiest dress. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Covers pleasing appearance and neatness. The dictionary also uses *pen* for fine weather, respectable, complimentary, cautious, and satisfactory/ample; many of these senses have their own natural contexts. Adverbial uses such as *oppføre seg pent* are not adjective forms.
