## nydelig

**English:** lovely; beautiful; delicious; excellent

**Topics:** describing, food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | nydelig | De så en nydelig utsikt fra toppen. | They saw a beautiful view from the summit. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | nydelig | Hun laget ei nydelig suppe. | She made a delicious soup. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | nydelig | Det var et nydelig måltid. | It was a lovely meal. |
| Positiv flertall | nydelige | Vi fikk nydelige blomster til festen. | We received lovely flowers for the party. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | nydelige | Den nydelige sangen rørte publikum. | The beautiful song moved the audience. |
| Komparativ | nydeligere | Solnedgangen var nydeligere enn jeg hadde ventet. | The sunset was more beautiful than I had expected. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | nydeligst | Av kakene smakte denne nydeligst. | Of the cakes, this one tasted the best. |
| Superlativ bestemt | nydeligste | Det var den nydeligste utsikten på hele turen. | It was the most beautiful view on the whole trip. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Covers delicious, beautiful, and very good/excellent senses. The dictionary also records an ironic use expressing skepticism or mild criticism; context determines that reading.
