## perfekt

**English:** perfect; flawless; excellent

**Topics:** describing, evaluation

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Positiv hunkjønn | perfekt | Hun fant ei perfekt løsning. | She found a perfect solution. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | perfekt | Vi fikk et perfekt resultat. | We got a perfect result. |
| Positiv flertall | perfekte | De hadde perfekte forhold for turen. | They had perfect conditions for the trip. |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; comparison inapplicable to the absolute ‘without flaws’ sense.

**Notes:** Means entirely without errors or shortcomings, hence excellent. The dictionary lists adjective agreement but no comparison. Although speakers sometimes use *mer/mest perfekt* colloquially, the absolute sense does not normally take comparison. The dictionary also gives an adverbial use meaning “perfectly”; that is not an adjective form.
