## korrupt

**English:** corrupt

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | korrupt | Han møtte en korrupt politiker. | He met a corrupt politician. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | korrupt | Hun avslørte ei korrupt ledergruppe. | She exposed a corrupt leadership team. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the positive agreement forms. Mer/mest comparison is a productive construction when degree of corruption is compared. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka gives the sense “that bribes or accepts bribes.” Periphrastic comparison is possible when degree of corruption is compared; no synthetic forms are listed.
