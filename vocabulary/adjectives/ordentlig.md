## ordentlig

**English:** proper; decent; respectable; regular

**Topics:** describing, society

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; forms are colloquial/uncommon in degrees.

**Notes:** The dictionary gives ‘ordinary/usual’ and ‘decent/proper’. It also lists adverbial uses, including an intensifying sense (“very”); those are separate from adjective inflection. The comparison forms are dictionary-listed but uncommon; analytical *mer/mest ordentlig* may be more natural in many contexts.
