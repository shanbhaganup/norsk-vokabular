## personlig

**English:** personal; private; firsthand

**Topics:** people, communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | personlig | Han fikk en personlig invitasjon. | He received a personal invitation. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | personlig | Hun delte ei personlig erfaring. | She shared a personal experience. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | personlig | Dette er et personlig valg. | This is a personal choice. |
| Positiv flertall | personlige | De personlige brevene ble levert tilbake. | The personal letters were returned. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | personlige | Den personlige samtalen gjorde inntrykk. | The personal conversation made an impression. |
| Komparativ | personligere | Etter hvert fikk teksten en personligere tone. | Over time, the text took on a more personal tone. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | personligst | Av brevene var dette personligst i tonen. | Of the letters, this one was the most personal in tone. |
| Superlativ bestemt | personligste | Hun skrev den personligste hilsenen. | She wrote the most personal greeting. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; degree forms are uncommon and context-dependent.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers something directed at or concerning one person, private/intimate, based on personal experience, done by oneself, and person-like. The listed synthetic comparison (*personligere, personligst, personligste*) is unusual; examples use a gradable tone/intimacy interpretation. In other contexts, an analytic construction may sound more natural.
