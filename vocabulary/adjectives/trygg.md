## trygg

**English:** safe; secure; confident

**Topics:** safety, emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | trygg | Han følte seg trygg i det nye huset. | He felt safe in the new house. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | trygg | Hun er trygg på at låsen virker. | She is confident that the lock works. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | trygt | Det er trygt å krysse veien her. | It is safe to cross the road here. |
| Positiv flertall | trygge | De trygge barna lekte i hagen. | The safe children played in the garden. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | trygge | Den trygge veien går langs elva. | The safe route runs along the river. |
| Komparativ | tryggere | Denne ruta er tryggere om vinteren. | This route is safer in winter. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | tryggest | Av alle alternativene virker denne tryggest. | Of all the options, this one seems safest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | tryggeste | Vi valgte den tryggeste ruta hjem. | We chose the safest route home. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers being free from danger and being free from doubt or calm. The examples use both safety and confidence senses. The adverb *trygt* is a related use, not an adjective form.
