## begeistret

**English:** enthusiastic

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | begeistret | Vi snakket om en begeistret elev. | We talked about an enthusiastic student. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | begeistret | Vi snakket om ei begeistret gruppe. | We talked about an enthusiastic group. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | begeistret | Vi snakket om et begeistret publikum. | We talked about an enthusiastic audience. |
| Positiv flertall | begeistrete | Vi snakket om to begeistrete deltakere. | We talked about two enthusiastic participants. |
| Positiv flertall | begeistrede | Vi snakket om to begeistrede deltakere. | We talked about two enthusiastic participants. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | begeistrete | Vi snakket om den begeistrete eleven. | We talked about the enthusiastic student. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | begeistrede | Vi snakket om den begeistrede eleven. | We talked about the enthusiastic student. |
| Komparativ | mer begeistret | Denne eleven er mer begeistret enn den andre. | This student is more enthusiastic than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest begeistret | Av de to er denne eleven mest begeistret. | Of the two, this student is the most enthusiastic. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest begeistrete | Vi snakket om den mest begeistrete eleven. | We talked about the most enthusiastic student. |
