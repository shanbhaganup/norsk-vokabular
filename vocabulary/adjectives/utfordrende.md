## utfordrende

**English:** challenging

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | utfordrende | Vi snakket om en utfordrende jobb. | We talked about a challenging job. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | utfordrende | Vi snakket om ei utfordrende oppgave. | We talked about a challenging task. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | utfordrende | Vi snakket om et utfordrende prosjekt. | We talked about a challenging project. |
| Positiv flertall | utfordrende | Vi snakket om to utfordrende jobber. | We talked about two challenging jobs. |
