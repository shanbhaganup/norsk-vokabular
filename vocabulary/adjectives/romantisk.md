## romantisk

**English:** romantic; picturesque; sentimental

**Topics:** emotions, culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | romantisk | De spiste middag på en romantisk restaurant. | They had dinner at a romantic restaurant. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | romantisk | Hun skrev ei romantisk novelle. | She wrote a romantic short story. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | romantisk | Det var et romantisk kjærlighetsforhold. | It was a romantic relationship. |
| Positiv flertall | romantiske | De romantiske diktene ble lest høyt. | The romantic poems were read aloud. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | romantiske | Den romantiske landsbyen lå ved fjorden. | The picturesque village was by the fjord. |
| Komparativ | mer romantisk | Kvelden ble mer romantisk da lysene ble tent. | The evening became more romantic when the lights were lit. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest romantisk | Av stedene var dette mest romantisk. | Of the places, this was the most romantic. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest romantiske | Hun valgte den mest romantiske filmen. | She chose the most romantic film. |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; analytical comparison used for the gradable mood/style senses.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers what belongs to Romanticism, something beautiful or atmospheric/picturesque, being guided by feelings and longings, and being rich in romance. No synthetic comparison is listed; *mer/mest* apply naturally to mood or romantic character, not the historical movement sense.
