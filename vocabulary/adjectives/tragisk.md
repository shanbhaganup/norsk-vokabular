## tragisk

**English:** tragic; disastrous; deeply sad

**Topics:** emotions, events

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | tragisk | Det var en tragisk ulykke på fjellet. | There was a tragic accident in the mountains. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | tragisk | Hun beskrev ei tragisk hending i romanen. | She described a tragic event in the novel. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | tragisk | Det var et tragisk utfall av konflikten. | It was a tragic outcome of the conflict. |
| Positiv flertall | tragiske | De tragiske hendelsene preget hele bygda. | The tragic events affected the whole village. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | tragiske | Den tragiske ulykken førte til en minnestund. | The tragic accident led to a memorial service. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer tragisk | Slutten på filmen var mer tragisk enn begynnelsen. | The ending of the film was more tragic than the beginning. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest tragisk | Av historiene var denne mest tragisk. | Of the stories, this one was the most tragic. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest tragiske | Det var den mest tragiske delen av fortellingen. | It was the most tragic part of the story. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement checked; mer/mest shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers something concerning a tragedy and something full of misfortune, sad or pessimistic. It also has adverbial use; that is not adjective inflection. No synthetic comparison is listed; mer/mest constructions are used for gradable intensity of tragedy.
