## vennlig

**English:** friendly; kind; pleasant

**Topics:** emotions, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | vennlig | Han ga en vennlig hilsen til naboen. | He gave the neighbour a friendly greeting. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | vennlig | Hun hadde ei vennlig stemme. | She had a friendly voice. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | vennlig | Det var et vennlig møte mellom kollegene. | It was a friendly meeting between the colleagues. |
| Positiv flertall | vennlige | De vennlige vertene ønsket alle velkommen. | The friendly hosts welcomed everyone. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | vennlige | Den vennlige tonen gjorde samtalen lettere. | The friendly tone made the conversation easier. |
| Komparativ | vennligere | Hun virket vennligere etter at de hadde snakket sammen. | She seemed friendlier after they had talked. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | vennligst | Av alle på kontoret var han vennligst mot besøkende. | Of everyone in the office, he was friendliest to visitors. |
| Superlativ bestemt | vennligste | Hun var den vennligste personen i resepsjonen. | She was the friendliest person at reception. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means welcoming, cheerful or pleasant, and can also describe an agreeable or inviting environment. The form *vennligst* is also used as a polite request word meaning “please”; that adverbial use is distinct from the superlative adjective.
