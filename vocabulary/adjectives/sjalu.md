## sjalu

**English:** jealous

**Topics:** emotions, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | sjalu | Han ble sjalu da vennen danset med henne. | He became jealous when his friend danced with her. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | sjalu | Hun var sjalu på søsterens nye kjæreste. | She was jealous of her sister’s new partner. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | sjalu | Barnet ble sjalu da babyen fikk oppmerksomhet. | The child became jealous when the baby got attention. |
| Positiv flertall | sjalu | De sjalu søsknene kranglet om arven. | The jealous siblings argued over the inheritance. |
| Positiv flertall, alternative | sjalue | De sjalue søsknene kranglet om arven. | The jealous siblings argued over the inheritance. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | sjalu | Den sjalu kjæresten ba om en forklaring. | The jealous partner asked for an explanation. |
| Positiv bestemt entall, alternative | sjalue | Den sjalue kjæresten ba om en forklaring. | The jealous partner asked for an explanation. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer sjalu | Han ble mer sjalu etter samtalen. | He became more jealous after the conversation. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest sjalu | Av alle vennene virket hun mest sjalu. | Of all the friends, she seemed the most jealous. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest sjalu | Hun var den mest sjalu personen i gruppa. | She was the most jealous person in the group. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement checked; mer/mest comparison shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary defines the word as suspicion or unease about a loved one’s fidelity or a rival taking one’s place, and also gives the broader synonym “envious.” The forms sjalu and sjalue are both accepted in plural and definite contexts; each is illustrated. Mer/mest are productive degree constructions rather than synthetic forms listed in the dictionary.
