## syk

**English:** sick; ill; unwell

**Topics:** health

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; accepted *syk/sjuk* variants separately exemplified.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists both *syk* and *sjuk* as accepted headword forms with parallel agreement and comparison paradigms. The reference record includes only the *syk* series; the *sjuk* alternatives have been added and exemplified. Other senses include being consumed by a longing or desire, abnormal or crazy, and an intensifying adverbial use; this entry focuses on illness. Substantivized uses are separate from adjective inflection.
