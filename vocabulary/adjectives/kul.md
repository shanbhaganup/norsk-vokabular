## kul

**English:** cool; fun; nice

**Topics:** describing, emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | kul | Han er en kul fyr. | He is a cool guy. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | kul | Det var ei kul forestilling. | It was a cool performance. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | kult | Det var et kult museum. | It was a cool museum. |
| Positiv flertall | kule | De har kule ideer. | They have cool ideas. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | kule | Vi så den kule filmen sammen. | We watched the cool film together. |
| Komparativ | kulere | Den nye restauranten er kulere enn den gamle. | The new restaurant is cooler than the old one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | kulest | Av alle aktivitetene var klatring kulest. | Of all the activities, climbing was the coolest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | kuleste | Hun valgte den kuleste jakka i butikken. | She chose the coolest jacket in the shop. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms and synthetic comparison verified.

**Notes:** This is the informal adjective meaning fun, nice or interesting (Bokmålsordboka entry kul 2 II). The reference also lists the neuter form kult as a source form; it is represented here as an agreement form under the lemma kul. The separate noun meaning a lump or swelling is not intended. Examples are original teaching sentences.
