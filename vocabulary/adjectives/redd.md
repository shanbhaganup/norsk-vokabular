## redd

**English:** afraid; scared; worried

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | redd | Han er redd for hunder. | He is afraid of dogs. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | redd | Hun ble redd da lyset gikk. | She got scared when the lights went out. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | redd | Barnet var redd for mørket. | The child was afraid of the dark. |
| Positiv flertall | redde | De redde barna holdt hverandre i hånda. | The frightened children held hands. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | redde | Den redde hunden gjemte seg under bordet. | The frightened dog hid under the table. |
| Komparativ | reddere | Jeg ble reddere da jeg hørte skrittene. | I became more frightened when I heard the footsteps. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | reddest | Av oss var han reddest for å gå inn alene. | Of us, he was the most afraid to go in alone. |
| Superlativ bestemt | reddeste | Hun var den reddeste deltakeren før start. | She was the most frightened participant before the start. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means feeling fear or being anxious/frightened. The common construction is *redd for* + noun or infinitive; the dictionary also uses *redd* in the sense “I’m afraid (that)” before a clause.
