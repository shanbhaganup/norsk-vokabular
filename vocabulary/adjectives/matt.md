## matt

**English:** dull; flat; weak; exhausted

**Topics:** describing, health, color

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | matt | Etter sykdommen følte han seg matt. | After the illness, he felt weak. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | matt | Hun syntes malingen var matt. | She thought the paint was dull. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | matt | Det var et matt lys i rommet. | There was a dull light in the room. |
| Positiv flertall | matte | De matte fargene passet godt sammen. | The muted colors went well together. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | matte | Den matte overflaten skjulte fingeravtrykkene. | The matte surface hid the fingerprints. |
| Komparativ | mattere | Denne fargen er mattere enn den blanke. | This color is duller than the glossy one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mattest | Av prøvene tørket denne mattest. | Of the samples, this one dried the dullest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | matteste | Velg den matteste finishen. | Choose the most matte finish. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** This entry focuses on the sense ‘lacking gloss / muted’, with examples also illustrating weak or exhausted use. The dictionary also lists *matt* in chess for a king that cannot be saved; that technical use is not detailed here. The cited article is adjective *matt 2 II*.
