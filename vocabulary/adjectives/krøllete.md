## krøllete

**English:** curly; crumpled

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | krøllete / krøllet | Han har krøllete / krøllet hår. | He has curly hair. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | krøllete / krøllet | Hun fant en krøllete / krøllet lapp. | She found a crumpled note. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | krøllete / krøllet | Et krøllete / krøllet ark lå på bordet. | A crumpled sheet lay on the table. |
| Positiv flertall | krøllete / krøllet | Krøllete / krøllete klær lå i kofferten. | Curly clothes were in the suitcase. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | krøllete / krøllet | Den krøllete / krøllete skjorta må strykes. | The crumpled shirt needs ironing. |
| Komparativ | mer krøllete | Håret hennes er mer krøllete enn før. | Her hair is curlier than before. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest krøllete | Av hundene har denne mest krøllete pels. | Of the dogs, this one has the curliest coat. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest krøllete | Dette er den mest krøllete skjorta. | This is the most crumpled shirt. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists krøllete and krøllet. The examples illustrate each variant in the first singular cells; “crumpled” is a distinct applicable sense.
