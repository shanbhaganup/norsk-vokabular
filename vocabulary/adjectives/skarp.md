## skarp

**English:** sharp; keen; severe

**Topics:** describing, perception

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | skarp | En skarp kniv ligger på bordet. | A sharp knife is on the table. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | skarp | Hun har ei skarp stemme. | She has a sharp voice. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | skarpt | Dette er et skarpt bilde. | This is a sharp image. |
| Positiv flertall | skarpe | Skarpe lyder vekket barnet. | Sharp sounds woke the child. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | skarpe | Den skarpe lukten forsvant. | The sharp smell disappeared. |
| Komparativ | skarpere | Denne kniven er skarpere enn den gamle. | This knife is sharper than the old one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | skarpest | Av knivene er denne skarpest. | Of the knives, this one is sharpest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | skarpeste | Dette er den skarpeste kniven. | This is the sharpest knife. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison checked against Bokmålsordboka; examples are original.

**Notes:** The adjective has several related senses, including keen, perceptive, intense, and severe. Bokmålsordboka also shows adverbial skarpt and the substantivized expression skyte med skarpt; neither is a separate noun record in the reference snapshot.
