## vanskelig

**English:** difficult; hard; complicated; troublesome

**Topics:** problem-solving, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | vanskelig | Han sto overfor en vanskelig oppgave. | He faced a difficult task. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | vanskelig | Hun måtte ta ei vanskelig avgjørelse. | She had to make a difficult decision. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | vanskelig | Det var et vanskelig spørsmål å svare på. | It was a difficult question to answer. |
| Positiv flertall | vanskelige | De vanskelige forholdene forsinket arbeidet. | The difficult conditions delayed the work. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | vanskelige | Den vanskelige saken krevde mer tid. | The difficult case required more time. |
| Komparativ | vanskeligere | Denne delen er vanskeligere enn den første. | This part is more difficult than the first. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | vanskeligst | Av oppgavene var denne vanskeligst. | Of the tasks, this one was the most difficult. |
| Superlativ bestemt | vanskeligste | Det var den vanskeligste avgjørelsen i år. | It was the most difficult decision this year. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary describes something that creates problems or is difficult to do, and also someone difficult to satisfy or get along with. Examples focus on tasks and situations. The adjective can appear in adverbial constructions, but these are not additional adjective forms.
