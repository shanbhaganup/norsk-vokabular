## bestandig

**English:** durable, resistant to chemical degradation

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | bestandig | Vi trenger en bestandig maling til stålet. | We need a durable paint for the steel. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | bestandig | Ei bestandig fugemasse beskytter skjøten mot saltvann. | A durable sealant protects the joint from salt water. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | bestandig | Et bestandig belegg verner metallet mot syra. | A resistant coating protects the metal from the acid. |
| Positiv flertall | bestandige | De bruker bestandige materialer i det kjemiske anlegget. | They use durable materials in the chemical plant. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | bestandige | Det bestandige belegget tålte flere måneder i saltvann. | The resistant coating withstood several months in salt water. |
| Komparativ | mer bestandig | Denne legeringen er mer bestandig mot saltvann enn den forrige. | This alloy is more resistant to salt water than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest bestandig | Av de tre beleggene er dette mest bestandig mot syre. | Of the three coatings, this one is the most resistant to acid. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest bestandige | De valgte den mest bestandige legeringen til tanken. | They chose the most durable alloy for the tank. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies positive agreement. Comparison with mer/mest is a productive construction. Examples are original.

**Notes:** The snapshot gloss “constant” is ambiguous. The adjective describes materials that resist degradation. Bokmålsordboka also lists an adverbial use meaning “always.” Use bestandig mot to specify what a material resists.
