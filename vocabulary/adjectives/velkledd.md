## velkledd

**English:** well-dressed; smartly dressed

**Topics:** clothing, appearance

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | velkledd | En velkledd gjest tok imot oss ved døra. | A well-dressed guest greeted us at the door. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | velkledd | Hun var velkledd til intervjuet. | She was well-dressed for the interview. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | velkledd | Et velkledd barn sto ved siden av foreldrene. | A well-dressed child stood beside the parents. |
| Positiv flertall | velkledde | De velkledde gjestene fylte restauranten. | The well-dressed guests filled the restaurant. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | velkledde | Den velkledde skuespilleren hilste på publikum. | The well-dressed actor greeted the audience. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer velkledd | Han var mer velkledd enn de andre på møtet. | He was better dressed than the others at the meeting. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest velkledd | Av gjestene var hun mest velkledd. | Of the guests, she was the best dressed. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest velkledde | Hun var den mest velkledde gjesten på festen. | She was the best-dressed guest at the party. |

**Form status:** Agreement follows the reference record and is supported by the Ordbøkene entry; mer/mest are productive constructions because no synthetic comparison is listed. Original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means wearing attractive or smart clothes. The cited reference dictionary page was not retrievable during this check; the agreement forms match the source record, while the official Nynorskordboka confirms the same *velkledd – velkledd – velkledde – velkledde* agreement pattern. Comparison is expressed productively with *mer/mest*.
