## viktig

**English:** important; significant; essential

**Topics:** work, decisions, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | viktig | Han fikk en viktig oppgave på jobben. | He was given an important task at work. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | viktig | Hun tok ei viktig avgjørelse for familien. | She made an important decision for the family. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | viktig | Dette er et viktig spørsmål for alle. | This is an important question for everyone. |
| Positiv flertall | viktige | De viktige dokumentene lå i en mappe. | The important documents were in a folder. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | viktige | Den viktige beskjeden kom sent på kvelden. | The important message arrived late in the evening. |
| Komparativ | viktigere | Søvn er viktigere enn enda en times arbeid. | Sleep is more important than another hour of work. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | viktigst | Det er viktigst å beholde roen. | The most important thing is to stay calm. |
| Superlativ bestemt | viktigste | Dette er den viktigste delen av planen. | This is the most important part of the plan. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means having much significance or importance. The dictionary also records a secondary sense meaning conceited or arrogant. The adjective can be substantivized, as in *det viktigste* (“the most important thing”); that is not a separate inflection.
