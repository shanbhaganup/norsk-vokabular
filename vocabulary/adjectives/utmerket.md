**Notes:** Means having distinguished oneself positively or being very good. Bokmålsordboka also lists the headword variant *utmerka*; its agreement paradigm remains to be checked before adding those alternative forms. The positive form also occurs adverbially, as in *maten smaker utmerket*; that is a related use, not adjective inflection.
