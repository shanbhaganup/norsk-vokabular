## verdifull

**English:** valuable; precious; highly regarded

**Topics:** money, work, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | verdifull | Han solgte en verdifull mynt på auksjon. | He sold a valuable coin at auction. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | verdifull | Hun tok vare på ei verdifull bok fra bestefaren. | She kept a valuable book from her grandfather. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | verdifullt | Dette er et verdifullt bidrag til arbeidet. | This is a valuable contribution to the work. |
| Positiv flertall | verdifulle | De verdifulle gjenstandene ble oppbevart trygt. | The valuable objects were stored safely. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | verdifulle | Det verdifulle rådet hjalp oss videre. | The valuable advice helped us move forward. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer verdifull | Denne erfaringen ble mer verdifull med tiden. | This experience became more valuable over time. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest verdifull | Av eiendelene var denne mest verdifull. | Of the possessions, this one was the most valuable. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest verdifulle | Det var det mest verdifulle rådet hun fikk. | It was the most valuable advice she received. |

**Form status:** Agreement verified; dictionary lists no synthetic comparison; mer/mest are productive for economic or personal value. Original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means having high economic value or being highly valued. Examples show both monetary and broader value. The dictionary does not list synthetic comparison; the examples use productive *mer/mest*.
