## variert

**English:** varied

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | variert | Vi snakket om en variert meny. | We talked about a varied menu. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | variert | Vi snakket om ei variert uke. | We talked about a varied week. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | variert | Vi snakket om et variert program. | We talked about a varied program. |
| Positiv flertall | varierte | Vi snakket om to varierte uker. | We talked about two varied weeks. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | varierte | Vi snakket om den varierte menyen. | We talked about the varied menu. |
| Komparativ | mer variert | Denne menyen er mer variert enn den andre. | This menu is more varied than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest variert | Av de to er denne menyen mest variert. | Of the two, this menu is the most varied. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest varierte | Vi snakket om den mest varierte menyen. | We talked about the most varied menu. |

**Form status:** Derived agreement and usage reviewed.

**Notes:** The compound or participial adjective follows variere. Comparison with mer and mest is a productive construction. NoW source form: “variert”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
