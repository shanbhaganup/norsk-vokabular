## negativ

**English:** negative; unfavorable; critical

**Topics:** emotions, evaluation

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | negativ | Han fikk en negativ beskjed. | He received negative news. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | negativ | Hun hadde en negativ holdning til planen. | She had a negative attitude toward the plan. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | negativt | Resultatet var et negativt signal. | The result was a negative signal. |
| Positiv flertall | negative | De negative sidene ble også diskutert. | The negative aspects were also discussed. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | negative | Den negative testen overrasket oss. | The negative test surprised us. |
| Komparativ | mer negativ | Stemningen ble mer negativ utover kvelden. | The mood became more negative as the evening went on. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest negativ | Av reaksjonene var hans mest negativ. | Of the reactions, his was the most negative. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest negative | Dette var den mest negative vurderingen. | This was the most negative assessment. |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; analytical comparison used for evaluative senses.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes negating, unfavorable, discouraging, unwilling/critical, opposite, medically negative, and below-zero senses. No synthetic comparison is listed; *mer/mest* are natural for the evaluative or critical senses but are not appropriate to every technical use.
