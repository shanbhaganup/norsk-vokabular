## lat

**English:** lazy; gradual (slope)

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | lat | Han er en lat student. | He is a lazy student. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | lat | Hun var lat hele søndagen. | She was lazy all Sunday. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | lat | Dette er et lat tempo. | This is a slow pace. |
| Positiv flertall | late | Late ungdommer kom sent i gang. | The lazy teenagers got started late. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | late | Den late katten sov videre. | The lazy cat kept sleeping. |
| Komparativ | latere | Han virker latere enn broren. | He seems lazier than his brother. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | latest | Av oss sto hun latest opp. | Of us, she got up latest/laziest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | lateste | Han er den lateste eleven i klassen. | He is the laziest student in the class. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The dictionary also uses lat for a gentle slope; examples here represent the person/effort sense. It gives the synthetic comparison latere/latest/lateste.
