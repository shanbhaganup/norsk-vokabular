## velstående

**English:** wealthy; prosperous; well-off

**Topics:** money, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | velstående | Han vokste opp i en velstående familie. | He grew up in a wealthy family. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | velstående | Hun kom fra ei velstående slekt. | She came from a prosperous family. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | velstående | De flyttet inn i et velstående hjem med god plass. | They moved into a well-off home with plenty of space. |
| Positiv flertall | velstående | Velstående innbyggere støttet prosjektet. | Wealthy residents supported the project. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | velstående | Den velstående naboen finansierte stipendet. | The wealthy neighbour funded the scholarship. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer velstående | Regionen ble mer velstående etter at fabrikken åpnet. | The region became more prosperous after the factory opened. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest velstående | Av kommunene var denne mest velstående. | Of the municipalities, this one was the most prosperous. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest velstående | Hun kom fra den mest velstående delen av byen. | She came from the wealthiest part of the city. |

**Form status:** Invariant positive form verified; mer/mest are productive constructions for the gradable wealth sense. Original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means having substantial financial means. The dictionary gives the same positive form across agreement contexts and lists no synthetic comparison; productive *mer/mest* is used for relative wealth.
