## moden

**English:** ripe; mature; ready

**Topics:** food, people, time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | moden | En moden fersken er søt. | The apple is ripe, but the pear is ripe. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | moden | Ei moden pære er søt. | Picked a ripe apple. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | modent | Kornet er modent nå. | The grain is ripe now. |
| Positiv flertall | modne | Tomatene er modne. | The tomatoes are ripe. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | modne | Den modne frukten ble høstet i går. | The ripe fruit was harvested yesterday. |
| Komparativ | modnere | De ventet til frukten ble modnere. | They waited until the fruit was riper. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | modnest | Denne pæren er modnest av alle. | These are the berries that ripened the ripest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | modneste | Vi plukket de modneste plommene. | We picked the ripest plums. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Covers fully developed or ready to eat, as well as mature and ready (for example, an idea or the time being right). The superlative *modnest* is unusual; the example makes its degree meaning explicit.
