## levende

**English:** living; alive; vivid

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | levende | En levende skapning trenger vann. | A living creature needs water. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | levende | Ei levende plante trenger lys. | A living plant needs light. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | levende | Et levende vesen reagerer på omgivelsene. | A living being reacts to its surroundings. |
| Positiv flertall | levende | Levende organismer finnes også under bakken. | Living organisms are also found underground. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | levende | Den levende pasienten ble fraktet til sykehuset. | The living patient was taken to the hospital. |
| Komparativ, vivid sense | mer levende | Denne framstillingen virker mer levende enn den forrige. | This account feels more vivid than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt, vivid sense | mest levende | Av alle fortellingene virker denne mest levende. | Of all the stories, this one seems the most vivid. |
| Superlativ bestemt, vivid sense | mest levende | Det var den mest levende framstillingen i utstillingen. | It was the most vivid account in the exhibition. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the invariable positive agreement forms. Comparison is a periphrastic construction in the gradable sense “vivid” and does not apply to the literal sense “alive.”

**Notes:** The reference gloss “living” matches the literal sense. Mer/mest are included only for the dictionary’s gradable “vivid, lively” sense. The word also has adverbial and substantival uses, which are separate from adjective inflection.
