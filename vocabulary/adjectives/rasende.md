## rasende

**English:** furious; enraged; tremendous (of speed or pace)

**Topics:** emotions, movement

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | rasende | En rasende mann forlot rommet. | A furious man left the room. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | rasende | Hun ble rasende over den urettferdige avgjørelsen. | She became furious about the unfair decision. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | rasende | Det var et rasende tempo i finalen. | The pace in the final was tremendous. |
| Positiv flertall | rasende | De rasende kundene krevde svar. | The furious customers demanded answers. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | rasende | Den rasende folkemengden ropte høyt. | The furious crowd shouted loudly. |
| Komparativ | mer rasende | Han ble mer rasende da ingen svarte. | He became more furious when nobody answered. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest rasende | Av alle var hun mest rasende over behandlingen. | Of all of them, she was the most furious about the treatment. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest rasende | Det var den mest rasende reaksjonen på møtet. | It was the most furious reaction at the meeting. |

**Form status:** Invariant agreement checked; analytical comparison used for emotional intensity.

**Notes:** The adjective is invariant across agreement forms. It means very angry and can also mean very great/fast, as in *rasende fart* or *rasende tempo*. It is also used as an intensifying adverb before another adjective; that is a separate use.
