## sjokkerende

**English:** shocking; disturbing

**Topics:** emotions, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | sjokkerende | Det var en sjokkerende beskjed. | It was shocking news. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | sjokkerende | Hun fortalte ei sjokkerende historie. | She told a shocking story. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | sjokkerende | Det var et sjokkerende funn. | It was a shocking discovery. |
| Positiv flertall | sjokkerende | De sjokkerende bildene ble vist på TV. | The shocking images were shown on TV. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | sjokkerende | Det sjokkerende synet gjorde alle stille. | The shocking sight silenced everyone. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer sjokkerende | Den nye avsløringen var mer sjokkerende enn den første. | The new revelation was more shocking than the first. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest sjokkerende | Av alle nyhetene var dette mest sjokkerende. | Of all the news, this was the most shocking. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest sjokkerende | Dette var den mest sjokkerende avsløringen. | This was the most shocking revelation. |

**Form status:** Invariable agreement checked; mer/mest comparison shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Present participle of sjokkere, also used adjectivally. As an adjective it is invariable for gender, number and definiteness. The dictionary also notes adverbial use (for example, sjokkerende dårlig); this entry covers the adjectival use. The degree constructions are productive, not synthetic forms listed by the dictionary.
