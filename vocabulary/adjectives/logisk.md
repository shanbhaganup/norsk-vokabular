## logisk

**English:** logical

**Topics:** reasoning

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | logisk | Han fant et logisk svar. | He found a logical answer. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | logisk | Hun fulgte en logisk rekkefølge. | She followed a logical sequence. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | logisk | Dette er et logisk resonnement. | This is a logical argument. |
| Positiv flertall | logiske | De logiske trinnene var tydelige. | The logical steps were clear. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | logiske | Den logiske forklaringen overbeviste oss. | The logical explanation convinced us. |
| Komparativ | mer logisk | Den nye forklaringen er mer logisk. | The new explanation is more logical. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest logisk | Av alternativene virker dette mest logisk. | Of the options, this seems the most logical. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest logiske | Dette er den mest logiske konklusjonen. | This is the most logical conclusion. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; examples are original teaching sentences.
