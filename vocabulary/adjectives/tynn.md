## tynn

**English:** thin; slender; diluted; sparse

**Topics:** describing, materials

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | tynn | Han skar en tynn skive av osten. | He cut a thin slice of the cheese. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | tynn | Hun fant ei tynn grein langs stien. | She found a thin branch beside the path. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | tynt | Dette er et tynt papirark. | This is a thin sheet of paper. |
| Positiv flertall | tynne | De tynne strekene ble vanskelige å se. | The thin lines became hard to see. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | tynne | Den tynne jakka var ikke varm nok. | The thin jacket was not warm enough. |
| Komparativ | tynnere | Isen er tynnere midt ute på vannet. | The ice is thinner farther out on the lake. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | tynnest | Av papirarkene er dette tynnest. | Of the sheets of paper, this one is thinnest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | tynneste | Hun valgte den tynneste streken på tegningen. | She chose the thinnest line in the drawing. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Dictionary senses include having little thickness or circumference, lean, runny or diluted, sparse, and lacking substance. Examples here focus on physical thinness. Adverbial *tynt* is a related use, not an adjective form.
