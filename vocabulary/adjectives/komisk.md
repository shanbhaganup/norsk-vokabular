## komisk

**English:** comic; funny

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | komisk | Han så en komisk film. | He watched a funny film. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | komisk | Hun så ei komisk forestilling. | She watched a funny performance. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | komisk | Det var et komisk øyeblikk. | It was a comic moment. |
| Positiv flertall | komiske | De komiske scenene fikk oss til å le. | The comic scenes made us laugh. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | komiske | Den komiske scenen fikk alle til å le. | The comic scene made everyone laugh. |
| Komparativ | mer komisk | Denne scenen er mer komisk enn den forrige. | This scene is funnier than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest komisk | Av alle scenene er denne mest komisk. | Of all the scenes, this one is the funniest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest komiske | Dette er den mest komiske scenen i stykket. | This is the funniest scene in the play. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the positive agreement forms. Mer/mest comparison is a productive construction illustrated with the laugh-inducing sense. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka has senses “that evokes laughter” and “relating to comedy.” Comparison here concerns the laugh-inducing sense and is formed periphrastically.
