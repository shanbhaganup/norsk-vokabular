## smal

**English:** narrow; slim; specialized

**Topics:** describing, size

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | smal | De gikk gjennom en smal passasje. | They walked through a narrow passage. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | smal | Hun fant ei smal gate bak torget. | She found a narrow street behind the square. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | smalt | Båten passerte et smalt sund. | The boat passed through a narrow strait. |
| Positiv flertall | smale | Smale veier fører opp til hytta. | Narrow roads lead up to the cabin. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | smale | Den smale broa var stengt for biler. | The narrow bridge was closed to cars. |
| Komparativ | smalere | Denne veien er smalere enn hovedveien. | This road is narrower than the main road. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | smalest | Elva er smalest ved den gamle brua. | The river is narrowest by the old bridge. |
| Superlativ bestemt | smaleste | De tok den smaleste stien gjennom skogen. | They took the narrowest path through the forest. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary also uses *smal* for being thin/slim and for work or writing directed at a small audience. Examples primarily illustrate width. The source gloss “narrow” is accurate for the core sense but not exhaustive.
