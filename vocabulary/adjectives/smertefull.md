## smertefull

**English:** painful

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | smertefull | Det var en smertefull skade. | It was a painful injury. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | smertefull | Hun hadde ei smertefull opplevelse. | She had a painful experience. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | smertefullt | Det var et smertefullt inngrep. | It was a painful procedure. |
| Positiv flertall | smertefulle | De smertefulle krampene kom tilbake. | The painful cramps returned. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | smertefulle | Den smertefulle behandlingen tok slutt etter en time. | The painful treatment ended after an hour. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer smertefull | Den andre behandlingen var mer smertefull enn den første. | The second treatment was more painful than the first. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest smertefull | Av inngrepene var dette mest smertefullt. | Of the procedures, this one was the most painful. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest smertefulle | Det var den mest smertefulle delen av behandlingen. | It was the most painful part of the treatment. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement checked; mer/mest shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means causing (considerable) pain. The dictionary lists no synthetic comparison; because degree of pain is gradable, the examples use productive mer/mest constructions rather than claiming dictionary-listed forms.
