## rød

**English:** red; ruddy

**Topics:** color, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | rød | Han kjøpte en rød bil. | He bought a red car. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | rød | Hun plukket ei rød rose. | She picked a red rose. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | rødt | De malte et rødt gjerde. | They painted a red fence. |
| Positiv flertall | røde | De røde lampene sto i vinduet. | The red lamps stood in the window. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | røde | Den røde kjolen hang i skapet. | The red dress was hanging in the closet. |
| Komparativ | rødere | Eplet ble rødere etter hvert. | The apple grew redder over time. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | rødest | Av eplene var dette rødest. | Of the apples, this one was the reddest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | rødeste | Hun valgte den rødeste genseren. | She chose the reddest sweater. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Primarily means having a color like blood. The dictionary also gives a political “radical” sense; this entry focuses on the color sense and its same adjective paradigm.
