## magisk

**English:** magical; enchanting

**Topics:** describing, fantasy

**Notes:** The dictionary covers what concerns or is characterized by magic, something with a special power or significance, and something that evokes fascination and wonder. It lists no synthetic comparison; *mer/mest* are used analytically for the gradable ‘enchanting’ sense.
