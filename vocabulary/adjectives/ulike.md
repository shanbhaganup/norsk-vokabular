## ulike

**English:** uneven; unequal

**Topics:** competition, numbers

**Form status:** The dictionary lists *ulike* as invariant in all agreement forms; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** This entry follows the distinct dictionary headword *ulike 2 II*, meaning not evenly matched or uneven, as in *en ulike kamp* and the fixed phrase *ulike tall* (“odd numbers”). It is not the ordinary plural/definite form of *ulik* meaning “different”; that form belongs to the separate lemma *ulik*. The sense here is uncommon and can sound formal or dated outside established uses.
