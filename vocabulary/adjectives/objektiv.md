## objektiv

**English:** objective, impartial

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | objektiv | Han skrev en objektiv rapport. | He wrote an objective report. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | objektiv | Hun ga en objektiv vurdering. | She gave an impartial assessment. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | objektivt | Dette er et objektivt mål. | This is an objective measure. |
| Positiv flertall | objektive | De objektive kriteriene var tydelige. | The objective criteria were clear. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | objektive | Den objektive framstillingen hjalp leserne. | The objective account helped readers. |
| Komparativ | mer objektiv | En uavhengig vurdering kan være mer objektiv. | An independent assessment can be more objective. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest objektiv | Av vurderingene var denne mest objektiv. | Of the assessments, this was the most objective. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest objektive | Dette er den mest objektive framstillingen. | This is the most objective account. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka sense/forms checked; examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary also attests adverbial objektivt and a distinct neuter noun objektiv meaning the lens nearest the object in an optical instrument; both cross-POS entries are included.
