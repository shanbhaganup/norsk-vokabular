## voldelig

**English:** violent; involving violence; prone to violence

**Topics:** society, conflict

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | voldelig | Politiet stanset en voldelig demonstrant. | The police stopped a violent demonstrator. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | voldelig | Hun forlot et voldelig forhold. | She left an abusive relationship. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | voldelig | Filmen inneholder et voldelig overfall. | The film contains a violent assault. |
| Positiv flertall | voldelige | Voldelige sammenstøt førte til skader. | Violent clashes caused injuries. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | voldelige | Den voldelige handlingen ble etterforsket. | The violent act was investigated. |
| Komparativ | voldeligere | Denne filmen er voldeligere enn den forrige. | This film is more violent than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | voldeligst | Av gruppene var denne voldeligst. | Of the groups, this one was the most violent. |
| Superlativ bestemt | voldeligste | Dette var den voldeligste episoden i serien. | This was the most violent episode in the series. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Covers both “involving or carried out with violence” and “using or inclined to use violence.” The comparison is appropriate for degrees of violence; it does not imply that the people or events are comparable in every context.
