## svart

**English:** black; dark; gloomy; illicit

**Topics:** describing, color

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | svart | Han kjøpte en svart sykkel. | He bought a black bicycle. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | svart | Hun fant ei svart veske under stolen. | She found a black bag under the chair. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | svart | Det var et svart hull på jakka. | There was a black hole in the jacket. |
| Positiv flertall | svarte | De svarte skoene sto ved døra. | The black shoes were by the door. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | svarte | Den svarte katten sov i vinduet. | The black cat slept in the window. |
| Komparativ | svartere | Kvelden ble svartere da skyene dekket månen. | The evening grew darker as clouds covered the moon. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | svartest | Av rommene var kjelleren svartest. | Of the rooms, the basement was the darkest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | svarteste | De gikk gjennom den svarteste delen av skogen. | They walked through the darkest part of the forest. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes the color black, darkness or desolation, dirty, densely crowded, bleak or disastrous, and illegal or undeclared activity (such as svart arbeid). It also has substantivized and adverbial uses; these are separate from adjective inflection. Examples focus on color and darkness.
