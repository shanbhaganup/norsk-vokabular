## rettferdig

**English:** fair; just; deserved

**Topics:** society, law

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | rettferdig | En rettferdig dommer lyttet til begge sider. | A fair judge listened to both sides. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | rettferdig | Hun foreslo ei rettferdig fordeling. | She proposed a fair distribution. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | rettferdig | Det var et rettferdig vedtak. | It was a just decision. |
| Positiv flertall | rettferdige | De rettferdige reglene gjelder for alle. | The fair rules apply to everyone. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | rettferdige | Den rettferdige løsningen fikk bred støtte. | The fair solution received broad support. |
| Komparativ | mer rettferdig | Den nye ordningen virker mer rettferdig enn den gamle. | The new arrangement seems fairer than the old one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest rettferdig | Av forslagene var dette mest rettferdig. | Of the proposals, this was the fairest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest rettferdige | De valgte den mest rettferdige løsningen. | They chose the fairest solution. |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; analytical comparison used for degree of fairness.

**Notes:** Means in accordance with law or morality, or considered correct, deserved or reasonable. The dictionary lists no synthetic comparison; *mer/mest* can compare how fair different decisions or distributions are. A religious sense is also listed.
