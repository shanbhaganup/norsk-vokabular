## takknemlig

**English:** grateful; thankful; gratifying

**Topics:** emotions, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | takknemlig | Han var takknemlig for hjelpen. | He was grateful for the help. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | takknemlig | Hun ble takknemlig da naboen tilbød seg å kjøre. | She was grateful when the neighbor offered a ride. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | takknemlig | Det var et takknemlig publikum som lyttet nøye. | It was an appreciative audience that listened carefully. |
| Positiv flertall | takknemlige | De takknemlige gjestene sendte et kort etterpå. | The grateful guests sent a card afterward. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | takknemlige | Det takknemlige blikket hennes sa alt. | Her grateful look said it all. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer takknemlig | Jeg ble mer takknemlig for støtten etter hvert. | I grew more grateful for the support over time. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest takknemlig | Av alle var han mest takknemlig for besøket. | Of them all, he was most grateful for the visit. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest takknemlige | De mest takknemlige mottakerne skrev personlige brev. | The most grateful recipients wrote personal letters. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement checked; mer/mest shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes feeling gratitude, showing gratitude, something easy to satisfy, and something that easily succeeds or pays off (for example, a gratifying subject for a book). The source gloss “grateful” covers the first two meanings. No synthetic degree forms are listed; mer/mest constructions are productive for the gradable grateful sense.
