## rask

**English:** fast; quick; healthy

**Topics:** describing, movement, health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | rask | Han er en rask løper. | He is a fast runner. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | rask | Hun er ei rask jente. | She is a fast girl. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | raskt | Det var et raskt svar. | It was a quick answer. |
| Positiv flertall | raske | De raske hestene løp først. | The fast horses ran first. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | raske | Den raske bilen svingte til venstre. | The fast car turned left. |
| Komparativ | raskere | Denne ruten er raskere enn den andre. | This route is faster than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | raskest | Av deltakerne løp hun raskest. | Of the participants, she ran the fastest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | raskeste | Han tok den raskeste veien hjem. | He took the fastest way home. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The main sense is quick or fast; the dictionary also gives “healthy/fresh” in expressions such as *frisk og rask*. The word is also used adverbially as *raskt*, which is not a separate adjective form.
