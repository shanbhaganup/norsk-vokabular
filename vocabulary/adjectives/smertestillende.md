## smertestillende

**English:** pain-relieving; analgesic

**Topics:** health, medicine

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | smertestillende | Legen foreslo en smertestillende tablett. | The doctor suggested a pain-relieving tablet. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | smertestillende | Hun fikk ei smertestillende sprøyte. | She was given a pain-relieving injection. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | smertestillende | De brukte et smertestillende middel. | They used a pain-relieving medicine. |
| Positiv flertall | smertestillende | Smertestillende medisiner kan gi bivirkninger. | Pain-relieving medicines can cause side effects. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | smertestillende | Den smertestillende medisinen virket raskt. | The pain-relieving medicine worked quickly. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer smertestillende | Dette middelet virker mer smertestillende enn det forrige. | This medicine is more effective at relieving pain than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest smertestillende | Av alternativene virket denne behandlingen mest smertestillende. | Of the options, this treatment seemed most effective at relieving pain. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest smertestillende | De valgte den mest smertestillende behandlingen. | They chose the treatment that relieved pain the most. |

**Form status:** Invariable agreement checked; mer/mest shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Invariable adjective meaning “that dulls or removes pain.” The dictionary also records substantivized use (*få smertestillende*); that is a separate syntactic use, not an inflected adjective form. The degree examples compare efficacy productively and are not synthetic forms listed in the dictionary.
