## offentlig

**English:** public; open to the public; governmental

**Topics:** society, government

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

| Positiv hunkjønn | offentlig | Hun ba om offentlig informasjon. | She requested public information. |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; comparison inapplicable to the public/institutional senses.

**Notes:** Describes something open to the public, concerning everyone, publicly known, or relating to government and the state. No comparison forms are listed; the senses are relational rather than gradable. *Offentlig* can also be used substantivally in *det offentlige* (“the public sector”).
