## kulturell

**English:** cultural

**Topics:** culture, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | kulturell | Han deltar i en kulturell debatt. | He is taking part in a cultural debate. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | kulturell | Hun arbeider i ei kulturell stiftelse. | She works at a cultural foundation. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | kulturelt | Museet er et kulturelt møtested. | The museum is a cultural meeting place. |
| Positiv flertall | kulturelle | Byen har mange kulturelle tilbud. | The city has many cultural activities. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | kulturelle | Den kulturelle arven blir tatt vare på. | The cultural heritage is being preserved. |
| Komparativ | mer kulturell | Festivalen gjør sentrum mer kulturell om sommeren. | The festival makes the town centre more cultural in the summer. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest kulturell | Av disse bydelene virker denne mest kulturell. | Of these districts, this one seems the most cultural. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest kulturelle | Det er den mest kulturelle delen av byen. | It is the most cultural part of the city. |

**Form status:** Agreement verified; comparison is periphrastic and context-dependent.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists agreement forms but no synthetic comparison. Mer/mest are productive here when comparing the degree to which a place or event has cultural activity; usage depends on context. Examples are original teaching sentences.
