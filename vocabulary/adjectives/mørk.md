## mørk

**English:** dark; dim; deep (of color or sound); bleak

**Topics:** describing, color, light

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | mørk | Vi gikk gjennom en mørk tunnel. | We walked through a dark tunnel. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | mørk | Hun hadde ei mørk jakke på seg. | She was wearing a dark jacket. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | mørkt | Det var et mørkt rom uten vinduer. | It was a dark room without windows. |
| Positiv flertall | mørke | Mørke skyer samlet seg over fjellet. | Dark clouds gathered over the mountain. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | mørke | Den mørke natta var helt stille. | The dark night was completely quiet. |
| Komparativ | mørkere | Himmelen ble mørkere før regnet kom. | The sky grew darker before the rain came. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mørkest | Dette rommet er mørkest om kvelden. | This room is darkest in the evening. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mørkeste | Hun valgte den mørkeste fargen. | She chose the darkest color. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers lack of light, near-black color, low-pitched sound, bleakness, and obscurity. This entry uses darkness and color examples; other senses are also valid with the same inflection.
