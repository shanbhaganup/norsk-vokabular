## tiltrekkende

**English:** attractive; appealing; alluring

**Topics:** describing, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | tiltrekkende | Han er en tiltrekkende person. | He is an attractive person. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | tiltrekkende | Hun fant ei tiltrekkende leilighet i sentrum. | She found an appealing apartment downtown. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | tiltrekkende | De presenterte et tiltrekkende tilbud. | They presented an attractive offer. |
| Positiv flertall | tiltrekkende | Tiltrekkende reisemål kan bli overfylte om sommeren. | Attractive destinations can become overcrowded in summer. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | tiltrekkende | Den tiltrekkende aromaen kom fra kjøkkenet. | The appealing aroma came from the kitchen. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer tiltrekkende | Toget ble et mer tiltrekkende alternativ etter prisfallet. | The train became a more attractive option after the price reduction. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest tiltrekkende | Av forslagene virket dette mest tiltrekkende. | Of the proposals, this seemed the most appealing. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest tiltrekkende | Hun valgte det mest tiltrekkende alternativet. | She chose the most attractive option. |

**Form status:** Invariable agreement checked; mer/mest shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Adjective derived from *tiltrekke*, meaning that something is appealing or looks attractive. It is invariable for gender, number and definiteness. The meaning is gradable, so mer/mest constructions are included as productive forms; they are not synthetic dictionary forms.
