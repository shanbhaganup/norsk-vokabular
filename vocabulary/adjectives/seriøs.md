## seriøs

**English:** serious; earnest; reliable; substantial (of art)

**Topics:** communication, character, culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | seriøs | Han stilte et seriøst spørsmål. | He asked a serious question. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | seriøs | Hun førte en seriøs diskusjon. | She led a serious discussion. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | seriøst | Dette er et seriøst forslag. | This is a serious proposal. |
| Positiv flertall | seriøse | De seriøse aktørene følger reglene. | The reputable operators follow the rules. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | seriøse | Den seriøse musikeren øvde hver dag. | The serious musician practised every day. |
| Komparativ | seriøsere | Denne framstillingen er seriøsere enn den forrige. | This treatment is more serious than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | seriøsest | Av kandidatene tok hun oppgaven seriøsest. | Of the candidates, she took the task most seriously. |
| Superlativ bestemt | seriøseste | Dette er den seriøseste diskusjonen vi har hatt. | This is the most serious discussion we have had. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers something meant to be taken seriously, something reliable and solid, and art with depth or high status and quality. The examples use the serious/reliable meanings. The adverb seriøst is a related adverbial form, not an adjective inflection.
