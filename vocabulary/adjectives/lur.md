## lur

**English:** clever; smart; cunning

**Topics:** describing, personality

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | lur | Han fant en lur løsning. | He found a clever solution. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | lur | Hun er ei lur jente. | She is a clever girl. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | lurt | Det var et lurt forslag. | It was a clever suggestion. |
| Positiv flertall | lure | De lurte barna løste gåten. | The clever children solved the riddle. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | lure | Den lure planen fungerte. | The clever plan worked. |
| Komparativ | lurere | Denne framgangsmåten er lurere. | This approach is cleverer. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | lurest | Av forslagene var dette lurest. | Of the suggestions, this was the cleverest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | lureste | Hun valgte den lureste løsningen. | She chose the cleverest solution. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** This entry covers the ‘clever/smart/cunning’ adjective sense (Bokmålsordboka sense 1). The dictionary also gives ‘far-sighted’ and ‘treacherous/deceptive’ senses; those meanings are excluded. The source's imported form *lurt* is the neuter adjective form, not a separate lemma.
