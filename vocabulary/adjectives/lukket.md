## lukket

**English:** closed

**Topics:** everyday

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | lukket | Han fant en lukket butikk. | He found a closed shop. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | lukket | Hun lukket en lukket dør bak seg. | She closed a closed door behind her. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | lukka / lukket | Et lukka / et lukket vindu holdt på varmen. | A closed window kept the heat in. |
| Positiv flertall | lukka / lukkede / lukkete | Lukka / lukkede / lukkete butikker åpner senere. | Closed shops open later. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | lukka / lukkede / lukkete | Den lukka / lukkede / lukkete døra var tung. | The closed door was heavy. |
| Komparativ | mer lukket | Dette rommet virker mer lukket enn det andre. | This room seems more closed off than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest lukket | Av rommene er dette mest lukket. | Of the rooms, this is the most closed off. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest lukkede | Dette er det mest lukkede rommet. | This is the most closed-off room. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Past participle of lukke. Bokmålsordboka accepts lukka, lukket, lukkede, lukkete; examples illustrate listed alternatives.
