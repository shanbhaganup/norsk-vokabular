## grei

**English:** straightforward; nice (of a person); fine

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | grei | Han er en grei kollega. | He is a nice colleague. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | grei | Hun ga ei grei forklaring. | She gave a clear explanation. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | greit | Vi fikk et greit svar. | We got a straightforward answer. |
| Positiv flertall | greie | De greie naboene hjalp oss. | The nice neighbors helped us. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | greie | Den greie løsningen sparte tid. | The straightforward solution saved time. |
| Komparativ | greiere | Denne ordningen er greiere for alle. | This arrangement is easier for everyone. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | greiest | Av forslagene er dette greiest. | Of the suggestions, this is the most straightforward. |
| Superlativ bestemt | greieste | Vi valgte den greieste løsningen. | We chose the most straightforward solution. |

**Form status:** Verified

**Notes:** The adjective has several related senses, including clear/simple, amiable (of a person), and in order/problem-free. Examples here use these ordinary adjective uses. The dictionary also lists an adverbial use of greit; that is not an adjective form.
