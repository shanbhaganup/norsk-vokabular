## vond

**English:** painful; unpleasant; harmful; difficult; angry; wicked

**Topics:** health, feelings, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | vond | Han har en vond tann. | He has a painful tooth. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | vond | Hun fikk ei vond overraskelse. | She got an unpleasant surprise. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | vondt | Barnet hadde et vondt sår på kneet. | The child had a painful wound on the knee. |
| Positiv flertall | vonde | De vonde minnene kom tilbake. | The painful memories came back. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | vonde | Den vonde lukta fylte rommet. | The unpleasant smell filled the room. |
| Komparativ | verre / vondere | Smerten ble verre etter turen. / Denne lukta er vondere enn den andre. | The pain got worse after the trip. / This smell is more unpleasant than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | verst / vondest | Av alle dagene var mandag verst. / Dette er vondest når jeg går. | Of all the days, Monday was the worst. / This hurts the most when I walk. |
| Superlativ bestemt | verste / vondeste | Det var den verste smerten hun hadde kjent. / Dette er det vondeste såret på armen. | It was the worst pain she had ever felt. / This is the most painful wound on the arm. |

**Form status:** Agreement and dictionary-listed comparison alternatives verified; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The adjective has several senses: unpleasant, painful or harmful; difficult/problematic; angry; and wicked. The comparison has two accepted series, *vond–verre–verst* and *vond–vondere–vondest*, with corresponding definite superlatives *verste* and *vondeste*. The alternatives are illustrated separately in each comparison cell. Adverbial *vondt* is a related use, not an adjective agreement form beyond neuter *vondt*.
