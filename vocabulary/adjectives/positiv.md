## positiv

**English:** positive; favorable; affirmative; optimistic

**Topics:** emotions, communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | positiv | Han fikk en positiv tilbakemelding. | He received positive feedback. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | positiv | Hun har ei positiv innstilling til endring. | She has a positive attitude toward change. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | positivt | Det var et positivt svar fra legen. | It was a positive answer from the doctor. |
| Positiv flertall | positive | De positive resultatene ga laget nytt håp. | The positive results gave the team new hope. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | positive | Den positive utviklingen fortsatte utover våren. | The positive trend continued into the spring. |
| Komparativ | mer positiv | Hun ble mer positiv etter møtet. | She became more positive after the meeting. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest positiv | Av reaksjonene var hans mest positiv. | Of the reactions, his was the most positive. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest positive | Dette var den mest positive vurderingen. | This was the most positive assessment. |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; analytical comparison used for the gradable attitude/evaluation senses.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers favorable or affirming, better than expected, encouraging, factual, positive test results, positive numbers, and the positive electrode. No synthetic comparison is listed; *mer/mest* apply to evaluative or attitudinal senses, not all technical uses.
