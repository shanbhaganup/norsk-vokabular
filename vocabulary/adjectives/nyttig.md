## nyttig

**English:** useful; helpful; beneficial

**Topics:** learning, health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | nyttig | Dette er en nyttig guide. | This is a useful guide. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | nyttig | Hun ga meg ei nyttig tilbakemelding. | She gave me helpful feedback. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | nyttig | Det var et nyttig verktøy. | It was a useful tool. |
| Positiv flertall | nyttige | Kurset ga oss nyttige råd. | The course gave us useful advice. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | nyttige | Den nyttige opplysningen hjalp oss videre. | The useful information helped us move forward. |
| Komparativ | nyttigere | Denne appen er nyttigere enn den gamle. | This app is more useful than the old one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | nyttigst | Av rådene var dette nyttigst. | Of the suggestions, this was the most useful. |
| Superlativ bestemt | nyttigste | Hun valgte det nyttigste hjelpemiddelet. | She chose the most useful aid. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means providing benefit or help. The dictionary lists *nyttigere, nyttigst,* and *nyttigste*. It also appears in fixed expressions such as *gjøre seg nyttig*; these are constructions rather than inflections.
