## kunstig

**English:** artificial, synthetic; unnatural

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | kunstig | Han fikk en kunstig hofte. | He got an artificial hip. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | kunstig | Hun brukte en kunstig blomst. | She used an artificial flower. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | kunstig | Dette er et kunstig søtstoff. | This is an artificial sweetener. |
| Positiv flertall | kunstige | De kunstige materialene er lette. | The artificial materials are light. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | kunstige | Den kunstige stemmen hørtes mekanisk ut. | The artificial voice sounded mechanical. |
| Komparativ | kunstigere | Denne blomsten ser kunstigere ut enn den andre. | This flower looks more artificial than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | kunstigst | Av stemmene hørtes denne kunstigst ut. | Of the voices, this sounded the most artificial. |
| Superlativ bestemt | kunstigste | Dette er den kunstigste løsningen. | This is the most artificial solution. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Dictionary also attests adverbial kunstig; a separate adverb entry records it. Covers both made by humans and unnatural senses.
