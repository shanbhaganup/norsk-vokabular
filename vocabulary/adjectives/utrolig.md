## utrolig

**English:** incredible; unbelievable; astonishing

**Topics:** describing, events

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | utrolig | Han fortalte en utrolig historie om reisen. | He told an incredible story about the trip. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | utrolig | Det var ei utrolig opplevelse for henne. | It was an incredible experience for her. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | utrolig | Vi fikk et utrolig tilbud på hytta. | We got an unbelievable offer on the cabin. |
| Positiv flertall | utrolige | De utrolige hendelsene ble omtalt i avisa. | The incredible events were reported in the newspaper. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | utrolige | Den utrolige nyheten spredte seg raskt. | The incredible news spread quickly. |
| Komparativ | utroligere | Den nye historien virket utroligere enn den første. | The new story seemed more unbelievable than the first. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | utroligst | Av fortellingene var denne utroligst. | Of the stories, this one was the most unbelievable. |
| Superlativ bestemt | utroligste | Det var den utroligste historien jeg hadde hørt. | It was the most incredible story I had heard. |

| Positiv intetkjønn, spelling alternative | utrulig | Det var et utrulig sammentreff. | It was an unbelievable coincidence. |
| Positiv flertall, spelling alternative | utrulige | De utrulige resultatene overrasket alle. | The incredible results surprised everyone. |

**Form status:** Both spelling and comparison paradigms verified in Bokmålsordboka; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The adjective means not to be believed or unlikely. Bokmålsordboka also accepts *utrulig*, with its own agreement and comparison forms. *Utrolig* is additionally used as an intensifying adverb meaning “very”; that use is not adjective inflection.
