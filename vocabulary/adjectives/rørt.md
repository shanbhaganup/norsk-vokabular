## rørt

**English:** moved; touched; emotionally affected

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | rørt | Han var en rørt gjest etter talen. | He was a moved guest after the speech. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | rørt | Hun ble rørt av brevet. | She was touched by the letter. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | rørt | Det var et rørt øyeblikk for familien. | It was an emotional moment for the family. |
| Positiv flertall | rørte | De rørte tilhørerne takket taleren. | The moved audience members thanked the speaker. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | rørte | Den rørte prisvinneren tørket tårene. | The moved award winner wiped away her tears. |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; comparison inapplicable to the emotional-state sense.

**Notes:** Means emotionally moved or touched. This is an adjectival perfect participle of *røre* (“to move emotionally”). The dictionary lists *rørt* and *rørte* agreement forms; it also records an adverbial use such as thanking someone *rørt*.
