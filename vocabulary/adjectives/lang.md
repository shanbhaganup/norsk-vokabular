## lang

**English:** long

**Topics:** describing, time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | lang | Vi gikk en lang tur langs elva. | We went for a long walk along the river. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | lang | Hun skrev ei lang melding til søsteren sin. | She wrote a long message to her sister. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | langt | De ventet et langt øyeblikk før de svarte. | They waited a long moment before answering. |
| Positiv flertall | lange | Barna har lange bein. | The children have long legs. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | lange | Den lange veien går gjennom skogen. | The long road goes through the forest. |
| Komparativ | lengre | Denne køen er lengre enn den ved inngangen. | This queue is longer than the one by the entrance. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | lengst | Av alle broene er denne lengst. | Of all the bridges, this one is the longest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | lengste | Det er den lengste reisen vi har tatt. | It is the longest journey we have taken. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms and synthetic comparison verified.

**Notes:** Lang can describe physical length, duration, clothing length or a tall person. This entry focuses on length and duration. The comparison is irregular: lang – lengre – lengst, with definite superlative lengste. Examples are original teaching sentences.
