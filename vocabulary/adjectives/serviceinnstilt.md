## serviceinnstilt

**English:** service-minded

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | serviceinnstilt | Vi snakket om en serviceinnstilt selger. | We talked about a service-minded salesperson. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | serviceinnstilt | Vi snakket om ei serviceinnstilt resepsjonist. | We talked about a service-minded receptionist. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | serviceinnstilt | Vi snakket om et serviceinnstilt personale. | We talked about a service-minded staff. |
| Positiv flertall | serviceinnstilte | Vi snakket om to serviceinnstilte selgere. | We talked about two service-minded salespeople. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | serviceinnstilte | Vi snakket om den serviceinnstilte selgeren. | We talked about the service-minded salesperson. |
| Komparativ | mer serviceinnstilt | Denne selgeren er mer serviceinnstilt enn den andre. | This salesperson is more service-minded than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest serviceinnstilt | Av de to er denne selgeren mest serviceinnstilt. | Of the two, this salesperson is the most service-minded. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest serviceinnstilte | Vi snakket om den mest serviceinnstilte selgeren. | We talked about the most service-minded salesperson. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and agreement checked.

**Notes:** Comparison with mer and mest is a productive construction. NoW source form: “serviceinnstilt”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
