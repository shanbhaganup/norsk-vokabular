## uakseptabel

**English:** unacceptable; intolerable

**Topics:** rules, behaviour, evaluation

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | uakseptabel | Det var en uakseptabel risiko for barna. | It was an unacceptable risk for the children. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | uakseptabel | Hun beskrev ei uakseptabel handling. | She described an unacceptable act. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | uakseptabelt | Dette er et uakseptabelt forslag. | This is an unacceptable proposal. |
| Positiv flertall | uakseptable | De uakseptable vilkårene ble avvist. | The unacceptable terms were rejected. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | uakseptable | Den uakseptable forsinkelsen fikk følger. | The unacceptable delay had consequences. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer uakseptabel | Forslaget ble enda mer uakseptabelt etter endringen. | The proposal became even more unacceptable after the change. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest uakseptabelt | Av alternativene var dette mest uakseptabelt for gruppa. | Of the options, this was most unacceptable to the group. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest uakseptable | Dette var den mest uakseptable delen av avtalen. | This was the most unacceptable part of the agreement. |

**Form status:** Agreement verified; mer/mest are productive constructions because no synthetic comparison is listed; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means something that cannot be accepted or approved. While the dictionary lists no synthetic comparison, degree can be expressed productively with *mer/mest* when comparing how unacceptable alternatives are.
