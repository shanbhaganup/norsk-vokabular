## våken

**English:** awake

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | våken | En pasient var våken. | A patient was awake. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | våken | Ei jente var våken. | A girl was awake. |
