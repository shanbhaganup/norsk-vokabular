## lik

**English:** equal; alike

**Topics:** describing, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | lik | Han er lik faren sin. | He resembles his father. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | lik | Hun er lik søsteren. | She resembles her sister. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | likt | Beløpet er likt for alle. | The amount is equal for everyone. |
| Positiv flertall | like | Like muligheter er viktige. | Equal opportunities matter. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | like | Den like fordelingen overrasket oss. | The equal distribution surprised us. |
| Komparativ | likere | Disse to løsningene er likere enn de gamle. | These two solutions are more alike than the old ones. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | likest | Av alle var denne likest den opprinnelige. | Of all of them, this was most like the original. |
| Superlativ bestemt | likeste | Hun valgte den likeste kopien. | She chose the most similar copy. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Dictionary lists adverbial likt (“equally”); that cross-POS use is filed separately. Source noun/verb labels do not match this Norwegian adjective: “critic/equal” nouns and “to equal” require other Norwegian lemmas; the separate noun homonym lik means “corpse.”
