## smart

**English:** clever; smart; stylish; advanced

**Topics:** describing, technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | smart | Han fant en smart måte å spare tid på. | He found a clever way to save time. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | smart | Hun kjøpte ei smart jakke med mange lommer. | She bought a stylish jacket with many pockets. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | smart | De installerte et smart system for lysene. | They installed a smart system for the lights. |
