## involvert

**English:** involved, taking part or implicated

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | involvert | En involvert nabo fortalte hva som hadde skjedd. | A neighbor who was involved explained what had happened. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | involvert | Ei involvert jente beskrev hendelsen for læreren. | A girl who was involved described the incident to the teacher. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | involvert | Et involvert firma trakk seg fra prosjektet. | A company involved in the project withdrew. |
| Positiv flertall | involverte | Involverte foreldre fikk en egen orientering. | Parents who were involved received a separate briefing. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | involverte | Det involverte laget fikk legge fram sin versjon. | The team involved was allowed to give its account. |
| Komparativ | mer involvert | Ida er mer involvert i planleggingen nå enn i fjor. | Ida is more involved in planning now than she was last year. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest involvert | Av alle foreldrene er Amir mest involvert i arbeidet. | Of all the parents, Amir is the most involved in the work. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest involverte | Den mest involverte forelderen tok ansvar for påmeldingen. | The parent who was most involved took charge of registration. |

**Form status:** Positive agreement follows the past-participle paradigm in Bokmålsordboka. Comparison with mer/mest is productive in this sense. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Source spelling involvert. Agreement follows the past participle of involvere, sense 2. Mer and mest express the degree of involvement. The degree readings fit ongoing participation particularly well.
