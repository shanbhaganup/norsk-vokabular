## modig

**English:** brave; courageous

**Topics:** emotions, personality

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | modig | Han var en modig brannmann. | He was a brave firefighter. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | modig | Hun er ei modig jente. | She is a brave girl. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | modig | Det var et modig valg. | It was a courageous choice. |
| Positiv flertall | modige | De modige barna gikk først. | The brave children went first. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | modige | Den modige kvinnen sa fra. | The brave woman spoke up. |
| Komparativ | modigere | Hun ble modigere etter hvert. | She became braver over time. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | modigst | Av alle deltakerne var han modigst. | Of all the participants, he was the bravest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | modigste | Hun tok den modigste avgjørelsen. | She made the bravest decision. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Covers fearless, bold or courageous. The dictionary also records a sorrowful sense and an intensifying adverbial use; those are outside this adjective sense entry.
