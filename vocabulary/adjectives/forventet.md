## forventet

**English:** expected

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | forventet | Han fikk den forventede støtten. | He received the expected support. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | forventet | Hun fikk den forventede beskjeden. | She received the expected message. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | forventet / forventa | Resultatet var forventet; resultatet var forventa. | The result was expected; the result was expected. |
| Positiv flertall | forventede / forventete / forventa | De forventede resultatene kom; de forventete resultatene kom; de forventa resultatene kom. | The expected results arrived. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | forventede / forventete / forventa | Den forventede løsningen fungerte; den forventete løsningen fungerte; den forventa løsningen fungerte. | The expected solution worked. |

**Form status:** Verified as participial adjective; examples illustrate listed alternatives.

**Notes:** Past participle of *forvente*. Ordbøkene lists *forventa* and *forventet* for indefinite singular neuter, and *forventa*, *forventede*, and *forventete* for plural and definite agreement. No comparison is used for this participial sense.
