## stressende

**English:** stressful

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | stressende | Vi snakket om en stressende jobb. | We talked about a stressful job. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | stressende | Vi snakket om ei stressende situasjon. | We talked about a stressful situation. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | stressende | Vi snakket om et stressende miljø. | We talked about a stressful environment. |
| Positiv flertall | stressende | Vi snakket om to stressende jobber. | We talked about two stressful jobs. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | stressende | Vi snakket om den stressende jobben. | We talked about the stressful job. |
| Komparativ | mer stressende | Denne jobben er mer stressende enn den andre. | This job is more stressful than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest stressende | Av de to er denne jobben mest stressende. | Of the two, this job is the most stressful. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest stressende | Vi snakket om den mest stressende jobben. | We talked about the most stressful job. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and agreement checked.

**Notes:** Comparison with mer and mest is a productive construction. NoW source form: “stressende”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
