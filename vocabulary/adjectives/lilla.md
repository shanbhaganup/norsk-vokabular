## lilla

**English:** purple; pale violet

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | lilla | Han kjøpte en lilla genser. | He bought a purple sweater. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | lilla | Hun valgte ei lilla skjorte. | She chose a purple shirt. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | lilla | Barnet fikk et lilla skjerf. | The child got a purple scarf. |
| Positiv flertall | lilla | Lilla blomster vokste langs gjerdet. | Purple flowers grew along the fence. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | lilla | Den lilla veska lå på stolen. | The purple bag was on the chair. |
| Komparativ, hue | mer lilla | Denne prøven er mer lilla enn den andre. | This sample is more purple than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt, hue | mest lilla | Av de tre prøvene er denne mest lilla. | Of the three samples, this one is the most purple. |
| Superlativ bestemt, hue | mest lilla | Dette er den mest lilla kjolen i utstillingen. | This is the most purple dress in the exhibition. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies that the positive form is invariable. Mer/mest comparison is a periphrastic construction used when the shade is compared.

**Notes:** The dictionary sense is pale violet or light bluish red. Degree comparisons describe a shade contrast and are contextual, not synthetic dictionary forms.
