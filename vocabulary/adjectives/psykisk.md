## psykisk

**English:** psychological; mental

**Topics:** health, mind

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | psykisk | Han opplevde et sterkt psykisk press. | He experienced intense psychological pressure. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | psykisk | Hun fikk ei psykisk belastning etter ulykken. | She experienced psychological strain after the accident. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | psykisk | Det var et psykisk problem som krevde hjelp. | It was a mental problem that required help. |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; comparison inapplicable to the mind-versus-body distinction.

**Notes:** Means relating to mental life or the mind, as opposed to physical. It is broader than *psykiatrisk*, which specifically concerns psychiatry and treatment of mental disorders. No comparison forms are listed.
