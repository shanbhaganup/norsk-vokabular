## deprimerende

**English:** depressing; disheartening

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | deprimerende | Dette er en deprimerende film. | This is a depressing film. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | deprimerende | Dette er ei deprimerende bok. | This is a depressing book. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | deprimerende | Dette er et deprimerende resultat. | This is a depressing result. |
| Positiv flertall | deprimerende | Vi fant deprimerende nyheter ved inngangen. | We found depressing news by the entrance. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | deprimerende | Dette er den deprimerende filmen. | This is the depressing film. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement verified; original teaching examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The adjective is invariant in agreement. Ordbøkene gives no synthetic comparison; mer/mest are used productively for degree.
