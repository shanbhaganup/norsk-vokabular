## skremmende

**English:** frightening; scary

**Topics:** emotions, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | skremmende | Det var en skremmende lyd fra kjelleren. | There was a frightening sound from the basement. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | skremmende | De så ei skremmende skygge på veggen. | They saw a frightening shadow on the wall. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | skremmende | Det var et skremmende syn. | It was a frightening sight. |
| Positiv flertall | skremmende | De skremmende historiene holdt barna våkne. | The frightening stories kept the children awake. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | skremmende | Det skremmende brølet kom nærmere. | The frightening roar came closer. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer skremmende | Den mørke skogen virket mer skremmende om natten. | The dark forest seemed more frightening at night. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest skremmende | Av alle scenene var denne mest skremmende. | Of all the scenes, this one was the most frightening. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest skremmende | Det var den mest skremmende delen av filmen. | It was the most frightening part of the film. |

**Form status:** Invariable agreement checked; mer/mest comparison shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Present participle of skremme, used adjectivally to describe something that causes fear. It is invariable for gender, number and definiteness. The dictionary lists it as a participle and illustrates adjectival and adverbial use. The productive degree constructions are not synthetic forms listed by the dictionary.
