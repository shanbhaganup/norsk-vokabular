## kreativ

**English:** creative

**Topics:** culture, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | kreativ | Han er en kreativ designer. | He is a creative designer. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | kreativ | Hun er ei kreativ kvinne. | She is a creative woman. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | kreativt | Barnet kom med et kreativt forslag. | The child came up with a creative suggestion. |
| Positiv flertall | kreative | Kreative elever laget en modell av papp. | Creative pupils made a model out of cardboard. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | kreative | Den kreative løsningen sparte oss for tid. | The creative solution saved us time. |
| Komparativ | mer kreativ | Hun ble mer kreativ da hun fikk ro. | She became more creative when she had peace and quiet. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest kreativ | Av deltakerne var Liv mest kreativ. | Of the participants, Liv was the most creative. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest kreative | Dette er den mest kreative ideen i konkurransen. | This is the most creative idea in the competition. |

**Form status:** Agreement verified; comparison is periphrastic.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists agreement forms but no synthetic comparative or superlative. Since the meaning is gradable, the examples use the productive mer/mest construction; these are constructions, not synthetic dictionary forms. Examples are original teaching sentences.
