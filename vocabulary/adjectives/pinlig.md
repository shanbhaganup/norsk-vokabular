## pinlig

**English:** embarrassing; awkward; uncomfortable

**Topics:** emotions, social situations

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | pinlig | Det oppsto en pinlig stillhet. | An awkward silence arose. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | pinlig | Hun fortalte ei pinlig historie fra skolen. | She told an embarrassing story from school. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | pinlig | Det var et pinlig øyeblikk for laget. | It was an embarrassing moment for the team. |
| Positiv flertall | pinlige | De pinlige spørsmålene gjorde ham ukomfortabel. | The embarrassing questions made him uncomfortable. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | pinlige | Den pinlige feilen ble raskt rettet. | The embarrassing mistake was quickly corrected. |
| Komparativ | pinligere | Situasjonen ble pinligere da alle begynte å le. | The situation became more embarrassing when everyone started laughing. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | pinligst | Av øyeblikkene var dette pinligst for meg. | Of the moments, this was the most embarrassing for me. |
| Superlativ bestemt | pinligste | Det var den pinligste feilen i rapporten. | It was the most embarrassing mistake in the report. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means causing embarrassment, awkwardness, or discomfort. The dictionary also uses *pinlig* as an intensifying adverb meaning “very”; that use is not an adjective form.
