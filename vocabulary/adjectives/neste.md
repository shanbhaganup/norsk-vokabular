## neste

**English:** next; following

**Topics:** time, sequence

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | neste | Vi tar neste buss. | We will take the next bus. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | neste | Hun kommer neste uke. | She is coming next week. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | neste | Neste møte starter klokka ni. | The next meeting starts at nine. |
| Positiv flertall | neste | De neste dagene blir travle. | The next few days will be busy. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | neste | Den neste stasjonen er Nationaltheatret. | The next station is Nationaltheatret. |
| Komparativ | — | — | — |
| Superlativ ubestemt | — | — | — |
| Superlativ bestemt | — | — | — |

**Form status:** Invariant agreement checked; comparison inapplicable to the sequential meaning.

**Notes:** The adjective is invariant across gender, number and definiteness. It means the nearest following item in a sequence, and can also refer to the one after the immediately upcoming item. Although often used before nouns without a separate article, *neste* is the dictionary-listed adjective form.
