## snill

**English:** kind; nice; gentle; well-behaved

**Topics:** describing, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | snill | Han er en snill nabo. | He is a kind neighbor. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | snill | Hun har ei snill datter. | She has a kind daughter. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | snilt | Det var et snilt tilbud. | It was a kind offer. |
| Positiv flertall | snille | De snille barna delte lekene sine. | The well-behaved children shared their toys. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | snille | Den snille hesten lot barnet klappe seg. | The gentle horse let the child pet it. |
| Komparativ | snillere | Prøv å være snillere mot deg selv. | Try to be kinder to yourself. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | snillest | Av alle naboene er hun snillest. | Of all the neighbors, she is the kindest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | snilleste | Han er den snilleste personen jeg kjenner. | He is the kindest person I know. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes being easy to get along with or obedient, kind and loving, generous, and friendly or obliging. It also has adverbial and interjectional uses; those are not adjective inflections. The source gloss “kind” covers the central meaning.
