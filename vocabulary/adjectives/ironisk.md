## ironisk

**English:** ironic, using or marked by irony

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | ironisk | Han la inn en ironisk replikk helt på slutten. | He added an ironic line right at the end. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | ironisk | Hun avsluttet innlegget med ei ironisk hilsen. | She ended the post with an ironic greeting. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | ironisk | Hun ga ham et ironisk kompliment om punktlighet. | She paid him an ironic compliment about his punctuality. |
| Positiv flertall | ironiske | Ironiske bemerkninger er lette å misforstå på nett. | Ironic remarks are easy to misunderstand online. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | ironiske | Den ironiske tonen forsvant da hun begynte å snakke om familien. | The ironic tone disappeared when she began talking about her family. |
| Komparativ | mer ironisk | Den nye tittelen er mer ironisk enn den gamle. | The new title is more ironic than the old one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest ironisk | Av de tre svarene var ditt mest ironisk. | Of the three replies, yours was the most ironic. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest ironiske | Den mest ironiske replikken kom helt på slutten. | The most ironic line came right at the end. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies positive agreement. Comparison with mer/mest is productive in this sense. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Source spelling ironisk. Ironisk has the same spelling in all three indefinite singular genders. Tone and wording can vary in how much irony they convey.
