## opprørt

**English:** upset; agitated; distressed

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | opprørt | Han ble opprørt over avgjørelsen. | He was upset about the decision. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | opprørt | Hun virket opprørt etter samtalen. | She seemed distressed after the conversation. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | opprørt | Det var et opprørt hav utenfor kysten. | There was a rough, turbulent sea off the coast. |
| Positiv flertall | opprørte | De opprørte elevene ba om en forklaring. | The upset students asked for an explanation. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | opprørte | Den opprørte forelderen ringte skolen. | The upset parent called the school. |
| Komparativ | mer opprørt | Hun ble mer opprørt da hun hørte hele historien. | She became more upset when she heard the whole story. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest opprørt | Av alle var han mest opprørt over behandlingen. | Of all of them, he was the most upset about the treatment. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest opprørte | Det var den mest opprørte reaksjonen jeg så. | It was the most agitated reaction I saw. |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; analytical comparison used for emotional intensity.

**Notes:** The adjective means unsettled/agitated, including a rough sea, or emotionally distressed and angry. It is the adjectival perfect participle of *opprøre*; the dictionary lists *opprørt* and the agreeing definite/plural form *opprørte*. No synthetic comparison is listed.
