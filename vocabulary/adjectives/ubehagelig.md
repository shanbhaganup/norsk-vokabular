## ubehagelig

**English:** unpleasant; uncomfortable; disagreeable

**Topics:** emotions, senses, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary gives two main senses: causing discomfort or aversion, and behaving rudely or unpleasantly toward someone. Examples illustrate both sensory and interpersonal discomfort. The word also occurs adverbially in some contexts; that is not adjective inflection.
