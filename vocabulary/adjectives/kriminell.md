## kriminell

**English:** criminal; unlawful

**Topics:** society, law

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | kriminell | Politiet etterforsker en kriminell handling. | The police are investigating a criminal act. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | kriminell | Hun ble dømt for ei kriminell handling. | She was convicted of a criminal act. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | kriminelt | Selskapet ble anklaget for et kriminelt forhold. | The company was accused of a criminal offence. |
| Positiv flertall | kriminelle | Retten behandlet flere kriminelle handlinger. | The court dealt with several criminal acts. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | kriminelle | Den kriminelle virksomheten ble stanset. | The criminal activity was stopped. |
| Komparativ | mer kriminell | Denne handlingen er mer kriminell enn den forrige. | This act is more criminal than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest kriminell | Av de beskrevne handlingene er denne mest kriminell. | Of the acts described, this one is the most criminal. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest kriminelle | Dette var den mest kriminelle handlingen i saken. | This was the most criminal act in the case. |

**Form status:** Agreement verified; comparison is periphrastic and context-dependent.

**Notes:** The dictionary gives no synthetic comparison. The mer/mest forms are grammatical periphrastic constructions, though comparison of a legal category can be context-dependent. Kriminell is also used substantivally and as an intensifying adverb; neither use is an adjective inflection. Examples are original teaching sentences.
