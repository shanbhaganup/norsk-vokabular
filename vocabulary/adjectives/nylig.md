## nylig

**English:** recent; recently

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | nylig | Han leste en nylig publisert rapport. | He read a recently published report. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | nylig | Hun fortalte om et nylig møte. | She described a recent meeting. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | nylig | Dette er et nylig vedtak. | This is a recent decision. |
| Positiv flertall | nylige | De nylige endringene er viktige. | The recent changes matter. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | nylige | Den nylige hendelsen ble undersøkt. | The recent event was investigated. |
