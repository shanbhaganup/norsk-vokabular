## utsøkt

**English:** exquisite; excellent; refined; ingenious

**Topics:** quality, food, art

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | utsøkt | Restauranten serverte en utsøkt dessert. | The restaurant served an exquisite dessert. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | utsøkt | Hun viste fram ei utsøkt tegning. | She showed an exquisite drawing. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | utsøkt | Dette var et utsøkt måltid. | This was an exquisite meal. |
| Positiv flertall | utsøkte | De utsøkte detaljene gjorde kjolen spesiell. | The exquisite details made the dress special. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | utsøkte | Den utsøkte smaken overrasket gjestene. | The exquisite taste surprised the guests. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer utsøkt | Den nye utgaven har en mer utsøkt utforming. | The new edition has a more refined design. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest utsøkt | Av dessertene var denne mest utsøkt. | Of the desserts, this one was the most exquisite. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest utsøkte | Det var den mest utsøkte retten på menyen. | It was the most exquisite dish on the menu. |

**Form status:** Agreement verified; dictionary lists no synthetic comparison; mer/mest are productive for the gradable quality sense. Original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary describes something very fine or high quality, and also something cunning or ingeniously devised. Examples use the high-quality sense. Degree is expressed with productive *mer/mest*; no synthetic comparison is listed. Some uses are emphatic or literary.
