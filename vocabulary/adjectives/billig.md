## billig

**English:** cheap; inexpensive

**Topics:** shopping, money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | billig | Vi fant en billig stol på loppemarkedet. | We found an inexpensive chair at the flea market. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | billig | Hun kjøpte ei billig lampe på salg. | She bought an inexpensive lamp on sale. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | billig | Butikken selger et billig kamera. | The shop sells an inexpensive camera. |
| Positiv flertall | billige | De billige billettene ble raskt utsolgt. | The cheap tickets sold out quickly. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | billige | Den billige jakka holdt seg godt. | The inexpensive jacket held up well. |
| Komparativ | billigere | Denne sykkelen er billigere enn den andre. | This bicycle is cheaper than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | billigst | Av alle modellene er denne billigst. | Of all the models, this one is the cheapest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | billigste | Vi valgte den billigste modellen. | We chose the cheapest model. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified for the price sense.

**Notes:** *Billig* also has a distinct figurative sense meaning “shabby, poor” (for example, a cheap joke or victory), which can be gradable too; this entry follows the reference's ordinary low-price sense.
