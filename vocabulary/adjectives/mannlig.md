## mannlig

**English:** male

**Topics:** people, biology

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The basic sense is pertaining to men or being male. Although comparison is unusual for biological sex, the dictionary lists *mannligere, mannligst,* and *mannligste*; these forms are most natural for a perceived or stylistic degree of masculinity, as in the examples.
