## vidunderlig

**English:** wonderful; marvelous; delightful

**Topics:** emotions, experiences

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | vidunderlig | De hadde en vidunderlig kveld sammen. | They had a wonderful evening together. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | vidunderlig | Hun fortalte ei vidunderlig historie. | She told a wonderful story. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | vidunderlig | Det var et vidunderlig øyeblikk. | It was a wonderful moment. |
| Positiv flertall | vidunderlige | De vidunderlige minnene varte lenge. | The wonderful memories lasted a long time. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | vidunderlige | Den vidunderlige utsikten tok pusten fra oss. | The wonderful view took our breath away. |
| Komparativ | vidunderligere | Ferien ble vidunderligere for hver dag. | The holiday became more wonderful with each day. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | vidunderligst | Av opplevelsene var denne vidunderligst. | Of the experiences, this one was most wonderful. |
| Superlativ bestemt | vidunderligste | Det var den vidunderligste reisen hun hadde tatt. | It was the most wonderful trip she had taken. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means having the quality of a wonder or evoking strong positive feelings. The word can also be used adverbially, as in *bollene smaker vidunderlig* (“the buns taste wonderful”); that use is not adjective inflection.
