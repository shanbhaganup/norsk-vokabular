## viss

**English:** certain; sure; definite; fixed

**Topics:** describing, ideas

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | viss | Han er viss på at toget kommer. | He is certain that the train is coming. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | viss | Hun er viss på svaret. | She is sure of the answer. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | visst | Et visst tegn tydet på regn. | A certain sign pointed to rain. |
| Positiv flertall | visse | Av visse grunner ble møtet utsatt. | For certain reasons, the meeting was postponed. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | visse | Den visse seieren virket nær. | The certain victory seemed close. |
| Komparativ | vissere | Etter kontrollen var hun vissere på målingen. | After checking, she was more certain about the measurement. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | vissest | Av alle var han vissest på resultatet. | Of everyone, he was the most certain about the result. |
| Superlativ bestemt | visseste | Dette var den visseste opplysningen vi hadde. | This was the most certain piece of information we had. |

**Form status:** Agreement and source-listed comparison forms verified; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The adjective means “sure/certain” or “specific/fixed,” depending on context. The separate conjunction *hvis* (meaning “if”) is a homonym/variant listed in the dictionary article and is not an adjective form or part of this entry. The source-listed synthetic comparison is uncommon in ordinary use; retain it as a dictionary paradigm.
