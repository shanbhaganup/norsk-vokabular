## bestemt

**English:** a certain, particular, specified

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

| Positiv hunkjønn | bestemt | Hun ba om ei bestemt låt på radioen. | She requested a particular song on the radio. |

**Notes:** The source gloss “certain” is used here in the sense of “particular” or “specified.” That sense identifies a particular choice, so degree comparison is inapplicable. The dictionary also lists the distinct senses “firm” and grammatical “definite.”
