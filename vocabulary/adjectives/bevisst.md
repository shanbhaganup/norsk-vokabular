## bevisst

**English:** conscious; deliberate

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | bevisst | Dette er en bevisst mann. | This is an aware man. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | bevisst | Dette er ei bevisst kvinne. | This is an aware woman. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | bevisst | Dette er et bevisst valg. | This is a deliberate choice. |
| Positiv flertall | bevisste | Vi fant bevisste mennesker ved inngangen. | We found aware people by the entrance. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement verified; original teaching examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The source also maps bevisst to the adverb deliberately. This entry records the adjective senses conscious/aware and deliberate; it does not treat the adverb as an inflection.
