## tung

**English:** heavy; weighty; burdensome; difficult

**Topics:** describing, work, emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | tung | Han løftet en tung sekk opp på hylla. | He lifted a heavy bag onto the shelf. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | tung | Hun bar ei tung kasse inn på lageret. | She carried a heavy box into the storeroom. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | tungt | Dette er et tungt bord å flytte alene. | This is a heavy table to move alone. |
| Positiv flertall | tunge | De tunge koffertene ble stående i gangen. | The heavy suitcases were left in the hallway. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | tunge | Den tunge døra knirket da den åpnet seg. | The heavy door creaked as it opened. |
| Komparativ | tyngre | Denne pakken er tyngre enn den andre. | This parcel is heavier than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | tyngst | Av eskene er denne tyngst. | Of the boxes, this one is the heaviest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | tyngste | Hun tok den tyngste kofferten først. | She took the heaviest suitcase first. |

**Form status:** Agreement and irregular comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary has several senses beyond physical weight, including difficult, burdensome, tiring and emotionally grave. These examples illustrate physical weight. Comparison is irregular: *tung – tyngre – tyngst – tyngste*. Adverbial *tungt* is a related use, not an adjective form.
