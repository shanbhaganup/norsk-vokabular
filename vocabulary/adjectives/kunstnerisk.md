## kunstnerisk

**English:** artistic

**Topics:** culture, art

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | kunstnerisk | Han har en kunstnerisk stil. | He has an artistic style. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | kunstnerisk | Hun har ei kunstnerisk utdanning. | She has an artistic education. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | kunstnerisk | Det var et kunstnerisk uttrykk. | It was an artistic expression. |
| Positiv flertall | kunstneriske | De viste fram kunstneriske arbeider. | They exhibited artistic works. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | kunstneriske | Den kunstneriske utformingen fikk ros. | The artistic design was praised. |
| Komparativ | mer kunstnerisk | Den nye scenografien er mer kunstnerisk enn den gamle. | The new set design is more artistic than the old one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest kunstnerisk | Av bidragene virket dette mest kunstnerisk. | Of the entries, this one seemed the most artistic. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest kunstneriske | Det var den mest kunstneriske løsningen av alle. | It was the most artistic solution of all. |

**Form status:** Agreement verified; comparison is periphrastic.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists agreement forms but no synthetic comparison. The adjective has gradable uses, so mer/mest can express comparison; these are periphrastic constructions. It can also be used substantivally, a separate use rather than an inflected form. Examples are original teaching sentences.
