## uvanlig

**English:** unusual; uncommon; rare; odd

**Topics:** describing, frequency

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | uvanlig | Vi la merke til en uvanlig fugl i hagen. | We noticed an unusual bird in the garden. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | uvanlig | Hun stilte ei uvanlig presis oppfølging. | She asked an unusually precise follow-up question. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | uvanlig | Det var et uvanlig syn på denne tiden av året. | It was an unusual sight at this time of year. |
| Positiv flertall | uvanlige | Uvanlige lyder kom fra kjelleren. | Unusual sounds came from the basement. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | uvanlige | Den uvanlige klesdrakten vakte oppsikt. | The unusual outfit attracted attention. |
| Komparativ | uvanligere | Slike feil er uvanligere nå enn før. | Such mistakes are less common now than before. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | uvanligst | Av funnene var dette uvanligst. | Of the findings, this was the most unusual. |
| Superlativ bestemt | uvanligste | Det var den uvanligste hendelsen i hele uka. | It was the most unusual event of the whole week. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means not usual, rare, abnormal or odd. *Uvanlig* is also an intensifying adverb before adjectives, as in *uvanlig vakker* (“unusually beautiful”); this is a separate use, not adjective inflection.
