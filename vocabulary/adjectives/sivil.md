## sivil

**English:** civil; civilian; noncriminal

**Topics:** society, law

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

**Notes:** Covers the dictionary senses “not connected with the military,” “not in uniform,” and “not concerning criminal law.” A distinct figurative context such as civil disobedience is also used in Norwegian. These senses express category or domain rather than a gradable quality.
