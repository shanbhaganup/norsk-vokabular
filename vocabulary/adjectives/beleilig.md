## beleilig

**English:** convenient

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | beleilig | Dette er en beleilig dag. | This is a convenient day. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | beleilig | Dette er ei beleilig anledning. | This is a convenient occasion. |
