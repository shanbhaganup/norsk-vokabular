## myk

**English:** soft; pliable; gentle

**Topics:** describing, touch

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | myk | Han sov på en myk madrass. | He slept on a soft mattress. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | myk | Hun la seg på ei myk pute. | She lay down on a soft pillow. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | mykt | De spiste mykt brød til suppa. | They ate soft bread with the soup. |
| Positiv flertall | myke | Små barn trenger myke tepper. | Small children need soft blankets. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | myke | Den myke genseren klødde ikke. | The soft sweater did not itch. |
| Komparativ | mykere | Denne stolen er mykere enn den andre. | This chair is softer than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mykest | Av madrassene var denne mykest. | Of the mattresses, this one was the softest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mykeste | Hun valgte den mykeste stolen. | She chose the softest chair. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The source entry follows the *myk* variant. Bokmålsordboka also accepts the parallel *mjuk* paradigm (*mjuk, mjukt, mjuke, mjukere, mjukest, mjukeste*); this entry retains the source's variant rather than mixing forms. The dictionary also uses the adjective for gradual, flexible, yielding, and caring qualities.
