## skuffet

**English:** disappointed; let down

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | skuffet | Han var skuffet over resultatet. | He was disappointed with the result. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | skuffet | Hun ble skuffet av den svake avslutningen. | She was disappointed by the weak ending. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | skuffa | Barnet var skuffa over at turen ble avlyst. | The child was disappointed that the trip was cancelled. |
| Positiv intetkjønn, alternative | skuffet | Barnet var skuffet over den korte besøksstunden. | The child was disappointed by the short visiting time. |
| Positiv flertall | skuffa | De skuffa tilskuerne gikk tidlig. | The disappointed spectators left early. |
| Positiv flertall, alternative | skuffede | De skuffede søkerne ba om en begrunnelse. | The disappointed applicants asked for an explanation. |
| Positiv flertall, alternative | skuffete | De skuffete kundene fikk pengene tilbake. | The disappointed customers got their money back. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | skuffa | Den skuffa spilleren satt stille i garderoben. | The disappointed player sat quietly in the locker room. |
| Positiv bestemt entall, alternative | skuffede | Den skuffede gjesten takket for kvelden. | The disappointed guest thanked them for the evening. |
| Positiv bestemt entall, alternative | skuffete | Den skuffete familien dro hjem tidligere enn planlagt. | The disappointed family went home earlier than planned. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer skuffet | Hun ble mer skuffet etter å ha lest svaret. | She became more disappointed after reading the answer. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest skuffet | Av alle ble han mest skuffet over avgjørelsen. | Of them all, he was the most disappointed by the decision. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest skuffede | De mest skuffede deltakerne fikk snakke med arrangøren. | The most disappointed participants got to speak with the organizer. |

**Form status:** Participial agreement alternatives verified; mer/mest shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Adjectival perfect participle of the verb *skuffe* “fail to meet hopes or expectations.” Neuter alternatives are *skuffa/skuffet*; plural and definite alternatives are *skuffa/skuffede/skuffete*, each separately illustrated. The reference record linked to the wrong homonym, *skuffe 3 III* “shovel snow”; the correct source is *skuffe 4 IV* “disappoint.” Comparison is shown with productive mer/mest constructions, not synthetic forms.
