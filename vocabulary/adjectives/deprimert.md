## deprimert

**English:** depressed; downcast

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | deprimert | Dette er en deprimert student. | This is a depressed student. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | deprimert | Dette er ei deprimert kvinne. | This is a depressed woman. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | deprimert | Dette er et deprimert barn. | This is a depressed child. |
| Positiv flertall | deprimerte | Vi fant deprimerte pasienter ved inngangen. | We found depressed patients by the entrance. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | deprimerte | Dette er den deprimerte pasienten. | This is the depressed patient. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement verified; original teaching examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Adjectival perfect participle of deprimere. Relative-degree examples use mer/mest with the emotional-state sense; they are not dictionary-listed synthetic forms.
