## simpel

**English:** shoddy; base; simple; ordinary

**Topics:** describing, quality

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | simpel | Han kjøpte en simpel dress til festen. | He bought a cheap-looking suit for the party. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | simpel | Det var ei simpel forklaring på problemet. | It was a simple explanation of the problem. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | simpelt | De kom med et simpelt forslag. | They came up with a plain proposal. |
| Positiv flertall | simple | De simple klærne passet godt til turen. | The plain clothes were well suited to the trip. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | simple | Den simple løsningen fungerte best. | The simple solution worked best. |
| Komparativ | simplere | Denne dressen er simplere enn den andre. | This suit is shabbier than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | simplest | Av alle forslagene var dette simplest. | Of all the proposals, this was the simplest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | simpleste | De valgte den simpleste løsningen. | They chose the simplest solution. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary has a negative-quality sense (“of low quality; shabby”) and a neutral sense (“simple, everyday, ordinary”). The English gloss “simple” alone could obscure the negative sense; examples cover both. Simpelt can also be used adverbially, a separate use from adjective agreement.
