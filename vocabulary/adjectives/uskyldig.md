## uskyldig

**English:** innocent; guiltless; harmless; naive

**Topics:** law, character, behaviour

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | uskyldig | Den uskyldige mannen ble løslatt. | The innocent man was released. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | uskyldig | Hun var uskyldig i saken. | She was innocent in the case. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | uskyldig | Det virket som et uskyldig spørsmål. | It seemed like an innocent question. |
| Positiv flertall | uskyldige | Retten frikjente de uskyldige tiltalte. | The court acquitted the innocent defendants. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | uskyldige | Den uskyldige spøken gjorde ingen skade. | The harmless joke caused no harm. |
| Komparativ | uskyldigere | Den andre forklaringen hørtes uskyldigere ut. | The other explanation sounded more innocent. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | uskyldigst | Av barna virket hun uskyldigst. | Of the children, she seemed the most innocent. |
| Superlativ bestemt | uskyldigste | Det var den uskyldigste spøken på festen. | It was the most harmless joke at the party. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified in Bokmålsordboka; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Covers being without guilt, morally innocent or naive, and harmless. The source English gloss reads “innocent / innocent,” a redundant gloss; this entry clarifies the distinct senses as guiltless, naive and harmless. The adjective also has adverbial use; that is not an adjective form.
