## privat

**English:** private; personal; not public

**Topics:** society, people

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | privat | Familien kjøpte en privat eiendom. | The family bought a private property. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | privat | Hun fortalte ei privat historie. | She told a private story. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | privat | Dette er et privat spørsmål. | This is a private matter. |
| Positiv flertall | private | De private foretakene ansatte flere. | The private companies hired more people. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | private | Den private skolen flyttet til sentrum. | The private school moved to the city center. |
| Komparativ | privatere | Hun ønsket en privatere samtale utenfor kontoret. | She wanted a more private conversation outside the office. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | privatest | Av rommene var dette privatest. | Of the rooms, this one was the most private. |
| Superlativ bestemt | privateste | De valgte det privateste rommet. | They chose the most private room. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; synthetic degree forms are contextually restricted.

**Notes:** Means not belonging to or concerning the public sector, concerning an individual, or not open to the general public. The dictionary lists *privatere, privatest,* and *privateste*, but these degrees are most plausible for privacy/accessibility and may sound unusual; analytic *mer/mest privat* is often more idiomatic.
