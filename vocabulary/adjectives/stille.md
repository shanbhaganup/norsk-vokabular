## stille

**English:** still; quiet; silent; shy

**Topics:** describing, sound

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | stille | Vi fant en stille kafé ved elva. | We found a quiet café by the river. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | stille | Hun tok ei stille stund for seg selv. | She took a quiet moment for herself. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | stille | Et stille hav lå foran oss. | A still sea lay before us. |
| Positiv flertall | stille | De stille barna lyttet til fortellingen. | The quiet children listened to the story. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | stille | Den stille eleven rakte opp hånden. | The quiet pupil raised her hand. |
| Komparativ | stillere | Biblioteket er stillere om morgenen. | The library is quieter in the morning. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | stillest | Av rommene er dette stillest. | Of the rooms, this one is the quietest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | stilleste | Vi fant den stilleste plassen i hagen. | We found the quietest spot in the garden. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The adjective includes not moving, quiet or without disturbance, taciturn, and shy. The adverbial use (for example, walking quietly) is separate from adjective inflection. Examples cover stillness, low noise and a quiet person.
