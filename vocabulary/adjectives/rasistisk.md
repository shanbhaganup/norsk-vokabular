## rasistisk

**English:** racist; relating to or characterized by racism

**Topics:** society, equality

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | rasistisk | Rapporten beskriver en rasistisk handling. | The report describes a racist act. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | rasistisk | De undersøkte ei rasistisk ytring. | They investigated a racist statement. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | rasistisk | Det var et rasistisk angrep. | It was a racist attack. |
| Positiv flertall | rasistiske | Skolen tok avstand fra rasistiske ytringer. | The school condemned racist statements. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | rasistiske | Den rasistiske kommentaren ble meldt inn. | The racist comment was reported. |
| Komparativ | mer rasistisk | Den nye formuleringen er mer rasistisk enn den opprinnelige. | The new wording is more racist than the original. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest rasistisk | Av kommentarene var denne mest rasistisk. | Of the comments, this one was the most racist. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest rasistiske | Dette var den mest rasistiske ytringen i rapporten. | This was the most racist statement in the report. |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; analytical comparison used to describe degree of racist content or characterization.

**Notes:** Means relating to or characterized by racism, or typical of racists. No synthetic comparison is listed; *mer/mest* can be used to compare how strongly an action, statement or policy expresses racism. Examples refer to conduct and statements.
