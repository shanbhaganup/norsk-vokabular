## stor

**English:** big; large; great; important

**Topics:** describing, size

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | stor | De kjøpte en stor sofa til stua. | They bought a large sofa for the living room. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | stor | Hun bor i ei stor leilighet. | She lives in a large apartment. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | stort | Det var et stort smil på ansiktet hans. | There was a big smile on his face. |
| Positiv flertall | store | Store bølger slo mot kaia. | Large waves crashed against the pier. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | store | Den store familien samlet seg til middag. | The large family gathered for dinner. |
| Komparativ | større | Den nye leiligheten er større enn den gamle. | The new apartment is larger than the old one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | størst | Av rommene er stua størst. | Of the rooms, the living room is the largest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | største | De besøkte den største byen i regionen. | They visited the largest city in the region. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and irregular comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Irregular comparison: *stor – større – størst – største*. The dictionary has many extended senses, including significant, important, famous or accomplished, and high-ranking. Examples use size and extent. Related adverbial and substantivized uses are not adjective forms.
