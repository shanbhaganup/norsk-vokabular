## skummel

**English:** eerie; creepy; sinister

**Topics:** emotions, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | skummel | Det var en skummel lyd i gangen. | There was a creepy sound in the hallway. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | skummel | Hun fortalte ei skummel historie. | She told a scary story. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | skummelt | Et skummelt strøk lå på den andre siden av elva. | A spooky neighborhood lay on the other side of the river. |
| Positiv flertall | skumle | De skumle skyggene beveget seg over veggen. | The eerie shadows moved across the wall. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | skumle | Den skumle mannen forsvant rundt hjørnet. | The sinister man disappeared around the corner. |
| Komparativ | skumlere | Huset virket skumlere om natten. | The house seemed creepier at night. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | skumlest | Av alle stedene virket kjelleren skumlest. | Of all the places, the basement seemed the creepiest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | skumleste | Det var den skumleste delen av fortellingen. | It was the scariest part of the story. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary sense includes gloomy or eerie, and a second sense of being devious or shady. Examples illustrate both. The English source gloss “creepy” covers only part of the meaning.
