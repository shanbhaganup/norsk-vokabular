**Notes:** The dictionary treats *verre* as a comparative meaning worse, more troublesome or poorer, with superlatives *verst – verste*. The reference record incorrectly labels *verre* as positive masculine/feminine; those positive cells are corrected here. The corresponding positive adjective depends on meaning and may be *vond* or *dårlig*. The adverbial use of *verre* without comparative meaning is separate.
