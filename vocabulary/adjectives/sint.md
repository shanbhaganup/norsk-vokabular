## sint

**English:** angry; mad

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | sint | Han ble sint da møtet ble avlyst. | He got angry when the meeting was cancelled. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | sint | Hun var sint på naboen. | She was angry with the neighbor. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | sint | Barnet ble sint da leken gikk i stykker. | The child got angry when the toy broke. |
| Positiv flertall | sinte | De sinte kundene ba om en forklaring. | The angry customers asked for an explanation. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | sinte | Den sinte læreren tok en pause. | The angry teacher took a break. |
| Komparativ | sintere | Han ble sintere da ingen svarte. | He became angrier when no one answered. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | sintest | Av oss ble jeg sintest over forsinkelsen. | Of us, I became the angriest about the delay. |
| Superlativ bestemt | sinteste | Den sinteste gjesten gikk før desserten. | The angriest guest left before dessert. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Describes feeling and showing anger. The adjective has the same positive form in masculine, feminine and neuter singular; sinte is used in plural and definite singular.
