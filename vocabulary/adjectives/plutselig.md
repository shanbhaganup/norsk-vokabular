## plutselig

**English:** sudden; unexpected

**Topics:** time, events

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; comparison inapplicable to the ‘happening abruptly’ sense.

**Notes:** Means happening abruptly and unexpectedly. The dictionary also lists an adverbial use meaning “suddenly”; that is a separate syntactic use, not an adjective inflection. No comparison forms are listed, and the event-quality is not normally graded.
