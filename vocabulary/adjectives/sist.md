## sist

**English:** last; previous

**Topics:** time, order

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | sist | Han var sist i mål. | He was last to reach the finish. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | sist | Hun var sist i køen. | She was last in the queue. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | sist | Laget var sist i mål. | The team was last to reach the finish. |
| Positiv flertall | siste | De siste dagene var varme. | The last few days were warm. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | siste | Den siste gjesten gikk hjem. | The last guest went home. |

**Form status:** Agreement and usage checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** This entry covers the adjective meaning “coming at the end” or “previous.” Siste is the plural and definite form. The singular form sist is also used predicatively with masculine, feminine and neuter subjects. Related adverbial uses such as sist fredag are not adjective inflections. Comparison is inapplicable because the adjective already denotes the final position in a sequence.
