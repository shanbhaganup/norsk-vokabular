## kvalm

**English:** nauseous; sick; sickening

**Topics:** health, describing

**Form status:** Dictionary forms and synthetic comparison verified.

**Notes:** The reference and dictionary sense includes a person feeling nauseous and something that causes nausea or disgust. The examples use both meanings. Kvalmt can also occur adverbially, but that is not an adjective form row. Examples are original teaching sentences.
