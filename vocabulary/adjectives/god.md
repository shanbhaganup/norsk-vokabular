## god

**English:** good

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | god | Han er en god lærer. | He is a good teacher. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | god | Hun er ei god venninne. | She is a good friend. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | godt | Dette er et godt forslag. | This is a good suggestion. |
| Positiv flertall | gode | Vi har gode venner. | We have good friends. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | gode | Den gode læreren hjelper oss. | The good teacher helps us. |
| Komparativ | bedre | Denne boka er bedre enn den andre. | This book is better than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | best | Denne boka er best. | This book is the best. |
| Superlativ bestemt | beste | Dette er den beste boka. | This is the best book. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword/forms checked; compound constructions and original example sentences supplied for teaching.

**Notes:** This adjective has irregular comparison: god → bedre → best. The definite superlative is beste. For other adjectives, use mer/mest only where the meaning permits comparison.
