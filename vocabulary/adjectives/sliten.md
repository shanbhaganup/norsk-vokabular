## sliten

**English:** tired; exhausted; worn

**Topics:** health, condition

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | sliten | Han var sliten etter den lange arbeidsdagen. | He was tired after the long workday. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | sliten | Hun følte seg sliten etter reisen. | She felt tired after the trip. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | slitent | Det gamle huset så slitent ut. | The old house looked worn. |
| Positiv flertall | slitne | De slitne barna sovnet på bussen. | The tired children fell asleep on the bus. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | slitne | Den slitne jakka trenger en reparasjon. | The worn jacket needs a repair. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer sliten | Etter nattevakten var hun mer sliten enn vanlig. | After the night shift, she was more tired than usual. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest sliten | Av oss var jeg mest sliten etter turen. | Of us, I was the most tired after the hike. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest slitne | De mest slitne løperne fikk hvile først. | The most tired runners were allowed to rest first. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement checked; mer/mest comparison shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers being tired after exertion and being visibly worn from use. It lists no synthetic comparison forms. Because tiredness is gradable, examples use productive mer/mest constructions; these are not claimed as dictionary-listed inflections.
