## komplett

**English:** complete; entire

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | komplett | Han leverte en komplett rapport. | He submitted a complete report. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | komplett | Hun leverte ei komplett søknad. | She submitted a complete application. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | komplett | Dette er et komplett sett. | This is a complete set. |
| Positiv flertall | komplette | Komplette søknader behandles først. | Complete applications are processed first. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | komplette | Den komplette rapporten ligger på bordet. | The complete report is on the table. |
| Komparativ | mer komplett | Denne oversikten er mer komplett enn den forrige. | This overview is more complete than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest komplett | Av alle oversiktene er denne mest komplett. | Of all the overviews, this one is the most complete. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest komplette | Vi leverte den mest komplette oversikten. | We submitted the most complete overview. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the positive agreement forms. Mer/mest comparison is a periphrastic construction used here when completeness is treated as a matter of degree. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists no synthetic comparison. Periphrastic comparison is possible when completeness is treated as a matter of degree; it may be pragmatically odd when “complete” means absolute.
