## tykk

**English:** thick; fat; dense

**Topics:** describing, materials

**Form status:** Agreement and both accepted comparison paradigms verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka accepts *tykk* and *tjukk*, each with its own agreement and comparison forms. The adjective has several senses, including having considerable thickness or mass, dense, and (of liquid) viscous. Examples here primarily illustrate physical thickness. Some uses of *tjukk* may sound more colloquial; *tykk* is the neutral choice in this entry.
