## latterlig

**English:** ridiculous; laughable

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | latterlig | Han stilte et latterlig spørsmål. | He asked a ridiculous question. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | latterlig | Hun kom med ei latterlig unnskyldning. | She came up with a ridiculous excuse. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | latterlig | Det var et latterlig forslag. | It was a ridiculous proposal. |
| Positiv flertall | latterlige | Latterlige påstander spredte seg på nettet. | Ridiculous claims spread online. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | latterlige | Den latterlige påstanden ble trukket tilbake. | The ridiculous claim was withdrawn. |
| Komparativ | latterligere | Dette spørsmålet er latterligere enn det forrige. | This question is more ridiculous than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | latterligst | Av alle forslagene var dette latterligst. | Of all the proposals, this was the most ridiculous. |
| Superlativ bestemt | latterligste | Dette var det latterligste spørsmålet på møtet. | This was the most ridiculous question at the meeting. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies agreement and synthetic comparison.

**Notes:** This entry uses the sense “that evokes laughter or ridicule,” equivalent to ridiculous or laughable. Latterlig is also used as an intensifying adverb, a separate use not included as an adjective form.
