## usikker

**English:** uncertain; unsafe; insecure; unsure

**Topics:** emotions, safety, knowledge

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | usikker | Han var usikker på hvilken vei han skulle ta. | He was unsure which way to take. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | usikker | Hun følte seg usikker under intervjuet. | She felt unsure during the interview. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | usikkert | Det er et usikkert resultat før kontrollen er ferdig. | It is an uncertain result before the review is complete. |
| Positiv flertall | usikre | De usikre prognosene gjorde planleggingen vanskelig. | The uncertain forecasts made planning difficult. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | usikre | Den usikre framtida bekymret dem. | The uncertain future worried them. |
| Komparativ | usikrere | Veien ble usikrere etter at snøen begynte å smelte. | The road became less safe after the snow began to melt. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | usikrest | Av de tre rutene var denne usikrest i mørket. | Of the three routes, this one was least safe in the dark. |
| Superlativ bestemt | usikreste | Det var den usikreste delen av turen. | It was the least safe part of the trip. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified in Bokmålsordboka; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes dangerous or risky, uncertain or unsettled, poorly founded or unreliable, lacking confidence, and unsteady. Examples illustrate several of these senses. The listed comparison forms are *usikrere – usikrest – usikreste*.
