## særlig

**English:** special; exceptional; particular

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | særlig | En særlig egenskap ble observert. | A special feature was observed. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | særlig | Ei særlig fordel ble observert. | A special advantage was observed. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | særlig | Et særlig tilfelle ble observert. | This case is special. |
| Positiv flertall | særlige | Særlige egenskaper ble observert. | Special features were observed. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | særlige | Den særlige fordelen ble observert. | The special advantage was observed. |
| Komparativ | særligere | Denne fordelen er særligere enn den forrige. | This advantage is more special than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | særligst | Denne fordelen er særligst av alle. | This advantage is the most special of all. |
| Superlativ bestemt | særligste | Dette er den særligste fordelen. | This is the most exceptional advantage. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka confirms the agreement and all three comparison forms; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The reference English gloss “particularly” does not match the adjective sense in Bokmålsordboka, which is “outside the ordinary; special.” The dictionary confirms særligere, særligst, særligste. It also marks adverbial uses of særlig; those are not additional adjective inflections.
