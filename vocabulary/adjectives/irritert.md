## irritert

**English:** annoyed, irritated

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | irritert | Han ble irritert av støyen. | He was annoyed by the noise. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | irritert | Hun var irritert på naboen. | She was annoyed with the neighbor. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | irritert | Barnet ble irritert over ventingen. | The child was irritated by the wait. |
| Positiv flertall | irriterte | De irriterte kundene ba om hjelp. | The annoyed customers asked for help. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | irriterte | Den irriterte læreren tok en pause. | The irritated teacher took a break. |
| Komparativ | mer irritert | Hun virket mer irritert etter møtet. | She seemed more annoyed after the meeting. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest irritert | Av alle var han mest irritert. | Of everyone, he was the most annoyed. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest irriterte | Hun var den mest irriterte personen i rommet. | She was the most annoyed person in the room. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene forms checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Adjectival past participle of irritere; comparison is analytic.
