## stinkende

**English:** foul-smelling; stinking

**Topics:** describing, senses

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | stinkende | En stinkende søppelsekk sto ved døra. | A foul-smelling garbage bag stood by the door. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | stinkende | Ei stinkende jakke hang i gangen. | A stinking jacket hung in the hallway. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | stinkende | Et stinkende avløp måtte renses. | A foul-smelling drain had to be cleaned. |
| Positiv flertall | stinkende | Stinkende matrester lå i beholderen. | Foul-smelling food scraps were in the container. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | stinkende | Den stinkende fisken ble pakket inn. | The foul-smelling fish was wrapped up. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer stinkende | Søpla ble mer stinkende etter flere varme dager. | The garbage became more foul-smelling after several hot days. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest stinkende | Av beholderne var denne mest stinkende. | Of the containers, this one smelled the worst. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest stinkende | Dette var den mest stinkende fisken på markedet. | This was the most foul-smelling fish at the market. |

**Form status:** Invariable agreement checked; mer/mest shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Present participle of *stinke*, used adjectivally to mean “smelling bad.” It is invariable for gender, number and definiteness. Smell intensity is gradable, so mer/mest constructions are included as productive examples, not synthetic dictionary forms.
