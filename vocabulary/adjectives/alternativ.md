**Topics:** General / unassigned

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked against Bokmålsordboka. The dictionary does not list comparative or superlative forms for this sense.

**Notes:** This adjective means allowing a choice between possibilities or standing apart from what is established. Its usual sense is relational rather than gradable, so comparative and superlative forms are inapplicable.
