## passelig

**English:** suitable

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | passelig | Vi snakket om en passelig stol. | We talked about a suitable chair. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | passelig | Vi snakket om ei passelig jakke. | We talked about a suitable jacket. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | passelig | Vi snakket om et passelig bord. | We talked about a suitable table. |
| Positiv flertall | passelige | Vi snakket om to passelige stoler. | We talked about two suitable chairs. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | passelige | Vi snakket om den passelige stolen. | We talked about the suitable chair. |
| Komparativ | mer passelig | Denne stolen er mer passelig enn den andre. | This chair is more suitable than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest passelig | Av de to er denne stolen mest passelig. | Of the two, this chair is the most suitable. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest passelige | Vi snakket om den mest passelige stolen. | We talked about the most suitable chair. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and agreement checked.

**Notes:** Comparison with mer and mest is a productive construction. NoW source form: “passelig”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
