## sporty

**English:** sporty; athletic; sporting; stylish

**Topics:** sport, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | sporty | Han kjøpte en sporty bil til helgeturene. | He bought a sporty car for weekend trips. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | sporty | Hun er ei sporty jente som går mye på ski. | She is an athletic girl who skis a lot. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | sporty | De valgte et sporty antrekk til turen. | They chose a sporty outfit for the trip. |
| Positiv flertall | sporty | De sporty klærne passet til fjellturen. | The sporty clothes were suitable for the mountain hike. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | sporty | Den sporty stasjonsvogna var rask og elegant. | The sporty station wagon was fast and elegant. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer sporty | Den nye bilen er mer sporty enn den gamle. | The new car is sportier than the old one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest sporty | Av antrekkene virket dette mest sporty. | Of the outfits, this looked the sportiest. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest sporty | Hun valgte den mest sporty jakka. | She chose the sportiest jacket. |

**Form status:** Invariable agreement checked; mer/mest shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers a person who does sports or outdoor activities; clothing or objects suited to sports; a vehicle that is fast and elegant; and sportsmanlike, fair conduct. It also lists adverbial use, which is separate from adjective inflection. Mer/mest are productive degree constructions, not synthetic dictionary forms.
