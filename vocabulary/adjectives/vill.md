## vill

**English:** wild; untamed; uncontrolled; unruly

**Topics:** nature, behaviour

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | vill | En vill hest løp over jordet. | A wild horse ran across the field. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | vill | Hun så ei vill geit oppe i lia. | She saw a wild goat up on the hillside. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | vilt | Et vilt dyr kom nær leiren. | A wild animal came near the camp. |
| Positiv flertall | ville | De ville blomstene vokste langs veien. | The wild flowers grew along the road. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | ville | Den ville elva rant gjennom dalen. | The wild river ran through the valley. |
| Komparativ | villere | Vinden ble villere utover natta. | The wind grew wilder through the night. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | villest | Av dansene var denne villest. | Of the dances, this one was wildest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | villeste | De gikk inn i den villeste delen av skogen. | They entered the wildest part of the forest. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers living in a natural state, rugged terrain, being disoriented, uncontrolled behaviour, and something irrational. Examples mainly use the natural or uncontrolled senses. The reference note “Source spelling/form: wild” is an English gloss accidentally placed in the spelling field; the Norwegian lemma is *vill*. The word also has adverbial uses such as *vilt*; these are not adjective forms.
