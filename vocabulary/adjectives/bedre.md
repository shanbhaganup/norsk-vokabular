**English:** better; improved; more suitable

**Topics:** describing, comparison

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified. Further comparison inapplicable: this lemma is already a comparative.

**Notes:** *Bedre* is itself the comparative of *god* and *bra*. Its adjective inflection is invariant across gender, number and definiteness. It has related meanings including greater skill, higher quality, recovery from illness and greater suitability; examples here use the quality/suitability sense. Superlative forms belong to *best*, not this lemma.
