## akutt

**English:** acute, sudden in onset or requiring immediate attention

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | akutt | Han fikk en akutt infeksjon etter reisen. | He developed an acute infection after the trip. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | akutt | Hun kjente ei akutt smerte i ryggen. | She felt a sudden pain in her back. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | akutt | Legen vurderte et akutt tilfelle av blindtarmbetennelse. | The doctor assessed an acute case of appendicitis. |
| Positiv flertall | akutte | Mottaket tar imot pasienter med akutte skader. | The emergency department sees patients with acute injuries. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | akutte | Den akutte betennelsen oppsto i løpet av natten. | The acute inflammation developed overnight. |
| Komparativ | mer akutt | Denne skaden er mer akutt enn den vi undersøkte i går. | This injury requires more immediate attention than the one we examined yesterday. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest akutt | Av de to sakene er denne mest akutt. | Of the two cases, this one is the most urgent. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest akutte | Den mest akutte saken ble behandlet først. | The most urgent case was dealt with first. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies positive agreement. Comparison with mer/mest is a productive construction. Examples are original.

**Notes:** The mer/mest constructions compare urgency. In the acute-versus-chronic classification, akutt marks a category.
