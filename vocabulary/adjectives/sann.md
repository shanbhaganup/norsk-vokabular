## sann

**English:** true; truthful; genuine

**Topics:** describing, communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | sann | Det var en sann historie om vennskap. | It was a true story about friendship. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | sann | Hun fortalte ei sann historie fra barndommen. | She told a true story from childhood. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | sant | Det er et sant rykte. | It is a true rumor. |
| Positiv flertall | sanne | De sanne opplysningene kom fram senere. | The true facts came to light later. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | sanne | Den sanne forklaringen var enkel. | The true explanation was simple. |
| Komparativ | sannere | Dette gir et sannere bilde av situasjonen. | This gives a truer picture of the situation. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | sannest | Av forklaringene var hennes sannest. | Of the explanations, hers was the truest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | sanneste | De søkte etter den sanneste versjonen av hendelsen. | They sought the truest version of the event. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means corresponding to reality or genuine; it can also mean honest/sincere. The dictionary also has interjectional, adverbial, and fixed-expression uses. The listed degree forms apply to truth/accuracy, not every idiomatic use.
