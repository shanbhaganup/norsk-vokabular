## slett

**English:** flat; smooth; poor; bad

**Topics:** describing, quality

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | slett | De fulgte en slett vei langs fjorden. | They followed a flat road along the fjord. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | slett | Hun hadde slett hår før hun klippet det kort. | She had straight hair before she cut it short. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | slett | Vi krysset et slett jorde. | We crossed a flat field. |
| Positiv flertall | slette | Slette steiner lå langs elvebredden. | Smooth stones lay along the riverbank. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | slette | Den slette veien gjorde sykkelturen enkel. | The smooth road made the bike ride easy. |
| Komparativ | slettere | Denne delen av veien er slettere enn den forrige. | This part of the road is flatter than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | slettest | Her er bakken slettest. | The ground is flattest here. |
| Superlativ bestemt | sletteste | Vi valgte den sletteste ruta gjennom terrenget. | We chose the flattest route through the terrain. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Covers a surface without major rises or depressions, smooth/flat hair, and the extended sense “bad” (for example, poor morals or clothing). The dictionary also lists adverbial and fixed-expression uses; those are not adjective forms. The source gloss “flat” does not cover every sense.
