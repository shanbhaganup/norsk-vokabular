## troverdig

**English:** credible; believable

**Topics:** communication, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | troverdig | Vitnet ga en troverdig forklaring. | The witness gave a credible explanation. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | troverdig | Hun presenterte ei troverdig historie. | She presented a believable story. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | troverdig | Dette virker som et troverdig svar. | This seems like a credible answer. |
| Positiv flertall | troverdige | Forskerne fant flere troverdige kilder. | The researchers found several credible sources. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | troverdige | Den troverdige forklaringen overbeviste juryen. | The credible explanation convinced the jury. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer troverdig | Den nye versjonen virker mer troverdig enn den første. | The new version seems more credible than the first. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest troverdig | Av de tre vitnene virket hun mest troverdig. | Of the three witnesses, she seemed the most credible. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest troverdige | Dette er den mest troverdige forklaringen vi har fått. | This is the most credible explanation we have received. |

**Form status:** Agreement checked; mer/mest are productive comparison constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means something one can believe and trust. The dictionary lists agreement forms but no synthetic comparison; the examples use the productive *mer/mest* construction for this gradable sense.
