## initiativrik

**English:** full of initiative

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | initiativrik | Vi snakket om en initiativrik leder. | We talked about a manager full of initiative. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | initiativrik | Vi snakket om ei initiativrik gruppe. | We talked about a group full of initiative. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | initiativrikt | Vi snakket om et initiativrikt lag. | We talked about a team full of initiative. |
| Positiv flertall | initiativrike | Vi snakket om to initiativrike ledere. | We talked about two managers full of initiative. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | initiativrike | Vi snakket om den initiativrike lederen. | We talked about the manager full of initiative. |
| Komparativ | mer initiativrik | Denne lederen er mer initiativrik enn den andre. | This manager is more full of initiative than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest initiativrik | Av de to er denne lederen mest initiativrik. | Of the two, this manager is the most full of initiative. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest initiativrike | Vi snakket om den mest initiativrike lederen. | We talked about the most full of initiative manager. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and agreement checked.

**Notes:** Comparison with mer and mest is a productive construction. NoW source form: “initiativrik”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
