## munter

**English:** cheerful, lively

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | munter | Han er en munter fyr. | He is a cheerful fellow. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | munter | Hun fortalte en munter historie. | She told a cheerful story. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | muntert | Det ble et muntert lag. | It became a lively gathering. |
| Positiv flertall | muntre | De muntre barna sang. | The cheerful children sang. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | muntre | Den muntre melodien fikk oss til å smile. | The cheerful melody made us smile. |
| Komparativ | muntrere | Han virket muntrere etter pausen. | He seemed cheerier after the break. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | muntrest | Hun var muntrest av alle. | She was the most cheerful of all. |
| Superlativ bestemt | muntreste | Dette var den muntreste gjengen. | This was the most cheerful group. |
