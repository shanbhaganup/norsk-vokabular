## titalls

**English:** in the tens; numbering from twenty to ninety

**Topics:** numbers, quantity

**Form status:** Invariable form and usage checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka classifies *titalls* as an adjective meaning expressible in tens or a number between 20 and 90. It is used as an invariable quantifier with plural amounts and nouns; the gender labels do not produce different spellings. Comparison is inapplicable to this numerical meaning.
