## motbydelig

**English:** disgusting; repulsive; revolting

**Topics:** emotions, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

| Positiv hankjønn | motbydelig | De fant en motbydelig larve i maten. | They found a disgusting caterpillar in the food. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | motbydelig | Han så ei motbydelig scene i filmen. | He saw a revolting scene in the film. |
