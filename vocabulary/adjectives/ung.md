## ung

**English:** young; youthful; recent

**Topics:** age, life stages

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | ung | Han er en ung lærer på skolen. | He is a young teacher at the school. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | ung | Hun er ei ung forsker med mange ideer. | She is a young researcher with many ideas. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | ungt | Et ungt tre sto ved inngangen. | A young tree stood by the entrance. |
| Positiv flertall | unge | De unge deltakerne stilte mange spørsmål. | The young participants asked many questions. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | unge | Den unge generasjonen bruker teknologien daglig. | The young generation uses the technology every day. |
| Komparativ | yngre | Broren min er yngre enn meg. | My brother is younger than me. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | yngst | Av søsknene er hun yngst. | Of the siblings, she is the youngest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | yngste | Han er den yngste læreren på skolen. | He is the youngest teacher at the school. |

**Form status:** Agreement and irregular comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Describes low age, something characteristic of youth, or something recently formed, such as *en ung vitenskap* (“a young science”). Comparison is irregular: *ung – yngre – yngst – yngste*. The adjective can also be substantivized, as in *de unge* (“young people”); that is not a separate form.
