## urolig

**English:** restless; uneasy; unsettled

**Topics:** emotions, movement

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | urolig | Han ble urolig da toget ikke kom. | He became uneasy when the train did not arrive. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | urolig | Hun følte seg urolig før undersøkelsen. | She felt uneasy before the examination. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | urolig | Det var et urolig døgn for hele familien. | It was a restless day for the whole family. |
| Positiv flertall | urolige | De urolige barna fikk ikke sove. | The restless children could not sleep. |
