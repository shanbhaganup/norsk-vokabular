## konkret

**English:** concrete; specific

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | konkret | Han ga en konkret beskjed. | He gave a specific message. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | konkret | Hun ga ei konkret forklaring. | She gave a specific explanation. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | konkret | Vi trenger et konkret svar. | We need a concrete answer. |
| Positiv flertall | konkrete | Konkrete forslag gjør møtet nyttig. | Concrete suggestions make the meeting useful. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | konkrete | Det konkrete eksempelet står i rapporten. | The specific example is in the report. |
| Komparativ | mer konkret | Dette eksempelet er mer konkret enn det forrige. | This example is more specific than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest konkret | Av alle eksemplene er dette mest konkret. | Of all the examples, this one is the most specific. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest konkrete | Dette er det mest konkrete eksempelet. | This is the most specific example. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the positive agreement forms. Mer/mest comparison is a productive construction illustrated with the tangible or specific sense. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists no synthetic comparison. Periphrastic comparison is natural in the sense “more specific/tangible.”
