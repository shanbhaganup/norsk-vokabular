## rå

**English:** raw; uncooked; unprocessed

**Topics:** food, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | rå | Han spiste en rå gulrot. | He ate a raw carrot. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | rå | Hun skar opp ei rå pære. | She cut up a raw pear. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | rått | De serverte et rått egg. | They served a raw egg. |
| Positiv flertall | rå | Rå grønnsaker passer godt i salaten. | Raw vegetables work well in the salad. |
| Positiv flertall | råe | De råe grønnsakene ble lagt på et fat. | The raw vegetables were placed on a platter. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | rå | Den rå fisken må holdes kald. | The raw fish must be kept cold. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | råe | Den råe fisken lå på is. | The raw fish were lying on ice. |
| Komparativ | råere | Denne poteten er råere enn den andre. | This potato is less cooked than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | råest | Av grønnsakene var denne råest. | Of the vegetables, this one was the least cooked. |
| Superlativ bestemt | råeste | Velg den råeste biten til carpaccioen. | Choose the rawest piece for the carpaccio. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; both plural/definite alternatives illustrated.

**Notes:** This entry focuses on not sufficiently cooked or fried. The dictionary also gives wet/soaked, unprocessed, coarse/vulgar, brutal, exorbitant, and colloquially “amazing” senses. Bokmålsordboka accepts both *rå* and *råe* in plural and definite forms; each is separately illustrated. The comparative is contextual and means less cooked/more raw.
