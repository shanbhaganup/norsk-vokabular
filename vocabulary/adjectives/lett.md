## lett

**English:** easy; not difficult

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | lett | Han fikk en lett oppgave på kurset. | He got an easy task in the course. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | lett | Hun fant ei lett løsning på problemet. | She found an easy solution to the problem. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | lett | Det var et lett spørsmål. | It was an easy question. |
| Positiv flertall | lette | Lette oppgaver sto først på prøven. | Easy tasks came first on the test. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | lette | Den lette øvelsen tok bare fem minutter. | The easy exercise took only five minutes. |
| Komparativ | lettere | Denne oppgaven er lettere enn den forrige. | This task is easier than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | lettest | Av alle spørsmålene var dette lettest. | Of all the questions, this was the easiest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | letteste | Dette er den letteste delen av kurset. | This is the easiest part of the course. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies agreement and synthetic comparison.

**Notes:** The entry focuses on the “not difficult” sense. Lett has other dictionary senses, including lightweight and mild. The adverbial use is separate from adjective inflection.
