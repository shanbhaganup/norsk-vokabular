## sikker

**English:** safe; secure; reliable; certain

**Topics:** safety, certainty

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | sikker | Han fant en sikker vei gjennom skogen. | He found a safe route through the forest. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | sikker | Hun valgte ei sikker løsning. | She chose a safe solution. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | sikkert | Dette er et sikkert tegn på framgang. | This is a reliable sign of progress. |
| Positiv flertall | sikre | De sikre svarene kom først. | The certain answers came first. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | sikre | Den sikre metoden sparte tid. | The reliable method saved time. |
| Komparativ | sikrere | Denne broen er sikrere enn den gamle. | This bridge is safer than the old one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | sikrest | Av rutene er denne sikrest om vinteren. | Of the routes, this one is safest in winter. |
| Superlativ bestemt | sikreste | De valgte den sikreste veien. | They chose the safest route. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The source gloss “sure” is narrowed here to dictionary adjective senses: safe or low-risk, reliable, and certain/convinced. The dictionary also lists adverbial sikkert (“probably”); that use is not an adjective form.
