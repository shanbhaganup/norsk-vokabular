## informativ

**English:** informative

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | informativ | Vi snakket om en informativ artikkel. | We talked about an informative article. |
