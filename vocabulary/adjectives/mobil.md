## mobil

**English:** mobile; portable

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | mobil | Han bruker en mobil enhet. | He uses a mobile device. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | mobil | Hun har en mobil arbeidsplass. | She has a mobile workplace. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | mobilt | Dette er et mobilt kjøkken. | This is a mobile kitchen. |
| Positiv flertall | mobile | Mobile team rykket ut raskt. | Mobile teams responded quickly. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | mobile | Den mobile klinikken besøker bygdene. | The mobile clinic visits rural areas. |
| Komparativ | mer mobil | Enheten er mer mobil enn den gamle modellen. | The device is more mobile than the old model. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest mobil | Av løsningene er denne mest mobil. | Of the solutions, this is the most mobile. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest mobile | Dette er den mest mobile klinikken. | This is the most mobile clinic. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka sense/forms checked; examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary also has a distinct masculine noun mobil (“mobile phone; mobile sculpture”); separate noun entry is included.
