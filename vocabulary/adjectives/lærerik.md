## lærerik

**English:** instructive; educational; informative

**Topics:** education

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | lærerik | Vi hadde en lærerik time. | We had an informative lesson. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | lærerik | Det var ei lærerik økt. | It was an instructive session. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | lærerikt | Kurset var et lærerikt tilbud. | The course was an educational offering. |
| Positiv flertall | lærerike | Elevene deltok i lærerike aktiviteter. | The students took part in educational activities. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | lærerike | Den lærerike turen ga oss mye å tenke på. | The instructive trip gave us a lot to think about. |
| Komparativ | mer lærerik | Denne boka er mer lærerik enn filmen. | This book is more informative than the film. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest lærerikt | Dette kurset var mest lærerikt av alle. | This course was the most educational of all. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest lærerike | Det var den mest lærerike delen av besøket. | It was the most informative part of the visit. |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; analytical comparison used because no synthetic degree forms are listed.

**Notes:** Means that one can learn a lot from something. The dictionary gives no synthetic comparison; *mer/mest* are used analytically for this gradable quality.
