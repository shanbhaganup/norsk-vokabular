## mye

**English:** much; a lot of; great (quantity or degree)

**Topics:** quantity

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | mye | Han har mye arbeid denne uka. | He has a lot of work this week. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | mye | Hun viste mye tålmodighet. | She showed a lot of patience. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | mye | Det kom mye regn i natt. | A lot of rain fell last night. |
| Positiv flertall | mye | Familien brukte mye penger på reisen. | The family spent a lot of money on the trip. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | — | — | — |
| Komparativ | mer | Vi trenger mer tid. | We need more time. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest | Hun fikk mest støtte fra familien. | She received the most support from her family. |
| Superlativ bestemt | meste | Det meste av maten ble spist. | Most of the food was eaten. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** As an adjective/quantifier, *mye* is invariable and used with mass nouns or plural quantity expressions; it does not normally modify a definite singular noun directly, so that cell is inapplicable. *Mye* is also used substantivally and adverbially; those are syntactic uses, not additional adjective forms. The working vocabulary already has [mye as an adverb](../adverbs/mye-amount.md); this entry is the distinct adjective/quantifier word type.
