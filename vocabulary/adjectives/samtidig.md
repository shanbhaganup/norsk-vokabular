## samtidig

**English:** simultaneous; contemporaneous

**Topics:** time, history

**Notes:** As an adjective, means occurring at the same time or belonging to the same period. The dictionary also lists an adverbial use (“at the same time”) and a substantival use (“a contemporary”); those are separate syntactic uses, not adjective forms.
