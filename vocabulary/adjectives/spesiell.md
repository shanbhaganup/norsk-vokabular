## spesiell

**English:** special; particular; unusual

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | spesiell | Han fikk en spesiell invitasjon. | He received a special invitation. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | spesiell | Det var ei spesiell anledning. | It was a special occasion. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | spesielt | De planla et spesielt arrangement. | They planned a special event. |
| Positiv flertall | spesielle | Hun har spesielle interesser. | She has particular interests. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | spesielle | Den spesielle formen gjorde gjenstanden lett å kjenne igjen. | The unusual shape made the object easy to recognize. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer spesiell | Denne utgaven er mer spesiell enn den vanlige. | This edition is more distinctive than the regular one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest spesiell | Av gjenstandene var denne mest spesiell. | Of the objects, this one was the most unusual. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest spesielle | Det var den mest spesielle dagen på reisen. | It was the most special day of the trip. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement checked; mer/mest shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means particular, distinct or unusual. The dictionary also lists adverbial *spesielt*; that is a separate use, not neuter adjective agreement. It gives no synthetic comparison, so the mer/mest forms are treated as productive constructions.
