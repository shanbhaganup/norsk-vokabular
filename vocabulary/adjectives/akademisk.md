## akademisk

**English:** academic, relating to universities or scholarly work

**Topics:** school

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | akademisk | Hun har en akademisk bakgrunn i historie. | She has an academic background in history. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | akademisk | Ei akademisk avhandling kan ta flere år å skrive. | An academic dissertation can take several years to write. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | akademisk | Byen har et akademisk miljø rundt universitetet. | The city has an academic community centered on the university. |
| Positiv flertall | akademiske | Vi leste akademiske artikler om dialekter. | We read academic articles about dialects. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | akademiske | Den akademiske debatten fortsatte etter seminaret. | The academic debate continued after the seminar. |
| Komparativ | mer akademisk | Språket i rapporten er mer akademisk enn i brosjyren. | The language in the report is more academic than in the brochure. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest akademisk | Av de tre tekstene er denne mest akademisk i stilen. | Of the three texts, this one is the most academic in style. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest akademiske | Den mest akademiske teksten inneholdt mange fotnoter. | The most academic text contained many footnotes. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies positive agreement. Comparison with mer/mest is a productive construction. Examples are original.

**Notes:** The mer/mest constructions compare how scholarly a style is. University affiliation itself is a category.
