## ærlig

**English:** honest; truthful; upright

**Topics:** relationships, character

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | ærlig | Han ga en ærlig forklaring på forsinkelsen. | He gave an honest explanation for the delay. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | ærlig | Hun svarte med ei ærlig vurdering. | She replied with an honest assessment. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | ærlig | Det var et ærlig forsøk på å rydde opp. | It was an honest attempt to put things right. |
| Positiv flertall | ærlige | De ærlige svarene gjorde møtet nyttig. | The honest answers made the meeting useful. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | ærlige | Den ærlige meningen hennes overrasket oss. | Her honest opinion surprised us. |
| Komparativ | ærligere | Han ble ærligere om hva som hadde skjedd. | He became more honest about what had happened. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | ærligst | Av deltakerne svarte hun ærligst. | Of the participants, she answered most honestly. |
| Superlativ bestemt | ærligste | Det var den ærligste samtalen vi hadde hatt. | It was the most honest conversation we had had. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Describes expressing the truth, acting truthfully, or following what is right. It also occurs adverbially, as in *svare ærlig* (“answer honestly”); that is a related use, not adjective inflection.
