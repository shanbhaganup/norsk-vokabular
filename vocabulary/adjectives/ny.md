## ny

**English:** new; recent; renewed

**Topics:** describing, time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | ny | Han kjøpte en ny sykkel. | He bought a new bicycle. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | ny | Hun begynte i ei ny stilling. | She started a new job. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | nytt | Vi trenger et nytt pass. | We need a new passport. |
| Positiv flertall | nye | De har nye naboer. | They have new neighbors. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | nye | Den nye læreren presenterte seg. | The new teacher introduced herself. |
| Komparativ | nyere | Denne modellen er nyere enn den forrige. | This model is newer than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | nyest | Av telefonene er denne nyest. | Of the phones, this one is the newest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | nyeste | Hun viste fram sin nyeste oppfinnelse. | She showed off her newest invention. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Covers recently made or acquired, previously unknown, replacing what came before, and renewed meanings. *Nye* is included as the source's merged plural/definite form.
