## våt

**English:** wet; soaked; rainy

**Topics:** weather, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | våt | Han hang en våt jakke til tørk. | He hung a wet jacket up to dry. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | våt | Hun tørket ei våt jakke ved ovnen. | She dried a wet jacket by the stove. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | vått | Hunden hadde et vått teppe i buret. | The dog had a wet blanket in its crate. |
| Positiv flertall | våte | De våte klærne lå i en kurv. | The wet clothes were in a basket. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | våte | Den våte veien glinset i lyset. | The wet road glistened in the light. |
| Komparativ | våtere | Håret ble våtere da regnet økte. | The hair got wetter as the rain increased. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | våtest | Av klærne var sokkene våtest. | Of the clothes, the socks were wettest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | våteste | Dette var den våteste sommeren på lenge. | This was the wettest summer in a long time. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Describes something containing or covered in liquid, or a period with much rain. The examples show wet objects and weather. *Vått* can also occur in substantivized expressions such as *verken vått eller tørt*; that is not an adjective form.
