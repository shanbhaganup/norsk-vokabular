## morsom

**English:** funny; amusing; entertaining

**Topics:** describing, entertainment

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | morsom | Han fortalte en morsom vits. | He told a funny joke. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | morsom | Hun så ei morsom forestilling. | She saw an entertaining show. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | morsomt | Det var et morsomt spill. | It was a fun game. |
| Positiv flertall | morsomme | Vi så noen morsomme filmer. | We watched some funny films. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | morsomme | Den morsomme historien fikk alle til å le. | The funny story made everyone laugh. |
| Komparativ | morsommere | Denne serien er morsommere enn den forrige. | This series is funnier than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | morsomst | Av lekene var dette morsomst. | Of the games, this was the most fun. |
| Superlativ bestemt | morsomste | Det var den morsomste kvelden på lenge. | It was the most fun evening in a long time. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Primarily means amusing or entertaining; the dictionary also uses it for something interesting or pleasant. Dictionary-listed comparison forms are included.
