## varm

**English:** warm; hot; heartfelt

**Topics:** weather, food, emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | varm | Han tok på seg en varm genser. | He put on a warm sweater. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | varm | Hun spiste ei varm suppe til lunsj. | She ate a warm soup for lunch. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | varmt | Hun tok et varmt bad etter turen. | She took a warm bath after the hike. |
| Positiv flertall | varme | De varme klærne lå klare på senga. | The warm clothes were ready on the bed. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | varme | Den varme vinden kom fra sør. | The warm wind came from the south. |
| Komparativ | varmere | Det blir varmere utover dagen. | It gets warmer as the day goes on. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | varmest | August var varmest av sommermånedene. | August was the warmest of the summer months. |
| Superlativ bestemt | varmeste | Dette var den varmeste dagen i juli. | This was the warmest day in July. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes having or giving off high temperature, retaining warmth, warm colours, intense emotions, heartfelt expression, and a fresh or current lead. Examples focus on physical warmth. *Varmt* may also be used adverbially or substantivally; those uses are not adjective forms.
