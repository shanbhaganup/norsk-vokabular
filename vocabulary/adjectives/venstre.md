## venstre

**English:** left; on the left-hand side

**Topics:** direction, body, politics

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | venstre | Han skadet venstre arm på trening. | He injured his left arm during practice. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | venstre | Hun løftet venstre hånd for å stille et spørsmål. | She raised her left hand to ask a question. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | venstre | Det venstre øyet var irritert. | The left eye was irritated. |
| Positiv flertall | venstre | De venstre hjulene måtte skiftes. | The left wheels had to be replaced. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | venstre | Den venstre døra var låst. | The left-hand door was locked. |
| Komparativ | — | Left/right direction is not gradable. | Left/right direction is not gradable. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | — | Left/right direction is not gradable. | Left/right direction is not gradable. |
| Superlativ bestemt | — | Left/right direction is not gradable. | Left/right direction is not gradable. |

**Form status:** Dictionary lists *venstre* unchanged across agreement forms; comparison is inapplicable to the directional sense.

**Notes:** Describes the side where the heart is normally located, a position in a field of view, or a political position. It can also be substantivized, as in *en rett venstre* (“a straight left [punch]”); that use is not a separate adjective form.
