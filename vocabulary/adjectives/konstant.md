## konstant

**English:** constant; continual

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | konstant | Han kjenner en konstant uro. | He feels a constant unease. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | konstant | Hun lever med ei konstant uro. | She lives with a constant unease. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | konstant | Det konstante presset merkes av alle. | The constant pressure is felt by everyone. |
| Positiv flertall | konstante | Konstante endringer gjør planlegging vanskelig. | Constant changes make planning difficult. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | konstante | Den konstante støyen forstyrrer arbeidet. | The constant noise disrupts the work. |
| Komparativ | mer konstant | Denne støyen er mer konstant enn den andre lyden. | This noise is more constant than the other sound. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest konstant | Av alle plagene er denne mest konstant. | Of all the nuisances, this one is the most constant. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest konstante | Dette er den mest konstante plagen. | This is the most constant nuisance. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies the positive agreement forms. Mer/mest comparison is a productive construction when constancy is treated as a matter of degree. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Also used adverbially with the meaning “continually”; that use is not an inflected adjective form. Periphrastic comparison is possible when constancy is treated as a matter of degree.
