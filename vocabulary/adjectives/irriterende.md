## irriterende

**English:** annoying, irritating

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | irriterende | En irriterende summelyd kom fra kjøleskapet. | An annoying buzzing sound came from the refrigerator. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | irriterende | Ei irriterende flue surret rundt lampen. | An annoying fly buzzed around the lamp. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | irriterende | Et irriterende varsel dukket opp hver gang jeg åpnet appen. | An annoying notification appeared every time I opened the app. |
| Positiv flertall | irriterende | Irriterende forsinkelser gjorde at vi mistet siste buss. | Annoying delays made us miss the last bus. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | irriterende | Den irriterende pipelyden sluttet først da jeg byttet batteri. | The annoying beeping stopped only when I changed the battery. |
| Komparativ | mer irriterende | Lyden er mer irriterende om natta enn om dagen. | The sound is more annoying at night than during the day. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest irriterende | Av alle avbruddene var det siste mest irriterende. | Of all the interruptions, the last was the most annoying. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest irriterende | Det mest irriterende varselet kunne heldigvis slås av. | Fortunately, the most annoying notification could be turned off. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka lists the present participle and its adjectival use. Positive agreement is invariable. Comparison with mer/mest is productive in this sense. Examples are original.

**Notes:** Source spelling irriterende. This is the invariable adjectival present participle of irritere in the sense ‘annoy’. It describes what causes annoyance. The related irritert describes someone who feels annoyed.
