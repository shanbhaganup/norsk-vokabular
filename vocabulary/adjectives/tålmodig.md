## tålmodig

**English:** patient; tolerant; persevering

**Topics:** emotions, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | tålmodig | Han var tålmodig med den nye eleven. | He was patient with the new student. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | tålmodig | Hun er tålmodig når barna stiller spørsmål. | She is patient when the children ask questions. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | tålmodig | Et tålmodig arbeid med detaljene ga et godt resultat. | Patient work on the details produced a good result. |
| Positiv flertall | tålmodige | De tålmodige foreldrene ventet utenfor. | The patient parents waited outside. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | tålmodige | Den tålmodige læreren forklarte oppgaven igjen. | The patient teacher explained the assignment again. |
| Komparativ | tålmodigere | Hun ble tålmodigere etter hvert som hun fikk erfaring. | She became more patient as she gained experience. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | tålmodigst | Han var tålmodigst av oss. | He was the most patient of us. |
| Superlativ bestemt | tålmodigste | Hun er den tålmodigste personen i gruppa. | She is the most patient person in the group. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Describes someone who waits calmly, accepts something calmly, or perseveres. The dictionary lists *tålmodigere – tålmodigst – tålmodigste*. The positive form can also be used adverbially in a sentence, but that use is not adjective inflection.
