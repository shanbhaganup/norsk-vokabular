## arrestert

**English:** arrested; detained by police

**Topics:** law, crime

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | arrestert | En arrestert mann ble avhørt i går. | An arrested man was questioned yesterday. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | arrestert | Ei arrestert kvinne fikk snakke med advokaten sin. | An arrested woman was allowed to speak with her lawyer. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | arrestert | Et arrestert vitne ble løslatt samme kveld. | An arrested witness was released that evening. |
| Positiv flertall | arresterte | De arresterte demonstrantene fikk juridisk hjelp. | The arrested demonstrators received legal assistance. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | arresterte | Den arresterte sjåføren nektet å svare. | The arrested driver refused to answer. |

**Notes:** Perfect participle of *arrestere*, used adjectivally here in the sense “detained by police.” Bokmålsordboka also records *arrestere* in the distinct sense “to point out that something is wrong”; this entry follows the source record's arrested-person sense.
