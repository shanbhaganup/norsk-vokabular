## slapp

**English:** limp; slack; tired; weak; lax

**Topics:** condition, health, behavior

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | slapp | Etter løpeturen følte han seg slapp. | He felt weak after the run. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | slapp | Hun hadde en slapp holdning etter den lange reisen. | She had a drooping posture after the long journey. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | slapt | Det var et slapt håndtrykk. | It was a limp handshake. |
| Positiv flertall | slappe | De slappe seilene hang nesten urørlige. | The slack sails hung almost motionless. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | slappe | Den slappe kontrollen førte til flere feil. | The lax supervision led to several mistakes. |
| Komparativ | slappere | Markedet er slappere denne måneden. | The market is slower this month. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | slappest | Etter arbeidsdagen var jeg slappest av alle. | After the workday, I was the most tired of all. |
| Superlativ bestemt | slappeste | Det var den slappeste kontrollen vi hadde sett. | It was the most lax supervision we had seen. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The adjective has several senses: not taut or hanging loosely; weak or tired; lazy or indifferent; ineffective; and describing a market with low demand. The existing expression *slappe av* is a distinct multiword expression, not a duplicate of this adjective.
