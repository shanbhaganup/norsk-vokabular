## villig

**English:** willing; ready; amenable

**Topics:** cooperation, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | villig | Han var villig til å hjelpe naboen. | He was willing to help the neighbour. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | villig | Hun fant ei villig deltaker til forsøket. | She found a willing participant for the experiment. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | villig | Barnet var villig til å hjelpe til. | The child was willing to help. |
| Positiv flertall | villige | De villige frivillige møtte opp tidlig. | The willing volunteers arrived early. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | villige | Den villige medarbeideren tok på seg oppgaven. | The willing employee took on the task. |
| Komparativ | villigere | Hun ble villigere til å prøve etter hvert. | She became more willing to try over time. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | villigst | Av gruppa var han villigst til å ta ansvar. | Of the group, he was most willing to take responsibility. |
| Superlativ bestemt | villigste | Hun var den villigste kandidaten til å reise. | She was the most willing candidate to travel. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means ready to do what is requested or required, or willing to agree or act without difficulty. The word also appears adverbially in some contexts, such as *fisken bet villig* (“the fish bit readily”); that is not an adjective form.
