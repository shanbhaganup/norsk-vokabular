## merkelig

**English:** strange; peculiar; unusual

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | merkelig | Han fortalte en merkelig historie. | He told a strange story. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | merkelig | Hun fikk ei merkelig melding. | She received a strange message. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | merkelig | Det var et merkelig sammentreff. | It was a strange coincidence. |
| Positiv flertall | merkelige | De hørte merkelige lyder fra kjelleren. | They heard strange sounds from the basement. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | merkelige | Den merkelige hendelsen ble omtalt i avisa. | The strange incident was reported in the newspaper. |
| Komparativ | merkeligere | Situasjonen ble merkeligere etter hvert. | The situation became stranger as time went on. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | merkeligst | Av alt hun sa, var dette merkeligst. | Of everything she said, this was the strangest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | merkeligste | Det var den merkeligste drømmen jeg har hatt. | It was the strangest dream I have ever had. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means strikingly strange or peculiar; the dictionary also gives ‘distinctive/interesting’. The comparative and superlative forms are listed in Bokmålsordboka.
