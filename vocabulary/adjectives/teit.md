## teit

**English:** silly; stupid; tight (of a rope)

**Topics:** describing, behavior

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | teit | Han kom med en teit kommentar i timen. | He made a silly comment in class. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | teit | Hun følte seg teit da hun glemte navnet. | She felt silly when she forgot the name. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | teit | Det var et teit forslag. | It was a stupid suggestion. |
| Positiv flertall | teite | De teite vitsene fikk ingen til å le. | The silly jokes made no one laugh. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | teite | Den teite feilen var lett å rette. | The stupid mistake was easy to fix. |
| Komparativ | teitere | Den andre vitsen var teitere enn den første. | The second joke was sillier than the first. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | teitest | Av forslagene var dette teitest. | Of the suggestions, this was the silliest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | teiteste | Det var den teiteste kommentaren i møtet. | It was the silliest comment in the meeting. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary has two senses: taut or without slack (of rope and similar things), and about a person, having poor ability to think or understand. Examples use the latter sense; the comparison forms apply to that sense. The source translation “silly” omits the taut-rope sense.
