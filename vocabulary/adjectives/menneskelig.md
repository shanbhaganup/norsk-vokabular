## menneskelig

**English:** human; humane; humane and kind

**Topics:** people, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | menneskelig | Han viste en menneskelig side. | He showed a human side. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | menneskelig | Hun ba om en menneskelig forklaring. | She asked for a humane explanation. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | menneskelig | Det var et menneskelig behov. | It was a human need. |
| Positiv flertall | menneskelige | Menneskelige feil kan få store følger. | Human errors can have serious consequences. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | menneskelige | Den menneskelige organismen trenger søvn. | The human organism needs sleep. |
| Komparativ | mer menneskelig | En personlig samtale virker mer menneskelig enn et standardsvar. | A personal conversation seems more human than a stock response. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest menneskelig | Av løsningene virket denne mest menneskelig. | Of the solutions, this one seemed the most humane. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest menneskelige | De valgte den mest menneskelige behandlingen. | They chose the most humane treatment. |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; analytical comparison used for the gradable humane/natural sense.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes ‘relating to or characteristic of humans’, ‘mild/humane/kind’, and ‘natural/easygoing’. It lists no synthetic comparison. Analytical *mer/mest* is used only for the gradable humane or natural sense; the species-related sense is not graded.
