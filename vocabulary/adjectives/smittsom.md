## smittsom

**English:** contagious; infectious; catching

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | smittsom | Legen undersøkte en smittsom sykdom. | The doctor examined a contagious disease. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | smittsom | Influensa er ei smittsom lidelse. | Influenza is a contagious illness. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | smittsomt | Dette er et smittsomt virus. | This is a contagious virus. |
| Positiv flertall | smittsomme | Smittsomme sykdommer sprer seg raskt. | Contagious diseases spread quickly. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | smittsomme | Den smittsomme infeksjonen krevde isolasjon. | The contagious infection required isolation. |
| Komparativ | smittsommere | Denne varianten er smittsommere enn den forrige. | This variant is more contagious than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | smittsomst | Av variantene var denne smittsomst. | Of the variants, this one was the most contagious. |
| Superlativ bestemt | smittsomste | Dette var den smittsomste varianten i utbruddet. | This was the most contagious variant in the outbreak. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary also has a figurative sense meaning tempting to imitate, as in a trend catching on. The examples use the literal disease-transmission sense.
