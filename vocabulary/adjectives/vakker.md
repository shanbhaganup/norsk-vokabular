## vakker

**English:** beautiful; lovely; moving

**Topics:** describing, nature, art

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | vakker | De fant en vakker sti langs elva. | They found a beautiful path along the river. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | vakker | Hun hadde på seg ei vakker drakt. | She was wearing a beautiful dress. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | vakkert | Det var et vakkert landskap rundt hytta. | There was beautiful scenery around the cabin. |
| Positiv flertall | vakre | De vakre blomstene sto i en vase. | The beautiful flowers stood in a vase. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | vakre | Den vakre utsikten strakte seg helt til fjorden. | The beautiful view stretched all the way to the fjord. |
| Komparativ | vakrere | Himmelen ble vakrere etter solnedgang. | The sky became more beautiful after sunset. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | vakrest | Av bygningene var denne vakrest. | Of the buildings, this one was most beautiful. |
| Superlativ bestemt | vakreste | Det var den vakreste talen under seremonien. | It was the most beautiful speech at the ceremony. |

**Form status:** Agreement and comparison verified; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Can describe attractive appearance, beautiful or moving qualities, or morally admirable actions. Examples illustrate appearance, scenery and a speech. The adverbial use *vakkert* is related but is not an adjective form.
