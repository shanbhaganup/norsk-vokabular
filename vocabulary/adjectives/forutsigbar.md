## forutsigbar

**English:** predictable

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | forutsigbar | Vi snakket om en forutsigbar reaksjon. | We talked about a predictable reaction. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | forutsigbar | Vi snakket om ei forutsigbar løsning. | We talked about a predictable solution. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | forutsigbart | Vi snakket om et forutsigbart resultat. | We talked about a predictable result. |
| Positiv flertall | forutsigbare | Vi snakket om to forutsigbare reaksjoner. | We talked about two predictable reactions. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | forutsigbare | Vi snakket om den forutsigbare reaksjonen. | We talked about the predictable reaction. |
| Komparativ | mer forutsigbar | Denne reaksjonen er mer forutsigbar enn den andre. | This reaction is more predictable than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest forutsigbar | Av de to er denne reaksjonen mest forutsigbar. | Of the two, this reaction is the most predictable. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest forutsigbare | Vi snakket om den mest forutsigbare reaksjonen. | We talked about the most predictable reaction. |
