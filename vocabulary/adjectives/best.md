## best

**English:** best; most suitable; finest

**Topics:** describing, comparison

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Superlativ ubestemt, hankjønn | best | Han er best i klassen. | He is the best in the class. |
| Superlativ ubestemt, hunkjønn | best | Hun er best på laget. | She is the best on the team. |
| Superlativ ubestemt, intetkjønn | best | Dette er best for barnet. | This is best for the child. |
| Superlativ ubestemt, flertall | beste | De beste spillerne trente først. | The best players trained first. |
| Superlativ bestemt | beste | Den beste spilleren fikk en medalje. | The best player received a medal. |
| Komparativ | — | — | — |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified. Comparative inapplicable: this lemma is already a superlative.

**Notes:** *Best* is the superlative of *god* and *bra*. As an adjective the indefinite singular/predicative form is *best*; attributive definite singular and plural take *beste*. It may also be used substantivally, but this entry documents adjective use. The examples use the “most suitable” sense.
