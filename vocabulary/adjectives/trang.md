## trang

**English:** narrow; cramped; tight; financially strained

**Topics:** describing, space

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | trang | De kjørte gjennom en trang dal. | They drove through a narrow valley. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | trang | Hun fant ei trang gate i den gamle bydelen. | She found a narrow street in the old district. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | trangt | Det var et trangt rom med bare ett vindu. | It was a cramped room with only one window. |
| Positiv flertall | trange | De trange buksene var vanskelige å ta av. | The tight trousers were difficult to take off. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | trange | Den trange passasjen førte ned til kjelleren. | The narrow passage led down to the basement. |
| Komparativ | trangere | Denne dalen er trangere enn den vi gikk gjennom i går. | This valley is narrower than the one we walked through yesterday. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | trangest | Av gatene er denne trangest. | Of the streets, this one is the narrowest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | trangeste | De måtte gå gjennom den trangeste delen av tunnelen. | They had to walk through the narrowest part of the tunnel. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes having too little space, a restricted outlook, and being in financial difficulty. Examples focus on physical space and fit. The adverbial use in expressions such as *bo trangt* is separate from adjective inflection.
