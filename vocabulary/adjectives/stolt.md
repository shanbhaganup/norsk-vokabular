## stolt

**English:** proud; dignified; ambitious

**Topics:** emotions, achievement

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | stolt | Han var stolt av sønnen sin. | He was proud of his son. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | stolt | Hun var stolt av innsatsen sin. | She was proud of her effort. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | stolt | Barnet var stolt over tegningen sin. | The child was proud of its drawing. |
| Positiv flertall | stolte | De stolte foreldrene tok mange bilder. | The proud parents took many pictures. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | stolte | Den stolte vinneren holdt en kort tale. | The proud winner gave a short speech. |
| Komparativ | stoltere | Hun ble stoltere av framgangen hver uke. | She grew prouder of the progress each week. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | stoltest | Av foreldrene var han stoltest etter seremonien. | Of the parents, he was proudest after the ceremony. |
| Superlativ bestemt | stolteste | Det var hennes stolteste øyeblikk. | It was her proudest moment. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes pride in someone or something one is connected to, excessive self-regard, something bringing honor, ambition, and stately appearance. Examples illustrate pride in family, effort and achievement. The source gloss is accurate for the central sense.
