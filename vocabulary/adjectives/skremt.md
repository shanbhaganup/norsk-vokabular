## skremt

**English:** frightened; scared

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | skremt | En skremt hund gjemte seg under bordet. | A frightened dog hid under the table. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | skremt | Ei skremt jente ringte foreldrene sine. | A frightened girl called her parents. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | skremt | Et skremt barn holdt moren i hånden. | A frightened child held its mother’s hand. |
| Positiv flertall | skremte | De skremte passasjerene gikk rolig ut av toget. | The frightened passengers calmly left the train. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | skremte | Den skremte katten gjemte seg i skapet. | The frightened cat hid in the cupboard. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer skremt | Etter det høye smellet virket hun mer skremt. | After the loud bang, she seemed more frightened. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest skremt | Av barna var han mest skremt av tordenen. | Of the children, he was most frightened by the thunder. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest skremte | De mest skremte gjestene forlot rommet først. | The most frightened guests left the room first. |

**Form status:** Participial agreement checked; mer/mest shown as productive constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Adjectival perfect participle of *skremme*. Masculine, feminine and neuter singular use *skremt*; plural and definite forms use *skremte*. The dictionary also records *skremt* as the perfect participle in compound verb forms; this entry covers its adjectival use. Comparison describes degree of fear and is shown with productive mer/mest constructions.
