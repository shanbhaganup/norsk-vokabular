## kraftig

**English:** strong; powerful; intense

**Topics:** describing, health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | kraftig | En kraftig vind blåste over fjorden. | A strong wind blew across the fjord. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | kraftig | Hun fikk ei kraftig forkjølelse i vinter. | She caught a bad cold this winter. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | kraftig | Et kraftig jordskjelv rammet området. | A powerful earthquake struck the area. |
| Positiv flertall | kraftige | Kraftige regnbyger traff kysten. | Heavy rain showers hit the coast. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | kraftige | Den kraftige vinden stanset ferga. | The strong wind stopped the ferry. |
| Komparativ | kraftigere | Vi trenger en kraftigere motor til båten. | We need a more powerful engine for the boat. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | kraftigst | Av de tre motorene er denne kraftigst. | Of the three engines, this one is the most powerful. |
| Superlativ bestemt | kraftigste | Dette er den kraftigste stormen på mange år. | This is the strongest storm in many years. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka verifies positive agreement and synthetic comparison.

**Notes:** Kraftig covers physical strength and high intensity; the examples illustrate intensity and force. The reference topic “emotions” is broad for this headword. This entry uses describing and health for its strength and intensity senses. Kraftig can also be used adverbially, but that is not an adjective inflection included here. Examples are original teaching sentences.
