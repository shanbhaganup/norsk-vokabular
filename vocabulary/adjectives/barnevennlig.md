## barnevennlig

**English:** child-friendly

**Topics:** travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | barnevennlig | Vi snakket om en barnevennlig park. | We talked about a child-friendly park. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | barnevennlig | Vi snakket om ei barnevennlig strand. | We talked about a child-friendly beach. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | barnevennlig | Vi snakket om et barnevennlig hotell. | We talked about a child-friendly hotel. |
| Positiv flertall | barnevennlige | Vi snakket om to barnevennlige parker. | We talked about two child-friendly parks. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | barnevennlige | Vi snakket om den barnevennlige parken. | We talked about the child-friendly park. |
| Komparativ | mer barnevennlig | Denne parken er mer barnevennlig enn den andre. | This park is more child-friendly than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest barnevennlig | Av de to er denne parken mest barnevennlig. | Of the two, this park is the most child-friendly. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest barnevennlige | Vi snakket om den mest barnevennlige parken. | We talked about the most child-friendly park. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and agreement checked.

**Notes:** Comparison with mer and mest is a productive construction. NoW source form: “barnevennlig”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
