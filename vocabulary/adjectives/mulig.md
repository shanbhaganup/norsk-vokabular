## mulig

**English:** possible; feasible; potential

**Topics:** describing, planning

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | mulig | Vi trenger en mulig løsning før fredag. | We need a possible solution before Friday. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | mulig | Hun foreslo ei mulig forklaring. | She suggested a possible explanation. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | mulig | Det er et mulig alternativ. | It is a possible alternative. |
| Positiv flertall | mulige | De vurderte flere mulige løsninger. | They considered several possible solutions. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | mulige | Den mulige årsaken ble undersøkt. | The possible cause was investigated. |
| Komparativ | — | — | — |

**Form status:** Agreement forms checked; comparison inapplicable to the possibility/feasibility sense.

**Notes:** Means capable of being done or occurring; it can also describe a potential person or explanation. Comparison is not normally used for this modal or potential meaning. Fixed degree expressions such as *så snart som mulig* are constructions, not adjective forms.
