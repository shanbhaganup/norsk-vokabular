## forsvunnet

**English:** missing; disappeared

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | forsvunnen / forsvunnet | En forsvunnen gutt ble funnet; en forsvunnet nøkkel ble funnet. | A missing boy was found; a missing key was found. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | forsvunnen / forsvunnet | En forsvunnen kvinne ble funnet; en forsvunnet veske ble funnet. | A missing woman was found; a missing bag was found. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | forsvunnet | Et forsvunnet brev lå under bordet. | A missing letter lay under the table. |
| Positiv flertall | forsvunne | De forsvunne barna kom hjem. | The missing children came home. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | forsvunne | Den forsvunne hunden kom tilbake. | The missing dog came back. |

**Form status:** Verified participle agreement; comparison inapplicable to this result-state sense.

**Notes:** Adjectival past participle of *forsvinne*. Bokmålsordboka lists both *forsvunnen* and *forsvunnet* before masculine/feminine nouns, *forsvunnet* before neuter nouns, and *forsvunne* in plural and definite use. Comparative and superlative are not used for this sense.
