## idiotisk

**English:** idiotic; extremely foolish

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | idiotisk | Det var et idiotisk forslag. | It was an idiotic proposal. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | idiotisk | Hun avviste ei idiotisk forklaring. | She rejected an idiotic explanation. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | idiotisk | Dette er et idiotisk påfunn. | This is an idiotic idea. |
| Positiv flertall | idiotiske | De idiotiske reglene ble fjernet. | The idiotic rules were removed. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | idiotiske | Det idiotiske påfunnet kostet oss dyrt. | The idiotic idea cost us dearly. |
| Komparativ | mer idiotisk | Den andre planen er enda mer idiotisk. | The other plan is even more idiotic. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest idiotisk | Av forslagene var dette mest idiotisk. | Of the proposals, this was the most idiotic. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest idiotiske | Det var det mest idiotiske påfunnet den dagen. | It was the most idiotic idea that day. |

**Form status:** Positive agreement forms verified; comparison is productive periphrasis.

**Notes:** The dictionary defines the meaning as very foolish and gives no synthetic comparison forms. Periphrastic *mer/mest* expresses degree; examples are original teaching sentences.
