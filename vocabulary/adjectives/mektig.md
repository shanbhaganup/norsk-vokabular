## mektig

**English:** powerful; mighty; imposing

**Topics:** power, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | mektig | En mektig leder holdt talen. | A powerful leader gave the speech. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | mektig | Hun er en mektig politiker. | She is a powerful politician. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | mektig | Vi så et mektig fossefall. | We saw an imposing waterfall. |
| Positiv flertall | mektige | De mektige kreftene formet landskapet. | The powerful forces shaped the landscape. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | mektige | Den mektige stormen nærmet seg kysten. | The powerful storm approached the coast. |
| Komparativ | mektigere | Landet ble mektigere etter alliansen. | The country became more powerful after the alliance. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mektigst | Av lederne var hun mektigst. | Of the leaders, she was the most powerful. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mektigste | Han møtte den mektigste personen i regionen. | He met the most powerful person in the region. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Covers having great power or influence and the related sense ‘large, imposing, overwhelming’. The dictionary also uses *mektig* as an intensifying adverb and for rich, filling food; those are separate uses, not adjective inflections.
