## skyldig

**English:** guilty; owing; obliged

**Topics:** law, responsibility

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | skyldig | Retten fant en skyldig mann. | The court found a guilty man. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | skyldig | Hun følte seg skyldig etter krangelen. | She felt guilty after the argument. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | skyldig | Barnet var skyldig i feilen. | The child was responsible for the mistake. |
| Positiv flertall | skyldige | De skyldige partene måtte betale erstatning. | The guilty parties had to pay compensation. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | skyldige | Den skyldige personen innrømmet alt. | The guilty person admitted everything. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer skyldig | Juryen mente at han var mer skyldig enn medhjelperen. | The jury believed he was more culpable than the accomplice. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest skyldig | Av de tiltalte var hun mest skyldig i forsømmelsen. | Of the accused, she was most responsible for the negligence. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest skyldige | Den mest skyldige parten måtte betale mest. | The most culpable party had to pay the most. |

**Form status:** Agreement checked; mer/mest shown as productive constructions; synthetic comparison unverified.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes being responsible for wrongdoing, owing money, and being obligated or indebted (as in *være noen takk skyldig*). The source gloss “guilty” covers only the first sense. The reference lists synthetic comparison *skyldigere/skyldigst/skyldigste*, but the current Ordbøkene entry gives no degree forms; these are not adopted as verified forms. Productive mer/mest constructions are used for gradable culpability.
