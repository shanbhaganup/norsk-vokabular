## verdt

**English:** worth; worthwhile

**Topics:** money, evaluation

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka lists *verd* and *verdt* as invariant spelling alternatives; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means having monetary or other value, being worthwhile, or deserving something. The dictionary lists both *verd* and *verdt* without agreement variation. Examples show both the shorter *verd* spelling and *verdt*. The following complement (such as a price, effort or infinitive clause) expresses what something is worth; it is not an inflected form.
