## glad

**English:** happy; glad

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | glad | Han er glad for den gode nyheten. | He is happy about the good news. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | glad | Hun ble glad da hun så vennene sine. | She was happy when she saw her friends. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | glad | Barnet ble glad for gaven. | The child was happy about the present. |
| Positiv flertall | glade | De glade barna løp ut i hagen. | The happy children ran out into the garden. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | glade | Den glade læreren hilste på klassen. | The happy teacher greeted the class. |
| Komparativ | gladere | Jeg blir gladere når solen skinner. | I get happier when the sun shines. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | gladest | Av alle ble hun gladest for resultatet. | Of everyone, she was happiest about the result. |
| Superlativ bestemt | gladeste | Han var den gladeste gjesten på festen. | He was the happiest guest at the party. |

**Form status:** Dictionary-listed agreement and comparison verified.

**Notes:** Synthetic comparison: gladere, gladest, gladeste.
