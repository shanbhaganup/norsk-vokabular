## glatt

**English:** smooth; slippery

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | glatt | Veien er glatt etter regnet. | The road is slippery after the rain. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | glatt | Ballen har en glatt overflate. | The ball has a smooth surface. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | glatt | Bordet har et glatt lokk. | The table has a smooth lid. |
| Positiv flertall | glatte | De glatte steinene lå langs stranden. | The smooth stones lay along the beach. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | glatte | Den glatte isen gjorde turen vanskelig. | The slippery ice made the trip difficult. |
| Komparativ | glattere | Denne overflaten er glattere enn den andre. | This surface is smoother than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | glattest | Av alle stoffene føles silke glattest. | Of all the fabrics, silk feels smoothest. |
| Superlativ bestemt | glatteste | Dette er den glatteste veien i området. | This is the slipperiest road in the area. |

**Form status:** Dictionary-listed agreement and comparison verified.

**Notes:** Synthetic comparison: glattere, glattest, glatteste. The dictionary also records adverbial uses; this entry covers adjective use.
