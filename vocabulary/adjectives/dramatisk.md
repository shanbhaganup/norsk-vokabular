## dramatisk

**English:** dramatic

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | dramatisk | Dette er en dramatisk film. | This is a dramatic film. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | dramatisk | Dette er ei dramatisk scene. | This is a dramatic scene. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | dramatisk | Dette er et dramatisk verk. | This is a dramatic work. |
| Positiv flertall | dramatiske | Vi fant dramatiske hendelser ved inngangen. | We found dramatic events by the entrance. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement verified; original teaching examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Adjective is invariant in agreement except plural/definite -e. Ordbøkene gives no synthetic comparison; mer/mest are natural for degree.
