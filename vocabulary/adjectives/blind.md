## blind

**English:** blind; without sight

**Topics:** health, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | blind | En blind musiker spilte piano på scenen. | A blind musician played piano on stage. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | blind | Ei blind jente lærte punktskrift på skolen. | A blind girl learned Braille at school. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | blindt | Hunden hjelper et blindt barn i hverdagen. | The dog helps a blind child in everyday life. |
| Positiv flertall | blinde | Blinde reisende kan bestille assistanse på stasjonen. | Blind travellers can book assistance at the station. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | blinde | Den blinde mannen gikk sammen med en ledsager. | The blind man walked with a companion. |
| Komparativ | blindere | Etter sykdommen ble han blindere på det ene øyet enn på det andre. | After the illness, he was more blind in one eye than in the other. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | blindest | Av de to øynene var det venstre blindest. | Of the two eyes, the left was the most blind. |
| Superlativ bestemt | blindeste | Det blindeste øyet reagerte ikke på lyset. | The most blind eye did not react to the light. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison forms verified.

**Notes:** This entry illustrates the literal sense “without sight.” Bokmålsordboka also records figurative senses and the adverbial form *blindt*, outside this adjective sense. Comparatives are contextually most natural for degrees of visual impairment.
