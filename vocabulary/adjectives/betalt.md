## betalt

**English:** paid; compensated

**Topics:** work, money

**Notes:** Perfect participle of *betale*, used adjectivally to describe a completed payment or an activity that receives compensation. The source record's gloss “paid” is interpreted in these senses. Expressions such as *bedre betalt* are possible, but the cited dictionary does not provide a degree paradigm for this participial adjective; its comparative and superlative forms remain unverified.
