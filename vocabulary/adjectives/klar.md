## klar

**English:** clear; ready

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | klar | Han ga et klart svar. | He gave a clear answer. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | klar | Hun er klar for reisen. | She is ready for the trip. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | klart | Vannet er klart. | The water is clear. |
| Positiv flertall | klare | De klare instruksene lå på bordet. | The clear instructions were on the table. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | klare | Den klare beskjeden nådde alle. | The clear message reached everyone. |
| Komparativ | klarere | Etter forklaringen ble alt klarere. | After the explanation, everything became clearer. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | klarest | Hun forklarte saken klarest. | She explained the matter most clearly. |
| Superlativ bestemt | klareste | Dette er den klareste beskjeden. | This is the clearest message. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene forms checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Two dictionary entries support clear and ready senses. The source “Verb” label is wrong for Norwegian klar; the distinct verb klare means manage/succeed.
