## detaljert

**English:** detailed

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | detaljert | Dette er en detaljert rapport. | This is a detailed report. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | detaljert | Dette er ei detaljert beskrivelse. | This is a detailed description. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | detaljert | Dette er et detaljert kart. | This is a detailed map. |
| Positiv flertall | detaljerte | Vi fant detaljerte planer ved inngangen. | We found detailed plans by the entrance. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | detaljerte | Dette er det detaljerte kartet. | This is the detailed map. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement verified; original teaching examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The source form is the same in masculine/feminine/neuter; plural and definite forms take -e. Productive mer/mest comparison is used because detail can vary by degree.
