## opptatt

**English:** busy; occupied; engaged

**Topics:** work, time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | opptatt | Han er en opptatt lege denne uka. | He is a busy doctor this week. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | opptatt | Hun er opptatt med et prosjekt. | She is busy with a project. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | opptatt | Det var et opptatt telefonnummer. | It was a busy phone line. |
| Positiv flertall | opptatte | De opptatte medarbeiderne tok en kort pause. | The busy employees took a short break. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | opptatte | Den opptatte plassen var reservert. | The occupied space was reserved. |
| Komparativ | mer opptatt | Jeg er mer opptatt i morgen enn i dag. | I am busier tomorrow than today. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | mest opptatt | Av oss var hun mest opptatt denne måneden. | Of us, she was the busiest this month. |
| Superlativ bestemt | mest opptatte | Dette var den mest opptatte perioden på kontoret. | This was the busiest period at the office. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement forms checked; analytical comparison used for workload/intensity.

**Notes:** The dictionary gives ‘working/busy’ and ‘not available/occupied’. *Opptatt av* can mean interested or engaged in something, and *opptatt med* means occupied with a task. No synthetic comparison is listed; *mer/mest* are used for the gradable busy sense.
