## stengt

**English:** closed; shut; blocked

**Topics:** places, condition

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Positiv hunkjønn | stengt | Ei stengt dør hindret dem i å gå inn. | A closed door kept them from entering. |
