## råtten

**English:** rotten; decayed; corrupt

**Topics:** food, describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | råtten | Han kastet en råtten potet. | He threw away a rotten potato. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | råtten | Hun fant ei råtten plomme i skåla. | She found a rotten plum in the bowl. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | råttent | De kastet et råttent eple. | They threw away a rotten apple. |
| Positiv flertall | råtne | Råtne blader lå på bakken. | Rotten leaves were lying on the ground. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | råtne | Den råtne frukten luktet sterkt. | The rotten fruit smelled strongly. |
| Komparativ | råtnere | Dette eplet er råtnere enn det andre. | This apple is more rotten than the other one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt | råtnest | Av fruktene var denne råtnest. | Of the fruits, this one was the most rotten. |
| Superlativ bestemt | råtneste | Han kastet den råtneste plommen. | He threw away the most rotten plum. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement and comparison checked; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Means decomposing/decayed and can describe something crumbly, weak, lazy, or morally corrupt. Examples focus on spoiled food. The dictionary lists *råtnere, råtnest,* and *råtneste*.
