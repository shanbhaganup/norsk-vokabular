## overskyet

**English:** overcast

**Topics:** weather

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | overskyet | Vi snakket om en overskyet dag. | We talked about an overcast day. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | overskyet | Vi snakket om ei overskyet natt. | We talked about an overcast night. |
