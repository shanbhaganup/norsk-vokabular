## skadet

**English:** injured; damaged

**Topics:** health, condition

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | skadet | En skadet syklist fikk hjelp. | An injured cyclist received help. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | skadet | En skadet kvinne ble undersøkt av legen. | An injured woman was examined by the doctor. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | skada | Et skada tre måtte felles. | A damaged tree had to be felled. |
| Positiv intetkjønn, alternative | skadd | Et skadd kne trenger ro. | An injured knee needs rest. |
| Positiv intetkjønn, alternative | skadet | Et skadet område ble sperret av. | A damaged area was cordoned off. |
| Positiv flertall | skada | Skada bygninger ble undersøkt. | Damaged buildings were inspected. |
| Positiv flertall, alternative | skadde | Skadde passasjerer fikk behandling. | Injured passengers received treatment. |
| Positiv flertall, alternative | skadede | Skadede varer ble erstattet. | Damaged goods were replaced. |
| Positiv flertall, alternative | skadete | Skadete planter ble fjernet fra bedet. | Damaged plants were removed from the flower bed. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | skada | Den skada armen ble bandasjert. | The injured arm was bandaged. |
| Positiv bestemt entall, alternative | skadde | Den skadde spilleren hvilte på benken. | The injured player rested on the bench. |
| Positiv bestemt entall, alternative | skadede | Det skadede utstyret ble sendt tilbake. | The damaged equipment was sent back. |
| Positiv bestemt entall, alternative | skadete | Det skadete området ble merket på kartet. | The damaged area was marked on the map. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer skadet | Etter ulykken var den ene vingen mer skadet enn den andre. | After the accident, one wing was more damaged than the other. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest skadet | Av alle bilene var denne mest skadet. | Of all the cars, this one was the most damaged. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest skadede | De mest skadede gjenstandene ble kastet. | The most damaged items were discarded. |

**Form status:** Participial agreement alternatives checked; comparison examples use productive mer/mest constructions; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** Adjectival perfect participle of skade. The dictionary lists *skadet* for masculine/feminine; neuter alternatives *skada / skadd / skadet*; and plural and definite alternatives *skada / skadde / skadede / skadete*. Each alternative is separately exemplified. The mer/mest constructions are productive descriptions of relative damage, not synthetic comparison forms listed in the entry.
