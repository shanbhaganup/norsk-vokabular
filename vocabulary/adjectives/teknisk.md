## teknisk

**English:** technical; practical; formal (requirements)

**Topics:** technology, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positiv hankjønn | teknisk | Vi fant en teknisk feil i anlegget. | We found a technical fault in the system. |
| Positiv hunkjønn | teknisk | Hun lærte ei teknisk ferdighet på kurset. | She learned a technical skill in the course. |
| Positiv intetkjønn | teknisk | Det var et teknisk krav i kontrakten. | It was a technical requirement in the contract. |
| Positiv flertall | tekniske | De tekniske hjelpemidlene gjorde arbeidet lettere. | The technical aids made the work easier. |
| Positiv bestemt entall | tekniske | Den tekniske løsningen fungerte godt. | The technical solution worked well. |
| Komparativ (productive construction) | mer teknisk | Denne forklaringen er mer teknisk enn den forrige. | This explanation is more technical than the previous one. |
| Superlativ ubestemt (productive construction) | mest teknisk | Av rapportene var denne mest teknisk. | Of the reports, this one was the most technical. |
| Superlativ bestemt (productive construction) | mest tekniske | Han leste den mest tekniske delen av rapporten. | He read the most technical part of the report. |

**Form status:** Dictionary agreement checked; mer/mest shown as productive constructions in the gradable skill/detail sense; original teaching examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers matters relating to technique or methods, practical/technical skill, and formal requirements or prerequisites. The source’s “technical” gloss covers the first sense. Mer/mest are productive comparisons of technical complexity or skill, not synthetic dictionary forms.
