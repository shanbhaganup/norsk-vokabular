## nei

**English:** no

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig: nei | Er posten åpen? – Nei, den stengte klokka fire. | Is the post office open? – No, it closed at four. |
| Høflig avslag | nei takk | Vil du ha mer kaffe? – Nei takk, jeg har nok. | Would you like more coffee? – No, thanks, I have enough. |

**Form status:** Spelling and usage checked in Bokmålsordboka. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source spelling ne is corrected to nei. The examples illustrate a negative reply and a polite refusal.
