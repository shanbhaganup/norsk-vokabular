## takk

**English:** thanks, thank you

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | interjeksjon | Takk, det var hyggelig sagt. | Thanks, that was kind of you to say. |
| Konstruksjon | takk for | Takk for hjelpen! | Thank you for your help! |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Takk can stand alone or be followed by for and what one is thanking someone for.
