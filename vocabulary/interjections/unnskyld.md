## unnskyld

**English:** sorry, excuse me

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unnskyldning | unnskyld | Unnskyld, jeg tok feil sekk. | Sorry, I took the wrong bag. |
| Henvendelse | unnskyld | Unnskyld, hvor er nærmeste apotek? | Excuse me, where’s the nearest pharmacy? |
