## enn

**English:** than

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | subjunksjon | Hun løp fortere enn jeg gjorde. | She ran faster than I did. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Møtet var kortere enn vi hadde ventet. | The meeting was shorter than we had expected. |
| Konstruksjon | komparativ + enn + leddsetning | Hun snakker høyere enn du tror. | She speaks louder than you think. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This use introduces a comparative clause.
