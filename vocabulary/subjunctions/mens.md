## mens

**English:** while, whereas

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | subjunksjon | Jeg ryddet mens hun lagde mat. | I tidied up while she cooked. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Vent her mens jeg henter bilen. | Wait here while I get the car. |
| Konstruksjon, kontrast | mens + leddsetning | Jeg liker kaffe, mens broren min foretrekker te. | I like coffee, whereas my brother prefers tea. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Mens can express simultaneous events or contrast.
