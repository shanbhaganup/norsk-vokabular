## inntil

**English:** until

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | subjunksjon | Regelen gjelder inntil vi får ny beskjed. | The rule applies until we receive further notice. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Vi holdt døra åpen inntil alle var inne. | We kept the door open until everyone was inside. |
| Konstruksjon | inntil at + leddsetning | De ble på stedet inntil at veien åpnet igjen. | They remained there until the road reopened. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Temporal inntil introduces an end point. The clause can follow inntil or inntil at.
