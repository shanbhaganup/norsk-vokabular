## at

**English:** that

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | subjunksjon | Hun sa at bussen var forsinket. | She said that the bus was late. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Jeg tror at han er hjemme. | I think that he’s at home. |
| Konstruksjon, leddsetning | at + subjekt + verbal | De visste at vi ikke hadde bil. | They knew that we didn’t have a car. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** At introduces a content clause.
