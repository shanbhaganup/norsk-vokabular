## hvis

**English:** if

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | subjunksjon | Jeg ringer hvis toget blir forsinket. | I’ll call if the train is delayed. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Hvis det regner, blir vi hjemme. | If it rains, we’ll stay home. |
| Konstruksjon, vilkår | hvis + leddsetning | Ring meg hvis du ikke finner veien. | Call me if you can’t find the way. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Hvis introduces a condition.
