## siden

**English:** since

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | subjunksjon | Jeg har bodd her siden jeg begynte på skolen. | I’ve lived here since I started school. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Vi har kjent hverandre siden vi var barn. | We’ve known each other since we were children. |
| Konstruksjon, leddsetning | siden + subjekt + verbal | Det har regnet siden vi kom hjem. | It has been raining since we got home. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Temporal siden can introduce a subordinate clause that identifies the starting event.
