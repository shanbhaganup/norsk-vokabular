## å

**English:** to (infinitive marker)

**Topics:** grammar

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Jeg liker å lese om kvelden. | I like to read in the evening. |
| Infinitivsmerke | å | Jeg liker å lese om kvelden. | I like to read in the evening. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka classifies the infinitive marker as a subjunksjon. Modal verbs commonly take a bare infinitive. NoW source form: “å (infinitive marker)”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
