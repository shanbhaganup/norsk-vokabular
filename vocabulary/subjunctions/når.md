## når

**English:** when, whenever

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | subjunksjon | Jeg ringer når jeg har funnet nøklene. | I’ll call when I’ve found the keys. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Hun tar toget når bilen står på verksted. | She takes the train when the car is at the repair shop. |
| Konstruksjon, leddsetning | når + subjekt + verbal | Når skolen begynner, får vi en ny timeplan. | When school starts, we’ll get a new timetable. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Temporal når introduces a subordinate clause. It is common for future or repeated events.
