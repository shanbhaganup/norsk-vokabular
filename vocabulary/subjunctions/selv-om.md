## selv om

**English:** although, even though

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | subjunksjon | Vi gikk tur selv om det regnet. | We went for a walk even though it was raining. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Hun kom selv om hun var trøtt. | She came even though she was tired. |
| Konstruksjon | selv om + leddsetning | Selv om bussen var sen, rakk vi møtet. | Although the bus was late, we made the meeting. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The fixed subjunction introduces a concession.
