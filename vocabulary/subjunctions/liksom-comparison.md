## liksom

**English:** as, just as

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | subjunksjon | Liksom faren gjorde før ham, står han opp klokka fem hver dag. | Just as his father did before him, he gets up at five every day. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Hun valgte å bli, liksom søsteren hadde gjort året før. | She chose to stay, just as her sister had done the year before. |
| Konstruksjon, alternativ skrivemåte | likesom + leddsetning | Likesom bestefaren gjorde før oss, planter vi poteter her hver vår. | Just as our grandfather did before us, we plant potatoes here every spring. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Here liksom or likesom introduces a comparison clause. Both spellings are accepted.
