## etter

**English:** after

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse | subjunksjon | Vi spiste etter at gjestene kom. | We ate after the guests arrived. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Hun ringte etter at møtet var slutt. | She called after the meeting ended. |
| Konstruksjon | etter at + leddsetning | Etter at snøen smeltet, åpnet veien igjen. | After the snow melted, the road reopened. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Temporal etter introduces a subordinate clause, often as etter at.
