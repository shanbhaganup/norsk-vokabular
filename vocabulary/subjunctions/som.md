## som

**English:** who, which, that, as

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relativ leddsetning | som | Jeg traff en nabo som nettopp hadde flyttet inn. | I met a neighbour who had just moved in. |
| Bøyning | ubøyelig | Jeg fant brevet som du lette etter. | I found the letter you were looking for. |
| Sammenligning | som + leddsetning | Gjør det som vi avtalte. | Do it as we agreed. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or constituent checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Som can introduce a relative clause or a comparison. In a relative clause, it is required when it serves as the subject.
