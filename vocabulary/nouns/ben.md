## ben

**English:** leg

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Barnet har et ben i gips. | The child has a leg in a cast. |
| Entall ubestemt | ben | Jeg har vondt i et ben. | I have pain in one leg. |
| Entall bestemt | benet | Benet er bandasjert. | The leg is bandaged. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ben | Hun trener ben hver uke. | She trains legs every week. |
| Flertall bestemt | bena | Bena er slitne etter turen. | The legs are tired after the hike. |
| Flertall bestemt | benene | Benene på bordet er av tre. | The table legs are made of wood. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** Both definite plural forms are retained. The English sense is the body part.
