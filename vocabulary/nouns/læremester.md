## læremester

**English:** teacher, mentor, master teacher

**Topics:** school

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Han ble en læremester for mange unge musikere. | He became a mentor to many young musicians. |
| Entall ubestemt | læremester | Hun fikk en læremester i treskjæring. | She found a teacher of woodcarving. |
| Entall bestemt | læremesteren | Læremesteren viste eleven hvordan verktøyet skulle brukes. | The teacher showed the pupil how to use the tool. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling, gender and inflection checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The English source gloss ‘master’ is broad. Bokmålsordboka defines this noun as a person who provides instruction, so this entry uses ‘teacher’ or ‘mentor’. All listed plural alternatives are retained and illustrated. The dictionary does not distinguish their relative frequency or register.
