## barnepike

**English:** nanny, babysitter

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Familien ansatte ei barnepike. | The family hired a nanny. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | De søkte etter en erfaren barnepike. | They were looking for an experienced nanny. |
| Entall ubestemt | barnepike | Som ung jobbet hun som barnepike. | When she was young, she worked as a nanny. |
| Entall bestemt | barnepika | Barnepika hentet barna på skolen. | The nanny picked up the children from school. |
| Entall bestemt | barnepiken | Barnepiken leste høyt for barna. | The nanny read aloud to the children. |
| Flertall ubestemt | barnepiker | Flere barnepiker fulgte barna til parken. | Several nannies accompanied the children to the park. |
| Flertall bestemt | barnepikene | Barnepikene byttet på å passe barna. | The nannies took turns looking after the children. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted. The term traditionally refers to a girl or woman who looks after children.
