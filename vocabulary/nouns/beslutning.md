## beslutning

**English:** decision

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Vi må ta ei beslutning før fredag. | We need to make a decision before Friday. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Styret utsatte en viktig beslutning. | The board postponed an important decision. |
| Entall ubestemt | beslutning | De kom fram til en felles beslutning. | They reached a joint decision. |
| Entall bestemt | beslutninga | Beslutninga ble tatt på møtet. | The decision was made at the meeting. |
| Entall bestemt | beslutningen | Hun forklarte beslutningen i et brev. | She explained the decision in a letter. |
| Flertall ubestemt | beslutninger | Lederne tok flere vanskelige beslutninger. | The managers made several difficult decisions. |
| Flertall bestemt | beslutningene | Vi skrev ned beslutningene fra møtet. | We wrote down the decisions from the meeting. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine forms are accepted.
