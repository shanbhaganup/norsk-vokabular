## parti

**English:** section; part of an area

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et parti av lia var dekket av snø. | A section of the hillside was covered in snow. |
| Entall ubestemt | parti | Vi gikk gjennom et smalt parti av skogen. | We walked through a narrow section of the forest. |
| Entall bestemt | partiet | Partiet rundt inngangen er nylig malt. | The section around the entrance was recently painted. |
| Flertall ubestemt | parti / partier | Flere parti / partier av fjellet er bratte. | Several sections of the mountain are steep. |
| Flertall bestemt | partia / partiene | Partia er merket på kartet. / Partiene er merket på kartet. | The sections are marked on the map. / The sections are marked on the map. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary sense 1 is a part of a whole or a bounded area. Other senses have separate entries; both plural forms shown.
