## men

**English:** lasting injury or impairment

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Han fikk et varig men etter fallet. | He suffered a lasting impairment after the fall. |
