## midt

**English:** middle; centre

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En midt kan skille begynnelsen fra slutten. | A middle can separate the beginning from the end. |
| Entall ubestemt | midt | Vi fant en midt mellom de to ytterpunktene. | We found a middle point between the two extremes. |
| Entall bestemt | midten | Midten av arket var brettet. | The middle of the sheet was folded. |
| Flertall ubestemt | midter | Flere midter ble markert på kartet. | Several middle points were marked on the map. |
| Flertall bestemt | midtene | Midtene på de tre strekningene ble målt. | The midpoints of the three stretches were measured. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source spelling midten is the definite singular; the headword is midt. The noun is distinct from adjectival/adverbial midt.
