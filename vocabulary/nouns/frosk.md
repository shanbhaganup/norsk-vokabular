## frosk

**English:** frog

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En frosk gjemte seg under et blad. | A frog hid under a leaf. |
| Entall ubestemt | frosk | En frosk hoppet over stien. | A frog hopped across the path. |
| Entall bestemt | frosken | Frosken satt ved dammen. | The frog sat by the pond. |
| Flertall ubestemt | frosker | Vi så flere frosker ved dammen. | We saw several frogs by the pond. |
| Flertall bestemt | froskene | Froskene forsvant ned i vannet. | The frogs disappeared into the water. |
