## frykt

**English:** fear; apprehension; reverence

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En frykt for høyder kan begrense hverdagen. | A fear of heights can limit daily life. |
| Entall ubestemt | frykt | Frykt kan gjøre det vanskelig å sove. | Fear can make it difficult to sleep. |
| Entall bestemt | frykten | Frykten hennes forsvant gradvis. | Her fear gradually disappeared. |
| Flertall ubestemt | frykter | Ulike frykter kan oppstå etter en ulykke. | Different fears can arise after an accident. |
| Flertall bestemt | fryktene | Fryktene hans påvirket hverdagen. | His fears affected daily life. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and paradigm checked against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists fear/apprehension as well as respect or reverence. Examples use the fear sense.
