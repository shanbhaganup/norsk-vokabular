## luksus

**English:** luxury

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En luksus som varmt vann var sjelden på hytta. | A luxury like hot water was rare at the cabin. |
| Entall ubestemt | luksus | For familien var en ferie ved sjøen en luksus. | For the family, a holiday by the sea was a luxury. |
| Entall bestemt | luksusen | Luksusen varte bare noen dager. | The luxury lasted only a few days. |
| Flertall ubestemt | luksuser | Små luksuser gjør hverdagen hyggeligere. | Small luxuries make everyday life more pleasant. |
| Flertall bestemt | luksusene | Luksusene ble valgt bort da utgiftene steg. | The luxuries were cut when expenses rose. |

**Form status:** Headword and applicable forms reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Often a mass noun; plural refers to separate luxuries.
