## kontroll

**English:** inspection; supervision; control

**Topics:** society, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kontroll av utstyret er nødvendig. | An inspection of the equipment is necessary. |
| Entall ubestemt | kontroll | Politiet gjennomførte en kontroll ved grensen. | The police carried out an inspection at the border. |
| Entall bestemt | kontrollen | Kontrollen tok bare noen minutter. | The inspection took only a few minutes. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kontroller | Veivesenet utfører kontroller av tunge kjøretøy. | The road authority carries out inspections of heavy vehicles. |
| Flertall bestemt | kontrollene | Kontrollene avdekket flere feil. | The inspections revealed several errors. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; examples and English gloss authored for this project.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists masculine inflection and plural kontroller/kontrollene. Kontroll can mean inspection or supervision, but also power or command (for example, ha kontroll over noe); the English gloss is deliberately broader than the single example context. The reference snapshot notes its source gender was corrected against the dictionary.
