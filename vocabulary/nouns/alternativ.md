## alternativ

**English:** alternative; option

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et alternativ kan være å ta bussen. | One alternative could be to take the bus. |
| Entall ubestemt | alternativ | Vi trenger et alternativ til den dyre løsningen. | We need an alternative to the expensive solution. |
| Entall bestemt | alternativet | Alternativet er å vente til i morgen. | The alternative is to wait until tomorrow. |
| Flertall ubestemt | alternativ | Det finnes flere alternativ for hjemreisen. | There are several options for getting home. |
| Flertall ubestemt | alternativer | Hun sammenlignet tre alternativer før hun bestemte seg. | She compared three alternatives before deciding. |
| Flertall bestemt | alternativa | Alternativa ble lagt fram for styret. | The alternatives were presented to the board. |
| Flertall bestemt | alternativene | Alternativene ble lagt fram for styret. | The alternatives were presented to the board. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** Both indefinite plural forms and both definite plural forms are listed in Bokmålsordboka. This entry uses the noun sense, meaning an option or possible course of action.
