## hals

**English:** neck; throat; neck-like part; neck of a garment

**Topics:** body, clothing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En hals kan bli øm når man er forkjølet. | A neck can become sore when you have a cold. |
| Entall ubestemt | hals | En hals må holdes varm om vinteren. | She had a red scarf around her neck. |
| Entall bestemt | halsen | Halsen på gitaren var laget av lønn. | The neck of the guitar was made of maple. |
| Flertall ubestemt | halser | Seilbåten hadde flere halser å feste tauene i. | The sailboat had several tacks to which the ropes could be fastened. |
| Flertall bestemt | halsene | Halsene på genserne var for trange. | The necklines of the sweaters were too tight. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists several related senses: the body part, throat, part of a garment, a narrow neck-like section, a boat's forepart, a sailing term, and a figurative suffix for a reckless person. Examples use more than one concrete sense; the specialized sailing plural halser is dictionary-attested.
