## strøm

**English:** flow; current

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En strøm av vann rant gjennom renna. | A flow of water ran through the channel. |
| Entall ubestemt | strøm | Det går en sterk strøm gjennom sundet. | A strong current runs through the strait. |
