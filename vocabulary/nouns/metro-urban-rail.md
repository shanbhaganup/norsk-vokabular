## metro

**English:** metro; underground railway

**Topics:** city

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En metro går under sentrum. | A metro runs beneath the city centre. |
| Entall ubestemt | metro | Byen planlegger metro til flyplassen. | The city is planning a metro line to the airport. |
| Entall bestemt | metroen | Metroen stopper ved museet. | The metro stops at the museum. |
| Flertall ubestemt | metroer | Flere metroer ble satt i trafikk i rushtiden. | Several metro trains were put into service during rush hour. |
| Flertall bestemt | metroene | Metroene går hvert femte minutt. | The metro trains run every five minutes. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword, masculine gender and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The examples use the urban rail system and its trains, both natural extensions of the source gloss `metro`.
