## bad

**English:** bath; bathroom; bathing place

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et bad etter skituren varmet barna. | A bath after the ski trip warmed the children up. |
| Entall ubestemt | bad | Etter turen tok hun et bad. | After the trip, she took a bath. |
| Entall bestemt | badet | Badet i leiligheten ble pusset opp i fjor. | The bathroom in the apartment was renovated last year. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bad | Kommunen har flere offentlige bad langs kysten. | The municipality has several public bathing facilities along the coast. |
| Flertall bestemt | bada / badene | Bada holder åpent hele sommeren. / Badene holder åpent hele sommeren. | The bathing facilities are open all summer. / The bathing facilities are open all summer. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The dictionary covers bathing, bathroom, bathing facilities and other senses. Examples use the bath/bathing-place senses; neuter mass uses such as “et bad av lys” are outside this entry.
