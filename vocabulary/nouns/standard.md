## standard

**English:** standard; norm

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En standard for sikkerhet gjelder i hele bransjen. | A safety standard applies throughout the industry. |
| Entall ubestemt | standard | Bedriften følger en internasjonal standard. | The company follows an international standard. |
| Entall bestemt | standarden | Standarden beskriver kravene til produktet. | The standard describes the product requirements. |
| Flertall ubestemt | standarder | Ulike standarder regulerer utslippene. | Different standards regulate emissions. |
| Flertall bestemt | standardene | Standardene blir revidert hvert femte år. | The standards are revised every five years. |
