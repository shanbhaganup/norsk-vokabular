## parfyme

**English:** scent of perfume

**Topics:** shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En parfyme fylte rommet med en søt lukt. | A perfume scent filled the room with a sweet smell. |
| Entall ubestemt | parfyme | Det luktet parfyme i gangen. | The hallway smelled of perfume. |
| Entall bestemt | parfymen | Parfymen hang igjen i skjerfet. | The perfume scent lingered in the scarf. |
| Flertall ubestemt | parfymer | Ulike parfymer blandet seg i butikken. | Different perfume scents mingled in the shop. |
| Flertall bestemt | parfymene | Parfymene i rommet var sterke. | The perfume scents in the room were strong. |

**Notes:** Dictionary sense 2: the smell of perfume; distinct from the perfume liquid sense in parfyme-liquid.
