## natt

**English:** night

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei natt sov vi under åpen himmel. | One night we slept under the open sky. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En natt sov vi under åpen himmel. | One night we slept under the open sky. |
| Entall ubestemt | natt | De kom hjem sent på natt. | They came home late at night. |
| Entall bestemt | natta | Natta var stille og stjerneklar. | The night was quiet and starry. |
| Entall bestemt | natten | Natten var stille og stjerneklar. | The night was quiet and starry. |
| Flertall ubestemt | netter | Lange netter gjør vinteren mørk. | Long nights make winter dark. |
| Flertall bestemt | nettene | Nettene ble lysere utover våren. | The nights grew brighter as spring progressed. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms reviewed; original examples authored for the labelled forms and senses.

**Notes:** The dictionary also uses natt in the fixed greeting god natt and for work performed at night. The companion entry covers the night-work sense; god natt is a phrase, not an inflection.
