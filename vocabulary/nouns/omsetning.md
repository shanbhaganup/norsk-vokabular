## omsetning

**English:** turnover; sales revenue

**Topics:** money, business

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei omsetning på fem millioner er realistisk. / En omsetning på fem millioner er realistisk. | A turnover of five million is realistic. / A turnover of five million is realistic. |
| Entall ubestemt | omsetning | Butikken hadde høy omsetning i desember. | The shop had high turnover in December. |
| Entall bestemt | omsetninga / omsetningen | Omsetninga steg i vår. / Omsetningen steg i vår. | Turnover rose this spring. / Turnover rose this spring. |
| Flertall ubestemt | omsetninger | Flere omsetninger ble registrert i helgen. | Several sales transactions were recorded over the weekend. |
| Flertall bestemt | omsetningene | Omsetningene ble ført i regnskapet. | The transactions were entered in the accounts. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Dictionary distinguishes buying and selling, a firm's total sales over a period, and throughput in a chemical/metallurgical process. The source 'turnover' aligns especially with revenue; some plural uses mean individual sales transactions. Both genders and definite forms illustrated.
