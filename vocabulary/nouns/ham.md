## ham

**English:** hide; coat (of an animal)

**Topics:** animals

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En ham beskytter dyret mot kulde. | An animal's coat protects it from the cold. |
| Entall ubestemt | ham | En ham beskytter dyret mot kulde. | An animal's coat protects it from the cold. |
| Entall bestemt | hammen | Hammen skifter hos enkelte dyr før vinteren. | The coat changes in some animals before winter. |
| Flertall ubestemt | hammer | Noen dyr skifter hammer etter årstiden. | Some animals change their coats with the seasons. |
| Flertall bestemt | hammene | Hammer fra ulike årstider ble sammenlignet. | Coats from different seasons were compared. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source attachment translates ham as the pronoun "him," but Bokmålsordboka lists this noun as an animal's seasonal outer covering, also used figuratively for outward appearance.
