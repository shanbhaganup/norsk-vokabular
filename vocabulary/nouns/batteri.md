## batteri

**English:** battery

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Lommelykta trenger et nytt batteri. | The torch needs a new battery. |

| Entall bestemt | batteriet | Batteriet må lades. | The battery needs charging. |
