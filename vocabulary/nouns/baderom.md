## baderom

**English:** bathroom

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Hver leilighet har et baderom. | Each flat has a bathroom. |
| Entall ubestemt | baderom | Huset har baderom i begge etasjer. | The house has a bathroom on each floor. |
| Entall bestemt | baderommet | Baderommet ble pusset opp i fjor. | The bathroom was renovated last year. |
| Flertall ubestemt | baderom | Vi trenger to baderom. | We need two bathrooms. |
| Flertall bestemt | baderomma | De to baderomma har samme fliser. | The two bathrooms have the same tiles. |
| Flertall bestemt | baderommene | Baderommene blir vasket hver morgen. | The bathrooms are cleaned every morning. |
