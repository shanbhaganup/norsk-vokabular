## avskjed

**English:** farewell, parting

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | De tok en varm avskjed på kaia. | They said a warm goodbye on the quay. |
| Entall ubestemt | avskjed | Hun tok avskjed med kollegene. | She said goodbye to her colleagues. |
| Entall bestemt | avskjeden | Avskjeden ble kort. | The farewell was brief. |
| Flertall ubestemt | avskjeder | Etter mange avskjeder var hun endelig på vei. | After many goodbyes, she was finally on her way. |
| Flertall bestemt | avskjedene | Han husket avskjedene på stasjonen. | He remembered the farewells at the station. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** These examples concern saying goodbye.
