## krem

**English:** cream

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Hun kjøpte en krem til desserten. | She bought a cream for dessert. |
| Entall ubestemt | krem | Jeg trenger krem til sausen. | I need cream for the sauce. |
| Entall bestemt | kremen | Kremen gjorde sausen fyldig. | The cream made the sauce rich. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kremer | Butikken selger både matkremer og hudkremer. | The shop sells both cooking creams and skin creams. |
| Flertall bestemt | kremene | Kremene står i kjøleskapet. | The creams are in the refrigerator. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Krem can refer to food cream or a cream product; the plural example uses products.
