## byråd

**English:** city councillor

**Topics:** city, politics

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En byråd svarte på spørsmål fra pressen. | A city councillor answered questions from the press. |
| Entall ubestemt | byråd | Hun er byråd for samferdsel. | She is the city councillor for transport. |
| Entall bestemt | byråden | Byråden la fram et nytt forslag. | The councillor presented a new proposal. |
| Flertall ubestemt | byråder | Flere byråder deltok i møtet. | Several city councillors took part in the meeting. |
| Flertall bestemt | byrådene | Byrådene møtte innbyggerne på torget. | The city councillors met residents in the square. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers an individual member of a city council (dictionary entry I), distinct from byråd meaning the governing body.
