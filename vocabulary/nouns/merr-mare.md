## merr

**English:** mare; adult female horse

**Topics:** nature

**Form status:** Ordbøkene gender alternatives and masculine plural paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine are accepted. The definite singular is `merra` with feminine inflection and `merren` with masculine inflection. The dictionary lists the plural `merrer / merrene` under masculine; feminine plural forms are not provided in the cited table.
