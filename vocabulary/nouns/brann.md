## brann

**English:** fire

**Topics:** safety, emergencies

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En brann brøt ut i bygningen i natt. | A fire broke out in the building last night. |
| Entall ubestemt | brann | Brann ødela flere hus i området. | Fire destroyed several houses in the area. |
| Entall bestemt | brannen | Brannen ble slukket før morgenen. | The fire was extinguished before morning. |
| Flertall ubestemt | branner | Tørre somre kan føre til skogbranner. | Dry summers can lead to forest fires. |
| Flertall bestemt | brannene | Brannene ble meldt til nødetatene. | The fires were reported to the emergency services. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.
