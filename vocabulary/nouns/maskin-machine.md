## maskin

**English:** machine

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En maskin sorterer pakkene automatisk. | A machine sorts the parcels automatically. |
| Entall ubestemt | maskin | Fabrikken kjøpte en maskin til den nye linjen. | The factory bought a machine for the new line. |
| Entall bestemt | maskinen | Maskinen stanser hvis døren åpnes. | The machine stops if the door is opened. |
| Flertall ubestemt | maskiner | Moderne maskiner bruker mindre strøm. | Modern machines use less electricity. |
| Flertall bestemt | maskinene | Maskinene blir kontrollert hver morgen. | The machines are checked every morning. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The source gender was corrected to masculine `en maskin`. Ordbøkene describes a device that converts supplied energy into mechanical work or another form of energy.
