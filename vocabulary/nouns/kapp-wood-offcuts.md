## kapp

**English:** offcuts; cut-off ends of timber or lumber

**Topics:** materials

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Vi fyrte med en kapp fra vedskjulet. | We burned a piece of wood offcut from the woodshed. |
| Entall ubestemt | kapp | Et kapp lå ved siden av sagen. | A wood offcut lay beside the saw. |
| Entall bestemt | kappen | Kappen var for kort til å brukes i møbelet. | The offcut was too short to use in the piece of furniture. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kapper | Verkstedet sorterer kapper etter treslag. | The workshop sorts wood offcuts by type of wood. |
| Flertall bestemt | kappene | Kappene ble lagt i en egen haug. | The offcuts were placed in a separate pile. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified where available; individual alternatives are listed separately with examples.

**Notes:** The reference record glosses kapp as “cloaks” and combines three homonyms. Bokmålsordboka confirms this entry means cut-off ends of timber or lumber, with masculine inflection. The cape and the invariable expression sense are separate entries below.
