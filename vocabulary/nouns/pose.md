## pose

**English:** bag; sack; pouch

**Topics:** shopping; household

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En pose ligger ved døra. | A bag is by the door. |
| Entall ubestemt | pose | Hun kjøpte en pose på butikken. | She bought a bag at the shop. |
| Entall bestemt | posen | Posen er laget av papir. | The bag is made of paper. |
| Flertall ubestemt | poser | Flere poser ble lagt i bilen. | Several bags were put in the car. |
| Flertall bestemt | posene | Posene står under benken. | The bags are under the bench. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling, gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Source gender corrected to masculine. The dictionary also has related senses for a bag’s contents and folds/wrinkles; this entry covers the source’s container sense.
