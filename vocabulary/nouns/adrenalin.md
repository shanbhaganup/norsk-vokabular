## adrenalin

**English:** adrenaline (US also epinephrine)

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Ordboka oppgir «et adrenalin» for å vise at ordet er intetkjønn. | The dictionary gives “et adrenalin” to show that the noun is neuter. |
| Entall ubestemt | adrenalin | Jeg kjente et rush av adrenalin da startskuddet gikk. | I felt a rush of adrenaline when the starting gun went off. |
| Entall bestemt | adrenalinet | Adrenalinet i kroppen gjorde det vanskelig å sitte stille. | The adrenaline in my body made it hard to sit still. |
| Flertall ubestemt | adrenalin | I flertall kan du skrive «adrenalin» i ubestemt form. | In the indefinite plural, you can write “adrenalin.” |
| Flertall bestemt | adrenalina | «Adrenalina» er en bestemt flertallsform i Bokmål. | “Adrenalina” is a definite plural form in Bokmål. |
| Flertall bestemt | adrenalinene | Ordboka fører også «adrenalinene» som bestemt flertallsform. | The dictionary also lists “adrenalinene” as a definite plural form. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** Usually a mass noun in both Norwegian and English. The article and plural examples cite the dictionary-listed forms in teaching sentences because ordinary use counts doses or preparations instead.
