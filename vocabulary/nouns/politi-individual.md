## politi

**English:** police officer (colloquial use of politi)

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En politi stod ved inngangen. | A police officer stood by the entrance. |
| Entall ubestemt | politi | Vitnet så en politi ved hjørnet. | The witness saw a police officer at the corner. |
| Entall bestemt | politien | Politien snakket med vitnet. | The police officer spoke with the witness. |
| Flertall ubestemt | politier | To politier kom til stedet. | Two police officers arrived at the scene. |
| Flertall bestemt | politiene | Politiene undersøkte området. | The police officers searched the area. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Masculine dictionary homonym 1 means a police officer/person; illustrated separately from the neuter institution sense. The source’s neuter form politiet belongs to the other headword entry.
