## ro

**English:** calm, quiet

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei ro senket seg over rommet. | A calm settled over the room. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En ro bredte seg blant passasjerene. | A calm spread among the passengers. |
| Entall ubestemt | ro | Hun fant ro etter en lang tur. | She found calm after a long walk. |
| Entall bestemt | roa | Roa i huset varte ikke lenge. | The quiet in the house did not last long. |
| Entall bestemt | roen | Roen kom tilbake da regnet sluttet. | The calm returned when the rain stopped. |
| Flertall ubestemt | roer | Flere roer i hverdagen kan hjelpe mot stress. | More moments of calm in daily life can help with stress. |
| Flertall bestemt | roene | De korte roene mellom arbeidsøktene var velkomne. | The brief periods of calm between work sessions were welcome. |

**Form status:** Forms checked against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The source combines forms from different homographs. For the calm sense, the dictionary gives roer/roene with masculine inflection. Rør/rørne belong to a different noun sense and are excluded. The feminine paradigm has no plural listed.
