## pilot

**English:** pilot; aircraft operator

**Topics:** transport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En pilot må følge sikkerhetsrutinene. | A pilot must follow the safety procedures. |
| Entall ubestemt | pilot | Selskapet ansatte en pilot i vår. | The company hired a pilot this spring. |
| Entall bestemt | piloten | Piloten kontrollerte instrumentene. | The pilot checked the instruments. |
| Flertall ubestemt | piloter | Flere piloter deltok på kurset. | Several pilots attended the course. |
| Flertall bestemt | pilotene | Pilotene ventet på værmeldingen. | The pilots waited for the weather forecast. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Source sense is the person who operates an aircraft. The dictionary also uses pilot as a compound modifier meaning preliminary/experimental.
