## pris

**English:** pinch of snuff

**Topics:** health; historical usage

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En pris snus ble lagt på lokket. | A pinch of snuff was placed on the lid. |
| Entall ubestemt | pris | Han tok seg en pris etter maten. | He took a pinch of snuff after the meal. |
| Entall bestemt | prisen | Prisen var liten. | The pinch was small. |
| Flertall ubestemt | priser | Han målte opp to priser snus. | He measured out two pinches of snuff. |
| Flertall bestemt | prisene | Prisene ble lagt i små papirposer. | The pinches were placed in small paper packets. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Source gender corrected to masculine according to Ordbøkene. Separate dictionary homonym 2: a pinch of snuff, commonly in the expression ta seg en pris. The plural forms are dictionary-listed but rare; examples illustrate the forms in this sense.
