## fyr

**English:** fire; heating fire

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En fyr brant i ovnen mens vi spiste. | A fire burned in the stove while we ate. |
| Entall ubestemt | fyr | Et fyr brant i peisen mens vi spiste. | A fire burned in the fireplace while we ate. |
| Entall bestemt | fyren | Fyren holdt hytta varm gjennom natta. | The fire kept the cabin warm through the night. |
| Flertall ubestemt | fyrer | Flere fyr ble tent langs stien for å holde varmen. | Several fires were lit along the trail to keep warm. |
| Flertall bestemt | fyrene | Fyrene ble slukket før alle gikk til sengs. | The fires were put out before everyone went to bed. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and inflection checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This dictionary sense also covers a furnace or heating system; the examples use a contained fire.
