## muldvarp

**English:** mole; secret infiltrator

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En muldvarp lekket informasjon til pressen. | A mole leaked information to the press. |
| Entall ubestemt | muldvarp | Gruppa mistenkte en muldvarp blant medlemmene. | The group suspected a mole among its members. |
| Entall bestemt | muldvarpen | Muldvarpen sendte rapporter til motparten. | The mole sent reports to the opposing side. |
| Flertall ubestemt | muldvarper | Muldvarper kan arbeide skjult i årevis. | Moles can work undercover for years. |
| Flertall bestemt | muldvarpene | Muldvarpene ble avslørt etter en lang etterforskning. | The moles were exposed after a long investigation. |
| Variant spelling, indefinite singular | moldvarp | En moldvarp lekket opplysninger fra gruppa. | A mole leaked information from the group. |
| Variant spelling, definite singular | moldvarpen | Moldvarpen ble avslørt. | The mole was exposed. |
| Variant spelling, indefinite plural | moldvarper | Moldvarper kan samle informasjon over tid. | Moles can gather information over time. |
| Variant spelling, definite plural | moldvarpene | Moldvarpene ble fjernet fra organisasjonen. | The moles were removed from the organization. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original teaching examples supplied for every listed form.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka 38914 also uses muldvarp figuratively for a person engaged in secret, clandestine activity; both muldvarp and moldvarp spellings are listed.
