## innkjøp

**English:** purchase; shopping; purchased goods

**Topics:** shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et innkjøp kan ta lengre tid enn ventet. | A shopping trip can take longer than expected. |
| Entall ubestemt | innkjøp | Hun gjorde et innkjøp på vei hjem. | She made a purchase on her way home. |
| Entall bestemt | innkjøpet | Innkjøpet ble levert i dag. | The purchase was delivered today. |
| Flertall ubestemt | innkjøp | Vi gjorde innkjøp før helgen. | We did the shopping before the weekend. |
| Flertall bestemt | innkjøpa | Innkjøpa står i gangen. | The purchases are in the hallway. |
| Flertall bestemt | innkjøpene | Innkjøpene står i gangen. | The purchases are in the hallway. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original teaching examples authored for this project.

**Notes:** Neuter noun. Dictionary accepts both innkjøpa and innkjøpene as definite plural. The dictionary distinguishes the act of buying from goods bought; the examples cover both readings.
