## bakgård

**English:** rear courtyard, backyard

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Det er en bakgård bak bygården. | There’s a courtyard behind the apartment building. |
| Entall ubestemt | bakgård | De fant en stille bakgård der de kunne sitte. | They found a quiet rear courtyard where they could sit. |
| Entall bestemt | bakgården | Syklene står i bakgården. | The bikes are in the rear courtyard. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bakgårder | Flere bakgårder i kvartalet har blitt gjort om til hager. | Several courtyards in the block have been turned into gardens. |
| Flertall bestemt | bakgårdene | Barna lekte i bakgårdene etter skolen. | The children played in the courtyards after school. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The examples concern the courtyard behind a building.
