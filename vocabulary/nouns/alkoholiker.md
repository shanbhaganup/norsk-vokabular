## alkoholiker

**English:** alcoholic (noun), person with alcohol dependence

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | I intervjuet fortalte en alkoholiker om veien til behandling. | In the interview, a person with alcohol dependence talked about how they got into treatment. |
| Entall ubestemt | alkoholiker | Han beskrev seg selv som alkoholiker. | He described himself as an alcoholic. |
| Entall bestemt | alkoholikeren | Alkoholikeren i romanen ber søsteren om hjelp. | The alcoholic in the novel asks his sister for help. |
| Flertall ubestemt | alkoholikere | Boka bygger på samtaler med alkoholikere og familiene deres. | The book draws on conversations with people with alcohol dependence and their families. |
| Flertall bestemt | alkoholikerne | Alkoholikerne som deltok i prosjektet, fortalte om støtten de hadde fått. | The people with alcohol dependence who took part in the project talked about the support they had received. |

**Form status:** Forms checked against NAOB and attested usage. The Bokmålsordboka article link is retained from the reference record. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** Both the Norwegian noun and the English gloss can sound labeling. The person-first English gloss is useful in neutral contexts.
