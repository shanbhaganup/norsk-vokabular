## bit

**English:** bite; piece; part

**Topics:** food, general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Han tok en bit av eplet. | He took a bite of the apple. |
| Entall ubestemt | bit | Jeg vil gjerne ha en bit til. | I would like one more piece. |
| Entall bestemt | biten | Biten av glasset lå på gulvet. | The piece of glass was on the floor. |
| Flertall ubestemt | biter | Skålen knuste i mange biter. | The bowl broke into many pieces. |
| Flertall bestemt | bitene | Bitene ble samlet opp forsiktig. | The pieces were carefully collected. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers the ordinary noun senses: a mouthful, a piece or part, and a share in the fixed definite form. The reference combines these senses with the computing unit (entry I).
