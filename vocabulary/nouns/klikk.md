## klikk

**English:** click; snap

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en / et | En klikk fra låsen varslet at døra var lukket. Et klikk på lenka åpnet sida. | A click from the lock signalled that the door was shut. A click on the link opened the page. |
