## middel

**English:** mean; average; average value

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En middel på 2000 tonn ble beregnet for uka. | A mean of 2,000 tonnes was calculated for the week. |
| Entall ubestemt | middel | Produksjonen lå nær en middel på 2000 tonn per uke. | Production was close to a mean of 2,000 tonnes per week. |
| Entall bestemt | middelen | Temperaturen ble sammenlignet med middelen for måneden. | The temperature was compared with the monthly average. |
| Flertall ubestemt | midler | Analytikerne sammenlignet midler fra ulike perioder. | The analysts compared averages from different periods. |
| Flertall bestemt | midlene | Midlene for de tre gruppene var nesten like. | The averages for the three groups were nearly equal. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene entry 2 masculine paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** This is the masculine `middel 2`, meaning a mean or average. Forms are `en middel – middelen – midler – midlene`. It is distinct from the neuter noun for a means/remedy and the masculine noun for wealth.
