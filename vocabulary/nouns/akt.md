## akt

**English:** act (division of a play or opera)

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei akt kan inneholde flere scener. | An act can contain several scenes. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En akt varer omtrent en halvtime. | An act lasts about half an hour. |
| Entall ubestemt | akt | Hun kommer inn på scenen i siste akt. | She comes onstage in the last act. |
| Entall bestemt | akta | Den første akta slutter med en sang. | The first act ends with a song. |
| Entall bestemt | akten | Den andre akten begynner etter pausen. | The second act begins after the intermission. |
| Flertall ubestemt | akter | Operaen har tre akter. | The opera has three acts. |
| Flertall bestemt | aktene | Vi rakk å kjøpe kaffe mellom aktene. | We had time to buy coffee between acts. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The imported gloss *act* is ambiguous, and the source topic was *food*. The working entry uses the theatrical sense in *akt I*, sense 4. Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted for this sense.
