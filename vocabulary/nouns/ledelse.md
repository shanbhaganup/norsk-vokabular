## ledelse

**English:** leadership; management

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En ledelse som lytter, bygger tillit. | Leadership that listens builds trust. |
| Entall ubestemt | ledelse | Skolen fikk en ny ledelse i høst. | The school got new management this autumn. |
| Entall bestemt | ledelsen | Ledelsen svarte på kritikken. | The management responded to the criticism. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ledelser | Flere ledelser deltok på seminaret. | Several management teams attended the seminar. |
| Flertall bestemt | ledelsene | Ledelsene møttes for å drøfte samarbeidet. | The leadership teams met to discuss cooperation. |

**Form status:** Headword and applicable forms reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Usually collective; plural refers to separate leadership teams.
