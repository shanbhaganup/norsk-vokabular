## IP-adresse

**English:** IP address

**Topics:** technology

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original teaching examples authored for this project.

**Notes:** Dictionary gives both masculine and feminine paradigms and recognizes IP-adresse and ip-adresse. Masculine forms are en IP-adresse, IP-adressen, IP-adresser, IP-adressene; feminine forms are ei/en IP-adresse, IP-adressa, IP-adresser, IP-adressene. Lowercase forms are also accepted. The reference record omits the gender distinction and repeats capitalization variants; this entry records the actual distinct grammatical forms.
