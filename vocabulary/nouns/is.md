## is

**English:** ice; frozen water

**Topics:** nature, food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En is dekker tjernet om vinteren. | Ice covers the pond in winter. |
| Entall ubestemt | is | Det ligger is på veien. | There is ice on the road. |
| Entall bestemt | isen | Isen er for tynn til å gå på. | The ice is too thin to walk on. |
| Flertall ubestemt | is | Det lå is langs bredden. | There was ice along the shore. |
| Flertall ubestemt | iser | På fjorden driver store iser sørover. | Large ice floes drift south on the fjord. |
| Flertall bestemt | isene | Isene smeltet i april. | The ice floes melted in April. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original teaching examples authored for this project.

**Notes:** Dictionary distinguishes frozen water or a sheet of ice, an ice cube, and an ice cream. This entry focuses on frozen water/ice sheets; source English “ice” retained. Plural is is or iser, definite plural isene. Source gender confirmed.
