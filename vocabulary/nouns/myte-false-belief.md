## myte

**English:** myth; widespread false belief

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En myte om søvn ble tilbakevist. | A myth about sleep was disproved. |
| Entall ubestemt | myte | Det er en myte at lynet aldri slår ned samme sted to ganger. | It is a myth that lightning never strikes the same place twice. |
| Entall bestemt | myten | Myten spredte seg raskt på nettet. | The myth spread quickly online. |
| Flertall ubestemt | myter | Slike myter kan påvirke folks valg. | Such myths can influence people’s choices. |
| Flertall bestemt | mytene | Mytene ble tilbakevist av forskerne. | The myths were disproved by the researchers. |
