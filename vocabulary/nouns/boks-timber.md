## boks

**English:** timber; squared wood

**Topics:** materials, construction

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En boks lå ved siden av plankene. | A squared timber beam lay beside the planks. |
| Entall ubestemt | boks | Boks brukes som bærekonstruksjon i noen eldre hus. | Squared timber is used as a support in some older houses. |
| Entall bestemt | boksen | Boksen ble målt før den ble saget til. | The beam was measured before it was cut to size. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bokser | Bygget har bokser av furu i takkonstruksjonen. | The building has squared pine beams in its roof structure. |
| Flertall bestemt | boksene | Boksene ble levert til byggeplassen. | The squared timbers were delivered to the construction site. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers timber with an approximately square cross-section (dictionary entry II), separate from boks meaning a container.
