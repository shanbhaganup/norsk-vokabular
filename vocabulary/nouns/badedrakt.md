## badedrakt

**English:** swimsuit

**Topics:** shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Hun kjøpte ei badedrakt til ferien. | She bought a swimsuit for the holiday. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Jeg pakket en badedrakt i sekken. | I packed a swimsuit in my bag. |
| Entall ubestemt | badedrakt | Bassenget krever badedrakt eller badebukse. | The pool requires a swimsuit or swim trunks. |
