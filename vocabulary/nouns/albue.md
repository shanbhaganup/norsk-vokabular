## albue

**English:** elbow

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Jeg fikk en albue i siden på den fulle bussen. | I caught an elbow in the ribs on the crowded bus. |
| Entall ubestemt | albue | Hun støttet seg på én albue mens hun leste. | She propped herself up on one elbow while she read. |
| Entall bestemt | albuen | Jeg slo albuen i dørkarmen. | I banged my elbow on the doorframe. |
| Flertall ubestemt | albuer | Musikerne satt så tett at de støtte borti hverandres albuer. | The musicians sat so close that they bumped elbows. |
| Flertall bestemt | albuene | Han lente albuene mot disken. | He rested his elbows on the counter. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The source gender was corrected to masculine. The examples refer to the joint in the arm.
