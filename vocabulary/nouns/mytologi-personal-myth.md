## mytologi

**English:** mythology surrounding a person or phenomenon

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mytologi rundt artisten vokste fram. | A mythology around the artist developed. |
