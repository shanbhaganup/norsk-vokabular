## profitt

**English:** profit; financial gain

**Topics:** money; business

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En profitt kan investeres på nytt. | A profit can be reinvested. |
| Entall ubestemt | profitt | Selskapet fikk profitt fra salget. | The company made a profit from the sale. |
| Entall bestemt | profitten | Profitten økte i fjor. | The profit increased last year. |
| Flertall ubestemt | profitter | Flere profitter ble ført i regnskapet. | Several profits were entered in the accounts. |
| Flertall bestemt | profittene | Profittene ble fordelt mellom eierne. | The profits were distributed among the owners. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** The dictionary defines profitt as financial gain or economic return. Plural forms are included and illustrated as separate profit amounts.
