## peis

**English:** fireplace; open hearth

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En peis gir varme på kalde kvelder. | A fireplace gives warmth on cold evenings. |
| Entall ubestemt | peis | Vi tente opp i en peis av stein. | We lit a fire in a stone fireplace. |
| Entall bestemt | peisen | Peisen var murt av lokal stein. | The fireplace was built of local stone. |
| Flertall ubestemt | peiser | Moderne hus har ofte elektriske peiser. | Modern houses often have electric fireplaces. |
| Flertall bestemt | peisene | Peisene ble kontrollert før vinteren. | The fireplaces were inspected before winter. |
