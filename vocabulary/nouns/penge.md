## penge

**English:** coin; money (plural penger)

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En penge lå på gulvet. | A coin was lying on the floor. |
| Entall ubestemt | penge | Hun fant en penge under bordet. | She found a coin under the table. |
| Entall bestemt | pengen | Pengen var gammel og slitt. | The coin was old and worn. |
| Flertall ubestemt | penger | De sparte penger til ferien. | They saved money for the holiday. |
| Flertall bestemt | pengene | Pengene lå i en skuff. | The money was in a drawer. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** The singular penge means a coin or banknote; plural penger is the ordinary word for money and can also mean monetary value or a sum. Source form penger is normalized to dictionary lemma penge.
