## ringe

**English:** large wooden vat

**Topics:** home, materials

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei ringe / En ringe ble brukt til å oppbevare melk. | A large wooden vat was used to store milk. |
| Entall ubestemt | ringe | De fant ei ringe på gården. | They found a large wooden vat on the farm. |
| Entall bestemt | ringa / ringen | Ringa / Ringen var laget av trestaver. | The vat was made of wooden staves. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ringer | Gamle ringer sto i uthuset. | Old vats stood in the shed. |
| Flertall bestemt | ringene | Ringene ble vist fram på bygdemuseet. | The vats were displayed at the local museum. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source English gloss “ring” is corrected: noun ringe means a large wooden vessel. It is distinct from the verb ringe and noun ring.
