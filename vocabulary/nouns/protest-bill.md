## protest

**English:** formal declaration that a bill of exchange was not accepted or paid

**Topics:** finance; law

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En protest ble registrert da vekselen ikke ble betalt. | A formal protest was registered when the bill of exchange was not paid. |
| Entall ubestemt | protest | Notarius publicus tok opp en protest. | The notary recorded a formal protest. |
| Entall bestemt | protesten | Protesten dokumenterte den manglende betalingen. | The protest documented the nonpayment. |
| Flertall ubestemt | protester | Flere protester ble ført i protokollen. | Several formal protests were entered in the record. |
| Flertall bestemt | protestene | Protestene ble sendt til banken. | The formal protests were sent to the bank. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Dictionary sense 2: a formal declaration that a bill of exchange was not accepted or paid on time. This technical financial/legal use is distinct from a general objection.
