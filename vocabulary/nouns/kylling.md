## kylling

**English:** chicken; young domestic fowl; chicken meat

**Topics:** animals, food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kylling klekket i natt. | A chick hatched last night. |
| Entall ubestemt | kylling | Vi så en kylling ved hønsehuset. | We saw a chicken near the henhouse. |
| Entall bestemt | kyllingen | Kyllingen fulgte etter høna. | The chick followed the hen. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kyllinger | Kyllinger trenger varme den første tiden. | Chicks need warmth at first. |
| Flertall bestemt | kyllingene | Kyllingene løp inn i hønsehuset. | The chickens ran into the henhouse. |

**Form status:** Verified against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry for spelling, gender, and paradigm; original teaching examples authored for this project.

**Notes:** This entry covers the animal sense and includes the related food sense in the English gloss; the paradigm is shared. Bokmålsordboka also records a figurative sense for an inexperienced young man, not covered here. The source gender was corrected to masculine.
