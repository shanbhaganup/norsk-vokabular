# rein

**English:** field ridge; strip of land

**Topics:** agriculture; land

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei rein | Ei rein av gras skiller jordene. | A grassy strip separates the fields. |

| Entall ubestemt | rein | Vi gikk langs en rein mellom åkrene. | We walked along a ridge between the fields. |
| Entall bestemt | reina | Reina var dekket av kort gress. | The ridge was covered with short grass. |
| Entall bestemt | reinen | Reinen var dekket av kort gress. | The ridge was covered with short grass. |

| Flertall bestemt | reinene | Reinene ble synlige etter pløyingen. | The ridges became visible after ploughing. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** This is dictionary homonym rein 1 (feminine or masculine): a grass-covered strip at a field edge or a long ridge of earth/gravel. Distinct from rein meaning the reindeer.
