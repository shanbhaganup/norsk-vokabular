**Topics:** sport

**Form status:** Ordbøkene gender options and full paradigms checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Both masculine and neuter are accepted. Masculine inflects `en maraton – maratonen – maratoner – maratonene`; neuter inflects `et maraton – maratonet – maraton / maratoner – maratona / maratonene`. Every alternative is shown. Ordbøkene also records a figurative sense for something that goes on for a long time; this entry covers the race.
