## drake

**English:** kite

**Topics:** outdoors

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En drake. | A kite. |
| Entall ubestemt | drake | Et barn sendte opp en drake på stranda. | A child flew a kite on the beach. |
| Entall bestemt | draken | Draken steg høyt i vinden. | The kite rose high in the wind. |
