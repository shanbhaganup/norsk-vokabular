## bryst

**English:** breast

**Topics:** body, health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et bryst kan produsere melk etter en fødsel. | A breast can produce milk after childbirth. |
| Entall ubestemt | bryst | Barnet ble lagt til brystet. | The baby was put to the breast. |
| Entall bestemt | brystet | Brystet ble undersøkt av legen. | The breast was examined by the doctor. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bryster | Hun hadde ømme bryster etter fødselen. | She had sore breasts after giving birth. |
| Flertall bestemt | brysta | Brysta var ømme etter fødselen. | The breasts were sore after giving birth. |
| Flertall bestemt | brystene | Brystene var ømme etter fødselen. | The breasts were sore after giving birth. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers the female breast sense (dictionary entry II), distinct from the general chest/torso sense. The dictionary lists bryster as the indefinite plural and both brysta and brystene as definite plurals.
