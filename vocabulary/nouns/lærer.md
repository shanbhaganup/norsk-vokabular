## lærer

**English:** teacher

**Topics:** school

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En lærer møtte foreldrene etter skoletid. | A teacher met the parents after school. |
| Entall ubestemt | lærer | Skolen søker en lærer i naturfag. | The school is looking for a science teacher. |
| Entall bestemt | læreren | Læreren forklarte oppgaven en gang til. | The teacher explained the task once more. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lærere | Kommunen trenger flere lærere. | The municipality needs more teachers. |
| Flertall bestemt | lærerne | Lærerne planla en fagdag sammen. | The teachers planned a subject day together. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling, gender and inflection checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This is the general noun for a person who teaches, especially school pupils. The feminine-specific noun *lærerinne* is a separate, older term and has its own entry.
