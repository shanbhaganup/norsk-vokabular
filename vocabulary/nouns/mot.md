## mot

**English:** courage

**Topics:** personal qualities

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et mot kan hjelpe oss gjennom en vanskelig tid. | Courage can help us through a difficult time. |
| Entall ubestemt | mot | Hun viste mot da hun sa fra. | She showed courage when she spoke up. |
| Entall bestemt | motet | Motet kom tilbake etter den gode nyheten. | Her courage returned after the good news. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mot | Folk trenger mot i ulike situasjoner. | People need courage in different situations. |
| Flertall bestemt | mota / motene | Mota / Motene deres vokste etter hvert som de fikk erfaring. | Their courage grew as they gained experience. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Entry 39214 is the courage sense. Entry 39215 is a separate, obsolete greeting/homonym and is cited for reference completeness only; it is not included in this sense. Plural uses are uncommon and refer to kinds or instances of courage.
