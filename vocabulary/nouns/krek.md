## krek

**English:** creature; wretched being (derogatory)

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | I eventyret bor det et lite krek i skogen. | A small creature lives in the forest in the fairy tale. |
| Entall ubestemt | krek | De kalte trollet et krek. | They called the troll a wretched creature. |
| Entall bestemt | kreket | Kreket gjemte seg under brua. | The creature hid under the bridge. |
| Flertall ubestemt | krek | I boka møter vi flere krek fra skogen. | In the book we meet several creatures from the forest. |
| Flertall bestemt, a-form | kreka | Kreka i fortellingen skremte barna. | The creatures in the story frightened the children. |
| Flertall bestemt | krekene | Krekene i fortellingen skremte barna. | The creatures in the story frightened the children. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Krek is often derogatory or contemptuous. The unchanged plural and two definite plural forms are illustrated separately.
