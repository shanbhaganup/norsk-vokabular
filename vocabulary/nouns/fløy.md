## fløy

**English:** wing; section of a building

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei fløy på fuglen var skadet. / En fløy på fuglen var skadet. | A wing on the bird was injured. / A wing on the bird was injured. |
| Entall ubestemt | fløy | Ei fløy på fuglen var skadet. | A wing on the bird was injured. |
| Entall bestemt | fløya / fløyen | Fløya / Fløyen er sterk nok til å fly med. | The wing is strong enough to fly with. |
| Flertall ubestemt | fløyer | Museet har to nye fløyer. | The museum has two new wings. |
| Flertall bestemt | fløyene | Fløyene på bygningen ble pusset opp. | The wings of the building were renovated. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted. Examples use the wing and building-wing senses.
