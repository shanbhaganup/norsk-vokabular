## forelder

**English:** parent

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En forelder må signere skjemaet. | A parent must sign the form. |
| Entall ubestemt | forelder | En forelder må signere skjemaet. | A parent must sign the form. |
| Entall bestemt | forelderen | Forelderen fulgte barnet til skolen. | The parent accompanied the child to school. |
| Flertall ubestemt | foreldre | Begge foreldre deltok på møtet. | Both parents attended the meeting. |
| Flertall bestemt | foreldra / foreldrene | Foreldra / Foreldrene kom tidlig til avslutningen. | The parents arrived early for the ceremony. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and listed inflection forms checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The attachment also incorrectly translates this lemma as “grandparent”; Bokmålsordboka defines it as mother or father of a child. Both definite plural alternatives are accepted.
