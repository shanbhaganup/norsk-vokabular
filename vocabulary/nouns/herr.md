## herr

**English:** Mr; form of address before a man's name or title

**Topics:** family, titles

**Form status:** Dictionary-verified as an invariable noun/title.

**Notes:** *Herr* is used before a man's name or title and does not take ordinary noun inflection or an article. The four number/definiteness cells are inapplicable because it functions as an invariable title. The abbreviation is *hr.*
