# pust

**English:** breathing; a pause for rest

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En pust kan bli raskere etter trening. | Breathing can become faster after exercise. |
| Entall ubestemt | pust | Hun trakk en pust før hun svarte. | She took a breath before answering. |
| Entall bestemt | pusten | Pusten hennes roet seg etter noen minutter. | Her breathing settled after a few minutes. |
| Flertall ubestemt | puster | Legen lyttet etter uregelmessige puster. | The doctor listened for irregular breaths. |
| Flertall bestemt | pustene | Pustene ble dypere da han slappet av. | The breaths grew deeper as he relaxed. |
