**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and alternatives checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Promoted from reference record word-0764. Bokmålsordboka accepts masculine and neuter gender. Its neuter paradigm has both kondom and kondomer as indefinite plural, and both kondoma and kondomene as definite plural. This entry illustrates every alternative in the reference paradigm. Use the dictionary sense “contraceptive sheath”; English condom is the corresponding term.
