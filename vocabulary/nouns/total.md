## total

**English:** total; sum

**Topics:** numbers, finance

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En total står nederst på kvitteringen. | A total appears at the bottom of the receipt. |
| Entall ubestemt | total | Jeg regnet ut en total for alle varene. | I calculated a total for all the items. |
| Entall bestemt | totalen | Totalen av terningene er tolv. | The total of the dice is twelve. |
| Flertall ubestemt | totaler | Rapporten viser totaler for hver måned. | The report shows totals for each month. |
| Flertall bestemt | totalene | Totalene står i den siste kolonnen. | The totals are in the last column. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** This is the masculine noun meaning an aggregate amount or sum, distinct from the adjective total.
