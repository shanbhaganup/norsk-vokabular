## askepott

**English:** an overlooked or underestimated person

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Hun beskrev seg selv som en askepott i bransjen. | She described herself as an overlooked figure in the industry. |
| Entall ubestemt | askepott | Klubben var en askepott i serien. | The club was an overlooked outsider in the league. |
| Entall bestemt | askepotten | Askepotten i turneringen slo favoritten. | The tournament’s Cinderella team beat the favourite. |
| Flertall ubestemt | askepotter | Turneringen ga flere askepotter en sjanse til å vise seg fram. | The tournament gave several underdogs a chance to show what they could do. |
| Flertall bestemt | askepottene | Askepottene fikk mest oppmerksomhet etter finalen. | The underdogs got the most attention after the final. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Lowercase askepott is a common noun. The fairy-tale character’s name is capitalized as Askepott.
