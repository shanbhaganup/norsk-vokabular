## klasse

**English:** class; group; category; standard

**Topics:** school, society, language

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei klasse begynner klokka åtte. / En klasse begynner klokka åtte. | A class starts at eight. / A class starts at eight. |
| Entall ubestemt | klasse | Hun går i en klasse med tjue elever. | She is in a class with twenty pupils. |
| Entall bestemt | klassa | Klassa skal på museum i morgen. | The class is going to a museum tomorrow. |
| Entall bestemt | klassen | Klassen skal på museum i morgen. | The class is going to a museum tomorrow. |
| Flertall ubestemt | klasser | Skolen har flere små klasser. | The school has several small classes. |
| Flertall bestemt | klassene | Klassene møtes i aulaen etter lunsj. | The classes meet in the assembly hall after lunch. |

**Form status:** Dictionary paradigm and senses reviewed.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists feminine and masculine gender; the feminine singular definite *klassa* and masculine *klassen* are separately illustrated. The source gloss "class" includes school class as well as broader groups and categories.
