## bevis

**English:** proof, evidence

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Hun fant et bevis på at meldingen var sendt. | She found proof that the message had been sent. |
| Entall ubestemt | bevis | De ba om bevis for påstanden. | They asked for evidence of the claim. |
| Entall bestemt | beviset | Beviset lå i den gamle e-posten. | The proof was in the old email. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bevis | Journalisten samlet flere bevis. | The journalist gathered several pieces of evidence. |
| Flertall ubestemt | beviser | De la fram nye beviser under møtet. | They presented new evidence at the meeting. |
| Flertall bestemt | bevisa | Bevisa pekte i samme retning. | The pieces of evidence pointed in the same direction. |
| Flertall bestemt | bevisene | Hun gikk gjennom bevisene på nytt. | She went over the evidence again. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The plural can refer to separate pieces of evidence. Both definite and both indefinite plural forms are accepted.
