## minutt

**English:** minute

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et minutt går fort når vi har det travelt. | A minute passes quickly when we are busy. |
| Entall ubestemt | minutt | Vent et minutt ved døra. | Wait a minute by the door. |
| Entall bestemt | minuttet | Minuttet var snart over. | The minute was almost over. |
