## bot

**English:** fine (financial penalty)

**Topics:** law, money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Hun fikk ei bot for feilparkering. | She received a fine for illegal parking. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Hun fikk en bot for feilparkering. | She received a fine for illegal parking. |
| Entall ubestemt | bot | Sjåføren måtte betale en bot. | The driver had to pay a fine. |
| Entall bestemt | bota | Bota ble betalt samme dag. | The fine was paid the same day. |
| Entall bestemt | boten | Boten ble betalt samme dag. | The fine was paid the same day. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bøter | Politiet skrev ut flere bøter. | The police issued several fines. |
| Flertall bestemt | bøtene | Bøtene må betales innen fristen. | The fines must be paid by the deadline. |

**Notes:** This entry covers the financial penalty sense. Both feminine and masculine forms are accepted; each definite singular form is illustrated.
