## grad

**English:** degree; step; rank; grade

**Topics:** measurement

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | EN grad er omtalt i teksten. | The grad is mentioned in the text. |
| Entall ubestemt | grad | Hun tok en grad i matematikk. | She earned a degree in mathematics. |
| Entall bestemt | graden | Graden av usikkerhet er liten. | The degree of uncertainty is small. |
| Flertall ubestemt | grader | Det er fem grader ute. | It is five degrees outside. |
| Flertall bestemt | gradene | Gradene steg utover dagen. | The temperatures rose during the day. |
