## avgang

**English:** departure; leaving

**Topics:** travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En avgang kan bli forsinket av dårlig vær. | A departure can be delayed by bad weather. |
| Entall ubestemt | avgang | Toget er klart til avgang. | The train is ready to depart. |
| Entall bestemt | avgangen | Avgangen fra plattform fire er flyttet. | The departure from platform four has been moved. |
| Flertall ubestemt | avganger | Busselskapet har avganger hvert kvarter. | The bus company has departures every fifteen minutes. |
| Flertall bestemt | avgangene | Avgangene til flyplassen går fra holdeplass B. | The departures to the airport leave from stop B. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Also used for leaving an office or school, as in å forberede sin avgang; the travel sense is illustrated throughout the paradigm.
