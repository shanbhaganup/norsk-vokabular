## post

**English:** post; station; position; paid post

**Topics:** work; society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En post ble satt opp ved grensa. | A guard post was set up at the border. |
| Entall ubestemt | post | Hun fikk en post på sykehuset. | She got a post at the hospital. |
| Entall bestemt | posten | Posten ble bemannet hele natta. | The post was staffed all night. |
| Flertall ubestemt | poster | Flere poster ble opprettet i avdelingen. | Several units were established in the department. |
| Flertall bestemt | postene | Postene ble fordelt mellom de ansatte. | The posts were distributed among the employees. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Distinct dictionary homonym 2: a watch/guard position, hospital unit, or paid position. The meanings share the core idea of an assigned post/position; this is separate from the mail sense.
