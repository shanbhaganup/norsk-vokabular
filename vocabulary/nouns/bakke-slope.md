## bakke

**English:** slope; hill

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bakke kan være glatt etter regn. | A slope can be slippery after rain. |
| Entall ubestemt | bakke | Syklene trillet ned en bratt bakke. | The bicycles rolled down a steep hill. |
| Entall bestemt | bakken | Huset ligger øverst i bakken. | The house is at the top of the hill. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bakker | Barna akte ned små bakker i parken. | The children sledded down small hills in the park. |
| Flertall bestemt | bakkene | Bakkene rundt gården er grønne om sommeren. | The hills around the farm are green in summer. |
