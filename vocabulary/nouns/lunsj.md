## lunsj

**English:** lunch

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Vi spiste en lunsj på terrassen. | We ate lunch on the terrace. |
| Entall ubestemt | lunsj | Hun lager lunsj til hele familien. | She is making lunch for the whole family. |
| Entall bestemt | lunsjen | Lunsjen står klar på kjøkkenbordet. | Lunch is ready on the kitchen table. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lunsjer | Kafeen serverer varme lunsjer hver dag. | The café serves hot lunches every day. |
| Flertall bestemt | lunsjene | Lunsjene på kurset er inkludert i prisen. | The lunches at the course are included in the price. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. Compound inflection is labelled derived where applicable. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The imported English sense is the meal eaten between breakfast and dinner. *Lunsj* can also refer to a formal or social lunch event; examples here use the meal sense.
