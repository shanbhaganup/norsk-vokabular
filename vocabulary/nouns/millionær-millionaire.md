**English:** millionaire

**Topics:** money

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword, masculine gender and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The dictionary defines a `millionær` as a person with a fortune of one million kroner or more. The English gloss “millionaire” is in the short scale.
