## stå

**English:** standstill; standing section

**Topics:** fixed expressions; sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | — | Forhandlingene gikk i stå etter midnatt. | The negotiations came to a standstill after midnight. |
| Entall ubestemt | — | Arbeidet gikk i stå da maskinen brøt sammen. | The work ground to a halt when the machine broke down. |
| Entall bestemt | — | Samtalen gikk helt i stå etter spørsmålet. | The conversation came to a complete standstill after the question. |
| Flertall ubestemt | — | Uten nye ideer kan prosjekter gå i stå. | Without new ideas, projects can grind to a halt. |
| Flertall bestemt | — | Kampene gikk i stå i den andre omgangen. | The matches came to a standstill in the second half. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** The noun is invariable and has no grammatical gender; it occurs especially in gå i stå. The reference snapshot's proposed inflection is corrected against Bokmålsordboka.
