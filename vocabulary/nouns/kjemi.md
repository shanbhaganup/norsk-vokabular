## kjemi

**English:** chemistry; chemistry between people

**Topics:** school, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Kjemi er et spennende fag. | Chemistry is an exciting subject. |
| Entall ubestemt | kjemi | Hun studerer kjemi på universitetet. | She studies chemistry at university. |
| Entall bestemt | kjemien | Kjemien mellom dem var tydelig. | The chemistry between them was obvious. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kjemier | Ulike kjemier kan oppstå mellom mennesker. | Different kinds of chemistry can arise between people. |
| Flertall bestemt | kjemiene | Kjemiene i laboratoriet ble undersøkt. | The chemical substances in the laboratory were examined. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and sense reviewed; paradigm verified except where noted.

**Notes:** The reference corrects the source gender to masculine. Bokmålsordboka confirms en kjemi and the forms kjemien, kjemier, kjemiene. The plural is used when referring to distinct kinds or instances; the subject-matter sense is ordinarily singular.
