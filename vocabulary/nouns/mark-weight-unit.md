## mark

**English:** mark (historical unit of weight)

**Topics:** history, measurements

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei mark kaffe veide omtrent et kvart kilo. | A mark of coffee weighed about a quarter of a kilogram. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mark kaffe veide omtrent et kvart kilo. | A mark of coffee weighed about a quarter of a kilogram. |
| Entall ubestemt | mark | Oppskriften fra arkivet oppgir én mark mel. | The recipe from the archive specifies one mark of flour. |
| Entall bestemt | marka | Marka med korn ble veid på nytt. | The mark of grain was weighed again. |
| Entall bestemt | marken | Marken med korn ble veid på nytt. | The mark of grain was weighed again. |
| Flertall ubestemt | merker | Varene veide til sammen fire merker. | The goods weighed four marks in total. |
| Flertall bestemt | merkene | Merkene ble omregnet til kilogram i margen. | The marks were converted to kilograms in the margin. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene entry 2 gender and masculine plural forms checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** This is the historical weight unit of about a quarter kilogram (`mark 2`), not `mark` meaning land, currency or worm. Both feminine and masculine singular are accepted; the cited table gives `merker / merkene` as masculine plural. The unit is historical.
