## veg

**English:** road; way

**Topics:** travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En veg går gjennom dalen. | A road runs through the valley. |
| Entall ubestemt | veg | Vi fulgte en smal veg gjennom dalen. | We followed a narrow road through the valley. |
