## bensin

**English:** petrol, gasoline

**Topics:** travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | De testet en bensin med høyere oktantall. | They tested a higher-octane petrol. |
| Entall ubestemt | bensin | Vi må fylle bensin før vi drar. | We need to fill up with petrol before we leave. |
| Entall bestemt | bensinen | Bensinen ble dyrere i går. | Petrol went up in price yesterday. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bensiner | Rapporten sammenligner flere bensiner med ulikt oktantall. | The report compares several types of petrol with different octane ratings. |
| Flertall bestemt | bensinene | De to bensinene ga omtrent samme forbruk. | The two types of petrol gave roughly the same fuel consumption. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The singular usually refers to the fuel as a substance. Plural forms can refer to different kinds of petrol.
