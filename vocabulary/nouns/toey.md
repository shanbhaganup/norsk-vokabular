## tøy

**English:** fabric; clothing

**Topics:** clothing; shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et tøystykke kan brukes til å tørke bordet. | A piece of fabric can be used to wipe the table. |
| Entall ubestemt | tøy | Butikken selger tøy i mange farger. | The shop sells fabric in many colours. |
| Entall bestemt | tøyet | Tøyet er mykt. | The fabric is soft. |
| Flertall ubestemt | tøyer | Vi undersøkte flere tøyer før vi valgte. | We examined several fabrics before choosing. |
| Flertall bestemt | tøya / tøyene | Tøya ble vasket. / Tøyene ble vasket. | The clothes were washed. / The fabrics were washed. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry; examples authored for this project.

**Notes:** This entry covers the neuter noun for fabric and clothing. The masculine homonym meaning “stretch; elasticity” is in a separate entry. All accepted neuter forms are illustrated.
