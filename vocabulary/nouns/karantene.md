## karantene

**English:** quarantine; temporary exclusion from an activity

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En karantene kan begrense smittespredning. | A quarantine can limit the spread of infection. |
| Entall ubestemt | karantene | Familien måtte i karantene etter reisen. | The family had to quarantine after the trip. |
| Entall bestemt | karantenen | Karantenen varte i flere dager. | The quarantine lasted several days. |
| Flertall ubestemt | karantener | Reglene beskrev ulike karantener for reisende. | The rules described different quarantines for travelers. |
| Flertall bestemt | karantenene | Karantinene ble avsluttet etter prøvesvarene. | The quarantines ended after the test results. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified where available; individual alternatives are listed separately with examples.

**Notes:** The source gives quarantine. The dictionary also records a figurative sense of temporary exclusion from an activity; both senses share this paradigm.
