## pepper

**English:** criticism; rebuke (figurative)

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En pepper kan være nyttig når den er saklig. | A rebuke can be useful when it is fair. |
| Entall ubestemt | pepper | Hun fikk pepper for den sene leveringen. | She was criticized for the late delivery. |
| Entall bestemt | pepperen | Pepperen fra publikum var hard. | The criticism from the audience was harsh. |
| Flertall ubestemt | peppere | Slike peppere kan gjøre debatten bedre. | Such criticisms can improve the debate. |
| Flertall ubestemt | peprer | Han fikk to peprer etter presentasjonen. | He received two rebukes after the presentation. |
| Flertall bestemt | pepperne | Peppere fra leserne kom i flere dager. | Criticisms from readers came in for several days. |
| Flertall bestemt | peprene | Pepperne i avisene ble diskutert på radio. | The criticisms in the newspapers were discussed on the radio. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Dictionary figurative sense “criticism, rebuke.” The count plurals are grammatical but less usual in this sense; each dictionary alternative is illustrated.
