## bord

**English:** table

**Topics:** home, furniture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et bord står ved vinduet. | A table stands by the window. |
| Entall ubestemt | bord | Vi trenger flere bord til gjestene. | We need tables for the guests. |
| Entall bestemt | bordet | Bordet er dekket til middag. | The table is set for dinner. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bord | Rommet har plass til flere bord. | The room has space for several tables. |
| Flertall ubestemt | border | Møterommet har to border. | The meeting room has two tables. |
| Flertall bestemt | borda | Borda ble flyttet ut på terrassen. | The tables were moved out onto the terrace. |
| Flertall bestemt | bordene | Bordene ble flyttet ut på terrassen. | The tables were moved out onto the terrace. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers the furniture sense (dictionary entry II), distinct from bord I meaning a decorative edge or border. Both dictionary-listed indefinite and definite plural forms are illustrated.
