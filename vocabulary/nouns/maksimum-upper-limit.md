## maksimum

**English:** maximum; upper limit

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et maksimum ble fastsatt for forbruket. | A maximum was set for consumption. |
| Entall ubestemt | maksimum | Systemet har et maksimum på hundre brukere. | The system has a maximum of one hundred users. |
| Entall bestemt | maksimumet | Maksimumet ble nådd før lunsj. | The maximum was reached before lunch. |
| Flertall ubestemt | maksima | Forskerne sammenlignet maksima i måleserien. | The researchers compared maxima in the measurement series. |
| Flertall ubestemt | maksimum | Målingene viste tre maksimum i løpet av dagen. | The measurements showed three maxima during the day. |
| Flertall ubestemt | maksimumer | De beregnede maksimumer lå nær hverandre. | The calculated maxima were close to one another. |
| Flertall bestemt | maksimaene | Maksimaene i de tre kurvene var tydelige. | The maxima in the three curves were clear. |
| Flertall bestemt | maksimuma | Maksimuma for hver måleserie ble registrert. | The maxima for each measurement series were recorded. |
| Flertall bestemt | maksimumene | Maksimumene ble kontrollert mot grenseverdiene. | The maxima were checked against the limit values. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword and all listed plural alternatives checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The source gives neuter and plural alternatives `maksima`, `maksimum`, `maksimumer`, with definite plural `maksimaene`, `maksimuma`, `maksimumene`; each is illustrated separately. The imported `maks` is not the headword or a plural form here and has been corrected to `maksimum`. Ordbøkene also records an adverbial use meaning “at most”; that is outside this noun entry.
