## rute

**English:** route

**Topics:** travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei rute / En rute kan føre gjennom fjellet. | A route can lead through the mountains. |
| Entall ubestemt | rute | Vi valgte ei rute langs kysten. | We chose a route along the coast. |
| Entall bestemt | ruta / ruten | Ruta / Ruten var stengt på grunn av ras. | The route was closed because of a landslide. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ruter | Flere ruter går mellom byene. | Several routes run between the cities. |
| Flertall bestemt | rutene | Rutene ble vist på kartet. | The routes were shown on the map. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry is the travel-route sense (dictionary 49210); 49209 is the separate square/window-pane sense. Both feminine and masculine forms are accepted.
