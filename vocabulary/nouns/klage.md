## klage

**English:** complaint

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | En klage på støyen kom fram til kommunen. | A complaint about the noise reached the municipality. |
| Entall ubestemt | klage | Hun sendte en klage. | She sent a complaint. |
| Entall bestemt | klaga / klagen | Klaga ble behandlet raskt. / Klagen ble behandlet raskt. | The complaint was handled quickly. / The complaint was handled quickly. |

| Flertall bestemt | klagene | Klagene ble undersøkt av tilsynet. | The complaints were investigated by the inspectorate. |

**Form status:** Headword and paradigm reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Feminine and masculine inflection are accepted; both definite singular forms are illustrated. This noun “complaint” is distinct from the verb klage “complain”.
