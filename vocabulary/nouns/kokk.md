## kokk

**English:** cook; chef

**Topics:** food, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kokk tilbereder maten på kjøkkenet. | A cook prepares the food in the kitchen. |
| Entall ubestemt | kokk | Hun arbeider som kokk på et hotell. | She works as a cook at a hotel. |
| Entall bestemt | kokken | Kokken serverte suppen med ferskt brød. | The cook served the soup with fresh bread. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kokker | Restauranten trenger flere kokker i sommer. | The restaurant needs more cooks this summer. |
| Flertall bestemt | kokkene | Kokkene planla menyen sammen. | The cooks planned the menu together. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; intended sense confirmed.
