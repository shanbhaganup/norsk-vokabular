## klima

**English:** climate

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et klima med milde vintre passer for oliventrær. | A climate with mild winters suits olive trees. |
| Entall ubestemt | klima | Forskerne undersøkte et klima som er i endring. | The researchers studied a changing climate. |
| Entall bestemt | klimaet | Klimaet langs kysten er fuktig. | The climate along the coast is humid. |
| Flertall ubestemt | klima / klimaer | Klima og klimaer varierer mellom øyene. | Climates vary between the islands. |
| Flertall bestemt | klimaene | Klimaene i regionen ble sammenlignet. | The climates in the region were compared. |

**Form status:** Headword and paradigm reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The word also has a figurative sense for a prevailing atmosphere.
