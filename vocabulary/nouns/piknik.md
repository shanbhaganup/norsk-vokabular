## piknik

**English:** picnic; outdoor meal outing

**Topics:** food; leisure

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En piknik passer godt i parken. | A picnic is well suited to the park. |
| Entall ubestemt | piknik | Familien planla en piknik ved sjøen. | The family planned a picnic by the sea. |
| Entall bestemt | pikniken | Pikniken ble flyttet inn på grunn av regnet. | The picnic was moved indoors because of the rain. |
| Flertall ubestemt | pikniker | De arrangerte to pikniker i sommer. | They organized two picnics this summer. |
| Flertall bestemt | piknikene | Piknikene ved vannet var populære. | The picnics by the water were popular. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Source meaning is retained as an outing with a meal outdoors.
