## hall

**English:** large hall; hall building

**Topics:** buildings, home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En hall ble reist ved skolen. | A hall was built at the school. |
| Entall ubestemt | hall | Kommunen planlegger en ny hall ved idrettsplassen. | The municipality is planning a new hall by the sports ground. |
| Entall bestemt | hallen | Hallen har plass til fem hundre mennesker. | The hall has room for five hundred people. |
| Flertall ubestemt | haller | Byen har flere haller til konserter og messer. | The town has several halls for concerts and fairs. |
| Flertall bestemt | hallene | Hallene åpner før publikum kommer. | The halls open before the audience arrives. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** This entry covers the masculine noun for a large room or a building containing one. The separate neuter sense meaning a slope or tilted position is in hall-slope.md; both link to the same merged reference record.
