## merke

**English:** brand

**Topics:** shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et merke kan si mye om kvaliteten. | A brand can say a lot about quality. |
| Entall ubestemt | merke | Hun valgte et merke hun kjenner godt. | She chose a brand she knows well. |
| Entall bestemt | merket | Merket selges i mange butikker. | The brand is sold in many shops. |
