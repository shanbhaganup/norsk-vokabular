## antibiotikum

**English:** antibiotic

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Legen skrev ut et antibiotikum. | The doctor prescribed an antibiotic. |

| Entall bestemt | antibiotikumet | Antibiotikumet må oppbevares kjølig. | The antibiotic must be kept cool. |
| Flertall ubestemt | antibiotika | To ulike antibiotika ble undersøkt. | Two different antibiotics were studied. |
