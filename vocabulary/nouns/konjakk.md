## konjakk

**English:** cognac

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En konjakk fra Frankrike sto på bordet. | A cognac from France stood on the table. |
| Entall ubestemt | konjakk | Han drikker sjelden konjakk. | He rarely drinks cognac. |
| Entall bestemt | konjakken | Konjakken ble servert etter middagen. | The cognac was served after dinner. |
| Flertall ubestemt | konjakker | Butikken selger flere konjakker fra ulike områder. | The shop sells several cognacs from different regions. |
| Flertall bestemt | konjakkene | Konjakkene på smakingen kom fra forskjellige produsenter. | The cognacs at the tasting came from different producers. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The uncountable drink sense is commonly used without an article or plural. The dictionary also records a count sense meaning a serving, bottle or glass of cognac. The plural examples refer to varieties from different regions or producers.
