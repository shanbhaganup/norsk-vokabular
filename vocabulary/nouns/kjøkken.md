## kjøkken

**English:** kitchen; kitchen staff

**Topics:** home, food, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et kjøkken bør ha godt arbeidslys. | A kitchen should have good task lighting. |
| Entall ubestemt | kjøkken | Leiligheten har et stort kjøkken. | The apartment has a large kitchen. |
| Entall bestemt | kjøkkenet | Kjøkkenet trenger nye skap. | The kitchen needs new cupboards. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kjøkken | Hotellet har tre kjøkken. | The hotel has three kitchens. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kjøkkener | Bygget har flere separate kjøkkener. | The building has several separate kitchens. |
| Flertall bestemt | kjøkkena | Kjøkkena på skolen ble pusset opp. | The kitchens at the school were renovated. |
| Flertall bestemt | kjøkkenene | Kjøkkenene på skolen ble pusset opp. | The kitchens at the school were renovated. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms and senses reviewed.

**Notes:** The plural alternatives and both definite plural forms follow Bokmålsordboka. Besides the room, *kjøkken* can denote the staff working in a kitchen; that use commonly appears in definite singular (for example, *hilse kjøkkenet*).
