## innspilling

**English:** recording; taping

**Topics:** media, music

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei innspilling tar tid. / En innspilling tar tid. | A recording takes time. / A recording takes time. |
| Entall ubestemt | innspilling | Vi gjorde ei innspilling i studio. | We made a recording in the studio. |
| Entall bestemt | innspillinga | Innspillinga ble ferdig på fredag. | The recording was finished on Friday. |
| Entall bestemt | innspillingen | Innspillingen høres klar ut. | The recording sounds clear. |
| Flertall ubestemt | innspillinger | Bandet har laget flere innspillinger. | The band has made several recordings. |
| Flertall bestemt | innspillingene | Innspillingene er lagret på en ekstern disk. | The recordings are saved on an external drive. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** Feminine and masculine inflection are both accepted. This noun refers to the act or product of recording.
