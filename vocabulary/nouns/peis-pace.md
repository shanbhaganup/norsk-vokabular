## peis

**English:** pace; assistance maintaining or increasing speed in a race

**Topics:** sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En peis kan hjelpe løperen med å holde farten. | A pacer can help the runner maintain speed. |
| Entall ubestemt | peis | Løperen fikk peis av en erfaren konkurrent. | The runner received pacing help from an experienced competitor. |
| Entall bestemt | peisen | Peisen satte tempoet opp mot mål. | The pacer raised the speed toward the finish. |
| Flertall ubestemt | peiser | Flere peiser hjalp feltet gjennom løpet. | Several pacers helped the field through the race. |
| Flertall bestemt | peisene | Peisene fordelte arbeidet mellom seg. | The pacers divided the work among themselves. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary peis 3 III is pronounced like English pace and means race pace or pacing assistance. The common fireplace sense is separate.
