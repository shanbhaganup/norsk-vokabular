## ild

**English:** fire

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En ild brant i peisen. | A fire burned in the fireplace. |
| Entall ubestemt | ild | De tente en ild ved hytta. | They lit a fire by the cabin. |
| Entall bestemt | ilden | Ilden varmet hele rommet. | The fire warmed the whole room. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ilder | Flere ilder brant langs skogkanten. | Several fires burned along the forest edge. |
| Flertall bestemt | ildene | Ildene ble slukket før natten. | The fires were put out before nightfall. |
