## skade

**English:** injury, damage

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En skade kan trenge lang tid på å gro. | An injury can take a long time to heal. |
| Entall ubestemt | skade | Han fikk en skade under kampen. | He got injured during the game. |
| Entall bestemt | skaden | Skaden på kneet ble undersøkt av legen. | The knee injury was examined by the doctor. |
| Flertall ubestemt | skader | Hun hadde flere mindre skader etter fallet. | She had several minor injuries after the fall. |
| Flertall bestemt | skadene | Skadene var heldigvis ikke alvorlige. | Fortunately, the injuries were not serious. |

**Form status:** Forms checked against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The noun covers bodily injury and damage to objects or places. These examples use bodily injury.
