## kontor

**English:** office; office room or department

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et kontor ligger i andre etasje. | An office is on the second floor. |
| Entall ubestemt | kontor | Hun arbeider på et kontor i sentrum. | She works in an office downtown. |
| Entall bestemt | kontoret | Kontoret åpner klokka åtte. | The office opens at eight. |
| Flertall ubestemt, uendret form | kontor | Firmaet har kontor i Oslo og Bergen. | The company has offices in Oslo and Bergen. |
| Flertall ubestemt, -er-form | kontorer | De nye kontorene har store vinduer. | The new offices have large windows. |
| Flertall bestemt, -a-form | kontora | Kontora i bygget deler møterom. | The offices in the building share meeting rooms. |
| Flertall bestemt, -ene-form | kontorene | Kontorene stenger tidligere på fredager. | The offices close earlier on Fridays. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and all listed forms checked; examples and English gloss authored for this project.

**Notes:** Singular kontor also occurs in expressions such as på kontor; the table illustrates the count noun and preserves both plural alternatives. The dictionary includes an office as a workplace, a person's office room and an institutional department. The reference snapshot has a broad “office” gloss and an inferred work topic.
