## løper

**English:** runner, running animal

**Topics:** sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En løper satte ny personlig rekord. | A runner set a new personal record. |
| Entall ubestemt | løper | Hunden er en rask løper. | The dog is a fast runner. |
| Entall bestemt | løperen | Løperen holdt farten helt til mål. | The runner kept up the pace all the way to the finish. |
| Flertall ubestemt | løpere | Mange løpere trente langs elva. | Many runners trained along the river. |
| Flertall bestemt | løperne | Løperne stilte opp ved startstreken. | The runners lined up at the starting line. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and inflection checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers a person or animal that runs. Bokmålsordboka also uses *løper* for a football attacker, a chess bishop and a narrow decorative cloth or mat; those senses have separate entries. The form pattern is the same across these senses.
