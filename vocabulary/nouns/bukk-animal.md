## bukk

**English:** buck; male goat or deer

**Topics:** animals, nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bukk sto på åskammen. | A buck stood on the ridge. |
| Entall ubestemt | bukk | Jeg så bukk i skogen. | I saw a buck in the forest. |
| Entall bestemt | bukken | Bukken beitet ved gjerdet. | The buck grazed by the fence. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bukker | Bukker går sammen med flokken om vinteren. | Bucks stay with the herd in winter. |
| Flertall bestemt | bukkene | Bukkene beitet på den andre siden av dalen. | The bucks grazed on the other side of the valley. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers the male animal sense. The dictionary also has separate senses for a trestle, a chess blunder and a bow.
