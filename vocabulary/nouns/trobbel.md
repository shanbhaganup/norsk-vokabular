## trøbbel

**English:** trouble; difficulty

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Article / gender | et (neuter) | Det oppsto et trøbbel underveis. | A problem arose along the way. |
| Entall ubestemt | trøbbel | Vi fikk trøbbel med bilen. | We had trouble with the car. |
| Entall bestemt | trøbbelet | Trøbbelet startet da motoren stanset. | The trouble began when the engine stopped. |
| Flertall ubestemt (1) | trøbbel | Flere trøbbel oppsto samtidig på reisen. | Several problems arose at the same time during the trip. |
| Flertall ubestemt (2) | trøbler | De tekniske trøblene forsinket arbeidet. | The technical troubles delayed the work. |
| Flertall bestemt (1) | trøbla | Trøbla vi møtte underveis, løste seg til slutt. | The troubles we encountered along the way were eventually resolved. |
| Flertall bestemt (2) | trøblene | Trøblene med leveransen er nå løst. | The troubles with the delivery have now been resolved. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified against Bokmålsordboka; rare count plural *trøbbel* illustrated in a countable context.
