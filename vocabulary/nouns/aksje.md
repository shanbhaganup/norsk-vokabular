## aksje

**English:** share (of stock)

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Hun kjøpte en aksje i selskapet. | She bought one share in the company. |
| Entall ubestemt | aksje | Han eier bare én aksje. | He owns only one share. |
| Entall bestemt | aksjen | Aksjen steg i verdi i løpet av uka. | The share rose in value over the course of the week. |
| Flertall ubestemt | aksjer | De kjøpte aksjer i flere selskaper. | They bought shares in several companies. |
| Flertall bestemt | aksjene | Hun solgte aksjene før sommeren. | She sold the shares before the summer. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The imported gloss *shares* was plural. The singular headword means one ownership share. The source gender was corrected to masculine.
