## hage

**English:** garden

**Topics:** home, plants

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En hage gir plass til både blomster og grønnsaker. | A garden provides room for both flowers and vegetables. |
| Entall ubestemt | hage | Vi anla hage bak huset. | We made a garden behind the house. |
| Entall bestemt | hagen | Hagen trenger vann etter flere varme dager. | The garden needs water after several hot days. |
| Flertall ubestemt | hager | Mange hager er åpne for publikum om sommeren. | Many gardens are open to the public in summer. |
| Flertall bestemt | hagene | Hagene i området blomstrer tidlig. | The gardens in the area bloom early. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** The noun means a garden or enclosed grazing ground. Bokmålsordboka also lists a separate verb hage (“do gardening”); this entry is the noun only.
