## politi

**English:** the police; police force

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et politi må følge loven. | A police force must follow the law. |
| Entall ubestemt | politi | Landet opprettet et politi etter reformen. | The country established a police force after the reform. |
| Entall bestemt | politiet | Politiet kom kort tid etter meldingen. | The police arrived shortly after the report. |
| Flertall ubestemt | politi | Flere politi samarbeidet i øvelsen. | Several police forces cooperated in the exercise. |
| Flertall ubestemt | politier | Ulike politier deltok i samarbeidet. | Different police forces took part in the cooperation. |
| Flertall bestemt | politia | Politia i regionen fikk nytt utstyr. | The police forces in the region received new equipment. |
| Flertall bestemt | politiene | Politiene i de to landene utvekslet erfaringer. | The police forces in the two countries exchanged experience. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Neuter dictionary homonym 2 refers to the public police authority or a member/department representing it. All plural alternatives are illustrated. The reference also merges inflected source form politiet into lemma politi.
