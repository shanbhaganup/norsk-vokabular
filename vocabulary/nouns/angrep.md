## angrep

**English:** attack (offensive play)

**Topics:** sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et raskt angrep ga laget en tidlig ledelse. | A quick attack gave the team an early lead. |
| Entall ubestemt | angrep | Laget gikk i angrep rett etter pausen. | The team went on the attack right after halftime. |
| Entall bestemt | angrepet | Angrepet endte med et skudd i stolpen. | The attack ended with a shot that hit the post. |
| Flertall ubestemt | angrep | Hjemmelaget satte i gang flere angrep langs høyresida. | The home team launched several attacks down the right side. |
| Flertall bestemt | angrepa | Angrepa ble farligere etter spillerbyttet. | The attacks became more threatening after the substitution. |
| Flertall bestemt | angrepene | De fleste angrepene startet med en lang pasning. | Most of the attacks began with a long pass. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The source sport tag points to the team-sport sense. Angrep is also the preterite of angripe, and angrepet is its past participle. See the [working verb entry](../verbs/angripe.md).
