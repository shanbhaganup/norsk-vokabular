## fugl

**English:** bird; bird meat; creature (figurative)

**Topics:** animals

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En fugl satt på gjerdet. | A bird sat on the fence. |
| Entall ubestemt | fugl | Vi så en fugl ved vannet. | We saw a bird by the water. |
| Entall bestemt | fuglen | Fuglen bygde rede i treet. | The bird built a nest in the tree. |
| Flertall ubestemt | fugler | Fugler trekker sørover om høsten. | Birds migrate south in autumn. |
| Flertall bestemt | fuglene | Fuglene samlet seg på taket. | The birds gathered on the roof. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and paradigm checked against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The dictionary also lists bird meat and figurative 'creature/person' senses. Examples use the animal sense.
