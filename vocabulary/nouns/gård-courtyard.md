## gård

**English:** courtyard; open space by or between buildings

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En grøt ble servert til lunsj. | A porridge was served for lunch. |
| Entall ubestemt | gård | Jeg fant en gård her. | I found a courtyard here. |
| Entall bestemt | gården | Gården lå på bordet. | The courtyard was on the table. |
| Flertall ubestemt | gårder | Vi så flere gårder i løpet av dagen. | We saw several courtyards during the day. |
| Flertall bestemt | gårdene | Gårdene var lette å kjenne igjen. | The courtyards were easy to recognize. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original bilingual teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The dictionary also records an open-space sense. Same paradigm as the farm sense; *gårdsplass* is the usual compound.
