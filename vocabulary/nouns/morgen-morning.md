## morgen

**English:** morning

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En morgen våknet vi før soloppgang. | One morning we woke before sunrise. |
| Entall ubestemt | morgen | Jeg går ofte en tur om morgenen. | I often go for a walk in the morning. |
| Entall bestemt | morgenen | Morgenen var kald og klar. | The morning was cold and clear. |
| Flertall ubestemt | morgener | Lyse morgener gjør det lettere å stå opp. | Bright mornings make it easier to get up. |
| Flertall ubestemt | morgner | Kalde morgner er vanlige her i april. | Cold mornings are common here in April. |
| Flertall bestemt | morgenene | Morgenene ved sjøen var stille. | The mornings by the sea were quiet. |
| Flertall bestemt | morgnene | Morgnene ble lysere utover våren. | The mornings grew brighter as spring progressed. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene forms verified; original examples written for each listed form.

**Notes:** The dictionary also uses morgen in the greeting god morgen and figuratively for the early beginning of a period. This entry focuses on the time of day. morgner/morgnene are accepted alternatives.
