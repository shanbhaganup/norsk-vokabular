## snor

**English:** string; cord

**Topics:** everyday life

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei snor henger fra gardinen. | A cord hangs from the curtain. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En snor henger fra gardinen. | A cord hangs from the curtain. |
| Entall ubestemt | snor | Han knyttet en snor rundt pakken. | He tied a string around the parcel. |
| Entall bestemt | snora | Snora på persiennen røyk. | The cord on the blind snapped. |
| Entall bestemt | snoren | Snoren rundt eska var rød. | The string around the box was red. |
| Flertall ubestemt | snorer | Vi trenger snorer til gavene. | We need strings for the presents. |
| Flertall bestemt | snorene | Snorene ligger i den øverste skuffen. | The cords are in the top drawer. |
