## advokat

**English:** lawyer (attorney)

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Jeg ba en advokat lese kontrakten. | I asked a lawyer to read the contract. |
| Entall ubestemt | advokat | De tok med seg advokat til møtet. | They brought a lawyer to the meeting. |
| Entall bestemt | advokaten | Advokaten forklarte hva brevet betydde. | The lawyer explained what the letter meant. |
| Flertall ubestemt | advokater | To advokater delte kontor i sentrum. | Two lawyers shared an office downtown. |
| Flertall bestemt | advokatene | Advokatene ba om mer tid til å lese dokumentene. | The lawyers asked for more time to read the documents. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The gloss refers to a legal professional authorized to provide legal assistance.
