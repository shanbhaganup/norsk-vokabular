## peis

**English:** vulgar slang: penis; beating (in fixed phrase)

**Topics:** vulgar slang

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary peis 2 II includes a vulgar anatomical sense and, in the fixed phrase få peis, a beating. This register is highly vulgar; the neutral fireplace sense is separate.
