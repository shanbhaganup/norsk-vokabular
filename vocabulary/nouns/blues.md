## blues

**English:** blues (music)

**Topics:** culture, music

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En blues kan ha en enkel form og et uttrykksfullt refreng. | A blues song can have a simple form and an expressive chorus. |
| Entall ubestemt | blues | Bandet spiller blues hver fredag. | The band plays blues every Friday. |
| Entall bestemt | bluesen | Bluesen utviklet seg i USA. | The blues developed in the United States. |
| Flertall ubestemt | blues | Hun skrev blues til forestillingen. | She wrote blues songs for the show. |
| Flertall bestemt | bluesene | Bluesene på albumet er spilt inn live. | The blues songs on the album were recorded live. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The name of the musical genre is often used as a mass noun; the plural can refer to distinct blues pieces or compositions.
