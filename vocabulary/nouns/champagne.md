## champagne

**English:** champagne

**Topics:** food, drinks

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En champagne fra regionen ble servert til maten. | A champagne from the region was served with the meal. |
| Entall ubestemt | champagne | De serverte champagne etter middagen. | They served champagne after dinner. |
| Entall bestemt | champagnen | Champagnen ble avkjølt før servering. | The champagne was chilled before serving. |
| Flertall ubestemt | champagner | Butikken fører champagner fra flere produsenter. | The shop stocks champagnes from several producers. |
| Flertall bestemt | champagnene | Champagnene ble sammenlignet under smakingen. | The champagnes were compared during the tasting. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Champagne is usually a mass noun; plural forms refer to different champagnes or bottles/varieties.
