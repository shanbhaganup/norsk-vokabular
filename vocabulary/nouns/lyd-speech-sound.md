## lyd

**English:** speech sound, phoneme

**Topics:** language

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En lyd kan ha ulik uttale i forskjellige dialekter. | A speech sound can be pronounced differently across dialects. |
| Entall ubestemt | lyd | Denne lyd-en finnes i mange språk. | This speech sound occurs in many languages. |
| Entall bestemt | lyden | Lyden står først i ordet. | The sound occurs at the beginning of the word. |
