## mose

**English:** moss (bryophyte)

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mose vokser på den fuktige steinen. | A moss grows on the damp stone. |
| Entall ubestemt | mose | Mose dekker bakken i skogen. | Moss covers the ground in the forest. |
| Entall bestemt | mosen | Mosen holder på fuktigheten. | The moss retains moisture. |
| Flertall ubestemt | moser | Ulike moser vokser på trestammer. | Different mosses grow on tree trunks. |
| Flertall bestemt | mosene | Mosene trives på den fuktige skogbunnen. | The mosses thrive on the damp forest floor. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene forms verified; original examples written for each listed form.

**Notes:** The dictionary also has senses for a moss bog and for lichen. This entry covers the ordinary plant sense; those are distinct meanings if imported as separate records.
