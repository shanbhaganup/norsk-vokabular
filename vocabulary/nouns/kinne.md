## kinne

**English:** cheek

**Topics:** body, health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kinne ble rød av kulden. | One cheek turned red from the cold. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei kinne ble rød av kulden. | One cheek turned red from the cold. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en (feminine paradigm) | En kinne ble rød av kulden. | One cheek turned red from the cold. |
| Entall ubestemt | kinne | Et slag traff henne på en kinne. | A blow struck her on one cheek. |
| Entall bestemt | kinnen | Kinnen var rød etter skituren. | The cheek was red after the ski trip. |
| Entall bestemt | kinna | Kinna var rød etter skituren. | The cheek was red after the ski trip. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kinner | Barna hadde røde kinner etter leken. | The children had red cheeks after playing. |
| Flertall bestemt | kinnene | Kinnene var kalde i vintervinden. | The cheeks were cold in the winter wind. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** The source definition is the anatomical cheek. Bokmålsordboka gives masculine *en kinne* with *kinnen*, and feminine *ei/en kinne* with *kinna*; plural forms are shared.
