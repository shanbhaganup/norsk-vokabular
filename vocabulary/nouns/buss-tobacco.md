## buss

**English:** piece of chewing tobacco

**Topics:** food, tobacco

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En buss med skråtobakk ble lagt på bordet. | A piece of chewing tobacco was placed on the table. |
| Entall ubestemt | buss | Han tok en buss skråtobakk fra boksen. | He took a piece of chewing tobacco from the tin. |
| Entall bestemt | bussen | Bussen var skåret fra en større bit. | The piece had been cut from a larger piece. |
| Flertall ubestemt | busser | Små busser av skråtobakk lå i eska. | Small pieces of chewing tobacco lay in the box. |
| Flertall bestemt | bussene | Bussene ble pakket hver for seg. | The pieces were wrapped separately. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers a small piece cut from chewing tobacco (dictionary entry II), separate from buss meaning a passenger vehicle.
