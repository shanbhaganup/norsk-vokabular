## sokkel

**English:** plinth

**Topics:** buildings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Vi restaurerte en sokkel. | We restored a plinth. |
| Entall ubestemt | sokkel | Vi restaurerte en sokkel. | We restored a plinth. |
| Entall bestemt | sokkelen | Vi restaurerte sokkelen. | We restored the plinth. |
