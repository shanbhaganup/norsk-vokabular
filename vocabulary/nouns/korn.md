## korn

**English:** grain; cereal crop; kernel

**Topics:** food, agriculture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et korn modner på åkeren. | A grain crop ripens in the field. |
| Entall ubestemt | korn | Bonden dyrker korn på åkeren. | The farmer grows grain in the field. |
| Entall bestemt | kornet | Kornet ble høstet i september. | The grain was harvested in September. |
| Flertall ubestemt | korn | Vi fant små korn i melet. | We found small grains in the flour. |
| Flertall bestemt, -a-form | korna | Korna fra planten ble malt til mel. | The grains from the plant were milled into flour. |
| Flertall bestemt, -ene-form | kornene | Kornene satt fast i silen. | The grains were stuck in the sieve. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and all listed forms checked; examples and English gloss authored for this project.

**Notes:** The lemma is commonly a mass noun for cereal crops or grain collectively, but also denotes individual kernels and other grain-like particles, allowing count readings. The plural is therefore illustrated with individual grains rather than treated as the default mass-noun use. Both definite plural alternatives are retained.
