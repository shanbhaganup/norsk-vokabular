## kjennelse

**English:** court ruling; judicial decision

**Topics:** society, law

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kjennelse ble kunngjort i dag. | A ruling was announced today. |
| Entall ubestemt | kjennelse | Retten avsa en kjennelse om besøksforbud. | The court issued a ruling on a restraining order. |
| Entall bestemt | kjennelsen | Kjennelsen ble sendt til partene. | The ruling was sent to the parties. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kjennelser | Domstolen offentliggjorde flere kjennelser. | The court published several rulings. |
| Flertall bestemt | kjennelsene | Kjennelsene ble gjennomgått av advokaten. | The rulings were reviewed by the lawyer. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and sense reviewed; paradigm verified except where noted.

**Notes:** The reference corrects the source gender to masculine. Bokmålsordboka confirms en and the paradigm kjennelse–kjennelsen–kjennelser–kjennelsene. This entry uses the legal sense “ruling that is not a judgment”.
