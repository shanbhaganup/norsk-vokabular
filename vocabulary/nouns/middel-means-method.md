## middel

**English:** means; method; resource

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et middel kan brukes til å rense vannet. | A means can be used to purify the water. |
| Entall ubestemt | middel | Legen anbefalte et middel mot allergien. | The doctor recommended a remedy for the allergy. |
| Entall bestemt | middelet | Middelet virket etter kort tid. | The remedy worked after a short time. |
| Flertall ubestemt | middel | Flere middel ble prøvd før behandlingen virket. | Several remedies were tried before the treatment worked. |
| Flertall ubestemt | midler | Kommunen satte av midler til hjelpearbeidet. | The municipality allocated funds for the relief work. |
| Flertall bestemt | midla | Midla ble fordelt mellom skolene. | The funds were distributed among the schools. |
| Flertall bestemt | midlene | Midlene ble fordelt mellom skolene. | The funds were distributed among the schools. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene entry 1 neuter paradigm and plural alternatives checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** This is `middel 1`, something used for a purpose (such as a method, tool or remedy); in the plural it can mean financial resources. The reference combined forms from this neuter noun and two separate masculine homonyms; those are documented separately.

**Notes:** The reference spelling midler was normalized to the headword middel for its “means/resources” sense. Its supplied dictionary citation points to a separate homonym meaning an intermediary; the intended sense and forms are verified at Bokmålsordboka 38224.
