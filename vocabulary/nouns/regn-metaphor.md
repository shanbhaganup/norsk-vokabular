# regn

**English:** shower; rain of something

**Topics:** figurative language; general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et regn av konfetti fylte lufta. | A shower of confetti filled the air. |
| Entall ubestemt | regn | Et regn av gnister kom fra sveiseapparatet. | A shower of sparks came from the welding machine. |
| Entall bestemt | regnet | Regnet av meldinger fortsatte utover kvelden. | The shower of messages continued into the evening. |
| Flertall ubestemt | regn | Flere regn av løv drev over plenen. | Several showers of leaves drifted across the lawn. |
| Flertall bestemt | regna | Regna av konfetti dekket gulvet. | The showers of confetti covered the floor. |
| Flertall bestemt | regnene | Regnene av gnister varte bare et øyeblikk. | The showers of sparks lasted only a moment. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Dictionary sense 2 is figurative: something falling or streaming down in large quantities. The neuter plural definite alternatives are both illustrated.
