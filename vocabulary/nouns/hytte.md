## hytte

**English:** cabin; cottage

**Topics:** home, travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei hytte ligger ved innsjøen. | A cabin is by the lake. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En hytte ligger ved innsjøen. | A cabin is by the lake. |
| Entall ubestemt | hytte | Familien leier hytte hver sommer. | The family rents a cabin every summer. |
| Entall bestemt | hytta | Hytta har utsikt over fjorden. | The cabin has a view of the fjord. |
| Entall bestemt | hytten | Hytten har utsikt over fjorden. | The cabin has a view of the fjord. |
| Flertall ubestemt | hytter | Mange hytter ligger langs denne veien. | Many cabins are located along this road. |
| Flertall bestemt | hyttene | Hyttene åpner vanligvis om våren. | The cabins usually open in spring. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** The source sense is the holiday or leisure cabin. The dictionary also uses *hytte* for a simple shelter, a smelting hut, and a cabin/compartment in a vehicle or boat. Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted in Bokmål. The source's dictionary link resolves correctly.
