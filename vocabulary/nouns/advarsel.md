## advarsel

**English:** warning

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Vaktmesteren satte opp en advarsel ved trappa. | The caretaker put up a warning by the stairs. |
| Entall ubestemt | advarsel | Skolen sendte ut advarsel om glatte veier. | The school sent out a warning about icy roads. |
| Entall bestemt | advarselen | Advarselen kom mens vi var på vei ut døra. | The warning came as we were heading out the door. |
| Flertall ubestemt | advarsler | Vi fikk flere advarsler om sterk vind. | We received several warnings about strong winds. |
| Flertall bestemt | advarslene | De utsatte båtturen etter å ha lest advarslene. | They postponed the boat trip after reading the warnings. |
