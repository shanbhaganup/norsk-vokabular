## syn

**English:** sight; vision

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et syn er nevnt i rapporten. | A sight is mentioned in the report. |
| Entall ubestemt | syn | Hun har godt syn. | She has good eyesight. |
| Entall bestemt | synet | Synet mitt ble undersøkt hos optikeren. | My eyesight was examined at the optician's. |
| Flertall ubestemt | syn | Legen spurte om hun hadde hatt synsforstyrrelser eller tap av syn. | The doctor asked whether she had experienced visual disturbances or loss of sight. |
