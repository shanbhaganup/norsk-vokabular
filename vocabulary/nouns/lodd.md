## lodd

**English:** weight (a physical object used for weighing)

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et lodd | Vekta ble kalibrert med et lodd på ett kilo. | The scale was calibrated with a one-kilogram weight. |
| Entall ubestemt | lodd | Hun hentet et mindre lodd fra esken. | She took a smaller weight from the box. |
| Entall bestemt | loddet | Loddet var for tungt for den lille vekta. | The weight was too heavy for the small scale. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lodd | Han la tre lodd på den ene vektskåla. | He put three weights on one pan of the balance. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lodder | Vi fant flere lodder av messing i skapet. | We found several brass weights in the cupboard. |
| Flertall bestemt | lodda | Lodda lå i en trekasse ved siden av vekta. | The weights were in a wooden box beside the scale. |
| Flertall bestemt | loddene | Loddene var merket med gram. | The weights were marked in grams. |

**Form status:** Gender, inflection and sense checked against Bokmålsordboka. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The snapshot combines three homographs. This entry follows *lodd* II for a physical weight. The general concept of weight is usually *vekt*. The share or fate sense and the lottery-ticket sense belong to separate dictionary articles.
