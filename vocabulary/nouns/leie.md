## leie

**English:** rent; rental payment

**Topics:** housing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei leie på dette nivået er vanskelig å betale. / En leie på dette nivået er vanskelig å betale. | Rent at this level is difficult to afford. / Rent at this level is difficult to afford. |
| Entall ubestemt | leie | Hun betalte ei leie for hybelen. / Hun betalte en leie for hybelen. | She paid rent for the room. / She paid rent for the room. |
| Entall bestemt | leia / leien | Leia ble økt ved nyttår. / Leien ble økt ved nyttår. | The rent was increased at New Year. / The rent was increased at New Year. |
| Flertall ubestemt | leier | Flere leier ble sammenlignet i rapporten. | Several rental payments were compared in the report. |
| Flertall bestemt | leiene | Leiene i sentrum har steget. | Rents in the centre have risen. |

**Form status:** Headword and applicable forms reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source mixes three homonyms. This entry follows leie 1, the feminine/masculine noun meaning renting or rental payment; neuter leie 3 means a location or position. The noun is also distinct from the verb leie “rent/lease”.
