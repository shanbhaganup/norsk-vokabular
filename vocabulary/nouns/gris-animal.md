## gris

**English:** pig

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En gris står i fjøset. | A pig is in the barn. |
| Entall ubestemt | gris | En gris sover i halmen. | A pig is sleeping in the straw. |
| Entall bestemt | grisen | Grisen spiser epler. | The pig is eating apples. |
| Flertall ubestemt | griser | Bonden har flere griser. | The farmer has several pigs. |
| Flertall bestemt | grisene | Grisene går ute om sommeren. | The pigs are outdoors in summer. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The reference merges the animal sense with a separate masculine homonym meaning grease (dictionary entry 20984). This entry covers the animal; the masculine paradigm applies.
