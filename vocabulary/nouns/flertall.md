## flertall

**English:** majority

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et flertall av naboene støttet forslaget. | A majority of the neighbors supported the proposal. |
| Entall ubestemt | flertall | Et flertall stemte for forslaget. | A majority voted for the proposal. |
| Entall bestemt | flertallet | Flertallet ønsket en ny avstemning. | The majority wanted a new vote. |
| Flertall ubestemt | flertall | De to flertallene var nesten like store. | The two majorities were almost equal in size. |
| Flertall bestemt | flertalla / flertallene | Flertalla / Flertallene i begge gruppene støttet planen. | The majorities in both groups supported the plan. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and listed inflection forms checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The singular collective sense “majority” is the source meaning; plural examples refer to majorities in separate groups.
