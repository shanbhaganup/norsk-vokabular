## kvalitet

**English:** quality

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kvalitet ved materialet er at det tåler frost. | One quality of the material is its resistance to frost. |
| Entall ubestemt | kvalitet | Denne varen har en kvalitet vi kan stole på. | This product has a quality we can trust. |
| Entall bestemt | kvaliteten | Kvaliteten ble kontrollert før levering. | The quality was checked before delivery. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kvaliteter | Flere kvaliteter gjør løsningen nyttig. | Several qualities make the solution useful. |
| Flertall bestemt | kvalitetene | Kvalitetene ved de to produktene ble sammenlignet. | The qualities of the two products were compared. |

**Form status:** Headword and applicable forms reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The noun can mean a characteristic or standard of excellence.
