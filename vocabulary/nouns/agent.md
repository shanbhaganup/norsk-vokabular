## agent

**English:** agent (sales representative)

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En agent viste oss de nye kjøkkenmaskinene. | A sales agent showed us the new kitchen appliances. |
| Entall ubestemt | agent | Fabrikken selger møblene gjennom agent i Sverige. | The factory sells its furniture through a sales agent in Sweden. |
| Entall bestemt | agenten | Agenten sendte en oppdatert prisliste til butikkene. | The sales agent sent an updated price list to the stores. |
| Flertall ubestemt | agenter | Selskapet har agenter i tre land. | The company has sales agents in three countries. |
| Flertall bestemt | agentene | Agentene fikk prøve produktene før lanseringen. | The sales agents got to try the products before the launch. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The source gloss *agent* is broad. These examples use the sales-representative sense. Bokmålsordboka also records the intelligence-agent sense.
