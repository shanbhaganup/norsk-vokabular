## alkohol

**English:** alcohol

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Etanol er en alkohol. | Ethanol is an alcohol. |
| Entall ubestemt | alkohol | Denne desserten inneholder alkohol. | This dessert contains alcohol. |
| Entall bestemt | alkoholen | Alkoholen i sausen kommer fra vinen. | The alcohol in the sauce comes from the wine. |
| Flertall ubestemt | alkoholer | Etanol og metanol er alkoholer. | Ethanol and methanol are alcohols. |
| Flertall bestemt | alkoholene | Elevene undersøkte kokepunktene til alkoholene i forsøket. | The students investigated the boiling points of the alcohols in the experiment. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** In the drinks sense, alkohol is normally a mass noun. The countable examples use the chemistry sense, in which different compounds can be counted.
