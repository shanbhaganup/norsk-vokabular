## kopp

**English:** cup; cupful

**Topics:** home, food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kopp står på bordet. | A cup is on the table. |
| Entall ubestemt | kopp | Jeg trenger en kopp til teen. | I need a cup for the tea. |
| Entall bestemt | koppen | Koppen har et blått mønster. | The cup has a blue pattern. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kopper | Vi kjøpte fire kopper på markedet. | We bought four cups at the market. |
| Flertall bestemt | koppene | Koppene står i skapet. | The cups are in the cupboard. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; examples and English gloss authored for this project.

**Notes:** This entry is the drinking-vessel sense (Bokmålsordboka kopp 1 I), including a measured amount such as a cupful. The distinct homonym kopp 2 II (“head of a nail,” among other uses) is not included in this sense entry.
