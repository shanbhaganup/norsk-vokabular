## feil

**English:** mistake; error

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Det var en feil i regnestykket. | There was a mistake in the calculation. |
| Entall ubestemt | feil | Jeg gjorde en feil da jeg fylte ut skjemaet. | I made a mistake when I filled in the form. |
| Entall bestemt | feilen | Feilen ble rettet med en gang. | The mistake was corrected immediately. |
| Flertall ubestemt | feil | Det finnes flere feil i den gamle rapporten. | There are several mistakes in the old report. |
| Flertall bestemt | feila / feilene | Feila / Feilene ble gjennomgått på møtet. | The mistakes were reviewed at the meeting. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The plural indefinite is identical in form to the singular. Both definite plural forms are accepted and illustrated separately. The reference lists feminine gender; the cited dictionary paradigm gives masculine gender, used here.
