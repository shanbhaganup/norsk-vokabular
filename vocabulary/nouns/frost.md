## frost

**English:** frost; freezing conditions

**Topics:** weather

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En frostnatt kan skade plantene. | A night of frost can damage the plants. |
| Entall ubestemt | frost | Det kom frost i natt. | There was frost last night. |
| Entall bestemt | frosten | Frosten la seg på bakken. | The frost settled on the ground. |
| Flertall ubestemt | froster | Tidlige froster kan skade frukttrærne. | Early frosts can damage the fruit trees. |
| Flertall bestemt | frostene | Frostene kom tidligere enn vanlig. | The frosts came earlier than usual. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and paradigm checked against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists several related senses, including subzero temperature, frozen ground, cold injury, a chill, and figurative coldness. Examples here use the weather sense.
