## bygg

**English:** building; structure

**Topics:** home, construction

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et bygg skal åpne neste år. | A building is due to open next year. |
| Entall ubestemt | bygg | Kommunen planlegger et nytt bygg ved skolen. | The municipality is planning a new building by the school. |
| Entall bestemt | bygget | Bygget rommer flere kontorer. | The building contains several offices. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bygg | Flere bygg står under oppføring. | Several buildings are under construction. |
| Flertall bestemt | bygga | Bygga skal få nye vinduer. | The buildings are to get new windows. |
| Flertall bestemt | byggene | Byggene skal få nye vinduer. | The buildings are to get new windows. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers a building or construction (dictionary entry II), distinct from barley (bygg I). The dictionary gives a neuter paradigm.
