## lokk

**English:** cap

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et lokk beskytter maten mot støv. | A lid protects the food from dust. |
| Entall ubestemt | lokk | Hun fant et lokk som passet til gryta. | She found a lid that fitted the pot. |
| Entall bestemt | lokket | Lokket satt fast på glasset. | The lid was stuck on the jar. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lokk | Lokk ble lagt på alle beholderne. | Lids were put on all the containers. |
| Flertall bestemt | lokka / lokkene | Lokka og lokkene ble vasket for hånd. | The lids were washed by hand. / The lids were washed by hand. |

**Form status:** Headword and applicable forms reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source combines homonyms; this entry follows neuter lokk 3, a loose or hinged cover for a container.
