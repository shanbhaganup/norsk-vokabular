## byråd

**English:** city council; executive council

**Topics:** city, politics

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et byråd ble valgt etter kommunevalget. | A city council was elected after the municipal election. |
| Entall ubestemt | byråd | Byråd styrer kommunen mellom valgene. | The executive council governs the municipality between elections. |
| Entall bestemt | byrådet | Byrådet la fram sitt forslag til budsjett. | The executive council presented its budget proposal. |
| Flertall ubestemt | byråd | Flere byråd samarbeider om regionplanen. | Several executive councils cooperate on the regional plan. |
| Flertall bestemt | byråda | Byråda møttes til en felles konferanse. | The executive councils met for a joint conference. |
| Flertall bestemt | byrådene | Byrådene møttes til en felles konferanse. | The executive councils met for a joint conference. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers the elected executive administration in a larger municipality (dictionary entry II), distinct from a byråd as an individual councillor.
