## brandy

**English:** brandy

**Topics:** food, drinks

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En brandy fra denne regionen har en mild smak. | A brandy from this region has a mild flavour. |
| Entall ubestemt | brandy | De serverte brandy etter middagen. | They served brandy after dinner. |
| Entall bestemt | brandyen | Brandyen ble lagret på eikefat. | The brandy was aged in oak barrels. |
| Flertall ubestemt | brandyer | Butikken fører brandyer fra flere land. | The shop stocks brandies from several countries. |
| Flertall bestemt | brandyene | Brandyene på smakingen hadde ulik alder. | The brandies at the tasting had different ages. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Brandy is usually a mass noun; the plural forms can refer to varieties of the drink.
