## ris

**English:** rice

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En ris vokser best i varmt klima. | Rice grows best in a warm climate. |
| Entall ubestemt | ris | Vi kokte ris til middagen. | We cooked rice for dinner. |
| Entall bestemt | risen | Risen ble servert med grønnsaker. | The rice was served with vegetables. |
| Flertall ubestemt | riser | Ulike riser har ulik konsistens. | Different kinds of rice have different textures. |
| Flertall bestemt | risene | Riser fra flere land ble sammenlignet. | Rice varieties from several countries were compared. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source mixes the masculine rice sense with neuter ris, a paper measure. This entry uses ris 1 (rice), masculine; neuter forms are not included.
