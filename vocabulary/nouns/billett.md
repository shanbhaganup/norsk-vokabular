## billett

**English:** ticket

**Topics:** travel, shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Jeg kjøpte en billett på stasjonen. | I bought a ticket at the station. |
| Entall ubestemt | billett | Hun trenger billett til konserten. | She needs a ticket for the concert. |
| Entall bestemt | billetten | Billetten ligger i vesken min. | The ticket is in my bag. |
| Flertall ubestemt | billetter | Vi bestilte tre billetter på nettet. | We booked three tickets online. |
| Flertall bestemt | billettene | Billettene ble kontrollert ved inngangen. | The tickets were checked at the entrance. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The examples use the admission or travel ticket sense.
