## kapp

**English:** cape; headland

**Topics:** geography

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et kapp stakk ut i havet. | A cape jutted out into the sea. |
| Entall ubestemt | kapp | Skipet nærmet seg et kapp langs kysten. | The ship approached a cape along the coast. |
| Entall bestemt | kappet | Vi kunne se kappet fra fyrtårnet. | We could see the cape from the lighthouse. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kapp | Kartet viser flere kapp langs denne kysten. | The map shows several capes along this coast. |
| Flertall bestemt | kappa | Kappa på øyas sørside er utsatt for sterk vind. | The capes on the south side of the island are exposed to strong winds. |
| Flertall bestemt | kappene | Kappene var tydelig merket på sjøkartet. | The capes were clearly marked on the nautical chart. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified where available; individual alternatives are listed separately with examples.

**Notes:** This is the geographic noun (a headland or cape). The definite plural has the accepted alternatives kappa and kappene; each is illustrated separately. In proper geographic names, Kapp is capitalized.
