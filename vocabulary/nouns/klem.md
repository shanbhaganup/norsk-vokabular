## klem

**English:** hug; pressure; force or grip; small opening

**Topics:** emotions, body, physical sensations

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Han ga meg en varm klem. | He gave me a warm hug. |
| Entall ubestemt | klem | Hun fikk en klem før hun dro. | She got a hug before she left. |
| Entall bestemt | klemmen | Klemmen gjorde henne glad. | The hug made her happy. |
| Flertall ubestemt | klemmer | Barna fikk hver sin klem. | The children each got a hug. |
| Flertall bestemt | klemmene | Klemmene var korte, men hjertelige. | The hugs were brief but heartfelt. |

**Form status:** Dictionary paradigm verified; examples illustrate the affectionate sense.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka also records pressure, force/grip, and a slight opening, including *på klem*. Source gloss ?hug? retained. The feminine form *klemma* belongs to the related noun *klemme*, not this lemma.
