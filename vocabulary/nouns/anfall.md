## anfall

**English:** attack, episode (sudden symptoms)

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Hun fikk et anfall av migrene på toget. | She had a migraine attack on the train. |
| Entall ubestemt | anfall | Hun hadde sitt første anfall som tenåring. | She had her first episode as a teenager. |
| Entall bestemt | anfallet | Anfallet gikk over etter noen minutter. | The episode passed after a few minutes. |
| Flertall ubestemt | anfall | Han hadde to anfall i løpet av helga. | He had two episodes over the weekend. |
| Flertall bestemt | anfalla | Anfalla kom som regel om natta. | The episodes usually occurred at night. |
| Flertall bestemt | anfallene | Hun skrev ned hvor lenge anfallene varte. | She wrote down how long the episodes lasted. |

**Form status:** Sense checked in Bokmålsordboka. Forms cross-checked against DinOrdbok. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The imported topic was society. The current Bokmålsordboka sense used here is a sudden episode of illness or symptoms, so the working topic is health. The appropriate English translation depends on context. NAOB also records a distinct attack or assault sense, chiefly archaic.
