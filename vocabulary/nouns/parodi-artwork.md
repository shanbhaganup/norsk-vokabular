## parodi

**English:** parody of a known work

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En parodi på diktet fikk publikum til å le. | A parody of the poem made the audience laugh. |
| Entall ubestemt | parodi | Hun skrev en parodi på et kjent drama. | She wrote a parody of a famous play. |
| Entall bestemt | parodien | Parodien overdriver originalens stil. | The parody exaggerates the original's style. |
| Flertall ubestemt | parodier | Flere parodier på romanen ble publisert. | Several parodies of the novel were published. |
| Flertall bestemt | parodiene | Parodiene ble lest høyt på scenen. | The parodies were read aloud on stage. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary sense 1 is a mocking imitation of a known work, especially literature. Person-caricature and failed-performance senses are separate entries.
