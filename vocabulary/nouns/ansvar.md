## ansvar

**English:** responsibility

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Hun fikk et stort ansvar. | She was given a great responsibility. |
| Entall ubestemt | ansvar | Han tar ansvar for feilene sine. | He takes responsibility for his mistakes. |
| Entall bestemt | ansvaret | Ansvaret ligger hos styret. | The board is responsible. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ansvar | De fordelte flere ulike ansvar mellom seg. | They divided several different responsibilities among themselves. |
| Flertall bestemt | ansvara | Ansvara er fordelt mellom to grupper. | The responsibilities are divided between two groups. |
| Flertall bestemt | ansvarene | Vi gjennomgikk ansvarene på møtet. | We went over the responsibilities at the meeting. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Plural forms refer to distinct responsibilities.
