## moral

**English:** lesson or precept conveyed by a story

**Topics:** language and stories

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En moral fra fortellingen var at man ikke skal dømme for raskt. | A lesson from the story was not to judge too quickly. |
| Entall ubestemt | moral | En moral fra fortellingen var at man ikke skal dømme for raskt. | A lesson from the story was not to judge too quickly. |
| Entall bestemt | moralen | Moralen i fortellingen var lett å forstå. | The moral of the story was easy to understand. |
| Flertall ubestemt | moraler | Gamle fortellinger kan ha tydelige moraler. | Old stories can have clear morals. |
| Flertall bestemt | moralene | Moralene i eventyrene diskuterte barna etterpå. | The children discussed the morals in the fairy tales afterward. |
