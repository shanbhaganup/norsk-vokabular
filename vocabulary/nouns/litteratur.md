## litteratur

**English:** literature; literary works

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En litteratur om emnet ligger på biblioteket. | A body of literature on the subject is in the library. |
| Entall ubestemt | litteratur | Hun studerer norsk litteratur. | She studies Norwegian literature. |
| Entall bestemt | litteraturen | Litteraturen fra perioden er godt bevart. | The literature from the period is well preserved. |
| Flertall ubestemt | litteraturer | Kurset sammenligner litteraturer fra ulike kulturer. | The course compares literatures from different cultures. |
| Flertall bestemt | litteraturene | Litteraturene i samlingen er ordnet etter språk. | The literatures in the collection are arranged by language. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Plural is uncommon and refers to distinct bodies of literature or traditions; singular is usually a mass noun.
