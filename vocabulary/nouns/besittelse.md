## besittelse

**English:** possession

**Topics:** law

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En besittelse av ulovlige våpen kan føre til straff. | Possession of illegal weapons can lead to a penalty. |
| Entall ubestemt | besittelse | Politiet fant et brev i hennes besittelse. | The police found a letter in her possession. |
| Entall bestemt | besittelsen | Besittelsen av huset ble overført til datteren. | Ownership of the house was transferred to the daughter. |
| Flertall ubestemt | besittelser | Museet sikret flere historiske besittelser for samlingen. | The museum secured several historical possessions for the collection. |
| Flertall bestemt | besittelsene | De oversjøiske besittelsene ble omtalt i arkivet. | The country’s overseas possessions were mentioned in the archive. |
