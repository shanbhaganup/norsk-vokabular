## lidenskap

**English:** passion

**Topics:** feelings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En lidenskap for musikk preget hele livet hennes. | A passion for music shaped her whole life. |
| Entall ubestemt | lidenskap | Han utviklet en lidenskap for fuglefoto. | He developed a passion for bird photography. |
