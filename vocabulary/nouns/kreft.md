## kreft

**English:** cancer; canker in plants

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Han fikk en kreft på epletreet. | A canker developed on the apple tree. |
| Entall ubestemt | kreft | Hun ble behandlet for kreft. | She was treated for cancer. |
| Entall bestemt | kreften | Kreften på greina ble skåret bort. | The canker on the branch was cut away. |
| Flertall ubestemt | krefter | Krefter på flere trær spredte seg raskt. | Cankers on several trees spread quickly. |
| Flertall bestemt | kreftene | Kreftene på epletrærne ble fjernet ved beskjæring. | The cankers on the apple trees were removed by pruning. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The reference gives feminine gender, but Bokmålsordboka lists masculine: en kreft. The plural is rare and is illustrated with the dictionary’s plant-disease sense; the common disease sense is normally uncountable.
