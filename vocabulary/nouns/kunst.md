## kunst

**English:** art; artistic work

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Artikkel, hankjønn | en kunst | En kunst kan utfordre måten vi ser verden på. | An art form can challenge how we see the world. |
| Entall ubestemt | kunst | Museet viser kunst fra flere land. | The museum displays art from several countries. |
| Entall bestemt | kunsten | Kunsten i rommet er laget av lokale kunstnere. | The art in the room was made by local artists. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kunster | Hun lærte seg flere kunster på kurset. | She learned several skills at the course. |
| Flertall bestemt | kunstene | Kunstene hans gjorde inntrykk på publikum. | His artistic tricks impressed the audience. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The main sense is art; *kunst* is commonly uncountable in this meaning. Plural *kunster* is illustrated with the dictionary's distinct countable senses of practiced skills and tricks, so the plural is not presented as ordinary plural of artworks. The dictionary gives masculine gender; the source gender was corrected.
