## mote

**English:** fashion; prevailing style

**Topics:** shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mote kan vare i mange år. | A fashion can last for many years. |
| Entall ubestemt | mote | Dette antrekket følger en ny mote. | This outfit follows a new fashion. |
| Entall bestemt | moten | Moten skifter fra sesong til sesong. | Fashion changes from season to season. |
| Flertall ubestemt | moter | Ulike moter påvirker klesbutikkene. | Different fashions influence clothing shops. |
| Flertall bestemt | motene | Motene fra 1990-tallet er tilbake. | The fashions from the 1990s are back. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original teaching examples supplied for every listed form.

**Notes:** The source dictionary citation is the fashion sense (mote 2). The homonymous word mote 1 has a different meaning (“steep slope”) and is outside this source gloss.
