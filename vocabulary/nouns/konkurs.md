## konkurs

**English:** bankruptcy

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En konkurs kan få store følger for de ansatte. | A bankruptcy can have serious consequences for the employees. |
| Entall ubestemt | konkurs | Selskapet unngikk konkurs. | The company avoided bankruptcy. |
| Entall bestemt | konkursen | Konkursen ble behandlet i retten. | The bankruptcy was dealt with in court. |
| Flertall ubestemt | konkurser | Banken har sett flere konkurser i år. | The bank has seen several bankruptcies this year. |
| Flertall bestemt | konkursene | Konkursene førte til mange tapte arbeidsplasser. | The bankruptcies led to many lost jobs. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The noun means the legal bankruptcy procedure. The dictionary separately lists konkurs as an adjective meaning unable to pay debts; that adjective is outside this noun entry.
