## lyn

**English:** lightning, flash of lightning

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et lyn lyste opp hele dalen. | A flash of lightning lit up the whole valley. |
| Entall ubestemt | lyn | Lyn kan være farlig for mennesker og dyr. | Lightning can be dangerous to people and animals. |
| Entall bestemt | lynet | Lynet slo ned i et tre på jordet. | The lightning struck a tree in the field. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lyn | Flere lyn lyste opp horisonten. | Several flashes of lightning lit up the horizon. |
| Flertall bestemt, alternativ 1 | lyna | Lyna kom uten lyd fordi uværet var langt unna. | The flashes of lightning came without sound because the storm was far away. |
| Flertall bestemt, alternativ 2 | lynene | Lynene var synlige over havet hele natta. | The flashes of lightning were visible over the sea all night. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. Compound inflection is labelled derived where applicable. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The noun can be mass-like when referring to the phenomenon and countable when referring to individual flashes. Both definite plural forms *lyna* and *lynene* are accepted.
