## lukke

**English:** close1

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et lukke på sekken gikk i stykker. | A fastening on the backpack broke. |
| Entall ubestemt | lukke | Syersken sydde et lukke i kåpa. | The seamstress sewed a closure into the coat. |
| Entall bestemt | lukket | Lukket på veska satt fast. | The fastening on the bag was stuck. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lukker | Flere lukker ble skiftet ut. | Several fastenings were replaced. |
| Flertall bestemt | lukka / lukkene | Lukka og lukkene på jakkene var slitte. | The fastenings on the jackets were worn. / The fastenings on the jackets were worn. |

**Form status:** Headword and applicable forms reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** A device for closing or fastening; the noun is distinct from the verb lukke “close”.
