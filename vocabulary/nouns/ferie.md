## ferie

**English:** holiday; vacation

**Topics:** travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En ferie ved sjøen passer oss godt. | A holiday by the sea suits us well. |
| Entall ubestemt | ferie | Vi trenger en ferie etter en travel vår. | We need a holiday after a busy spring. |
| Entall bestemt | ferien | Ferien begynner på mandag. | The holiday begins on Monday. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ferier | Hun har hatt mange fine ferier i Norge. | She has had many lovely holidays in Norway. |
