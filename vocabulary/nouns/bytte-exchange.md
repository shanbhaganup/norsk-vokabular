## bytte

**English:** exchange; trade

**Topics:** shopping, general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et bytte av plasser gjorde gruppen mer effektiv. | An exchange of places made the group more efficient. |
| Entall ubestemt | bytte | De gjorde et bytte av leilighet med venner. | They exchanged apartments with friends. |
| Entall bestemt | byttet | Byttet ble avtalt på forhånd. | The exchange was agreed in advance. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bytter | Flere bytter av varer ble registrert. | Several exchanges of goods were recorded. |
| Flertall bestemt | bytta | Bytta ble registrert i systemet. | The exchanges were recorded in the system. |
| Flertall bestemt | byttene | Byttene ble registrert i systemet. | The exchanges were recorded in the system. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers an exchange or swap (dictionary entry I, sense 1), distinct from something taken by force, which the dictionary also calls bytte.
