## blindvei

**English:** dead-end street; dead end

**Topics:** city, transport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Vi måtte snu fordi veien endte i en blindvei. | We had to turn around because the road ended in a dead end. |
| Entall ubestemt | blindvei | Gaten ender i en blindvei. | The street ends in a dead end. |
| Entall bestemt | blindveien | Blindveien ligger bak skolen. | The dead-end street is behind the school. |
| Flertall ubestemt | blindveier | Kartet viser to blindveier i området. | The map shows two dead-end streets in the area. |
| Flertall bestemt | blindveiene | Blindveiene er skiltet tydelig. | The dead-end streets are clearly signposted. |

**Notes:** The source spelling blindvei is retained; the dictionary also accepts blindveg with its own paradigm.
