## herre

**English:** lord; master; gentleman

**Topics:** family, people, titles

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en (masculine) | En herre ventet ved inngangen. | A gentleman was waiting by the entrance. |
| Entall ubestemt | herre | Vi møtte en herre på toget. | We met a gentleman on the train. |
| Entall bestemt | herren | Herren åpnet døren for gjestene. | The gentleman opened the door for the guests. |
| Flertall ubestemt | herrer | Flere herrer deltok på møtet. | Several gentlemen attended the meeting. |
| Flertall bestemt | herrene | Herrene satte seg ved bordet. | The gentlemen sat down at the table. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** This entry covers the common noun meaning a male person of rank, a master or a gentleman. The reference gloss *gentlemen* is the English plural; the Norwegian lemma is singular *herre*. The capitalized divine name *Herren* is a distinct use and is not the sense documented here.
