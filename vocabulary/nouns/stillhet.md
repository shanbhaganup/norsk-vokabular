## stillhet

**English:** silence

**Topics:** feelings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei stillhet senket seg over rommet. | A silence fell over the room. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En stillhet senket seg over rommet. | A silence fell over the room. |
| Entall ubestemt | stillhet | Hun foretrekker stillhet når hun leser. | She prefers silence when she reads. |
| Entall bestemt | stillheta | Stillheta etter uværet virket nesten uvirkelig. | The silence after the storm seemed almost unreal. |
| Entall bestemt | stillheten | Stillheten etter uværet virket nesten uvirkelig. | The silence after the storm seemed almost unreal. |
| Flertall ubestemt | stillheter | Fjellet byr på stillheter som er sjeldne i byen. | The mountains offer silences that are rare in the city. |
| Flertall bestemt | stillhetene | Stillhetene mellom replikkene gjorde scenen sterkere. | The silences between the lines made the scene more powerful. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted; plural refers to distinct periods or experiences of silence.
