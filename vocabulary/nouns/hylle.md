## hylle

**English:** shelf; shelving unit

**Topics:** home, furniture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei hylle står over skrivebordet. | A shelf is above the desk. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En hylle står over skrivebordet. | A shelf is above the desk. |
| Entall ubestemt | hylle | Jeg trenger ei hylle til bøkene. | I need a shelf for the books. |
| Entall bestemt | hylla | Hylla er festet godt til veggen. | The shelf is firmly attached to the wall. |
| Entall bestemt | hyllen | Hyllen er festet godt til veggen. | The shelf is firmly attached to the wall. |
| Flertall ubestemt | hyller | Vi monterte to hyller på kjøkkenet. | We installed two shelves in the kitchen. |
| Flertall bestemt | hyllene | Hyllene fyller hele veggen. | The shelves cover the whole wall. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes a shelf or shelving unit and, in a separate sense, a ledge in a rock face. The source English gloss and topic concern the household shelf. Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted in Bokmål. The snapshot's dictionary URL was mismatched; the cited direct entry has the paradigm used here.
