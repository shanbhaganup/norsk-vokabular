## mørke

**English:** darkness; lack of light

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et mørke senket seg over dalen. | A darkness settled over the valley. |
| Entall ubestemt | mørke | Vi gikk gjennom skogen i mørke. | We walked through the forest in darkness. |
| Entall bestemt | mørket | Mørket kom tidlig om vinteren. | Darkness came early in winter. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mørker | Lange mørker preget vintermånedene. | Long periods of darkness marked the winter months. |
| Flertall bestemt | mørka | Mørka om vinteren varte lenge. | The periods of darkness in winter lasted a long time. |
| Flertall bestemt | mørkene | Mørkene i nord kan være lange. | The dark periods in the north can be long. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms reviewed; original examples authored for the labelled forms and senses.

**Notes:** Sense 1 of Bokmålsordboka 39870. The dictionary also distinguishes ignorance and evil/theological darkness in companion entries. The article also lists a separate related headword mørker, whose paradigm is not merged here.
