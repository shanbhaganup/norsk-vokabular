## mistenkt

**English:** suspect (person)

**Topics:** law

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mistenkt ble avhørt. | A suspect was questioned. |
| Entall ubestemt | mistenkt | Politiet avhørte en mistenkt. | The police questioned a suspect. |
| Entall bestemt | mistenkte | Den mistenkte ventet på advokaten. | The suspect waited for the lawyer. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mistenkte | Flere mistenkte ble avhørt. | Several suspects were questioned. |
| Flertall bestemt | mistenkte | De mistenkte fikk hver sin advokat. | The suspects each received a lawyer. |

**Form status:** Substantivized adjective paradigm; forms checked as productive nominal use.

**Notes:** This is the person noun formed from adjectival participle mistenkt. The dictionary’s Nynorsk entry explicitly records substantivized use; Bokmål examples also productively use den mistenkte/de mistenkte. The source English verb “suspect” maps to Norwegian verb mistenke, not mistenkt; that existing verb is linked below.
