## ord

**English:** word

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et ord kan uttrykke en idé. | A word can express an idea. |
| Entall ubestemt | ord | Et ord kan ha flere betydninger. | A word can have several meanings. |
| Entall bestemt | ordet | Jeg slo opp et ord i ordboka. | I looked up a word in the dictionary. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ord | Ordet står i første avsnitt. | The word is in the first paragraph. |
| Flertall bestemt | orda / ordene | De lærte mange nye ord. | They learned many new words. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Dictionary has several figurative and extended senses, including speech, statement, promise and mention. This entry covers the language-unit sense. Indefinite plural is identical in spelling to singular; both definite-plural alternatives shown.
