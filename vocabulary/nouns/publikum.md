# publikum

**English:** public; audience

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et publikum kan bestå av mennesker i alle aldre. | An audience can consist of people of all ages. |
| Entall ubestemt | publikum | Museet inviterer publikum til en gratis omvisning. | The museum invites the public to a free guided tour. |
| Entall bestemt | publikumet | Publikumet i salen ventet stille. | The audience in the hall waited quietly. |
| Entall bestemt | publikummet | Publikummet på festivalen sang med. | The audience at the festival sang along. |
| Flertall ubestemt | publikum | To publikum fulgte debatten fra hver sin sal. | Two audiences followed the debate from separate halls. |
| Flertall bestemt | publikuma | Publikuma ved de to forestillingene reagerte ulikt. | The audiences at the two performances reacted differently. |
| Flertall bestemt | publikumene | Publikumene fikk hver sin korte introduksjon. | The audiences each received a brief introduction. |
| Flertall bestemt | publikumma | Publikumma fra begge salene møttes i foajeen. | The audiences from both halls met in the foyer. |
| Flertall bestemt | publikummene | Publikummene på de to festivaldagene hadde ulik størrelse. | The audiences on the two festival days were of different sizes. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and paradigm checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Collective noun. Bokmålsordboka lists both the general public and a group attending an event; the article and plural alternatives shown are dictionary forms. Plural is used when comparing distinct audiences.
