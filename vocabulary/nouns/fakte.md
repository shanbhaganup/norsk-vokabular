## fakte

**English:** facts

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei fakte står på listen. | a gesture is the relevant noun. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En fakte står på listen. | a gesture is the relevant noun. |
| Entall ubestemt | fakte | Ei fakte kan virke tilgjort. | A gesture can seem affected. |
| Entall bestemt (1) | fakta | Den fakta hun gjorde med hendene, virket tilgjort. | The gesture she made with her hands seemed affected. |
| Entall bestemt (2) | fakten | Fakten hennes virket tilgjort. | Her mannerism seemed affected. |
| Flertall ubestemt | fakter | Hun gjorde fakter med hendene. | She gestured with her hands. |
| Flertall bestemt | faktene | Faktene hennes virket overdrevne. | Her gestures seemed exaggerated. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; authored teaching examples.

**Notes:** Paradigm follows the cited Bokmålsordboka entry. Examples are original teaching sentences. The noun is especially common in plural use. The source spelling is fakte; fakta is a definite singular alternative, distinct from the neuter noun faktum.
