# radiostasjon

**English:** radio station

**Topics:** technology; media

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En radiostasjon sender nyheter hver time. | A radio station broadcasts news every hour. |
| Entall ubestemt | radiostasjon | Hun jobber i en lokal radiostasjon. | She works at a local radio station. |
| Entall bestemt | radiostasjonen | Radiostasjonen sendte et nødvarsel. | The radio station broadcast an emergency alert. |
| Flertall ubestemt | radiostasjoner | Flere radiostasjoner dekket valget. | Several radio stations covered the election. |
| Flertall bestemt | radiostasjonene | Radiostasjonene delte sendernettet. | The radio stations shared the transmitter network. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and paradigm checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The dictionary distinguishes a business that produces and broadcasts programmes from a station that sends and receives radio signals; both uses share this paradigm. Source gender corrected to masculine (en).
