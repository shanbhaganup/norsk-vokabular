## garderobe

**English:** wardrobe; cloakroom

**Topics:** shopping, clothing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En garderobe står langs veggen på soverommet. | A wardrobe stands along the bedroom wall. |
| Entall ubestemt | garderobe | Vi kjøpte en garderobe med skyvedører. | We bought a wardrobe with sliding doors. |
| Entall bestemt | garderoben | Garderoben på hotellet er åpen hele dagen. | The cloakroom at the hotel is open all day. |
| Flertall ubestemt | garderober | De nye garderobene har god plass til klær. | The new wardrobes have plenty of room for clothes. |
| Flertall bestemt | garderobene | Garderobene i teateret er fulle av kostymer. | The dressing rooms in the theater are full of costumes. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; examples authored for this project.

**Notes:** *Garderobe* can mean a piece of furniture for storing clothes or a cloakroom/dressing room. The forms are the same for both senses.
