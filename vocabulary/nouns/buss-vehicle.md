## buss

**English:** bus

**Topics:** travel, transport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En buss kjørte inn til sentrum. | A bus drove into the city centre. |
| Entall ubestemt | buss | Vi tok buss til flyplassen. | We took the bus to the airport. |
| Entall bestemt | bussen | Bussen stopper ved skolen. | The bus stops by the school. |
| Flertall ubestemt | busser | Flere busser står ved terminalen. | Several buses are at the terminal. |
| Flertall bestemt | bussene | Bussene går hvert kvarter. | The buses run every fifteen minutes. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers the motor vehicle for passenger transport (dictionary entry I). A separate dictionary entry uses buss for a piece of chewing tobacco.
