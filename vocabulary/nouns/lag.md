## lag

**English:** team

**Topics:** sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gender/article | et | Et lag spiller på hjemmebane. | A team is playing at home. |
| Singular indefinite | lag | Vi heier på et lag fra Tromsø. | We support a team from Tromsø. |
| Singular definite | laget | Laget vant kampen. | The team won the match. |
