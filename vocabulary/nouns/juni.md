## juni

**English:** June

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En juni med lyse netter passer godt for fjellturer. | A June with bright nights is ideal for mountain hikes. |
| Entall ubestemt | juni | Vi dro dit en juni for noen år siden. | We went there one June a few years ago. |
| Entall bestemt | junien | Junien var kjøligere enn mai. | June was cooler than May. |
| Flertall ubestemt | junier | Flere junier har hatt tidlig blomstring. | Several Junes have had early flowering. |
| Flertall bestemt | juniene | Juniene i nord er lyse nesten hele døgnet. | The Junes in the north are bright almost around the clock. |

**Form status:** Headword and paradigm reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Plural forms refer to instances of the month across different years or calendars; ordinary dates use the month name without an article.
