## garanti

**English:** guarantee; warranty

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En garanti gir kunden ekstra trygghet. | A guarantee gives the customer extra peace of mind. |
| Entall ubestemt | garanti | En garanti gir kunden ekstra trygghet. | A guarantee gives the customer extra peace of mind. |
| Entall bestemt | garantien | Garantien gjelder i fem år. | The warranty is valid for five years. |
| Flertall ubestemt | garantier | Butikken tilbyr garantier på flere produkter. | The shop offers warranties on several products. |
| Flertall bestemt | garantiene | Garantiene dekker både deler og arbeid. | The warranties cover both parts and labor. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The noun can refer to a promise or a product warranty; examples show both related uses.
