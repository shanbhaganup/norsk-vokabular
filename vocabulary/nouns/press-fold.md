## press

**English:** crease; pressed fold (in clothing)

**Topics:** clothing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En press gjør buksa penere. | A crease makes the trousers look neater. |
| Entall ubestemt | press | Han strøk en press i buksa. | He ironed a crease into the trousers. |
| Entall bestemt | pressen | Pressen holder seg etter vask. | The crease remains after washing. |
| Flertall ubestemt | presser | Flere presser var synlige i stoffet. | Several creases were visible in the fabric. |
| Flertall bestemt | pressene | Pressene ble strøket på nytt. | The creases were ironed again. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Distinct masculine homonym 1 means a crease or pressed fold, especially in trousers. The reference also gives neuter press, the pressure sense, in press-pressure.md.
