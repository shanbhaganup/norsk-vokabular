## sinn

**English:** mind; mood

**Topics:** people

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et sinn påvirkes av både søvn og stress. | The mind is affected by both sleep and stress. |
| Entall ubestemt | sinn | Hun hadde et rolig sinn før prøven. | She had a calm mind before the test. |
| Entall bestemt | sinnet | Sinnet hans var fullt av spørsmål. | His mind was full of questions. |
| Flertall ubestemt | sinn | Ulike sinn reagerer forskjellig på press. | Different minds react differently to pressure. |
| Flertall bestemt | sinna / sinnene | Sinna / Sinnene i gruppa fant ro gjennom meditasjon. | The minds in the group found calm through meditation. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both definite plural forms are accepted; examples use the mind/person sense.
