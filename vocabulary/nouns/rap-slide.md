# rap

**English:** slide; landslide

**Topics:** nature; geology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et rap løsnet fra den bratte skråningen. | A small slide broke loose from the steep slope. |
| Entall ubestemt | rap | Et rap gikk i fjellsida etter det kraftige regnet. | A small slide occurred on the mountainside after the heavy rain. |
| Entall bestemt | rapet | Rapet stengte en del av stien. | The slide blocked part of the trail. |
| Flertall ubestemt | rap | Flere rap gikk i den ustabile skråningen. | Several small slides occurred on the unstable slope. |
| Flertall bestemt | rapa | Rapa ble registrert etter uværet. | The slides were recorded after the storm. |
| Flertall bestemt | rapene | Rapene ble registrert etter uværet. | The slides were recorded after the storm. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and paradigm checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Dictionary entry rap 2 is neuter and denotes an utgliding/ras, a rare or specialized word. The source’s English gloss “rap” is misleading; this sense means a slide or small landslide, not the music genre.
