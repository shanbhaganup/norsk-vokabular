## nedlasting

**English:** download

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei nedlasting / En nedlasting kan ta litt tid. | A download can take a little while. |
