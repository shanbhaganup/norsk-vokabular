## hai

**English:** shark; figuratively, a greedy or ruthless person

**Topics:** nature, people

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En hai svømte forbi båten. | A shark swam past the boat. |
| Entall ubestemt | hai | Dykkeren fikk øye på hai i det klare vannet. | The diver spotted a shark in the clear water. |
| Entall bestemt | haien | Haien holdt seg langt fra stranden. | The shark stayed far from the beach. |
| Flertall ubestemt | haier | Flere haier lever i norske farvann. | Several sharks live in Norwegian waters. |
| Flertall bestemt | haiene | Haiene fulgte stimen av småfisk. | The sharks followed the school of small fish. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists both the fish and a figurative sense for a greedy, ruthless person. The same inflection applies to both; the paradigm examples illustrate the literal sense.
