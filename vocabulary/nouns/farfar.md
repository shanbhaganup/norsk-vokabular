## farfar

**English:** paternal grandfather

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Min farfar bor i nærheten av oss. | My paternal grandfather lives near us. |
| Entall ubestemt | farfar | Jeg besøkte farfar på søndag. | I visited my paternal grandfather on Sunday. |
| Entall bestemt | farfaren | Farfaren min lærte meg ? fiske. | My paternal grandfather taught me to fish. |
| Flertall ubestemt | farfedre | To farfedre ventet utenfor skolen. | Two paternal grandfathers waited outside the school. |
| Flertall bestemt | farfedrene | Farfedrene delte historier fra barndommen. | The paternal grandfathers shared stories from childhood. |
