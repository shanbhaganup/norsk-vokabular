## mysterium

**English:** mystery

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et mysterium holdt leserne våkne. | A mystery kept the readers awake. |
| Entall ubestemt | mysterium | Hun løste et gammelt mysterium. | She solved an old mystery. |
| Entall bestemt | mysteriet | Mysteriet ble aldri helt forklart. | The mystery was never fully explained. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mysterier | Flere mysterier står igjen etter etterforskningen. | Several mysteries remain after the investigation. |
| Flertall bestemt | mysteria / mysteriene | Mysteria / Mysteriene fra øya fascinerte forskerne. | The mysteries from the island fascinated the researchers. |
