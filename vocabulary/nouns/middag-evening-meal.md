## middag

**English:** dinner; main meal

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En middag med venner kan vare lenge. | A dinner with friends can last a long time. |
| Entall ubestemt | middag | Vi inviterte naboene på middag. | We invited the neighbours to dinner. |
| Entall bestemt | middagen | Middagen står klar på bordet. | Dinner is ready on the table. |
| Flertall ubestemt | middager | Familien spiser ofte middager sammen i helgene. | The family often eats dinners together at weekends. |
| Flertall bestemt | middagene | Middagene på hotellet begynner klokken sju. | Dinner at the hotel begins at seven. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword, masculine gender and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** `Middag` can mean the main meal (often in the evening) and the middle of the day/noon. The entry focuses on the meal; the time sense is noted because it is also dictionary-listed.
