## olje

**English:** oil

**Topics:** food, energy

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei olje / En olje kan brukes i matlaging. | An oil can be used in cooking. |
| Entall ubestemt | olje | Hun helte olje i panna. | She poured oil into the pan. |
