## meter

**English:** metre stick; measuring stick

**Topics:** home, school

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En meter lå i verktøykassen. | A metre stick was in the toolbox. |
| Entall ubestemt | meter | Hun tok fram en meter fra verktøykassen. | She took a metre stick out of the toolbox. |
| Entall bestemt | meteren | Meteren viste at hyllen var for lang. | The metre stick showed that the shelf was too long. |
| Flertall ubestemt | metere | Skolen kjøpte nye metere til sløydsalen. | The school bought new metre sticks for the woodworking room. |
| Flertall bestemt | meterne | Meterne ble hengt tilbake på veggen. | The metre sticks were hung back on the wall. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** This is `meter 2`, meaning a metre stick and used in compounds for something a specified number of metres long. Its indefinite plural is `metere`, unlike the zero plural for the length unit.
