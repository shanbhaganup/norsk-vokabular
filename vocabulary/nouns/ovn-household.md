## ovn

**English:** oven; stove

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En ovn kan brukes til baking. | An oven can be used for baking. |
| Entall ubestemt | ovn | Vi kjøpte en ny ovn til kjøkkenet. | We bought a new oven for the kitchen. |
| Entall bestemt | ovnen | Ovnen var varm før gjestene kom. | The oven was hot before the guests arrived. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ovner | Moderne ovner bruker mindre strøm. | Modern ovens use less electricity. |
| Flertall bestemt | ovnene | Ovnene på kjøkkenet ble rengjort. | The ovens in the kitchen were cleaned. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary also lists an industrial construction for heating or smelting; the entry covers the household oven sense.
