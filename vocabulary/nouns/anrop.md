## anrop

**English:** call (phone)

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Jeg fikk et anrop fra et ukjent nummer. | I got a call from an unknown number. |
| Entall ubestemt | anrop | Telefonen viste ett ubesvart anrop. | The phone showed one missed call. |
| Entall bestemt | anropet | Anropet ble brutt da vi kjørte inn i tunnelen. | The call dropped as we drove into the tunnel. |
| Flertall ubestemt | anrop | Sentralbordet mottok mange anrop før lunsj. | The switchboard received many calls before lunch. |
| Flertall bestemt | anropa | De siste anropa kom like før stengetid. | The last calls came just before closing time. |
| Flertall bestemt | anropene | Hun sjekket anropene fra dagen før. | She checked the calls from the previous day. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The examples use the phone sense. Et ubesvart anrop is a missed call.
