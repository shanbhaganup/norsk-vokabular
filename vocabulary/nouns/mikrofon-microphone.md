## mikrofon

**English:** microphone

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mikrofon sto på talerstolen. | A microphone stood on the lectern. |
| Entall ubestemt | mikrofon | Hun koblet en mikrofon til høyttaleren. | She connected a microphone to the speaker. |
| Entall bestemt | mikrofonen | Mikrofonen fanget opp hver lyd. | The microphone picked up every sound. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mikrofoner | Mikrofoner ble plassert rundt scenen. | Microphones were placed around the stage. |
| Flertall bestemt | mikrofonene | Mikrofonene ble slått av etter sendingen. | The microphones were turned off after the broadcast. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword, masculine gender and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The source gender was corrected to masculine `en mikrofon`. The dictionary sense is a device that converts sound vibrations into electrical signals for recording or amplification.
