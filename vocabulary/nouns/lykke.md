## lykke

**English:** happiness, good fortune, luck

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | De ønsket henne ei lykke som skulle vare. | They wished her a happiness that would last. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Han fant en lykke han aldri hadde kjent før. | He found a happiness he had never known before. |
| Entall ubestemt | lykke | Lykke kan komme når man minst venter det. | Happiness can come when you least expect it. |
| Entall bestemt, hunkjønn | lykka | Lykka varte ikke lenge. | The good fortune did not last long. |
| Entall bestemt, hankjønn | lykken | Lykken snudde da toget rakk fram i tide. | Fortune turned when the train arrived on time. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lykker | Hun opplevde små lykker i hverdagen. | She experienced small moments of happiness in everyday life. |
| Flertall bestemt | lykkene | Lykken hennes var knyttet til familien og musikken. | Her sources of happiness were tied to family and music. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. Compound inflection is labelled derived where applicable. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka records both good fortune and a deep, lasting feeling of well-being. The source’s English gloss ‘happiness’ covers the latter; examples include both related dictionary senses.
