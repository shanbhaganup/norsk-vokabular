## kirurg

**English:** surgeon

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kirurg forklarte inngrepet. | A surgeon explained the procedure. |
| Entall ubestemt | kirurg | Sykehuset ansatte en kirurg. | The hospital hired a surgeon. |
| Entall bestemt | kirurgen | Kirurgen snakket med pasienten. | The surgeon spoke with the patient. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kirurger | To kirurger deltok i operasjonen. | Two surgeons took part in the operation. |
| Flertall bestemt | kirurgene | Kirurgene møttes etter operasjonen. | The surgeons met after the operation. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, sense and paradigm verified.

**Notes:** The reference and Bokmålsordboka identify kirurg as masculine and give the paradigm kirurg–kirurgen–kirurger–kirurgene.
