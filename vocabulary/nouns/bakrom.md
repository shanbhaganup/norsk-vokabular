## bakrom

**English:** back room

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Butikken har et lite bakrom. | The shop has a small back room. |
| Entall ubestemt | bakrom | De trenger et bakrom til varelageret. | They need a back room to store stock. |
| Entall bestemt | bakrommet | Hun hentet esken fra bakrommet. | She fetched the box from the back room. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bakrom | Lokalet har to bakrom. | The premises have two back rooms. |
| Flertall bestemt | bakromma | Bakromma ble ryddet før oppussingen. | The back rooms were cleared before the renovation. |
| Flertall bestemt | bakrommene | De bruker bakrommene til oppbevaring. | They use the back rooms for storage. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both definite plural endings are accepted.
