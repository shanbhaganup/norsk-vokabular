## konsekvens

**English:** consequence

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En konsekvens av tørken var lavere avlinger. | One consequence of the drought was lower yields. |
| Entall ubestemt | konsekvens | Hun måtte ta en konsekvens av valget sitt. | She had to face a consequence of her choice. |
| Entall bestemt | konsekvensen | Konsekvensen ble tydelig etter noen uker. | The consequence became clear after a few weeks. |
| Flertall ubestemt | konsekvenser | Flere konsekvenser ble vurdert i rapporten. | Several consequences were considered in the report. |
| Flertall bestemt | konsekvensene | Konsekvensene rammet særlig små bedrifter. | The consequences particularly affected small businesses. |

**Form status:** Headword and paradigm reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** An effect that follows from an action or event.
