## hode

**English:** head

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et hode beskytter hjernen. | A head protects the brain. |
| Entall ubestemt | hode | Barnet tegnet et hode. | The child drew a head. |
| Entall bestemt | hodet | Hodet verket etter den lange reisen. | The head ached after the long journey. |
| Flertall ubestemt | hoder | Flere hoder snudde seg mot døra. | Several heads turned towards the door. |
| Flertall bestemt | hoda / hodene | Hoda var våte etter regnet. / Hodene var våte etter regnet. | The heads were wet after the rain. / The heads were wet after the rain. |
