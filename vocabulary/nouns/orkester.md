## orkester

**English:** orchestra

**Topics:** culture, music

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et orkester spiller sammen. | An orchestra performs together. |
| Entall ubestemt | orkester | Et orkester kan ha over hundre musikere. | An orchestra can have over a hundred musicians. |
| Entall bestemt | orkesteret | Vi hørte et orkester spille klassisk musikk. | We heard an orchestra play classical music. |
| Flertall ubestemt | orkester / orkestre | Orkesteret øvde hele ettermiddagen. | The orchestra rehearsed all afternoon. |
| Flertall bestemt | orkestera / orkestrene | Et lite orkester spilte på torget. / Flere orkestre spilte under festivalen. | A small orchestra played in the square. / Several orchestras played during the festival. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both plural alternatives and both definite-plural alternatives are illustrated. Dictionary also lists orchestra pit and orchestra seats as extended senses.
