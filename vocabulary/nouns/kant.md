## kant

**English:** edge; rim

**Topics:** objects

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kant på arket var revet. | An edge of the sheet was torn. |
| Entall ubestemt | kant | Hun skar av en kant på stoffet. | She cut off an edge of the fabric. |
| Entall bestemt | kanten | Kanten av stupet var sperret av. | The edge of the cliff was cordoned off. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kanter | Skarpe kanter ble slipt ned. | Sharp edges were filed down. |
| Flertall bestemt | kantene | Kantene på bordplata var avrundet. | The edges of the tabletop were rounded. |

**Form status:** Headword and paradigm reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source English gloss “edge” corresponds to kant 1, which is masculine. The linked reference citation points to kant 2, a separate sense with both masculine and neuter paradigms; this entry follows the edge sense and uses the masculine paradigm. Source material conflated these homonyms.
