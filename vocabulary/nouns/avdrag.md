## avdrag

**English:** instalment

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Hun betalte et avdrag på lånet. | She paid an instalment on the loan. |
| Entall ubestemt | avdrag | Vi betaler et avdrag hver måned. | We pay an instalment every month. |
| Entall bestemt | avdraget | Avdraget forfaller på fredag. | The instalment is due on Friday. |
| Flertall ubestemt | avdrag | De mangler to avdrag på lånet. | They have two instalments left on the loan. |
| Flertall bestemt | avdraga | Avdraga ble trukket automatisk fra kontoen. | The instalments were debited from the account automatically. |
| Flertall bestemt | avdragene | Hun betalte de siste avdragene i mai. | She paid the last instalments in May. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The examples show repayments on a loan. Both definite plural endings are accepted.
