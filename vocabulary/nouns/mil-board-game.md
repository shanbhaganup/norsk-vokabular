## mil

**English:** mill (two-player board game)

**Topics:** games

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordklasse / bruk | substantiv, navn på brettspill | Vi spilte mil etter middagen. | We played mill after dinner. |
| Bøying | ubøyelig: mil | Mil er et brettspill for to personer. | Mill is a board game for two people. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene identifies this noun as invariable; the form and game sense are checked.

**Notes:** This is `mil 2`, the board game also called `mølle`, and the act of forming three pieces in a row in that game. The dictionary gives it as invariable and does not supply a gender or separate singular/plural paradigm; this is distinct from the inflected distance unit `mil 1`.
