## begynnelse

**English:** beginning, start

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Det var en god begynnelse. | It was a good start. |
| Entall ubestemt | begynnelse | Historien har en overraskende begynnelse. | The story has a surprising beginning. |
| Entall bestemt | begynnelsen | Jeg gikk glipp av begynnelsen av filmen. | I missed the beginning of the film. |
| Flertall ubestemt | begynnelser | Hun skrev tre ulike begynnelser til fortellingen. | She wrote three different openings for the story. |
| Flertall bestemt | begynnelsene | Vi sammenlignet begynnelsene på de to tekstene. | We compared the openings of the two texts. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The phrase i begynnelsen means ‘at first’ or ‘in the beginning’.
