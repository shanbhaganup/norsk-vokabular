## ankomst

**English:** arrival

**Topics:** travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En ankomst etter midnatt må meldes på forhånd. | An arrival after midnight must be reported in advance. |
| Entall ubestemt | ankomst | Ankomst til hotellet er mulig fra klokka tre. | Arrival at the hotel is possible from three o’clock. |
| Entall bestemt | ankomsten | Ankomsten til toget ble forsinket. | The train’s arrival was delayed. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ankomster | Flyplassen registrerte mange ankomster i morges. | The airport recorded many arrivals this morning. |
| Flertall bestemt | ankomstene | Ankomstene fra Bergen var forsinket. | The arrivals from Bergen were delayed. |
