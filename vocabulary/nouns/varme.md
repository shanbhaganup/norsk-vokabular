## varme

**English:** heat

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En varme fra ovnen fyller rommet. | A warmth from the oven fills the room. |
| Entall ubestemt | varme | Vi trenger varme i dette rommet. | We need heat in this room. |
| Entall bestemt | varmen | Varmen fra ovnen holder stua lun. | The heat from the oven keeps the living room cozy. |
| Flertall ubestemt | varmer | Flere varmer fra ovnene holder drivhuset lunt. | Several sources of heat from the stoves keep the greenhouse warm. |
| Flertall bestemt | varmene | Varmene fra ovnene fordeler seg jevnt i huset. | The heat from the stoves spreads evenly through the house. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms and gender checked; original teaching examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Formene gjelder varme i betydningen høy temperatur eller varmeenergi.
