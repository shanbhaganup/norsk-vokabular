# rap

**English:** belch

**Topics:** health; everyday language

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and paradigm checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Dictionary entry rap 1 means a belch (air rising from the stomach), with both masculine and neuter paradigms. Masculine and neuter alternatives are preserved and demonstrated. The English “rap” meaning a music genre is spelled rapp in Bokmålsordboka and is not this lemma.
