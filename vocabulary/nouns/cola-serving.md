## cola

**English:** serving of cola

**Topics:** food, drinks

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En cola kostet tretti kroner. | A cola cost thirty kroner. |
| Entall ubestemt | cola | Hun bestilte cola uten is. | She ordered a cola without ice. |
| Entall bestemt | colaen | Colaen sto ved siden av maten. | The cola was next to the food. |
| Flertall ubestemt | colaer | Vi bestilte fire colaer til bordet. | We ordered four colas for the table. |
| Flertall bestemt | colaene | Colaene kom sammen med maten. | The colas arrived with the food. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers a portion, bottle or glass of the drink (dictionary sense 2), distinct from cola as the beverage.
