## ros

**English:** praise

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En ros kan oppmuntre noen til å fortsette. | Praise can encourage someone to continue. |
| Entall ubestemt | ros | Hun fikk en ros fra læreren. | She received praise from the teacher. |
| Entall bestemt | rosen | Rosen gjorde ham glad. | The praise made him happy. |
| Flertall ubestemt | roser | Laget fikk mange roser fra kritikerne. | The team received a lot of praise from the critics. |
| Flertall bestemt | rosene | Rosene fra juryen ble lest opp etter forestillingen. | The praise from the jury was read out after the performance. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The plural examples make clear that roser means words of praise, not roses (flowers).
