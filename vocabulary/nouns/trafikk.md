## trafikk

**English:** traffic

**Topics:** transport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En tett trafikk av biler passerte brua. | A steady stream of cars crossed the bridge. |
| Entall ubestemt | trafikk | Det var lite trafikk på veien søndag morgen. | There was little traffic on the road on Sunday morning. |
| Entall bestemt | trafikken | Trafikken sto stille ved tunnelen. | Traffic was at a standstill by the tunnel. |
