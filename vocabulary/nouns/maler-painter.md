## maler

**English:** painter; decorator

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En maler arbeider på huset. | A painter is working on the house. |
| Entall ubestemt | maler | Vi trenger maler til oppussingen. | We need a painter for the renovation. |
| Entall bestemt | maleren | Maleren valgte en bred pensel. | The painter chose a wide brush. |
| Flertall ubestemt | malere | To malere restaurerte veggmaleriet. | Two painters restored the mural. |
| Flertall bestemt | malerne | Malerne stilte ut arbeidene sine. | The painters exhibited their works. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Ordbøkene includes both a tradesperson who paints and an artist who paints pictures. The examples show the occupation broadly.
