## forlovede

**English:** fiancé or fiancée

**Topics:** family

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and paradigm checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists forlovede as a masculine noun meaning the person one is engaged to; English fiancé/fiancée is gendered, so the gloss names both.
