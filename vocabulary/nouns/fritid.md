## fritid

**English:** free time; leisure time

**Topics:** sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei fritid med plass til lesing er verdifull. / En fritid med plass til lesing er verdifull. | She has free time filled with a lot of reading. / She has free time filled with a lot of reading. |
| Entall ubestemt | fritid | Barn trenger fritid etter skolen. | Children need free time after school. |
| Entall bestemt | fritida / fritiden | Fritida hennes er full av aktiviteter. / Fritiden hennes er full av aktiviteter. | Her free time is full of activities. / Her free time is full of activities. |
| Flertall ubestemt | fritider | I ulike fritider har han prøvd mange hobbyer. | During different periods of free time, he has tried many hobbies. |
| Flertall bestemt | fritidene | Fritidene mellom sesongene var korte. | The periods of free time between seasons were short. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** This is normally a mass noun in the singular. Plural forms refer to separate periods of leisure. Feminine singular definite is fritida; masculine is fritiden.
