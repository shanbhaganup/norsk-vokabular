## lader

**English:** charger

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Article, masculine | en lader | Jeg kjøpte en lader til telefonen. | I bought a charger for the phone. |
| Singular indefinite | lader | En lader følger ikke alltid med telefonen. | A charger is not always included with the phone. |
| Singular definite | laderen | Laderen ligger i sekken. | The charger is in the backpack. |
| Plural indefinite, -e | ladere | Butikken har flere ladere på lager. | The shop has several chargers in stock. |
| Plural definite | laderne | Laderne ligger på hylla. | The chargers are on the shelf. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka gives the charger noun as masculine, with plural *ladere* and definite plural *laderne*. The reference gender/plural alternatives came from a different homograph, and its dictionary link pointed to that homograph; both have been corrected.
