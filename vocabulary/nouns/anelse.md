## anelse

**English:** intuition; faint idea

**Topics:** thoughts

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei anelse av uro gjorde henne oppmerksom. / En anelse av uro gjorde henne oppmerksom. | A faint feeling of unease made her alert. / A faint feeling of unease made her alert. |
| Entall ubestemt | anelse | Jeg hadde en anelse om at møtet var avlyst. | I had a feeling that the meeting had been cancelled. |
| Entall bestemt | anelsen | Anelsen viste seg å stemme. | The hunch turned out to be right. |
| Flertall ubestemt | anelser | Hun fikk flere anelser om hva som hadde skjedd. | She had several inklings of what had happened. |
| Flertall bestemt | anelsene | Anelsene hennes ble bekreftet senere. | Her inklings were confirmed later. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The attachment gloss “clue” is misleading for this noun; anelse means a hunch, intuition, or faint impression. For a concrete clue, use ledetråd.
