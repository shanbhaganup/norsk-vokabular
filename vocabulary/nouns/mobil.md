## mobil

**English:** mobile phone; mobile sculpture

**Topics:** technology, art

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mobil ligger på bordet. | A mobile phone is lying on the table. |
| Entall ubestemt | mobil | Hun kjøpte en ny mobil. | She bought a new mobile phone. |
| Entall bestemt | mobilen | Mobilen hennes er fulladet. | Her phone is fully charged. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mobiler | Mange mobiler har gode kameraer. | Many mobile phones have good cameras. |
| Flertall bestemt | mobilene | Mobilene må være avslått under møtet. | The phones must be switched off during the meeting. |

**Form status:** Dictionary noun and regular paradigm checked; examples are original.

**Notes:** Separate masculine noun mobil (short for mobiltelefon; also a type of moving sculpture) from adjective mobil (“mobile”).
