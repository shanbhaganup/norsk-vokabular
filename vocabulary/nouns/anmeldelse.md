## anmeldelse

**English:** review (of a book, film, performance, etc.)

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Avisa trykte en anmeldelse av den nye filmen. | The newspaper printed a review of the new film. |
| Entall ubestemt | anmeldelse | Hun sendte inn sin første anmeldelse til studentavisa. | She submitted her first review to the student newspaper. |
| Entall bestemt | anmeldelsen | Anmeldelsen fikk meg til å lese boka. | The review made me want to read the book. |
| Flertall ubestemt | anmeldelser | Tidsskriftet publiserer anmeldelser av nye romaner. | The journal publishes reviews of new novels. |
| Flertall bestemt | anmeldelsene | Anmeldelsene var blandede etter premieren. | The reviews were mixed after the premiere. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The source gender was corrected to masculine. This entry follows the source’s culture sense. The police-report sense is distinct and remains outside this batch.
