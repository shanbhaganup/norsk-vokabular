## smerte

**English:** pain

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En smerte i ryggen gjorde det vanskelig å løfte. | A pain in my back made it difficult to lift. |
| Entall ubestemt | smerte | Jeg kjente en smerte i kneet. | I felt a pain in my knee. |
| Entall bestemt | smerten | Smerten gikk over etter en stund. | The pain went away after a while. |
| Flertall ubestemt | smerter | Flere smerter kan ha samme årsak. | Several pains can have the same cause. |
| Flertall bestemt | smertene | Smertene ble svakere etter behandlingen. | The pains became weaker after the treatment. |
