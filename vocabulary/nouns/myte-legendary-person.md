## myte

**English:** legend; person regarded as legendary

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En myte fra musikkhistorien besøkte skolen. | A legend from music history visited the school. |
| Entall ubestemt | myte | Hun er en myte i norsk jazz. | She is a legend in Norwegian jazz. |
| Entall bestemt | myten | Myten møtte publikum etter konserten. | The legend met the audience after the concert. |
