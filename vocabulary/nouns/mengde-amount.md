## mengde

**English:** amount; quantity; large number

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mengde snø lå på veien. | A quantity of snow lay on the road. |
| Entall ubestemt | mengde | Vi trenger en liten mengde olje. | We need a small amount of oil. |
| Entall bestemt | mengden | Mengden vann i beholderen ble målt. | The amount of water in the container was measured. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mengder | Det kom store mengder regn i natt. | Large amounts of rain fell last night. |
| Flertall bestemt | mengdene | Mengdene ble fordelt mellom gruppene. | The quantities were divided among the groups. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword, masculine gender and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The source gender was corrected to masculine `en mengde`. The dictionary sense covers a quantity, a large number, and in mathematics a set of elements; the examples use quantity.
