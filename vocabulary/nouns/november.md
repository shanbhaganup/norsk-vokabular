## november

**English:** November

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En november kan være mild langs kysten. | A November can be mild along the coast. |
| Entall ubestemt | november | Vi flyttet hit i november. | We moved here in November. |
| Entall bestemt | novemberen | Novemberen i fjor var svært våt. | Last November was very wet. |
| Flertall ubestemt | novemberer | Flere novemberer på rad har vært milde. | Several Novembers in a row have been mild. |
| Flertall bestemt | novemberene | Novemberene vi husker best, var fulle av snø. | The Novembers we remember best were full of snow. |
