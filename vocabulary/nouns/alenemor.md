## alenemor

**English:** single mother

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei alenemor startet gruppa i fjor. | A single mother started the group last year. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En alenemor fortalte om hverdagen sin. | A single mother talked about her daily life. |
| Entall ubestemt | alenemor | Hun var alenemor mens barna gikk på barneskolen. | She was a single mother while her children were in elementary school. |
| Entall bestemt | alenemora | Alenemora i romanen flytter til en liten by. | The single mother in the novel moves to a small town. |
| Entall bestemt | alenemoren | Alenemoren fikk hjelp av naboene da hun flyttet. | The single mother got help from her neighbors when she moved. |
| Flertall ubestemt | alenemødre | Flere alenemødre arrangerte en lekedag i parken. | Several single mothers organized a play day in the park. |
| Flertall ubestemt | alenemødrer | Kommunen inviterte alenemødrer til et kveldsmøte om barnehagetilbudet. | The municipality invited single mothers to an evening meeting about childcare. |
| Flertall bestemt | alenemødrene | Alenemødrene i gruppa byttet på å hente barna. | The single mothers in the group took turns picking up the children. |

**Form status:** Compound and meaning documented in NAOB. The extended paradigm follows the final element mor in Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** NAOB documents the compound and its usual plural alenemødre. The feminine forms and the source variant alenemødrer follow the Bokmålsordboka paradigm of the final element mor. Alenemødrer is presented as a compound-derived variant.
