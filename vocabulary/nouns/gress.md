## gress

**English:** grass

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et gress vokser her. | A grass grows here. |
| Entall ubestemt | gress | Det vokser gress langs stien. | Grass grows along the path. |
| Entall bestemt | gresset | Gresset er vått etter regnet. | The grass is wet after the rain. |
| Flertall ubestemt | gress | Det ligger gress på bakken. | There is grass on the ground. |
| Flertall bestemt | gressa / gressene | Gressa / gressene ble klippet i går. | The grass was cut yesterday. / The grasses were cut yesterday. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Mass use is most common; plural forms are dictionary-listed. The dictionary also lists gras/graset/gras/gras(a)/grasene as an accepted spelling variant, omitted here to keep this lemma entry focused.
