## børs

**English:** stock exchange; exchange

**Topics:** money, finance

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En børs åpnet for handel i dag. | A stock exchange opened for trading today. |
| Entall ubestemt | børs | Selskapet ble notert på børs. | The company was listed on the stock exchange. |
| Entall bestemt | børsen | Børsen stengte tidligere enn vanlig. | The stock exchange closed earlier than usual. |
| Flertall ubestemt | børser | Flere børser holdt åpent på mandag. | Several stock exchanges were open on Monday. |
| Flertall bestemt | børsene | Børsene reagerte ulikt på nyheten. | The stock exchanges reacted differently to the news. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** A market for trading securities, currencies or commodities.
