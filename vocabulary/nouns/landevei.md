## landevei

**English:** country road

**Topics:** transport

| Gender/article | en | En landevei går gjennom dalen. | A country road runs through the valley. |
| Singular indefinite | landevei | Vi fulgte en smal landevei. | We followed a narrow country road. |
| Singular definite | landeveien | Landeveien var glatt etter regnet. | The country road was slippery after the rain. |
