## bydel

**English:** district; borough

**Topics:** city

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bydel fikk nytt bibliotek. | A city district got a new library. |
| Entall ubestemt | bydel | Hun bor i en bydel nær sentrum. | She lives in a district near the centre. |
| Entall bestemt | bydelen | Bydelen arrangerer en festival hver sommer. | The district holds a festival every summer. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bydeler | Flere bydeler ligger langs elva. | Several districts lie along the river. |
| Flertall bestemt | bydelene | Bydelene samarbeider om kollektivtransport. | The districts cooperate on public transport. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** An administratively or geographically defined part of a city.
