## barn

**English:** child

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et barn vinket fra vinduet. | A child waved from the window. |
| Entall ubestemt | barn | Hun lærte å svømme som barn. | She learned to swim as a child. |
| Entall bestemt | barnet | Barnet sov i vogna. | The child was asleep in the pram. |
| Flertall ubestemt | barn | Tre barn lekte i sandkassa. | Three children were playing in the sandpit. |
| Flertall bestemt | barna | Barna gikk hjem etter skolen. | The children went home after school. |
| Flertall bestemt | barnene | Barnene fikk hver sin blyant. | Each of the children was given a pencil. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The indefinite plural has the same spelling as the singular. Both definite plural forms are accepted.
