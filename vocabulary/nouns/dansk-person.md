## dansk

**English:** Dane

**Topics:** people, countries

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En dansk kom inn i butikken. | A Dane came into the shop. |
| Entall ubestemt | dansk | Han er dansk og bor i Oslo. | He is Danish and lives in Oslo. |
| Entall bestemt | dansken | Dansken snakket med naboen. | The Dane spoke to the neighbour. |
| Flertall ubestemt | dansker | Dansker besøker byen hele året. | Danes visit the town all year. |
| Flertall bestemt | danskene | Danskene kom med toget. | The Danes came by train. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This masculine noun names a man from Denmark; the neuter noun dansk for the Danish language has a separate entry.
