## centimeter

**English:** centimeter

**Topics:** numbers, measurement

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En centimeter er en hundredel av en meter. | A centimeter is one hundredth of a meter. |
| Entall ubestemt | centimeter | Det manglet en centimeter på lengden. | The length was short by one centimeter. |
| Entall bestemt | centimeteren | Centimeteren er delt inn i millimeter. | The centimeter is divided into millimeters. |
| Flertall ubestemt | centimeter | Stoffet er to centimeter bredt. | The fabric is two centimeters wide. |
| Flertall bestemt | centimeterne | De siste centimeterne ble målt nøye. | The last centimeters were measured carefully. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The indefinite plural is identical to the singular. The plural forms occur when a number or measurement makes the quantity clear.
