## magasin

**English:** magazine

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et magasin lå på bordet. | A magazine was lying on the table. |
| Entall ubestemt | magasin | Hun leser magasin på toget. | She reads magazines on the train. |
| Entall bestemt | magasinet | Magasinet kommer ut hver måned. | The magazine is published every month. |
| Flertall ubestemt | magasin | Biblioteket har flere magasin om natur. | The library has several magazines about nature. |
| Flertall ubestemt | magasiner | Biblioteket abonnerer på magasiner for barn. | The library subscribes to magazines for children. |
| Flertall bestemt | magasina | Magasina om friluftsliv ligger på øverste hylle. | The magazines about outdoor life are on the top shelf. |
| Flertall bestemt | magasinene | Magasinene ble delt ut på messen. | The magazines were handed out at the fair. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword and alternatives checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The dictionary gives neuter, zero-plural alternatives `magasin` and `magasiner`, and definite plurals `magasina` and `magasinene`. The source sense is the periodical; Ordbøkene also lists storage, firearm and broadcast senses.
