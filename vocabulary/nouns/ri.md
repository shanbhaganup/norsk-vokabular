## ri

**English:** sudden bout; spell

**Topics:** health, general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei ri / En ri med hoste vekket barnet. | A bout of coughing woke the child. |
| Entall ubestemt | ri | Hun fikk ei ri av latter. | She had a fit of laughter. |
| Entall bestemt | ria / rien | Ria / Rien gikk snart over. | The bout soon passed. |
| Flertall ubestemt | rier | Plutselige rier av smerte kom og gikk. | Sudden bouts of pain came and went. |
| Flertall bestemt | riene | Riene med uvær stengte fjellveien. | The spells of bad weather closed the mountain road. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source English gloss “ride” does not match ri; the dictionary sense is a sudden bout, fit or short spell. Both feminine and masculine forms are accepted.
