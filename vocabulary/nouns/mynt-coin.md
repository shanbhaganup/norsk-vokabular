## mynt

**English:** coin

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mynt lå på fortauet. | A coin was lying on the pavement. |
| Entall ubestemt | mynt | Hun fant en mynt under sofaen. | She found a coin under the sofa. |
| Entall bestemt | mynten | Mynten var gammel og slitt. | The coin was old and worn. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mynter | Samlingen inneholder mynter fra mange land. | The collection contains coins from many countries. |
| Flertall bestemt | myntene | Myntene ble oppbevart i et album. | The coins were kept in an album. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original teaching examples authored for the labelled sense and forms.

**Notes:** The dictionary also records a coin face, currency/money, and a place where coins are made; this entry follows the source coin sense.
