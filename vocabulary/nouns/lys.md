## lys

**English:** light

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Vi så et lys i det fjerne. | We saw a light in the distance. |
| Entall ubestemt | lys | Gardinen slipper inn lys. | The curtain lets in light. |
| Entall bestemt | lyset | Lyset fra vinduet falt på gulvet. | The light from the window fell on the floor. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lys | Det blinket to lys på taket. | Two lights flashed on the roof. |
| Flertall bestemt | lysa | Lysa langs veien sluknet. | The lights along the road went out. |
| Flertall bestemt | lysene | De tente lysene på scenen. | They turned on the lights on stage. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The adverb list’s source gloss identifies the noun. Uncountable light has singular use, while the plurals below refer to individual lights.
