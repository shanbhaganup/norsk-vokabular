## kaffe

**English:** coffee; coffee beans or the drink, a serving, or a light meal with coffee

**Topics:** food, drink

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | en kaffe står ved inngangen. | A coffee is by the entrance. |
| Entall ubestemt | kaffe | Hun kjøpte kaffe fra et lokalt brenneri. | She bought coffee from a local roastery. |
| Entall bestemt | kaffen | Kaffen står på bordet. | The coffee is on the table. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kaffer | Kafeen serverer flere kaffer fra ulike land. | The café serves several coffees from different countries. |
| Flertall bestemt | kaffene | Kaffene på menyen har ulike smaksnoter. | The coffees on the menu have different tasting notes. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and listed paradigms checked against Bokmålsordboka.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists beans, the drink, a serving, and a light meal with coffee. Plural forms are used for kinds or servings; the singular is normally used for the drink as a mass noun.
