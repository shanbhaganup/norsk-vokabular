## bukk

**English:** blunder (in chess)

**Topics:** games, chess

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bukk kostet spilleren partiet. | A blunder cost the player the game. |
| Entall ubestemt | bukk | Han gjorde en bukk i åpningen. | He made a blunder in the opening. |
| Entall bestemt | bukken | Bukken avgjorde partiet. | The blunder decided the game. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bukker | Flere bukker gjorde stillingen håpløs. | Several blunders made the position hopeless. |
| Flertall bestemt | bukkene | Bukkene ble analysert etter partiet. | The blunders were analysed after the game. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers a very poor chess move (dictionary entry II), distinct from the animal and equipment senses.
