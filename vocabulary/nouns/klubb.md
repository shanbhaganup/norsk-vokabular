## klubb

**English:** club; organized group or association

**Topics:** leisure, society, city

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En klubb møtes her hver onsdag. | A club meets here every Wednesday. |
| Entall ubestemt | klubb | Hun meldte seg inn i en klubb for turgåere. | She joined a hiking club. |
| Entall bestemt | klubben | Klubben arrangerer en konsert i kveld. | The club is organizing a concert tonight. |
| Flertall ubestemt | klubber | Flere klubber deltar i turneringen. | Several clubs are taking part in the tournament. |
| Flertall bestemt | klubbene | Klubbene deler på treningshallen. | The clubs share the sports hall. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; examples authored.

**Notes:** The reference's feminine classification was corrected to masculine according to Bokmålsordboka. Examples use the organized-group sense; context can also give *klubb* the meaning ?nightclub.?
