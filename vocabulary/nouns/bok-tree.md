## bok

**English:** beech; beech wood

**Topics:** nature, trees

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei bok vokser ved stien. | A beech tree grows beside the path. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bok vokser ved stien. | A beech tree grows beside the path. |
| Entall ubestemt | bok | Bok trives best i næringsrik jord. | Beech thrives best in nutrient-rich soil. |
| Entall bestemt | boka | Boka ved porten er svært gammel. | The beech tree by the gate is very old. |
| Entall bestemt | boken | Boken ved porten er svært gammel. | The beech tree by the gate is very old. |
| Flertall ubestemt | boker | Boker står langs hele alléen. | Beech trees line the entire avenue. |
| Flertall bestemt | bokene | Bokene langs alléen får gule blader om høsten. | The beech trees along the avenue get yellow leaves in autumn. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers the tree and its wood (dictionary entry II), separate from bok meaning a book. The source combines both dictionary entries; boker/bokene are the forms for this tree sense. Feminine singular forms are also accepted.
