## kai

**English:** quay; landing or loading structure for vessels

**Topics:** city, travel, transport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | en kai står ved inngangen. | A quay is by the entrance. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | ei kai står ved inngangen. | A quay is by the entrance. |
| Entall ubestemt | kai | En kai ble bygget langs fjorden. | A quay was built along the fjord. |
| Entall bestemt | kaien | Kaien ligger nær sentrum. | The quay is near the town centre. |
| Entall bestemt | kaia | Kaia ligger nær sentrum. | The quay is near the town centre. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kaier | Skipene legger til ved flere kaier. | The ships dock at several quays. |
| Flertall bestemt | kaiene | Kaiene ble inspisert før vinteren. | The quays were inspected before winter. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and listed paradigms checked against Bokmålsordboka.

**Notes:** Both masculine and feminine gender are accepted. Bokmålsordboka lists the feminine definite singular kaia and a complete masculine paradigm; the plural forms are shared.
