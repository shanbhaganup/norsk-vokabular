## bråk

**English:** noise; commotion

**Topics:** daily life

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et bråk fra gata vekket oss. | A noise from the street woke us. |
| Entall ubestemt | bråk | Det var mye bråk utenfor. | There was a lot of noise outside. |

| Flertall ubestemt | bråk | Folk klaget over bråk fra byggeplassen. | People complained about noise from the construction site. |

**Notes:** The reference source merges distinct dictionary entries and includes forms belonging to another sense or spelling. This entry follows the neuter noun meaning noise/commotion; feminine forms *bråka* and *bråken* and forms *bræker/brækene* are excluded from this paradigm.
