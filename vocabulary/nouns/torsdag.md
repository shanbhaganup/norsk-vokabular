## torsdag

**English:** Thursday

**Topics:** time; weekdays

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En torsdag passer best for møtet. | A Thursday works best for the meeting. |
| Entall ubestemt | torsdag | Vi har et møte på torsdag. | We have a meeting on Thursday. |
| Entall bestemt | torsdagen | Torsdagen var uvanlig travel. | Thursday was unusually busy. |
