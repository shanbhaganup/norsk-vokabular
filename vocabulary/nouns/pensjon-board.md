## pensjon

**English:** board and lodging; full board

**Topics:** travel, lodging

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En pensjon kan være inkludert i hotellprisen. | Board and lodging may be included in the hotel price. |
| Entall ubestemt | pensjon | Vi bestilte pensjon på hotellet for hele uka. | We booked board and lodging at the hotel for the whole week. |
| Entall bestemt | pensjonen | Pensjonen omfattet frokost og middag. | The board included breakfast and dinner. |
| Flertall ubestemt | pensjoner | Flere pensjoner tilbys i området. | Several full-board options are offered in the area. |
| Flertall bestemt | pensjonene | Pensjonene ble sammenlignet før bestilling. | The board options were compared before booking. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary sense 2 means board and lodging, as in full board at a hotel; retirement benefit is in the separate pensjon-retirement entry.
