## statistikk

**English:** statistic; statistical data

**Topics:** data

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En statistikk fra i fjor viser samme tendens. | A statistic from last year shows the same trend. |
| Entall ubestemt | statistikk | Jeg fant en statistikk om boligprisene. | I found a statistic about housing prices. |
| Entall bestemt | statistikken | Statistikken bygger på svar fra tusen familier. | The statistic is based on responses from a thousand families. |
| Flertall ubestemt | statistikker | Flere statistikker peker i samme retning. | Several statistics point in the same direction. |
| Flertall bestemt | statistikkene | Statistikkene fra de to årene kan sammenlignes. | The statistics from the two years can be compared. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** Statistics about local schools were published today.

**Notes:** Used for an individual numerical finding or a set of collected statistical data.
