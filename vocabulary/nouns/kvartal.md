## kvartal

**English:** quarter; three-month period

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et kvartal begynner i januar. | A quarter begins in January. |
| Entall ubestemt | kvartal | Bedriften rapporterer resultatene hvert kvartal. | The company reports its results every quarter. |
| Entall bestemt | kvartalet | Kvartalet var bedre enn forventet. | The quarter was better than expected. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kvartal | Hun jobbet der i fire kvartal. | She worked there for four quarters. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kvartaler | Hun jobbet der i fire kvartaler. | She worked there for four quarters. |
| Flertall bestemt | kvartala | Kvartala ble avsluttet i mars. | The quarters ended in March. |
| Flertall bestemt | kvartalene | Kvartalene ble avsluttet i mars. | The quarters ended in March. |

**Notes:** Three-month-period sense; source plural normalized to lemma. Dictionary confirms neuter, correcting source gender. Also means city block, a distinct sense.
