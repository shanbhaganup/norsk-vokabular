## kamel

**English:** camel; especially the two-humped camel

**Topics:** animals, nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | en kamel står ved inngangen. | A camel is by the entrance. |
| Entall ubestemt | kamel | En kamel kan gå lenge uten vann. | A camel can go a long time without water. |
| Entall bestemt | kamelen | Kamelen bar forsyninger over sanden. | The camel carried supplies across the sand. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kameler | Kameler lever i tørre områder. | Camels live in dry regions. |
| Flertall bestemt | kamelene | Kamelene hvilte i skyggen. | The camels rested in the shade. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and listed paradigms checked against Bokmålsordboka.

**Notes:** The dictionary uses kamel for the general camel category and specifically for the two-humped camel. The idiom svelge/sluke kameler (“swallow camels”) is related usage, not an inflected form.
