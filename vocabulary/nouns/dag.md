## dag

**English:** day

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En dag begynner etter soloppgangen. | A day begins after sunrise. |
| Entall ubestemt | dag | Vi reiser om en dag. | We leave in one day. |
| Entall bestemt | dagen | Dagen var varm og lys. | The day was warm and bright. |
| Flertall ubestemt | dager | Dager blir kortere utover høsten. | Days get shorter through autumn. |
| Flertall bestemt | dagene | Dagene før ferien gikk sakte. | The days before the holiday passed slowly. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Day can mean daylight or a 24-hour period. The examples show both everyday uses.
