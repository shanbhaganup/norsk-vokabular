## blod

**English:** blood

**Topics:** health, body

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et blodspor ble funnet på gulvet. | A bloodstain was found on the floor. |
| Entall ubestemt | blod | Blod transporterer oksygen rundt i kroppen. | Blood carries oxygen around the body. |
| Entall bestemt | blodet | Blodet ble undersøkt på laboratoriet. | The blood was examined in the laboratory. |
| Flertall ubestemt | blod | Ulike blod kan reagere forskjellig ved en transfusjon. | Different blood supplies can react differently in a transfusion. |
| Flertall bestemt | bloda | Bloda fra prøvene ble analysert hver for seg. | The blood samples were analysed separately. |
| Flertall bestemt | blodene | Blodene fra prøvene ble analysert hver for seg. | The blood samples were analysed separately. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Blod is normally a mass noun in this sense. Plural forms are used when distinguishing blood supplies or samples; both definite plural alternatives are illustrated.
