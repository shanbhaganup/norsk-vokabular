## forhold

**English:** relationship

**Topics:** relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et godt forhold krever tillit. | A good relationship requires trust. |
| Entall ubestemt | forhold | De snakket om et nært forhold mellom søsknene. | They talked about a close relationship between the siblings. |

| Flertall ubestemt | forhold | Familien har hatt flere vanskelige forhold å håndtere. | The family has had several difficult circumstances to deal with. |
