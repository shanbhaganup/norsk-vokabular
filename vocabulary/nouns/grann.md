## grann

**English:** a tiny amount; a bit

**Topics:** people

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et grann er nok salt i suppa. | A tiny amount is enough salt in the soup. |
| Entall ubestemt | grann | Det skal bare et grann pepper til. | It only takes a tiny bit of pepper. |
| Entall bestemt | grannet | Grannet av salt satte prikken over i-en. | The tiny amount of salt made all the difference. |
| Flertall ubestemt | grann | Det var bare grann igjen i posen. | There was only a tiny bit left in the bag. |
| Flertall bestemt | granna / grannene | Granna ble fordelt i koppene. / Grannene ble fordelt i koppene. | The tiny amounts were divided among the cups. / The tiny amounts were divided among the cups. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original bilingual teaching examples.

**Notes:** The reference gloss “neighbor” is incorrect for this record: Bokmålsordboka defines noun *grann* as a very small amount or a crumb. The neighbor word is *granne*. The adjective *grann* is a separate word type; see adjectives/grann-adjective.md.
