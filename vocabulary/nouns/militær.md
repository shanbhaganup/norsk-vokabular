**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This noun means a country’s armed forces or military service and is conventionally used in definite singular (for example, være i militæret). The dictionary lists paradigm forms, but ordinary use does not support natural examples for the indefinite singular or plurals in this sense; those cells are inapplicable here. Distinct from adjective militær.
