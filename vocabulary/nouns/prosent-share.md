## prosent

**English:** percentage share; cut; stake

**Topics:** money; business

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En prosent av salget går til forhandleren. | A percentage share of the sales goes to the retailer. |
| Entall ubestemt | prosent | Agenten får prosent av salget. | The agent receives a percentage of the sales. |
| Entall bestemt | prosenten | Prosenten ble utbetalt ved månedsslutt. | The share was paid out at the end of the month. |
| Flertall ubestemt | prosenter | Flere prosenter av inntekten gikk til provisjon. | Several percentage shares of the income went to commission. |
| Flertall bestemt | prosentene | Prosentene ble fordelt mellom partnerne. | The percentage shares were distributed among the partners. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Dictionary homonym 2 means a percentage share, such as a share of sales. Its written paradigm is mostly the same as prosent 1, but the lexical sense is kept separate.
