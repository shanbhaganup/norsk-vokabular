## forfølgelse

**English:** persecution

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Vi dokumenterte en forfølgelse. | We documented a persecution. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and applicable inflections checked.
