## bolle

**English:** bun; meatball; fish ball

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bolle med kanel passer godt til kaffen. | A cinnamon bun goes well with coffee. |
| Entall ubestemt | bolle | Han kjøpte bolle på bakeriet. | He bought a bun at the bakery. |
| Entall bestemt | bollen | Bollen var fortsatt varm. | The bun was still warm. |
| Flertall ubestemt | boller | Vi serverte boller etter turen. | We served buns after the hike. |
| Flertall bestemt | bollene | Bollene ble satt fram på et fat. | The buns were placed on a platter. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers the small round baked good and round meat or fish dumpling sense (dictionary entry II), separate from bolle meaning a bowl.
