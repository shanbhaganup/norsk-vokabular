## omkamp

**English:** rematch; replay

**Topics:** sports

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En omkamp ble satt opp etter uavgjort. | A rematch was scheduled after the draw. |
| Entall ubestemt | omkamp | Laget krevde omkamp etter regelbruddet. | The team demanded a rematch after the rule violation. |
| Entall bestemt | omkampen | Omkampen spilles på lørdag. | The rematch will be played on Saturday. |
| Flertall ubestemt | omkamper | Turneringen har hatt flere omkamper. | The tournament has had several rematches. |
| Flertall bestemt | omkampene | Omkampene ble flyttet på grunn av været. | The rematches were moved because of the weather. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Dictionary also records a figurative sense of renewed discussion or treatment; the entry follows the sports sense and notes the figurative extension.
