## brudgom

**English:** groom

**Topics:** family, weddings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En brudgom ventet ved alteret. | A groom waited by the altar. |
| Entall ubestemt | brudgom | Brudgom og brud hilste på gjestene. | Groom and bride greeted the guests. |
| Entall bestemt | brudgommen | Brudgommen holdt en kort tale. | The groom gave a short speech. |
| Flertall ubestemt | brudgommer | Flere brudgommer stilte opp til fotografering. | Several grooms lined up for photographs. |
| Flertall bestemt | brudgommene | Brudgommene ventet utenfor salen. | The grooms waited outside the hall. |

**Notes:** The examples illustrate a man on his wedding day.
