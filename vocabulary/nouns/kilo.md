## kilo

**English:** kilo; kilogram

**Topics:** measurement, food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kilo koster femti kroner. | A kilo costs fifty kroner. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et kilo koster femti kroner. | A kilo costs fifty kroner. |
| Entall ubestemt | kilo (masculine) | Vi kjøpte en kilo epler. | We bought a kilo of apples. |
| Entall ubestemt | kilo (neuter) | Vi kjøpte et kilo epler. | We bought a kilo of apples. |
| Entall bestemt | kiloen | Kiloen kostet femti kroner. | The kilo cost fifty kroner. |
| Entall bestemt | kiloet | Kiloet kostet femti kroner. | The kilo cost fifty kroner. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kilo (masculine) | Butikken solgte to kilo appelsiner. | The shop sold two kilos of oranges. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kilo (neuter) | De bestilte fem kilo kaffe. | They ordered five kilos of coffee. |
| Flertall bestemt | kiloene (masculine) | Kiloene med poteter står på lageret. | The kilos of potatoes are in storage. |
| Flertall bestemt | kiloa (neuter) | Kiloa med poteter står på lageret. | The kilos of potatoes are in storage. |
| Flertall bestemt | kiloene (neuter) | Kiloene med poteter står på lageret. | The kilos of potatoes are in storage. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka permits both masculine and neuter. Definite plural *kiloene* is shared by both genders; neuter also permits *kiloa*. Each gender-specific form and alternative is represented separately. *Kilo* is used as a measure word in examples.
