## narr

**English:** fool; person mocked by others

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En narr lot seg ikke vippe av pinnen. | A fool refused to be rattled. |
| Entall ubestemt | narr | Han oppførte seg som en narr. | He behaved like a fool. |
| Entall bestemt | narren | Narren ble ledd av på torget. | The fool was laughed at in the square. |
| Flertall ubestemt | narrer | Narrer blir ofte brukt som figurer i eventyr. | Fools are often used as characters in fairy tales. |
| Flertall bestemt | narrene | Narrene i fortellingen lærte av feilene sine. | The fools in the story learned from their mistakes. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms reviewed; original examples authored for the labelled forms and senses.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka also records a historical court jester sense and “joke” in older usage. The source gloss “fool” maps to the person sense.
