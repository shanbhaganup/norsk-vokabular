## hodepine

**English:** headache; difficult problem (figurative)

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei hodepine for kommunen var den forsinkede byggesaken. | The delayed construction case was a headache for the municipality. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En hodepine for kommunen var den forsinkede byggesaken. | A headache can make the workday difficult. |
| Entall ubestemt | hodepine | Den nye regelen ble hodepine for mange. | The new rule became a headache for many. |
| Entall bestemt | hodepina | Hodepina for ledelsen var den lave bemanningen. | The staffing shortage was the management’s headache. |
| Entall bestemt | hodepinen | Hodepinen for ledelsen var den lave bemanningen. | The headache eased after an hour. |
| Flertall ubestemt | hodepiner | Slike hodepiner tar tid å løse. | Such problems take time to solve. |
| Flertall bestemt | hodepinene | Hodepinene tok flere måneder å løse. | The problems took several months to solve. |
