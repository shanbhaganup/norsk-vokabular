## bord

**English:** decorative border; edging

**Topics:** clothing, design

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bord langs kanten ga plagget et særpreg. | A decorative border along the edge made the garment distinctive. |
| Entall ubestemt | bord | Bord kan være vevd eller brodert. | Decorative edging can be woven or embroidered. |
| Entall bestemt | borden | Borden rundt gardinen har et blomstermønster. | The border around the curtain has a floral pattern. |
| Flertall ubestemt | border | Gardinene har border i ulike farger. | The curtains have borders in different colours. |
| Flertall bestemt | bordene | Bordene på teppet ble sydd for hånd. | The borders on the rug were sewn by hand. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers a contrasting decorative edge (dictionary entry I), distinct from bord meaning a table. The source combines two homonyms and their paradigms.
