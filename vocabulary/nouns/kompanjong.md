## kompanjong

**English:** business partner; companion

**Topics:** work, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kompanjong kan dele ansvaret for firmaet. | A business partner can share responsibility for the company. |
| Entall ubestemt | kompanjong | Hun fant en kompanjong til den nye virksomheten. | She found a partner for the new business. |
| Entall bestemt | kompanjongen | Kompanjongen hennes kjenner markedet godt. | Her business partner knows the market well. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kompanjonger | De to kompanjongene startet firmaet sammen. | The two partners started the company together. |
| Flertall bestemt | kompanjongene | Kompanjongene signerte avtalen i går. | The partners signed the agreement yesterday. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and paradigm checked; sense reviewed; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Promoted from reference record word-0761. Bokmålsordboka lists “medeier i et firma” and “følgesvenn; kamerat”; the examples focus on the business-partner sense. The broader English gloss “partner” is narrowed here to avoid ambiguity.
