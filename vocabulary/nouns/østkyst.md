## østkyst

**English:** east coast

**Topics:** geography

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En østkyst kan ha et annet klima enn vestkysten. | An east coast can have a different climate from the west coast. |
| Entall ubestemt | østkyst | De besøkte en østkyst med mange fjorder. | They visited an east coast with many fjords. |
| Entall bestemt | østkysten | Østkysten får ofte sterk vind. | The east coast often gets strong winds. |
| Flertall ubestemt | østkyster | Landet har to østkyster. | The country has two east coasts. |
| Flertall bestemt | østkystene | Østkystene vender mot åpent hav. | The east coasts face the open sea. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** The headword denotes a coast on the eastern side of a land area.
