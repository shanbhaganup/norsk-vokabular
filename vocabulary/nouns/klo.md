## klo

**English:** claw; pincer; claw-like gripping part

**Topics:** animals, nature, tools

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel (feminine) | ei | Ei klo kan være skarp. | A claw can be sharp. |
| Kjønn / artikkel (masculine inflection) | en | En klo kan være skarp. | A claw can be sharp. |
| Entall ubestemt | klo | Katten har en klo som er skadet. | The cat has a claw that is injured. |
| Entall bestemt (feminine) | kloa | Kloa på ankeret satt fast i tauet. | The claw on the anchor caught in the rope. |
| Entall bestemt (masculine) | kloen | Kloen på hummeren var stor. | The lobster's claw was large. |
| Flertall ubestemt | klør | Ørnen bruker klør til å gripe byttet. | The eagle uses its claws to seize its prey. |
| Flertall bestemt | klørne | Klørne på katten må klippes. | The cat's claws need trimming. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; both gender paradigms distinguished.

**Notes:** Covers an animal's claw, a crustacean's pincer, and claw-like tools or parts; figurative uses are also recorded. The reference supplies the inflected source form *klør*; this entry restores lemma *klo*. *Kloa* is feminine definite singular and *kloen* masculine definite singular; plural forms are shared.
