## ost

**English:** cheese

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En ost passer godt på brødskiva. | Cheese goes well on bread. |
| Entall ubestemt | ost | Vi kjøpte en ost fra gården. | We bought a cheese from the farm. |
| Entall bestemt | osten | Osten modnes i kjelleren. | The cheese is maturing in the cellar. |
| Flertall ubestemt | oster | Butikken selger oster fra flere regioner. | The shop sells cheeses from several regions. |
| Flertall bestemt | ostene | Ostene ble lagt på fatet. | The cheeses were placed on the platter. |
