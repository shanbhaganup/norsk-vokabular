## bokser

**English:** boxer shorts; boxers

**Topics:** clothing, shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Han kjøpte en bokser i butikken. | He bought a pair of boxer shorts in the shop. |
| Entall ubestemt | bokser | Denne bokseren er laget av bomull. | These boxer shorts are made of cotton. |
| Entall bestemt | bokseren | Bokseren ligger i skuffen. | The boxer shorts are in the drawer. |
| Flertall ubestemt | boksere | Butikken har boksere i flere farger. | The shop has boxer shorts in several colours. |
| Flertall bestemt | bokserne | Bokserne i denne størrelsen er utsolgt. | The boxer shorts in this size are sold out. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists bokser as a short form of boksershorts (entry I, sense 3), distinct from a person who boxes and the dog breed.
