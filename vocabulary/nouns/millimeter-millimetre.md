## millimeter

**English:** millimetre

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En millimeter er en tusendels meter. | A millimetre is one thousandth of a metre. |
| Entall ubestemt | millimeter | Det mangler én millimeter på bredden. | The width is one millimetre short. |
| Entall bestemt | millimeteren | Millimeteren ble markert på linjalen. | The millimetre was marked on the ruler. |
| Flertall ubestemt | millimeter | Det falt tolv millimeter regn i natt. | Twelve millimetres of rain fell last night. |
| Flertall bestemt | millimeterne | Millimeterne ble kontrollert med skyvelære. | The millimetres were checked with callipers. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword, masculine gender and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The plural is identical to the singular (`millimeter`); the definite plural is `millimeterne`. The dictionary also uses the word in the expression `på millimeteren`, meaning “exactly”.
