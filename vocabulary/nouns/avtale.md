## avtale

**English:** agreement, appointment

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Vi inngikk en avtale med naboen. | We made an agreement with the neighbour. |
| Entall ubestemt | avtale | Jeg har en avtale klokka to. | I have an appointment at two. |
| Entall bestemt | avtalen | Hun måtte flytte avtalen til fredag. | She had to move the appointment to Friday. |
| Flertall ubestemt | avtaler | Han har to avtaler etter lunsj. | He has two appointments after lunch. |
| Flertall bestemt | avtalene | Vi skrev avtalene inn i kalenderen. | We entered the appointments in the calendar. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The noun can refer to an arrangement or an appointment.
