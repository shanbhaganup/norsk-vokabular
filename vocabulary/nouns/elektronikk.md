## elektronikk

**English:** electronics

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En elektronikk står på listen. | a electronics is the relevant noun. |
| Entall ubestemt | elektronikk | Hun studerer elektronikk på skolen. | She studies electronics at school. |
| Entall bestemt | elektronikken | Elektronikken i radioen må repareres. | The electronics in the radio need to be repaired. |
| Flertall ubestemt | elektronikker | Butikken viser ulike typer elektronikk. | The store displays different kinds of electronics. |
| Flertall bestemt | elektronikkene | Elektronikkene ble undersøkt hver for seg. | The electronic devices were examined separately. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; authored teaching examples.

**Notes:** Paradigm follows the cited Bokmålsordboka entry. Examples are original teaching sentences. Plural forms are uncommon for the mass sense and refer to kinds or collections; use the mass noun in ordinary contexts.
