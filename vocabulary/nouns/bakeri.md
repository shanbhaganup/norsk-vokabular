## bakeri

**English:** bakery

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Det ligger et bakeri ved torget. | There’s a bakery by the square. |
| Entall ubestemt | bakeri | Hun jobber på et bakeri. | She works at a bakery. |
| Entall bestemt | bakeriet | Bakeriet åpner klokka sju. | The bakery opens at seven. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bakeri | Byen har flere små bakeri. | The town has several small bakeries. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bakerier | To nye bakerier åpnet i fjor. | Two new bakeries opened last year. |
| Flertall bestemt | bakeria | Bakeria i byen deltok på matfestivalen. | The town’s bakeries took part in the food festival. |
| Flertall bestemt | bakeriene | Vi prøvde brød fra begge bakeriene. | We tried bread from both bakeries. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both indefinite and both definite plural forms are accepted.
