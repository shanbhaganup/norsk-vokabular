## skitt

**English:** dirt; filth

**Topics:** home, nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En skitt kan sette flekker på gulvet. | A bit of dirt can leave stains on the floor. |
| Entall ubestemt | skitt | En skitt fra støvelen falt på matta. | A piece of dirt from the boot fell on the rug. |
| Entall bestemt | skitten | Skitten satt fast under skoen. | The dirt was stuck under the shoe. |
| Flertall ubestemt | skitter | Skitter fra måkene lå igjen på kaia. | Bird droppings were left on the pier. |
| Flertall bestemt | skittene | Skittene langs gangveien ble fjernet om morgenen. | The droppings along the path were removed in the morning. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Skitt has several senses, including dirt, excrement and a disliked person; examples use physical dirt/filth. Some plural contexts are coarse.
