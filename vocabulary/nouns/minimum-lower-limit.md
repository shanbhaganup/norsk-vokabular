## minimum

**English:** minimum; lower limit

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et minimum ble satt for bemanningen. | A minimum was set for staffing. |
| Entall ubestemt | minimum | Rapporten anbefaler et minimum på tre ansatte. | The report recommends a minimum of three employees. |
| Entall bestemt | minimumet | Minimumet for støtteordningen ble justert. | The minimum for the support scheme was adjusted. |
| Flertall ubestemt | minima | Grafen har to minima ved endepunktene. | The graph has two minima at the endpoints. |
| Flertall ubestemt | minimum | Målingene viste tre minimum i løpet av forsøket. | The measurements showed three minima during the experiment. |
| Flertall ubestemt | minimumer | Forskerne sammenlignet minimumer fra ulike modeller. | The researchers compared minima from different models. |
| Flertall bestemt | minimaene | Minimaene i kurvene lå nær hverandre. | The minima in the curves were close to one another. |
| Flertall bestemt | minimuma | Minimuma for hvert område ble ført inn i tabellen. | The minima for each area were entered in the table. |
| Flertall bestemt | minimumene | Minimumene for hvert område ble ført inn i tabellen. | The minima for each area were entered in the table. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword and all listed plural alternatives checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Neuter noun with plural alternatives `minima`, `minimum`, `minimumer` and definite plurals `minimaene`, `minimuma`, `minimumene`; each form is illustrated. Ordbøkene also uses `minimum` adverbially to mean “at least”; that use is outside this noun entry.
