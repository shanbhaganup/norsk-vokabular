## brennstoff

**English:** fuel; combustible material

**Topics:** technology, energy

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et brennstoff må oppbevares trygt. | A fuel must be stored safely. |
| Entall ubestemt | brennstoff | Dette brennstoffet brenner rent. | This fuel burns cleanly. |
| Entall bestemt | brennstoffet | Brennstoffet ble lagret i en lukket beholder. | The fuel was stored in a closed container. |
| Flertall ubestemt | brennstoff | Ulike brennstoff brukes i industrien. | Different fuels are used in industry. |
| Flertall ubestemt | brennstoffer | Industrien prøver ut flere brennstoffer. | Industry is testing several fuels. |

| Flertall bestemt | brennstoffene | Brennstoffene ble sammenlignet i forsøket. | The fuels were compared in the experiment. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Brennstoff is neuter. Both indefinite and definite plural alternatives are accepted and shown separately; the plural refers to types of fuel.
