## myndighet

**English:** authority; power to act or decide

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei myndighet til å avgjøre saken må være tydelig. | An authority to decide the matter must be clearly established. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En myndighet til å avgjøre saken må være tydelig. | An authority to decide the matter must be clearly established. |
| Entall ubestemt | myndighet | Hun har myndighet til å godkjenne søknaden. | She has the authority to approve the application. |
| Entall bestemt | myndigheta | Myndigheta hans var begrenset. | His authority was limited. |
| Entall bestemt | myndigheten | Myndigheten hennes var begrenset. | Her authority was limited. |
| Flertall ubestemt | — | The abstract authority sense is normally singular; plural denotes public agencies. | This plural cell is inapplicable to the abstract sense. |
| Flertall bestemt | — | The abstract authority sense is normally singular; plural denotes public agencies. | This plural cell is inapplicable to the abstract sense. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original teaching examples authored for the labelled sense and forms.

**Notes:** Sense 1 of Bokmålsordboka 39712. The source gloss authorities points primarily to its distinct plural public-agency sense, which is in a companion entry. Singular forms are used for the abstract authority sense. The plural forms belong to the separate public-agency sense and are marked inapplicable here.
