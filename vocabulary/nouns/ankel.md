## ankel

**English:** ankle

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En ankel stakk fram under teppet. | An ankle stuck out from under the blanket. |
| Entall ubestemt | ankel | Etter turen hadde hun én hoven ankel. | After the hike she had one swollen ankle. |
| Entall bestemt | ankelen | Han vred ankelen da han gikk av bussen. | He twisted his ankle getting off the bus. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ankler | Etter flyturen hadde hun hovne ankler. | After the flight her ankles were swollen. |
| Flertall bestemt | anklene | Mudderet rakk oss til anklene. | The mud came up to our ankles. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The source gender was corrected to masculine. The plural forms drop the unstressed e before l.
