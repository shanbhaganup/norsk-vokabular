## sett

**English:** set; collection

**Topics:** home, sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et sett kan inneholde alt utstyret vi trenger. | A set can contain all the equipment we need. |
