## møte

**English:** meeting

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et møte begynner om ti minutter. | A meeting starts in ten minutes. |
| Entall ubestemt | møte | Vi planlegger et møte med læreren. | We are planning a meeting with the teacher. |
| Entall bestemt | møtet | Møtet ble flyttet til torsdag. | The meeting was moved to Thursday. |
| Flertall ubestemt | møter | Hun har møter hele formiddagen. | She has meetings all morning. |
| Flertall bestemt | møta | Møta på skolen starter klokka ni. | The meetings at the school start at nine. |
| Flertall bestemt | møtene | Møtene på skolen starter klokka ni. | The meetings at the school start at nine. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The noun has a meeting or gathering sense. Both definite plural forms møta and møtene are accepted.
