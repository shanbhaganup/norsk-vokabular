# ran

**English:** robbery

**Topics:** law; crime

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et ran ble meldt til politiet. | A robbery was reported to the police. |
| Entall ubestemt | ran | Ran mot butikken skjedde sent på kvelden. | The robbery at the shop happened late in the evening. |
| Entall bestemt | ranet | Ranet ble etterforsket av politiet. | The robbery was investigated by the police. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ran | Flere ran ble meldt i området. | Several robberies were reported in the area. |
| Flertall bestemt | rana | Rana skjedde på ulike steder. | The robberies happened in different places. |
| Flertall bestemt | ranene | Ranene skjedde på ulike steder. | The robberies happened in different places. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and paradigm checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The dictionary defines this as theft involving (a threat of) violence, rather than theft in general. Neuter plural definite alternatives are both illustrated.
