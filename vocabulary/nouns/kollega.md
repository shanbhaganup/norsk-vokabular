## kollega

**English:** colleague; coworker

**Topics:** work, people

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kollega hjalp meg med rapporten. | A colleague helped me with the report. |
| Entall ubestemt | kollega | Jeg spiste lunsj med en kollega. | I had lunch with a colleague. |
| Entall bestemt | kollegaen | Kollegaen min begynner klokka ni. | My colleague starts at nine. |
| Flertall ubestemt (kollegaer) | kollegaer | To kollegaer deltok på møtet. | Two colleagues attended the meeting. |
| Flertall ubestemt (kolleger) | kolleger | Flere kolleger jobbet hjemmefra. | Several colleagues worked from home. |
| Flertall bestemt (kollegaene) | kollegaene | Kollegaene hennes inviterte henne på kaffe. | Her colleagues invited her for coffee. |
| Flertall bestemt (kollegene) | kollegene | Kollegene mine kjenner planen. | My colleagues know the plan. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; alternatives retained.

**Notes:** Both plural patterns are accepted: *kollegaer/kollegaene* and *kolleger/kollegene*. The reference’s inferred work topic fits the workplace sense; the word can also mean someone in the same profession. Teaching examples are original.
