## frukt

**English:** fruit; plant's fruit; result (figurative)

**Topics:** food and plants

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei frukt og en frukt lå i skålen. | A piece of fruit lay in the bowl. (Both feminine and masculine articles are accepted.) |
| Entall ubestemt | frukt | Butikken selger frukt fra lokale gårder. | The shop sells fruit from local farms. |
| Entall bestemt | frukta / frukten | Frukta var moden. / Frukten var moden. | The fruit was ripe. / The fruit was ripe. |
| Flertall ubestemt | frukter | Eksotiske frukter lå på bordet. | Exotic fruits were on the table. |
| Flertall bestemt | fruktene | Fruktene fra hagen ble delt mellom naboene. | The fruits from the garden were shared among the neighbors. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and paradigm checked against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine forms are accepted. The dictionary has edible fruit, botanical fruit, and figurative 'good result' senses. The examples here use edible fruit.
