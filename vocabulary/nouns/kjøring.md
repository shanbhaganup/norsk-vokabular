## kjøring

**English:** driving; a drive

**Topics:** transport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Kjøring i dårlig vær krever ekstra oppmerksomhet. | Driving in bad weather requires extra care. |
| Entall ubestemt | kjøring | Vi planla en kjøring langs kysten. | We planned a drive along the coast. |
| Entall bestemt | kjøringa / kjøringen | Kjøringa ble avlyst. / Kjøringen ble avlyst. | The drive was cancelled. / The drive was cancelled. |

| Flertall bestemt | kjøringene | Kjøringene ble analysert av forskerne. | The drives were analysed by the researchers. |
