## koffert

**English:** suitcase

**Topics:** travel, shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En koffert står ved døra. | A suitcase is standing by the door. |
| Entall ubestemt | koffert | Jeg trenger en koffert til reisen. | I need a suitcase for the trip. |
| Entall bestemt | kofferten | Kofferten veier mer enn jeg trodde. | The suitcase weighs more than I thought. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kofferter | De pakket klærne i to kofferter. | They packed their clothes in two suitcases. |
| Flertall bestemt | koffertene | Koffertene ble levert ved innsjekkingsskranken. | The suitcases were handed in at the check-in desk. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** Means a large case used for personal luggage on trips. The reference notes travel as an inferred topic, consistent with the dictionary definition. Its source-gender correction is confirmed: *koffert* is masculine. Teaching examples are original.
