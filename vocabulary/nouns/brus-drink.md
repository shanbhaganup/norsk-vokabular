## brus

**English:** fizzy drink; soda

**Topics:** food, drinks

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En brus står i kjøleskapet. | A fizzy drink is in the fridge. |
| Entall ubestemt | brus | Hun kjøpte brus til pikniken. | She bought fizzy drinks for the picnic. |
| Entall bestemt | brusen | Brusen er uten sukker. | The fizzy drink is sugar-free. |
| Flertall ubestemt | brus | Butikken selger brus med ulike smaker. | The shop sells fizzy drinks in different flavours. |
| Flertall bestemt | brusene | Brusene ble satt kaldt før festen. | The fizzy drinks were chilled before the party. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers the carbonated drink (dictionary entry I), distinct from brus meaning a rushing or rustling sound.
