## bed

**English:** garden bed, flower bed

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Hun anla et bed ved inngangen. | She made a flower bed by the entrance. |
| Entall ubestemt | bed | De sådde gulrøtter i et bed. | They sowed carrots in a garden bed. |
| Entall bestemt | bedet | Det vokser tulipaner i bedet. | Tulips grow in the bed. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bed | Skolehagen har tre bed. | The school garden has three beds. |
| Flertall bestemt | beda | Beda ble luket før sommerferien. | The beds were weeded before the summer holiday. |
| Flertall bestemt | bedene | Vi vannet bedene om kvelden. | We watered the beds in the evening. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Norwegian bed refers to a garden plot. A bed for sleeping is ei or en seng.
