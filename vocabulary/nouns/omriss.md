## omriss

**English:** outline

**Topics:** art, general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et omriss viser formen uten detaljene. | An outline shows the shape without the details. |
| Entall ubestemt | omriss | Barnet tegnet et omriss av huset. | The child drew an outline of the house. |
| Entall bestemt | omrisset | Omrisset ble klippet ut av pappen. | The outline was cut out of the cardboard. |
| Flertall ubestemt | omriss | Omriss av flere blader ble tegnet på arket. | Outlines of several leaves were drawn on the sheet. |
| Flertall bestemt | omrissa / omrissene | Omrissa / Omrissene rundt figurene ble gjort tydeligere. | The outlines around the figures were made clearer. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both definite plural forms are accepted.
