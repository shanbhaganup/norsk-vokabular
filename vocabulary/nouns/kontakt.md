## kontakt

**English:** contact

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kontakt mellom skolene ble etablert i vår. | A contact between the schools was established this spring. |
| Entall ubestemt | kontakt | Vi har kontakt med læreren. | We are in contact with the teacher. |
| Entall bestemt | kontakten | Kontakten mellom gruppene ble bedre. | The contact between the groups improved. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kontakter | Hun har kontakter i flere land. | She has contacts in several countries. |
| Flertall bestemt | kontaktene | Kontaktene hennes hjalp til med arrangementet. | Her contacts helped with the event. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The source gloss includes interpersonal connection. The dictionary also lists a person one is in contact with and an electrical socket or outlet. Source sense retained without treating these related meanings as a separate entry.
