## pause

**English:** break; pause

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En pause kan gjøre det lettere å konsentrere seg. | A break can make it easier to concentrate. |
| Entall ubestemt | pause | Vi tok en pause etter den lange turen. | We took a break after the long hike. |
| Entall bestemt | pausen | Pausen varer i femten minutter. | The break lasts fifteen minutes. |
| Flertall ubestemt | pauser | Korte pauser hjelper meg å holde fokus. | Short breaks help me stay focused. |
| Flertall bestemt | pausene | Pausene mellom øktene var korte. | The breaks between sessions were short. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary gives the masculine paradigm and defines pause as a temporary stop or break.
