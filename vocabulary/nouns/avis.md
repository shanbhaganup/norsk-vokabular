## avis

**English:** newspaper

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Han kjøpte ei avis på stasjonen. | He bought a newspaper at the station. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Hun jobber for en lokal avis. | She works for a local newspaper. |
| Entall ubestemt | avis | Jeg leste en avis mens jeg ventet. | I read a newspaper while I waited. |
| Entall bestemt | avisa | Avisa kom tidlig i dag. | The newspaper arrived early today. |
| Entall bestemt | avisen | Avisen trykte bildet på forsida. | The newspaper printed the picture on the front page. |
| Flertall ubestemt | aviser | Han abonnerer på to aviser. | He subscribes to two newspapers. |
| Flertall bestemt | avisene | Avisene lå på kjøkkenbordet. | The newspapers were on the kitchen table. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted.
