## ark

**English:** dormer; roof window projection

**Topics:** housing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En ark med sprossevindu slipper lys inn på loftet. | A dormer with a divided-light window lets light into the attic. |
| Entall ubestemt | ark | Huset har en ark mot hagen. | The house has a dormer facing the garden. |
| Entall bestemt | arken | Arken ble restaurert sammen med resten av taket. | The dormer was restored along with the rest of the roof. |
| Flertall ubestemt | arker | To arker gir loftet lys fra hver side. | Two dormers bring light into the attic from each side. |
| Flertall bestemt | arkene | Arkene på den gamle bygningen er bevart. | The dormers on the old building have been preserved. |
