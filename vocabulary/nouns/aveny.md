## aveny

**English:** avenue

**Topics:** city

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Vi krysset en bred aveny. | We crossed a broad avenue. |
| Entall ubestemt | aveny | Hotellet ligger ved en aveny med trær på begge sider. | The hotel is beside an avenue lined with trees. |
| Entall bestemt | avenyen | Avenyen var stengt for trafikk. | The avenue was closed to traffic. |
| Flertall ubestemt | avenyer | Byplanen viser to nye avenyer. | The city plan shows two new avenues. |
| Flertall bestemt | avenyene | Avenyene møtes ved torget. | The avenues meet at the square. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** An avenue is typically a broad street.
