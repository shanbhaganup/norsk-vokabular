## kort

**English:** card

**Topics:** games

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et kort manglet i kortstokken. | A card was missing from the deck. |
| Entall ubestemt | kort | Hun trakk et kort fra bunken. | She drew a card from the pile. |
| Entall bestemt | kortet | Kortet lå med bildesiden ned. | The card lay face down. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kort | Vi kjøpte nye kort til spillet. | We bought new cards for the game. |
| Flertall bestemt | korta / kortene | Korta / Kortene ble blandet før runden. | The cards were shuffled before the round. / The cards were shuffled before the round. |

**Form status:** Headword and paradigm reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The examples use a playing card; both definite plural variants are listed. This noun is distinct from the adjective kort “short”.
