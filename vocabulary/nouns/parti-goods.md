## parti

**English:** batch; lot of goods

**Topics:** business

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et parti varer kom fram i morges. | A batch of goods arrived this morning. |
| Entall ubestemt | parti | Butikken kjøpte et parti klær fra fabrikken. | The shop bought a lot of clothes from the factory. |
| Entall bestemt | partiet | Partiet ble solgt til redusert pris. | The batch was sold at a reduced price. |
| Flertall ubestemt | parti / partier | Flere parti / partier varer ble levert. | Several batches of goods were delivered. |
| Flertall bestemt | partia / partiene | Partia ble fordelt mellom butikkene. / Partiene ble fordelt mellom butikkene. | The lots were distributed among the shops. / The lots were distributed among the shops. |
