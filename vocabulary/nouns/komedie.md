## komedie

**English:** comedy; comic play or film

**Topics:** culture, entertainment

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En komedie står på programmet i kveld. | A comedy is on the programme tonight. |
| Entall ubestemt | komedie | Vi så en komedie på kino. | We watched a comedy at the cinema. |
| Entall bestemt | komedien | Komedien fikk publikum til å le. | The comedy made the audience laugh. |
| Flertall ubestemt | komedier | Hun liker komedier med varm humor. | She likes comedies with warm humour. |
| Flertall bestemt | komediene | Komediene på festivalen var svært ulike. | The comedies at the festival were very different. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** Selects the film or play genre sense. Bokmålsordboka also uses *komedie* figuratively for a farcical event or pretence. The source’s gender correction is confirmed: masculine *en komedie*. Teaching examples are original.
