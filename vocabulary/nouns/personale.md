## personale

**English:** staff

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et personale trenger tydelig ledelse. | A staff needs clear leadership. |
| Entall ubestemt | personale | Sykehuset ansatte et nytt personale. | The hospital hired new staff. |
| Entall bestemt | personalet | Personalet møttes før vakta startet. | The staff met before the shift began. |
| Flertall ubestemt | personaler | Personalene ved de to skolene samarbeidet. | The staffs at the two schools worked together. |
| Flertall bestemt | personala / personalene | Personala / Personalene fikk opplæring i systemet. | The staff groups received training in the system. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Personale is usually a collective noun; plural forms refer to separate staff groups and are uncommon.
