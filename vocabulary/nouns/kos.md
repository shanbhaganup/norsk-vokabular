## kos

**English:** cosiness; a cuddle

**Topics:** emotions, family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kos foran peisen gjør godt på en kald kveld. | Some cosiness in front of the fireplace feels good on a cold evening. |
| Entall ubestemt | kos | Vi hadde kos hjemme etter turen. | We had a cosy time at home after the trip. |
| Entall bestemt | kosen | Kosen ved peisen varte hele kvelden. | The cosiness by the fireplace lasted all evening. |
| Flertall ubestemt | koser | Barna fikk koser av bestemor før leggetid. | The children got cuddles from Grandma before bedtime. |
| Flertall bestemt | kosene | Kosene fra bestemor gjorde barna glade. | The cuddles from Grandma made the children happy. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm verified; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The reference gloss *cosiness* is narrow: the dictionary also has the meaning “a cuddle” (compare *få en kos*). The two source dictionary anchors are homographs with identical masculine paradigms; neither supports feminine inflection.
