## nettside

**English:** webpage; website page

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei nettside viser åpningstidene. | A webpage shows the opening hours. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En nettside viser åpningstidene. | A webpage shows the opening hours. |
| Entall ubestemt | nettside | Butikken har ei nettside med praktisk informasjon. | The shop has a webpage with useful information. |
| Entall bestemt | nettsida | Nettsida ble oppdatert i går. | The webpage was updated yesterday. |
| Entall bestemt | nettsiden | Nettsiden ble oppdatert i går. | The webpage was updated yesterday. |
| Flertall ubestemt | nettsider | Norske nettsider bruker ofte .no-adresser. | Norwegian websites often use .no addresses. |
| Flertall bestemt | nettsidene | Nettsidene ble oversatt til engelsk. | The webpages were translated into English. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms reviewed; original examples authored for the labelled forms and senses.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted. nettside usually refers to an individual page; nettsted is a related but distinct headword for a site or collection of pages.
