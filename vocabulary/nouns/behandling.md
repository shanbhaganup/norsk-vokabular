## behandling

**English:** treatment

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Hun fikk ei ny behandling på sykehuset. | She received a new treatment at the hospital. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Legen foreslo en annen behandling. | The doctor suggested a different treatment. |
| Entall ubestemt | behandling | Han fikk behandling for skaden. | He received treatment for the injury. |
| Entall bestemt | behandlinga | Behandlinga begynner på mandag. | The treatment starts on Monday. |
| Entall bestemt | behandlingen | Behandlingen tok tre uker. | The treatment took three weeks. |
| Flertall ubestemt | behandlinger | Klinikken tilbyr flere behandlinger. | The clinic offers several treatments. |
| Flertall bestemt | behandlingene | Forskerne sammenlignet behandlingene. | The researchers compared the treatments. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The examples concern medical treatment. Both feminine and masculine forms are accepted.
