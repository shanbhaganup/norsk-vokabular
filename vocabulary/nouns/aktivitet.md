## aktivitet

**English:** activity (something people do)

**Topics:** sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Treneren foreslo en aktivitet som alle kunne være med på. | The coach suggested an activity everyone could join in. |
| Entall ubestemt | aktivitet | Hvert lag valgte sin egen aktivitet. | Each team chose its own activity. |
| Entall bestemt | aktiviteten | Aktiviteten begynner med en kort oppvarming. | The activity begins with a short warm-up. |
| Flertall ubestemt | aktiviteter | Idrettslaget tilbyr aktiviteter for barn hele sommeren. | The sports club offers activities for children all summer. |
| Flertall bestemt | aktivitetene | Vi flyttet aktivitetene inn da det begynte å regne. | We moved the activities indoors when it started raining. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The source gender was corrected to masculine. The examples use the countable sense, as in a planned or recreational activity.
