## pass

**English:** care; supervision

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et pass av små barn krever oppmerksomhet. | Caring for young children requires attention. |
| Entall ubestemt | pass | Foreldrene ordnet pass av hunden mens de reiste. | The parents arranged care for the dog while they traveled. |
| Entall bestemt | passet | Passet av barna varte til foreldrene kom hjem. | The child care lasted until the parents came home. |
| Flertall ubestemt | pass | Flere pass av kjæledyr ble avtalt før ferien. | Several pet-sitting arrangements were made before the holiday. |
| Flertall bestemt | passa / passene | Passa ble fordelt mellom naboene. / Passene ble fordelt mellom naboene. | The care duties were divided among the neighbours. / The care duties were divided among the neighbours. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary homonym pass 4 IV means care or supervision, as in pass og stell av barn. Other pass homonyms have separate entries.
