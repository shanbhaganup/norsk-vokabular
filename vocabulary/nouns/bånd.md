## bånd

**English:** ribbon; band

**Topics:** clothing, objects

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et bånd kan brukes som pynt. | A ribbon can be used as decoration. |
| Entall ubestemt | bånd | Hun knyttet et bånd rundt pakken. | She tied a ribbon around the package. |
| Entall bestemt | båndet | Båndet holder esken lukket. | The ribbon keeps the box closed. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bånd | Vi trenger bånd i flere farger. | We need ribbons in several colours. |
| Flertall bestemt | bånda | Bånda ble lagt i en skuff. | The ribbons were put in a drawer. |
| Flertall bestemt | båndene | Båndene på gavene er blå. | The ribbons on the presents are blue. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** The entry focuses on a physical ribbon/band. *Bånd* also occurs in compounds and idioms such as *knytte bånd* (“form bonds”); those are not separate inflected forms.
