## ramme

**English:** frame; framework

**Topics:** home, general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei ramme / En ramme kan beskytte bildet. | A frame can protect the picture. |
| Entall ubestemt | ramme | Han kjøpte ei ramme til fotografiet. | He bought a frame for the photograph. |
| Entall bestemt | ramma / rammen | Ramma / Rammen var laget av eik. | The frame was made of oak. |
| Flertall ubestemt | rammer | Flere rammer ble malt i sterke farger. | Several frames were painted in bright colours. |
| Flertall bestemt | rammene | Rammene ble hengt opp i gangen. | The frames were hung in the hallway. |
