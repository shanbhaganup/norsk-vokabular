## narr

**English:** jester; comic performer (historical)

**Topics:** history, culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En narr moret hoffet med vitser. | A jester entertained the court with jokes. |
| Entall ubestemt | narr | Kongen lot en narr opptre under festen. | The king had a jester perform during the celebration. |
| Entall bestemt | narren | Narren bar en fargerik drakt. | The jester wore a colorful costume. |
| Flertall ubestemt | narrer | Narrer underholdt gjestene ved hoffet. | Jesters entertained the guests at court. |
| Flertall bestemt | narrene | Narrene opptrådte mellom måltidene. | The jesters performed between courses. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms reviewed; original examples authored for this sense.

**Notes:** Historical sense 1 of Bokmålsordboka 40167: a comically dressed person employed to amuse people. The common “fool” sense is covered in the companion entry. The dictionary also records narr meaning “joke” in older usage, usually noted in compounds.
