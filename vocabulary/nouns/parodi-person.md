## parodi

**English:** caricature or distorted imitation of a person

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En parodi på ordføreren ble vist på scenen. | A caricature of the mayor was shown on stage. |
| Entall ubestemt | parodi | Komikeren laget en parodi på en kjent politiker. | The comedian created a caricature of a well-known politician. |
| Entall bestemt | parodien | Parodien på sjefen var lett å kjenne igjen. | The caricature of the boss was easy to recognize. |
| Flertall ubestemt | parodier | Flere parodier på kjendiser ble delt på nettet. | Several celebrity caricatures were shared online. |
| Flertall bestemt | parodiene | Parodiene gjorde publikum nysgjerrige. | The caricatures made the audience curious. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary sense 2 is an imitation or distorted image of a person; the artwork and fiasco senses have separate entries.
