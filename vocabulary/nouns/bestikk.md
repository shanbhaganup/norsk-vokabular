## bestikk

**English:** cutlery

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Hun arvet et bestikk i sølv. | She inherited a set of silver cutlery. |
| Entall ubestemt | bestikk | Vi trenger bestikk til seks personer. | We need cutlery for six people. |
| Entall bestemt | bestikket | Bestikket ligger i øverste skuff. | The cutlery is in the top drawer. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bestikk | Butikken selger flere bestikk i ulike mønstre. | The shop sells several cutlery sets in different patterns. |
| Flertall bestemt | bestikka | Bestikka i utstillingen var laget av sølv. | The cutlery sets on display were made of silver. |
| Flertall bestemt | bestikkene | Hun sammenlignet bestikkene før hun valgte ett. | She compared the cutlery sets before choosing one. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The reference combines homographs with different genders. Cutlery uses the neuter paradigm. The plural can refer to separate sets.
