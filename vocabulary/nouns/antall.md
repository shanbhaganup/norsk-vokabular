## antall

**English:** number, count

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et stort antall elever meldte seg på. | A large number of pupils signed up. |
| Entall ubestemt | antall | Skjemaet spør om antall deltakere. | The form asks for the number of participants. |
| Entall bestemt | antallet | Antallet økte fra mai til juni. | The number rose from May to June. |
| Flertall ubestemt | antall | Vi sammenlignet ulike antall i beregningene. | We compared different counts in the calculations. |
| Flertall bestemt | antalla | Antalla for de to dagene stemmer ikke. | The counts for the two days don’t match. |
| Flertall bestemt | antallene | Hun førte antallene inn i tabellen. | She entered the counts in the table. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The plural refers to multiple separate counts.
