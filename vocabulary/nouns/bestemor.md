## bestemor

**English:** grandmother

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei bestemor ventet ved porten. | A grandmother was waiting by the gate. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bestemor leste høyt for barna. | A grandmother read aloud to the children. |
| Entall ubestemt | bestemor | Hun har ei bestemor som bor på landet. | She has a grandmother who lives in the countryside. |
| Entall bestemt | bestemora | Bestemora hennes dyrker tomater. | Her grandmother grows tomatoes. |
| Entall bestemt | bestemoren | Han ringte bestemoren etter skolen. | He called his grandmother after school. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bestemødre | To bestemødre hjalp til med maten. | Two grandmothers helped with the food. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bestemødrer | Barnehagen fikk besøk av tre bestemødrer. | Three grandmothers visited the kindergarten. |
| Flertall bestemt | bestemødrene | Begge bestemødrene kom på konserten. | Both grandmothers came to the concert. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Feminine and masculine inflections are accepted. Bestemødre and bestemødrer are both accepted indefinite plurals.
