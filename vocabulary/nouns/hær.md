## hær

**English:** army; a large group (figurative)

**Topics:** society, military

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En hær forsvarte grensen. | An army defended the border. |
| Entall ubestemt | hær | Landet bygde opp en hær etter krigen. | The country built up an army after the war. |
| Entall bestemt | hæren | Hæren rykket fram gjennom dalen. | The army advanced through the valley. |
| Flertall ubestemt | hærer | Flere hærer møttes på sletta. | Several armies met on the plain. |
| Flertall bestemt | hærene | Hærene inngikk en våpenhvile. | The armies agreed to a ceasefire. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** Also used figuratively for a large number or group, as in *en hel hær av frivillige* (“a whole army of volunteers”). The source's gender agrees with the dictionary. The snapshot's dictionary citation is mismatched; the corrected entry is cited below.
