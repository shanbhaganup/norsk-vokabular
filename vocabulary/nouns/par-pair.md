## par

**English:** pair; couple; a few

**Topics:** numbers, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et par kan bestå av to like ting. | A pair can consist of two matching things. |
| Entall ubestemt | par | Jeg kjøpte et par sko. | I bought a pair of shoes. |
| Entall bestemt | paret | Paret danset hele kvelden. | The couple danced all evening. |
| Flertall ubestemt | par | Vi bestilte tre par med ski. | We ordered three pairs of skis. |
| Flertall bestemt | para / parene | Para stilte seg ved døra. / Parene stilte seg ved døra. | The pairs stood by the door. / The pairs stood by the door. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary entry par 1 I includes matching items, couples, pairs in activities, and 'a few'. A separate golf sense is in par-golf.
