## klient

**English:** client; person receiving professional or public assistance

**Topics:** work, health, public services

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En klient snakket med advokaten. | A client spoke with the lawyer. |
| Entall ubestemt | klient | Advokaten møtte en ny klient i dag. | The lawyer met a new client today. |
| Entall bestemt | klienten | Klienten fikk råd om saken. | The client received advice about the case. |
| Flertall ubestemt | klienter | Kontoret hjelper klienter fra hele kommunen. | The office helps clients from across the municipality. |
| Flertall bestemt | klientene | Klientene venter på timeavtalen sin. | The clients are waiting for their appointments. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; examples authored.

**Notes:** Includes professional-services and public-assistance meanings; the dictionary also records a computing sense for a program accessing a server. This entry covers the person sense in the reference.
