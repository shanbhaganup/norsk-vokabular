## vitne

**English:** witness

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et vitne forklarte hva det hadde sett. | A witness explained what they had seen. |
| Entall ubestemt | vitne | Politiet ba et vitne bli igjen. | The police asked a witness to stay. |
| Entall bestemt | vitnet | Vitnet svarte rolig på spørsmålene. | The witness answered the questions calmly. |
| Flertall ubestemt | vitner | To vitner ventet utenfor rettssalen. | Two witnesses waited outside the courtroom. |
| Flertall bestemt | vitna | Vitna ble avhørt hver for seg. | The witnesses were questioned separately. |
| Flertall bestemt | vitnene | Vitnene fortalte den samme historien. | The witnesses told the same story. |

**Form status:** Forms checked against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The definite plural forms vitna and vitnene are both accepted.
