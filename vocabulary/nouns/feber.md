## feber

**English:** fever

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Han har en feber som kommer tilbake om kvelden. | He has a fever that returns in the evening. |
