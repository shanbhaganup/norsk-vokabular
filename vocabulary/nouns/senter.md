## senter

**English:** centre; center

**Topics:** places, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et senter kan tilby flere tjenester under ett tak. | A centre can offer several services under one roof. |
| Entall ubestemt | senter | Vi møttes på et senter i sentrum. | We met at a centre downtown. |
| Entall bestemt | senteret | Senteret stenger klokka åtte. | The centre closes at eight. |
| Flertall ubestemt | senter / sentre | Flere senter / sentre tilbyr helsehjelp. | Several centres offer health care. |
| Flertall bestemt | sentera / sentrene | Sentera / Sentrene ble åpnet for publikum. | The centres were opened to the public. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source mixes masculine senter 1 (a sports position) and neuter senter 2. This entry uses the neuter centre sense, dictionary 50965; forms from 50964 are not mixed in.
