# psykolog

**English:** psychologist; perceptive judge of character

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En psykolog arbeider på klinikken. | A psychologist works at the clinic. |
| Entall ubestemt | psykolog | Jeg bestilte time hos en psykolog. | I booked an appointment with a psychologist. |
| Entall bestemt | psykologen | Psykologen forklarte øvelsen rolig. | The psychologist explained the exercise calmly. |
| Flertall ubestemt | psykologer | Flere psykologer deltok på seminaret. | Several psychologists attended the seminar. |
| Flertall bestemt | psykologene | Psykologene møttes etter konferansen. | The psychologists met after the conference. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and paradigm checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists both a person professionally trained in psychology and, figuratively, a perceptive judge of character. The two senses share this paradigm.
