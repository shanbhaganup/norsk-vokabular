## jøde

**English:** Jew; a person belonging to the Jewish people or religion

**Topics:** society, religion

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | en jøde står ved inngangen. | A Jew is by the entrance. |
| Entall ubestemt | jøde | En jøde fortalte om familiens tradisjoner. | A Jewish person spoke about the family’s traditions. |
| Entall bestemt | jøden | Jøden fortalte om familiens tradisjoner. | The Jewish person spoke about the family’s traditions. |
| Flertall ubestemt | jøder | Jøder feirer høytider på ulike måter. | Jewish people celebrate holidays in different ways. |
| Flertall bestemt | jødene | Jødene i byen møttes til en markering. | The Jewish people in the town gathered for a commemoration. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and listed paradigms checked against Bokmålsordboka.

**Notes:** The reference entry’s English gloss “jew / jews” is normalized to respectful English usage. The dictionary distinguishes people belonging to the Jewish people and people who follow Judaism.
