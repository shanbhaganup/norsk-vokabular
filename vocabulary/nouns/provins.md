## provins

**English:** province; region; administrative or geological area

**Topics:** geography; society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En provins kan ha sitt eget parlament. | A province can have its own parliament. |
| Entall ubestemt | provins | De besøkte en provins nord i landet. | They visited a province in the north of the country. |
| Entall bestemt | provinsen | Provinsen ligger langt fra hovedstaden. | The province lies far from the capital. |
| Flertall ubestemt | provinser | Flere provinser ble rammet av tørken. | Several provinces were affected by the drought. |
| Flertall bestemt | provinsene | Provinsene fikk større selvstyre. | The provinces gained greater autonomy. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Source gender corrected to masculine. Dictionary senses include a region/district, an area far from the capital, an administrative territory, and a geological region; these uses are grouped under the broad source sense.
