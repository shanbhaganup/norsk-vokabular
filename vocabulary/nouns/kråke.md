## kråke

**English:** hooded crow

**Topics:** nature, animals

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Artikkel, hunkjønn | ei kråke | Ei kråke satt på gjerdet. | A hooded crow sat on the fence. |
| Artikkel, hankjønn | en kråke | En kråke satt på gjerdet. | A hooded crow sat on the fence. |
| Entall ubestemt | kråke | Jeg hørte kråke utenfor vinduet. | I heard a crow outside the window. |
| Entall bestemt, hunkjønn | kråka | Kråka fant mat ved veien. | The crow found food by the road. |
| Entall bestemt, hankjønn | kråken | Kråken fløy opp fra bakken. | The crow flew up from the ground. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kråker | Kråker samler seg ofte på jordet. | Crows often gather in the field. |
| Flertall bestemt | kråkene | Kråkene forsvant da bonden kom. | The crows disappeared when the farmer arrived. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The bird sense is used. The word also occurs figuratively as a compound-final element for an annoying person and in the expression *stupe kråke*; those are not separate senses of this entry. The reference spelling was encoding-corrupted; the canonical spelling is *kråke*.
