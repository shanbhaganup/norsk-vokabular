## bølge

**English:** wave

**Topics:** nature, communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei bølge slo mot land. / En bølge slo mot land. | A wave broke against the shore. / A wave broke against the shore. |
| Entall ubestemt | bølge | Vi så ei stor bølge fra stranda. | We saw a large wave from the beach. |
| Entall bestemt | bølga | Bølga skylte over brygga. | The wave washed over the pier. |
| Entall bestemt | bølgen | Bølgen traff båten. | The wave hit the boat. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bølger | Bølger slo mot klippene. | Waves broke against the cliffs. |
| Flertall bestemt | bølgene | Bølgene roet seg utover kvelden. | The waves calmed down as the evening went on. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** Feminine and masculine inflection are both accepted. The reference source labels “wave”; the examples use the literal water-wave sense.
