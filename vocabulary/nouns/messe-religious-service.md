## messe

**English:** Mass; religious service

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei messe begynner klokken elleve. | A Mass begins at eleven o’clock. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En messe begynner klokken elleve. | A Mass begins at eleven o’clock. |
| Entall ubestemt | messe | Familien gikk til messe før lunsj. | The family went to Mass before lunch. |
| Entall bestemt | messa | Messa ble holdt i den lille kirken. | The Mass was held in the small church. |
| Entall bestemt | messen | Messen ble holdt i den lille kirken. | The Mass was held in the small church. |
| Flertall ubestemt | messer | Kirken arrangerer messer på flere språk. | The church holds services in several languages. |
| Flertall bestemt | messene | Messene denne uken har ulike temaer. | The services this week have different themes. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene entry 1 genders and masculine plural forms checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** This is the religious noun `messe 1`, including a service with communion, a Protestant service and a musical work based on a fixed liturgical text. Both feminine and masculine singular are accepted; the dictionary gives plural forms under masculine. Kept separate from `messe` meaning trade fair and dining room.
