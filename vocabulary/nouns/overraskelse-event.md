## overraskelse

**English:** surprise; unexpected event or thing

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En overraskelse ventet henne hjemme. | A surprise was waiting for her at home. |
| Entall ubestemt | overraskelse | Vi hadde planlagt en overraskelse til bursdagen. | We had planned a surprise for the birthday. |
| Entall bestemt | overraskelsen | Overraskelsen var en tur til fjells. | The surprise was a trip to the mountains. |
| Flertall ubestemt | overraskelser | Forestillingen inneholdt mange overraskelser. | The performance contained many surprises. |
| Flertall bestemt | overraskelsene | Overraskelsene gjorde festen minneverdig. | The surprises made the party memorable. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary distinguishes this concrete sense from the feeling sense; see the separate overraskelse-feeling entry.
