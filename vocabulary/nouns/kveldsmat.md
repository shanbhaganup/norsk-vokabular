## kveldsmat

**English:** evening meal; supper

**Topics:** food, daily life

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kveldsmat med brød og ost passer godt etter turen. | An evening meal of bread and cheese is just right after the walk. |
| Entall ubestemt | kveldsmat | Vi spiser kveldsmat klokka åtte. | We eat supper at eight o’clock. |
| Entall bestemt | kveldsmaten | Kveldsmaten står på kjøkkenbordet. | The supper is on the kitchen table. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kveldsmater | Hotellet tilbyr to kveldsmater for gjestene. | The hotel offers two evening meals for the guests. |
| Flertall bestemt | kveldsmatene | Kveldsmatene ble servert etter møtet. | The evening meals were served after the meeting. |

**Form status:** Verified against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry for spelling, gender, and paradigm; original teaching examples authored for this project.

**Notes:** The ordinary meal sense is often used without an article, as in *spise kveldsmat*. The dictionary nevertheless lists count forms, illustrated here in a context where separate meals are meant. The reference gender tag was corrected to masculine, matching the dictionary.
