## bukse

**English:** trousers; pants

**Topics:** clothing, shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei bukse lå på stolen. | A pair of trousers lay on the chair. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bukse lå på stolen. | A pair of trousers lay on the chair. |
| Entall ubestemt | bukse | Hun kjøpte bukse til turen. | She bought trousers for the trip. |
| Entall bestemt | buksa | Buksa trenger en knapp. | The trousers need a button. |
| Entall bestemt | buksen | Buksen trenger en knapp. | The trousers need a button. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bukser | Butikken selger bukser i flere lengder. | The shop sells trousers in several lengths. |
| Flertall bestemt | buksene | Buksene ligger i skapet. | The trousers are in the cupboard. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine gender are accepted. Each definite singular alternative is illustrated; the plural forms are shared.
