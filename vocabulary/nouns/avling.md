## avling

**English:** crop, harvest

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Bonden fikk ei god avling. | The farmer got a good harvest. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | De ventet en stor avling i år. | They expected a large harvest this year. |
| Entall ubestemt | avling | De fikk dårlig avling etter tørken. | They had a poor harvest after the drought. |
| Entall bestemt | avlinga | Avlinga ble større enn ventet. | The harvest was bigger than expected. |
| Entall bestemt | avlingen | Frosten skadet avlingen. | The frost damaged the crop. |
| Flertall ubestemt | avlinger | Flere avlinger slo feil. | Several crops failed. |
| Flertall bestemt | avlingene | Regnet reddet avlingene. | The rain saved the crops. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine forms are accepted.
