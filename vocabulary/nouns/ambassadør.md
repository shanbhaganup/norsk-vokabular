## ambassadør

**English:** ambassador

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En ambassadør åpnet utstillingen. | An ambassador opened the exhibition. |
