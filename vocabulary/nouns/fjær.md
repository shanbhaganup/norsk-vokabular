## fjær

**English:** feather

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Form status:** Dictionary paradigm checked against the linked entry. Examples are original.

**Notes:** The reference links two homonyms: this entry is fjær meaning “feather” (136668), not fjær meaning “spring” (15128). The feather entry has zero plural; fjærer belongs to the spring sense and is excluded. Both feminine and masculine singular forms are listed: fjæra uses feminine gender and fjæren masculine gender.
