## park

**English:** park; landscaped green area

**Topics:** city

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En park gir rom for lek og hvile. | A park provides space for play and rest. |
| Entall ubestemt | park | Vi møttes i en park ved elva. | We met in a park by the river. |
| Entall bestemt | parken | Parken er full av blomster om sommeren. | The park is full of flowers in summer. |
| Flertall ubestemt | parker | Byen har flere parker. | The city has several parks. |
| Flertall bestemt | parkene | Parkene blir ryddet hver vår. | The parks are cleaned every spring. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary also uses park for attraction areas, activity/production areas, and protected natural areas; this entry follows the city green-space sense.
