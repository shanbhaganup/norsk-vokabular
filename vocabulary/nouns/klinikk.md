## klinikk

**English:** clinic; hospital or department for treatment; doctor's or dentist's practice

**Topics:** health, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Hun arbeider på en klinikk i sentrum. | She works at a clinic in the centre. |
| Entall ubestemt | klinikk | Byen åpnet en ny klinikk i fjor. | The town opened a new clinic last year. |
| Entall bestemt | klinikken | Klinikken tar imot pasienter hver dag. | The clinic receives patients every day. |
| Flertall ubestemt | klinikker | Flere klinikker tilbyr vaksinasjon. | Several clinics offer vaccination. |
| Flertall bestemt | klinikkene | Klinikkene samarbeider med sykehuset. | The clinics cooperate with the hospital. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; examples authored.

**Notes:** Covers a hospital or hospital department, a doctor's or dentist's practice, a veterinary treatment facility, and practical medical teaching. The reference's feminine category was corrected to masculine, matching the dictionary paradigm.
