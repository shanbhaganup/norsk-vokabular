## bordtennis

**English:** table tennis

**Topics:** sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bordtennis ble arrangert etter skolen. | A table-tennis match was held after school. |
| Entall ubestemt | bordtennis | De spiller bordtennis hver tirsdag. | They play table tennis every Tuesday. |
| Entall bestemt | bordtennisen | Bordtennisen begynner klokken seks. | The table-tennis match begins at six. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bordtenniser | Klubben arrangerer to bordtenniser i helgen. | The club is holding two table-tennis matches this weekend. |
| Flertall bestemt | bordtennisene | Bordtennisene ble spilt i gymsalen. | The table-tennis matches were played in the gym. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The plural forms refer to separate games or matches of table tennis.
