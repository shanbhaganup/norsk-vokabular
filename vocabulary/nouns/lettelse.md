## lettelse

**English:** relief

**Topics:** feelings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En lettelse kom over henne da prøven var ferdig. | A sense of relief came over her when the test was over. |
| Entall ubestemt | lettelse | Han kjente en lettelse etter telefonen. | He felt relief after the phone call. |
| Entall bestemt | lettelsen | Lettelsen var tydelig i ansiktet hennes. | The relief was clear on her face. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lettelser | Flere lettelser fulgte etter at saken var løst. | Several moments of relief followed once the matter was settled. |
| Flertall bestemt | lettelsene | Lettelsene kom gradvis gjennom uka. | The moments of relief came gradually during the week. |

**Notes:** Plural refers to separate instances of relief.
