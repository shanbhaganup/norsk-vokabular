## knapp

**English:** button; knob; key; on-screen icon

**Topics:** clothing, technology, everyday objects

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En knapp på jakka var løs. | A button on the jacket was loose. |
| Entall ubestemt | knapp | Jeg sydde fast en knapp før møtet. | I sewed on a button before the meeting. |
| Entall bestemt | knappen | Trykk på knappen for å starte maskinen. | Press the button to start the machine. |
| Flertall ubestemt | knapper | Tastaturet har mange små knapper. | The keyboard has many small keys. |
| Flertall bestemt | knappene | Knappene på skjermen er tydelig merket. | The buttons on the screen are clearly labelled. |

**Form status:** Noun forms verified; examples authored.

**Notes:** This is the noun only. Bokmålsordboka also lists an adjective *knapp* (?scarce, limited, terse?), a separate word type. The noun includes clothing fasteners, knobs, activating controls, keyboard keys and on-screen icons.
