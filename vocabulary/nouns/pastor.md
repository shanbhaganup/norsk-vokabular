## pastor

**English:** pastor; Protestant minister

**Topics:** religion

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En pastor besøkte menigheten på søndag. | A pastor visited the congregation on Sunday. |
| Entall ubestemt | pastor | Menigheten ansatte en pastor i fjor. | The congregation hired a pastor last year. |
| Entall bestemt | pastoren | Pastoren ledet gudstjenesten. | The pastor led the service. |
| Flertall ubestemt | pastorer | Flere pastorer deltok på kurset. | Several pastors attended the course. |
| Flertall bestemt | pastorene | Pastorene møttes etter gudstjenesten. | The pastors met after the service. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary lists an alternate stress pattern in the plural pronunciation; the spelling and inflection remain pastorer / pastorene.
