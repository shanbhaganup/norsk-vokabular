## cowboy

**English:** cowboy (reckless or rule-breaking person)

**Topics:** people, informal

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En cowboy ignorerte sikkerhetsreglene. | A rule-breaking person ignored the safety rules. |
| Entall ubestemt | cowboy | Han oppførte seg som cowboy på jobben. | He acted like a cowboy at work. |
| Entall bestemt | cowboyen | Cowboyen tok snarveier uten å spørre. | The rule-breaker took shortcuts without asking. |
| Flertall ubestemt | cowboyer | Noen cowboyer fulgte ikke rutinene. | Some rule-breakers did not follow the procedures. |
| Flertall bestemt | cowboyene | Cowboyene ble advart av lederen. | The rule-breakers were warned by the manager. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers the figurative sense of someone who ignores norms and rules (dictionary sense 2), distinct from a ranch hand.
