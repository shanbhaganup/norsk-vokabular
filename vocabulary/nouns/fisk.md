## fisk

**English:** fish; fish as food

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En fisk svømmer i bukta. | A fish is swimming in the bay. |
| Entall ubestemt | fisk | En fisk svømte forbi båten. | A fish swam past the boat. |
| Entall bestemt | fisken | Fisken på tallerkenen var fersk. | The fish on the plate was fresh. |
| Flertall ubestemt | fisk / fisker | Vi så mye fisk i fjorden. / Vi så mange fisker i fjorden. | We saw a lot of fish in the fjord. / We saw many fish in the fjord. |
| Flertall bestemt | fiskene | Fiskene i akvariet fikk mat. | The fish in the aquarium were fed. |
