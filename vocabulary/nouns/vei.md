## vei

**English:** road; way

**Topics:** travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En vei går til stasjonen. | A road leads to the station. |
| Entall ubestemt | vei | Vi tok en annen vei hjem. | We took another way home. |
| Entall bestemt | veien | Veien til stasjonen er kort. | The road to the station is short. |
| Flertall ubestemt | veier | Flere veier fører til sentrum. | Several roads lead to the city centre. |
| Flertall bestemt | veiene | Veiene er glatte etter regnet. | The roads are slippery after the rain. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry; teaching examples authored for this project.

**Notes:** The source gloss “way” is broad; this entry covers a route or road. The spelling veg is an accepted Bokmål variant and has its own reference record. Source spelling and forms were checked against the reference snapshot.
