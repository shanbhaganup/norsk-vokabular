## panikk

**English:** panic

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En panikk kan spre seg raskt i en folkemengde. | Panic can spread quickly through a crowd. |
| Entall ubestemt | panikk | Det brøt ut panikk da alarmen gikk. | Panic broke out when the alarm sounded. |
| Entall bestemt | panikken | Panikken avtok da folk fikk informasjon. | The panic subsided when people received information. |
| Flertall ubestemt | panikker | Gjentatte panikker oppsto under evakueringen. | Repeated panics arose during the evacuation. |
| Flertall bestemt | panikkene | Panikkene ble omtalt i rapporten. | The panic episodes were described in the report. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** The noun is commonly used as a mass noun; dictionary-listed plurals are rare and refer to separate episodes.
