## bud

**English:** bid; offer (at auction)

**Topics:** shopping, money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et bud kom inn like før fristen. | A bid came in just before the deadline. |
| Entall ubestemt | bud | Hun la inn bud på leiligheten. | She placed a bid on the apartment. |
| Entall bestemt | budet | Budet var høyere enn prisantydningen. | The bid was higher than the asking price. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bud | Flere bud kom inn samme dag. | Several bids came in on the same day. |
| Flertall bestemt | buda | Buda ble vurdert av selgeren. | The bids were considered by the seller. |
| Flertall bestemt | budene | Budene ble vurdert av selgeren. | The bids were considered by the seller. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers an offer of a particular price, especially at an auction, distinct from bud meaning a message or messenger.
