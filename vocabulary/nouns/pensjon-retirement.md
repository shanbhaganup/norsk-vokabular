## pensjon

**English:** pension; retirement benefit

**Topics:** money, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En pensjon gir inntekt etter arbeidslivet. | A pension provides income after working life. |
| Entall ubestemt | pensjon | Hun får pensjon fra neste måned. | She will receive a pension starting next month. |
| Entall bestemt | pensjonen | Pensjonen hennes blir utbetalt hver måned. | Her pension is paid every month. |
| Flertall ubestemt | pensjoner | Ulike pensjoner følger ulike regler. | Different pensions follow different rules. |
| Flertall bestemt | pensjonene | Pensjonene ble justert ved nyttår. | The pensions were adjusted at New Year. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary sense 1 is a regular payment after retirement, disability or the death of a provider. The lodging-and-board sense has a separate entry.
