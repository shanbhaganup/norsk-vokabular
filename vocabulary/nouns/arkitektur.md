## arkitektur

**English:** architecture

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Byen har en arkitektur som er preget av trehus. | The city has an architectural style shaped by its wooden houses. |
| Entall ubestemt | arkitektur | Hun studerer arkitektur i Oslo. | She studies architecture in Oslo. |
| Entall bestemt | arkitekturen | Arkitekturen i bydelen har endret seg. | The architecture of the neighbourhood has changed. |
| Flertall ubestemt | arkitekturer | Teamet vurderte to arkitekturer for det nye datasystemet. | The team considered two architectures for the new computer system. |
| Flertall bestemt | arkitekturene | De to arkitekturene har ulike krav til lagring. | The two architectures have different storage requirements. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Singular commonly denotes architecture as a field or as a style. Plurals can denote distinct designs, including technical system architectures.
