## promenade

**English:** promenade; leisurely walk; walkway

**Topics:** city; leisure

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En promenade langs elva passer godt på en mild kveld. | A promenade along the river is pleasant on a mild evening. |
| Entall ubestemt | promenade | Vi tok en promenade etter middagen. | We took a leisurely walk after dinner. |
| Entall bestemt | promenaden | Promenaden går langs hele strandlinja. | The promenade runs along the entire shoreline. |
| Flertall ubestemt | promenader | Flere promenader fører ned til vannet. | Several promenades lead down to the water. |
| Flertall bestemt | promenadene | Promenadene er populære blant både fastboende og turister. | The promenades are popular with both residents and tourists. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** The dictionary has two related senses: a leisurely walk without a fixed destination and a walking path or promenade.
