## birdie

**English:** birdie

**Topics:** sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Hun fikk en birdie på det siste hullet. | She made a birdie on the last hole. |
| Entall ubestemt | birdie | Spilleren fikk birdie på hull tre. | The player made a birdie on hole three. |
| Entall bestemt | birdien | Birdien sikret henne ledelsen. | The birdie gave her the lead. |
| Flertall ubestemt | birdier | Han fikk to birdier på runden. | He made two birdies during the round. |
| Flertall bestemt | birdiene | Birdiene kom på de vanskeligste hullene. | The birdies came on the most difficult holes. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** In golf, a birdie is a score of one stroke under par on a hole.
