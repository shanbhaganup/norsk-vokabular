## plass

**English:** place; location; space

**Topics:** places

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En plass ligger mellom de gamle husene. | A square lies between the old buildings. |
| Entall ubestemt | plass | Vi fant en plass ved vinduet. | We found a place by the window. |
| Entall bestemt | plassen | Plassen var full av folk. | The square was full of people. |
| Flertall ubestemt | plasser | Det finnes flere plasser langs elva. | There are several places along the river. |
| Flertall bestemt | plassene | Plassene i salen var opptatt. | The seats in the hall were occupied. |
