## antrekk

**English:** outfit

**Topics:** shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Hun valgte et enkelt antrekk. | She chose a simple outfit. |
| Entall ubestemt | antrekk | Jeg trenger et antrekk til festen. | I need an outfit for the party. |
| Entall bestemt | antrekket | Antrekket passet godt til anledningen. | The outfit suited the occasion. |
| Flertall ubestemt | antrekk | Han prøvde tre antrekk før han bestemte seg. | He tried on three outfits before deciding. |
| Flertall bestemt | antrekka | Antrekka hang klare til forestillingen. | The outfits were hanging ready for the performance. |
| Flertall bestemt | antrekkene | Vi pakket antrekkene i hver sin pose. | We packed the outfits in separate bags. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both definite plural endings are accepted.
