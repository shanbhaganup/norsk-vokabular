## avkom

**English:** offspring

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | De fulgte et avkom fra hvert kull. | They monitored one offspring from each litter. |
| Entall ubestemt | avkom | Hunnen fikk avkom om våren. | The female produced offspring in spring. |
| Entall bestemt | avkommet | Avkommet holdt seg nær moren. | The offspring stayed close to its mother. |
| Flertall ubestemt | avkom | Forskerne fulgte tre avkom fra samme kull. | The researchers tracked three offspring from the same litter. |
| Flertall bestemt | avkomma | Avkomma fra de to kullene ble veid. | The offspring from the two litters were weighed. |
| Flertall bestemt | avkommene | Avkommene vokste opp i hvert sitt område. | The offspring grew up in separate areas. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Avkom can refer to one offspring or several. Both definite plural endings are accepted.
