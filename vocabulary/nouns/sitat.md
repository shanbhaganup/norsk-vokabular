## sitat

**English:** quotation

**Topics:** writing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et sitat bør gjengis nøyaktig. | A quotation should be reproduced accurately. |
| Entall ubestemt | sitat | Hun brukte et sitat fra romanen. | She used a quotation from the novel. |
| Entall bestemt | sitatet | Sitatet sto øverst i artikkelen. | The quotation was at the top of the article. |
| Flertall ubestemt | sitat / sitater | Sitat / Sitater fra intervjuet ble tatt med i rapporten. | Quotations from the interview were included in the report. |
| Flertall bestemt | sitata / sitatene | Sitata / Sitatene ble kontrollert mot opptaket. | The quotations were checked against the recording. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both indefinite plural variants and both definite plural variants are accepted.
