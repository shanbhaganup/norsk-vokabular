## plate

**English:** record; disc

**Topics:** music

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei plate / En plate kan inneholde mange sanger. | A record can contain many songs. |
| Entall ubestemt | plate | Hun kjøpte ei plate med jazzmusikk. | She bought a jazz record. |
| Entall bestemt | plata / platen | Plata / Platen ble spilt på den gamle platespilleren. | The record was played on the old turntable. |
| Flertall ubestemt | plater | Bandet ga ut to plater i fjor. | The band released two records last year. |
| Flertall bestemt | platene | Platene står i hylla ved vinduet. | The records are on the shelf by the window. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The entry uses the disc/record sense; both feminine and masculine forms are accepted.
