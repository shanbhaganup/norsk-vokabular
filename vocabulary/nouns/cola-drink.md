## cola

**English:** cola

**Topics:** food, drinks

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En cola står i kjøleskapet. | A cola is in the fridge. |
| Entall ubestemt | cola | De kjøpte cola til pikniken. | They bought cola for the picnic. |
| Entall bestemt | colaen | Colaen ble servert kald. | The cola was served cold. |
