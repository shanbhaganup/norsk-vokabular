## kompleks

**English:** complex; group of related things or buildings; inferiority complex

**Topics:** buildings, psychology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et kompleks ligger ved stasjonen. | A complex is located by the station. |
| Entall ubestemt | kompleks | Vi besøkte et kompleks av kontorer. | We visited an office complex. |
| Entall bestemt | komplekset | Komplekset har flere innganger. | The complex has several entrances. |
| Flertall ubestemt | komplekser | Nye komplekser bygges utenfor sentrum. | New complexes are being built outside the center. |
| Flertall bestemt | kompleksene | Kompleksene ligger nær hverandre. | The complexes are close to one another. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka noun and paradigm checked; examples are original.

**Notes:** Separate noun homograph from adjective kompleks (“made up of many parts”). Dictionary noun senses include a related whole, a group of buildings or rooms, and an inferiority complex.
