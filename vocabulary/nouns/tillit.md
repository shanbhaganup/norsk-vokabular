## tillit

**English:** trust

**Topics:** feelings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei tillit mellom kollegene vokste gradvis. | Trust between the colleagues grew gradually. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En tillit mellom kollegene vokste gradvis. | Trust between the colleagues grew gradually. |
| Entall ubestemt | tillit | Samarbeidet bygger på tillit. | The collaboration is built on trust. |
| Entall bestemt | tillita | Tillita mellom partene ble styrket. | The trust between the parties was strengthened. |
| Entall bestemt | tilliten | Tilliten mellom partene ble styrket. | The trust between the parties was strengthened. |
| Flertall ubestemt | tilliter | Ulike tilliter kan oppstå i et tett samarbeid. | Different forms of trust can arise in a close collaboration. |
| Flertall bestemt | tillitene | Tillitene mellom gruppene utviklet seg ulikt. | The relationships of trust between the groups developed differently. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** Både feminin og maskulin bøying er oppført. Flertall er sjeldent og viser til ulike former for tillitsforhold.
