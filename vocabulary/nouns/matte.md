## matte

**English:** mathematics (colloquial short form)

**Topics:** education

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En matte kan være krevende uten øvelse. | Maths can be challenging without practice. |
| Entall ubestemt | matte | Jeg har matte etter lunsj. | I have maths after lunch. |
| Entall bestemt | matten | Matten begynner klokka ett. | Maths starts at one. |
| Flertall ubestemt | matter | Flere matter står på timeplanen denne uka. | Several maths classes are on the timetable this week. |
| Flertall bestemt | mattene | Mattene på skolen begynner etter sommerferien. | The maths courses at the school begin after the summer holiday. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** For the school subject, Bokmålsordboka lists masculine inflection; the source conflates this with the separate feminine noun for a floor covering. The dictionary gives the full masculine paradigm, including plural matter / mattene. Cross-POS: matte also occurs as an adjective in the reference; that is distinct.
