## bud

**English:** messenger; courier

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et bud kom med pakken før lunsj. | A courier brought the package before lunch. |
| Entall ubestemt | bud | Sykehuset sendte bud etter legen. | The hospital sent for the doctor. |
| Entall bestemt | budet | Budet leverte brevet personlig. | The messenger delivered the letter in person. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bud | Flere bud arbeidet for firmaet. | Several couriers worked for the company. |
| Flertall bestemt | buda | Buda leverte pakkene på morgenen. | The couriers delivered the parcels in the morning. |
| Flertall bestemt | budene | Budene leverte pakkene på morgenen. | The couriers delivered the parcels in the morning. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers a person who carries letters, goods or other messages; it is distinct from bud meaning a message or a price offer.
