## avdeling

**English:** department, section

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Sykehuset åpnet ei ny avdeling. | The hospital opened a new department. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Hun leder en avdeling med ti ansatte. | She runs a department with ten employees. |
| Entall ubestemt | avdeling | Han ble overført til en annen avdeling. | He was transferred to another department. |
| Entall bestemt | avdelinga | Avdelinga flyttet til andre etasje. | The department moved to the second floor. |
| Entall bestemt | avdelingen | Avdelingen stenger klokka fire. | The department closes at four. |
| Flertall ubestemt | avdelinger | Firmaet har tre avdelinger i byen. | The company has three branches in the city. |
| Flertall bestemt | avdelingene | Avdelingene deler møterom. | The departments share meeting rooms. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted.
