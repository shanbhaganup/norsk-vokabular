## prosent

**English:** percent; percentage rate

**Topics:** numbers; finance

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En prosent tilsvarer én hundredel. | One percent is one hundredth. |
| Entall ubestemt | prosent | Banken tilbyr tre prosent rente. | The bank offers three percent interest. |
| Entall bestemt | prosenten | Prosenten ble rundet av til nærmeste hele tall. | The percentage was rounded to the nearest whole number. |
| Flertall ubestemt | prosent | Det ble bare noen prosent forskjell mellom målingene. | There was only a few percent difference between the measurements. |
| Flertall ubestemt | prosenter | Flere prosenter ble lagt til grunn i beregningen. | Several percentage rates were used in the calculation. |
| Flertall bestemt | prosentene | Prosentene i tabellen er avrundet. | The percentages in the table are rounded. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Dictionary homonym 1: a rate or number expressed per hundred. Source forms also link dictionary entry 2, meaning a specific percentage share, which is in prosent-share.md.
