## biologi

**English:** biology

**Topics:** school, science

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Han studerer biologi på universitetet. | He studies biology at the university. |
| Entall ubestemt | biologi | Biologi handler om levende organismer. | Biology is about living organisms. |
| Entall bestemt | biologien | Biologien på dette kurset er krevende. | The biology in this course is demanding. |
| Flertall ubestemt | biologier | De to biologiene beskriver livets utvikling på ulike måter. | The book compares different biological systems from the same period. |
| Flertall bestemt | biologiene | De to biologiene forklarer arten på forskjellige måter. | The biological systems in the report describe different academic approaches. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Biology is usually a mass noun when naming the school subject; the plural forms can refer to distinct biological systems or approaches and are less common.
