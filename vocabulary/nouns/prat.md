## prat

**English:** talk; conversation; chatter

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En prat kan gjøre dagen bedre. | A conversation can make the day better. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et prat på ti minutter kan være nyttig. | A ten-minute conversation can be useful. |
| Entall ubestemt | prat | Vi tok en prat etter møtet. | We had a chat after the meeting. |
| Entall bestemt | praten | Praten varte i en time. | The conversation lasted an hour. |
| Entall bestemt | pratet | Pratet gikk om været og reisen. | The talk was about the weather and the trip. |
| Flertall ubestemt | prater | De hadde flere prater i løpet av uka. | They had several conversations during the week. |
| Flertall ubestemt | prat | Det ble mye prat i pausen. | There was a lot of talk during the break. |
| Flertall bestemt | pratene | Pratene med naboen var hyggelige. | The conversations with the neighbor were pleasant. |
| Flertall bestemt | prata | Prata fortsatte til langt på kveld. | The talk continued late into the evening. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Ordbøkene accepts masculine and neuter gender. Masculine forms are en prat, praten, prater, pratene; neuter forms are et prat, pratet, prat, prata. Each gender-specific alternative is illustrated.
