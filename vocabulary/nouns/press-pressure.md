## press

**English:** pressure; strain; stress

**Topics:** society; health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et press kan påvirke avgjørelsen. | Pressure can affect the decision. |
| Entall ubestemt | press | Hun opplevde et press fra omgivelsene. | She experienced pressure from those around her. |
| Entall bestemt | presset | Presset på familien økte. | The pressure on the family increased. |
| Flertall ubestemt | press | Flere press må håndteres samtidig. | Several pressures must be handled at the same time. |
| Flertall bestemt | pressa | Pressa mot gruppa ble sterkere. | The pressure on the group became stronger. |
| Flertall bestemt | pressene | Pressene i markedet påvirket prisene. | The pressures in the market affected prices. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Distinct neuter homonym 2 means physical pressure, strain or external pressure. The source also gives masculine press “crease”; that sense is in press-fold.md.
