## budbringer

**English:** messenger; spokesperson

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En budbringer kom med nyheten. | A messenger brought the news. |
| Entall ubestemt | budbringer | Hun ble valgt som budbringer for gruppen. | She was chosen as the group's messenger. |
| Entall bestemt | budbringeren | Budbringeren leverte brevet til mottakeren. | The messenger delivered the letter to the recipient. |
| Flertall ubestemt | budbringere | Budbringere reiste mellom landsbyene. | Messengers travelled between the villages. |
| Flertall bestemt | budbringerne | Budbringerne kom fram før kvelden. | The messengers arrived before evening. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** A person who carries a message or speaks on behalf of a cause.
