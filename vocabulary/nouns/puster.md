## puster

**English:** bellows

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En puster kan gi luft til peisen. | A pair of bellows can supply air to the fireplace. |
| Entall ubestemt | puster | Vi fant en puster på loftet. | We found a pair of bellows in the attic. |
| Entall bestemt | pusteren | Pusteren lå ved vedkurven. | The bellows were next to the wood basket. |
| Flertall ubestemt | pustere | Gamle pustere ble stilt ut på museet. | Old bellows were displayed at the museum. |
| Flertall bestemt | pusterne | Pusterne var laget av lær og tre. | The bellows were made of leather and wood. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source English gloss “breathing” does not match the Norwegian noun puster, which means bellows. The breathing noun is pust/pusting; the noun puster is distinct from the verb form puster.
