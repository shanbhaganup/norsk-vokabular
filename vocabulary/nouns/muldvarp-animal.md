## muldvarp

**English:** mole (burrowing mammal)

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En muldvarp gravde en gang under plenen. | A mole dug a tunnel under the lawn. |
| Entall ubestemt | muldvarp | En muldvarp lever mesteparten av livet under jorda. | A mole lives most of its life underground. |
| Entall bestemt | muldvarpen | Muldvarpen etterlot små jordhauger. | The mole left small mounds of soil. |
| Flertall ubestemt | muldvarper | Muldvarper graver ganger i bakken. | Moles dig tunnels in the ground. |
| Flertall bestemt | muldvarpene | Muldvarpene holder seg skjult under bakken. | The moles stay hidden underground. |
| Variant spelling, indefinite singular | moldvarp | En moldvarp graver ganger i jorda. | A mole digs tunnels in the soil. |
| Variant spelling, definite singular | moldvarpen | Moldvarpen kom opp ved gjerdet. | The mole came up by the fence. |
| Variant spelling, indefinite plural | moldvarper | Moldvarper lever under bakken. | Moles live underground. |
| Variant spelling, definite plural | moldvarpene | Moldvarpene laget hauger i plenen. | The moles made mounds in the lawn. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original teaching examples supplied for every listed form.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka 38914 accepts both muldvarp and moldvarp, each with the same paradigm. The entry covers the animal sense; the dictionary also records a figurative “infiltrator” sense in the companion entry.
