## minister

**English:** minister; government minister

**Topics:** society

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Entall ubestemt | minister | Velgerne møtte en minister på torget. | Voters met a minister in the square. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword and all plural alternatives checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists `ministere`, `ministre` and `ministrer` as indefinite plural alternatives, and `ministerne` / `ministrene` as definite plural alternatives. Some alternatives are less common; each source-listed form is illustrated. The main sense is a member of a government; the dictionary also has a historical diplomatic sense.
