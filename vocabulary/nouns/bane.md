## bane

**English:** track, playing field

**Topics:** sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Kommunen anla en bane ved skolen. | The municipality built a playing field by the school. |
| Entall ubestemt | bane | Lagene trenger en bane med flomlys. | The teams need a floodlit playing field. |
| Entall bestemt | banen | Spillerne gikk ut på banen. | The players went out onto the pitch. |
| Flertall ubestemt | baner | Klubben har to baner til trening. | The club has two practice pitches. |
| Flertall bestemt | banene | Banene ble stengt etter snøfallet. | The pitches were closed after the snowfall. |

**Notes:** The examples focus on sports facilities.
