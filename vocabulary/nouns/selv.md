## selv

**English:** self; identity

**Topics:** people

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et selv kan formes gjennom erfaringer. | A self can be shaped through experience. |
| Entall ubestemt | selv | Hun ville finne et selv som passet bedre til livet hennes. | She wanted to find a self that fit her life better. |
| Entall bestemt | selvet | Selvet hennes endret seg gjennom årene. | Her sense of self changed over the years. |
| Flertall ubestemt | selv | Ulike selv kan komme fram i ulike situasjoner. | Different selves can emerge in different situations. |
| Flertall bestemt | selva / selvene | Selva / Selvene i romanen ble skildret fra flere sider. | The selves in the novel were portrayed from several sides. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The noun selv means one’s own self or identity; it is distinct from the reflexive pronoun/adjective selv. Plural forms refer to identities or selves.
