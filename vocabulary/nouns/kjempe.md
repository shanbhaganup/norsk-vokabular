## kjempe

**English:** giant; exceptional person

**Topics:** people

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | En kjempe voktet porten. | A giant guarded the gate. |
| Entall ubestemt | kjempe | Vi møtte en kjempe på fjellstien. | We met a giant on the mountain trail. |
| Entall bestemt | kjempa / kjempen | Kjempa bar en diger stein. / Kjempen løftet porten alene. | The giant carried a huge stone. / The giant lifted the gate alone. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kjemper | Kjemper fra flere land dukket opp i fortellingen. | Giants from several countries appeared in the story. |
| Flertall bestemt | kjempene | Kjempene gikk over sletta ved solnedgang. | The giants crossed the plain at sunset. |

**Form status:** Headword and paradigm reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Noun meaning a giant or an exceptionally strong or capable person; distinct from the verb kjempe and adjectival prefix use.
