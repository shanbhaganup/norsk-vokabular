## konsert

**English:** concert

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En konsert finner sted i parken i kveld. | A concert is taking place in the park tonight. |
| Entall ubestemt | konsert | Vi skal på konsert etter jobb. | We are going to a concert after work. |
| Entall bestemt | konserten | Konserten begynner klokka åtte. | The concert begins at eight. |
| Flertall ubestemt | konserter | Bandet spiller konserter i flere byer. | The band is playing concerts in several cities. |
| Flertall bestemt | konsertene | Konsertene ble utsolgt på kort tid. | The concerts sold out quickly. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The source gloss refers to a public musical performance. The dictionary also uses konsert for a musical work for solo instrument and orchestra. Source gender was corrected in the reference snapshot to match the dictionary.
