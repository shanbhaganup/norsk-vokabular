## blits

**English:** flash; camera flash

**Topics:** photography

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En blits kan lyse opp et mørkt rom. | A flash can light up a dark room. |
| Entall ubestemt | blits | Fotografen brukte blits innendørs. | The photographer used a flash indoors. |
| Entall bestemt | blitsen | Blitsen gikk av akkurat da hun smilte. | The flash went off just as she smiled. |
| Flertall ubestemt | blitser | Små blitser er montert rundt kameraet. | Small flashes are mounted around the camera. |
| Flertall bestemt | blitsene | Blitsene på scenen blinket i takt med musikken. | The flashes on stage blinked in time with the music. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The dictionary also uses blits for a flash of light generally and for a historical air raid; examples here teach the camera sense.
