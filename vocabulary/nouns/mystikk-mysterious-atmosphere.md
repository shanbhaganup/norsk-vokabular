## mystikk

**English:** mystery; enigmatic atmosphere

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mystikk omga det gamle huset. | An air of mystery surrounded the old house. |
| Entall ubestemt | mystikk | Det hviler mystikk over den gamle bygningen. | An air of mystery hangs over the old building. |
| Entall bestemt | mystikken | Mystikken gjorde historien spennende. | The mystery made the story exciting. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mystikker | Flere mystikker preget fortellingen. | Several mysteries shaped the story. |
| Flertall bestemt | mystikkene | Mystikkene rundt forsvinningen ble aldri løst. | The mysteries surrounding the disappearance were never solved. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original teaching examples authored for the labelled sense and forms.

**Notes:** Sense 2 of Bokmålsordboka 39773. This is distinct from mysticism as a religious or philosophical practice.
