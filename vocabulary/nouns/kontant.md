## kontant

**English:** cash

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kontantbetaling er mulig her. | A cash payment is possible here. |
| Entall ubestemt | kontant | Han hadde én kontant i lommeboka. | He had one cash note in his wallet. |
| Entall bestemt | kontanten | Kontanten lå igjen på disken. | The cash note had been left on the counter. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kontanter | Mange betaler fortsatt med kontanter. | Many people still pay with cash. |
| Flertall bestemt | kontantene | Kontantene ble lagt i kassa. | The cash was put in the till. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The noun is used mostly in the plural for ready money. Bokmålsordboka also has kontant as an adjective, which is a separate word type. The reference source listed the inflected form kontanter as a source form; the canonical lemma is kontant.
