## lapp

**English:** patch

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gender/article | en | En lapp dekker hullet i buksa. | A patch covers the hole in the trousers. |
| Singular indefinite | lapp | Hun sydde en lapp på jakka. | She sewed a patch onto the jacket. |
| Singular definite | lappen | Lappen holdt stoffet sammen. | The patch held the fabric together. |
| Plural indefinite | lapper | Vi bruker lapper til å reparere klær. | We use patches to repair clothes. |
| Plural definite | lappene | Lappene sitter godt fast. | The patches are firmly attached. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** This entry covers the patch sense; other senses of *lapp* have separate meanings and may need separate entries.
