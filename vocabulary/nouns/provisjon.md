## provisjon

**English:** commission (payment for arranging a sale)

**Topics:** money; business

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En provisjon kan være en prosent av salget. | A commission can be a percentage of the sale. |
| Entall ubestemt | provisjon | Megleren fikk provisjon for handelen. | The broker received commission for the transaction. |
| Entall bestemt | provisjonen | Provisjonen ble utbetalt neste måned. | The commission was paid the following month. |
| Flertall ubestemt | provisjoner | Flere provisjoner ble registrert i systemet. | Several commissions were recorded in the system. |
| Flertall bestemt | provisjonene | Provisjonene ble beregnet på nytt. | The commissions were recalculated. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Source gender corrected to masculine. Ordbøkene defines this as percentage-based remuneration received by an intermediary for completing a transaction.
