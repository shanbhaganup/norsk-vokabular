## frokost

**English:** breakfast

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En frokost med egg holder meg mett lenge. | A breakfast with eggs keeps me full for a long time. |
| Entall ubestemt | frokost | Vi spiser frokost før klokka åtte. | We eat breakfast before eight o’clock. |
| Entall bestemt | frokosten | Frokosten står klar på bordet. | Breakfast is ready on the table. |
| Flertall ubestemt | frokoster | Hotellet serverer frokoster hele dagen. | The hotel serves breakfasts all day. |
| Flertall bestemt | frokostene | Frokostene på hotellet var inkludert i prisen. | The breakfasts at the hotel were included in the price. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Usually a meal noun; plural refers to separate breakfasts. The dictionary also uses frokost for the time of day when breakfast is eaten.
