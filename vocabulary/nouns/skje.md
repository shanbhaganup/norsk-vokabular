## skje

**English:** spoon

**Topics:** food, home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei skje / En skje kan røre i teen. | A spoon can stir the tea. |
| Entall ubestemt | skje | Hun tok ei skje fra skuffen. | She took a spoon from the drawer. |
| Entall bestemt | skjea / skjeen | Skjea / Skjeen lå ved siden av koppen. | The spoon was beside the cup. |
| Flertall ubestemt | skjeer | Skjeer av tre tåler ikke oppvaskmaskin. | Wooden spoons do not tolerate the dishwasher. |
| Flertall bestemt | skjeene | Skjeene ble lagt i bestikkskuffen. | The spoons were put in the cutlery drawer. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine forms are accepted. Cross-POS: skje is also a verb meaning “happen”; that is a separate entry.
