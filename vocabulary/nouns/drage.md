## drage

**English:** dragon

**Topics:** mythology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En drage. | A dragon. |
| Entall ubestemt | drage | En drage voktet skatten. | A dragon guarded the treasure. |
| Entall bestemt | dragen | Dragen fløy over fjellet. | The dragon flew over the mountain. |
