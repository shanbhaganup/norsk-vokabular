## bank

**English:** bank, financial institution

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Det ligger en bank ved torget. | There’s a bank by the square. |
| Entall ubestemt | bank | Hun byttet til en annen bank. | She switched to a different bank. |
| Entall bestemt | banken | Banken sendte henne et nytt kort. | The bank sent her a new card. |
| Flertall ubestemt | banker | Tre banker har kontor i sentrum. | Three banks have offices downtown. |
| Flertall bestemt | bankene | Han sammenlignet rentene hos bankene. | He compared the interest rates offered by the banks. |

**Notes:** The reference combines the forms of several homographs. This entry uses the masculine paradigm of the financial institution.
