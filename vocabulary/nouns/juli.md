## juli

**English:** July

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En juli med mye regn gjorde høyonna krevende. | A rainy July made the hay harvest challenging. |
| Entall ubestemt | juli | De giftet seg en juli for ti år siden. | They got married one July ten years ago. |
| Entall bestemt | julien | Julien var varm og tårr. | July was warm and dry. |
| Flertall ubestemt | julier | Flere julier har vårt uvanlig varme. | Several Julys have been unusually warm. |
| Flertall bestemt | juliene | Juliene i måleserien viser en tydelig trend. | The Julys in the data series show a clear trend. |

**Form status:** Headword and paradigm reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Plural forms refer to instances of the month across different years or calendars; ordinary dates use the month name without an article.
