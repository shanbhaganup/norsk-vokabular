## kunnskap

**English:** knowledge

**Topics:** school, learning

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Artikkel, hankjønn | en kunnskap | En kunnskap jeg fikk på kurset, har vært nyttig. | A piece of knowledge I gained in the course has been useful. |
| Entall ubestemt | kunnskap | Hun har kunnskap om lokal historie. | She has knowledge of local history. |
| Entall bestemt | kunnskapen | Kunnskapen ble delt med hele klassen. | The knowledge was shared with the whole class. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kunnskaper | Elevene viste gode kunnskaper i matematikk. | The pupils showed good knowledge of mathematics. |
| Flertall bestemt | kunnskapene | Kunnskapene hans kom fra mange års arbeid. | His knowledge came from many years of work. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The dictionary gives masculine gender; the source gender was corrected. Singular *kunnskap* is normally used as an uncountable noun; plural forms refer to bodies or areas of knowledge. The singular article example uses a bounded kind of knowledge.
