## fe

**English:** fairy

**Topics:** culture, folklore

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en / et | En fe / Et fe dukket opp i fortellingen. | A fairy appeared in the story. |
| Entall ubestemt | fe | Barnet tegnet en fe ved siden av slottet. | The child drew a fairy beside the castle. |
| Entall bestemt | feen / feet | Feen / Feet hjalp hovedpersonen hjem. | The fairy helped the main character get home. |
| Flertall ubestemt | fe / feer | I sagnet møter helten fe / feer i skogen. | In the legend, the hero meets fairy / fairies in the forest. |
| Flertall bestemt | fea / feene | Fea / Feene voktet den gamle brønnen. | The fairies guarded the old well. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists separate entries and paradigms: masculine fe (feen, feer, feene) and neuter fe (feet, fe, fea). The neuter forms are uncommon and are retained because they are listed. The plural-indefinite form fe is singular in spelling; context marks the plural use. Each accepted alternative has an example.
