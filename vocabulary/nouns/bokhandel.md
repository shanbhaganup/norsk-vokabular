## bokhandel

**English:** bookshop; bookstore

**Topics:** shopping, town

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bokhandel åpnet i sentrum. | A bookshop opened in the town centre. |
| Entall ubestemt | bokhandel | Hun arbeider i bokhandel. | She works in a bookshop. |
| Entall bestemt | bokhandelen | Bokhandelen stenger klokken seks. | The bookshop closes at six. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bokhandler | Byen har to bokhandler. | The town has two bookshops. |
| Flertall bestemt | bokhandlene | Bokhandlene arrangerer en lesekveld. | The bookshops are organising a reading evening. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** A shop that sells books.
