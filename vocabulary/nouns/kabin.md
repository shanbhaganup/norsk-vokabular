## kabin

**English:** cabin; compartment on a ship or in an aircraft

**Topics:** travel, transport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | en kabin står ved inngangen. | A cabin is by the entrance. |
| Entall ubestemt | kabin | Passasjerene gikk inn i kabin etter ombordstigningen. | The passengers entered the cabin after boarding. |
| Entall bestemt | kabinen | Kabinen var rolig under flyturen. | The cabin was quiet during the flight. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kabiner | Flyet har kabiner med ulike setetyper. | The aircraft has cabins with different seat types. |
| Flertall bestemt | kabinene | Kabinene på skipet ble gjort klare. | The cabins on the ship were prepared. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and listed paradigms checked against Bokmålsordboka.

**Notes:** Used for a small room aboard a boat or ship, and for the passenger or crew compartment in an aircraft.
