## minus

**English:** minus sign; negative feature; disadvantage

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et minus foran tallet viser at verdien er negativ. | A minus sign before the number shows that the value is negative. |
| Entall ubestemt | minus | Det er minus ved forslaget at det koster så mye. | A drawback of the proposal is that it costs so much. |
| Entall bestemt | minuset | Minuset ved løsningen er tidsbruken. | The drawback of the solution is the time it takes. |
| Flertall ubestemt | minus | Flere minus i regnskapet må undersøkes. | Several negative figures in the accounts need to be investigated. |
| Flertall ubestemt | minuser | Produktet har flere minuser enn konkurrentens modell. | The product has more drawbacks than the competitor’s model. |
| Flertall bestemt | minusa | Minusa ved begge alternativene ble diskutert. | The drawbacks of both options were discussed. |
| Flertall bestemt | minusene | Minusene ved begge alternativene ble diskutert. | The drawbacks of both options were discussed. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword, neuter gender and plural alternatives checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Neuter noun with zero plural `minus` or plural `minuser`, and definite plural `minusa` or `minusene`. The dictionary covers the negative sign and, figuratively, a negative aspect or deficit. Each plural option is illustrated.
