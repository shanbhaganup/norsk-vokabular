## presse

**English:** press coverage; publicity

**Topics:** media; communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei god presse kan hjelpe en ny bok. | Good press can help a new book. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En god presse kan hjelpe en ny bok. | Good press can help a new book. |
| Entall ubestemt | presse | Filmen fikk ei positiv presse. | The film received positive press. |
| Entall ubestemt | presse | Filmen fikk en positiv presse. | The film received positive press. |

| Entall bestemt | pressen | Pressen rundt lanseringen var omfattende. | The publicity around the launch was extensive. |
| Flertall ubestemt | presser | Flere presser om prosjektet kom samme uke. | Several rounds of coverage about the project appeared in the same week. |
| Flertall bestemt | pressene | Pressene før valget påvirket debatten. | The press coverage before the election influenced the debate. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Dictionary sense 5: coverage or publicity in newspapers and similar media. Kept distinct from the media institutions and journalists themselves; both genders and inflection alternatives are illustrated.
