## løk

**English:** onion

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Han skar en løk i tynne skiver. | He sliced an onion thinly. |
| Entall ubestemt | løk | Suppa trenger litt løk og pepper. | The soup needs some onion and pepper. |
| Entall bestemt | løken | Løken begynte å bli myk i panna. | The onion began to soften in the pan. |
| Flertall ubestemt | løker | Markedet solgte røde og gule løker. | The market sold red and yellow onions. |
| Flertall bestemt | løkene | Løkene ble lagt i kurven sammen med potetene. | The onions were put in the basket with the potatoes. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling, gender and inflection checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source spelling and meaning refer to onion used as food. Bokmålsordboka article 34423 includes both the Allium plant and its edible bulb. Article 34424 is a separate homograph meaning a deep, slow-moving stream, which is outside this entry.
