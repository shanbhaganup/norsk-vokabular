## væske

**English:** liquid; fluid

**Topics:** health, science

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling, gender and forms checked; examples authored for teaching.

**Notes:** Feminine and masculine are accepted. The feminine paradigm has *væska*; the dictionary lists *væsken* with the masculine paradigm.
