## allé

**English:** avenue; tree-lined road

**Topics:** city

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En allé leder fra parken til slottet. | An avenue leads from the park to the palace. |
| Entall ubestemt | allé / alle | Vi gikk langs en allé med gamle lindetrær. / Vi gikk langs en alle med gamle lindetrær. | We walked along an avenue lined with old lime trees. / We walked along an avenue lined with old lime trees. |
| Entall bestemt | alléen / alleen | Alléen fører fram til hovedinngangen. / Alleen fører fram til hovedinngangen. | The avenue leads to the main entrance. / The avenue leads to the main entrance. |
| Flertall ubestemt | alléer / alleer | Flere alléer ble plantet rundt godset. / Flere alleer ble plantet rundt godset. | Several avenues were planted around the estate. / Several avenues were planted around the estate. |
| Flertall bestemt | alléene / alleene | Alléene ble stengt for biltrafikk. / Alleene ble stengt for biltrafikk. | The avenues were closed to car traffic. / The avenues were closed to car traffic. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The reference anchor is headed alle, but its cited Bokmålsordboka entry identifies the noun as allé, with alle as an accepted spelling. This is distinct from the determiner/pronoun alle (“all/everyone”).
