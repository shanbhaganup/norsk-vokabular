## mandag

**English:** Monday

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mandag i april ble uvanlig varm. | A Monday in April became unusually warm. |
| Entall ubestemt | mandag | Vi møtes på mandag. | We are meeting on Monday. |
| Entall bestemt | mandagen | Mandagen etter ferien føltes lang. | The Monday after the holiday felt long. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mandager | Jeg jobber hjemme på mandager. | I work from home on Mondays. |
| Flertall bestemt | mandagene | Mandagene i mai passer best for møtet. | The Mondays in May work best for the meeting. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The indefinite singular `mandag` is also used without an article when naming a weekday, as in `på mandag`. The grammatical gender is masculine.
