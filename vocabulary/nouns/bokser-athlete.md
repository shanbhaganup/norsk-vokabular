## bokser

**English:** boxer (athlete)

**Topics:** sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bokser vant kampen på poeng. | A boxer won the fight on points. |
| Entall ubestemt | bokser | Han er bokser på landslaget. | He is a boxer on the national team. |
| Entall bestemt | bokseren | Bokseren trente hver morgen. | The boxer trained every morning. |
| Flertall ubestemt | boksere | To boksere møttes i finalen. | Two boxers met in the final. |
| Flertall bestemt | bokserne | Bokserne hilste på hverandre etter kampen. | The boxers greeted each other after the match. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers a person who competes in boxing (dictionary entry I). The dictionary also uses bokser as a short form of boksershorts and has a separate entry for the dog breed.
