## bungalow

**English:** bungalow

**Topics:** home, buildings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bungalow lå ved stranden. | A bungalow stood by the beach. |
| Entall ubestemt | bungalow | De leide bungalow på ferie. | They rented a bungalow on holiday. |
| Entall bestemt | bungalowen | Bungalowen hadde en stor terrasse. | The bungalow had a large terrace. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bungalower | Området har mange bungalower. | The area has many bungalows. |
| Flertall bestemt | bungalowene | Bungalowene ligger nær sjøen. | The bungalows are near the sea. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** A low house, often with a single storey.
