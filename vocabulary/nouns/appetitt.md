## appetitt

**English:** appetite

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Han har en sunn appetitt. | He has a healthy appetite. |
| Entall ubestemt | appetitt | Hun hadde liten appetitt etter turen. | She had little appetite after the trip. |
| Entall bestemt | appetitten | Appetitten kom tilbake etter noen dager. | Her appetite returned after a few days. |
| Flertall ubestemt | appetitter | De to barna har svært ulike appetitter. | The two children have very different appetites. |
| Flertall bestemt | appetittene | Foreldrene måtte ta hensyn til de ulike appetittene rundt bordet. | The parents had to take the different appetites around the table into account. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Appetitt is usually singular for a person’s desire to eat. Plural is possible when discussing distinct appetites.
