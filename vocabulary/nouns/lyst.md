## lyst

**English:** desire, inclination, urge

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Hun fikk ei lyst til å lære italiensk. | She felt a desire to learn Italian. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Han kjente en sterk lyst til å reise. | He felt a strong desire to travel. |
| Entall ubestemt | lyst | Jeg har lyst til å gå en tur. | I feel like going for a walk. |
| Entall bestemt, hunkjønn | lysta | Lysta til å utforske nye steder kom tilbake. | The desire to explore new places returned. |
| Entall bestemt, hankjønn | lysten | Lysten på noe søtt gikk over. | The craving for something sweet passed. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling, gender and inflection checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The imported source gender was corrected to feminine or masculine according to Bokmålsordboka. This entry focuses on an inclination or desire, including the usual construction *ha lyst til/på*. The dictionary also records sexual desire and the sense ‘pleasure’.
