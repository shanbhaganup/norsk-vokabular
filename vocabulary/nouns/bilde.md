## bilde

**English:** picture, image

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Han tok et bilde av fjellet. | He took a picture of the mountain. |
| Entall ubestemt | bilde | Hun hengte opp et bilde i gangen. | She hung a picture in the hall. |
| Entall bestemt | bildet | Bildet ble tatt i fjor. | The picture was taken last year. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bilder | Vi så på gamle bilder fra skolen. | We looked at old pictures from school. |
| Flertall bestemt | bilda | Bilda lå i ei eske på loftet. | The pictures were in a box in the attic. |
| Flertall bestemt | bildene | Han sendte bildene til familien. | He sent the pictures to his family. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both definite plural endings are accepted.
