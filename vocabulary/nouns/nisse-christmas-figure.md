## nisse

**English:** Christmas elf; Santa Claus figure

**Topics:** holidays

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En nisse delte ut pakker på julefesten. | A Christmas elf handed out presents at the holiday party. |
| Entall ubestemt | nisse | Barna ventet på en nisse utenfor huset. | The children waited for a Christmas elf outside the house. |
| Entall bestemt | nissen | Nissen kom med pakker til barna. | Santa’s helper brought presents to the children. |
| Flertall ubestemt | nisser | Nisser sto i vinduene på julemarkedet. | Christmas elves stood in the windows at the Christmas market. |
| Flertall bestemt | nissene | Nissene delte ut små gaver. | The Christmas elves handed out small gifts. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms reviewed; original examples authored for the labelled forms and senses.

**Notes:** Sense 3 of Bokmålsordboka 40856, corresponding to julenisse. “Gnome” in the source translation may mislead English readers; Santa’s helper or Christmas elf is usually clearer.
