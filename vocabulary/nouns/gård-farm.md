## gård

**English:** farm; rural property

**Topics:** nature

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En grøt ble servert til lunsj. | A porridge was served for lunch. |
| Entall ubestemt | gård | Jeg fant en gård her. | I found a farm here. |
