## trøye

**English:** sweater; undershirt; short jacket worn with a bunad

**Topics:** shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Article / gender (feminine) | ei | Hun kjøpte ei trøye til turen. | She bought a sweater for the trip. |
| Article / gender (masculine) | en | Han kjøpte en trøye til turen. | He bought a sweater for the trip. |
| Entall ubestemt | trøye | Jeg trenger en varm trøye. | I need a warm sweater. |
| Entall bestemt (feminine) | trøya | Trøya ligger i skapet. | The sweater is in the cupboard. |
| Entall bestemt (masculine) | trøyen | Trøyen ligger i skapet. | The sweater is in the cupboard. |
| Flertall ubestemt | trøyer | Butikken fikk inn flere ulltrøyer og bomullstrøyer. | The shop received several wool and cotton sweaters. |
| Flertall bestemt | trøyene | Trøyene henger på stativet. | The sweaters are hanging on the rack. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; feminine and masculine singular paradigms distinguished.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka gives feminine *trøya* and masculine *trøyen*; plural forms are shared. The source English gloss “sweater” is retained; the dictionary also includes an upper-body garment and a short bunad jacket.
