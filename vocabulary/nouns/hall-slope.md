## hall

**English:** tilt; slope (rare noun)

**Topics:** landscape

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et hall kan være vanskelig å se på avstand. | A slope can be difficult to see from a distance. |
| Entall ubestemt | hall | Terrenget har hall mot øst. | The terrain slopes toward the east. |
| Entall bestemt | hallet | Hallet i bakken ble tydelig etter regnet. | The slope in the ground became clear after the rain. |
| Flertall ubestemt | hall / haller | Et hall lå nord for gården. / Flere haller lå nord for gården. | A slope lay north of the farm. / Several slopes lay north of the farm. |
| Flertall bestemt | halla / hallene | Halla i landskapet ble grønne om våren. / Hallene i landskapet ble grønne om våren. | The slopes in the landscape turned green in spring. / The slopes in the landscape turned green in spring. |

**Form status:** Headword and gender verified; inflected neuter forms beyond the dictionary's illustrated plural are Unverified.

**Notes:** Rare neuter noun meaning a slanted position or a place that slopes. The reference merges it with masculine hall (“large hall”) and lists overlapping plural variants; this entry isolates the neuter sense. The cited dictionary illustrates hallene for this sense; the source also lists hall/haller and halla/hallene in its merged record. Confirmed vs uncertain forms are distinguished here: hallet follows the dictionary's neuter paradigm if available; remaining plurals follow source record and need review.
