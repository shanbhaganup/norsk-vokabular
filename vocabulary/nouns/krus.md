## krus

**English:** mug; cup with a handle

**Topics:** home, food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Artikkel, intetkjønn | et krus | Hun fylte et krus med te. | She filled a mug with tea. |
| Entall ubestemt | krus | Jeg kjøpte et krus på markedet. | I bought a mug at the market. |
| Entall bestemt, intetkjønn | kruset | Kruset står ved vasken. | The mug is by the sink. |
| Flertall ubestemt, uendret | krus | To krus sto på benken. | Two mugs stood on the counter. |
| Flertall bestemt, a-form | krusa | Krusa på hylla tilhører bestefar. | The mugs on the shelf belong to Grandfather. |
| Flertall bestemt | krusene | Krusene er klare til servering. | The mugs are ready to be served. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** This entry is the drinking vessel, which is neuter. Bokmålsordboka gives two indefinite plural options and two definite plural options. The reference snapshot merged this sense with the unrelated masculine/neuter noun *krus* meaning curliness or flattery; that second sense is excluded here.
