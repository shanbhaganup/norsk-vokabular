## gull

**English:** gold; precious metal

**Topics:** materials, nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et gull kan finnes i små mengder i bergarten. | Gold can be found in small amounts in the rock. |
| Entall ubestemt | gull | Ringen er laget av gull. | The ring is made of gold. |
| Entall bestemt | gullet | Gullet i gruven ble fraktet bort. | The gold in the mine was transported away. |
| Flertall ubestemt | gull | Smykkene inneholder gull fra ulike gruver. | The jewelry contains gold from different mines. |
| Flertall bestemt | gulla | Gulla i samlingen hadde ulik renhet. | The golds in the collection had different purities. |
| Flertall bestemt | gullene | Gullene i samlingen hadde ulik renhet. | The golds in the collection had different purities. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original bilingual teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** *Gull* is normally a mass noun when it means the metal. The plural forms refer to kinds or separate samples. Both dictionary-listed definite plural alternatives are illustrated.
