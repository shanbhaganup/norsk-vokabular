## innskudd

**English:** deposit; payment

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et innskudd sikrer reservasjonen. | A deposit secures the reservation. |
| Entall ubestemt | innskudd | Vi betalte et innskudd for leiligheten. | We paid a deposit for the apartment. |
| Entall bestemt | innskuddet | Innskuddet refunderes etterpå. | The deposit is refunded afterwards. |
| Flertall ubestemt | innskudd | Banken tilbyr innskudd uten gebyr. | The bank offers deposits without a fee. |
| Flertall bestemt | innskudda | Innskudda ble registrert samme dag. | The deposits were recorded the same day. |
| Flertall bestemt | innskuddene | Innskuddene ble registrert samme dag. | The deposits were recorded the same day. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original teaching examples authored for this project.

**Notes:** Neuter noun. Dictionary accepts innskudda and innskuddene. It also lists the senses act of paying in, an inserted passage, and money deposited; this entry focuses on a money deposit.
