## foredrag

**English:** lecture; talk

**Topics:** school

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et foredrag begynner klokka ti. | A lecture starts at ten. |
| Entall ubestemt | foredrag | Et foredrag begynner klokka ti. | A lecture starts at ten. |
