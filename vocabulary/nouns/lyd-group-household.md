## lyd

**English:** group of people, household, family

**Topics:** people

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Det bodde en stor lyd på gården i gamle dager. | A large household lived on the farm in the old days. |
| Entall ubestemt | lyd | En lyd bodde i huset ved elva. | A household lived in the house by the river. |
| Entall bestemt | lyden | Lyden samlet seg rundt bordet. | The household gathered around the table. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lyder | Flere lyder deltok i bygdas feiring. | Several families took part in the village celebration. |
| Flertall bestemt | lydene | Lydene i grenda kjente hverandre godt. | The households in the hamlet knew one another well. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. Compound inflection is labelled derived where applicable. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This is the homographic noun for a group of people or a household, separate from *lyd* meaning an acoustic sound. It is uncommon in present-day everyday speech and occurs especially in compounds and set phrases. The forms are dictionary-listed; examples are authored to illustrate them.
