## akademi

**English:** academy (specialized school or training institution)

**Topics:** school

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Hun søkte på et akademi for dans. | She applied to a dance academy. |
| Entall ubestemt | akademi | Byen fikk sitt første akademi for animasjon. | The city got its first animation academy. |
| Entall bestemt | akademiet | Akademiet tilbyr kveldskurs i fotografi. | The academy offers evening classes in photography. |
| Flertall ubestemt | akademi | Landet har to akademi som utdanner skuespillere. | The country has two academies that train actors. |
| Flertall ubestemt | akademier | Flere akademier tilbyr kurs for unge musikere. | Several academies offer courses for young musicians. |
| Flertall bestemt | akademia | De to akademia deler øvingslokaler. | The two academies share rehearsal spaces. |
| Flertall bestemt | akademiene | Akademiene holder åpen dag i september. | The academies hold an open day in September. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** Both plural patterns are accepted. In the plural examples, *akademia* means ‘the academies’. The same spelling can also mean ‘academia’ as a separate word.
