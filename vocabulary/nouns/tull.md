## tull

**English:** nonsense; foolishness; a bundle or tangle (masculine homonym)

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Article / gender (neuter, nonsense) | et | Det var bare et tull fra start til slutt. | It was nonsense from beginning to end. |
| Entall ubestemt (neuter) | tull | Ikke snakk tull. | Don’t talk nonsense. |
| Entall bestemt (neuter) | tullet | Tullet tok slutt da læreren kom inn. | The fooling around stopped when the teacher came in. |
| Flertall ubestemt (neuter, variant 1) | tull | Barna fant på tull hele ettermiddagen. | The children were up to nonsense all afternoon. |
| Flertall ubestemt (neuter, variant 2) | tull | Han fortalte oss flere tull om reisen. | He told us several silly stories about the trip. |
| Flertall bestemt (neuter, variant 1) | tulla | Tulla på festen varte ikke lenge. | The joking at the party did not last long. |
| Flertall bestemt (neuter, variant 2) | tullene | Tullene hans gjorde møtet vanskelig å ta alvorlig. | His bits of nonsense made the meeting hard to take seriously. |
| Article / gender (masculine homonym) | en | En tull med klær lå på gulvet. | A bundle of clothes lay on the floor. |
| Entall ubestemt (masculine homonym) | tull | Garnet gikk i tull. | The yarn became tangled. |
| Entall bestemt (masculine homonym) | tullen | Tullen av garn var vanskelig å løse opp. | The tangle of yarn was hard to undo. |
| Flertall ubestemt (masculine homonym) | tuller | To tuller med klær lå ved døra. | Two bundles of clothes lay by the door. |
| Flertall bestemt (masculine homonym) | tullene | Tullene med klær ble båret opp på loftet. | The bundles of clothes were carried up to the attic. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; masculine and neuter homonyms are separated by feature and sense.

**Notes:** The reference merges two Bokmålsordboka homonyms. Neuter *tull* means nonsense, foolishness, or joking; its accepted definite plural forms are *tulla* and *tullene*. Masculine *tull* means a bundle/tangle, disorder, or a foolish person; its paradigm is *tull – tullen – tuller – tullene*. Examples are authored and illustrate the relevant homonymous sense.
