## ting

**English:** assembly; legislative body

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et ting samler representanter. | An assembly brings representatives together. |
| Entall ubestemt | ting | Et ting kan samle utsendinger fra flere lag. | An assembly can bring delegates from several clubs together. |
| Entall bestemt | tinget | Tinget møtes i Bergen i år. | The assembly meets in Bergen this year. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ting | Flere ting har behandlet saken. | Several assemblies have considered the matter. |
| Flertall bestemt | tinga / tingene | Tinga vedtok nye regler. / Tingene vedtok nye regler. | The assemblies adopted new rules. / The assemblies adopted new rules. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and forms checked; teaching examples authored.

**Notes:** This neuter noun denotes an assembly or legislative body; the common-gender word *ting* “thing/matter” is a separate entry.
