## kalkun

**English:** turkey; the bird, its meat, or a flop (film)

**Topics:** food, animals, film

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | en kalkun står ved inngangen. | A turkey is by the entrance. |
| Entall ubestemt | kalkun | Vi serverte kalkun på julaften. | We served turkey on Christmas Eve. |
| Entall bestemt | kalkunen | Kalkunen ble stekt i ovnen. | The turkey was roasted in the oven. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kalkuner | Gården holder kalkuner ute på beitet. | The farm keeps turkeys out in the pasture. |
| Flertall bestemt | kalkunene | Kalkunene søkte ly under trærne. | The turkeys sought shelter under the trees. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and listed paradigms checked against Bokmålsordboka.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists the bird, its meat, and informally a pretentious but completely unsuccessful film (a flop). The example sentences use the bird sense.
