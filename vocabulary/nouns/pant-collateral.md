## pant

**English:** pledge; collateral for a loan

**Topics:** money, law

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et pant kan sikre et lån. | Collateral can secure a loan. |
| Entall ubestemt | pant | Banken tok pant i eiendommen. | The bank took a lien on the property. |
| Entall bestemt | pantet | Pantet ble slettet etter at lånet var nedbetalt. | The lien was removed after the loan was repaid. |
| Flertall ubestemt | pant | Flere pant ble registrert i grunnboka. | Several pledges were registered in the land register. |
| Flertall bestemt | panta / pantene | Panta ble frigitt. / Pantene ble frigitt. | The pledges were released. / The pledges were released. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Separate neuter homonym pant 2 II, security for a loan or, figuratively, a guarantee. Both definite-plural forms shown.
