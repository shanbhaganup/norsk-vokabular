## kjøtt

**English:** meat; flesh; soft plant tissue

**Topics:** food, body

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et kjøtt kan ha ulik smak og konsistens. | A type of meat can have a different taste and texture. |
| Entall ubestemt | kjøtt | Vi spiser kjøtt bare én gang i uka. | We eat meat only once a week. |
| Entall bestemt | kjøttet | Kjøttet trenger mer tid i ovnen. | The meat needs more time in the oven. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kjøtt | Ulike kjøtt passer til ulike retter. | Different meats suit different dishes. |
| Flertall bestemt | kjøtta | Kjøtta fra gården selges lokalt. | The meats from the farm are sold locally. |
| Flertall bestemt | kjøttene | Kjøttene fra gården selges lokalt. | The meats from the farm are sold locally. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms and senses reviewed.

**Notes:** *Kjøtt* is commonly a mass noun; plural is used when referring to kinds or varieties. Bokmålsordboka also has broader senses for animal tissue, seafood flesh, plant tissue, and a biblical use. The source gloss "meat" was broadened to cover the dictionary entry.
