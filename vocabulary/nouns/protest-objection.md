## protest

**English:** protest; objection; dissent

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En protest kan uttrykke sterk motstand. | A protest can express strong opposition. |
| Entall ubestemt | protest | De sendte en protest til kommunen. | They sent a protest to the municipality. |
| Entall bestemt | protesten | Protesten ble hørt av ledelsen. | The protest was heard by the management. |
