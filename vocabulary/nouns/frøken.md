## frøken

**English:** Miss; unmarried or young woman (older title); formerly a female schoolteacher

**Topics:** titles and forms of address

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei frøken og en frøken ventet ved døren. | A young woman waited by the door. (Both feminine and masculine articles are accepted.) |
| Entall ubestemt | frøken | En frøken ventet ved døren. | A young woman waited by the door. |
| Entall bestemt | frøkna / frøkenen | Frøkna kom tidlig. / Frøkenen kom tidlig. | The young woman arrived early. / The young woman arrived early. |
| Flertall ubestemt | frøkner | Flere frøkner arbeidet på skolen før. | Several female teachers worked at the school in the past. |
| Flertall bestemt | frøknene | Frøknene møttes etter undervisningen. | The female teachers met after class. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and paradigm checked against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine paradigms are listed: feminine frøkna and masculine frøkenen in the definite singular. Dictionary senses include a title for a young/unmarried woman and the older term for a female schoolteacher; examples reflect both.
