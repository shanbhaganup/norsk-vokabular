## dal

**English:** valley; dale

**Topics:** nature, geography

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En dal ligger mellom fjellene. | A valley lies between the mountains. |
| Entall ubestemt | dal | Veien følger dal gjennom fjellene. | The road follows a valley through the mountains. |
| Entall bestemt | dalen | Dalen er grønn om sommeren. | The valley is green in summer. |
| Flertall ubestemt | daler | Brede daler skjærer gjennom området. | Broad valleys cut through the region. |
| Flertall bestemt | dalene | Dalene ble formet av isbreer. | The valleys were formed by glaciers. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** A large, elongated depression in the terrain; the dictionary also uses dal for a settlement or district in a valley.
