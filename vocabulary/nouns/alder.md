## alder

**English:** age

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Entall ubestemt | alder | Barnets alder avgjør hvilken gruppe det skal være i. | The child’s age determines which group they will be in. |
