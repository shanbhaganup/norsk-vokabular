## mil

**English:** Norwegian mile (10 kilometres)

**Topics:** numbers, travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei mil er ti kilometer. | A Norwegian mile is ten kilometres. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mil er ti kilometer. | A Norwegian mile is ten kilometres. |
| Entall ubestemt | mil | Det er én mil til stasjonen. | It is one Norwegian mile to the station. |
| Entall bestemt | mila | Mila mellom bygdene går gjennom skogen. | The ten-kilometre stretch between the villages passes through the forest. |
| Entall bestemt | milen | Milen mellom bygdene går gjennom skogen. | The ten-kilometre stretch between the villages passes through the forest. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mil | De syklet fire mil før pausen. | They cycled forty kilometres before the break. |
| Flertall bestemt | milene | Milene på den gamle veien gikk sakte unna. | The miles on the old road passed slowly. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene entry 1 gender options and masculine plural paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The Norwegian `mil` is 10 km; it is not the English mile. Both feminine and masculine are accepted in the singular, with definite forms `mila` / `milen`. The dictionary gives masculine plural `mil – milene`; the separate board-game noun `mil 2` is invariable and has its own entry.
