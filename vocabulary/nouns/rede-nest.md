# rede

**English:** nest; home

**Topics:** nature; home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et rede ligger høyt i treet. | A nest lies high in the tree. |
| Entall ubestemt | rede | Fuglene bygde et rede i ospa. | The birds built a nest in the aspen. |
| Entall bestemt | redet | Redet var tomt etter at ungene fløy ut. | The nest was empty after the young birds flew away. |
| Flertall ubestemt | reder | Flere reder lå skjult i skråningen. | Several nests lay hidden on the slope. |

| Flertall bestemt | redene | Redene ble undersøkt av biologen. | The nests were examined by the biologist. |
