## yrke

**English:** profession; occupation

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et (intetkjønn) | Et yrke kan kreve lang utdanning. | A profession can require a long education. |
| Entall ubestemt | yrke | Hun valgte et yrke innen helse. | She chose a profession in health care. |
| Entall bestemt | yrket | Yrket passer godt for ham. | The profession suits him well. |
| Flertall ubestemt | yrker | Mange yrker krever samarbeid. | Many professions require cooperation. |
| Flertall bestemt | yrka / yrkene | Yrka har endret seg over tid. / Yrkene har endret seg over tid. | The professions have changed over time. / The professions have changed over time. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling, gender and forms checked; examples authored for teaching.

**Notes:** This is the neuter noun meaning occupation or profession. The similarly spelled feminine/masculine entry in Bokmålsordboka (69621) has a different meaning (“weekdays”) and is not included. Both *yrka* and *yrkene* are accepted definite plurals.
