## anke

**English:** appeal

**Topics:** law

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En anke kan føre til en ny behandling av saken. | An appeal can lead to a new hearing of the case. |
