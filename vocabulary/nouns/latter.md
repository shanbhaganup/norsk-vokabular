## latter

**English:** laughter; a laugh

**Topics:** feelings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En latter fylte rommet da historien var ferdig. | Laughter filled the room when the story ended. |
| Entall ubestemt | latter | Hun slapp ut en latter under forestillingen. | She let out a laugh during the performance. |
| Entall bestemt | latteren | Latteren runget gjennom salen. | The laughter echoed through the hall. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lattere | Ulike lattere kan røpe stemninger. | Different laughs can reveal moods. |
| Flertall bestemt | latterne | Latterne fra publikum blandet seg med applausen. | The audience’s laughs blended with the applause. |

**Form status:** Headword and applicable forms reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Usually a mass noun; plural refers to distinct instances or kinds of laughter.
