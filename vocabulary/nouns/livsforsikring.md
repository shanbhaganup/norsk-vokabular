## livsforsikring

**English:** life insurance, life insurance policy

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei livsforsikring | De tegnet ei livsforsikring da de kjøpte hus. | They took out a life insurance policy when they bought a house. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en livsforsikring | Hun har en livsforsikring gjennom jobben. | She has a life insurance policy through work. |
| Entall ubestemt | livsforsikring | Jeg vurderer å kjøpe livsforsikring. | I’m considering buying life insurance. |
| Entall bestemt | livsforsikringa | Livsforsikringa dekker begge foreldrene. | The life insurance policy covers both parents. |
| Entall bestemt | livsforsikringen | Hun sa opp livsforsikringen i fjor. | She cancelled the life insurance policy last year. |
| Flertall ubestemt | livsforsikringer | Rådgiveren sammenlignet tre livsforsikringer. | The adviser compared three life insurance policies. |
| Flertall bestemt | livsforsikringene | Livsforsikringene har ulike vilkår. | The life insurance policies have different terms. |

**Form status:** Gender, inflection and sense checked against Bokmålsordboka. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The singular can refer to an individual policy, and the plural to separate policies. Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted.
