## bur

**English:** cage; pen

**Topics:** home, animals

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et bur sto i hjørnet av rommet. | A cage stood in the corner of the room. |
| Entall ubestemt | bur | Kaninen går tilbake i bur om natten. | The rabbit goes back into its cage at night. |
| Entall bestemt | buret | Buret må rengjøres hver uke. | The cage must be cleaned every week. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bur | Dyrebutikken selger bur i flere størrelser. | The pet shop sells cages in several sizes. |
