## gang

**English:** time; occasion

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Jeg spurte en gang. | I asked once. |
| Entall ubestemt | gang | Prøv en gang til. | Try one more time. |
| Entall bestemt | gangen | Det gikk bedre denne gangen. | It went better this time. |
