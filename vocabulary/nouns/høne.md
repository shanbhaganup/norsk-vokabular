## høne

**English:** hen; female chicken

**Topics:** animals, food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei høne går rundt i hagen. | A hen is walking around in the garden. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En høne går rundt i hagen. | A hen is walking around in the garden. |
| Entall ubestemt | høne | Vi har høne til middag i dag. | We are having chicken for dinner today. |
| Entall bestemt | høna | Høna la et egg i redet. | The hen laid an egg in the nest. |
| Entall bestemt | hønen | Hønen la et egg i redet. | The hen laid an egg in the nest. |
| Flertall ubestemt | høner | Fem høner og en hane bor i hønsehuset. | Five hens and a rooster live in the chicken coop. |
| Flertall bestemt | hønene | Hønene kaklet da bonden kom. | The hens clucked when the farmer arrived. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted. The dictionary also uses *høne* for females of some other bird species and in insect names; this entry follows the common hen sense in the source.
