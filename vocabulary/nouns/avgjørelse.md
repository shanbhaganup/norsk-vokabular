## avgjørelse

**English:** decision

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Styret tok en avgjørelse i går. | The board made a decision yesterday. |
| Entall ubestemt | avgjørelse | Hun ventet på en avgjørelse fra komiteen. | She was waiting for a decision from the committee. |
| Entall bestemt | avgjørelsen | Avgjørelsen ble kunngjort på fredag. | The decision was announced on Friday. |
| Flertall ubestemt | avgjørelser | Dommeren måtte ta flere vanskelige avgjørelser. | The referee had to make several difficult decisions. |
| Flertall bestemt | avgjørelsene | Vi gikk gjennom avgjørelsene fra forrige møte. | We went over the decisions from the previous meeting. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The noun can refer to an ordinary decision or a formal ruling.
