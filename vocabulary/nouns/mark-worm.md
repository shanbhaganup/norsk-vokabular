## mark

**English:** worm; earthworm; larva

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en mark / en makk | En mark / en makk lå i den fuktige jorda. | A worm lay in the damp soil. |
| Entall ubestemt | mark / makk | Jeg fant mark / makk under steinen. | I found a worm under the stone. |
| Entall bestemt | marken / makken | Marken / makken krøp ned i jorda. | The worm crawled down into the soil. |
| Flertall ubestemt | marker / makker | Fiskeren brukte marker / makker som agn. | The angler used worms as bait. |
| Flertall bestemt | markene / makkene | Marker / makker ble oppbevart i en liten boks. | The worms were kept in a small box. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword alternatives and paradigms checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Ordbøkene lists the masculine forms as `makk – makken – makker – makkene` and `mark – marken – marker – markene`. Each alternative is illustrated. The reference record incorrectly combined this plural paradigm with the land, weight and currency homonyms; those are in separate entries. This sense includes earthworms and similar wormlike animals or larvae.
