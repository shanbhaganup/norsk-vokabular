## våpen

**English:** weapon; arms

**Topics:** general, conflict

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et (intetkjønn) | Politiet fant et våpen i bilen. | The police found a weapon in the car. |
| Entall ubestemt | våpen | Hun kjøpte et våpen til samlingen. | She bought a weapon for the collection. |
| Entall bestemt | våpenet | Våpenet ligger i et låst skap. | The weapon is in a locked cabinet. |
| Flertall ubestemt | våpen | Museet viser gamle våpen. | The museum displays old weapons. |
| Flertall bestemt | våpna / våpnene | Våpna ble samlet inn. / Våpnene ble samlet inn. | The weapons were collected. / The weapons were collected. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling, gender and forms checked; examples authored for teaching.

**Notes:** Both *våpna* and *våpnene* are accepted definite plurals. The reference anchor combines the meanings “arms,” “gun” and “weapon”; this entry covers the weapon sense.
