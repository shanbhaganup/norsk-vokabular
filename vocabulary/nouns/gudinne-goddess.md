## gudinne

**English:** goddess

**Topics:** religion, mythology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei gudinne ble æret i tempelet. | A goddess was honored at the temple. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En gudinne ble æret i tempelet. | A goddess was honored at the temple. |
| Entall ubestemt | gudinne | Fortellingen handler om ei gudinne. | The story is about a goddess. |
| Entall bestemt | gudinna | Gudinna våket over skogen. | The goddess watched over the forest. |
| Entall bestemt | gudinnen | Gudinnen våket over skogen. | The goddess watched over the forest. |
| Flertall ubestemt | gudinner | Flere gudinner er nevnt i sagaen. | Several goddesses are mentioned in the saga. |
| Flertall bestemt | gudinnene | Gudinnene fikk hvert sitt tempel. | The goddesses each received a temple. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original bilingual teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine gender are accepted. The feminine definite singular *gudinna* and masculine definite singular *gudinnen* are both dictionary-listed; each alternative is illustrated.
