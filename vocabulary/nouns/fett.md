## fett

**English:** fat (substance)

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et fett kan brukes i matlaging. | A fat can be used in cooking. |
| Entall ubestemt | fett | Fett gir maten smak og energi. | Fat gives food flavor and energy. |
| Entall bestemt | fettet | Fettet stivnet da det ble kaldt. | The fat solidified when it got cold. |
| Flertall ubestemt | fett | Ulike fett finnes i maten vi spiser. | Different fats are found in the food we eat. |
| Flertall bestemt | fetta | Fetta i kjøttet smeltet under steking. | The fats in the meat melted during cooking. |
| Flertall bestemt | fetene | Fetene i prøvene ble analysert. | The fats in the samples were analyzed. |

**Form status:** Dictionary paradigm checked; use of plural forms in this sense is uncommon and flagged for review.

**Notes:** The singular is chiefly a mass noun. Plural forms are dictionary-listed but uncommon; the plural examples use contrastive/countable contexts. The definite plural forms differ from expected regular inflection and are retained as listed.
