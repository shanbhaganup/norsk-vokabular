## makt

**English:** power; authority; force

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei makt kan påvirke hele samfunnet. | A power can influence the whole society. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En makt kan påvirke hele samfunnet. | A power can influence the whole society. |
| Entall ubestemt | makt | Hun fikk makt til å endre reglene. | She gained power to change the rules. |
| Entall bestemt | makta | Makta lå hos folkevalgte. | The power lay with elected representatives. |
| Entall bestemt | makten | Makten lå hos folkevalgte. | The power lay with elected representatives. |
| Flertall ubestemt | makter | Flere makter møttes til forhandlinger. | Several powers met for negotiations. |
| Flertall bestemt | maktene | Maktene inngikk en avtale. | The powers made an agreement. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword, gender options and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine are accepted. The corresponding definite singulars are `makta` (feminine) and `makten` (masculine). The word covers authority, strength/force, coercion and a state or institution with power; plural examples use political powers as entities.
