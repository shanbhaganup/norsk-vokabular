## internett

**English:** internet

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et internett kan koble sammen mange nettverk. | An internet can connect many networks. |
| Entall ubestemt | internett | Vi bruker internett til å finne informasjon. | We use the internet to find information. |
| Entall ubestemt | Internett | Internett gjør det lett å dele informasjon. | The Internet makes it easy to share information. |
| Entall bestemt | internettet | Internettet var utilgjengelig i morges. | The internet was unavailable this morning. |
| Entall bestemt | internettet | Jeg leste nyheten på internettet i morges. | I read the news on the internet this morning. |
| Flertall ubestemt | internett | Flere internett kan være koblet sammen. | Several internets can be connected. |
| Flertall bestemt | internetta | Internetta ble utviklet uavhengig av hverandre. | The internets were developed independently. |
| Flertall bestemt | internettene | Internettene ble utviklet uavhengig av hverandre. | The internets were developed independently. |
| Flertall bestemt | internetta | Flere små internetta kan være koblet sammen. | Several small internets can be connected. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original teaching examples authored for this project.

**Notes:** Dictionary recognizes both internett and Internett as headword spellings and gives the same paradigm. Capitalization alternatives are recorded; when sentence-initial, lowercase and uppercase spellings are graphically indistinguishable. Singular and plural usages differ by context.
