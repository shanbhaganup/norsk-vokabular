## betaling

**English:** payment

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Vi venter på ei betaling fra kunden. | We’re waiting for a payment from the customer. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Hun mottok en betaling i går. | She received a payment yesterday. |
| Entall ubestemt | betaling | Butikken krever betaling på forhånd. | The shop requires payment in advance. |
| Entall bestemt | betalinga | Betalinga gikk gjennom med en gang. | The payment went through immediately. |
| Entall bestemt | betalingen | Betalingen ble registrert på fredag. | The payment was recorded on Friday. |
| Flertall ubestemt | betalinger | Han gjorde tre betalinger samme dag. | He made three payments on the same day. |
| Flertall bestemt | betalingene | Vi kontrollerte betalingene mot fakturaene. | We checked the payments against the invoices. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted.
