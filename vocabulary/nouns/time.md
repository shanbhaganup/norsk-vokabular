## time

**English:** hour

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En time går fort når samtalen er god. | An hour passes quickly when the conversation is good. |
| Entall ubestemt | time | Vi ventet i en time på bussen. | We waited an hour for the bus. |
| Entall bestemt | timen | Timen begynner klokka ni. | The class starts at nine. |
| Flertall ubestemt | timer | Turen tar omtrent tre timer. | The trip takes about three hours. |
| Flertall bestemt | timene | Timene etter lunsj gikk raskt. | The hours after lunch passed quickly. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** Here time means a 60-minute hour; the same spelling also has a school-lesson sense.
