## armheving

**English:** push-up

**Topics:** sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Hun tok ei armheving med knærne i gulvet. | She did a push-up with her knees on the floor. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Han klarte en armheving til. | He managed one more push-up. |
| Entall ubestemt | armheving | Jeg prøvde å ta en armheving. | I tried to do a push-up. |
| Entall bestemt | armhevinga | Den siste armhevinga var tung. | The last push-up was hard. |
| Entall bestemt | armhevingen | Treneren viste hvordan armhevingen skulle utføres. | The coach showed how to do the push-up. |
| Flertall ubestemt | armhevinger | Vi tok ti armhevinger som oppvarming. | We did ten push-ups as a warm-up. |
| Flertall bestemt | armhevingene | Hun telte armhevingene høyt. | She counted the push-ups aloud. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted.
