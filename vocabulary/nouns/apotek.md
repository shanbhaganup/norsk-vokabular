## apotek

**English:** pharmacy

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Det ligger et apotek ved stasjonen. | There’s a pharmacy by the station. |
| Entall ubestemt | apotek | Vi lette etter et apotek som var åpent. | We looked for a pharmacy that was open. |
| Entall bestemt | apoteket | Apoteket stenger klokka seks. | The pharmacy closes at six. |
| Flertall ubestemt | apotek | Bydelen har tre apotek. | The neighbourhood has three pharmacies. |
| Flertall ubestemt | apoteker | Det åpnet to apoteker i sentrum i fjor. | Two pharmacies opened downtown last year. |
| Flertall bestemt | apoteka | Apoteka i sentrum holder åpent på lørdager. | The pharmacies downtown are open on Saturdays. |
| Flertall bestemt | apotekene | Apotekene har ulike åpningstider. | The pharmacies have different opening hours. |

**Notes:** Both indefinite and both definite plural forms are accepted.
