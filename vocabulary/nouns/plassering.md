## plassering

**English:** placement; location

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei plassering / En plassering nær vinduet gir godt lys. | A position near the window gives good light. |
| Entall ubestemt | plassering | Vi fant ei plassering for sykkelen. | We found a place for the bicycle. |
| Entall bestemt | plasseringa / plasseringen | Plasseringa / Plasseringen av skiltene ble endret. | The placement of the signs was changed. |
| Flertall ubestemt | plasseringer | Flere plasseringer ble prøvd før vi bestemte oss. | Several positions were tried before we decided. |
| Flertall bestemt | plasseringene | Plasseringene ble merket på kartet. | The locations were marked on the map. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine forms are accepted.
