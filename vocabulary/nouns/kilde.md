## kilde

**English:** source; origin; spring

**Topics:** information, nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kilde ligger oppe i lia. | A spring is located higher up the slope. |
| Entall ubestemt | kilde | Rapporten viser til en kilde. | The report refers to a source. |
| Entall bestemt | kilden | Kilden til elva tørket ut. | The river’s source dried up. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kilder | Vi brukte flere kilder i oppgaven. | We used several sources in the assignment. |
| Flertall bestemt | kildene | Kildene står oppført bakerst. | The sources are listed at the end. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** Covers the source senses of spring, origin and source of information; the dictionary gives these under one lemma with the same paradigm. The dictionary also permits feminine *kjelde*, a distinct spelling and lemma not included by this source record.
