# rekkverk

**English:** railing; guardrail

**Topics:** home; construction

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et rekkverk beskytter kanten av terrassen. | A railing protects the edge of the terrace. |
| Entall ubestemt | rekkverk | De monterte et rekkverk ved trappa. | They installed a railing by the stairs. |
| Entall bestemt | rekkverket | Rekkverket må festes bedre. | The railing needs to be secured better. |
| Flertall ubestemt | rekkverk | Brua har rekkverk på begge sider. | The bridge has railings on both sides. |
| Flertall bestemt | rekkverka | Rekkverka ble malt i sommer. | The railings were painted this summer. |
| Flertall bestemt | rekkverkene | Rekkverkene ble malt i sommer. | The railings were painted this summer. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The neuter noun means a structure of bars or similar with a handrail, especially beside stairs, bridges or terraces. Both definite plural alternatives are included.
