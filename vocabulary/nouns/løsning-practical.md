## løsning

**English:** solution, answer, practical arrangement

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | De fant ei løsning som alle kunne godta. | They found a solution everyone could accept. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Komiteen foreslo en løsning på konflikten. | The committee proposed a solution to the conflict. |
| Entall ubestemt | løsning | Vi trenger en løsning før fristen. | We need a solution before the deadline. |
| Entall bestemt, hunkjønn | løsninga | Løsninga fungerte bedre enn vi hadde ventet. | The solution worked better than we had expected. |
| Entall bestemt, hankjønn | løsningen | Løsningen ble presentert på møtet. | The solution was presented at the meeting. |
| Flertall ubestemt | løsninger | Ingen av løsningene passet for alle. | None of the solutions suited everyone. |
| Flertall bestemt | løsningene | Løsningene ble vurdert av en ekstern gruppe. | The solutions were assessed by an external group. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and inflection checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers a solution as an answer, explanation or practical way to deal with a problem. Bokmålsordboka also records several related action senses in this article and has a separate homographic article for a chemical solution, entered separately.
