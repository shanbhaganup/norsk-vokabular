## brorskap

**English:** brotherhood

**Topics:** society, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et brorskap ble dannet av studentene. | A brotherhood was formed by the students. |
| Entall ubestemt | brorskap | Brorskap bygger på tillit og fellesskap. | Brotherhood is built on trust and community. |
| Entall bestemt | brorskapet | Brorskapet støttet de nye medlemmene. | The brotherhood supported its new members. |
| Flertall ubestemt | brorskap | Flere brorskap deltok i møtet. | Several brotherhoods took part in the meeting. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Brorskap can mean a relationship among brothers, solidarity, or an organisation with shared aims.
