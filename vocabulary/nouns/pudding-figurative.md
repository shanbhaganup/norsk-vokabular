# pudding

**English:** a limp or sluggish person (colloquial)

**Topics:** informal

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Etter skituren var jeg en pudding. | After the ski trip I was completely wiped out. |
| Entall ubestemt | pudding | Han føler seg som en pudding etter nattevakten. | He feels like a limp rag after the night shift. |
| Entall bestemt | puddingen | I komedien kaller vennene ham puddingen når han mangler energi. | In the comedy, his friends call him a limp rag when he has no energy. |
| Flertall ubestemt | puddinger | Etter den lange marsjen var de to puddinger. | After the long march, the two of them were completely spent. |
| Flertall bestemt | puddingene | I den gamle komedien blir puddingene stadig ertet for latskapen. | In the old comedy, the sluggish characters are constantly teased for being lazy. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and paradigm checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka sense 2 is figurative and colloquial. Plural examples refer to people characterized as sluggish; this sense is separate from the food sense.
