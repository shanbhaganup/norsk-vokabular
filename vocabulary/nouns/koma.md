## koma

**English:** coma

**Topics:** health, medicine

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En koma kan vare i flere dager. | A coma can last for several days. |
| Entall ubestemt | koma | Pasienten lå i koma etter ulykken. | The patient was in a coma after the accident. |
| Entall bestemt | komaen | Komaen varte i nesten en uke. | The coma lasted for almost a week. |
| Flertall ubestemt | komaer | Legen hadde behandlet pasienter som hadde vært i komaer av ulik varighet. | The doctor had treated patients who had been in comas of different durations. |
| Flertall bestemt | komaene | Komaene som omtales i studien, hadde ulike årsaker. | The comas discussed in the study had different causes. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; plural use is uncommon.

**Notes:** Selects *koma* meaning prolonged, deep unconsciousness; the same lemma also names a comet’s coma and an optical aberration. The dictionary-listed plural is uncommon because the noun usually denotes a state. The reference’s gender correction is confirmed: masculine *en koma*. Teaching examples are original.
