**Form status:** Ordbøkene entry 3 gender and masculine plural paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** This is `mark 3`, an old Norwegian currency unit and a former German and Finnish currency unit. Both feminine and masculine are accepted in the singular; the dictionary gives zero plural `mark` and definite plural `markene` for masculine. Kept separate from the weight, land and worm senses.
