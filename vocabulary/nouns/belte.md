## belte

**English:** belt

**Topics:** shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Hun kjøpte et nytt belte. | She bought a new belt. |
| Entall ubestemt | belte | Buksa sitter bedre med belte. | The trousers fit better with a belt. |
| Entall bestemt | beltet | Han strammet beltet ett hakk. | He tightened his belt one notch. |
| Flertall ubestemt | belter | Butikken selger belter i mange farger. | The shop sells belts in many colours. |
| Flertall bestemt | belta | De svarte belta hang ved prøverommet. | The black belts were hanging by the fitting room. |
| Flertall bestemt | beltene | Hun la beltene i skuffen. | She put the belts in the drawer. |

**Notes:** These examples concern a belt worn with clothing. Both definite plural endings are accepted.
