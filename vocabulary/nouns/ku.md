## ku

**English:** cow; adult female bovine that has calved

**Topics:** nature, animals, food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Artikkel, hunkjønn | ei ku | Ei ku sto ved grinda. | A cow stood by the gate. |
| Artikkel, hankjønn | en ku | En ku sto ved grinda. | A cow stood by the gate. |
| Entall ubestemt | ku | Bonden kjøpte ku til gården. | The farmer bought a cow for the farm. |
| Entall bestemt, hunkjønn | kua | Kua rautet etter kalven. | The cow mooed for her calf. |
| Entall bestemt, hankjønn | kuen | Kuen gikk rolig over tunet. | The cow walked calmly across the farmyard. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kuer | Flere kuer beitet på jordet. | Several cows grazed in the field. |
| Flertall ubestemt, alternativ | kyr | Kyr beitet på jordet. | Cows grazed in the field. |
| Flertall bestemt | kuene | Kuene ventet ved fôrhekken. | The cows waited by the feed rack. |
| Flertall bestemt, alternativ | kyrne | Kyrne gikk inn i fjøset. | The cows went into the barn. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted. Both plural paradigms are shown. The source also contained *kyr* as a source form; it is a plural of *ku*, not a separate lemma. The primary sense here is the adult female animal.
