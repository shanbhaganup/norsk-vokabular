## kikkert

**English:** binoculars; telescope

**Topics:** technology, nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kikkert ligger i sekken. | A pair of binoculars is in the backpack. |
| Entall ubestemt | kikkert | Hun tok med kikkert på turen. | She brought binoculars on the trip. |
| Entall bestemt | kikkerten | Kikkerten gjør fuglen lettere å se. | The binoculars make the bird easier to see. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kikkerter | Butikken selger kikkerter i flere størrelser. | The shop sells binoculars in several sizes. |
| Flertall bestemt | kikkertene | Kikkertene ligger i skapet. | The binoculars are in the cupboard. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** *Kikkert* is grammatically countable in Norwegian even when English commonly uses “binoculars.” The source gloss “binoculars” is retained; the dictionary sense is an optical instrument for viewing distant objects.
