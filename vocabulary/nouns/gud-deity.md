## gud

**English:** god; deity

**Topics:** religion

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En gud ble dyrket ved tempelet. | A god was worshipped at the temple. |
| Entall ubestemt | gud | I fortellingen møter hun en gud. | In the story she meets a god. |
| Entall bestemt | guden | Guden beskyttet byen, trodde de. | They believed the god protected the city. |
| Flertall ubestemt | guder | Romerne dyrket mange guder. | The Romans worshipped many gods. |
| Flertall bestemt | gudene | De norrøne gudene står sentralt i mytene. | The Norse gods are central to the myths. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original bilingual teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Covers the common noun for a supernatural being worshipped in polytheistic or general religious contexts. The proper name *Gud* is a distinct use and is documented separately.
