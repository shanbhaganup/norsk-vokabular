## loft

**English:** attic

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et loft | Huset har et loft med to små vinduer. | The house has an attic with two small windows. |
| Entall ubestemt | loft | Vi leter etter et hus med et stort loft. | We’re looking for a house with a large attic. |
| Entall bestemt | loftet | Vi oppbevarer vinterklærne på loftet. | We keep our winter clothes in the attic. |
| Flertall ubestemt | loft | To av husene har uinnredede loft. | Two of the houses have unfinished attics. |

**Notes:** This entry covers the room or floor directly under a roof. Bokmålsordboka also lists other senses, including a traditional timber outbuilding.
