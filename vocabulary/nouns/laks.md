## laks

**English:** salmon

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gender/article | en | En laks svømte opp elva. | A salmon swam up the river. |
| Singular indefinite | laks | Vi spiste laks til middag. | We ate salmon for dinner. |
| Singular definite | laksen | Laksen var fersk. | The salmon was fresh. |
| Plural indefinite, unchanged | laks | Elva er full av laks om høsten. | The river is full of salmon in autumn. |
| Plural indefinite, -er | lakser | Flere lakser svømte mot strømmen. | Several salmon swam against the current. |
| Plural definite | laksene | Laksene svømte oppover elva. | The salmon swam up the river. |
