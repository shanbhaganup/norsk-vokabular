## avgift

**English:** fee, levy

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | De innførte ei avgift på parkering. | They introduced a parking fee. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Vi betalte en avgift for å bruke veien. | We paid a fee to use the road. |
| Entall ubestemt | avgift | Det kreves avgift ved innkjøring. | A fee is charged on entry. |
| Entall bestemt | avgifta | Avgifta ble satt ned i januar. | The fee was reduced in January. |
| Entall bestemt | avgiften | Avgiften er inkludert i prisen. | The fee is included in the price. |
| Flertall ubestemt | avgifter | Butikken betaler flere avgifter. | The shop pays several levies. |
| Flertall bestemt | avgiftene | Hun la sammen avgiftene før hun betalte. | She added up the fees before paying. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted.
