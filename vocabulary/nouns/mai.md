## mai

**English:** May

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mai kan være kald i Nord-Norge. | A May can be cold in northern Norway. |
| Entall ubestemt | mai | Vi reiser til Bergen i mai. | We are travelling to Bergen in May. |
| Entall bestemt | maien | Maien var uvanlig varm det året. | That May was unusually warm that year. |
| Flertall ubestemt | maier | Flere maier har vært kalde på kysten. | Several Mays have been cold on the coast. |
| Flertall bestemt | maiene | Maiene på 2010-tallet varierte mye. | The Mays in the 2010s varied a lot. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Plural forms refer to separate calendar years and are uncommon; ordinary month names are generally used in singular.
