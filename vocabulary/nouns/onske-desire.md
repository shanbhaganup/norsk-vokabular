## ønske

**English:** wish; desire

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et (intetkjønn) | Et ønske kan være vanskelig å oppfylle. | A wish can be difficult to fulfil. |
| Entall ubestemt | ønske | Hun hadde et ønske om å reise. | She had a wish to travel. |
| Entall bestemt | ønsket | Ønsket hennes ble oppfylt. | Her wish came true. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ønsker | Barna skrev ned tre ønsker. | The children wrote down three wishes. |
| Flertall bestemt | ønska / ønskene | Ønska ble lest høyt. / Ønskene ble lest høyt. | The wishes were read aloud. / The wishes were read aloud. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling, gender and forms checked; examples authored for teaching.

**Notes:** The verb *ønske* is a separate word-type entry. Both *ønska* and *ønskene* are accepted definite plurals.
