## medium

**English:** medium; means of communication

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et medium kan spre informasjon raskt. | A medium can spread information quickly. |
| Entall ubestemt | medium | Radio er et medium for nyheter. | Radio is a medium for news. |
| Entall bestemt | mediet | Mediet når mange lyttere hver dag. | The medium reaches many listeners every day. |
