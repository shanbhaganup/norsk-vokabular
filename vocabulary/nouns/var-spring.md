## vår

**English:** spring (season)

**Topics:** time, seasons

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en (hankjønn) | En vår med mye sol gjør meg glad. | A spring with lots of sunshine makes me happy. |
| Entall ubestemt | vår | Vi håper på en tidlig vår. | We are hoping for an early spring. |
| Entall bestemt | våren | Våren begynner ofte med skiftende vær. | Spring often begins with changeable weather. |
| Flertall ubestemt | vårer | Noen vårer er kaldere enn andre. | Some springs are colder than others. |
| Flertall bestemt | vårene | De siste vårene har vært milde. | The last few springs have been mild. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling, gender and forms checked; examples authored for teaching.

**Notes:** This is the seasonal noun sense. The reference’s “ours” is a separate determiner sense and is not included here.
