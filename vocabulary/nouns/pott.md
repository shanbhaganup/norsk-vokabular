## pott

**English:** pot; small vessel

**Topics:** household; shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En pott står på hylla. | A pot is on the shelf. |
| Entall ubestemt | pott | Hun satte en pott på komfyren. | She put a pot on the stove. |
| Entall bestemt | potten | Potten er full av suppe. | The pot is full of soup. |
| Flertall ubestemt | potter | Flere potter ble satt til avkjøling. | Several pots were set out to cool. |
| Flertall bestemt | pottene | Pottene står i skapet. | The pots are in the cupboard. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Source “pots” is interpreted as the vessel sense. The dictionary also lists an older liquid measure, a betting pool, and a sum set aside for distribution; those senses are outside this source gloss.
