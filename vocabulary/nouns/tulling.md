## tulling

**English:** fool; silly or foolish person

**Topics:** people

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Article / gender | en (masculine) | En tulling ropte fra bakerste rad. | A fool shouted from the back row. |
| Entall ubestemt | tulling | Ikke vær en tulling. | Don’t be a fool. |
| Entall bestemt | tullingen | Tullingen glemte nøklene igjen. | The fool forgot the keys again. |
| Flertall ubestemt | tullinger | Noen tullinger sperret inngangen. | Some fools blocked the entrance. |
| Flertall bestemt | tullingene | Tullingene ble bedt om å roe seg. | The fools were asked to calm down. |

**Form status:** Masculine dictionary paradigm verified against the cited sense.

**Notes:** The reference combines this “fool” sense with the separate feminine/masculine noun meaning fooling, joking, or teasing. That action noun is not an inflection of this person noun; its feminine form *tullinga* is therefore excluded here.
