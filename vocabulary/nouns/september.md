## september

**English:** September

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En september kan være varm i Sør-Norge. | A September can be warm in southern Norway. |
| Entall ubestemt | september | Vi flyttet hit i september. | We moved here in September. |
| Entall bestemt | septemberen | Septemberen i fjor var tørr. | Last September was dry. |
| Flertall ubestemt | septemberer | Flere septemberer på rad har vært varme. | Several Septembers in a row have been warm. |
| Flertall bestemt | septemberene | Septemberene på 1990-tallet var kjøligere. | The Septembers in the 1990s were cooler. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Plural forms refer to separate calendar years and are uncommon.
