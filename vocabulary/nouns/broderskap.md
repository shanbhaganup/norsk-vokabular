## broderskap

**English:** brotherhood

**Topics:** society, relationships

**Notes:** The dictionary pages cited in the reference describe the element skap and its gender patterns, rather than a complete paradigm for broderskap. The reference's masculine form is therefore left unverified; neuter forms are supported by the compound and by the related brorskap.
