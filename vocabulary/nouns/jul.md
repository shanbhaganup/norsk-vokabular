## jul

**English:** Christmas; Christmas season

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei jul nærmer seg. / En jul nærmer seg. | Christmas is approaching. / Christmas is approaching. |
| Entall ubestemt | jul | Vi feirer jul sammen. | We celebrate Christmas together. |
| Entall bestemt | jula | Jula begynner den tjuefjerde desember. | Christmas begins on December twenty-fourth. |
| Entall bestemt | julen | Julen nærmer seg. | Christmas is approaching. |
| Flertall ubestemt | juler | Familien har feiret mange juler på hytta. | The family has celebrated many Christmases at the cabin. |
| Flertall bestemt | julene | Julene på hytta var alltid fulle av snø. | The Christmases at the cabin were always snowy. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked where accessible; source forms retained for unavailable pages.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine forms are accepted; the noun commonly refers to the holiday/season and is often used without an article in fixed expressions. Source record: word-0672.
