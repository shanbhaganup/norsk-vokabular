## sekund

**English:** second (unit of time)

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et sekund går fort når vi venter. | A second passes quickly when we are waiting. |
| Entall ubestemt | sekund | Hun kom fram et sekund før meg. | She arrived one second before me. |
| Entall bestemt | sekundet | Sekundet var avgjørende for resultatet. | The second was decisive for the result. |
| Flertall ubestemt | sekund / sekunder | Sekund / Sekunder gikk før lyset ble tent. | A second / Seconds passed before the light came on. |
| Flertall bestemt | sekunda / sekundene | Sekunda / Sekundene ble telt høyt. | The seconds were counted aloud. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source combines two homonyms: masculine sekund 1 is a musical interval; neuter sekund 2 is the time unit taught here. The neuter paradigm is used. Both indefinite plural forms and both definite plural forms are listed and illustrated.
