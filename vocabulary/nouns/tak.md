## tak

**English:** ceiling

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et tak med synlige bjelker gir rommet særpreg. | A ceiling with exposed beams gives the room character. |
| Entall ubestemt | tak | Vi malte taket i stua hvitt. | We painted the living-room ceiling white. |
| Entall bestemt | taket | Takets farge gjør rommet lysere. | The ceiling’s colour makes the room brighter. |
| Flertall ubestemt | tak | Huset har høye tak i alle rom. | The house has high ceilings in every room. |
| Flertall bestemt | taka | Taka i den gamle bygården er dekorert. | The ceilings in the old apartment building are decorated. |
| Flertall bestemt | takene | Takene ble malt under oppussingen. | The ceilings were painted during the renovation. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** Her betyr tak den innvendige flaten øverst i et rom. De to dictionary citations in the source distinguish homonyms.
