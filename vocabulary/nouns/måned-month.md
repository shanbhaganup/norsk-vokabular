## måned

**English:** month

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En måned går fort når man har mye å gjøre. | A month passes quickly when you have a lot to do. |
| Entall ubestemt | måned | Vi leier hytta en måned hver sommer. | We rent the cabin for a month every summer. |
| Entall bestemt | måneden | Måneden begynner med en fridag. | The month begins with a day off. |
| Flertall ubestemt | måneder | De bodde i Tromsø i seks måneder. | They lived in Tromsø for six months. |
| Flertall bestemt | månedene | Månedene gikk raskere enn vi hadde ventet. | The months went by faster than we expected. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms reviewed; original examples authored for the labelled forms and senses.

**Notes:** The dictionary also uses månad/måned in dates and distinguishes a lunar period from a calendar month; this entry covers the general month sense.
