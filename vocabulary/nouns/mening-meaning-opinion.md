## mening

**English:** meaning; opinion; intention

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei mening kan uttrykkes på flere måter. | A meaning can be expressed in several ways. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mening kan uttrykkes på flere måter. | A meaning can be expressed in several ways. |
| Entall ubestemt | mening | Jeg søker mening i det hun sa. | I am looking for meaning in what she said. |
| Entall bestemt | meninga | Meninga med spørsmålet var uklar. | The meaning of the question was unclear. |
| Entall bestemt | meningen | Meningen med spørsmålet var uklar. | The meaning of the question was unclear. |
| Flertall ubestemt | meninger | Deltakerne hadde ulike meninger om forslaget. | The participants had different opinions about the proposal. |
| Flertall bestemt | meningene | Meningene deres kom fram i rapporten. | Their opinions were presented in the report. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene accepted genders and forms checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine are accepted; the corresponding definite singular forms are `meninga` and `meningen`. The dictionary covers a thought or meaning, an opinion or conviction, and intention/purpose. The English gloss from the source focuses on “meaning”; the examples include that sense and the closely related opinion sense.
