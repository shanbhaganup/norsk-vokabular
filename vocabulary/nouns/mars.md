## mars

**English:** March

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mars kan by på både sol og snø. | A March can bring both sun and snow. |
| Entall ubestemt | mars | Vi flyttet hit i mars. | We moved here in March. |
| Entall bestemt | maren | Maren i fjor var svært mild. | Last March was very mild. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mars | Flere mars på rad har vært våte. | Several Marches in a row have been wet. |
| Flertall bestemt | marsene | Marsene de siste årene har vært skiftende. | The Marches in recent years have been changeable. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The definite singular is maren. Plurals refer to different years and are uncommon.
