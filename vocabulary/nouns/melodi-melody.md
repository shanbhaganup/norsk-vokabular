## melodi

**English:** melody; tune

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En melodi fylte rommet. | A melody filled the room. |
| Entall ubestemt | melodi | Hun nynnet en melodi fra filmen. | She hummed a tune from the film. |
| Entall bestemt | melodien | Melodien var lett å huske. | The melody was easy to remember. |
| Flertall ubestemt | melodier | Komponisten skrev melodier for barn. | The composer wrote melodies for children. |
| Flertall bestemt | melodiene | Melodiene fra forestillingen ble spilt inn. | The melodies from the performance were recorded. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword, masculine gender and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The source gender was corrected to masculine. Ordbøkene also uses `melodi` for the intonation of language and figuratively for a recurring theme.
