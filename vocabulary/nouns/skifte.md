## skifte

**English:** change; shift

**Topics:** work, general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et skifte kan endre hvordan arbeidet blir gjort. | A change can alter how the work is done. |
| Entall ubestemt | skifte | Vi merket et skifte i været. | We noticed a change in the weather. |
| Entall bestemt | skiftet | Skiftet i ledelsen ble kunngjort i går. | The change in leadership was announced yesterday. |
| Flertall ubestemt | skifter | Flere skifter skjedde i løpet av året. | Several changes occurred during the year. |
| Flertall bestemt | skifta / skiftene | Skifta / Skiftene i planen krevde nye avtaler. | The changes in the plan required new agreements. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both definite plural forms are accepted. Cross-POS: skifte is also a verb; this entry is the noun.
