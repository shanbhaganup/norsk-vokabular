## arbeid

**English:** work, piece of work

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Hun leverte et grundig arbeid. | She turned in a thorough piece of work. |
| Entall ubestemt | arbeid | Det ligger mye arbeid bak utstillingen. | A lot of work went into the exhibition. |
| Entall bestemt | arbeidet | Arbeidet tok lengre tid enn planlagt. | The work took longer than planned. |
| Flertall ubestemt | arbeid | Museet stilte ut flere arbeid av samme kunstner. | The museum exhibited several works by the same artist. |
| Flertall ubestemt | arbeider | Han publiserte flere vitenskapelige arbeider. | He published several scientific works. |
| Flertall bestemt | arbeida | Arbeida fra den perioden ble vist i en egen sal. | The works from that period were shown in a separate room. |
| Flertall bestemt | arbeidene | Hun samlet arbeidene sine i en mappe. | She collected her works in a folder. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Work in general is uncountable. The plural examples refer to individual pieces of work.
