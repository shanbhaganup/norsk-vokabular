## brannmur

**English:** firewall (computer security)

**Topics:** technology, computing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En brannmur beskytter nettverket. | A firewall protects the network. |
| Entall ubestemt | brannmur | Brannmur hindrer uønsket tilgang. | A firewall prevents unauthorised access. |
| Entall bestemt | brannmuren | Brannmuren blokkerte den ukjente forbindelsen. | The firewall blocked the unknown connection. |
| Flertall ubestemt | brannmurer | Moderne brannmurer overvåker nettverkstrafikken. | Modern firewalls monitor network traffic. |
| Flertall bestemt | brannmurene | Brannmurene må oppdateres jevnlig. | The firewalls must be updated regularly. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers the computer-security sense of brannmur; the dictionary also records a wall used to prevent the spread of fire.
