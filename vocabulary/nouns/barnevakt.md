## barnevakt

**English:** babysitter

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Vi har bestilt ei barnevakt til lørdag. | We’ve booked a babysitter for Saturday. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | De trenger en barnevakt i kveld. | They need a babysitter tonight. |
| Entall ubestemt | barnevakt | Hun jobbet som barnevakt i ferien. | She worked as a babysitter during the holiday. |
| Entall bestemt | barnevakta | Barnevakta kom klokka seks. | The babysitter arrived at six o’clock. |
| Entall bestemt | barnevakten | Barnevakten fant fram et brettspill. | The babysitter got out a board game. |
| Flertall ubestemt | barnevakter | Familien kjenner to barnevakter i nabolaget. | The family knows two babysitters in the neighbourhood. |
| Flertall bestemt | barnevaktene | Barnevaktene fikk hver sin nøkkel. | Each babysitter was given a key. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted. The word can apply to a babysitter of any gender.
