## dom

**English:** cathedral

**Topics:** architecture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En dom. | A cathedral. |
| Entall ubestemt | dom | Byen har en dom fra middelalderen. | The city has a cathedral from the Middle Ages. |
| Entall bestemt | domen | Domen ligger nær torget. | The cathedral is near the square. |
| Flertall ubestemt | domer | Landet har flere domer. | The country has several cathedrals. |
