## omgang

**English:** social contact; interaction

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En omgang med naboene kan være hyggelig. | Interaction with the neighbours can be pleasant. |
| Entall ubestemt | omgang | Hun har omgang med mange i nabolaget. | She has contact with many people in the neighbourhood. |
| Entall bestemt | omgangen | Omgangen med kundene krever omtanke. | Interaction with customers requires consideration. |
| Flertall ubestemt | omganger | Ulike omganger mellom gruppene ble fulgt opp. | Different interactions between the groups were followed up. |
| Flertall bestemt | omgangene | Omgangene med publikum foregikk etter forestillingen. | The interactions with the audience took place after the performance. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Separate dictionary homonym omgang 2. It also includes use/handling and sexual contact senses; this entry follows the source English gloss 'interaction'. The plural is dictionary-listed but less common in the social-contact sense; review idiomaticity.
