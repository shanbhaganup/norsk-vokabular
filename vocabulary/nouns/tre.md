## tre

**English:** tree; wood (material)

**Topics:** nature, plants

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et tre vokser ved gjerdet. | A tree is growing by the fence. |
| Entall ubestemt | tre | Hun plantet et tre i hagen. | She planted a tree in the garden. |
| Entall bestemt | treet | Treet mistet flere greiner i stormen. | The tree lost several branches in the storm. |
| Flertall ubestemt, kortform | tre | Det står mange tre langs veien. | There are many trees along the road. |
| Flertall ubestemt | trær | Det står mange trær langs veien. | There are many trees along the road. |
| Flertall bestemt, a-form | trea | Trea i hagen trenger vann. | The trees in the garden need water. |
| Flertall bestemt | trærne | Trærne langs elva gir god skygge. | The trees along the river provide good shade. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The dictionary accepts both the unchanged plural *tre* and *trær*, and definite plurals *trea* and *trærne*. They are illustrated separately. The material sense “wood” is related to the same dictionary entry.
