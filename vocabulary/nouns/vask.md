## vask

**English:** wash; washing

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En vask av bilen tar en time. | A wash of the car takes an hour. |
| Entall ubestemt | vask | Bilen trenger en grundig vask. | The car needs a thorough wash. |
| Entall bestemt | vasken | Vasken av bilen er endelig ferdig. | The washing of the car is finally finished. |
| Flertall ubestemt | vasker | To vasker av bilen i uka holder den ren. | Two washes of the car a week keep it clean. |
