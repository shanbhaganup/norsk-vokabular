## kjøp

**English:** purchase; buying; sale transaction

**Topics:** shopping, business

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et kjøp kan angres innen fristen. | A purchase can be cancelled within the deadline. |
| Entall ubestemt | kjøp | Dette var et godt kjøp. | This was a good buy. |
| Entall bestemt | kjøpet | Kjøpet ble registrert på kundens konto. | The purchase was recorded on the customer's account. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kjøp | Nettbutikken registrerte flere kjøp i går. | The online shop recorded several purchases yesterday. |
| Flertall bestemt | kjøpa | Kjøpa ble levert på samme dag. | The purchases were delivered on the same day. |
| Flertall bestemt | kjøpene | Kjøpene ble levert på samme dag. | The purchases were delivered on the same day. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms and principal senses reviewed.

**Notes:** The headword covers the act of acquiring something for payment, a trade agreement, and something purchased. Both listed definite plural alternatives are illustrated. The expression *på kjøpet* is a construction, not an inflectional form.
