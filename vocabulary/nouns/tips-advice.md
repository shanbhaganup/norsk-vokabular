## tips

**English:** tip; piece of advice

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et tips kan være nyttig. | A tip can be useful. |
| Entall ubestemt | tips | Hun ga meg et nyttig tips. | She gave me a useful tip. |
| Entall bestemt | tipset | Tipset hjalp oss med å finne veien. | The tip helped us find the way. |
| Flertall ubestemt | tips | Politiet fikk flere tips fra publikum. | The police received several tips from the public. |
| Flertall bestemt | tipsa / tipsene | Tipsa ble undersøkt grundig. / Tipsene ble undersøkt grundig. | The tips were investigated thoroughly. / The tips were investigated thoroughly. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and forms checked; teaching examples authored.

**Notes:** The reference listed *tipser* as an alternative plural, but it is the plural of the related noun *tipser* (“informant”), not of this neuter sense of *tips*. It is excluded here.
