## lund

**English:** grove, small group of trees

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Vi fant en lund med bjørketrær ved stien. | We found a grove of birch trees beside the path. |
| Entall ubestemt | lund | En liten lund skjermer benken mot vinden. | A small grove shelters the bench from the wind. |
| Entall bestemt | lunden | Lunden ligger mellom jordet og elva. | The grove lies between the field and the river. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lunder | Gamle lunder omgir flere av gårdene. | Old groves surround several of the farms. |
| Flertall bestemt | lundene | Lundene gir ly for småfuglene. | The groves provide shelter for small birds. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. Compound inflection is labelled derived where applicable. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers a small stand of trees. Bokmålsordboka also records a consecrated outdoor place under the same noun.
