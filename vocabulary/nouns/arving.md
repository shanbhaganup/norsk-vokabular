## arving

**English:** heir

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En arving tok kontakt med advokaten. | An heir contacted the lawyer. |
| Entall ubestemt | arving | Testamentet nevnte bare én arving. | The will named only one heir. |
| Entall bestemt | arvingen | Arvingen fikk beskjed per brev. | The heir was notified by letter. |
| Flertall ubestemt | arvinger | Flere arvinger møtte opp hos advokaten. | Several heirs showed up at the lawyer’s office. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Arving denotes someone who inherits or is entitled to inherit.
