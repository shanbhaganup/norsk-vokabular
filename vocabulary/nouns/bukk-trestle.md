## bukk

**English:** trestle; sawhorse

**Topics:** tools, construction

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bukk støttet planken mens han sagde. | A sawhorse supported the plank while he sawed. |
| Entall ubestemt | bukk | Planken ligger på bukk. | The plank is resting on a sawhorse. |
| Entall bestemt | bukken | Bukken står i verkstedet. | The sawhorse is in the workshop. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bukker | De brukte bukker til å holde stillaset oppe. | They used trestles to support the scaffolding. |
| Flertall bestemt | bukkene | Bukkene ble satt tilbake i boden. | The trestles were put back in the shed. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers a stand on legs used as a support (dictionary entry I, sense 3), distinct from the animal sense.
