## omgang

**English:** round; round of a game; turn

**Topics:** communication, sports

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En omgang varer i tjue minutter. | A period lasts twenty minutes. |
| Entall ubestemt | omgang | Laget spilte en omgang før pausen. | The team played one half before the break. |
| Entall bestemt | omgangen | Omgangen endte uten mål. | The period ended without a goal. |
| Flertall ubestemt | omganger | De spilte to omganger i finalen. | They played two halves in the final. |
| Flertall bestemt | omgangene | Omgangene var jevne. | The periods were evenly matched. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Multiple dictionary senses under entry 1 include round/section, a beating, treatment, rotation, and a knitting row; examples here focus on round/turn.
