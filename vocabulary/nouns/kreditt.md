## kreditt

**English:** credit; loan; deferred payment

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kreditt kan gjøre det mulig å kjøpe nødvendig utstyr. | Credit can make it possible to buy necessary equipment. |
| Entall ubestemt | kreditt | Bedriften fikk kreditt fra banken. | The business received credit from the bank. |
| Entall bestemt | kreditten | Kreditten må betales tilbake innen sommeren. | The loan must be repaid by the summer. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kreditter | Banken tilbyr ulike kreditter til små bedrifter. | The bank offers different credit facilities to small businesses. |
| Flertall bestemt | kredittene | Kredittene hadde ulike renter og vilkår. | The credit facilities had different interest rates and terms. |

**Form status:** Gender and sense verified in Bokmålsordboka; the article view did not expose the inflection table in this verification pass, so the reference paradigm remains flagged for direct confirmation.

**Notes:** The dictionary includes financial trust, deferred payment, loans and figurative credit/praise. Do not confuse *kreditt* with the distinct accounting term *kredit*.
