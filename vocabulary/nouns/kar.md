## kar

**English:** man; fellow

**Topics:** people

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kar ventet ved porten med en bukett. | A fellow waited by the gate with a bouquet. |
| Entall ubestemt | kar | De møtte en kar fra nabogården. | They met a man from the neighbouring farm. |
| Entall bestemt | karen | Karen hjalp til med å bære kassene. | The man helped carry the boxes. |
| Flertall ubestemt | karer | To karer ryddet snø fra inngangen. | Two men cleared snow from the entrance. |
| Flertall bestemt | karene | Karene tok en pause etter arbeidet. | The men took a break after the work. |

**Form status:** Headword and paradigm reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source gloss “fellow” corresponds to masculine kar 1. The linked source paradigm also includes neuter kar 2, meaning a vessel; those forms belong to a distinct homonym and are not mixed into this entry.
