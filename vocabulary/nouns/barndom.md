## barndom

**English:** childhood

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En barndom med trygge voksne gir et godt utgangspunkt. | A childhood with caring adults provides a good foundation. |
| Entall ubestemt | barndom | Hun hadde en barndom nær sjøen. | She had a childhood near the sea. |
| Entall bestemt | barndommen | Barndommen hennes var preget av lange somre på gården. | Her childhood was shaped by long summers on the farm. |
| Flertall ubestemt | barndommer | Romanen skildrer barndommer i tre ulike land. | The novel portrays childhoods in three different countries. |
| Flertall bestemt | barndommene | Barndommene deres var svært forskjellige. | Their childhoods were very different. |
