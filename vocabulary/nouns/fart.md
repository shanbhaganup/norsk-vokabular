## fart

**English:** speed; pace

**Topics:** transport, movement

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei fart / En fart på femti kilometer i timen er lav her. | A speed of fifty kilometres per hour is low here. |
| Entall ubestemt | fart | Bilen holder jevn fart på motorveien. | The car maintains a steady speed on the motorway. |
| Entall bestemt | farta / farten | Farta / Farten må ned gjennom bygda. | The speed must be reduced through the village. |
| Flertall ubestemt | farter | Løperne holdt ulike farter i bakken. | The runners kept different paces on the hill. |
| Flertall bestemt | fartene | Fartene i de to løpene var svært forskjellige. | The speeds in the two races were very different. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine gender are accepted; the inflection alternatives are illustrated individually. The plural is used for distinct speeds or paces.
