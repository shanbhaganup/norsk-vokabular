**English:** manhood; adulthood as a man

**Topics:** family

**Form status:** Gender and singular sense verified in Ordbøkene; plural forms carried from the reference paradigm but not independently verified in the accessible dictionary page. Plural examples are editorial and the plural is unusual for this abstract noun.

**Notes:** Ordbøkene's entry describes being an adult man, adult age, noble character/courage, and also a male sexual organ. This entry focuses on adulthood/manhood. The reference lists `manndommer` and `manndommene`, but the dictionary page available during review did not expose a paradigm; treat the plural as uncertain and review before relying on it.
