## barnerom

**English:** child’s room, children’s bedroom

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Vi maler et barnerom i helga. | We’re painting a child’s room this weekend. |
| Entall ubestemt | barnerom | De gjorde om loftet til et barnerom. | They converted the attic into a child’s room. |
| Entall bestemt | barnerommet | Det var stille på barnerommet. | It was quiet in the child’s room. |
| Flertall ubestemt | barnerom | Huset har to barnerom. | The house has two children’s bedrooms. |
| Flertall bestemt | barneromma | Barneromma ligger i andre etasje. | The children’s bedrooms are upstairs. |
| Flertall bestemt | barnerommene | De satte opp nye hyller på barnerommene. | They put up new shelves in the children’s bedrooms. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both definite plural endings are accepted.
