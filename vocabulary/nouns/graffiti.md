## graffiti

**English:** graffiti; wall writing

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | EN graffiti er omtalt i teksten. | The graffiti is mentioned in the text. |
| Entall ubestemt | graffiti | Politiet fotograferte en graffiti. | The police photographed a piece of graffiti. |
| Entall bestemt | graffitien | Graffitien ble malt over. | The graffiti was painted over. |
| Flertall ubestemt | graffitier | Flere graffitier dukket opp i tunnelen. | Several pieces of graffiti appeared in the tunnel. |
| Flertall bestemt | graffitiene | Graffitiene ble dokumentert av kommunen. | The graffiti pieces were documented by the municipality. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original bilingual teaching examples.

**Notes:** The Bokmålsordboka classifies graffiti as masculine. The plural forms follow the source snapshot; the count use is less common than mass use.
