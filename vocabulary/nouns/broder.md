## broder

**English:** brother

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En broder hjalp oss med flyttingen. | A brother helped us with the move. |
| Entall ubestemt | broder | Han omtaler vennen som sin broder. | He refers to his friend as his brother. |
| Entall bestemt | broderen | Broderen kom på besøk i helgen. | The brother came to visit at the weekend. |
| Flertall ubestemt | brødre | De to brødrene arbeider sammen. | The two brothers work together. |
| Flertall bestemt | brødrene | Brødrene møttes på stasjonen. | The brothers met at the station. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Broder is an informal, friendly term for bror.
