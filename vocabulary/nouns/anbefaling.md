## anbefaling

**English:** recommendation

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei anbefaling fra legen gjorde henne tryggere. / En anbefaling fra legen gjorde henne tryggere. | A recommendation from the doctor reassured her. / A recommendation from the doctor reassured her. |
| Entall ubestemt | anbefaling | Jeg fikk en anbefaling om å bestille time tidlig. | I got a recommendation to book an appointment early. |
| Entall bestemt | anbefalinga / anbefalingen | Anbefalinga var lett å følge. / Anbefalingen var lett å følge. | The recommendation was easy to follow. / The recommendation was easy to follow. |
| Flertall ubestemt | anbefalinger | Vi ba om anbefalinger fra tidligere kunder. | We asked for recommendations from previous customers. |
| Flertall bestemt | anbefalingene | Anbefalingene hjalp oss med å velge hotell. | The recommendations helped us choose a hotel. |

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted.
