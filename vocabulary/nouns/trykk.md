## trykk

**English:** pressure; force; printed text (depending on sense)

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Article / gender (masculine, blow/impact) | en | En trykk mot døra fikk den til å åpne seg. | A shove against the door made it open. |
| Article / gender (neuter, pressure/print) | et | Et trykk mot underlaget holder teltet på plass. | Pressure against the ground keeps the tent in place. |
| Entall ubestemt (masculine, blow/impact) | trykk | Han fikk en trykk midt i ansiktet. | He took a blow right in the face. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms transcribed from the reference; the source groups several dictionary entries. Masculine forms are confined to the dictionary’s “blow/impact” sense; neuter forms cover pressure and printed text. The neuter definite plural alternatives are separately illustrated.

**Notes:** The source record merges several dictionary homonyms and gender paradigms. The masculine “blow/impact” paradigm and neuter pressure/printing paradigms are distinguished in the examples; no single paradigm should be inferred across the homonyms.
