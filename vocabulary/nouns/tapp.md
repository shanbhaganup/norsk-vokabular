## tapp

**English:** pin; plug

**Topics:** tools

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En tapp holder de to delene sammen. | A pin holds the two parts together. |
| Entall ubestemt | tapp | Møbelet mangler en tapp i det ene hjørnet. | The piece of furniture is missing a pin in one corner. |
| Entall bestemt | tappen | Tappen passer nøyaktig i hullet. | The pin fits exactly into the hole. |
| Flertall ubestemt | tapper | Snekkeren lagde nye tapper til ramma. | The carpenter made new pegs for the frame. |
| Flertall bestemt | tappene | Tappene ble limt fast i treverket. | The pegs were glued into the wood. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** Oppføringen dekker tapp som en liten bolt eller plugg; sjekk kildens andre homonym separat.
