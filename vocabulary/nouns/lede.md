## lede

**English:** disgust; loathing

**Topics:** feelings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En lede ved volden vokste etter hvert. | A loathing of the violence grew over time. |
| Entall ubestemt | lede | Han kjente en lede ved den uærlige framgangsmåten. | He felt disgust at the dishonest method. |
| Entall bestemt | leden | Leden hans var tydelig for alle. | His disgust was clear to everyone. |
| Flertall ubestemt | leder | Ulike leder kan oppstå etter langvarig press. | Different feelings of loathing can arise after prolonged pressure. |
| Flertall bestemt | ledene | Lederne ble beskrevet i dagboka. | The feelings of loathing were described in the diary. |

**Form status:** Headword and applicable forms reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Deep aversion; distinct from the verb lede “lead”.
