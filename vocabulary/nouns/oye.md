## øye

**English:** eye

**Topics:** health, body

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et (intetkjønn) | Et øye reagerte på det sterke lyset. | One eye reacted to the bright light. |
| Entall ubestemt | øye | Hun fikk støv i et øye. | She got dust in one eye. |
| Entall bestemt | øyet | Øyet hennes var rødt. | Her eye was red. |
| Flertall ubestemt, øye-paradigme | øyer | Han hadde blå øyer. | He had blue eyes. |
| Flertall ubestemt, øyne-paradigme | øyne | Hun lukket øynene. | She closed her eyes. |
| Flertall bestemt, øye-paradigme | øya / øyene | Øya hans var slitne. / Øyene hans var slitne. | His eyes were tired. / His eyes were tired. |
| Flertall bestemt, øyne-paradigme | øynene | Øynene hennes var lukket. | Her eyes were closed. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling, gender and forms checked; examples authored for teaching.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka accepts *øyer* and *øyne* as indefinite plural, and *øya*, *øyene* and *øynene* as definite plural. The table gives each listed alternative an example; forms with different stems are kept clear.
