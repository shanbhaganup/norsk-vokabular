## brus

**English:** rush; roar; rustle

**Topics:** nature, sound

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et brus fra fossen fylte dalen. | A roar from the waterfall filled the valley. |
| Entall ubestemt | brus | Vi hørte brus fra elva om natten. | We heard the rush of the river at night. |
| Entall bestemt | bruset | Bruset fra bladene stilnet etter hvert. | The rustling of the leaves gradually died down. |
| Flertall ubestemt | brus | Ulike brus fra skogen kunne høres i mørket. | Different rushing sounds from the forest could be heard in the dark. |
| Flertall bestemt | brusa | Brusa fra vannet og vinden blandet seg. | The roars of the water and wind blended together. |
| Flertall bestemt | brusene | Brusene fra vannet og vinden blandet seg. | The rushing sounds of the water and wind blended together. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.
