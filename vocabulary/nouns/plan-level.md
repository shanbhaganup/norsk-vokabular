## plan

**English:** plane; level

**Topics:** space; abstract concepts

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et plan kan være helt vannrett. | A plane can be completely horizontal. |
| Entall ubestemt | plan | Veien krysser jernbanen i et plan. | The road crosses the railway on one level. |
| Entall bestemt | planet | Planet ligger under takflaten. | The plane lies beneath the roof surface. |
| Flertall ubestemt | plan | Veikrysset har to plan. | The road junction has two levels. |
| Flertall bestemt | plana | Plana i modellen er parallelle. | The planes in the model are parallel. |
| Flertall bestemt | planene | Planene i modellen møtes i én linje. | The planes in the model meet in one line. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Dictionary homonym plan 2, neuter: a plane or level. Both accepted definite plurals are illustrated.
