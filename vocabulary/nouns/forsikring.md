## forsikring

**English:** insurance; insurance cover

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei forsikring kan dekke skader. / En forsikring kan dekke skader. | A policy can cover damage. / An insurance policy can cover damage. |
| Entall ubestemt | forsikring | Familien kjøpte ei forsikring for huset. | The family bought insurance for the house. |
| Entall bestemt | forsikringa / forsikringen | Forsikringa dekker vannskaden. / Forsikringen dekker vannskaden. | The policy covers the water damage. / The insurance covers the water damage. |
