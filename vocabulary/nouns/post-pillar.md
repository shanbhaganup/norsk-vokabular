## post

**English:** post; pillar; support

**Topics:** construction

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En post står midt i låvedøra. | A post stands in the middle of the barn door. |
| Entall ubestemt | post | De satte en post ved porten. | They placed a post by the gate. |
| Entall bestemt | posten | Posten støtter taket. | The post supports the roof. |
| Flertall ubestemt | poster | Flere poster holder gjerdet oppe. | Several posts hold up the fence. |
| Flertall bestemt | postene | Postene ble skiftet ut i sommer. | The posts were replaced this summer. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Distinct dictionary homonym 1: a pillar, support or pole. The source’s broad “mail” translation does not describe this sense; it is included because its dictionary entry is merged into the reference record.
