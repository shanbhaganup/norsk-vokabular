## reise

**English:** journey; trip

**Topics:** travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei reise / En reise kan gi nye perspektiver. | A journey can offer new perspectives. |
| Entall ubestemt | reise | Vi planla ei reise langs kysten. | We planned a trip along the coast. |
| Entall bestemt | reisa / reisen | Reisa / Reisen tok nesten fem timer. | The journey took almost five hours. |
| Flertall ubestemt | reiser | Flere reiser ble kansellert på grunn av været. | Several trips were cancelled because of the weather. |
| Flertall bestemt | reisene | Reisene deres gikk gjennom små kystbyer. | Their journeys went through small coastal towns. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine forms are accepted. Cross-POS: reise is also a verb; that is a distinct entry.
