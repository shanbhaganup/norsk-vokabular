## mark

**English:** open land; countryside; ground

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei mark strakte seg mellom skogen og elva. | An open field stretched between the forest and the river. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mark strakte seg mellom skogen og elva. | An open field stretched between the forest and the river. |
| Entall ubestemt | mark | Vi gikk gjennom skog og mark. | We walked through forest and open country. |
| Entall bestemt | marka | Marka var frossen etter nattefrosten. | The ground was frozen after the overnight frost. |
| Entall bestemt | marken | Marken var frossen etter nattefrosten. | The ground was frozen after the overnight frost. |
| Flertall ubestemt | marker | Bøndene arbeidet ute på marker langt fra bygda. | The farmers worked in fields far from the village. |
| Flertall bestemt | markene | Markene rundt gården lå under snø. | The fields around the farm were covered in snow. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene entry 1 gender and masculine plural paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** This is `mark 1` (uncultivated/open land, terrain or ground), not the weight, currency or animal homonyms. Both feminine and masculine are accepted in the singular; the cited inflection table gives the plural `marker / markene` under masculine. Do not combine these forms with the other `mark` entries.
