## pulver

**English:** powder

**Topics:** materials, food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et pulver kan løses opp i vann. | A powder can dissolve in water. |
| Entall ubestemt | pulver | Hun helte et pulver i bollen. | She poured a powder into the bowl. |
