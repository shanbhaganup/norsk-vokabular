## rest

**English:** remainder; rest

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En rest kan brukes i en ny rett. | A leftover can be used in a new dish. |
| Entall ubestemt | rest | Jeg sparte en rest av brødet. | I saved a piece of the bread. |
| Entall bestemt | resten | Resten av maten står i kjøleskapet. | The rest of the food is in the refrigerator. |
| Flertall ubestemt | rester | Rester etter middagen ble pakket bort. | Leftovers from dinner were put away. |
| Flertall bestemt | restene | Restene ble varmet opp dagen etter. | The leftovers were reheated the next day. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Examples use the remaining-part sense, not the separate meaning “remains/traces”.
