## kø

**English:** queue; line

**Topics:** everyday life

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kø dannet seg utenfor bakeriet. | A queue formed outside the bakery. |
| Entall ubestemt | kø | Vi sto i en kø ved inngangen. | We stood in a queue by the entrance. |
| Entall bestemt | køen | Køen beveget seg sakte framover. | The queue moved forward slowly. |
| Flertall ubestemt | køer | Flere køer oppsto etter konserten. | Several queues formed after the concert. |
| Flertall bestemt | køene | Køene ble kortere da flere luker åpnet. | The queues got shorter when more counters opened. |

**Form status:** Headword and applicable forms reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The examples use people waiting their turn.
