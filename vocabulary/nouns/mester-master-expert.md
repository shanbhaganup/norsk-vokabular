## mester

**English:** master; expert; champion

**Topics:** work, sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mester viste lærlingene teknikken. | A master showed the apprentices the technique. |
| Entall ubestemt | mester | Hun ble mester i sjakk som tenåring. | She became a chess champion as a teenager. |
| Entall bestemt | mesteren | Mesteren ga råd til den nye lærlingen. | The master gave advice to the new apprentice. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mestere | Flere mestere underviste på verkstedet. | Several master craftspeople taught at the workshop. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mestre | Landets fremste mestre møttes til turnering. | The country’s top masters met for a tournament. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mestrer | Tre mestrer tok imot lærlingene. | Three masters welcomed the apprentices. |
| Flertall bestemt | mesterne | Mesterne delte ut prisene etter finalen. | The champions handed out the prizes after the final. |
| Flertall bestemt | mestrene | Mestrene delte ut prisene etter finalen. | The champions handed out the prizes after the final. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword and listed plural alternatives checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Ordbøkene lists `mestere`, `mestre` and `mestrer` as indefinite plural alternatives and `mesterne` / `mestrene` as definite plural alternatives. The word can refer to a teacher/mentor, a craftsperson with a master certificate or a highly skilled person/champion. Some plural variants are less frequent; all source-listed alternatives are illustrated.
