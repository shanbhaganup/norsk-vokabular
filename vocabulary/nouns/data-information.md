## data

**English:** data

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et data ble registrert i forsøket. | One data point was recorded in the experiment. |
| Entall ubestemt | data | Et data ble registrert i forsøket. | One data point was recorded in the experiment. |
| Entall bestemt | dataet | Dataet ble kontrollert før analysen. | The data was checked before the analysis. |
| Flertall ubestemt | data | Vi samlet inn data fra mange kilder. | We collected data from many sources. |
| Flertall bestemt | dataene | Dataene ble analysert av forskerne. | The data were analyzed by the researchers. |

**Form status:** Source paradigm recorded; dictionary forms checked.

**Notes:** Neuter sense for information/data. The source also merges the masculine short form for datamaskin; see the separate entry. Singular count use ?et data? is less common than plural or mass use; examples are constructed as countable data points.
