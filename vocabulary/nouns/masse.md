## masse

**English:** mass

**Topics:** science, materials

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En masse kan endre form under trykk. | A mass can change shape under pressure. |
| Entall ubestemt | masse | Masse samler seg i bunnen av røret. | Mass collects at the bottom of the pipe. |
| Entall bestemt | massen | Massen ble varmet opp forsiktig. | The mass was heated carefully. |
| Flertall ubestemt | masser | Ulike masser reagerer forskjellig på varme. | Different masses react differently to heat. |
| Flertall bestemt | massene | Massene ble veid før forsøket. | The masses were weighed before the experiment. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The scientific material sense is countable in technical contexts; masse also occurs as a quantity word and adjective, which are separate uses.
