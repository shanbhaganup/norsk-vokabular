## orden

**English:** order; arrangement; system

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En orden kan beskrive et system. | An order can describe a system. |
| Entall ubestemt | orden | En orden gjør arbeidsdagen enklere. | Order makes the workday easier. |
| Entall bestemt | ordenen | Bøkene står i alfabetisk orden. | The books are arranged in alphabetical order. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ordener | Ordenen i arkivet ble gjenopprettet. | Order in the archive was restored. |
| Flertall bestemt | ordenene | Flere ordener finnes i biologisk klassifikasjon. | Several orders exist in biological classification. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Dictionary includes several distinct senses such as sequence, orderly state, lawfulness, biological order, association and decoration. Examples cover common order/system senses; `orden` remains distinct from the different lemma `ordre`.
