## frø

**English:** seed; beginning or young creature (figurative)

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et frø spirte i potten. | A seed sprouted in the pot. |
| Entall ubestemt | frø | Jeg sådde et frø i vår. | I sowed a seed this spring. |
| Entall bestemt | frøet | Frøet spirte etter noen dager. | The seed sprouted after a few days. |
| Flertall ubestemt | frø | Frø spirer best i fuktig jord. | Seeds germinate best in moist soil. |
| Flertall bestemt | frøa / frøene | Frøa lå i en papirpose. / Frøene lå i en papirpose. | The seeds were in a paper bag. / The seeds were in a paper bag. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and paradigm checked against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The definite plural alternatives frøa and frøene are both dictionary-listed and separately illustrated. The dictionary also records figurative senses such as a beginning or a young creature; examples use the seed sense.
