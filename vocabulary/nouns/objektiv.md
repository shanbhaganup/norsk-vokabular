## objektiv

**English:** objective lens

**Topics:** photography, science

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et objektiv ligger i veska. | A lens is in the bag. |
| Entall ubestemt | objektiv | Kameraet trenger et nytt objektiv. | The camera needs a new lens. |
| Entall bestemt | objektivet | Objektivet er rent. | The lens is clean. |
| Flertall ubestemt | objektiver | Butikken selger flere objektiver. | The store sells several lenses. |
| Flertall bestemt | objektivene | Objektivene passer til kameraet. | The lenses fit the camera. |

**Form status:** Dictionary noun and paradigm checked; examples are original.

**Notes:** Separate neuter noun homograph objektiv (“the lens nearest the object in an optical instrument”) from adjective objektiv (“impartial”).
