## grøt

**English:** thick, soft mass; mush

**Topics:** food, texture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En grøt av kjeks og saft lå igjen i bollen. | A mush of biscuits and juice was left in the bowl. |
| Entall ubestemt | grøt | Kjeksene ble tygd til grøt. | The biscuits were chewed into mush. |
| Entall bestemt | grøten | Grøten i munnen var vanskelig å svelge. | The mush in the mouth was difficult to swallow. |
| Flertall ubestemt | grøter | De beskrev grøter med ulik tykkelse i rapporten. | They described mushes of different thicknesses in the report. |
| Flertall bestemt | grøtene | Grøtene i prøvene hadde ulik konsistens. | The mushes in the samples had different textures. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original bilingual teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The dictionary gives this as a distinct sense of *grøt*. Plural forms are count uses referring to separate kinds or samples of the mass.
