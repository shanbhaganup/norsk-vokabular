## allianse

**English:** alliance

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | De to partiene dannet en allianse før valget. | The two parties formed an alliance before the election. |
| Entall ubestemt | allianse | Forslaget fikk støtte fra en bred politisk allianse. | The proposal received support from a broad political alliance. |
| Entall bestemt | alliansen | Alliansen ble oppløst da partiene ble uenige om budsjettet. | The alliance dissolved when the parties disagreed on the budget. |
| Flertall ubestemt | allianser | Små partier inngår ofte allianser for å få gjennomslag. | Small parties often form alliances to get their proposals adopted. |
| Flertall bestemt | alliansene | Alliansene endret seg da nye partier kom inn i kommunestyret. | The alliances shifted when new parties joined the municipal council. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The source gender was corrected to masculine. An allianse typically involves cooperation or mutual support.
