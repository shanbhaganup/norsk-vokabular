## nisse

**English:** nisse; Scandinavian household spirit

**Topics:** folklore

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En nisse skulle passe på gården. | A nisse was supposed to look after the farm. |
| Entall ubestemt | nisse | I folketroen bor en nisse gjerne nær mennesker. | In folklore, a nisse often lives near people. |
| Entall bestemt | nissen | Nissen fikk grøt satt ut i låven. | The nisse was left porridge in the barn. |
| Flertall ubestemt | nisser | Gamle fortellinger beskriver nisser som små menn. | Old stories describe nisser as little men. |
| Flertall bestemt | nissene | Nissene holdt til i uthusene. | The nisser lived in the outbuildings. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms reviewed; original examples authored for the labelled forms and senses.

**Notes:** Sense 1 of Bokmålsordboka 40856. The dictionary also has the Christmas figure and a derogatory “fool” sense; the Christmas figure appears in a companion entry and the insult sense is outside the source gloss.
