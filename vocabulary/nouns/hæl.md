## hæl

**English:** heel (of the foot, a sock, or footwear)

**Topics:** body, clothing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En hæl kan bli øm etter en lang tur. | A heel can become sore after a long walk. |
| Entall ubestemt | hæl | Skoen har en lav hæl. | The shoe has a low heel. |
| Entall bestemt | hælen | Hælen på støvelen løsnet. | The heel on the boot came loose. |
| Flertall ubestemt | hæler | Hun kjøpte sko med brede hæler. | She bought shoes with wide heels. |
| Flertall bestemt | hælene | Hælene på støvlene er slitt. | The heels on the boots are worn. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** The source English gloss *heels* is plural; the lemma is singular *hæl*. The dictionary confirms the same noun for the back of a foot and the back/lower part of a sock or shoe.
