## fryser

**English:** freezer

**Topics:** home appliances

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En fryser står i kjelleren. | A freezer is in the basement. |
| Entall ubestemt | fryser | Vi kjøpte en ny fryser. | We bought a new freezer. |
| Entall bestemt | fryseren | Fryseren må avrimes. | The freezer needs defrosting. |
| Flertall ubestemt | frysere | Restauranten har to frysere. | The restaurant has two freezers. |
| Flertall bestemt | fryserne | Fryserne står på lageret. | The freezers are in storage. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and paradigm checked against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** This noun means a freezer (also a deep freezer). Distinguish it from the verb fryser, 'is freezing'.
