## løgner

**English:** liar

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Han er en løgner som stadig finner på historier. | He is a liar who keeps making up stories. |
| Entall ubestemt | løgner | En løgner kan skade tilliten i en gruppe. | A liar can damage trust in a group. |
| Entall bestemt | løgneren | Løgneren innrømmet til slutt hva han hadde gjort. | The liar finally admitted what he had done. |
| Flertall ubestemt | løgnere | Flere løgnere ble dømt etter at saken kom for retten. | Several liars were convicted after the case went to court. |
| Flertall bestemt | løgnerne | Løgnerne ble avslørt da historiene deres ikke stemte overens. | The liars were exposed when their stories did not match. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling, gender and inflection checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This headword means a person who lies. It is a separate noun from *løgner*, the plural of *løgn*, despite the identical spelling.
