## manus

**English:** script

**Topics:** media, writing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et manus kan endres under prøvene. | A script can be changed during rehearsals. |
| Entall ubestemt | manus | Regissøren leste et manus før møtet. | The director read a script before the meeting. |
| Entall bestemt | manuset | Manuset manglet en avslutning. | The script lacked an ending. |
| Flertall ubestemt | manus / manuser | Flere manus / manuser kom inn til vurdering. | Several scripts were submitted for review. |
| Flertall bestemt | manusa / manusene | Manusa / Manusene ble delt ut til skuespillerne. | The scripts were handed out to the actors. |
