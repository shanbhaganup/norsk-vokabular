## streik

**English:** strike

**Topics:** work; society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En streik kan påvirke busstilbudet. | A strike can affect bus services. |
| Entall ubestemt | streik | De ansatte varslet streik etter forhandlingene. | The employees announced a strike after the negotiations. |
| Entall bestemt | streiken | Streiken startet ved midnatt. | The strike began at midnight. |
| Flertall ubestemt | streiker | Streiker førte til at flere tog ble stående. | Strikes caused several trains to remain halted. |
| Flertall bestemt | streikene | Streikene ble avsluttet etter en ny avtale. | The strikes ended after a new agreement. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** Noun; the verb is streike.
