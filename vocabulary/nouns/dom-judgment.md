## dom

**English:** judgment

**Topics:** law

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En dom. | A judgment. |
| Entall ubestemt | dom | Retten avsa en dom etter høringen. | The court delivered a judgment after the hearing. |
| Entall bestemt | dommen | Dommen ble anket. | The judgment was appealed. |
| Flertall ubestemt | dommer | Retten avsa flere dommer den dagen. | The court delivered several judgments that day. |
| Flertall bestemt | dommene | Dommene ble publisert på nett. | The judgments were published online. |

**Form status:** Source paradigm recorded; dictionary forms checked.

**Notes:** Sense: a court judgment or an assessment. This entry uses the judgment paradigm; the separate cathedral sense has a different paradigm.
