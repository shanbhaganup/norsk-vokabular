## alt

**English:** alto voice; alto singer

**Topics:** music

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En alt synger vanligvis den laveste stemmen i koret. | An alto usually sings the lowest voice in the choir. |
| Entall ubestemt | alt | Hun synger alt i et lokalt kor. | She sings alto in a local choir. |
| Entall bestemt | alten | Alten begynner på den dype tonen. | The alto starts on the low note. |
| Flertall ubestemt | alter | Koret trenger flere alter til konserten. | The choir needs more altos for the concert. |
| Flertall bestemt | altene | Altene øvde med pianisten før konserten. | The altos rehearsed with the pianist before the concert. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** A separate masculine noun homonym. The imported English gloss “everything” does not describe this sense.
