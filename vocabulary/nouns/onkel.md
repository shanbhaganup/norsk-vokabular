## onkel

**English:** uncle

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En onkel kom på besøk i går. | An uncle came to visit yesterday. |
| Entall ubestemt | onkel | Jeg har en onkel i Bergen. | I have an uncle in Bergen. |
| Entall bestemt | onkelen | Onkelen min bor i nord. | My uncle lives in the north. |
| Flertall ubestemt | onkler | Barna har flere onkler. | The children have several uncles. |
| Flertall bestemt | onklene | Onklene samlet seg rundt bordet. | The uncles gathered around the table. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Dictionary also records older uses for an older male relative or children's entertainer, and slang for police. This entry follows the kinship sense in the source record.
