## slip

**English:** polished surface; abrasive finish

**Topics:** craft, materials

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En slip kan gi skiene bedre glid. | A ski base finish can give skis better glide. |
| Entall ubestemt | slip | Skismøreren prøvde en slip som passet føret. | The ski technician tried a finish suited to the snow conditions. |
| Entall bestemt | slipen | Slipen på skiene ble kontrollert før rennet. | The finish on the skis was checked before the race. |
| Flertall ubestemt | sliper | Ulike sliper ble testet på banen. | Different finishes were tested on the course. |
| Flertall bestemt | slipene | Slipene ga ulikt feste på snøen. | The finishes gave different grip on the snow. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source gloss “slip” is broad; Bokmålsordboka defines slip as a polished surface or ground edge, including a ski base finish.
