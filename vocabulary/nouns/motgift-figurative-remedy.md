## motgift

**English:** antidote; remedy against a harmful tendency

**Topics:** society, ideas

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei motgift mot hat kan være åpen dialog. | Open dialogue can be an antidote to hatred. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En motgift mot hat kan være åpen dialog. | Open dialogue can be an antidote to hatred. |
| Entall ubestemt | motgift | Humor kan være motgift mot pessimisme. | Humor can be an antidote to pessimism. |
| Entall bestemt | motgifta | Motgifta mot intoleranse må brukes aktivt. | The antidote to intolerance must be actively used. |
| Entall bestemt | motgiften | Motgiften mot intoleranse må brukes aktivt. | The antidote to intolerance must be actively used. |
| Flertall ubestemt | motgifter | Kunst og humor er motgifter mot fanatisme. | Art and humor are antidotes to fanaticism. |
| Flertall bestemt | motgiftene | Motgiftene mot fordommer må være langsiktige. | The antidotes to prejudice must be long-term. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original examples authored for this sense.

**Notes:** Figurative sense of Bokmålsordboka 39253: a means of counteracting a phenomenon. The same source record also describes the literal antidote sense in the companion health entry. Feminine and masculine singular forms are accepted.
