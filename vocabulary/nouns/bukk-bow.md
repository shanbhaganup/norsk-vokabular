## bukk

**English:** bow (inclination)

**Topics:** greetings, formal occasions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et bukk viste respekt for verten. | A bow showed respect for the host. |
| Entall ubestemt | bukk | Han hilste med et bukk. | He greeted them with a bow. |
| Entall bestemt | bukket | Bukket var kort og høflig. | The bow was brief and polite. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bukk | De utvekslet bukk før forestillingen. | They exchanged bows before the performance. |
| Flertall bestemt | bukka | Bukka ble fulgt av applaus. | The bows were followed by applause. |
| Flertall bestemt | bukkene | Bukkene ble fulgt av applaus. | The bows were followed by applause. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers a greeting made by bending the head and upper body; it is neuter, unlike the masculine animal and equipment senses.
