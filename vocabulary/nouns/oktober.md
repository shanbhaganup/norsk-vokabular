## oktober

**English:** October

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En oktober kan være mild ved kysten. | An October can be mild by the coast. |
| Entall ubestemt | oktober | Vi flyttet hit i oktober. | We moved here in October. |
| Entall bestemt | oktoberen | Oktoberen i fjor var regnfull. | Last October was rainy. |
| Flertall ubestemt | oktoberer | Flere oktoberer på rad har vært varme. | Several Octobers in a row have been warm. |
| Flertall bestemt | oktoberene | Oktoberene på 1990-tallet var kjøligere her. | The Octobers in the 1990s were cooler here. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Plural forms refer to separate calendar years and are uncommon.
