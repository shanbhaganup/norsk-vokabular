## andel

**English:** share, portion

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Hun betalte en andel av reparasjonskostnadene. | She paid a share of the repair costs. |
| Entall ubestemt | andel | Alle må dekke sin andel av utgiftene. | Everyone has to cover their share of the expenses. |
| Entall bestemt | andelen | Han solgte andelen sin i hytta til søsteren. | He sold his share in the cabin to his sister. |
| Flertall ubestemt | andeler | De kjøpte andeler i det lokale samvirket. | They bought shares in the local cooperative. |
| Flertall bestemt | andelene | Andelene i selskapet ble fordelt mellom arvingene. | The shares in the company were divided among the heirs. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The source gender was corrected to masculine. Andel can refer to a share of costs or a stake in something. Aksje is the specific word for a share of stock.
