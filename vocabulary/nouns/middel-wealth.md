## middel

**English:** wealth; property

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En middel ble overført til arvingene. | An estate was transferred to the heirs. |
| Entall ubestemt | middel | Familien hadde middel nok til å kjøpe gården. | The family had enough wealth to buy the farm. |
| Entall bestemt | middelen | Familien forvaltet middelen etter testamentet. | The family managed the estate according to the will. |
| Flertall ubestemt | midler | Gamle dokumenter beskriver store midler på gården. | Old documents describe considerable wealth on the farm. |
| Flertall bestemt | midlene | Midlene ble fordelt mellom arvingene. | The assets were divided among the heirs. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene entry 3 masculine paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** This is `middel 3`, meaning wealth or property, not the average sense (`middel 2`) or the neuter noun for means/remedies (`middel 1`). The dictionary gives the same masculine forms as `middel 2`.
