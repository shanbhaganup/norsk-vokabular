## skrekk

**English:** horror; fear

**Topics:** culture, feelings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En skrekk kan sitte i lenge etter en vond opplevelse. | A fear can linger long after a bad experience. |
| Entall ubestemt | skrekk | Hun kjente en skrekk for mørket som barn. | As a child, she felt a fear of the dark. |
| Entall bestemt | skrekken | Skrekken slapp taket da lyset kom på. | The fear eased when the light came on. |
| Flertall ubestemt | skrekker | Ulike skrekker preger fortellingene. | Different fears shape the stories. |
| Flertall bestemt | skrekkene | Skrekkene hans ble mindre etter hvert. | His fears gradually became smaller. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Examples use fear as an emotion; the word also names the horror genre.
