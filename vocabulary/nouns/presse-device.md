## presse

**English:** press (device for pressing)

**Topics:** tools; household

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei presse brukes til å presse epler. | A press is used to press apples. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En presse brukes til å presse epler. | A press is used to press apples. |
| Entall ubestemt | presse | Hun kjøpte ei presse til verkstedet. | She bought a press for the workshop. |
| Entall ubestemt | presse | De kjøpte en presse til verkstedet. | They bought a press for the workshop. |
| Entall bestemt | pressa | Pressa står i kjelleren. | The press is in the basement. |
| Entall bestemt | pressen | Pressen står i kjelleren. | The press is in the basement. |
| Flertall ubestemt | presser | Flere presser brukes i produksjonen. | Several presses are used in production. |
| Flertall bestemt | pressene | Pressene må rengjøres etter bruk. | The presses must be cleaned after use. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Dictionary sense 1: a device used to press something. Separate from a printing press, the news media/journalists, and favorable or unfavorable press coverage. Both genders and gender-specific singular forms are included.
