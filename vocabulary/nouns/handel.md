## handel

**English:** trade; commerce

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En handel mellom landene ble inngått i vår. | A trade agreement between the countries was made this spring. |
| Entall ubestemt | handel | En handel mellom landene ble inngått i vår. | A trade agreement between the countries was made this spring. |
| Entall bestemt | handelen | Handelen med fisk økte i fjor. | The fish trade grew last year. |
| Flertall ubestemt | handler | Små handler på nettet blir registrert automatisk. | Small online transactions are recorded automatically. |
| Flertall bestemt | handlene | Handlene ble gjennomført på ulike tidspunkter. | The transactions took place at different times. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The dictionary plural handler/handlene can refer to individual transactions; the singular is the general trade sense.
