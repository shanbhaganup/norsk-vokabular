## sete

**English:** seat

**Topics:** transport, home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et sete må være godt festet i bilen. | A seat must be securely fastened in the car. |
| Entall ubestemt | sete | Han fant et sete ved vinduet. | He found a seat by the window. |
| Entall bestemt | setet | Setet var ledig. | The seat was available. |
| Flertall ubestemt | seter | Flere seter sto tomme på bussen. | Several seats were empty on the bus. |
| Flertall bestemt | seta / setene | Seta / Setene ble rengjort etter turen. | The seats were cleaned after the trip. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both definite plural variants are accepted; a seat for a vehicle is the sense used.
