## pleiehjem

**English:** nursing home (older term for a nursing home)

**Topics:** home; care

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et pleiehjem lå ved den gamle kirken. | A nursing home stood beside the old church. |
| Entall ubestemt | pleiehjem | Kommunen bygde et pleiehjem for eldre. | The municipality built a nursing home for older people. |
| Entall bestemt | pleiehjemmet | Pleiehjemmet ble senere renovert. | The nursing home was later renovated. |
| Flertall ubestemt | pleiehjem | Flere pleiehjem ble lagt ned. | Several nursing homes were closed. |
| Flertall bestemt | pleiehjemma | Pleiehjemma i området samarbeider. | The nursing homes in the area cooperate. |
| Flertall bestemt | pleiehjemmene | Pleiehjemmene fikk nye kjøkken. | The nursing homes got new kitchens. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** The dictionary marks this institutional sense as an older term for sykehjem. A second historical sense, a private home raising foster children, is outside the source gloss.
