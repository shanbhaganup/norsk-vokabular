## behov

**English:** need

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Det er et stort behov for flere boliger. | There is a great need for more housing. |
| Entall ubestemt | behov | Hun har behov for en pause. | She needs a break. |
| Entall bestemt | behovet | Behovet for hjelp økte om vinteren. | The need for help increased in winter. |
| Flertall ubestemt | behov | Barn og voksne har ulike behov. | Children and adults have different needs. |
| Flertall bestemt | behova | Planen tar hensyn til behova i bygda. | The plan takes the village’s needs into account. |
| Flertall bestemt | behovene | Vi gikk gjennom behovene sammen. | We went over the needs together. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The construction behov for identifies what is needed. Both definite plural endings are accepted.
