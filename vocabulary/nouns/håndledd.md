## håndledd

**English:** wrist

**Topics:** health, body

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et håndledd kan bli overbelastet. | A wrist can become strained. |
| Entall ubestemt | håndledd | Hun skadet håndledd i fallet. | She injured a wrist in the fall. |
| Entall bestemt | håndleddet | Håndleddet er litt hovent. | The wrist is a little swollen. |
| Flertall ubestemt | håndledd | Begge håndledd ble undersøkt av legen. | Both wrists were examined by the doctor. |
| Flertall bestemt | håndledda | Håndledda verket etter treningen. | The wrists ached after the workout. |
| Flertall bestemt | håndleddene | Håndleddene verket etter treningen. | The wrists ached after the workout. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** The dictionary accepts both *håndledd* and the spelling variant *handledd*. For the source lemma *håndledd*, the definite plural alternatives *håndledda* and *håndleddene* are both listed and illustrated.
