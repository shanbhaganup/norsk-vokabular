## trekk

**English:** feature; characteristic

**Topics:** description

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et viktig trekk ved boka er den tørre humoren. | An important feature of the book is its dry humour. |
| Entall ubestemt | trekk | Et typisk trekk ved arten er den lange halen. | A typical feature of the species is its long tail. |
| Entall bestemt | trekket | Trekket ved løsningen var enkelheten. | The feature of the solution was its simplicity. |
| Flertall ubestemt | trekk | Flere trekk skiller de to artene. | Several features distinguish the two species. |
| Flertall bestemt | trekka | Trekka i portrettet ble framhevet med lys. | The features in the portrait were emphasized with light. |
| Flertall bestemt | trekkene | Trekkene i portrettet ble framhevet med lys. | The features in the portrait were emphasized with light. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** This entry covers the neuter sense “feature; characteristic.” The masculine homonym meaning “draft” has separate inflections and is not included. Bokmålsordboka accepts the plural definite forms *trekka* and *trekkene*.
