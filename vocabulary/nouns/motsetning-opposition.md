## motsetning

**English:** opposite; contrast

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei motsetning mellom planene ble tydelig. | A contrast between the plans became clear. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En motsetning mellom planene ble tydelig. | A contrast between the plans became clear. |
| Entall ubestemt | motsetning | Jeg ser en motsetning i argumentet. | I see a contradiction in the argument. |
| Entall bestemt | motsetninga | Motsetninga mellom dem var åpenbar. | The contrast between them was obvious. |
| Entall bestemt | motsetningen | Motsetningen mellom dem var åpenbar. | The contrast between them was obvious. |
| Flertall ubestemt | motsetninger | De to partiene har store motsetninger. | The two parties have major differences. |
| Flertall bestemt | motsetningene | Motsetningene ble diskutert på møtet. | The disagreements were discussed at the meeting. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original teaching examples supplied for every listed form.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine forms are accepted in the singular; plural forms are shared.
