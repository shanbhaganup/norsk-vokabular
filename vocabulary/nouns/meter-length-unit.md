## meter

**English:** metre (unit of length)

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En meter tilsvarer hundre centimeter. | One metre equals one hundred centimetres. |
| Entall ubestemt | meter | Avstanden er én meter. | The distance is one metre. |
| Entall bestemt | meteren | Meteren ble lagt langs kanten av bordet. | The metre was laid along the edge of the table. |
| Flertall ubestemt | meter | Veggen er fire meter lang. | The wall is four metres long. |
| Flertall bestemt | meterne | De siste meterne av løypa var bratte. | The last metres of the course were steep. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** This is `meter 1`, the unit of length. Its plural is identical to the singular (`meter`); the separate countable noun for a measuring stick has plural `metere` and is documented in another entry.
