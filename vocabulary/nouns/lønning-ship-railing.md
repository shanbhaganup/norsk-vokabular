## lønning

**English:** deck railing, bulwark

**Topics:** transport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En lønning går langs kanten av dekket. | A railing runs along the edge of the deck. |
| Entall ubestemt | lønning | Båten fikk ny lønning rundt akterdekket. | The boat got a new railing around the aft deck. |
| Entall bestemt | lønningen | Lønningen var malt hvit. | The railing was painted white. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lønninger | Verftet reparerte lønninger på flere småbåter. | The shipyard repaired railings on several small boats. |
| Flertall bestemt | lønningene | Lønningene på fartøyene ble kontrollert før avgang. | The railings on the vessels were checked before departure. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and inflection checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This is the separate nautical homograph, a railing around the deck of a small vessel. It is distinct from *lønning* meaning pay. The word is specialized maritime vocabulary.
