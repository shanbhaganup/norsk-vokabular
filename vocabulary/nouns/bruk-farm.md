## bruk

**English:** farm; farm holding

**Topics:** rural life, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et bruk ligger ved skogkanten. | A farm lies at the edge of the forest. |
| Entall ubestemt | bruk | De kjøpte et bruk i dalen. | They bought a farm in the valley. |
| Entall bestemt | bruket | Bruket har både skog og jord. | The farm has both woodland and farmland. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bruk | Flere bruk ligger langs elva. | Several farms lie along the river. |
| Flertall bestemt | bruka | Bruka i dalen drives av lokale familier. | The farms in the valley are run by local families. |
| Flertall bestemt | brukene | Brukene i dalen drives av lokale familier. | The farms in the valley are run by local families. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers a separately operated farm or holding (dictionary entry II), distinct from bruk meaning use. The neuter plural has two accepted definite forms.
