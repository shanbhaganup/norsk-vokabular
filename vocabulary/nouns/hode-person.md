## hode

**English:** person (counted by head)

**Topics:** people

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Det kom ett hode til møtet. | One person came to the meeting. |
| Entall ubestemt | hode | Billetten koster hundre kroner per hode. | The ticket costs one hundred kroner per person. |
| Entall bestemt | hodet | Prisen per hodet ble regnet feil. | The price per person was calculated incorrectly. |
| Flertall ubestemt | hoder | De telte alle hoder i gruppa. | They counted everyone in the group. |
| Flertall bestemt | hoda / hodene | Hoda i køen fikk utdelt nummer. / Hodene i køen fikk utdelt nummer. | The people in the queue were given numbers. / The people in the queue were given numbers. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** Collective/count sense meaning a person, as in per head; singular definite is shown only in a natural counting construction.
