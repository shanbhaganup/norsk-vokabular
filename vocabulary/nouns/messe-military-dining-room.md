## messe

**English:** mess; dining room for military or ship personnel

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei messe lå ved siden av lugarene. | A mess room was next to the cabins. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En messe lå ved siden av lugarene. | A mess room was next to the cabins. |
| Entall ubestemt | messe | Mannskapet spiste i en messe etter vakten. | The crew ate in a mess room after their watch. |
| Entall bestemt | messa | Messa om bord var åpen hele kvelden. | The mess aboard was open all evening. |
| Entall bestemt | messen | Messen om bord var åpen hele kvelden. | The mess aboard was open all evening. |
| Flertall ubestemt | messer | Skipene hadde egne messer for ulike grupper. | The ships had separate mess rooms for different groups. |
| Flertall bestemt | messene | Messene på basen ble pusset opp i sommer. | The mess rooms at the base were renovated this summer. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene entry 3 genders and masculine paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** This is `messe 3`, a dining and common room for military personnel, ship crews or similar groups. The reference groups it with two unrelated senses of `messe`. Forms follow the dictionary: feminine/masculine singular alternatives and masculine plural `messer / messene`.
