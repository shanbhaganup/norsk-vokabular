# reke

**English:** shrimp

**Topics:** food; animals

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei reke lå på kanten av tallerkenen. | A shrimp lay at the edge of the plate. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En reke lå på kanten av tallerkenen. | A shrimp lay at the edge of the plate. |
| Entall ubestemt | reke | Hun la ei reke på brødskiva. | She put a shrimp on the slice of bread. |
| Entall bestemt | reka | Reka var fersk. | The shrimp was fresh. |
| Entall bestemt | reken | Reken var fersk. | The shrimp was fresh. |
| Flertall ubestemt | reker | Vi bestilte reker med majones. | We ordered shrimp with mayonnaise. |
| Flertall bestemt | rekene | Rekene ble servert kalde. | The shrimp were served cold. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Dictionary homonym reke 1 refers to the crustacean (including deep-water shrimp). Both feminine and masculine forms are accepted.
