# rabatt

**English:** flower bed; planted median strip

**Topics:** garden; transport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En rabatt rammer inn blomsterhagen. | A flower bed frames the garden. |
| Entall ubestemt | rabatt | Vi plantet lavendel i en rabatt langs muren. | We planted lavender in a flower bed along the wall. |
| Entall bestemt | rabatten | Rabatten mellom veibanene var gresskledd. | The median strip between the roadways was grass-covered. |
| Flertall ubestemt | rabatter | Kommunen anla nye rabatter ved inngangen. | The municipality created new flower beds by the entrance. |
| Flertall bestemt | rabattene | Rabattene langs veien ble klippet i juni. | The roadside planted strips were mowed in June. |
