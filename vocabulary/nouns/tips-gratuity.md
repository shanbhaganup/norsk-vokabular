## tips

**English:** tip; gratuity

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En tips på ti prosent er vanlig. | A tip of ten per cent is customary. |
| Entall ubestemt | tips | Vi la igjen tips etter middagen. | We left a tip after dinner. |
| Entall bestemt | tipsen | Tipsen var allerede med på regningen. | The tip was already included in the bill. |
| Flertall ubestemt | tips | Han gir gjerne tips når servicen er god. | He gladly leaves tips when the service is good. |
| Flertall bestemt | tipsene | Tipsene går til personalet. | The tips go to the staff. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and forms checked; teaching examples authored.

**Notes:** This masculine noun means extra payment for service; neuter *tips* “piece of advice/information” is a separate entry.
