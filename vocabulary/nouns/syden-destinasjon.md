## Syden-destinasjon

**English:** southern holiday destination

**Topics:** travel

**Form status:** Derived compound forms from the cited headword.

**Notes:** The compound follows the inflection of its final element, destinasjon. Syden is a colloquial Norwegian name for sunny destinations to the south, often the Mediterranean. NoW source form: “Syden-destinasjon”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
