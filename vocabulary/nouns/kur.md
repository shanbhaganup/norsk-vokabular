## kur

**English:** cure; course of treatment

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kur kan vare i flere uker. | A course of treatment can last several weeks. |
| Entall ubestemt | kur | Legen foreslo en kur mot allergien. | The doctor suggested a treatment for the allergy. |
| Entall bestemt | kuren | Kuren hjalp mot symptomene. | The treatment helped with the symptoms. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kurer | Noen kurer krever regelmessige kontroller. | Some treatments require regular checkups. |
| Flertall bestemt | kurene | Kurene ble tilpasset hver pasient. | The treatments were tailored to each patient. |

**Form status:** Verified against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry for spelling, gender, and paradigm; original teaching examples authored for this project.

**Notes:** Kur 1 is the medical sense matching source cure. Homonymous kur 2 means courtship or ingratiation and is a distinct sense.
