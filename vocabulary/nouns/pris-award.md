## pris

**English:** prize; award

**Topics:** culture; achievement

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En pris deles ut etter konkurransen. | A prize is awarded after the competition. |
| Entall ubestemt | pris | Hun vant en pris for romanen. | She won a prize for the novel. |
| Entall bestemt | prisen | Prisen gikk til årets beste film. | The award went to the year’s best film. |
| Flertall ubestemt | priser | Flere priser ble delt ut under festivalen. | Several prizes were awarded during the festival. |
| Flertall bestemt | prisene | Prisene ble overrakt på scenen. | The awards were presented on stage. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Source gender corrected to masculine according to Ordbøkene. Dictionary homonym 1, sense 2: an award for good performance or a competition prize. Distinct from monetary price, praise and snuff pinch.
