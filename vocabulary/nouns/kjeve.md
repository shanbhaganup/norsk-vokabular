## kjeve

**English:** jaw; jaw of a tool

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kjeve kan bli øm etter behandlingen. | A jaw can become sore after the treatment. |
| Entall ubestemt | kjeve | Han skadet en kjeve i fallet. | He injured a jaw in the fall. |
| Entall bestemt | kjeven | Kjeven gjorde vondt da hun tygde. | Her jaw hurt when she chewed. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kjever | Verktøyet har to kjever som holder røret fast. | The tool has two jaws that hold the pipe in place. |
| Flertall bestemt | kjevene | Kjevene på skrustikka må rengjøres. | The jaws of the vice need cleaning. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and sense reviewed; paradigm verified except where noted.

**Notes:** The reference corrects the source gender to masculine. Bokmålsordboka confirms en and the paradigm kjeve–kjeven–kjever–kjevene. The word can refer to either jaw of the mouth or a gripping part of a tool; examples illustrate both senses.
