## karriere

**English:** career; professional path; in riding, a full gallop

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En karriere kan ta uventede retninger. | A career can take unexpected directions. |
| Entall ubestemt | karriere | Hun ønsker seg en karriere innen forskning. | She wants a career in research. |
| Entall bestemt | karrieren | Karrieren hennes startet i en liten bedrift. | Her career began at a small company. |
| Flertall ubestemt | karrierer | Noen karrierer krever mange års utdanning. | Some careers require many years of education. |
| Flertall bestemt | karrierene | Karrierene deres utviklet seg forskjellig. | Their careers developed differently. |
