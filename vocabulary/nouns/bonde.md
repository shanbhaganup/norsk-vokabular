## bonde

**English:** farmer

**Topics:** work, rural life

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bonde arbeider ute i dag. | A farmer is working outdoors today. |
| Entall ubestemt | bonde | Hun er bonde på en gård ved fjorden. | She is a farmer on a farm by the fjord. |
| Entall bestemt | bonden | Bonden solgte grønnsaker på torget. | The farmer sold vegetables at the market. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bønder | Bønder møttes på bygdemøtet. | Farmers met at the local community meeting. |
| Flertall bestemt | bøndene | Bøndene delte erfaringer om avlingene. | The farmers shared experiences about their crops. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The examples illustrate a person who runs a farm.
