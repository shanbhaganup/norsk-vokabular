## kontonummer

**English:** bank account number

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et kontonummer identifiserer bankkontoen. | An account number identifies the bank account. |
| Entall ubestemt | kontonummer | Kunden må oppgi et kontonummer. | The customer must provide an account number. |
| Entall bestemt | kontonummeret | Kontonummeret står på fakturaen. | The account number is on the invoice. |
| Flertall ubestemt, uendret form | kontonummer | Vi har lagret to kontonummer for kunden. | We have saved two account numbers for the customer. |
| Flertall ubestemt, -numre-form | kontonumre | Banken kontrollerte kontonumre fra hele landet. | The bank checked account numbers from across the country. |
| Flertall bestemt, -a-form | kontonummera | Kontonummera i listen er anonymisert. | The account numbers in the list are anonymized. |
| Flertall bestemt, -ene-form | kontonumrene | Kontonumrene må bekreftes før utbetaling. | The account numbers must be confirmed before payment. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and all listed forms checked; examples and English gloss authored for this project.

**Notes:** The dictionary permits uninflected kontonummer and kontonummera alongside kontonumre and kontonumrene. All alternatives are illustrated. Singular meaning is a customer number on a bank account; English “account number” is made explicit as “bank account number.”
