## blogg

**English:** blog

**Topics:** technology, media

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Hun startet en blogg om friluftsliv. | She started a blog about outdoor life. |
| Entall ubestemt | blogg | Han skriver blogg om norsk mat. | He writes a blog about Norwegian food. |
| Entall bestemt | bloggen | Bloggen får mange lesere hver uke. | The blog gets many readers every week. |
| Flertall ubestemt | blogger | Flere blogger dekker samme tema. | Several blogs cover the same topic. |
| Flertall bestemt | bloggene | Bloggene ble samlet på én nettside. | The blogs were collected on one website. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** A blog is a journal or diary published online.
