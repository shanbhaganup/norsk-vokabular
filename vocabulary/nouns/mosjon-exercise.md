## mosjon

**English:** exercise; physical activity

**Topics:** sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mosjon om dagen kan gjøre godt. | A bout of exercise a day can be beneficial. |
| Entall ubestemt | mosjon | Hun får mosjon ved å sykle til jobb. | She gets exercise by cycling to work. |
| Entall bestemt | mosjonen | Mosjonen holder ham i god form. | The exercise keeps him in good shape. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mosjoner | Ulike mosjoner passer for ulike aldre. | Different types of exercise suit different ages. |
| Flertall bestemt | mosjonene | Mosjonene på programmet foregår ute. | The exercise activities on the program take place outdoors. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene forms verified; original examples written for each listed form.

**Notes:** The dictionary defines light exercise for health. The listed plural is less common and is illustrated as distinct kinds of exercise.
