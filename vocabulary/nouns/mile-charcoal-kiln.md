## mile

**English:** charcoal kiln; covered wood stack burned to make charcoal

**Topics:** history, work

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mile ble bygd i skogen. | A charcoal kiln was built in the forest. |

| Entall bestemt | mila | Mila brant i flere døgn. | The charcoal kiln burned for several days. |
| Entall bestemt | milen | Milen brant i flere døgn. | The charcoal kiln burned for several days. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene entry 1 gender options and masculine paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** This is `mile 1`, a covered conical stack of wood burned to produce charcoal. Both feminine and masculine singular inflection are accepted; `miler / milene` are the masculine plural forms. The source record combined this with `mile 2`, meaning an English mile, whose plural is `miles`.
