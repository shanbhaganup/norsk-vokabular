## mat

**English:** food; nourishment

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mat fra hjemlandet kan vekke gode minner. | A food from one's home country can bring back fond memories. |
| Entall ubestemt | mat | Vi trenger mat før den lange turen. | We need food before the long hike. |
| Entall bestemt | maten | Maten ble servert varm. | The food was served hot. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mater | Restauranten serverer mater fra flere regioner. | The restaurant serves foods from several regions. |
| Flertall bestemt | matene | Matene på menyen har ulikt opphav. | The foods on the menu come from different origins. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword, gender and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** The source gender was corrected to masculine `en mat`. Singular `mat` is commonly a mass noun; the dictionary-listed plural `mater` is count usage for kinds of food and is less common. Do not use the plural mechanically for ordinary mass-noun contexts.
