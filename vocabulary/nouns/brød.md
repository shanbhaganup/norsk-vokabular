## brød

**English:** bread; loaf

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et brød sto til avkjøling på benken. | A loaf of bread was cooling on the counter. |
| Entall ubestemt | brød | Vi kjøpte brød på bakeriet. | We bought bread at the bakery. |
| Entall bestemt | brødet | Brødet er ferskt. | The bread is fresh. |
| Flertall ubestemt | brød | Butikken selger brød fra flere bakerier. | The shop sells breads from several bakeries. |
| Flertall bestemt | brøda | Brøda ble lagt i kurven. | The loaves were placed in the basket. |
| Flertall bestemt | brødene | Brødene ble lagt i kurven. | The loaves were placed in the basket. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Brød is usually a mass noun; plural forms refer to loaves or varieties of bread. Both definite plural alternatives are accepted and shown.
