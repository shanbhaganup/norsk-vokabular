## blodprøve

**English:** blood test; blood sample

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Hun tok ei blodprøve før timen. | She had a blood test before the appointment. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Hun tok en blodprøve før timen. | She had a blood test before the appointment. |
| Entall ubestemt | blodprøve | Legen bestilte en blodprøve før behandlingen. | The doctor ordered a blood test before the treatment. |
| Entall bestemt | blodprøva | Blodprøva viste normale verdier. | The blood test showed normal values. |
| Entall bestemt | blodprøven | Blodprøven viste normale verdier. | The blood test showed normal values. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both masculine and feminine gender are accepted. Feminine singular definite blodprøva and masculine blodprøven are illustrated; the dictionary gives the shared plural under the masculine pattern.
