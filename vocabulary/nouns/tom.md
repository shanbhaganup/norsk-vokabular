## tom

**English:** cord; line

**Topics:** outdoors

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En tom holdt garnet på plass i båten. | A line kept the net in place in the boat. |
| Entall ubestemt | tom | Fiskeren festet garnet til en tom. | The fisher tied the net to a cord. |
| Entall bestemt | tommen | Tommen var slitt etter mange turer. | The line was worn after many trips. |
| Flertall ubestemt | tommer | Flere tommer lå til tørk på brygga. | Several cords were laid out to dry on the dock. |
| Flertall bestemt | tommene | Tommene ble rullet sammen før avreise. | The cords were coiled before departure. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** The reference gloss “inches” is incorrect for this dictionary entry: tom means a cord or line.
