## bekymring

**English:** worry, concern

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Hun delte ei bekymring med læreren. | She shared a concern with the teacher. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Naboen tok opp en bekymring på møtet. | The neighbour raised a concern at the meeting. |
| Entall ubestemt | bekymring | Han uttrykte bekymring for været. | He expressed concern about the weather. |
| Entall bestemt | bekymringa | Bekymringa forsvant da hun fikk svar. | Her worry went away when she got an answer. |
| Entall bestemt | bekymringen | Bekymringen vokste da ingen svarte. | The concern grew when nobody answered. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bekymringer | De snakket om sine bekymringer. | They talked about their worries. |
| Flertall bestemt | bekymringene | Læreren lyttet til bekymringene deres. | The teacher listened to their concerns. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted.
