## bosted

**English:** residence; place of residence

**Topics:** home, personal information

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et bosted må registreres i skjemaet. | A place of residence must be registered on the form. |
| Entall ubestemt | bosted | Hun oppga bosted da hun søkte. | She gave her place of residence when she applied. |
| Entall bestemt | bostedet | Bostedet hennes ligger nær sentrum. | Her residence is near the centre. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bosteder | Kommunen tilbyr flere bosteder for studenter. | The municipality offers several residences for students. |
| Flertall bestemt | bostedene | Bostedene ligger i ulike bydeler. | The residences are in different districts. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** A place where a person lives.
