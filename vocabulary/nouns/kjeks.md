## kjeks

**English:** biscuit; cookie; cracker

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kjeks ligger på tallerkenen. | A biscuit is on the plate. |
| Entall ubestemt | kjeks | Hun tok en kjeks til kaffen. | She had a biscuit with her coffee. |
| Entall bestemt | kjeksen | Kjeksen smakte av kanel. | The biscuit tasted of cinnamon. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kjeks | Vi kjøpte kjeks til turen. | We bought biscuits for the trip. |
| Flertall bestemt | kjeksene | Kjeksene ligger i boksen. | The biscuits are in the tin. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and sense reviewed; paradigm verified except where noted.

**Notes:** The requested source sense is the crisp baked food. The reference lists kjeks / kjekser as plural alternatives, but the linked Bokmålsordboka entry 29459 gives the paradigm en kjeks–kjeksen–kjeks–kjeksene; this working entry follows that dictionary paradigm and omits unsupported kjekser. Other dictionary entries homographic with kjeks have separate senses (a boat and a fishing tool); those are outside this source record’s food sense.
