## kapp

**English:** race; competition (in the fixed expression om kapp)

**Topics:** expressions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | — (ubøyelig substantiv; no article in the fixed expression om kapp) | De løp om kapp langs stranden. | They raced one another along the beach. |
| Entall ubestemt | — (invariable; occurs in the fixed expression om kapp) | Hun syklet om kapp med broren sin. | She raced her brother on her bicycle. |
| Entall bestemt | — (no definite form is used in this fixed expression) | Barna løp om kapp til porten. | The children raced to the gate. |
| Flertall ubestemt | — (no plural form; this is an invariable noun used in a fixed expression) | To lag rodde om kapp over fjorden. | Two teams raced one another rowing across the fjord. |
| Flertall bestemt | — (no plural definite form; this is an invariable noun used in a fixed expression) | De to konkurrentene svømte om kapp. | The two competitors raced one another swimming. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified where available; individual alternatives are listed separately with examples.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists kapp 3 as an invariable noun, used in fixed expressions such as om kapp and i kapp. The noun is grammatically distinct from the forms of the verb kappes; examples here use the noun in om kapp.
