## pike

**English:** girl

**Topics:** people

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei pike / En pike ventet på bussen. | A girl was waiting for the bus. |
| Entall ubestemt | pike | Ei pike leste ved vinduet. | A girl was reading by the window. |
| Entall bestemt | pika / piken | Pika / Piken vinket til læreren. | The girl waved to the teacher. |
| Flertall ubestemt | piker | Piker og gutter deltok i leken. | Girls and boys took part in the game. |
| Flertall bestemt | pikene | Pikene gikk sammen hjem fra skolen. | The girls walked home from school together. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source had malformed forms pikeen, pikeer and pikeene; the accepted feminine/masculine forms are piken/pika, piker and pikene. This word may sound dated in everyday speech; jente is more usual. The additional reference IDs include other senses/homonyms and are not used for the “girl” sense.
