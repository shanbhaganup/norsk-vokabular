## marked

**English:** market; marketplace

**Topics:** shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et marked åpner på torget på lørdag. | A market opens in the square on Saturday. |
| Entall ubestemt | marked | Vi besøkte et marked med lokale råvarer. | We visited a market with local produce. |
| Entall bestemt | markedet | Markedet stenger klokken fire. | The market closes at four o’clock. |
| Flertall ubestemt | marked | Byen arrangerer marked flere ganger i året. | The town holds markets several times a year. |
| Flertall ubestemt | markeder | Flere markeder selger håndlagde varer. | Several markets sell handmade goods. |
| Flertall bestemt | markeda | Markeda i regionen trekker mange besøkende. | The markets in the region attract many visitors. |
| Flertall bestemt | markedene | Markedene i regionen trekker mange besøkende. | The markets in the region attract many visitors. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword and plural alternatives checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Neuter noun with zero plural `marked` or `markeder`, and definite plurals `markeda` or `markedene`. Ordbøkene also uses the word for supply and demand, a group of potential buyers, and a bazaar-like event.
