## kvarter

**English:** quarter of an hour; 15-minute period

**Topics:** time, numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et kvarter er femten minutter. | A quarter of an hour is fifteen minutes. |
| Entall ubestemt | kvarter | Vi ventet et kvarter på toget. | We waited a quarter of an hour for the train. |
| Entall bestemt | kvarteret | Kvarteret gikk fort. | The quarter of an hour passed quickly. |
| Flertall ubestemt, unchanged form | kvarter | Vi ventet to kvarter før neste avgang. | We waited for two quarter-hours before the next departure. |
| Flertall ubestemt, -er form | kvarterer | Vi ventet to kvarterer før neste avgang. | We waited for two quarter-hours before the next departure. |
| Flertall bestemt, -a form | kvartera | Kvartera på møtet gikk fort. | The quarter-hours at the meeting passed quickly. |
| Flertall bestemt, -ene form | kvarterene | Kvarterene på møtet gikk fort. | The quarter-hours at the meeting passed quickly. |

**Form status:** Verified against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry for spelling, gender, and paradigm; original teaching examples authored for this project.

**Notes:** This entry follows the reference time-unit sense. *Kvarter* also has other meanings, including a city district; those are not included here. The two indefinite plural alternatives and both definite plural forms are each illustrated.
