## brudekjole

**English:** wedding dress

**Topics:** clothing, weddings

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Hun fant en brudekjole som passet perfekt. | She found a wedding dress that fit perfectly. |
| Entall ubestemt | brudekjole | En brudekjole kan være enkel eller pyntet. | A wedding dress can be simple or ornate. |
| Entall bestemt | brudekjolen | Brudekjolen ble lagt forsiktig i esken. | The wedding dress was carefully placed in the box. |
| Flertall ubestemt | brudekjoler | Butikken viser brudekjoler i flere stiler. | The shop displays wedding dresses in several styles. |
| Flertall bestemt | brudekjolene | Brudekjolene ble hengt tilbake etter prøvingen. | The wedding dresses were hung back after the fitting. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** A dress worn by a bride, usually for the wedding ceremony.
