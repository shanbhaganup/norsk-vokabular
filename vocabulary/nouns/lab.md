## lab

**English:** lab

**Topics:** science

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En lab ved universitetet fikk nytt utstyr. | A lab at the university received new equipment. |
| Entall ubestemt | lab | Forskerne arbeidet i en lab hele natta. | The researchers worked in a lab all night. |
| Entall bestemt | laben | Laben åpner igjen på mandag. | The lab reopens on Monday. |
| Flertall ubestemt | laber | Flere laber deltok i prosjektet. | Several labs took part in the project. |
| Flertall bestemt | labene | Labene delte resultatene sine. | The labs shared their results. |

**Form status:** Headword and applicable forms reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Correction: Bokmålsordboka gives laben/laber/labene for lab, a short form of laboratorium. The source snapshot mistakenly mixes in labb forms.
