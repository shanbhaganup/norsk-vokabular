## kniv

**English:** knife

**Topics:** kitchen, tools

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kniv ligger på skjærefjøla. | A knife is lying on the cutting board. |
| Entall ubestemt | kniv | Hun brukte en skarp kniv til å skjære brødet. | She used a sharp knife to cut the bread. |
| Entall bestemt | kniven | Kniven må vaskes etter bruk. | The knife must be washed after use. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kniver | Butikken selger kniver i mange størrelser. | The shop sells knives in many sizes. |
| Flertall bestemt | knivene | Knivene ligger i den øverste skuffen. | The knives are in the top drawer. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified.

**Notes:** Covers the ordinary cutting-tool sense; Bokmålsordboka also records a cutting part in a machine. Teaching examples are original.
