## luke

**English:** hatch, small door or cover

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Mekanikeren åpnet ei luke i gulvet. | The mechanic opened a hatch in the floor. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Vi fant en luke bak skapet. | We found a hatch behind the cupboard. |
| Entall ubestemt | luke | Båten har ei luke foran på dekket. | The boat has a hatch at the front of the deck. |
| Entall bestemt, hunkjønn | luka | Luka var låst fra innsiden. | The hatch was locked from the inside. |
| Entall bestemt, hankjønn | luken | Luken fører ned til lasterommet. | The hatch leads down to the hold. |
| Flertall ubestemt | luker | Arbeiderne inspiserte alle luker på taket. | The workers inspected all the hatches on the roof. |
| Flertall bestemt | lukene | Lukene må sikres før skipet legger fra kai. | The hatches must be secured before the ship leaves the quay. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. Compound inflection is labelled derived where applicable. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry uses the sense of a small door or cover closing an opening. The dictionary also records an opening or service hatch and figurative gaps. Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted.
