## januar

**English:** January

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En januar med lite snø overrasket skientusiastene. | A January with little snow surprised the skiing enthusiasts. |
| Entall ubestemt | januar | Vi flyttet inn en januar for mange år siden. | We moved in one January many years ago. |
| Entall bestemt | januaren | Januaren var kaldere enn vanlig. | January was colder than usual. |
| Flertall ubestemt | januarer | Flere januarer har vårt milde de siste årene. | Several Januaries have been mild in recent years. |
| Flertall bestemt | januarene | Januarene i måleserien ble sammenlignet. | The Januaries in the data series were compared. |

**Form status:** Headword and paradigm reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Plural forms refer to instances of the month across different years or calendars; ordinary dates use the month name without an article.
