## kake

**English:** cake; baked pastry, oven-baked bread, shaped food mass, or hardened lump

**Topics:** food

**Notes:** Both masculine and feminine gender are accepted. The dictionary includes several related senses: fine baked goods, oven-baked bread, a shaped mass of food, and a hardened lump. Masculine and feminine definite singular forms are listed; the remaining paradigm is common to both.
