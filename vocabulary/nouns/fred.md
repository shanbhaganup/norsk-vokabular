## fred

**English:** peace

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En fred mellom landene varte i mange år. | A peace between the countries lasted for many years. |
| Entall ubestemt | fred | Innbyggerne håpet på fred. | The residents hoped for peace. |
| Entall bestemt | freden | Freden kom etter lange forhandlinger. | Peace came after long negotiations. |
| Flertall ubestemt | freder | Historikeren sammenlignet flere freder mellom rikene. | The historian compared several peace settlements between the kingdoms. |
| Flertall bestemt | fredene | Fredene mellom statene varte ulikt lenge. | The peace settlements between the states lasted for different lengths of time. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Plural forms refer to distinct peace periods or settlements and are much less common than the mass-noun singular.
