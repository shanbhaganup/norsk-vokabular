## alibi

**English:** alibi

**Topics:** culture

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Vitnet ga henne et alibi. | The witness gave her an alibi. |
| Entall ubestemt | alibi | Begge de mistenkte manglet alibi. | Both suspects lacked an alibi. |
| Entall bestemt | alibiet | Hotellkvitteringen bekreftet alibiet hans. | The hotel receipt confirmed his alibi. |

**Notes:** The examples use the sense familiar from crime fiction, evidence that someone was elsewhere when a crime took place.
