**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and paradigm checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The source gloss “breasts” is incorrect for puppe. Bokmålsordboka defines puppe as the insect stage between larva and fully developed insect (current headword page: https://ordbokene.no/bm/puppe). “Breast” is pupp, a different lemma and not migrated by this record. The dictionary accepts both masculine and feminine forms; definite alternatives are illustrated individually.
