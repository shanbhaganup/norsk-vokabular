## fredag

**English:** Friday

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En fredag i juni drar vi til hytta. | One Friday in June, we are going to the cabin. |
| Entall ubestemt | fredag | Vi møtes på fredag. | We are meeting on Friday. |
| Entall bestemt | fredagen | Fredagen før ferien var travel. | The Friday before the holiday was busy. |
| Flertall ubestemt | fredager | Hun jobber hjemme på fredager. | She works from home on Fridays. |
| Flertall bestemt | fredagene | Fredagene i desember går fort. | The Fridays in December go by quickly. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Day names are normally used without an article when naming a weekday; these are the noun forms.
