## frue

**English:** lady; wife

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei frue ventet utenfor. / En frue ventet utenfor. | A lady was waiting outside. / A lady was waiting outside. |
| Entall ubestemt | frue | Ei frue ventet utenfor. | A lady was waiting outside. |
| Entall bestemt | frua / fruen | Frua kom først. / Fruen kom først. | The lady arrived first. / The lady arrived first. |
| Flertall ubestemt | fruer | Flere fruer deltok på festen. | Several ladies attended the party. |
| Flertall bestemt | fruene | Fruene satte seg ved vinduet. | The ladies sat down by the window. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and paradigm checked against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted. The feminine definite singular frua and masculine fruen are both listed. The dictionary also includes a historical sense 'mistress/ruler' and a form of address; examples use the ordinary sense 'lady/wife'.
