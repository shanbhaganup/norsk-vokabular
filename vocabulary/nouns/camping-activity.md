## camping

**English:** camping; camping holiday

**Topics:** travel, outdoor life

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En camping i fjellet kan være kald om natten. | A camping holiday in the mountains can be cold at night. |
| Entall ubestemt | camping | Vi liker camping om sommeren. | We enjoy camping in the summer. |
| Entall bestemt | campingen | Campingen ble avlyst på grunn av regnet. | The camping trip was cancelled because of the rain. |
| Flertall ubestemt | campinger | Familien dro på flere campinger i fjor. | The family went on several camping trips last year. |
| Flertall bestemt | campingene | Campingene varte fra en helg til to uker. | The camping trips lasted from a weekend to two weeks. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers staying in a tent, caravan or motorhome, distinct from camping as a short form of campingplass.
