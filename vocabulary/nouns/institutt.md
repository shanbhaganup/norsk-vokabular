## institutt

**English:** institute

**Topics:** education, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et institutt åpner et nytt laboratorium. | An institute is opening a new laboratory. |
| Entall ubestemt | institutt | Hun arbeider ved et institutt for språkforskning. | She works at an institute for linguistics. |
| Entall bestemt | instituttet | Instituttet publiserte en rapport. | The institute published a report. |
| Flertall ubestemt | institutt | Flere institutt deltok i undersøkelsen. | Several institutes took part in the study. |
