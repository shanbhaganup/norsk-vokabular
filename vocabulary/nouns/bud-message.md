## bud

**English:** message; warning; commandment

**Topics:** communication, religion

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et bud kom fram til landsbyen. | A message reached the village. |
| Entall ubestemt | bud | De fikk bud om at veien var stengt. | They received word that the road was closed. |
| Entall bestemt | budet | Budet om endringen kom sent. | The message about the change arrived late. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bud | De gamle bud står skrevet på tavlen. | The old commandments are written on the board. |
| Flertall bestemt | buda | Buda ble lest høyt for gruppen. | The commandments were read aloud to the group. |
| Flertall bestemt | budene | Budene ble lest høyt for gruppen. | The commandments were read aloud to the group. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers a message, warning, rule or commandment. The dictionary also records a bid and a person who delivers messages; those senses are kept separate.
