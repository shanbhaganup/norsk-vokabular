## pant

**English:** refundable bottle deposit

**Topics:** money, shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En pant blir refundert når du leverer flasken. | A bottle deposit is refunded when you return the bottle. |
| Entall ubestemt | pant | Flasken har en pant på tre kroner. | The bottle has a deposit of three kroner. |
| Entall bestemt | panten | Panten står på kvitteringen. | The deposit is shown on the receipt. |
| Flertall ubestemt | panter | Butikken endret panter på flere flasker. | The shop changed deposits on several bottles. |
| Flertall bestemt | pantene | Pantene ble tilbakebetalt i kassen. | The deposits were refunded at the checkout. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Separate masculine homonym pant 1 I, the refundable addition to a product's price; collateral is in pant-collateral.
