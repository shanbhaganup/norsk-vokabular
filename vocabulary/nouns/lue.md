## lue

**English:** cap, beanie, soft hat

**Topics:** shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei lue | Jeg strikket ei lue til niesa mi. | I knitted a hat for my niece. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en lue | Han kjøpte en lue før skituren. | He bought a hat before the ski trip. |
| Entall ubestemt | lue | Ta med deg en varm lue i morgen. | Bring a warm hat tomorrow. |
| Entall bestemt | lua | Hun trakk lua ned over ørene. | She pulled her hat down over her ears. |
| Entall bestemt | luen | Luen lå igjen på benken. | The hat was left behind on the bench. |
| Flertall ubestemt | luer | Butikken selger luer av ull. | The shop sells wool hats. |
| Flertall bestemt | luene | Barna hengte luene til tørk. | The children hung their hats up to dry. |

**Form status:** Gender, inflection and sense checked against Bokmålsordboka. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The snapshot’s ‘hat’ is broad. *Lue* I refers to soft headwear, such as a knitted hat or a cap with a visor. The flame sense belongs to a separate dictionary article. Both feminine and masculine inflection are accepted.
