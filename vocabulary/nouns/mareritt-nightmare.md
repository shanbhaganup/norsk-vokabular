## mareritt

**English:** nightmare; frightening dream

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et mareritt vekket henne midt på natten. | A nightmare woke her in the middle of the night. |
| Entall ubestemt | mareritt | Jeg hadde mareritt etter den skumle filmen. | I had a nightmare after the scary film. |
| Entall bestemt | marerittet | Marerittet føltes helt virkelig. | The nightmare felt completely real. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mareritt | Noen barn får mareritt når de er syke. | Some children have nightmares when they are ill. |
| Flertall bestemt | mareritta | Mareritta kom tilbake flere netter på rad. | The nightmares returned several nights in a row. |
| Flertall bestemt | marerittene | Marerittene gjorde det vanskelig å sove. | The nightmares made it difficult to sleep. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword and alternatives checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Neuter noun with zero plural `mareritt`; definite plural alternatives are `mareritta` and `marerittene`. Ordbøkene also uses the word figuratively for a horrifying situation or experience.
