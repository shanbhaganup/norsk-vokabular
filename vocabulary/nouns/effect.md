## effekt

**English:** effect; impact

**Topics:** daily life, society, language

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En liten endring fikk stor effekt. | A small change had a big effect. |
| Entall ubestemt | effekt | En liten endring fikk stor effekt. | A small change had a big effect. |
| Entall bestemt | effekten | Effekten av tiltaket kom etter noen uker. | The effect of the measure appeared after a few weeks. |
| Flertall ubestemt | effekter | Tiltakene hadde positive effekter på fraværet. | The measures had positive effects on absenteeism. |
| Flertall bestemt | effektene | Effektene ble målt gjennom hele året. | The effects were measured throughout the year. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original Norwegian examples supplied for each form.

**Notes:** Also used for power output in physics and for personal belongings in plural; examples focus on impact.
