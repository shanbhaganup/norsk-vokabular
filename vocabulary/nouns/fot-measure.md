## fot

**English:** foot (unit of length)

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En fot er omtrent 30,48 centimeter. | A foot is about 30.48 centimetres. |
| Entall ubestemt | fot | En fot er omtrent 30,48 centimeter. | A foot is about 30.48 centimetres. |
| Entall bestemt | foten | Foten ble brukt som lengdemål i eldre tid. | The foot was used as a unit of length in earlier times. |
| Flertall ubestemt | fot | Målene i katalogen er oppgitt i fot. | The measurements in the catalogue are given in feet. |
| Flertall bestemt | fotene | Fotene i tabellen viser lengden på hver del. | The feet in the table show the length of each part. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and inflection checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The two dictionary records distinguish the length unit from the body-part sense. The plural alternatives follow the two dictionary paradigms.
