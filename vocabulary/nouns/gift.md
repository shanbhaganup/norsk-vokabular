## gift

**English:** poison

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Planten inneholder ei gift som virker raskt. | The plant contains a fast-acting poison. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Frosken skiller ut en gift gjennom huden. | The frog secretes a poison through its skin. |
| Entall ubestemt | gift | Frøene inneholder gift. | The seeds contain poison. |
| Entall bestemt | gifta | Gifta var vanskelig å påvise. | The poison was difficult to detect. |
| Entall bestemt | giften | Giften kom fra en sopp. | The poison came from a mushroom. |
| Flertall ubestemt | gifter | Forskerne undersøkte to gifter fra havet. | The researchers studied two poisons from the sea. |
| Flertall bestemt | giftene | Giftene virker på ulike måter. | The poisons act in different ways. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine inflections are accepted. The plural can refer to different poisons.
