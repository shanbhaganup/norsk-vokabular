## lugar

**English:** cabin, berth

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Vi fikk en lugar med utsikt mot havna. | We got a cabin with a view of the harbour. |
| Entall ubestemt | lugar | Skipet har lugar til fire personer. | The ship has a cabin for four people. |
| Entall bestemt | lugaren | Lugaren var liten, men ryddig. | The cabin was small but tidy. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lugarer | De bestilte to lugarer for overfarten. | They booked two cabins for the crossing. |
| Flertall bestemt | lugarene | Lugarene ligger på dekk tre. | The cabins are on deck three. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. Compound inflection is labelled derived where applicable. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** In this sense, a *lugar* is a small living and sleeping cabin on a boat or ship. The dictionary also records a larger common room or lounge on passenger ships.
