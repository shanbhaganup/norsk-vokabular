## brøkdel

**English:** fraction; small part

**Topics:** numbers, mathematics

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En brøkdel av beløpet manglet. | A fraction of the amount was missing. |
| Entall ubestemt | brøkdel | Bare en brøkdel av publikum kom tidsnok. | Only a fraction of the audience arrived on time. |
| Entall bestemt | brøkdelen | Brøkdelen ble rundet av til to desimaler. | The fraction was rounded to two decimal places. |
| Flertall ubestemt | brøkdeler | Brøkdeler kan legges sammen. | Fractions can be added together. |
| Flertall bestemt | brøkdelene | Brøkdelene ble skrevet på tavla. | The fractions were written on the board. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** A fraction of a whole; the dictionary also uses brøkdel figuratively for a small part.
