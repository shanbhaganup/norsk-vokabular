## oppdrag

**English:** assignment; task

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et oppdrag kan ta flere dager. | An assignment can take several days. |
| Entall ubestemt | oppdrag | Hun fikk et oppdrag fra kommunen. | She received an assignment from the municipality. |
| Entall bestemt | oppdraget | Oppdraget må være ferdig før fredag. | The assignment must be finished before Friday. |
| Flertall ubestemt | oppdrag | De fikk oppdrag fra flere kunder. | They received assignments from several clients. |
