## godt

**English:** sweets; candy

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Godt kan smelte raskt i munnen. | A sweet can melt quickly in your mouth. |
| Entall ubestemt | godt | Godt kan smelte raskt i munnen. | A sweet can melt quickly in your mouth. |
| Entall bestemt | godtet | Godtet lå i en skål på bordet. | The candy was in a bowl on the table. |
| Flertall ubestemt | godter | Barna valgte godter fra papirposen. | The children chose sweets from the paper bag. |
| Flertall bestemt | godtene | Godtene ble delt mellom gjestene. | The sweets were shared among the guests. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This is the neuter food sense ("candy/sweets"), separate from the historical school-grade noun. The attested plural entry is godter/godtene.
