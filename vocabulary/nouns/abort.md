## abort

**English:** abortion

**Topics:** health

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Hun fortalte legen at hun ønsket en abort. | She told the doctor she wanted an abortion. |
| Entall ubestemt | abort | Valget om abort var hennes. | The decision to have an abortion was hers. |
| Entall bestemt | aborten | Hun fikk tilbud om en samtale etter aborten. | She was offered a follow-up conversation after the abortion. |
| Flertall ubestemt | aborter | Klinikken fører statistikk over hvor mange aborter den utfører. | The clinic keeps records of how many abortions it performs. |
| Flertall bestemt | abortene | Abortene ble registrert uten pasientenes navn. | The abortions were recorded without the patients’ names. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** The source gender was corrected to masculine. The examples use the induced sense. In the expression *spontan abort*, the usual English translation is *miscarriage*.
