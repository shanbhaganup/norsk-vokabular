## kappløp

**English:** race; competition; race against time

**Topics:** sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et kappløp begynner ved startstreken. | A race begins at the starting line. |
| Entall ubestemt | kappløp | Laget arrangerer et kappløp rundt banen. | The team is organizing a race around the track. |
| Entall bestemt | kappløpet | Kappløpet ble avgjort på oppløpet. | The race was decided in the final stretch. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kappløp | Flere kappløp står på programmet i dag. | Several races are on today’s program. |
| Flertall bestemt | kappløpa | Kappløpa samlet mange tilskuere. | The races drew many spectators. |
| Flertall bestemt | kappløpene | Kappløpene ble sendt direkte på radio. | The races were broadcast live on the radio. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified where available; individual alternatives are listed separately with examples.

**Notes:** The dictionary gives the same form kappløp in singular and indefinite plural, and accepts kappløpa / kappløpene as definite plural. The sense includes literal and figurative competition; examples use the literal sporting sense.
