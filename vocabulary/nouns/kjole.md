## kjole

**English:** dress; gown

**Topics:** shopping, clothing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kjole henger ved vinduet. | A dress hangs by the window. |
| Entall ubestemt | kjole | Hun kjøpte en kjole til festen. | She bought a dress for the party. |
| Entall bestemt | kjolen | Kjolen passer perfekt. | The dress fits perfectly. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kjoler | Butikken selger kjoler i mange farger. | The shop sells dresses in many colours. |
| Flertall bestemt | kjolene | Kjolene er utstilt i vinduet. | The dresses are displayed in the window. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and sense reviewed; paradigm verified except where noted.

**Notes:** The reference corrects the source gender to masculine. Bokmålsordboka confirms en and the paradigm kjole–kjolen–kjoler–kjolene. The dictionary includes additional specialized garment senses; this entry covers the ordinary dress sense.
