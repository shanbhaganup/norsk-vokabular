## bok

**English:** book

**Topics:** school, reading

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei bok lå på bordet. | A book lay on the table. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bok lå på bordet. | A book lay on the table. |
| Entall ubestemt | bok | Hun lånte ei bok på biblioteket. | She borrowed a book from the library. |
| Entall bestemt | boka | Boka handler om en familie. | The book is about a family. |
| Entall bestemt | boken | Boken handler om en familie. | The book is about a family. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bøker | Biblioteket kjøpte nye bøker. | The library bought new books. |
| Flertall bestemt | bøkene | Bøkene står på den nederste hyllen. | The books are on the bottom shelf. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers the written work (dictionary entry I). The source's form boker belongs to the separate tree sense bok II and is not an accepted plural of this sense. Both feminine and masculine singular forms are accepted.
