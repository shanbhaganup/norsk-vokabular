## OL

**English:** Olympic Games

**Topics:** sports

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

| Kjønn / artikkel | et | De arrangerte et OL. | They hosted an edition of the Olympic Games. |

**Notes:** The abbreviation stands for olympiske leker. English uses plural agreement. A Norwegian plural can refer to several editions. NoW source form: “OL”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
