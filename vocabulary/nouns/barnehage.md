## barnehage

**English:** kindergarten, preschool

**Topics:** school

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Kommunen åpnet en ny barnehage. | The municipality opened a new kindergarten. |
| Entall ubestemt | barnehage | Hun jobber i en barnehage. | She works at a kindergarten. |
| Entall bestemt | barnehagen | Barnehagen stenger klokka fem. | The kindergarten closes at five. |
| Flertall ubestemt | barnehager | Det er tre barnehager i bydelen. | There are three kindergartens in the neighbourhood. |
| Flertall bestemt | barnehagene | Barnehagene planla en felles tur. | The kindergartens planned an outing together. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** In Norway, a barnehage provides early childhood care and education.
