## chili

**English:** chili (spice)

**Topics:** food, spices

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En chili kan gi suppen ekstra varme. | A chili spice can add heat to the soup. |
| Entall ubestemt | chili | Hun brukte chili i sausen. | She used chili in the sauce. |
| Entall bestemt | chilien | Chilien ble blandet inn til slutt. | The chili spice was mixed in at the end. |
| Flertall ubestemt | chilier | Butikken selger chilier med ulik styrke. | The shop sells chili spices with different levels of heat. |
| Flertall bestemt | chiliene | Chiliene ble oppbevart i lufttette glass. | The chili spices were stored in airtight jars. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers the very hot dried and crushed spice (dictionary sense 2), distinct from the fresh chili fruit.
