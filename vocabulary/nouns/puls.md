# puls

**English:** pulse; beat; rhythm

**Topics:** health; technology; music

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En puls kan måles ved håndleddet. | A pulse can be measured at the wrist. |
| Entall ubestemt | puls | Etter løpeturen hadde hun høy puls. | After the run, her pulse was high. |
| Entall bestemt | pulsen | Pulsen sank da han satte seg ned. | His pulse slowed when he sat down. |
| Flertall ubestemt | pulser | Sensoren registrerer korte pulser i signalet. | The sensor records short pulses in the signal. |
| Flertall bestemt | pulsene | Pulsene i lysstrålen kom med jevne mellomrom. | The pulses in the beam of light came at regular intervals. |

**Notes:** Covers the dictionary senses: arterial/heartbeat rate, brief electrical change or particle/light beam, and regular rhythm. The same plural paradigm applies across these related senses.
