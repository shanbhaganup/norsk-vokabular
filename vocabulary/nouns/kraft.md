## kraft

**English:** force; power

**Topics:** general, science

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel, hankjønn | en | En kraft virker på gjenstanden fra siden. | A force acts on the object from the side. |
| Kjønn / artikkel, hunkjønn | ei / en | Ei kraft holder døra åpen. / En kraft holder døra åpen. | A force holds the door open. / A force holds the door open. |
| Entall ubestemt | kraft | Hun trengte kraft til å løfte lokket. | She needed strength to lift the lid. |
| Entall bestemt, hankjønn | kraften | Kraften fra vinden presset båten mot land. | The force of the wind pushed the boat toward shore. |
| Entall bestemt, hunkjønn | krafta | Krafta i tauet holdt lasten på plass. | The force in the rope kept the load in place. |
| Flertall ubestemt, hankjønn | krefter | Kreftene virket i motsatt retning. | The forces acted in opposite directions. |
| Flertall bestemt, hankjønn | kreftene | Kreftene i naturen former landskapet. | The forces of nature shape the landscape. |

**Form status:** Dictionary-supported masculine and feminine singular alternatives and masculine plural forms verified; feminine plural forms are unverified and not asserted.

**Notes:** This is *kraft* 1, physical force/power. The source combines *krefter/kreftene* for this sense with *krafter/kraftene*, which belong to homographic *kraft* 2, broth/stock. The broth forms are therefore excluded from this entry.
