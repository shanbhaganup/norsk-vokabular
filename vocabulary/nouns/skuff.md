## skuff

**English:** drawer

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En skuff kan holde småting samlet. | A drawer can keep small items together. |
| Entall ubestemt | skuff | Hun la nøklene i en skuff. | She put the keys in a drawer. |
| Entall bestemt | skuffen | Skuffen var full av gamle bilder. | The drawer was full of old pictures. |
| Flertall ubestemt | skuffer | Flere skuffer sto åpne. | Several drawers were open. |
| Flertall bestemt | skuffene | Skuffene ble ryddet før flyttingen. | The drawers were cleared out before the move. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The reference gloss “disappointing” is incorrect for skuff; Bokmålsordboka entry 53779 is the noun “drawer”. The related noun skuffelse means disappointment.
