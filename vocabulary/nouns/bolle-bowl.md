## bolle

**English:** bowl

**Topics:** kitchen, food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En bolle med salat sto på bordet. | A bowl of salad stood on the table. |
| Entall ubestemt | bolle | Hun la frukten i en bolle. | She put the fruit in a bowl. |
| Entall bestemt | bollen | Bollen er laget av glass. | The bowl is made of glass. |
| Flertall ubestemt | boller | Skapet inneholder flere boller. | The cupboard contains several bowls. |
| Flertall bestemt | bollene | Bollene står på kjøkkenhyllen. | The bowls are on the kitchen shelf. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers the deep dish sense (dictionary entry I), distinct from the baked good bolle II.
