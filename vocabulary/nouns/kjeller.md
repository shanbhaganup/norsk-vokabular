## kjeller

**English:** basement; cellar

**Topics:** home

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En kjeller ligger under huset. | There is a basement under the house. |
| Entall ubestemt | kjeller | Huset har en tørr kjeller. | The house has a dry basement. |
| Entall bestemt | kjelleren | Vi oppbevarer syklene i kjelleren. | We keep the bicycles in the basement. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kjellere | Gamle hus kan ha kalde kjellere. | Old houses can have cold basements. |
| Flertall bestemt | kjellerne | Kjellerne i bygget trenger bedre lys. | The basements in the building need better lighting. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and sense reviewed; paradigm verified except where noted.

**Notes:** The reference record and Bokmålsordboka confirm masculine gender and the paradigm kjeller–kjelleren–kjellere–kjellerne. The dictionary also has historical newspaper-related uses; this entry focuses on the home/storage sense in the reference record.
