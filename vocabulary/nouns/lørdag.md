## lørdag

**English:** Saturday

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En lørdag i juni dro vi til sjøen. | One Saturday in June we went to the seaside. |
| Entall ubestemt | lørdag | Vi reiser på lørdag. | We are travelling on Saturday. |
| Entall bestemt | lørdagen | Lørdagen ble varm og solrik. | Saturday turned warm and sunny. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lørdager | Noen lørdager jobber hun på biblioteket. | Some Saturdays she works at the library. |
| Flertall bestemt | lørdagene | Lørdagene i juli gikk fort. | The Saturdays in July went by quickly. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and inflection checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The source gloss is capitalized to match English day-name spelling. The noun is used for the sixth day of the week and in dates.
