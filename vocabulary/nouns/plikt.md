## plikt

**English:** duty; obligation

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei plikt følger med vervet. | An obligation comes with the position. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En plikt følger med vervet. | An obligation comes with the position. |
| Entall ubestemt | plikt | Jeg har plikt til å møte opp. | I have a duty to attend. |
| Entall bestemt | plikta | Plikta står i avtalen. | The obligation is stated in the agreement. |
| Entall bestemt | plikten | Plikten gjelder alle ansatte. | The duty applies to all employees. |
| Flertall ubestemt | plikter | Arbeidstakerne har flere plikter. | The employees have several duties. |
| Flertall bestemt | pliktene | Pliktene er beskrevet i reglementet. | The duties are described in the regulations. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** The dictionary accepts feminine and masculine forms; both articles and both definite singular alternatives are illustrated. Dictionary entry 45259 is a separate nautical homonym meaning a deck/space in a boat, not the source sense.
