## lyd

**English:** sound, noise

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En lyd kom fra gangen. | A sound came from the hallway. |
| Entall ubestemt | lyd | Jeg hørte en lyd utenfor vinduet. | I heard a sound outside the window. |
| Entall bestemt | lyden | Lyden fra alarmen vekket oss. | The sound of the alarm woke us. |
| Flertall ubestemt, tellelig | lyder | Instrumentet lager flere merkelige lyder. | The instrument makes several strange sounds. |
| Flertall ubestemt, massenavn | lyd | Rommet var fullt av lyd under konserten. | The room was full of sound during the concert. |
| Flertall bestemt | lydene | Lydene fra skogen ble svakere utover kvelden. | The sounds from the forest grew fainter as evening went on. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked. Compound inflection is labelled derived where applicable. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry covers an acoustic sound, based on dictionary article 36171. The snapshot also merges *lyd* II for a group or household and *lyd* III for a speech sound. Those homographic nouns have separate entries. The acoustic noun’s ordinary plural is *lyder*.
