## stemme

**English:** voice; vote

**Topics:** communication; society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En stemme fra salen stilte et kritisk spørsmål. | A voice from the audience asked a critical question. |
| Entall ubestemt | stemme | Hun har en klar stemme og avgir alltid sin stemme ved valg. | She has a clear voice and always casts her vote in elections. |
| Entall bestemt | stemmen | Stemmen hans skalv mens stemmene ble telt opp. | His voice trembled while the votes were counted. |
| Flertall ubestemt | stemmer | Flere stemmer ba om ordet, og kandidatene fikk like mange stemmer. | Several voices asked to speak, and the candidates received the same number of votes. |
| Flertall bestemt | stemmene | Stemmene fra koret fylte rommet, og stemmene fra valglokalet ble telt i natt. | The choir’s voices filled the room, and the votes from the polling station were counted overnight. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference snapshot; original teaching examples supplied here.

**Notes:** Both voice and vote are common meanings. The noun also has a homographic verb entry; this file covers the voice/vote noun senses.
