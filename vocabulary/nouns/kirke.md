## kirke

**English:** church; Christian congregation

**Topics:** culture

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and sense reviewed; paradigm verified except where noted.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka recognizes both masculine and feminine gender. It lists kirka as the feminine definite singular and also accepts the masculine pattern en kirke–kirken. The reference’s combined kirka / kirken form is separated by gender here. The lemma covers both the church building and a Christian denomination/community.
