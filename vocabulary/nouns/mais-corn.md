## mais

**English:** corn; maize

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mais vokste ved gjerdet. | A corn plant grew by the fence. |
| Entall ubestemt | mais | Vi spiser mais til middag. | We are eating corn for dinner. |
| Entall bestemt | maisen | Maisen på åkeren er snart moden. | The corn in the field will soon be ripe. |
| Flertall ubestemt | maiser | Bonden dyrker maiser med ulike egenskaper. | The farmer grows corn plants with different traits. |
| Flertall bestemt | maisene | Maisene på forsøksfeltet modnet til ulik tid. | The corn plants in the trial field ripened at different times. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene headword and paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Singular `mais` commonly names the crop or food as a mass noun; plural forms are used here for countable plants or varieties. Ordbøkene includes both the plant and its grains/flour.
