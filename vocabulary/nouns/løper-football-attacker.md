## løper

**English:** attacking player, forward

**Topics:** sport

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Treneren satte inn en løper mot slutten av kampen. | The coach brought on a forward near the end of the match. |
| Entall ubestemt | løper | Laget manglet en løper på høyresiden. | The team lacked a forward on the right wing. |
| Entall bestemt | løperen | Løperen fant rom bak forsvarerne. | The forward found space behind the defenders. |
| Flertall ubestemt | løpere | De raske løperne presset høyt. | The fast forwards pressed high. |
| Flertall bestemt | løperne | Løperne byttet plass flere ganger. | The forwards switched positions several times. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and inflection checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This entry follows the dictionary’s football sense ‘attacking player/forward’. The identical noun also means a runner, chess bishop and narrow decorative cloth or mat; those senses have separate entries.
