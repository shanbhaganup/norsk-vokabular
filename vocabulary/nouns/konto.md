## konto

**English:** account (bank, bookkeeping or online)

**Topics:** money, technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En konto kan brukes til betalinger. | An account can be used for payments. |
| Entall ubestemt | konto | Jeg åpnet en konto i banken. | I opened an account at the bank. |
| Entall bestemt | kontoen | Kontoen står i hennes navn. | The account is in her name. |
| Flertall ubestemt, -i-form | konti | Banken tilbyr flere typer konti. | The bank offers several types of accounts. |
| Flertall ubestemt, -er-form | kontoer | Mange kunder har kontoer i flere banker. | Many customers have accounts at several banks. |
| Flertall bestemt, -iene-form | kontiene | Kontiene ble kontrollert av revisoren. | The accounts were checked by the auditor. |
| Flertall bestemt, -ene-form | kontoene | Kontoene på nettstedet er fortsatt aktive. | The accounts on the website are still active. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and all listed forms checked; examples and English gloss authored for this project.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists both konti/kontiene and kontoer/kontoene. The senses include financial accounts, bookkeeping accounts and accounts for online services; the examples distinguish those contexts. The reference snapshot notes that its source gender was corrected against the dictionary.
