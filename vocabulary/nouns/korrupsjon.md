## korrupsjon

**English:** corruption; bribery

**Topics:** society, law

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En korrupsjonssak rystet kommunen. | A corruption case shook the municipality. |
| Entall ubestemt | korrupsjon | Journalisten avslørte korrupsjon i forvaltningen. | The journalist exposed corruption in public administration. |
| Entall bestemt | korrupsjonen | Korrupsjonen svekket tilliten til myndighetene. | The corruption weakened trust in the authorities. |
| Flertall ubestemt | korrupsjoner | Rapporten beskriver korrupsjoner i flere land. | The report describes instances of corruption in several countries. |
| Flertall bestemt | korrupsjonene | Korrupsjonene ble gransket av en uavhengig kommisjon. | The cases of corruption were investigated by an independent commission. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; examples and English gloss authored for this project.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka defines the core sense as the act of bribing or accepting bribes. The plural forms are real and are used here for individual cases or instances; the singular is the usual abstract reading. The reference record’s English gloss “corruption” is accurate but broad.
