## overraskelse

**English:** surprise; feeling of being surprised

**Topics:** emotions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En overraskelse kan være både gledelig og uventet. | A feeling of surprise can be both pleasant and unexpected. |
| Entall ubestemt | overraskelse | Til min store overraskelse var døra allerede åpen. | To my great surprise, the door was already open. |
| Entall bestemt | overraskelsen | Overraskelsen hennes var tydelig. | Her surprise was obvious. |
| Flertall ubestemt | overraskelser | Gjentatte overraskelser gjorde henne usikker. | Repeated feelings of surprise made her uncertain. |
| Flertall bestemt | overraskelsene | Overraskelsene gikk gradvis over. | The feelings of surprise gradually passed. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; teaching examples are original.

**Notes:** Dictionary distinguishes the feeling of being surprised from a surprising thing or event. This entry covers the feeling sense.
