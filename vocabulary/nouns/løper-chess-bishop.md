## løper

**English:** bishop (chess piece)

**Topics:** games

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En løper dekker de lange diagonalene. | A bishop controls the long diagonals. |
| Entall ubestemt | løper | Hun byttet en løper mot et tårn. | She exchanged a bishop for a rook. |
| Entall bestemt | løperen | Løperen står beskyttet bak bonden. | The bishop is protected behind the pawn. |
| Flertall ubestemt | løpere | Begge spillerne hadde løpere igjen. | Both players still had bishops. |
| Flertall bestemt | løperne | Løperne peker mot hver sin farge på brettet. | The bishops move on different-coloured squares. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and inflection checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This is the chess piece that moves diagonally. The same noun has other dictionary senses, including a person who runs, a football attacker and a narrow decorative cloth or mat; those senses have separate entries.
