## lekse

**English:** homework; lesson

**Topics:** school

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Artikkel, hunkjønn | ei lekse | Hun fikk ei lekse i matematikk. | She got a math assignment. |
| Artikkel, hankjønn | en lekse | Hun fikk en lekse i matematikk. | She got a math assignment. |
| Entall ubestemt | lekse | Jeg må gjøre ei lekse før middag. | I have to do an assignment before dinner. |
| Entall bestemt, a-form | leksa | Leksa var vanskelig. | The assignment was difficult. |
| Entall bestemt, en-form | leksen | Den leksen var vanskelig. | That assignment was difficult. |
| Flertall ubestemt | lekser | Elevene gjør lekser etter skolen. | The students do homework after school. |
| Flertall bestemt | leksene | Leksene ligger i sekken. | The homework is in the backpack. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The noun is feminine or masculine; both article choices are illustrated. The plural is used idiomatically for school assignments.
