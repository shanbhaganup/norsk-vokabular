## ordre

**English:** order; command; purchase order

**Topics:** general, business

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En ordre kan angi hva som skal gjøres. | An order can state what is to be done. |
| Entall ubestemt | ordre | En ordre må være tydelig. | An order must be clear. |
| Entall bestemt | ordren | Sjefen ga en ordre før avreise. | The manager gave an order before departure. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ordrer | Ordren ble sendt til leverandøren. | The purchase order was sent to the supplier. |
| Flertall bestemt | ordrene | Butikken mottok flere ordrer fra utlandet. | The shop received several orders from abroad. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Dictionary distinguishes a command/directive from a purchase order. `ordre` is a separate lemma from `orden`; source gloss broadly translates both as “order”.
