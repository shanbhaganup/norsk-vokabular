## orm

**English:** worm; snake

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En orm lever i jorda. | A worm lives in the soil. |
| Entall ubestemt | orm | En orm krøp gjennom det våte gresset. | A worm crawled through the wet grass. |
| Entall bestemt | ormen | Barna fant en orm under steinen. | The children found a worm under the stone. |
| Flertall ubestemt | ormer | Ormen forsvant inn mellom røttene. | The worm disappeared among the roots. |
| Flertall bestemt | ormene | Flere ormer lever i jorda. | Several worms live in the soil. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Dictionary includes snakes, legless lizards, invertebrate worms, some insect larvae, and a computer virus called an orm. Examples use the common animal sense.
