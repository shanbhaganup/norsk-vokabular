## barnebarn

**English:** grandchild

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Hun fikk et barnebarn i mai. | She had a grandchild in May. |
| Entall ubestemt | barnebarn | De passer et barnebarn hver fredag. | They look after a grandchild every Friday. |
| Entall bestemt | barnebarnet | Barnebarnet kom på besøk. | The grandchild came to visit. |
| Flertall ubestemt | barnebarn | Han har fire barnebarn. | He has four grandchildren. |
| Flertall bestemt | barnebarna | Hun bakte boller til barnebarna. | She baked buns for her grandchildren. |
| Flertall bestemt | barnebarnene | Han skrev et kort til hvert av barnebarnene. | He wrote a card to each of his grandchildren. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The noun is neuter and takes two definite plural endings.
