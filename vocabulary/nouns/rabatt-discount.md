# rabatt

**English:** discount; price reduction

**Topics:** shopping; money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En rabatt gjelder hvis du kjøper to varer. | A discount applies if you buy two items. |
| Entall ubestemt | rabatt | Kunden fikk rabatt på den gamle modellen. | The customer got a discount on the older model. |
| Entall bestemt | rabatten | Rabatten ble trukket fra i kassa. | The discount was deducted at the checkout. |
| Flertall ubestemt | rabatter | Butikken tilbyr rabatter på vinterklær. | The shop offers discounts on winter clothes. |
| Flertall bestemt | rabattene | Rabattene gjelder til månedsskiftet. | The discounts are valid until the end of the month. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword, gender and paradigm checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Dictionary homonym rabatt 2 means a price reduction and matches the source gloss; distinct from rabatt 1, a flower bed or median strip.
