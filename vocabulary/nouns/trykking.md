## trykking

**English:** printing

**Topics:** work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei | Ei trykking av boka ble bestilt før jul. | A print run of the book was ordered before Christmas. |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En trykking av boka ble bestilt før jul. | A print run of the book was ordered before Christmas. |
| Entall ubestemt | trykking | Trykking av etikettene skjer lokalt. | Printing of the labels is done locally. |
| Entall bestemt | trykkinga | Trykkinga startet etter at korrekturen var godkjent. | Printing began after the proof was approved. |
| Entall bestemt | trykkingen | Trykkingen startet etter at korrekturen var godkjent. | Printing began after the proof was approved. |
