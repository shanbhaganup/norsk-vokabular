## midnatt

**English:** midnight

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | ei / en | Ei midnatt / En midnatt kan være lys nordpå om sommeren. | A midnight can be bright in the north in summer. |
| Entall ubestemt | midnatt | Vi kom fram rett før midnatt. | We arrived just before midnight. |
| Entall bestemt | midnatta / midnatten | Midnatta / Midnatten var stille i byen. | The midnight was quiet in the city. |
| Flertall ubestemt | midnetter | Flere midnetter på rad var uvanlig varme. | Several midnights in a row were unusually warm. |
| Flertall bestemt | midnettene | Midnettene på turen var korte. | The midnights on the trip were brief. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and applicable forms checked; examples and translations are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Both feminine and masculine forms are accepted; plural use is uncommon and refers to separate nights.
