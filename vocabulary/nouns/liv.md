## liv

**English:** life; lives

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et liv kan forandre seg raskt. | A life can change quickly. |
| Entall ubestemt | liv | Hun lever et rolig liv på landet. | She lives a quiet life in the countryside. |
| Entall bestemt | livet | Livet i byen er travelt. | Life in the city is busy. |
| Flertall ubestemt | liv | Mange liv gikk tapt i ulykken. | Many lives were lost in the accident. |
| Flertall bestemt, -a | liva | Liva deres ble snudd på hodet. | Their lives were turned upside down. |
| Flertall bestemt, -ene | livene | Livene deres ble snudd på hodet. | Their lives were turned upside down. |
