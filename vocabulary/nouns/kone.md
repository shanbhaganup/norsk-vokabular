## kone

**English:** wife

**Topics:** family, relationships

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel, hunkjønn | ei | Ei kone ventet utenfor huset. | A wife waited outside the house. |
| Kjønn / artikkel, hankjønn | en | En kone ventet utenfor huset. | A wife waited outside the house. |
| Entall ubestemt | kone | Han har kone og to barn. | He has a wife and two children. |
| Entall bestemt, hunkjønn | kona | Kona hans jobber på sykehuset. | His wife works at the hospital. |
| Entall bestemt, hankjønn | konen | Konen hans jobber på sykehuset. | His wife works at the hospital. |
| Flertall ubestemt | koner | Mange koner deltok i møtet. | Many wives took part in the meeting. |
| Flertall bestemt | konene | Konene deres kom litt senere. | Their wives arrived a little later. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and paradigm checked; sense reviewed; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Promoted from reference record word-0765. Both feminine inflection (ei kone, kona) and masculine inflection (en kone, konen) are accepted in Bokmål. The examples deliberately illustrate both accepted gender patterns.
