## beist

**English:** beast, brute

**Topics:** nature

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | I eventyret voktet et beist brua. | In the fairy tale, a beast guarded the bridge. |
| Entall ubestemt | beist | Hun tegnet et beist med tre hoder. | She drew a beast with three heads. |
| Entall bestemt | beistet | Beistet gjemte seg i skogen. | The beast hid in the forest. |
| Flertall ubestemt | beist | To beist sperret veien i fortellingen. | Two beasts blocked the road in the story. |
| Flertall bestemt | beista | Beista brølte da porten åpnet seg. | The beasts roared when the gate opened. |
| Flertall bestemt | beistene | Barna lo av de klønete beistene. | The children laughed at the clumsy beasts. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Beist is often expressive. Both definite plural endings are accepted.
