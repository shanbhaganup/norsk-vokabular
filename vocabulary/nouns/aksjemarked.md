## aksjemarked

**English:** stock market

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et aksjemarked knytter sammen kjøpere og selgere. | A stock market connects buyers and sellers. |
| Entall ubestemt | aksjemarked | Landet fikk sitt første aksjemarked på 1990-tallet. | The country got its first stock market in the 1990s. |
| Entall bestemt | aksjemarkedet | Aksjemarkedet falt etter nyheten. | The stock market fell after the news. |
| Flertall ubestemt | aksjemarked | Rapporten sammenligner tre aksjemarked i Europa. | The report compares three stock markets in Europe. |
| Flertall ubestemt | aksjemarkeder | Flere aksjemarkeder holdt stengt på mandag. | Several stock markets were closed on Monday. |
| Flertall bestemt | aksjemarkeda | De nordiske aksjemarkeda reagerte ulikt på nyheten. | The Nordic stock markets reacted differently to the news. |
| Flertall bestemt | aksjemarkedene | Aksjemarkedene i Asia åpnet tidligere. | The stock markets in Asia opened earlier. |

**Form status:** Verified against Bokmålsordboka. Original teaching examples and translations.

**Notes:** Both indefinite plural forms and both definite plural forms are accepted in Bokmål.
