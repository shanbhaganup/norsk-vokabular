## iskrem

**English:** ice cream

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En iskrem med jordbær smeltet i sola. | A strawberry ice cream melted in the sun. |
| Entall ubestemt | iskrem | Hun kjøpte en iskrem på vei hjem. | She bought an ice cream on her way home. |
| Entall bestemt | iskremen | Iskremen sto litt for lenge på bordet. | The ice cream sat on the table too long. |
| Flertall ubestemt | iskremer | Barna bestilte tre iskremer etter badet. | The children ordered three ice creams after swimming. |
| Flertall bestemt | iskremene | Iskremene ble servert med bær. | The ice creams were served with berries. |

**Form status:** Headword and paradigm reviewed against Bokmålsordboka; examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The noun denotes frozen dessert as a mass noun and can also denote a serving, as in the countable examples.
