## motstand

**English:** opposition; resistance

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En motstand kan bremse framdriften. | Resistance can slow progress. |
| Entall ubestemt | motstand | Prosjektet møtte en motstand fra naboene. | The project met opposition from the neighbours. |
| Entall bestemt | motstanden | Motstanden mot planen økte. | Opposition to the plan increased. |
| Flertall ubestemt | motstander | Flere motstander ble registrert under høringen. | Several objections were recorded during the consultation. |
