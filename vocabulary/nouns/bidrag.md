## bidrag

**English:** contribution

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Hun ga et viktig bidrag til prosjektet. | She made an important contribution to the project. |
| Entall ubestemt | bidrag | Han skrev et bidrag til jubileumsboka. | He wrote a contribution to the anniversary book. |
| Entall bestemt | bidraget | Bidraget gjorde det mulig å trykke boka. | The contribution made it possible to print the book. |
| Flertall ubestemt | bidrag | Redaksjonen fikk tre bidrag til neste nummer. | The editors received three contributions for the next issue. |
| Flertall bestemt | bidraga | Bidraga kom fra flere skoler. | The contributions came from several schools. |
| Flertall bestemt | bidragene | Hun takket for alle bidragene. | She thanked everyone for their contributions. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The examples illustrate something contributed to a shared effort.
