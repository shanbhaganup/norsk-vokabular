## krystall

**English:** crystal; crystalline material

**Topics:** nature, science

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Artikkel, hankjønn | en krystall | En krystall glitret i steinen. | A crystal glittered in the rock. |
| Artikkel, intetkjønn | et krystall | Et krystall vokste langsomt i hulen. | A crystal grew slowly in the cave. |
| Entall ubestemt | krystall | Hun studerer hvordan krystall dannes. | She is studying how crystals form. |
| Entall bestemt, hankjønn | krystallen | Krystallen lå på laboratoriebenken. | The crystal lay on the laboratory bench. |
| Entall bestemt, intetkjønn | krystallet | Krystallet hadde en klar, symmetrisk struktur. | The crystal had a clear, symmetrical structure. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Both masculine and neuter genders and both plural patterns are listed. The unchanged plural and a-form are less transparent alternatives and are shown in scientific contexts.
