## dato

**English:** date; calendar date

**Topics:** daily life, society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En dato ble satt for møtet. | A date was set for the meeting. |
| Entall ubestemt | dato | Vi må finne en dato som passer alle. | We need to find a date that works for everyone. |
| Entall bestemt | datoen | Datoen står øverst på brevet. | The date is at the top of the letter. |
| Flertall ubestemt | datoer | Flere datoer er ledige i juni. | Several dates are available in June. |
| Flertall bestemt | datoene | Datoene ble bekreftet av arrangøren. | The dates were confirmed by the organizer. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; Norwegian teaching examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** Dictionary also records dato in the expression av gammel dato; this entry teaches the calendar sense.
