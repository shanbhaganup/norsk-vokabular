## fraksjon

**English:** fraction; faction

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En fraksjon i partiet foreslo endringer. | A faction in the party proposed changes. |
| Entall ubestemt | fraksjon | En liten fraksjon krevde en ny avstemning. | A small faction demanded a new vote. |
| Entall bestemt | fraksjonen | Fraksjonen fikk støtte fra flere medlemmer. | The faction received support from several members. |
| Flertall ubestemt | fraksjoner | Flere fraksjoner var representert på møtet. | Several factions were represented at the meeting. |
| Flertall bestemt | fraksjonene | Fraksjonene ble enige om et kompromiss. | The factions agreed on a compromise. |

**Form status:** Dictionary headword and listed forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** In this entry, "fraction" means a subgroup within a political party or association; the English gloss "fraction" is broad.
