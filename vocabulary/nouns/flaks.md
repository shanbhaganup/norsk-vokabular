## flaks

**English:** luck; a flapping movement

**Topics:** general

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en (luck) / et (flapping movement) | En flaks reddet laget. / Et flaks fra vingen løftet støv. | A stroke of luck saved the team. / A flap of the wing lifted dust. |
| Entall ubestemt | flaks | Hun hadde flaks og rakk toget. / Fuglen gjorde et flaks med vingene. | She was lucky and caught the train. / The bird gave a flap of its wings. |
| Entall bestemt | flaksen / flakset | Flaksen hans snudde. / Flakset var så kraftig at løvet virvlet opp. | His luck changed. / The flap was so strong that the leaves swirled up. |
| Flertall ubestemt | flaks / flakser | Jeg håper på litt flaks. / Fuglen gjorde tre flakser med vingene. | I hope for a little luck. / The bird made three flaps with its wings. |
| Flertall bestemt | flaksa / flaksene | Flaksa var på hennes side. / Flaksene fra vingene sendte støv opp i lufta. | Luck was on her side. / The flaps of the wings sent dust into the air. |

**Form status:** Two dictionary entries with separate gender and inflection patterns.

**Notes:** The masculine luck sense is generally a mass noun; plural uses are unusual. The neuter sense means a flapping movement. Paradigms are taken from the reference and should be checked carefully against both homonym entries.
