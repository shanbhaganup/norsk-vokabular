## barneskole

**English:** primary school

**Topics:** school

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | Kommunen bygde en ny barneskole. | The municipality built a new primary school. |
| Entall ubestemt | barneskole | Hun underviser på en barneskole. | She teaches at a primary school. |
| Entall bestemt | barneskolen | Barneskolen ligger ved idrettsplassen. | The primary school is next to the sports ground. |
| Flertall ubestemt | barneskoler | Bydelen har tre barneskoler. | The neighbourhood has three primary schools. |
| Flertall bestemt | barneskolene | Barneskolene samarbeider om en turdag. | The primary schools are organizing an outing together. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** In Norway, barneskole normally covers the first seven school years.
