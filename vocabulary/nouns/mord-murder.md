## mord

**English:** murder

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et mord rystet lokalsamfunnet. | A murder shook the local community. |
| Entall ubestemt | mord | Politiet etterforsker et mord. | The police are investigating a murder. |
| Entall bestemt | mordet | Mordet ble omtalt i avisa. | The murder was reported in the newspaper. |
| Flertall ubestemt | mord | Romanen handler om flere mord. | The novel is about several murders. |
| Flertall bestemt | morda | Morda i boka blir etterforsket av en privatdetektiv. | The murders in the book are investigated by a private detective. |
| Flertall bestemt | mordene | Mordene fikk stor oppmerksomhet. | The murders attracted a lot of attention. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene forms verified; original examples written for each listed form.

**Notes:** Dictionary also records an obsolete legal use for premeditated murder; the main modern sense here is the act of murdering. Zero plural is dictionary-listed.
