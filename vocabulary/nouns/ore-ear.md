## øre

**English:** ear

**Topics:** health, body

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et (intetkjønn) | Et øre kan oppfatte svake lyder. | An ear can detect faint sounds. |
| Entall ubestemt | øre | Barnet har vondt i et øre. | The child has pain in one ear. |
| Entall bestemt | øret | Øret hennes ble rødt i kulden. | Her ear turned red in the cold. |
| Flertall ubestemt, øre-paradigme | ører | Etter konserten ringte det for ørene hans. | After the concert, his ears were ringing. |
| Flertall ubestemt, øyne-paradigme | øyne | I den eldre dialekten brukte de «øyne» om ører. | In the older dialect they used “øyne” to mean ears. |
| Flertall bestemt, øre-paradigme | øra / ørene | Øra hennes var kalde. / Ørene hennes var kalde. | Her ears were cold. / Her ears were cold. |
| Flertall bestemt, øyne-paradigme | øynene | I dialekten brukte de «øynene» om ørene. | In the dialect they used “øynene” to mean ears. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling, gender and forms checked; examples authored for teaching.

**Notes:** The body-part sense has the neuter paradigm. Bokmålsordboka accepts the plural stems *ører* and *øyne*, with definite plural *øra/ørene* and *øynene* respectively. The *øyne* paradigm is rare/older; its examples identify it explicitly as a dialect form to avoid confusion with the ordinary word for eyes.
