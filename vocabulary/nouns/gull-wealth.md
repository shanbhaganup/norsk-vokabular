## gull

**English:** wealth; riches

**Topics:** money

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et gammelt uttrykk forbinder gods og gull med rikdom. | An old expression associates property and gold with wealth. |
| Entall ubestemt | gull | I eventyret lovet kongen gull og grønne skoger. | In the fairy tale the king promised riches and every advantage. |
| Entall bestemt | gullet | Gullet etter arven ble satt inn i banken. | The inherited wealth was deposited in the bank. |
| Flertall ubestemt | gull | Gamle fortellinger handler ofte om gods og gull. | Old stories often concern property and riches. |
| Flertall bestemt | gulla | Gulla i uttrykket viser til rikdommer. | The “golds” in the expression refers to riches. |
| Flertall bestemt | gullene | Gullene i uttrykket viser til rikdommer. | The “golds” in the expression refers to riches. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; the plural uses are uncommon and refer to separate stores or kinds of wealth.

**Notes:** Distinct from the literal metal sense. The dictionary gives *rikdom, penger* (“wealth, money”) for this meaning; plural inflection is possible but uncommon. Examples distinguish the dictionary paradigm from mass-like idiomatic use.
