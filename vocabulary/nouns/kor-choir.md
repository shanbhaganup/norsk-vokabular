## kor

**English:** choir

**Topics:** culture, music

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et kor øver i kirken hver tirsdag. | A choir rehearses in the church every Tuesday. |
| Entall ubestemt | kor | Skolen startet et kor for nybegynnere. | The school started a choir for beginners. |
| Entall bestemt | koret | Koret skal synge på konserten. | The choir will sing at the concert. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kor | Flere kor deltok på festivalen. | Several choirs took part in the festival. |
| Flertall bestemt, -a-form | kora | Kora møttes før forestillingen. | The choirs met before the performance. |
| Flertall bestemt, -ene-form | korene | Korene sang hver sin sang. | The choirs sang one song each. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and all listed forms checked; examples and English gloss authored for this project.

**Notes:** The dictionary lists neuter kor and both definite plural forms, kora and korene. The selected sense is a group of singers; kor also has other meanings, including backing singers, an improvised musical passage, a group speaking in unison, and the choir area of a church.
