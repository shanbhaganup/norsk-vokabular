## folk

**English:** people; a people

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et folk bodde langs elva. | A people lived along the river. |
| Entall ubestemt | folk | Et folk bodde langs elva. | A people lived along the river. |
| Entall bestemt | folket | Folket samlet seg på torget. | The people gathered in the square. |
| Flertall ubestemt | folk | Mange folk ventet utenfor. | Many people waited outside. |
| Flertall bestemt | folka / folkene | Folka / Folkene i bygda kjenner hverandre. | The people in the village know one another. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms recorded in the reference.

**Notes:** Collective noun; plural *folk* commonly takes plural agreement. Definite plural alternatives are accepted. Source forms *folket* and *folks* are inflected forms, not separate lemmas.
