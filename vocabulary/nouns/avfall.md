## avfall

**English:** waste

**Topics:** environment

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et avfall som inneholder kjemikalier må leveres separat. | Waste containing chemicals must be disposed of separately. |
| Entall ubestemt | avfall | Fabrikken sorterer avfall før det sendes til gjenvinning. | The factory sorts waste before it is sent for recycling. |
| Entall bestemt | avfallet | Avfallet fra byggeplassen ble fraktet bort. | The waste from the construction site was taken away. |
| Flertall ubestemt | avfall | Laboratoriet undersøkte ulike avfall fra industrien. | The laboratory examined different industrial wastes. |
| Flertall bestemt | avfalla / avfallene | Avfalla ble veid før behandling. / Avfallene ble veid før behandling. | The waste materials were weighed before treatment. / The waste materials were weighed before treatment. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** Mass noun; plural forms refer to separate types or batches of waste. The determiner in the article example is an editorial teaching sentence, not a claim that the countable use is the usual one.
