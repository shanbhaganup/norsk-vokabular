## motor

**English:** engine; motor

**Topics:** technology

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En motor driver båten framover. | An engine propels the boat forward. |
| Entall ubestemt | motor | Verkstedet installerte en ny motor. | The workshop installed a new engine. |
| Entall bestemt | motoren | Motoren stoppet midt på veien. | The engine stopped in the middle of the road. |
| Flertall ubestemt | motorer | Moderne motorer bruker mindre drivstoff. | Modern engines use less fuel. |
| Flertall bestemt | motorene | Motorene blir kontrollert før avreise. | The engines are checked before departure. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms verified; original teaching examples supplied for every listed form.

**Notes:** The dictionary also records a figurative “driving force” sense; the source English gloss and technology topic indicate the mechanical sense.
