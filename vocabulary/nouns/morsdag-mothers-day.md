## morsdag

**English:** Mother’s Day

**Topics:** family

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En morsdag feires hver februar i Norge. | Mother’s Day is celebrated each February in Norway. |
| Entall ubestemt | morsdag | Vi planlegger frokost på senga til morsdag. | We are planning breakfast in bed for Mother’s Day. |
| Entall bestemt | morsdagen | Morsdagen faller på en søndag i år. | Mother’s Day falls on a Sunday this year. |
| Flertall ubestemt | morsdager | Ulike land feirer morsdager på forskjellige datoer. | Different countries celebrate Mother’s Days on different dates. |
| Flertall bestemt | morsdagene | Morsdagene i de to landene faller på ulike datoer. | Mother’s Days in the two countries fall on different dates. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene forms verified; original examples written for each listed form.

**Notes:** The dictionary specifies that Mother’s Day in Norway is celebrated on the second Sunday in February.
