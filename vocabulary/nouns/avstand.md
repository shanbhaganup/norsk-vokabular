## avstand

**English:** distance; gap

**Topics:** travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En avstand på to meter gir plass til rullestolen. | A distance of two metres leaves room for the wheelchair. |
| Entall ubestemt | avstand | Hold avstand til kanten av plattformen. | Keep your distance from the edge of the platform. |
| Entall bestemt | avstanden | Avstanden mellom husene er kort. | The distance between the houses is short. |
| Flertall ubestemt | avstander | De målte avstander mellom trærne i parken. | They measured distances between the trees in the park. |
| Flertall bestemt | avstandene | Avstandene mellom holdeplassene varierer. | The distances between the stops vary. |

**Form status:** Dictionary forms checked; original teaching examples supplied.

**Notes:** The dictionary also uses avstand figuratively for a social or conceptual gap.
