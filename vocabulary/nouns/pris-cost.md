## pris

**English:** price; cost

**Topics:** money; shopping

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En pris kan endre seg raskt. | A price can change quickly. |
| Entall ubestemt | pris | Vi betalte en lav pris for billettene. | We paid a low price for the tickets. |
| Entall bestemt | prisen | Prisen på kaffe har økt. | The price of coffee has risen. |
| Flertall ubestemt | priser | Flere priser ble sammenlignet før kjøpet. | Several prices were compared before the purchase. |
| Flertall bestemt | prisene | Prisene varierer fra butikk til butikk. | Prices vary from shop to shop. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Dictionary homonym 1, sense 1: the amount paid for a good or service. Separate from an award, praise, and the homonymous snuff pinch (pris-snuff.md). Source gender corrected to masculine.
