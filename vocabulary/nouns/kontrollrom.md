## kontrollrom

**English:** control room

**Topics:** technology, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |

**Notes:** The noun is neuter, with uninflected indefinite plural and both definite plural alternatives. It denotes a room with technical equipment used to monitor a process or operation.
