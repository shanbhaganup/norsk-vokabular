## mile

**English:** English mile (1,609 metres)

**Topics:** numbers, travel

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En mile er litt over halvannen kilometer. | An English mile is a little over one and a half kilometres. |
| Entall ubestemt | mile | Løpet var én mile langt. | The race was one mile long. |
| Entall bestemt | milen | Milen tilsvarer 1 609 meter. | The mile equals 1,609 metres. |
| Flertall ubestemt | miles | De løp tre miles langs kysten. | They ran three miles along the coast. |
| Flertall bestemt | milene | Milene ble målt med GPS. | The miles were measured with GPS. |

**Form status:** Ordbøkene entry 2 masculine paradigm checked; examples authored for this entry.

**Notes:** This is `mile 2`, the English mile, pronounced like English *mile*. Its plural is `miles`; the separate charcoal-kiln homonym has plural `miler`.
