## kveg

**English:** cattle; livestock

**Topics:** animals, farming

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Et kveg beitet på jordet. | Some cattle grazed in the field. |
| Entall ubestemt | kveg | Gården har kveg av flere raser. | The farm has cattle of several breeds. |
| Entall bestemt | kveget | Kveget ble flyttet til en annen innhegning. | The cattle were moved to another enclosure. |
| Flertall ubestemt | kveg | Flere kveg beitet nær veien. | Several head of cattle grazed near the road. |
| Flertall bestemt | kvega / kvegene | Kvega ble vaksinert i vår. / Kvegene ble vaksinert i vår. | The cattle were vaccinated this spring. / The cattle were vaccinated this spring. |

**Form status:** Verified against the cited Bokmålsordboka entry for spelling, gender, and paradigm; original teaching examples authored for this project.

**Notes:** *Kveg* is a collective noun for livestock; the plural is the same shape as the indefinite singular. The two definite plural alternatives are illustrated separately.
