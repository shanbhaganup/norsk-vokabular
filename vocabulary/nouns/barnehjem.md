## barnehjem

**English:** children’s home, orphanage

**Topics:** society

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | et | Byen hadde et barnehjem ved parken. | The town had a children’s home by the park. |
| Entall ubestemt | barnehjem | Hun bodde på barnehjem som liten. | She lived in a children’s home when she was little. |
| Entall bestemt | barnehjemmet | Barnehjemmet ble lagt ned i 1975. | The children’s home closed in 1975. |
| Flertall ubestemt | barnehjem | Kommunen drev to barnehjem. | The municipality ran two children’s homes. |
| Flertall bestemt | barnehjemma | Barnehjemma fikk nye lokaler. | The children’s homes moved into new premises. |
| Flertall bestemt | barnehjemmene | Historikeren fant bilder av barnehjemmene. | The historian found photographs of the children’s homes. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The word denotes a residential home for children. Orphanage fits when the context specifically concerns orphans.
