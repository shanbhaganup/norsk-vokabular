## post

**English:** item; agenda point; accounting entry

**Topics:** work; finance

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en | En post står først på programmet. | One item comes first on the program. |
| Entall ubestemt | post | Regnskapet mangler en post. | The accounts are missing an entry. |
| Entall bestemt | posten | Posten på programmet ble flyttet. | The item on the program was moved. |
| Flertall ubestemt | poster | Flere poster ble lagt til regnskapet. | Several entries were added to the accounts. |
| Flertall bestemt | postene | Postene på dagsordenen ble gjennomgått. | The items on the agenda were reviewed. |

**Form status:** Dictionary gender and paradigm checked in Ordbøkene; original teaching examples supplied for each listed form and alternative.

**Notes:** Distinct dictionary homonym 3: an item/point on a program or an accounting entry. Related uses are kept together as an item in a sequence or record.
