## skur (harvesting; harvest)

**English:** harvesting; harvest

**Topics:** weather, work

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kjønn / artikkel | en skur | En skur av tømmer tok tid. | Timber sawing took time. |
| Entall ubestemt | skur | En skur av korn foregikk for hånd. | A harvest of grain was done by hand. |
| Entall bestemt | skuren | Skuren var snart ferdig. | The harvest was nearly finished. |
| Flertall ubestemt | skurer | Flere skurer fulgte etter hverandre. | Several harvests followed one another. |
| Flertall bestemt | skurene | Skurene var ferdige før regnet kom. | The harvests were finished before the rain came. |
