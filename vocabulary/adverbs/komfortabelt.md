## komfortabelt

**English:** comfortably

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | komfortabelt | Vi satt komfortabelt hele veien. | We sat comfortably the whole way. |
| Komparativ, konstruksjon | mer komfortabelt | Man sitter mer komfortabelt med en pute i ryggen. | You sit more comfortably with a cushion behind your back. |
| Superlativ, konstruksjon | mest komfortabelt | Jeg sitter mest komfortabelt på den brede benken. | I am most comfortable sitting on the wide bench. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Adverbial use of komfortabel. Mer and mest are productive degree constructions here.
