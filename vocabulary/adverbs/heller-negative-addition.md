## heller

**English:** either, neither (with a negative)

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | heller | Hun vil heller ikke være med på turen. | She does not want to join the trip either. |
| Komparativ | — | Den additive bruken har ingen gradbøying. | The additive use has no degree forms. |
| Superlativ | — | Den additive bruken har ingen gradbøying. | The additive use has no degree forms. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka usage checked. The example is an original teaching sentence.

**Notes:** Additive heller occurs with a negative and means ‘either’. The preference sense ‘rather’ is covered under [gjerne](gjerne.md).
