## omtenksomt

**English:** thoughtfully, considerately

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | omtenksomt | Hun ordnet det omtenksomt slik at alle fikk sitte sammen. | She thoughtfully arranged for everyone to sit together. |
| Komparativ, konstruksjon | mer omtenksomt | Han oppførte seg mer omtenksomt da han forstod situasjonen. | He behaved more considerately once he understood the situation. |
| Superlativ, konstruksjon | mest omtenksomt | Av alle gjestene oppførte hun seg mest omtenksomt. | Of all the guests, she behaved most considerately. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Adverbial use of omtenksom. The degree rows use productive mer and mest constructions.
