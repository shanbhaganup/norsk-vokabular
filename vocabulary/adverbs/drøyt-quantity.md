## drøyt

**English:** a little over, just over

**Topics:** numbers

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | drøyt | Turen tok drøyt tre timer. | The trip took a little over three hours. |
| Komparativ | — | Adverbet gradbøyes ikke i denne bruken. | The adverb has no comparison forms in this use. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Before a quantity, drøyt means a little more than the stated amount. The source’s ‘excessively’ does not fit this construction.
