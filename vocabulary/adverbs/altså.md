## altså

**English:** so; therefore; that is

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Konklusjon | altså | Fristen er i dag, så vi må altså sende brevet nå. | The deadline is today, so we have to send the letter now. |
| Presisering | altså | Vi kommer om to dager, altså på torsdag. | We’ll arrive in two days, that is, on Thursday. |
