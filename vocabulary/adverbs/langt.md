## langt

**English:** far

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | langt | Vi gikk langt før vi fant en åpen butikk. | We walked a long way before we found a shop that was open. |
| Komparativ | lenger | Vi gikk lenger i dag enn i går. | We walked farther today than yesterday. |
| Superlativ | lengst | De minste barna gikk lengst av alle. | The youngest children walked the farthest of all. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** These forms compare distance. The snapshot’s standalone lenger record also overlaps with the degree forms of lenge.
