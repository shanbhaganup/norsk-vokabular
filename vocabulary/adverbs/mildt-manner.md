## mildt

**English:** gently, mildly

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | mildt | Hun svarte mildt selv om hun var uenig. | She answered gently even though she disagreed. |
| Komparativ | mildere | Han snakket mildere da han så at hun var lei seg. | He spoke more gently when he saw she was upset. |
| Superlativ | mildest | Hun snakket mildest til de yngste barna. | She spoke most gently to the youngest children. |

**Notes:** Adverbial use of mild. The degree forms compare gentleness.
