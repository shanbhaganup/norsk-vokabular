## aktivt

**English:** actively

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform / positiv | aktivt | Elevene deltok aktivt i debatten. | The pupils took an active part in the debate. |
| Komparativ, konstruksjon | mer aktivt | Hun deltar mer aktivt nå enn i fjor. | She participates more actively now than last year. |
| Superlativ, konstruksjon | mest aktivt | De yngste medlemmene deltok mest aktivt i samtalen. | The youngest members participated most actively in the conversation. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Bokmålsordboka lists this adverbial use under the adjective aktiv. Mer aktivt and mest aktivt are productive degree constructions.
