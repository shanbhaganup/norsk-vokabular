## brått

**English:** suddenly, abruptly

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | brått | Samtalen tok brått slutt. | The conversation came to an abrupt end. |
| Komparativ | bråere | Bussen stoppet bråere enn jeg ventet. | The bus stopped more abruptly than I expected. |
| Superlativ | bråest | Sjåføren bremset bråest i den siste svingen. | The driver braked most abruptly on the last bend. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms or use checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The adverbial use of brå describes the abruptness of an action or event.
