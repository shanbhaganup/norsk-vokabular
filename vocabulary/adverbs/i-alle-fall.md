## i alle fall

**English:** at least, in any case

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | i alle fall | Jeg kan i alle fall hjelpe deg med å bære. | I can at least help you carry things. |
| Grunnform, alternativ | iallfall | Vi bør iallfall gi dem beskjed. | We should at least let them know. |
| Komparativ | — | Adverbet gradbøyes ikke i denne bruken. | The adverb has no comparison forms in this use. |
| Superlativ | — | Adverbet gradbøyes ikke i denne bruken. | The adverb has no comparison forms in this use. |
