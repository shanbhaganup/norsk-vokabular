## ikke

**English:** not

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | ikke | Jeg hørte ikke hva hun sa. | I didn’t hear what she said. |
| Grunnform, leddsetning | ikke | Hun sa at hun ikke hadde nøkkelen. | She said that she didn’t have the key. |
| Komparativ | — | Adverbet gradbøyes ikke i denne bruken. | The adverb has no comparison forms in this use. |
| Superlativ | — | Adverbet gradbøyes ikke i denne bruken. | The adverb has no comparison forms in this use. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** In a neutral main clause ikke normally follows the finite verb. In a neutral subordinate clause it normally precedes the finite verb.
