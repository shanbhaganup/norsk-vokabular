## mye

**English:** much, a lot

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | mye | Hun leser mye om vinteren. | She reads a lot in winter. |
| Komparativ | mer | Jeg leser mer nå enn før. | I read more now than I used to. |
| Superlativ | mest | Hun leser mest i feriene. | She reads the most during holidays. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The forms mer and mest compare amount or extent. The source record mest is included here as the superlative.
