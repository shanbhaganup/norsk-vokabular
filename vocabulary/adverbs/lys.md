## lys

**English:** fully; completely (in fixed expressions)

**Topics:** describing

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform / positiv | lys | Han var lys levende etter ulykken. | He was very much alive after the accident. |
| Komparativ | — | — | — |
| Superlativ | — | — | — |

**Form status:** Invariable adverb verified in Bokmålsordboka.

**Notes:** This intensifying adverb means “completely” in fixed combinations such as lys levende and lys våken. It does not form comparative or superlative degrees in this use. The reference snapshot associates the source form with light from the sun or a lamp; that gloss belongs with the noun sense, while this entry follows the dictionary-confirmed adverb sense. Separate from adjective lys and noun lys. Reference entry [added-87d26ba0269e](../../reference/vocabulary-4472/word-types/adverbs.md#added-87d26ba0269e).
