## framme

**English:** there; at the destination

**Topics:** directions

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ankomst | framme | Vi er framme om ti minutter. | We’ll be there in ten minutes. |
| Ankomst | fremme | Bussen er fremme ved stasjonen. | The bus has arrived at the station. |
| Komparativ | — | — | This adverbial use has no degree inflection. |
| Superlativ | — | — | This adverbial use has no degree inflection. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** Framme and fremme are parallel spellings. The expression være framme is common upon arrival. NoW source form: “framme”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
