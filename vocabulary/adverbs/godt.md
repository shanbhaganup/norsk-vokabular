## godt

**English:** well

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | godt | Jeg sov godt i natt. | I slept well last night. |
| Komparativ | bedre | Jeg sover bedre med vinduet åpent. | I sleep better with the window open. |
| Superlativ | best | Hun sover best når rommet er kjølig. | She sleeps best when the room is cool. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Adverbial use of god. The degree forms are better and best in English.
