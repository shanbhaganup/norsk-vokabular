## åpenlyst

**English:** openly, plainly

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | åpenlyst | De kritiserte åpenlyst avgjørelsen. | They openly criticized the decision. |
| Komparativ, konstruksjon | mer åpenlyst | Hun kritiserte forslaget mer åpenlyst enn før. | She criticized the proposal more openly than before. |
| Superlativ, konstruksjon | mest åpenlyst | Han protesterte mest åpenlyst av oss alle. | Of all of us, he objected most openly. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Adverbial use of åpenlys. The mer and mest rows illustrate productive comparison.
