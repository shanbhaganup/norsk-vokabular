## grovt

**English:** coarsely, roughly

**Topics:** food

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | grovt | Hakk nøttene grovt før du blander dem i røra. | Roughly chop the nuts before mixing them into the batter. |
| Komparativ | grovere | Hakk nøttene grovere enn mandlene. | Chop the nuts more coarsely than the almonds. |
| Superlativ | grovest | Vi hakket valnøttene grovest. | We chopped the walnuts most coarsely. |
| Superlativ, alternativ | grøvst | Hun hakket hasselnøttene grøvst. | She chopped the hazelnuts most coarsely. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The cooking sense is an adverbial use of grov. Both dictionary-listed superlative spellings are illustrated.
