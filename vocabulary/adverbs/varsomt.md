## varsomt

**English:** carefully, cautiously

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | varsomt | Hun løftet glasset varsomt ut av esken. | She carefully lifted the glass out of the box. |
| Komparativ | varsommere | Løft den andre esken varsommere. | Lift the other box more carefully. |
| Superlativ | varsomst | Hun behandlet de eldste brevene varsomst. | She handled the oldest letters most carefully. |
