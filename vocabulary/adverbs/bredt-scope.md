## bredt

**English:** widely, broadly

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | bredt | Vi søkte bredt etter nye medlemmer. | We searched widely for new members. |
| Grunnform, alternativ | breit | De satset breit for å nå flere velgere. | They took a broad approach to reach more voters. |
| Komparativ | bredere | Vi må lete bredere denne gangen. | We need to look more widely this time. |
| Komparativ, alternativ | breiere | Partiet må tenke breiere for å nå nye velgere. | The party needs to think more broadly to reach new voters. |
| Superlativ | bredest | Det er denne gruppa som favner bredest. | This is the group with the broadest reach. |
| Superlativ, alternativ | breiest | De prøvde å favne breiest mulig. | They tried to reach as wide a range of people as possible. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms or use checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** This adverbial use of bred/brei refers to scope. Both spelling families are illustrated.
