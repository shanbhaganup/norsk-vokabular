## lenge

**English:** for a long time

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | lenge | Vi ventet lenge på bussen. | We waited a long time for the bus. |
| Komparativ | lenger | Kan du bli litt lenger? | Can you stay a little longer? |
| Superlativ | lengst | Hun ventet lengst av alle. | She waited the longest of all. |
| Konstruksjon, negasjon | ikke lenger | Han bor ikke lenger i byen. | He no longer lives in the city. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** These forms compare duration. In negative contexts, ikke lenger means ‘no longer’ or ‘not anymore’.
