## vidt

**English:** widely

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | vidt | Saken ble vidt omtalt i pressen. | The matter was widely covered in the press. |
| Komparativ | videre | Nyheten spredte seg videre enn vi ventet. | The news spread farther than we expected. |
| Superlativ | videst | Av alle oppslagene spredte dette seg videst. | Of all the stories, this one spread the farthest. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Adverbial use of vid. The degree examples show extent of circulation.
