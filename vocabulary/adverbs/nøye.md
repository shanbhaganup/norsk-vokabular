## nøye

**English:** carefully, closely

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | nøye | Les instruksjonene nøye før du begynner. | Read the instructions carefully before you start. |
| Komparativ | nøyere | Vi undersøkte den andre prøven nøyere. | We examined the second sample more closely. |
| Superlativ | nøyest | Hun sjekket datoene nøyest. | She checked the dates most carefully. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The degree forms compare attention to detail.
