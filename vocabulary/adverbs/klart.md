## klart

**English:** clearly

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | klart | Hun forklarte regelen klart. | She explained the rule clearly. |
| Komparativ | klarere | Han forklarte det klarere andre gang. | He explained it more clearly the second time. |
| Superlativ | klarest | Av alle talerne formulerte hun seg klarest. | Of all the speakers, she expressed herself most clearly. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Adverbial use of klar. The degree forms compare clarity of expression.
