## ofte

**English:** often

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | ofte | Vi spiser ofte middag ute om sommeren. | We often eat dinner outdoors in summer. |
| Komparativ | oftere | Hun tar bussen oftere om vinteren. | She takes the bus more often in winter. |
| Superlativ | oftest | Vi møtes oftest på tirsdager. | We usually meet on Tuesdays. |

**Notes:** The three English glosses in the snapshot correspond to the three degree forms.
