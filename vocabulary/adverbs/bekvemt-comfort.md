## bekvemt

**English:** comfortably

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | bekvemt | Hun satt bekvemt med føttene på en krakk. | She sat comfortably with her feet on a stool. |
| Komparativ | bekvemmere | Jeg sitter bekvemmere her enn på benken. | I sit more comfortably here than on the bench. |
| Superlativ | bekvemmest | Jeg sitter bekvemmest ved vinduet. | I am most comfortable sitting by the window. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka forms or use checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Bekvemt is the adverbial use of bekvem. The degree forms can describe how comfortably someone sits or travels.
