## seint

**English:** late

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | seint | Bussen kom seint i går. | The bus arrived late yesterday. |
| Grunnform, alternativ | sent | Vi kom sent hjem. | We got home late. |
| Komparativ | seinere | Jeg kommer seinere enn planlagt. | I’ll arrive later than planned. |
| Komparativ, alternativ | senere | Hun ringte senere på kvelden. | She called later that evening. |
| Superlativ | seinest | Han kom seinest av oss tre. | He arrived latest of the three of us. |
| Superlativ, alternativ | senest | Pakken som ble sendt sist, kom også senest fram. | The parcel sent last also arrived last. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** The adverbial degree forms follow the accepted sein and sen spelling families.
