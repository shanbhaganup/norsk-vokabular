## snart

**English:** soon

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | snart | Vi er snart framme. | We’ll be there soon. |
| Komparativ | snarere | Møtet kom i gang snarere enn vi hadde ventet. | The meeting got started sooner than we had expected. |
| Superlativ | snarest | Send meg de nye målene snarest. | Send me the new measurements as soon as possible. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Snarere and snarest are the temporal degree forms. The adverbial superlative also expresses urgency and absorbs the separate snarest source record.
