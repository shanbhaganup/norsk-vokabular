## sakte

**English:** slowly

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | sakte | Døra lukket seg sakte. | The door closed slowly. |
| Komparativ | saktere | Hun kjørte saktere da det begynte å snø. | She drove more slowly when it started to snow. |
| Superlativ | saktest | Av alle bilene kjørte den blå saktest. | Of all the cars, the blue one was travelling slowest. |
