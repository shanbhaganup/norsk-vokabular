## negativt

**English:** negatively, unfavourably

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | negativt | Flere naboer reagerte negativt på forslaget. | Several neighbours reacted negatively to the proposal. |
| Komparativ, konstruksjon | mer negativt | De reagerte mer negativt denne gangen. | They reacted more negatively this time. |
| Superlativ, konstruksjon | mest negativt | Foreldrene reagerte mest negativt på endringen. | The parents reacted most negatively to the change. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Adverbial use of negativ. The mer and mest rows compare how unfavourable the reaction was.
