## strengt

**English:** strictly, sternly

**Topics:** communication

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | strengt | Læreren snakket strengt til klassen. | The teacher spoke sternly to the class. |
| Komparativ | strengere | Hun håndhever reglene strengere nå. | She enforces the rules more strictly now. |
| Superlativ | strengest | Han håndhever reglene strengest av alle. | He enforces the rules most strictly of all. |
