## da

**English:** then; at that time

**Topics:** time

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tid | da | Jeg bodde i Bergen da. | I lived in Bergen then. |
| Følge | da | Da tar vi neste buss. | Then we’ll take the next bus. |
| Komparativ | — | — | This adverbial use has no degree inflection. |
| Superlativ | — | — | This adverbial use has no degree inflection. |

**Form status:** Dictionary spelling and usage checked.

**Notes:** In this use da refers back to a time or marks the consequence of a stated situation. NoW source form: “da”. The bilingual examples are original teaching sentences.
