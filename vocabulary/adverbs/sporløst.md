## sporløst

**English:** without a trace

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | sporløst | Den røde kofferten forsvant sporløst fra lageret. | The red suitcase disappeared from the storeroom without a trace. |
| Komparativ | — | Bruken uttrykker fullstendig fravær av spor. | This use expresses a complete absence of traces. |
| Superlativ | — | Bruken uttrykker fullstendig fravær av spor. | This use expresses a complete absence of traces. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka attests this use under forsvinne. The example is an original teaching sentence.

**Notes:** Adverbial use of sporløs. It describes a disappearance that leaves no trace.
