## klokt

**English:** wisely

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | klokt | Hun handlet klokt da hun ba om hjelp. | She acted wisely when she asked for help. |
| Komparativ | klokere | Han handlet klokere denne gangen. | He acted more wisely this time. |
| Superlativ | klokest | Av de to handlet hun klokest. | Of the two, she acted most wisely. |
