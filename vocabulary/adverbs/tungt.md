## tungt

**English:** heavily

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | tungt | Hun pustet tungt etter løpet. | She was breathing heavily after the race. |
| Komparativ | tyngre | Han pustet tyngre da bakken ble brattere. | He breathed more heavily when the hill got steeper. |
| Superlativ | tyngst | Hun pustet tyngst de siste meterne. | She breathed most heavily in the last few metres. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Adverbial use of tung. The examples show degrees of heaviness in breathing.
