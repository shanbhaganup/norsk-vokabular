## dypt

**English:** deeply, deep

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | dypt | Hun pustet dypt før hun svarte. | She took a deep breath before answering. |
| Komparativ | dypere | Han pustet dypere etter øvelsen. | He breathed more deeply after the exercise. |
| Superlativ | dypest | Hun pustet dypest da hun lå på ryggen. | She breathed most deeply when she was lying on her back. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Adverbial dypt is the neuter form of dyp and can take the adjective’s degree forms in this use.
