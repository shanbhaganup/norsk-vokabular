## fritt

**English:** freely, without restriction

**Topics:** General / unassigned

| Form / feature | Value | Norwegian example | English translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grunnform | fritt | Her kan hundene løpe fritt. | Here the dogs can run freely. |
| Komparativ | friere | Hundene beveger seg friere uten bånd. | The dogs move more freely without leads. |
| Superlativ | friest | Hun snakker friest når hun kjenner dem hun snakker med. | She speaks most freely when she knows the people she is talking to. |

**Form status:** Bokmålsordboka headword or usage checked. Examples are original teaching sentences.

**Notes:** Adverbial use of fri. The degree forms compare freedom from restriction.
