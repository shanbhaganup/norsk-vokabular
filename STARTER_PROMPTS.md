# Starter prompts

## Add a word

Add the Bokmål verb «forstå» using templates/02-verb.md. Check vocabulary/ and the reference snapshot for duplicates first. If it already exists in the reference, promote it to a working entry rather than counting it as new. Verify applicable forms and write one natural Norwegian example with an English translation for every form. Update indexes and the migration log.

## Import a list

Compare the list in imports/ with the working vocabulary and reference snapshot. Normalize inflected forms and ignore genuine duplicates. Add only new headword/type/sense entries with verified forms, bilingual examples and source notes. Keep uncertain translations in review/ and produce a merge report.

## Review an entry

Review vocabulary/verbs/skrive.md against its cited dictionary entry. Check every form and its example, including tense context, participle agreement and passive usage. Retain accepted alternatives and identify any uncertain points.

## Practise a topic

Use topics/family.md to give me five short translation exercises. Wait for my answers, then correct them and show the relevant forms. Keep the working vocabulary files unchanged during practice.
