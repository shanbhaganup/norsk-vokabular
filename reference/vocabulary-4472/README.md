## Workbook guide

### Entries

2931

### Language

Norwegian Bokmål. English meanings are retained from the supplied vocabulary lists and may be imperfect.

### Coverage

25 of 28 supplied pages retrieved. Time, travel and work pages could not be retrieved after repeated attempts.

### Topic filtering

Use the Topics filter on each word-type sheet. A word can have several topics. General / unassigned means no topic was supplied on the retrieved pages.

### Missing topic pages

Time, travel and work tags are editorial assignments to words found on the other pages. They are marked in Notes and do not reconstruct the missing pages.

### Deduplication

One row per normalized lemma and word type. Inflected source entries are merged into their lemma. A spelling used as both noun and verb remains on both sheets.

### Source corrections

Incorrect source genders and obvious spelling/headword errors are corrected. Original spellings remain in Source forms merged.

### Noun forms

Entall = singular; flertall = plural; ubestemt = indefinite; bestemt = definite. Article variants and accepted inflection alternatives are separated by /.

### Noun use

Dictionary plurals are included even for mass nouns where they refer to kinds or servings. Plural-only words have no singular. Some uninflected words do not take an ordinary indefinite article.

### Verb tenses

Simple forms come from the dictionary. Compound tense columns combine har/hadde/skal/vil/ville/skulle with dictionary forms. These are grammatical templates, not exhaustive statements of idiomatic usage.

### Modal verbs

Future and conditional templates for modals and impersonal verbs can be uncommon or context-dependent. Norwegian does not change verb forms by person or number.

### Passive and participles

S-passive and present participle have columns. The final tagged-forms column also includes participle agreement and all other forms supplied by the dictionary. Bli-passive is formed with blir/ble/har blitt + past participle where the verb allows it.

### Adjectives

Includes masculine/feminine/neuter, plural, definite singular, comparative and both superlative forms where the dictionary supplies them.

### Comparison with mer/mest

A dash for comparison means no synthetic form was supplied. Mer + adjective and mest + adjective can be used when its meaning is gradable; many absolute adjectives are normally not compared.

### Adverbs

Adverbs are normally invariable. For adjective-derived adverbs, comparison may come from the adjective entry, as identified in Notes.

### Genitive

Productive genitive is normally formed with -s; after s, x or z, use an apostrophe. It is not expanded as a separate dictionary form for every noun.

### Dash (—)

No dictionary form supplied, not applicable, or not verified. Check Form status and Notes. It does not always mean the form is impossible.

### Form status

Dictionary forms = direct lookup; dictionary participle forms = agreement from the related verb; derived compound forms = formed using the final element or base verb. Unverified source entry = no verified paradigm.

### Homographs

Where the dictionary has several entries of the same spelling and word type, their forms may be combined. Meaning and usage determine the correct paradigm. The unrelated rare verb være (to sense) is excluded from være (to be).
